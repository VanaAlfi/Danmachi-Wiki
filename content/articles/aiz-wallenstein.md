**Aiz Wallenstein** is a swordswoman of [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]], known by the title *Kenki*, which Sword Oratoria renders as *Sword Princess*.[@fm01-aiz, fm04-kenki, so04-princess] In DanMachi 1 she saves [[bell-cranell|Bell Cranell]] from a [[minotaur|Minotaur]], and his longing for her drives his Skill, [[skills#liaris-freese|Liaris Freese]].[@fm01-aiz, fm08-skill] Besides Kenki she has an unofficial nickname, *Senki* ({{ja|戦姫}}, a play on Kenki), which adventurers gave her after she wiped out a horde of monsters of Level 5 strength by herself; the English prints it as *senki* ("Lady of Combat") in DanMachi 1, "Battle Princess" in Sword Oratoria 1 and "War Princess" in Sword Oratoria 12. As a child she was called the *Doll Princess*.[@fm01-aiz-senki, so01-aiz-battle-princess, so12-aiz-war-princess, ar02-aiz-doll-princess] She is also known as the *Monster Slayer*.[@ss02-aiz-monster-slayer] She is the heroine of Sword Oratoria.[@so01-heroine]

## Abilities

- **Airiel** is her wind magic. Sword Oratoria connects the wind to her mother.[@so03-update, so09-wind]
- **[[skills#avenger|Avenger]]** is a rare Skill whose effect is a ravenous surge of power; its targets are monsters. Loki ranks its potency highest among all followers.[@so12-avenger] Her Sword Oratoria 10 Status sheet says its effect grows with the depth of her hatred.[@airiel.so10-sheet] In Sword Oratoria 11 she trains with [[ottar|Ottar]] for seven days and learns to control its black flame rather than be consumed by it.[@so11-ottar]
- **Development Abilities:** Hunter, Immunity and Knight, all gained before Level 5; she gains none at Level 5, and Spirit Healing at Level 6 (see [[#levels|Levels]]). Her last Level 5 Status raises her Magic to S 900, although "Virtually no one ever achieved the highest S rating".[@so01-aiz-dev-abilities, so03-aiz-last-level5]
- **Weapon:** Her sword is [[desperate|Desperate]], a weapon with the trait Durandal, "the Unbreaking".[@so01-aiz-desperate]

## Magic {#magic}

{{nocite}} Aiz Wallenstein's spells, with their incantations as printed in the English novels. The combined [[magic|Magic]] page describes every spell on this wiki together.

### Airiel {#airiel}

**Airiel** is Aiz Wallenstein's magic, and her only spell. It is an enchantment: currents of wind gather around her body and sword, raising her attack power and speed and protecting her.[@airiel.so01-airiel, airiel.fm10-airiel] Aiz recognises it as her mother's wind.[@airiel.so09-mother]

- **User:** Aiz Wallenstein[@airiel.so01-airiel]
- **Type:** Wind enchantment[@airiel.so01-airiel, airiel.fm10-airiel]
- **Status entry:** Enchantment; wind element; chant "Awaken, Tempest"[@airiel.so01-sheet, airiel.so09-sheet, airiel.so10-sheet]
- **First cast:** On Floor 12, almost a year after she joined Loki Familia at seven (Sword Oratoria 9 recollection)[@airiel.so09-first]
- **Chant:** Awaken, Tempest (very short; she says only *Tempest*, written *awaken*)[@airiel.so01-airiel, airiel.so01-ja]
- **Stronger form:** Rage, Tempest (said *Tempest*, written *rage*)[@airiel.so02-rage, airiel.so07-rage, airiel.so02-ja]

#### Status entry {#airiel-status-entry}

Aiz's three illustrated Status sheets list Airiel as her only magic, each time with the same three notes: an enchantment, wind element, and the chant "Awaken, Tempest". Her Level 5 sheet in Sword Oratoria 1 calls it a "Magical Endowment (Enchantment)"; her Level 1 sheet in Sword Oratoria 9 and her Level 6 sheet in Sword Oratoria 10 say only "Enchantment"; the three Japanese sheets read the same.[@airiel.so01-sheet, airiel.so09-sheet, airiel.so10-sheet] The Sword Oratoria 10 sheet lists her Skill [[skills#avenger|Avenger]] separately; the two earlier sheets show her Skills only as "???".[@airiel.so10-sheet, airiel.so01-sheet, airiel.so09-sheet]

#### Incantation {#airiel-incantation}

{{nocite}} Airiel's chant is a short phrase, so every printed form can be given in full. The forms below are recorded separately; this wiki does not combine them into one "complete" chant.

In the Japanese, each call is written with a meaning in kanji and a reading printed over it, and the reading is what Aiz says aloud. She says only *Tempest*; the written word changes with the moment (*awaken*, *rage*, *O white wind*, *activate*). The English prints the written meaning together with the spoken name, as in "Awaken, Tempest", or in some volumes the spoken name alone.[@airiel.so01-ja, airiel.so02-ja, airiel.so11-ja, airiel.so12-ja]

{{nocite}} **Lil Rafaga**

*Lil Rafaga* is not a separate chant or spell. It is the name of Aiz's finishing move, a charge along the wind; she calls it out because [[loki|Loki]] joked that shouting a skill's name makes it stronger.[@airiel.so01-rafaga]

#### Effects {#airiel-effects}

- **Speed and attack.** With Airiel her strikes kill in one or two swings, and a borrowed blade is pushed to breaking point by it.[@airiel.so01-twoslash] Later in Sword Oratoria 1 another weapon falls to pieces under Airiel and her aggressive fighting.[@airiel.so01-blade]
- **Protection.** Its wind armour turns aside blood and, in her first use, a wyvern's fire.[@airiel.fm10-airiel, airiel.so09-first]
- **Sensing.** On the surface, where wind can flow freely, she rides part of it on the wind and senses the vibrations of the monsters' roars, which lets her find them quickly.[@airiel.so01-sense]
- **Scale.** In Sword Oratoria 7 the intensified wind is more than the whole hall can hold and pours on into the passages beyond.[@airiel.so07-rage]

#### Costs and limits {#airiel-costs-and-limits}

Holding Airiel at full power for too long exhausts her; in Sword Oratoria 2 its last traces give out mid-fight.[@airiel.so02-overuse] During training with [[ottar|Ottar]] in Sword Oratoria 11 she runs out of Mind before noon, after which the fight is purely physical.[@airiel.so11-mind] A figure for its cost was not located in the checked text.

#### Avenger {#airiel-avenger}

Tempest Avenger is Airiel joined with Aiz's rare Skill Avenger, a black, violent wind. In Sword Oratoria 12 the black flame nearly consumes her; after it subsides, a pure white "Tempest" carries her into the decisive exchange with Levis.[@airiel.so12-avenger, airiel.so12-white] Having lost to Levis in Sword Oratoria 7, Aiz wins this last exchange: Levis's chest is ripped open, her limbs crumble, and "The creature’s entire body collapsed, turning into a formless pile of soot."[@so07-aiz-lost, so12-levis-end] Avenger is a Skill, not part of the spell.

#### History {#airiel-history}

In Sword Oratoria 9's recollection, almost a year after joining Loki Familia at seven, Aiz first speaks "Awaken, Tempest!" on Floor 12 when [[riveria|Riveria]] shouts at her to call it forth, and her wind blows away the black wyvern's mouth and upper jaw.[@airiel.so09-first] In Sword Oratoria 3, Riveria reports that the red-haired woman Levis first spoke the name "Aria" just after seeing Airiel.[@airiel.so03-aria]

## Family

Aiz tells the creature [[levis|Levis]] that Aria is her mother.[@so03-aria] Riveria confirms that Aiz has spirit blood.[@so05-spirit] Her memories in Sword Oratoria 10 recall losing her mother and then her father when her home was destroyed.[@so10-parents] Her swordsmanship comes from her father: "Her father’s sword techniques lived on in Aiz."[@so09-aiz-father-sword] During [[elegia|Elegia]] in DanMachi 15 she promises a child to defeat the dragon of the last of the [[three-great-quests|Three Great Quests]], and "for Aiz the grudge would be personal".[@fm15-aiz-dragon]

Her favourite food is [[jyaga-maru-kun|Jyaga Maru Kun]], one of her "secret guilty pleasures"; Loki first bought it for her in her early years with Loki Familia.[@so04-jmk, so01-jmk, so09-jmk]

Aiz leaves flowers at the grave of the ancient hero [[albert|Albert]], also called Valdstejn. Bell notices the likeness between *Valdstejn* and *Wallenstein* and suspects a connection, but it remains his theory.[@fm15-grave, fm16-albert, fm20-albert]

## Sword Oratoria and Astrea Record {#sword-oratoria-and-astrea-record}

**Childhood.** Aiz comes to Loki Familia at seven, and from the start "Aiz wished for power."[@so09-aiz-power] [[riveria|Riveria]] teaches her and Finn and Gareth train her (see [[#levels|Levels]]).[@so09-joined] About a year later her Status stops rising; after a quarrel in which she shouts at Riveria, "I’m not your doll!", she runs into the Dungeon alone, where the god [[thanatos|Thanatos]], guarded by followers of the [[evils|Evils]], confronts her and a wyvern attacks. Her wind awakens, and afterward Riveria tells her, "Aiz…I can’t be your mother…but…I want to be by your side."[@so09-aiz-wyvern] Astrea Record 2 recalls that an evil god lured her, then Level 1, "down to the twelfth floor" and there "unsealed his arcanum and summoned the Black Wyvern".[@ar02-aiz-wyvern]

**The Great Conflict.** As a child she fights the Evils in the [[great-conflict|Great Conflict]], although Riveria "had never wanted Aiz to take part in the battle in the first place" and warns her, "Never forget that it’s people you’re fighting, not monsters."[@ar02-aiz-war] In the rain she duels the masked [[lyu-leon|Lyu]], who tells her, "I see why they call you the Sword Princess".[@ar02-aiz-lyu] Beside Riveria she attacks [[alfia|Alfia]], and afterward calls her "Stronger than Finn! Stronger than anyone!"[@ar02-aiz-alfia] On [[floor-18|Floor 18]] in Astrea Record 3 the dragon [[delphyne|Delphyne]] throws her into a rage: "Aiz’s face twisted in madness and rage."[@ar03-aiz-rage] Riveria sees that "She’s combining her magic with her skill!"[@ar03-aiz-skill] Lyu and [[alize-lovell|Alize]] bring her back to her senses, and her wind-wrapped sword "shattered the purple orb into a million pieces".[@ar03-aiz-orb] Riveria then scolds and embraces her: "You idiot, Aiz. Never do something so stupid ever again."[@ar03-aiz-riveria]

**Sword Oratoria.** When Sword Oratoria begins, her Level 5 Status has stopped growing: "Aiz was stuck."[@so01-aiz-stuck] After the red-haired [[levis|Levis]] overpowers her in [[rivira|Rivira]], she curses herself ("Aiz Wallenstein, such a weakling.") and stays behind to defeat the [[floor-37|Floor 37]] floor boss alone.[@so02-aiz-weakling, so02-udaeus] Her real reason for training [[bell-cranell|Bell]] is "to learn his secret—to discover what was behind his extraordinary, remarkable growth".[@so04-aiz-secret] On [[floor-59|Floor 59]], after [[lefiya|Lefiya]]'s spell turns aside the [[corrupted-spirit|demi-spirit]]'s attack, "she swung her sword of wind with everything she had".[@so04-aiz-spirit] When she lets the vouivre [[wiene|Wiene]] go in Sword Oratoria 10, "Aiz broke her vow, the precious promise she’d made with herself: that monsters were to be killed."[@so10-aiz-vow] She asks [[ottar|Ottar]] to train her, and [[freya|Freya]] agrees on a condition: "Let’s make a contract, Sword Princess."[@so11-aiz-contract] Loki later explains that "The corrupted spirit first noticed her existence when she used Airiel in the Dungeon after joining my familia."[@so12-aiz-noticed] In her final fight with Levis the black flame nearly consumes her; "Aiz had defeated Levis with his ringing bell," and without it she "would have been consumed by the black flames".[@so12-aiz-bell]
