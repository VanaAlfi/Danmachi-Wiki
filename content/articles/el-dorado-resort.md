**El Dorado Resort** is [[orario|Orario]]'s number one gambling establishment, known as "the Paradise City’s Grand Casino" after the city of Santorio Vega that financed it.[@fc01-owner, fc01-grand] It is the setting of "Crush the Grand Casino!" in Familia Chronicle 1, in which [[lyu-leon|Lyu]] and [[syr-flover|Syr]] rescue a girl bought by its owner.[@fc01-grand, fc01-anna]

## The casinos of Orario {#casinos}

Orario once lacked entertainment. To satisfy the gods, the city welcomed foreign money and know-how from places such as [[geography#world-powers-and-nations-maelstra|Mayrustra]], the Country of Opera, and the Paradise City, Santorio Vega. The Theater and the casinos of the Shopping District came from this.[@fc01-district] Gambling became the city's biggest industry after magic-stone items. The Guild had to tread carefully around the casinos, and their foreign investors took full control, making them practically extraterritorial: "the one place in Orario that the law can’t reach".[@fc01-district] {{inference}} The *Maelstra* of Sword Oratoria 14, whose "famed opera" Finn wants to see, is probably the same country.[@so14-maelstra]

- **El Dorado Resort:** financed and established by Santorio Vega, it has the Guild's cooperation and contracts [[ganesha-familia|Ganesha Familia]] for security.[@fc01-grand, fc01-district]
- **Entry:** only the rich get in. Holders of a Gold Card have special passes, and Bell enters on the introduction of [[mord-latro|Mord]], who holds one.[@fc01-entry, fc01-bell]
- **The vault:** under the casino, an [[metals#adamantite|adamantite]] vault holds its riches, a room befitting a place billed as "The Golden City".[@fc01-vault]
- **Santorio Vega:** Sword Oratoria 13 lists it among the world's lands as "the city of pleasures".[@so13-lands]

## Terry Cervantes (Ted) {#ted}

**Ted** is a dwarf who runs El Dorado Resort as **Terry Cervantes** ({{ja|テリー・セルバンティス}})[@ted.fc01-ja], in a fine black suit, with slicked-back hair and a well-kept beard.[@fc01-meeting] He was once the bookie of an establishment that ran illegal gambling dens in Orario. His goddess was exiled and returned to the heavens, but her Status remains on his back. [[astrea-familia|Astrea Familia]] once caught him in a roundup of criminals.[@fc01-ted, fc01-astrea] He begged the goddess [[astrea|Astrea]] for mercy with his forehead to the floor, and she granted it with compassion, perhaps wanting to believe that he could change.[@fc01-ted-astrea]

- **The stolen name:** the real Terry Cervantes, sent from Santorio Vega, died in an accident shortly before arriving; Ted seized on the death and took his identity, bribed Santorio Vega's investigators, and had his bodyguards dispose of people who knew the real man.[@fc01-identity] He owed his success in the role to connections made in the city's Dark Age.[@fc01-ted-identity]
- **As owner:** he was apparently the one pulling the strings behind the Corporation and the street toughs, and he bought [[#anna-kreiz|Anna Kreiz]].[@fc01-ted-anna] A lecherous owner, he flaunted a collection of beautiful women before his guests, and he believed himself the king of the gambling paradise, even of Orario.[@fc01-ted-collection, fc01-ted-king] Two of his bodyguards passed themselves off as the bounty hunter Black Fist and the assassin Black Cat, skilled enough to keep even a Level 4 like Lyu from attacking carelessly, until she declared them impostors.[@fc01-ted-bodyguards]
- **Exposure:** Lyu, who had once cornered him and recognised his methods, told him he was not of Santorio Vega and that his name was Ted, and in a panic he ordered his bodyguards to kill her.[@fc01-ted-exposed] As his bodyguards fell he dragged Anna off with a dwarf's strength into an underground vault of adamantite, but Lyu's magic blew it open, for he had been sold inferior goods, and her punch sent him into a pile of gold coins.[@fc01-ted-vault] When he came to, [[syr-flover|Syr Flover]] whispered something in his ear that left him stiff and gasping, and he was still in a daze when [[ganesha-familia|Ganesha Familia]] arrived.[@fc01-ted-syr]
- **Fate:** he confessed everything under the Guild's questioning, and Santorio Vega cut him loose. He is held in one of the Guild's solitary cells, still shaking.[@fc01-identity, fc01-after] The women he had kept were reportedly given generous compensation by the Guild and Santorio Vega and sent home, and El Dorado Resort, under a new owner, went on as a gambling paradise.[@fc01-ted-fate]

## Anna Kreiz {#anna-kreiz}

**Anna Kreiz** is the daughter of Karen and Huey Kreiz, who work in a small magic-stone store in the western district.[@fc01-kreiz] She is pretty and good-natured, has turned down proposals from male gods, and delivers flowers across the city for the shop where she works.[@fc01-target] At the casino Terry presents her among his mistresses ("Pleased to…meet you…My name is Anna."), attractive enough that the wealthy guests are "nodding in appreciation"; she does not understand the card game and is "on the verge of tears" as he pulls her close.[@fc01-anna-vip] When Ted drags her into the vault, she throws herself flat just before it explodes, and Lyu, still disguised as the count, "wrapped one arm around Anna’s waist as she blushed".[@fc01-anna-vault] At the carriage home she begins to confess her love to her rescuer ("you put your life on the line to save me") until Lyu reveals: "I’m a woman, just like you". "The romantic tale that had set her heart aflutter crumbled pitifully."[@fc01-anna-confess] Later she catches her father drinking in a bar: "You promised you wouldn’t drink anymore!"[@fc01-anna-father] In Minor Myths and Legends 2 she sighs at her window, her mother sees "the face of a girl in love", and she confesses that she has fallen in love with "The brave, dashing young woman who saved me", to her parents' dismay.[@ss02-anna-sigh, ss02-anna]

## Huey and Karen Kreiz {#huey-and-karen-kreiz}

**Huey and Karen Kreiz** ({{ja|ヒューイ}}, {{ja|カレン}}, family name {{ja|クレーズ}})[@fc01-kreiz-ja] are a married couple and the parents of [[#anna-kreiz|Anna Kreiz]], whose family is helped by [[lyu-leon|Lyu Leon]].[@fc01-parents, fc01-family, fc01-promise] They earn their living by manufacturing [[magic-stone|magic-stone]] products and helping at a shop, and live in [[orario|Orario]]'s western district before Huey's gambling costs them their house.[@fc01-parents, fc01-kreiz-wager]
