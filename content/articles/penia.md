**Penia** is the goddess who rules over poverty.[@so07-penia] She settled in [[daedalus-street|Daedalus Street]] centuries ago and is called its ruler; she has no followers of her own.[@so07-centuries, so10-ruler, so12-cover] In Sword Oratoria 12 [[loki|Loki]] reveals that [[dionysus|Dionysus]] made her drunk on his divine wine and converted his own followers to her, as [[penia-familia|Penia Familia]]. He then killed her during the first assault on [[knossos|Knossos]], so that the sealing of their Statuses made it look as if he himself had been sent back.[@so12-puppet, so12-convert, so12-killed]

## The goddess of poverty

Penia looks like an old woman. She wears rags that barely pass for a robe, and her long white hair hangs in tangles; her divine nature keeps her skin clean, but not her dignity.[@so07-penia] Loki says she is a joke among the gods, "Penia the disease", who in Heaven went about snatching everyone's savings: "poverty on legs".[@so07-penia] Penia complains that only [[hestia|Hestia]] does not treat her like a babbling old kook, and Loki later counts her among Hestia's odd connections.[@so07-hestia, so11-hestia]

In the square of Daedalus Street she preaches that wealth corrupts the soul. Her listeners pile up offerings of money at her feet, while she gorges on meat and wine. To Loki Familia it looks like hypocrisy, but she gives most of the offerings back to the poor of Daedalus Street, and nearly everyone in the slums supports her. [[riveria|Riveria]] calls her "the matriarch of the slums".[@so07-preaching, so07-giving] It is less a Familia than a new religion, since she has never formed an actual Familia.[@so07-preaching]

## Sword Oratoria 7 and 10

In Sword Oratoria 7 Loki asks whether she has seen the man in the portrait they are hunting. Penia says she has never seen him around there, and that she would not hand over information so freely anyway; offered money, she storms off with her offerings.[@so07-centuries, so07-refusal]

In Sword Oratoria 10 she meets [[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz]] in Daedalus Street during the hunt for the Xenos. She tells Aiz that the slum is "far too rich", full of love, cooperation and happy people. She prefers the age when monsters ran wild and everyone was unhappy, calling it "an honorable poverty".[@so10-ruler, so10-rich, so10-poverty] Aiz answers that this is not true, and Penia, delighted, calls it the difference between a god's view and a mortal's. As Aiz leaves to answer a monster's cry, Penia calls after her: "Live like your life depends on it".[@so10-poverty, so10-farewell]

## Dionysus's scapegoat

In Sword Oratoria 12, Loki tells Dionysus that the god sent back in the pillar of light was Penia, not him; the children of the slums had told her they had not seen Lady Penia lately.[@so12-pillar] Dionysus, who had made himself drunk on his divine wine, had also got Penia drunk and made her his puppet. He then converted his own followers to her: all the [[dionysus-familia|Dionysus Familia]] adventurers in the first assault on Knossos, from his second-in-command Aura Moriel down, had in reality been Penia Familia.[@so12-puppet, so12-convert] He chose her because a goddess of poverty with no followers had no one to notice what was happening.[@so12-cover] Three of his followers who sobered up during their Status update fled, and a creature killed them; Dionysus then blamed their deaths on an unknown enemy.[@so12-three] That Familia is described on [[penia-familia|Penia Familia]].

Loki recognised the wine: when they first met Penia in Daedalus Street, she was holding a bottle with the same overflowing-goblet label as the one in Dionysus's cellar. Dionysus admits he had warned her not to drink more than necessary, and that she had been drinking extra behind his back.[@so12-label] During the first assault he separated from Loki and the adventurers and, where his scapegoat had been brought beforehand, killed the bound and gagged Penia with a dagger. When she was sent back, Aura and the others, their abilities sealed, were wiped out.[@so12-killed] He later draws the same grape-handled dagger again.[@so12-dagger]

In Sword Oratoria 13 [[royman|Royman]]'s official account tells the city that "Dionysius Familia", its god and "Goddess Penia of Daedalus Street" died in the struggle against a confederate of [[ikelos|Ikelos]], and thanks them for their sacrifice.[@so13-account]
