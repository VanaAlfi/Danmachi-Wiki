**Falgar Batros** is a [[races#war-tiger|war tiger]] of [[hermes-familia|Hermes Familia]], a big greatsword fighter who joined at about the same time as [[asfi|Asfi]] and works as her aide.[@ar02-gate, ar02-speech, ar02-aide] In Astrea Record 2, after the death of the Familia's captain [[hermes-familia#lydis|Lydis]], it is Falgar who persuades the despairing Asfi that she is the right person to lead.[@ar02-speech]

## Character

Falgar stands head and shoulders above his peers.[@ar02-gate] Astrea Record 2 says that he and Asfi have shared many joys and sorrows, and that her brains and his brawn make a fearsome combination; apart from [[hermes|Hermes]] and Lydis, no one knows Asfi as well as he does.[@ar02-speech] In Astrea Record 1 Asfi complains that she is vice-captain only because Hermes forced her, and that Falgar would have suited the post much better.[@ar01-vice] He in turn admits, with a smile, that part of the reason he urged her to lead was that he does not want to be captain himself.[@ar02-speech]

| Astrea Record 2 | At a barricaded city gate he pleads with a panicking crowd not to leave, then carries people away from the Evils' fire bombs.[@ar02-gate] He tells Asfi, now captain after Lydis's death, that her plans would leave the east of the city undefended; when [[gareth|Gareth]] asks Hermes Familia to track the enemy leaders, Falgar answers for her.[@ar02-aide] He then urges her on, as recorded below. He and the rest of the Familia join Asfi against the cultists; his greatsword cuts through their lines while her bombs sow confusion.[@ar02-deputy] When [[olivas-act|Olivas]] toys with the fallen Asfi, Falgar cannot cut his way through to her, and the Familia is left on its last legs.[@ar02-olivas] Too injured to fight, he later asks the crowd to lend [[astrea-familia|Astrea Familia]] their strength.[@ar02-crowd] |
| Astrea Record 3 | Walking through the emptied city with [[raul-nord|Raul]], he calls it a ghost town and recognises [[noir-dyne-and-bahra|Noir]], "the Bowstring Blade".[@ar03-ghost] He defends the southern stronghold, the Casino, with dozens of the [[ishtar-familia#berbera|Berbera]]; when Asfi arrives he orders the defenders to stop defending and attack.[@ar03-casino, ar03-charge] He cannot leave his post to save the mages being killed, knowing the civilians would die if he did.[@ar03-post] When [[ottar|Ottar]] wipes out a horde of monsters with a single slash, Falgar names it "the power of a Level Seven".[@ar03-level7] |

When Asfi, in Astrea Record 2, begs him to take over because she cannot be the captain they need, Falgar takes her by the shoulders. Like Hermes, he says, he thinks she is the best person for the job because she cares more than anyone else. She worries herself sick because she knows what needs to be done, and her analysis is "no less than Braver's".[@ar02-speech] The narration calls this the fulfilment of his promise to Lydis to watch over and support the new captain. Asfi recovers and gives her orders, and she sends him to lead the Familia with Thane while she scouts alone.[@ar02-speech] At the end of the volume she tells him, "You were right, Falgar. I do know what needs to be done."[@ar02-end]
