**Abnormal Resistance** is a [[development-ability|Development Ability]] that appears on Status cards printed in Sword Oratoria.[@so06-cards, so12-lefiya]

## Where it appears

| Card | Rank | Other abilities on the card |
|---|---|---|
| Aiz Wallenstein, Sword Oratoria 6 (Level 6) | G | Hunter G, Swordsman H, Spirit Healing I[@so06-cards] |
| [[lefiya|Lefiya Viridis]], Sword Oratoria 6 | I | Mage H[@so06-cards] |
| Lefiya Viridis, Sword Oratoria 12 (Level 4) | I | Conjure H, Magic Resistance I[@so12-lefiya] |

## Immunity {#immunity}

[[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz]]'s cards in Sword Oratoria 1 and 11 list *Immunity* at rank G in the same position where her Sword Oratoria 6 card has Abnormal Resistance G.[@so01-aiz, so11-aiz, so06-cards] Immunity is described in DanMachi 4 as protecting against monster poison and other ailments.[@fm04-immunity]

> [!INFERENCE] Probably one ability under two names
> Because the positions and ranks match, Abnormal Resistance is probably another English rendering of Immunity, in the same way that Aiz's cards alternate Knight with Swordsman and Regen with Spirit Healing. The novels do not say so outright.[@so01-aiz, so06-cards, so11-aiz]
