A **quest** is a request that an adventurer undertakes in exchange for a reward supplied by the client.[@so01-definition] Clients include [[familia|Familia]] groups, merchants and the [[guild|Guild]], while ordinary citizens can place requests through other venues.[@so01-definition, fm04-clients] The word also names the [[three-great-quests|Three Great Quests]], the charge laid on Orario to slay three powerful monsters that escaped from the Dungeon in ancient times.[@fm06-quest-great]

## Posting, acceptance and payment {#posting-acceptance-and-payment}

Guild notices are pinned to a bulletin board and record the requested work, reward, client signature and Familia [[emblem|emblem]].[@fm04-posting] [[lilliluka-erde|Lilliluka Erde]] explains that the Guild's posted clients include Familias and merchants, while taverns gather ordinary citizens' requests and work the Guild does not approve.[@fm04-clients] Adventurers remove chosen notices and submit them at reception, where registration prevents duplicate acceptance.[@so02-registration] After completion, they present the contract and proof or requested items at the counter to receive the reward held by the Guild.[@so02-registration] Available work includes collecting monster drops and protecting caravans, and successfully completing official quests contributes to the Guild's evaluation and a Familia's [[familia-rank|rank]].[@so02-work] Most quests concern the middle levels and below, so most of those issued by Familias are taken by upper-class adventurers.[@fm04-quest-levels] A mission is different: by issuing one, an urgent quest, the Guild can requisition the power and resources of every Familia.[@so03-quest-mission]
