**Apollo** is a god known among the gods as *Phallus the Passionate*.[@fm06-apollo] In DanMachi 5 he declares that he will claim [[bell-cranell|Bell Cranell]] as his own, and in DanMachi 6 he challenges [[hestia|Hestia]] to a [[war-game|War Game]] to get him.[@fm05-apollo, fm06-apollo] His Familia is described on [[apollo-familia|Apollo Familia]].

## Character {#character}

- **A god who loves too much:** in DanMachi 8 Bell recalls that Apollo is also called by his title Phallus and "is a god who loves too much": once a child captures his interest, "he loves them fully and deeply until the end". When one of his children dies he weeps "day in and day out", wears the child's trinket "day and night" and treats a tree growing from the grave "like a holy site".[@fm08-loves] He once asked Hestia to marry him, which is why she dislikes him.[@fm06-banquet, fm08-loves]
- **Passion:** Bell sees in him "Passion that has gone too far, burning bright like the sun", "A god whose desires carry him to comical lengths". Man or woman does not matter to him, and "Once he sees something he likes, he’ll stop at nothing in his lustful pursuit"; [[daphne|Daphne]] says he "chases any child he likes to the ends of the earth. At least until he has them."[@fm06-banquet, fm06-chases]
- **Bell:** Apollo "had a habit of indulging in everything new and fresh" and probably first heard of Bell with the rumours of a new record holder; his craving for the boy "was on the verge of driving him insane".[@fm06-craving]
- **A byword:** in DanMachi 20 the gods still say, "We don’t need another Apollo."[@fm20-another]

## The War Game

Apollo hosts a Celebration, at a venue managed by the Guild in the north of Orario, to which each deity must bring a dressed-up follower ("what could be more delightful?").[@fm06-banquet, ss01-venue] There, with his followers "crying as if on cue", he blames Hestia Familia for the brawl in a bar, and after Hestia's refusal makes his declaration of a War Game; his Familia then hunts Bell through the city, and in front of his manor Hestia throws Luan's glove into his face.[@fm06-banquet, fm06-glove] Apollo stages a grievance to force the War Game, and under pressure agrees to allow one outsider, who must come from a Familia outside [[orario|Orario]]; at the Denatus, [[freya|Freya]] goads "the god who loves too passionately" ("My, my, Apollo. Are you frightened?"), and many male gods side with her.[@fm06-apollo, fm06-outsider, fm06-freya] In the Castle Siege his Familia defends. Bell defeats his captain, [[hyacinthus|Hyacinthus]], in single combat, and [[hestia-familia|Hestia Familia]] wins.[@fm06-hyacinthus] Hestia then takes all the money held in Apollo's name; facing her afterwards, Apollo finds that "Even in Tenkai, he had never seen Hestia be so terrifying."[@fm06-money, fm06-terrifying]

## Exile

After losing, Apollo releases his followers, disbands [[apollo-familia|Apollo Familia]] and is escorted out of Orario for good; he is exiled, not sent back to the heavens. Only a small group, including Hyacinthus, leaves with him; other former members go their own ways.[@fm06-apollo, fm08-alumni] His three-storey manor passes to Hestia Familia and becomes its new home.[@fm06-manor]
