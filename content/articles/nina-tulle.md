**Nina Tulle** is a thirteen-year-old half-elf, a Level 2 student of the [[school-district|School District]]'s [[balder#balder-class|Balder Class]] and the younger sister of [[eina-tulle|Eina Tulle]].[@fm19-nina] She chooses to become an adventurer and sets her sights on [[hestia-familia|Hestia Familia]].[@fm19-nina]

## Magic

- **[[#magia-kreis|Magia Kreis]]** heals at range, passes Mind to others and lengthens buffs from magic and items.[@fm20-kreis, fm20-buffs]
- [[#lagriell-krisheim|Lagriell Krisheim]] is a rare spell she manifests in DanMachi 19: a healing barrier that clears poison, paralysis and other negative effects and prevents curses and mental attacks, though not direct attacks.[@fm19-lagriell]

### Magia Kreis {#magia-kreis}

**Magia Kreis** is a ranged healing spell used by Nina Tulle, a half-elf student of the [[school-district|School District]] and the younger sister of [[bell-cranell|Bell]]'s [[guild|Guild]] adviser, [[eina-tulle|Eina]].[@magia-kreis.fm19-nina, magia-kreis.fm20-kreis] Besides healing, it passes Mind to others and makes buffs from magic and items last longer.[@magia-kreis.fm20-kreis, magia-kreis.fm20-buffs]

- **User:** Nina Tulle[@magia-kreis.fm20-kreis]
- **Effects:** Healing at range; Mind transfer; longer buffs[@magia-kreis.fm20-kreis, magia-kreis.fm20-buffs]
- **Chant:** One short line (quoted below)[@magia-kreis.fm19-kris, magia-kreis.fm20-chant]
- **Printed as:** Magia Kris (DanMachi 19); Magia Kreis (DanMachi 20)[@magia-kreis.fm19-kris, magia-kreis.fm20-kreis]

#### Name {#magia-kreis-name}

DanMachi 19 prints the spell as *Magia Kris*; DanMachi 20 prints *Magia Kreis*.[@magia-kreis.fm19-kris, magia-kreis.fm20-kreis] This wiki uses the later form.

#### Incantation {#magia-kreis-incantation}

{{nocite}} The chant is a single short line and is printed with the same words each time, so it is given in full.

- **Chant and release:** "Swaying stem, breath of white. Sing of flowers, and of the pristine hill—Magia Kreis!"[@magia-kreis.fm20-chant]
- DanMachi 19 prints the same line ending "—Magia Kris!".[@magia-kreis.fm19-kris]
- The DanMachi 20 chant in the Japanese is {{ja|揺れる聖輪、吐息は白く。花々謳う、清浄の丘|yureru seirin, toiki wa shiroku. hanabana utau, seijō no oka}}. Its first noun, *seirin*, means holy ring, where the English prints "stem".[@magia-kreis.fm20-chant]
- In DanMachi 20 she chants the line first, ending "I’m ready!"; when Lilly and Lyu press her to heal Welf and Mikoto at range instead of having them fall back, she releases it: "M-Magia Kreis!"[@magia-kreis.fm20-buffs]

When cast, fragments of white magic like flower petals envelop the targets.[@magia-kreis.fm19-kris, magia-kreis.fm20-buffs]

#### Effects {#magia-kreis-effects}

In DanMachi 20, while Nina interns with [[hestia-familia|Hestia Familia]], she tells the party that Magia Kreis also extends buffs from magic and items. [[haruhime|Haruhime]]'s Level Boost, which lasts twenty minutes at Level 2, is observed to last about five minutes longer under it.[@magia-kreis.fm20-buffs, magia-kreis.fm20-twenty] Nina uses it again during the fighting later in the volume.[@magia-kreis.fm20-later]

Nina has a second, separate spell, [[#lagriell-krisheim|Lagriell Krisheim]], a healing barrier that clears poison, paralysis and other negative effects and prevents curses and mental attacks, though it cannot stop direct physical or magic attacks.[@magia-kreis.fm19-lagriell]

{{nocite}} Notable uses and open questions for Magia Kreis are on the combined page: [[magic#magia-kreis|Magic § Magia Kreis]].

### Lagriell Krisheim {#lagriell-krisheim}

**Lagriell Krisheim** is a rare magic that Nina Tulle manifests, a barrier that "could cleanse any and every debuff".[@lagriell-krisheim.fm19-effect] Her other spell is [[#magia-kreis|Magia Kreis]].

- **User:** Nina Tulle[@lagriell-krisheim.fm19-cast]
- **Type:** Rare magic: a purifying healing barrier[@lagriell-krisheim.fm19-effect]
- **Blocks:** Debuffs, poison, paralysis, curses, mental attacks; not direct attacks[@lagriell-krisheim.fm19-effect]
- **Chant:** Six printed pieces (DanMachi 19)[@lagriell-krisheim.fm19-chant, lagriell-krisheim.fm20-dragma]

#### Incantation {#lagriell-krisheim-incantation}

DanMachi 19 prints six pieces of the chant as Nina casts it, given in full at [[magic#lagriell-krisheim-incantation|Magic § Lagriell Krisheim]]; DanMachi 20 prints only pieces of the same chant.[@lagriell-krisheim.fm19-chant, lagriell-krisheim.fm20-camp, lagriell-krisheim.fm20-dragma]

She casts it on the run, imitating the Concurrent Casting she had only just been taught.[@lagriell-krisheim.fm19-cast, lagriell-krisheim.fm19-chant] |

#### Effect {#lagriell-krisheim-effect}

A field of white flowers, or of dancing white feathers or petals, bursts out around her.[@lagriell-krisheim.fm19-cast]

- **Cleansing:** a midsize healing barrier that clears poison, paralysis and other negative effects, and also keeps out curses and psychological attacks.[@lagriell-krisheim.fm19-effect]
- **Healing:** it fills everyone inside with a cleansing light that heals continuously, and it pushes back even a [[monsters#blue-dragon|blue dragon]]'s aurora breath.[@lagriell-krisheim.fm19-effect]
- **Miasma:** in the [[geography#the-north-of-the-continent-land-beyond-the-wall|black wasteland]] of DanMachi 20 it purifies the poisonous air around their camp so the group can rest.[@lagriell-krisheim.fm20-camp] Later it keeps out a centipede dragon's miasma, the "dragma" that impedes healing magic.[@lagriell-krisheim.fm20-dragma]

#### Limits {#lagriell-krisheim-limits}

- **Direct attacks:** it is a ward, not a barrier, so it has no way to stop direct physical and magic attacks; when the dragon gives up its breath and dives to bite, Nina can do nothing.[@lagriell-krisheim.fm19-effect]
- **Strain:** holding the field drains her. She sinks to her knees against the miasma and, exhausted, calls [[bell-cranell|Bell]] by his cover name.[@lagriell-krisheim.fm20-dragma] At the camp she falls asleep as soon as she is persuaded to rest.[@lagriell-krisheim.fm20-camp]

{{nocite}} Notable uses and open questions for Lagriell Krisheim are on the combined page: [[magic#lagriell-krisheim|Magic § Lagriell Krisheim]].

## History

In DanMachi 19, when [[bell-cranell|Bell]] joins her class at the School District under the name Rapi Flemish, Nina, a model student, shows him around the school and helps him catch up in his lessons.[@fm19-rapi] Rapi joins the class's 3rd Squad, which she leads; its other members were all moved there from other squads after causing trouble, and it is called the "worst party".[@fm19-squad] When Eina calls out to her at the Guild, Nina runs away; Eina tells Bell that Nina, six years younger, is her sister, and that she herself left home for the School District when Nina was born and almost never went back.[@fm19-sister] Later, cut off with her squad in the Dungeon, Nina confesses to Bell that she enrolled only because Eina had, without a goal of her own, and that she regrets it.[@fm19-footsteps]

In DanMachi 19 she makes peace with Eina.[@fm19-reconcile] Waiting for Eina on her way to work, Nina introduces herself as her younger sister, admits that she was always copying her, and tells her that she could never become Eina Tulle but now has a dream of her own as Nina Tulle.[@fm19-reunion] In DanMachi 20 she arrives at [[hearthstone-manor|Hearthstone Manor]] as an intern, with [[balder|Balder]]'s permission, three days after [[bell-cranell|Bell]] leaves the School District. Hestia welcomes her but leaves open whether she will join.[@fm20-intern] The School District later cuts off all internships, forcing her to leave.[@fm20-cut] Bell sees winning the [[orariad|Orariad]] as a way for her to join without trouble.[@fm20-orariad] She takes part in the [[floors-29-31-and-32#floor-29|Floor 29]] mission and the journey north.[@fm20-nina] In the far north [[leon-verdenberg|Professor Leon]] fights Bell in "a trial by fire" and lets Nina heal him, saying he had asked her along for that purpose; with her help and Leon's, Bell then defeats a centipede dragon.[@fm20-trial]
