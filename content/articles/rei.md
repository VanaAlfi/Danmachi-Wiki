**Rei** is a golden [[monsters#siren|siren]] and one of the founding [[xenos|Xenos]].[@fm09-rei] Disguised on Floor 19, she asks [[bell-cranell|Bell]]'s party whether coexistence is possible, and her song later guides them into the Hidden Village.[@fm09-rei]

## Appearance and powers {#appearance}

Rei is a "golden-feathered siren" whose arms are wings.[@fm09-gold] Under her mask of blood in DanMachi 10 she has "long, dark golden hair, eyes as blue as the sky, and elegant facial features on par with the elves".[@fm10-bete] She sings: to welcome [[wiene|Wiene]] and the guests from the surface to the Hidden Village she flies onto the tallest lamp and lifts "a beautiful voice".[@fm09-song] In battle she sends out waves of sound and shoots feathers from her wings, and in [[knossos|Knossos]] her echolocation lets the Xenos sense their surroundings.[@fm11-feathers, fm11-wave, so11-echo]

## Character {#character}

With [[lido|Lido]] and [[gros|Gros]], Rei has been one of the Xenos "from the start", and she and Lido lead the first groups out when the Xenos move.[@fm09-founders, fm10-leaders] It is Rei who describes the Xenos' dealings with the Guild to Bell's party as "give and take".[@fm09-founders] She is gentle with the younger Xenos: when Wiene frets over Bell's safety she wraps her wings around the girl to calm her.[@fm11-wiene] When the other Xenos remark on how popular Bell is with human women, she says only "…No comment"; teased about her fondness for him, she asks what they are trying to say, and Lido adds that "Her two dreams are to fly in the sky aboveground and be held by the one she loves".[@ss01-dreams]
