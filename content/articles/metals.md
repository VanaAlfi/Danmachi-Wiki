**Rare metals** are the materials of the best weapons and armour in the English novels. Some come from the [[dungeon|Dungeon]] itself: on DanMachi 14's expedition [[welf-crozzo|Welf]] finds "a natural Dungeon ingot, glittering with the sheen of rare metal", and in Sword Oratoria 2 Tiona is delighted to dig up rare metal by chance while breaking a Dungeon wall to make a resting place.[@fm14-ingot, so02-rare] Gnomes, the spirits of the earth, love rare metals, ores and jewels and can find veins of minerals and ores.[@so14-gnomes]

{{nocite}} This page gathers the named metals and ores, one searchable section per metal, like the [[magic|Magic]] and [[monsters|Monsters]] pages. The weapons and armour made from them are on [[equipment|Equipment]].

## Adamantite {#adamantite}

"Adamantite was among the finest of rare metals." ({{ja|超硬金属}}, written *super-hard metal*)[@adamantite.fm10-ja] It is exceedingly hard, which makes it difficult to process and forge; even famous High Smiths struggle to control it.[@fm14-adamantite] It is mined in the Dungeon, and the purest comes from the deep levels.[@fc01-vault, fc01-purity] [[welf-crozzo|Welf Crozzo]] says adamantite is found only in the Dungeon, rarely falling from its walls; finds in the upper levels are reported, but what is brought back comes mostly from the lower and deep levels; being hard to find, it is apparently worth far more than magic stones.[@fm04-adamantite-origin] He adds that in some monsters the fangs and claws take on its metallic properties, markedly weaker than the original ore.[@fm04-adamantite-monster-properties] Adamantite and other special minerals make the Dungeon magnetic, preventing the use of compasses.[@fm05-adamantite-magnetism] In Familia Chronicle 1 Ted, who runs the casino El Dorado Resort under a false name, buys large amounts of it from merchants and Familias to build its underground vault.[@fc01-vault] [[lyu-leon|Lyu Leon]] explains that its hardness is tied to its purity.[@fc01-adamantite-purity] She judges the casino vault's material to come from the upper and middle levels, weak enough for her magic to penetrate.[@fc01-adamantite-purity]

[[knossos|Knossos]] has "hallways constructed with adamantite" behind its orichalcum doors.[@fm10-knossos, so07-knossos] The adamantite there is covered with stone.[@fm10-adamantite-knossos]

In Sword Oratoria 1 Tiona's [[urga|Urga]] is a custom weapon forged from a large quantity of adamantite.[@so01-adamantite-urga] In DanMachi 10 [[fels|Fels]]'s [[equipment#golem|Golem]] is made entirely of deep-level, high-purity adamantite and valued at more than one billion valis.[@fm10-adamantite-golem] In DanMachi 14 the natural ingot Welf finds on the expedition is adamantite, and he forges it into a magic sword in the Dungeon itself, an act even [[tsubaki|Tsubaki Collbrande]] had never attempted.[@fm14-adamantite-forge] In DanMachi 15 Welf's own magic sword is forged from adamantite too.[@fm15-adamantite-sword] In DanMachi 20 Welf alloys adamantite with [[#mythril|mythril]] for Bell's custom greatsword, using metal [[hephaistos|Hephaistos]] awarded him after the Familia war.[@fm20-adamantite-alloy]

**Dir adamantite** is "a lighter, processed version of the metal".[@fm14-adamantite] Welf forges Bell's armour from it: durable and lightweight, by DanMachi 12 it is on its "fifth reincarnation".[@fm12-armor] In DanMachi 13 its shield, which had withstood even the black minotaur's blows, is demolished.[@fm13-shield] In DanMachi 15 a [[healer|healer]]'s cast is made of dir adamantite instead of plaster.[@fm15-cast] The text prints both *dir adamantite* and *dir-adamantite*; the Japanese reads it *dir* in every volume, written *processed adamantite* {{small|printed *dual adamantite* in DanMachi 10, 11 and 13, and *dual-adamantite* in DanMachi 11}}, for the same armour (see [[equipment#pyonkichi|Pyonkichi]]).[@fm12-armor, fm14-adamantite, fm10-dual, fm11-dual, fm13-dual, fm10-dir-ja]

## Mythril {#mythril}

"Lighter and stronger than iron, mythril was also far more malleable and easier to work with."[@fm01-mythril] The [[hestia-knife|Hestia Knife]] is forged from Hephaistos's mythril ({{ja|精製金属}}, written *refined metal*; in Sword Oratoria 11 {{ja|真の銀}}, written *true silver*).[@mythril.fm07-ja, mythril.so11-ja, fm01-knife] Forged mythril is very strong (wound in layers, it keeps even an upper-class adventurer from breaking free quickly) and very conductive of magic.[@fm07-shackles] Welf explains that mithril is easy to work with and conducts magical power well, so "Mithril weapons are common for fighters who combine magic and ordinary combat ability".[@fm12-welf]

- Bete's mythril boots, [[equipment#frosvirt|Frosvirt]], absorb magic.[@so03-frosvirt]
- In Meren a mythril grating seals the sewer outlet against large monsters, and expensive ingots such as mythril are used on rich men's boats or large ships.[@so06-grate, so06-boats]
- In Sword Oratoria 11 [[asfi|Asfi]] holds up a mythril orb, the key she made for Knossos.[@so11-key]

**Name:** DanMachi 1, 7, 8 and 10, Astrea Record 3 and the Sword Oratoria volumes print *mythril*; DanMachi 12, 18 and 20 print *mithril*.[@fm01-mythril, fm12-welf]

## Orichalcum {#orichalcum}

Orichalcum ({{ja|最硬金属}}, written *hardest metal*)[@orichalcum.fm10-ja] is "a masterpiece ingot that can be forged into unbreakable Durandal-class items" and "surpasses even adamantite". It is the hardest rare metal in the world, made by blending various materials with human and demi-human techniques, and all but impossible to destroy.[@fm10-orichalcum] Sword Oratoria 7 calls it the master ingot of the Durandal from which Superior-grade weapons such as Aiz's [[desperate|Desperate]] are forged, "easily the highest-grade material in the entire world"; its method of manufacture, like mythril's, is said to have been first established in the Ancient Times, when the monsters forced the races to unite.[@so07-orichalcum] That was before smithing abilities or the gods' blessing existed.[@so07-orichalcum-antiquity]

The doors of [[knossos|Knossos]] are orichalcum.[@fm10-knossos, so07-knossos] In DanMachi 10, when Bell holds out the magic item, the crimson jewel set in the door responds and the door rises open.[@fm10-orichalcum-doors] Rarer than adamantite, it is used only for the doors at key points.[@so07-orichalcum-scarcity] In Sword Oratoria 11 three pillars of adamantite or [[#valmars|valmars]] brace opened doors against being shut from afar, and the doors' weight cannot break them.[@so11-orichalcum-braces] In the same volume a divine pillar of light smashes even the orichalcum doors.[@so11-orichalcum-pillar]

The [[school-district|School District]]'s Alchemy Department is "a world-leader in rare metals work, including the manufacture of orichalcum", and its orichalcum is famed as the master ingot (written *hardest refined metal*).[@fm19-alchemy, fm19-master-ingot-ja] It turns out a steady amount every year, more than anywhere else in the world.[@fm19-orichalcum-alchemy] In DanMachi 19 its orichalcum production is up forty percent on the year before, and the [[guild|Guild]] means to use it for a giant shaft into the Dungeon without needing adamantite or other alloys; in DanMachi 20 the shaft is to carry people and materials to the lower floors as an elevator.[@fm19-shaft, fm20-shaft] It would reach even the deep levels.[@fm20-orichalcum-elevator] In DanMachi 19 the Guild would requisition the School District's whole supply, so that monsters could not break the shaft, and route it around the areas where floor bosses appear.[@fm19-orichalcum-shaft] With [[goibniu-familia|Goibniu Familia]]'s help, almost all the orichalcum doors of Knossos are to be stripped and reused for the shaft.[@fm19-orichalcum-recycle] [[eina-tulle|Eina]], a graduate of the School District, objects that the master ingots also reinforce the Hringhorni's hull and are the School District's treasure and pride.[@fm19-orichalcum-hull] In DanMachi 20 the School District's students demand that the requisition be withdrawn, or all recruitment and internships will be put on hold, and the quarrel goes to the [[orariad|Orariad]], whose winner may demand anything of the loser: the Guild could claim all the orichalcum or be forced to return it all.[@fm20-orichalcum-demand, fm20-orichalcum-stakes] After the [[orariad|Orariad]] the requisition is cancelled, and the School District demands a part in the shaft project.[@fm20-orichalcum-settlement]

## Damascus {#damascus}

Damascus steel ({{ja|ダマスカス}})[@damascus.fm10-ja] is "a foreign material used to forge weapons".[@fm10-damascus] In Sword Oratoria 9 Aiz's custom sword, made for her by [[goibniu-familia|Goibniu Familia]], is made of Damascus imported from outside the city; its other name is "rippled steel", for the wave pattern on the blade.[@so09-damascus] The sword cracks in her fight with a dragon.[@so09-cracks] Minor Myths and Legends 2 shows weapons forged from Damascus steel for sale in a market.[@ss02-market]

## Scarletite {#scarletite}

"Scarletite is a miraculous metal created in the Far East", no less than orichalcum in its properties and value; in Sword Oratoria 13 a School District student argues for recycling it into orichalcum.[@so13-scarletite] Another student rejects the formula as substitution rather than sublimation.[@so13-orichalcum-experiment] See also [[far-east|Far East]].[@so13-scarletite]

## Valmars {#valmars}

Valmars ({{ja|白剛石}}, written *white hard stone*)[@varmath.fm12-ja] is "a high-quality Dungeon ore" found only in the lower levels. [[hephaistos|Hephaistos]] gives some to [[welf-crozzo|Welf]], who forges [[ouka|Ouka]]'s battle-ax [[equipment#kougou|Kougou]] from it and his shield from the leftovers.[@fm12-varmath, fm14-varmath] Shields "made of hard white valmars" hold against a monster's charge in DanMachi 13, but in DanMachi 14 Ouka sees "the hard valmars surface" melting like wax.[@fm13-valmars, fm14-valmars] It is "a material that’s also used for weapon and gear manufacturing": the [[school-district|School District]] has arenas built of it, among them "an arena hewn from valmars ore" where classes compete.[@fm19-arena, so13-arena] In Sword Oratoria 10 "countless tools made of Valmars" are broken digging through a wall of the labyrinth, and in Sword Oratoria 6 the great seal at the bottom of Lolog Lake looks to be "constructed of either seiros or varmath".[@so10-valmars, so06-seal]

**Name:** the Japanese reads it *Valmars* in every volume; DanMachi 13, 14 and 19 and Sword Oratoria 13 print *valmars* and Sword Oratoria 10 *Valmars* {{small|printed *varmath* in DanMachi 12 and 14 and Sword Oratoria 6}}. *Varmath* and Fandom's *Valmarth* are search aliases.[@fm12-varmath, fm13-valmars, so10-valmars, so06-seal, fm12-valmars-ja]

## Seiros {#seiros}

Seiros ({{ja|白聖石}}, written *white holy stone*)[@seiros.so06-ja] is a white ore that "mages were rather fond of"; [[lefiya|Lefiya]]'s staff [[equipment#forests-teardrop|Forest's Teardrop]] is made from it.[@so03-seiros] When [[lyu-leon|Lyu]] ran away from her home, she took "only some seiros ore to use as traveling money".[@fm15-seiros] In the [[geography#elven-forests-alf-royal-woods|Alf Royal Woods]], the high elves' home, the castle at the base of the [[holy-tree|royal sacred tree]] is "built of seiros" ([[riveria|Riveria]]'s room is near its top).[@so14-seiros] In Familia Chronicle 1, after Syr asks favours of the gods at a casino to back up her and Lyu's disguise as the Count and Countess of Felnas, gossip in the hall says that Felnas, a small country with economic difficulties, has found "a mountain of seiros in a forest within their territory".[@fc01-seiros] The seal at Lolog Lake may be seiros or valmars.[@so06-seal]
