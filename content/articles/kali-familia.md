**Kali Familia** is the Familia of the goddess Kali, which rules [[telskyura|Telskyura]], a land of Amazon warriors far to the southeast of [[orario|Orario]].[@so06-telskyura] Its members grow strong through rites, fights to the death in an arena against captured monsters and against each other.[@so06-telskyura, so06-rule] [[hyrute-sisters|Tiona and Tione Hyrute]] were born and raised there before joining [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]].[@so06-telskyura] In Sword Oratoria 6 the Familia comes to Port Meren at [[ishtar|Ishtar]]'s request, and Kali makes the twins re-enact the rite against their former mentors, Argana and Bache.[@so06-ishtar, so06-demand, so06-warning]

## Kali

{{nocite}} The goddess has her own page: [[kali|Kali]].

Kali is a copper-skinned goddess with blood-red hair and the stature of a child. She wears a necklace of fake bones and a fanged mask.[@so06-arrival] Sword Oratoria 6 calls her a goddess of war, blood and mayhem whose sole purpose in descending was war itself.[@so06-demand] [[loki|Loki]] compares her rule to [[ares|Ares]]'s over [[ares#kingdom-of-rakia|Rakia]], a "nation-state familia", and cannot stand her.[@so06-telskyura] Since Kali came to power the rites have gone on without end; her blessing makes the Amazons' fights more violent, and they revere her as their one and only god.[@so06-childhood]

## Telskyura and the rites

[[riveria|Riveria]] describes Telskyura as one of the few world powers besides Orario, a nation of blood and war that is isolated, so little is known of it. She says the only men allowed there are slaves or are kept for breeding.[@so06-telskyura] Its girls receive the Falna as soon as they are born and are made to fight [[monsters#goblin|goblins]] before they can talk; the strong are honoured, and the defeated are put to work.[@so06-childhood] Survivors of the rites are called True Warriors, and the crowd's cry *Se wehga* means roughly "Thou art the true warrior".[@so06-rule, so06-argana-past]

The rites' rule seemed to forbid fights between girls of the same room, but that was a misunderstanding: once they had grown close, they were made to kill one another. This is how Tione came to kill her friend Seldas.[@so06-rule] Survivors are chosen as students by senior Amazons; Bache chose Tiona and Argana chose Tione.[@so06-mentors] Tione later sees the logic of Telskyura as killing Level 3s to make Level 4s and Level 4s to make Level 5s.[@so06-argana-past] The twins escaped a rite that would have made them fight each other, and travelled from country to country before they joined Loki Familia five years before Sword Oratoria 6.[@so06-escape, so06-telskyura]

> [!NOTE] Island or peninsula?
> Anakity calls Telskyura "a peninsular country far to Orario’s southeast"; Riveria answers that it is "a solitary island surrounded on all sides by sea and cliffs". Tione's memories call it "the forsaken island", and Minor Myths and Legends 2 "the island nation".[@so06-telskyura, so06-childhood, ss02-meren]

## Argana Kalif {#argana-kalif}

Argana is the elder of the twin captains, Tione's former mentor, and the warrior whose outlook is closest to Kali's.[@so06-mentors, so06-argana-past] She drinks her opponents' blood in battle, and alone among the Amazons of Telskyura she has an alias, Kalima, recognised by Kali herself; her curse of the same name is described under [[magic#kalima|Kalima]].[@so06-argana-past] She reached Level 6 by killing Belnas, a rival candidate for captain.[@so06-elnea] In the rite of Sword Oratoria 6 she fights Tione, and [[finn-deimne|Finn]] ends the fight with [[magic#hell-finegas|Hell Finegas]].[@so06-finn]

Argana fights smiling. When the sisters clash at Meren, her "crescent moon smile" does not falter even under Tione's "bone-crushing grip"; her reading of her enemy's next move is "uncanny", leaving "no question as to her Level-6 rank", and she fends off Tione's Berserk-driven punches "with ease".[@so06-argana-meren] Years before, she had taken a liking to "the young girl who couldn’t be broken", her tongue twitching "like a snake’s at the sight of all that blood".[@so06-argana-tione] In the rite at sea she tells Tione that killing her own brethren brings her "elation": "They’re not gone—they’re inside me!"[@so06-argana-creed] Kali sets Argana's "relentless fighting spirit" against Tione's anger, "both the same, ever-enduring and ever-abiding", and when Finn lands on the deck Argana cannot hide her astonishment at his strength.[@so06-war-end]

Defeated by a man, she falls in love with him, as Amazons do with strong men who beat them.[@so12-arrival, ss02-orario] In Minor Myths and Legends 2 she runs through Orario demanding that [[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz]] tell her where Finn is, and Aiz points her toward [[twilight-manor|Twilight Manor]].[@ss02-orario] In Sword Oratoria 12 she arrives at Knossos calling for "my husband", to Tione's fury, then fights the demi-spirit beside her.[@so12-arrival, so12-argana] Rivals in love, the two still "looked exactly like teacher and student as they stood shoulder to shoulder" before the charge.[@so12-argana-tione]

## Bache Kalif {#bache-kalif}

Bache is the younger twin and Tiona's former mentor. In Tiona's childhood she read her the books Tiona loved.[@so06-mentors, so06-elnea] Silent "as ever", she keeps her mouth hidden by "her ever-present neckerchief".[@so06-bache-silent, so06-bache-past] In training she looked down at the child Tiona "icy and unfeeling", the first fear Tiona ever felt; yet after each session she read her a story, bewildered at first, while Tiona urged "What happens next?"[@so06-bache-past] Ten years later Tiona finds her colder still, with "the icy aura of antipathy around her" and eyes that glint "beneath the strands of her sandy bangs".[@so06-bache-look] She reached Level 6 by killing Elnea, the other rival for captain.[@so06-elnea] Her poison enchantment, [[magic#velgas|Velgas]], earned her the name Poison Queen.[@so06-velgas] It coats her right hand, and its poison "guaranteed a swift kill"; as a girl Tiona was traumatised by "that coolheaded warrior woman calmly exterminating her prey", and Kali boasts that the venom of Dungeon monsters "doesn’t hold a candle to my Bache’s". At Level 6 the enchantment grew in power and range, so that she can cover her whole body "the same as Aiz did with her Airiel".[@so06-velgas-power] It guards as well as strikes: every blow Tiona lands poisons her further.[@so06-bache-poison] At Meren she alone overpowers a small Loki Familia group, and Kali Familia carries off [[lefiya|Lefiya]].[@so06-raid] In the rite Tiona defeats her.[@so06-tiona] In Sword Oratoria 7 Tiona shrugs off her friends' worry over a later wound by recalling "the stuff I got hit with in Meren".[@so07-tiona] Because she was beaten before she saw any of Loki Familia's men fight, she is the one Amazon of the Familia who does not fall in love; Kali says she is "safe".[@ss02-bache, ss02-orario] In Minor Myths and Legends 2 she chases her sister through Orario, and in Sword Oratoria 12 she fights beside Tiona at Knossos, where her poison fist rots a demi-spirit's tentacle "a blackish purple".[@ss02-orario, so12-arrival, so12-bache]

## At Meren (Sword Oratoria 6)

Ishtar first wrote to Kali more than a year earlier and won her over with envoys and gifts of armour. Her plan was for [[ishtar-familia|Ishtar Familia]] to attack [[freya-familia|Freya Familia]] from the front while Kali's warriors, brought into Orario by freight, struck from behind. Kali accepted for the chance to fight the strongest Familia in Orario.[@so06-ishtar] When she learns that Loki Familia is in Meren, she demands, before anything else, a fight between her captains and the twins she lost. She wants to see which pair of sisters grew stronger, those who left or those who stayed.[@so06-demand]

Kali Familia attacks Lefiya's group and takes Lefiya, then leaves the Hyrute sisters a warning: the Trickster emblem slashed in the sign of the rites. Tione decides that they must go alone and sever their ties with Telskyura.[@so06-raid, so06-warning] When the rest of Loki Familia arrives, it quickly overwhelms Kali's warriors, and a humiliated Kali is left to Loki's mockery.[@so06-defeat]

Afterwards Loki Familia heals Bache and the other wounded Amazons and sends them back to their ship, while Kali is taken along for questioning. Loki demands that she leave Tiona and Tione alone and end the killing, or at least release those who do not want to take part in the rites; Kali mocks the idea. She laments that all her warriors except Bache have lost to a man, and fears for Telskyura's future.[@so06-epilogue] Minor Myths and Legends 2 shows the result: the defeated Amazons have fallen for the Loki Familia men who beat them.[@ss02-bache]
