**Daedalus** is a historical craftsman credited with Orario's buildings and the design of [[knossos|Knossos]], whose descendants inherit his unfinished labyrinth and notebook.[@fm10-history, fm10-project, so10-legacy] [[fels|Fels]] identifies Daedalus as a human craftsman active about a thousand years earlier, crediting him with [[babel|Babel]] and buildings foundational to [[orario|Orario]].[@fm10-history] The narration describes [[knossos|Knossos]] as Daedalus's conception, built by his descendants.[@so10-legacy]

## Reported history {#reported-history}

[[fels|Fels]] says he has heard that Daedalus was one of [[ouranos|Ouranos]]'s few followers, and reports that his behavior changed after entering the Dungeon before he disappeared.[@fm10-history]

[[fels|Fels]] also attributes Orario's old underground waterways to him.[@fm10-history]

Sword Oratoria 7 identifies Daedalus as the designer whose name became [[daedalus-street|Daedalus Street]]'s name, with construction beginning about a thousand years earlier.[@so07-name]
