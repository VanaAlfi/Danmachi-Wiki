**Royman Mardeel** is an elf and the head of [[guild|the Guild]].[@fm09-royman] The Guild's true leader is the god [[ouranos|Ouranos]]; Royman passes on Ouranos's secret mission in DanMachi 9 without being told the secret of the [[xenos|Xenos]].[@fm09-royman, fm09-network]

His nickname is "the Guild’s Pig". He has served at the Guild for over a century, and his extravagant life since he reached his post has left him obese.[@fm09-pig] He is "hated by every elf in Orario"; even [[riveria|Riveria]] considers him the "shame of her race".[@so02-pig, fc01-pig] Sword Oratoria 2 says he rose to head of the Guild more than ten years earlier and, at "over 150 years old", gives "the final yes or no for every major decision" while living in luxury.[@so02-age]

## Character {#character}

The narration paints him as "Corrupt, rotund", oozing money and power, and in Sword Oratoria 12 as faithful to his own self-interest, a man who "would use anything in his power".[@fc01-pig, so12-devoted] He is no fool; [[hermes|Hermes]] calls him "a shrewd one".[@fm11-shrewd] Ouranos answers that he has twice anyone's greed but is capable and sincere in wanting the city to develop, and Sword Oratoria 12 says his feelings for Orario are genuine and stronger than anyone's; otherwise Ouranos would never have given him the Guild's highest post.[@fm11-ouranos-view, so12-genuine]

Above all he fears for the city's standing. In the dark age of Astrea Record 1 he insists that it is urgent to proclaim Orario's power within and beyond the city, while [[allen-fromel|Allen]] calls him a "fat pig" who only cares about keeping his seat; in Astrea Record 2 [[finn-deimne|Finn]] tells him: "Chin up, Royman. No more of that wailing."[@ar01-allen, ar02-finn] In Familia Chronicle 1 he orders the factions to clear a casino's main hall first, "desperate to prevent Orario’s reputation from dropping".[@fc01-casino] In DanMachi 18, "more scared than anyone of seeing the city’s strength drop", he keeps [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]] out of the War Game; though he had tasted the full *invasion* of [[freya|Freya]]'s charm, he still sides with her Familia for the city's sake.[@fm18-fear]

He is hard on those below him. In DanMachi 9 he looks [[eina-tulle|Eina]] over, grunts "like a pig" and berates her, and in DanMachi 19 he shouts her down when she argues against the Shaft plan.[@fm09-eina, fm19-shaft] Sword Oratoria 13 says that the deeds of [[dionysus|Dionysus]], the true mastermind, were never told: telling them would have contradicted Royman's public account, and there was regard for the fear of the gods and for the followers' dignity.[@so13-dionysus]
