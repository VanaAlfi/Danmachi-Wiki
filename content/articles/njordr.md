**Njörðr** is a god of fishing and the head of [[njordr-familia|Njörðr Familia]], the fishing Familia of [[njordr-familia#port-meren|Port Meren]], southwest of [[orario|Orario]].[@so06-fishing, so06-motive, fc01-chloe] He is a friend of [[loki|Loki]] from their time in Heaven.[@so06-meet, so06-lies] In Sword Oratoria 6 Loki discovers that, to save Meren's fisheries, he released [[monsters#violas|violas]] into Lolog Lake and the nearby sea, in return for helping a pale man from Orario's sewers smuggle goods out of Meren.[@so06-pact] In Sword Oratoria 7 [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]] identifies that man as [[barca-perdix|Barca Perdix]].[@so07-barca]

## The god

Njörðr has brown hair pulled back into a ponytail, untanned skin, and the muscular build of a man of the sea; he is about as tall as [[bete-loga|Bete]].[@so06-meet] Sword Oratoria 6 calls him "a god of the rod": he descended to the lower world to reap the bounty of its waters.[@so06-motive] [[riveria|Riveria]] says he did much for her before she came to live in Orario, and that the lengths he went to for travellers on their way to the city were extraordinary.[@so06-meet, so06-lies] Loki, who has known him since Heaven, says gods find it hard to tell when another god is lying, but that Njörðr was never good at it.[@so06-lies] In Familia Chronicle 1 [[chloe|Chloe]] thinks of him as "a good god": handsome, tall and caring for children.[@fc01-good]

## Njörðr Familia {#familia}

Njörðr's Familia runs the fishing of Port Meren, southwest of Orario; its captain, Rod, says the god has protected the city for generations.[@so06-fishing, so06-rod, fc01-chloe] The Familia, its home Nóatún and [[njordr-familia#port-meren|the port]] are described on [[njordr-familia|Njörðr Familia]].

## The viola bargain

| Who | Part in the scheme (Sword Oratoria 6) |
|---|---|
| Njörðr | Released the violas into the lake and sea; kept fishing while they thinned out the monsters; helped smuggle the pale man's goods out of Meren.[@so06-pact, so06-roles] |
| Rubart, Guild Branch chief | Controlled the flow of [[magic-stone|magic stones]] from Guild headquarters, which were stolen for the powder; kept Meren from signalling Orario for help.[@so06-roles, so06-rubart] |
| Borg Murdock, head of Meren | Processed the powder in the basement of his estate and saw that every ship got a batch.[@so06-roles] |
| [[ishtar|Ishtar]] Familia | Carried the violas from Orario to Meren, in return for Njörðr making room for its activities in Meren.[@so06-ishtar, so06-portrait] |

When violas surface in Lolog Lake and later attack the port, Loki Familia investigates. Loki suspects Njörðr because he avoided her eyes, and she follows the trail to a sea cave. There his captain Rod tries to take the blame, until Njörðr steps forward.[@so06-lies, so06-cave] The "magic dust" that Meren's fishermen scattered to ward off monsters held crushed magic stones mixed with fish parts; the violas, which prefer monsters' magic stones even to human flesh, went after it and the other monsters instead of the boats.[@so06-dust]

Njörðr explains that the monsters in the world's seas had become too many over the past five hundred years. Meren's fish had fallen to dangerous levels, and his followers kept dying at sea; Rod's father and grandfather were among them. He had thought of militarising his Familia as Poseidon had, but his men would never have accepted it.[@so06-motive] Six or seven years earlier a viola washed down Orario's sewers into the lake. He followed its trail into the sewers, where a deathly pale man with long bangs offered him the monsters in return for a few favours.[@so06-pact] Ships without the powder were still at risk, which he called a small price to pay compared with the men he had been losing.[@so06-roles] Asked whether he ever dealt with the [[evils|Evils]]' remnants or the human-monster creatures, he says he does not know what they are, so he assumes not.[@so06-ishtar]

Loki promises to keep his part secret but reports the violas to the Guild, and he can no longer use them.[@so06-ishtar] The Guild hushes up the affair. Nothing happens to Njörðr or Borg, given their influence in Meren, beyond a promise of allegiance to the Guild, and Rubart alone takes the fall.[@so06-coverup] Njörðr then forcibly carves his blessing into Rubart's back and makes him one of his fishermen, as the only atonement he can offer.[@so06-rubart-joins] He tells Loki he never looked inside the boxes he smuggled: probably valuables, sometimes alcohol, and once a box that made a horrible ruckus, perhaps something alive.[@so06-portrait] He draws Loki a portrait of the man from the sewers.[@so06-portrait]

In Sword Oratoria 7 [[hyrute-sisters|Tiona and Tione]] recognise a man in [[knossos|Knossos]] as "the guy from the picture" who made the deal with the fishing god of Meren; he concedes that "something like that happened" and gives his name as Barca.[@so07-barca] The narration says the Evils, Barca and the other Daedalus descendants included, lacked the money to complete Knossos, which is why they made the contract to profit from Meren's black market.[@so07-funds]

## Chloe

Before the main series, Chloe saw Njörðr taking freight from a shady group to smuggle into Orario. She used this to make him update her Status: she converted and contracted with him, though he lives outside the city.[@fc01-chloe] He had joined the smuggling on the grounds that it was necessary for the fish, but she did not know the details.[@fc01-chloe] He knew she was the assassin Black Cat and told no one, and put up with her demands as if to give her a moment's rest.[@fc01-good] When she settles in at the [[the-benevolent-mistress|Benevolent Mistress]], he and Demeter visit with seafood and produce; Chloe thanks him, and he pats her head, ending their arrangement.[@fc01-farewell]
