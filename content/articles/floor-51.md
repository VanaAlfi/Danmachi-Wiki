**Floor 51** of the [[dungeon|Dungeon]] is a maze in the deep levels and contains the [[monsters#cadmus|Cadmus]] Springs.[@so01-layout, so01-water] Its walls, floor and ceiling are flat, with intersecting passages and graphite-colored walls.[@so01-layout, so04-color] A steep slope connects it to an opening in the western wall of [[floor-50|Floor 50]].[@so01-connection]
