**Tsukuyomi** is a goddess whom [[mikoto|Yamato Mikoto]] trusts.[@fm15-goddess] She wears her blue hair tied up, while her robes are described as hardly befitting a goddess.[@fm15-goddess] [[takemikazuchi|Takemikazuchi]] names her among the goddesses who would welcome [[haruhime|Haruhime]] at their shrine in the [[far-east|Far East]].[@fm07-welcome]

|---|---|

| DanMachi 8 | He offers [[mikoto|Yamato Mikoto]] the alternative of Tsukuyomi's Familia, but she chooses his.[@fm08-familia] |

| DanMachi 15 | She tells [[mikoto|Yamato Mikoto]] to protect her and draw her into play until she forgets her sense of obligation.[@fm15-advice] |
