The [[dungeon|Dungeon]] contains several **safe points**, floors where monsters are not born, reducing the risk of sudden attacks and providing resting places.[@so01-camp] Their safety has limits: monsters migrate into [[floor-18|the eighteenth floor]], and [[rivira|Rivira]] remains vulnerable to attacks.[@ar03-arrivals, fm05-point]

## Named places and events {#named-places-and-events}

| Volume | Events |
|---|---|
| DanMachi 5 | [[floor-18|Floor 18]] is the first safe point encountered from the surface, used as a staging area for expeditions farther down.[@fm05-point] |
| DanMachi 5 | A [[goliath|Goliath]] is exceptionally born there; the [[guild|Guild]] classifies the floor-boss appearance as a Calamity.[@fm05-irregular] |
| Sword Oratoria 1 | [[floor-50|Floor 50]] is a safe point where [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]] establishes its expedition base camp.[@so01-camp] |

| DanMachi 12 | An [[magic-stone|enhanced species]] waits at a lower-level safe point and kills adventurers whose guard is down.[@fm12-ambush] |
| DanMachi 14 | The first safe point in the [[dungeon#deep-floors|deep levels]] is on Floor 39.[@fm14-first-deep] |
| DanMachi 14 | [[lyu-leon|Lyu]] considers the stream on [[floor-37|Floor 37]] a safe zone because no monsters attack there, describing it as probably that floor's only paradise.[@fm14-stream] |
