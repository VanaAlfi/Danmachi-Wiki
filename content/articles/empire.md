**The Empire** is a world power, named among destinations and careers open to the [[school-district|School District]]'s students.[@so13-power, fm19-career] It is among the powers with multiple people of [[level|Level]] 3 or higher.[@fc01-territory]

## Mentions by volume {#mentions-by-volume}

| Volume | Events |
|---|---|
| Familia Chronicle 1 | [[runoa|Runoa Faust]] is born in a certain territory of "the empire", far from [[orario|Orario]], and loses her family in the war that puts her country under it; that empire is not named in the checked text, and the story names the Empire, with the [[altena|Great Country of Magic]], among the few world powers holding several people of Level 3 or higher.[@fc01-territory] |
| Astrea Record 2 | Reports from the [[evils|Evils]]' allies confirm unrest in the Empire and other powers from the [[great-conflict|Great Conflict]] onward.[@ar02-unrest] |
| Astrea Record 3 | A speaker asks whether the group should have prioritized helping powers such as the Empire instead of liberating towns and villages along its route.[@ar03-help] |
| Sword Oratoria 13 | [[lefiya|Lefiya Viridis]] is told that her fame has reached the Empire, which is also listed among destinations for students.[@so13-fame, so13-power] |
| Sword Oratoria 14 | A foreign minister from a national Familia in an Empire vassal state tries to recruit [[gareth|Gareth Landrock]].[@so14-vassal] |
| Familia Chronicle 3 | The Empire is named among the places placing bulk weapon orders with [[zolingam|Solingen]].[@fc03-orders] |
| DanMachi 19 | [[christia-elvia|Chris]] (Christia Elvia) wants to become an imperial knight and change from within a country he calls corrupt and given over to colonial rule.[@fm19-chris] |
| DanMachi 20 | A student recalls the Empire's secret attempt to recruit [[leon-verdenberg|Leon Verdenberg]] as an example of demands that prompted student resistance.[@fm20-recruit] |
| Minor Myths and Legends 2 | At [[ali#shalzad|Shalzad]]'s ball, [[ali|Ali]] sees the prime minister of the Empire, which has the world's largest territory.[@ss02-minister] |

[@fm19-career]: FM19 | Chapter 3: School Life in Another World | Imperial knights are a possible student career.
[@ar02-unrest]: AR02 | Chapter 5: Banquet of Evil | The Evils' Faithful cause unrest in distant powers including the Empire. The Japanese edition (file c3MH, paragraph 61) gives the unrest in the Empire and the other great powers since the Great Conflict as confirmed from their comrades' reports; the English prints it as a direct account of the Faithful's local acts of terrorism.
[@ar03-help]: AR03 | Chapter 6: The Nameless Heroes | A speaker asks whether the group should be helping world powers such as the empire.

[@so14-vassal]: SO14 | Chapter 3: The Dwarf’s Embarking | A national-familia foreign minister tries to recruit Gareth. The Japanese edition (file part0032, paragraph 80) says the minister was from a vassal state of the Empire.

[@fm19-chris]: FM19 | Chapter 5: My Dream | Chris wants to be an imperial knight and change the country from within. The Japanese edition (file part0025, paragraph 773) has him call it a corrupt country that does nothing but colonial rule.
[@fm20-recruit]: FM20 | Chapter 1: Orario Rumble | A student recalls the Empire's attempted recruitment of Leon and student resistance. The Japanese edition (file J10, paragraph 835) calls it top-secret headhunting; the English prints "the empire tried to forcibly recruit Professor Leon".
[@ss02-minister]: SS02 | Mirage in a Sea of Sand | Ali sees the prime minister of the Empire at Shalzad's ball. The Japanese edition (file part0075, paragraph 39) describes the Empire as having the world's largest territory; the English prints "the largest empire in the world".
