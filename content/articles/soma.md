**Soma** is the god of [[soma-familia|Soma Familia]]. Called the God of Sake, he came down to the mortal realm to brew all kinds of alcohol, and he cares for little else.[@so11-sake, fm02-hobby] His [[#divine-wine|divine wine]], which bears his name, became the reward that set his followers against one another, among them his child [[lilliluka-erde|Lilliluka Erde]].[@fm02-wine, fm06-reward] When Lilly resists the wine in DanMachi 6, he stops the fighting in his Familia, releases her to [[hestia-familia|Hestia Familia]] and apologises to her.[@fm06-stop, fm06-apology]

## Appearance and character

Soma's long, unkempt hair hangs over his face and hides his eyes, which are black as ink; DanMachi 10 says he looks "more like a hermit than a being from a higher plane".[@fm06-corner, fm15-young-lilly, fm10-visit] He wears a dirty robe.[@fm06-apology] Sword Oratoria 11 calls him "gloominess incarnate", an introverted god who is rarely seen out and about.[@so11-sake]

He keeps apart from the other gods. He is famous for never attending their Celebrations or answering social calls, and Lilly says he "doesn’t talk to any other gods or goddesses, ever".[@fm02-recluse, fm02-lilly] [[loki|Loki]], who loves his wine, calls him "that idiot" and "the wise ol’ hermit of the gods": a god with only his hobby in his head, "Not barbaric or evil, just purely livin’ fer his craft". Eina senses that Loki's scorn is partly fear and awe.[@fm02-hobby, fm02-monster] Loki also teases him as a "God of Hobbies".[@so11-sake] Few things move him: when Loki bowed and asked politely to taste his real wine, he answered "I refuse", the first emotion she had seen him show.[@fm02-refuse]

His craft sets him apart in the mortal realm. Ouranos counts "Soma’s wine" with Hephaistos's forging and Freya's beauty among the authorities a deity may use in this world, and DanMachi 17 calls him the god "who could brew the wine of the gods".[@fm17-authority] He is also one of the gods who know how to hide a Status.[@fm08-hiding]

## Divine wine (soma) {#divine-wine}

Soma grows the ingredients, mixes them and brews the wine himself; Loki calls him "a real monster" who has "driven his hobby ta utter perfection".[@fm02-monster] DanMachi 15 shows him grinding plants with a mortal and pestle in the corner of his room.[@fm15-young-lilly] One sip of the wine can bring more happiness than anything else, and Loki first met it as a drinker: "It was love at first taste!"[@fm02-wine]

Making wine is expensive, and Soma founded his Familia for his hobby, so he gave its members a prize to make them work harder: the wine. Soma Familia then had more members than its god's reputation would draw, because "The children who follow him aren’t there fer the idiot, but the Soma itself."[@fm02-wine] Loki explains that there is no withdrawal and that its hold is temporary; drinkers who stop recover, and the strongest members build up a tolerance.[@fm02-effects] The conclusion Eina draws is that Soma was not at fault for the state of his Familia, but that he set it in motion and did nothing to end it.[@fm02-effects]

From Soma's own point of view, DanMachi 6 explains, he only rewarded his followers with delicious wine, and instead of thanking him they turned on each other for more. Disillusioned, he lost all interest in them, though he "harbored no ill will".[@fm06-reward] He keeps the wine in a large building he bought for it, and by Sword Oratoria 11 his Familia has a brewery near [[daedalus-street|Daedalus Street]], apart from its home.[@fm06-cellar, so11-sake]

The English volumes print the wine as *Soma* (DanMachi 2), *soma* with *Divine Wine* or *divine wine* (DanMachi 6 and 10, Sword Oratoria 11), and *divine liquor* (DanMachi 15).[@fm02-wine, fm06-reward, fm10-visit, so11-brew, fm15-young-lilly]

In Sword Oratoria 11 Soma, passing Loki near Daedalus Street, suddenly stops: "I smell a gods’ brew." His nose leads them into the cellar of [[dionysus|Dionysus]]'s manor, where he identifies a bottle of divine wine that is "even more complete than mine".[@so11-brew] In Sword Oratoria 12, with Loki and Hermes, he says that with it "even a god could get wasted", with a reluctance to accept that someone has made a better divine wine than his, and an intense curiosity.[@so12-dionysus] Sword Oratoria 12 later calls Dionysus's scheme, made possible by that wine, "a farce sparked by the creation of a liquor that far surpassed Soma’s".[@so12-farce]

## Soma and Lilly

DanMachi 15 recalls that the young Lilly, wandering the Familia's home in hunger, met him in a hallway. The other followers were terrified of him, but he silently led her to his room and gave her fried potato morsels, and she began to visit his chambers. After she, too, had drunk the wine and become a "wine-thirsty ghoul", she never noticed his "hopeless, despondent gaze" following her.[@fm15-young-lilly] By DanMachi 2 Lilly holds a grudge against him and believes that he never did anything for his followers.[@fm02-grudge]

After Bell's testimony in DanMachi 3 the Guild investigates Soma's management, and in DanMachi 4 the gods at the [[denatus|Denatus]] gossip that he had to give up his one and only hobby. In DanMachi 6 the Guild has already fined him for neglecting his followers.[@fm03-warning, fm04-gossip, fm06-fines]

When Lilly asks him in DanMachi 6 to let her leave, he sits muttering in a corner and leaves the matter to his commander, [[soma-familia|Zanis]], with "…It’s up to you."[@fm06-corner] Later, held at his Familia's wine-storage building while Hestia's allies fight in its courtyard, she reaches his room at the top of its tower, and he dismisses her: "What good are the words of a child who succumbs to wine so…easily?" He gives her a cup of the wine, and she resists it. Seeing a mortal change before his eyes for the first time leaves him speechless.[@fm06-cellar, fm06-test] He silences Zanis, throws the empty bottle into the courtyard from the balcony and orders "Stop fighting." No member thinks of disobeying a god who had never shown interest in anything but his hobby.[@fm06-stop]

[[hestia|Hestia]] pledges the [[hestia-knife|Hestia Knife]] as security for Lilly's release fee, and he accepts and converts Lilly to her. Alone with him, Hestia tells him that Lilly "grew strong as a result of your neglect" and that he should think about why she changed.[@fm06-conversion] After the [[war-game|War Game]] Lilly pays him. As she leaves, he speaks to her for the first time: "Lilliluka Erde…I have done you wrong." "…Make sure to take care of your health." Then he packs away the wine bottles on his shelves and fills the gaps with wineglasses.[@fm06-apology]

By DanMachi 10 he has stopped using the wine as a reward, and his Familia's strange behaviour has all but ended. When Hestia visits and takes offence at a remark of his, he apologises feebly, then summons the dwarf Chandra to help her; Zanis, dismissed, sits in a cell with his Status sealed by Soma.[@fm10-visit] In DanMachi 15 he senses that Lilly has grown, but he will not approach her: "I abandoned her once." Alone, he congratulates her from his window.[@fm15-senses]
