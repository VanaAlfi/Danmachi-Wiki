A **Monster Rex** is a monster of a kind the Dungeon allows only one of on a floor at a time, and which takes much longer than ordinary monsters to be reborn after it is killed. The [[guild|Guild]] has known of them since ancient times. Each is said to be two or more Levels above the other monsters on its floor, and adventurers call them "floor bosses"; usually many adventurers must work together to bring one down.[@fm02-rex]

On Floors 11 and 12, where there is no Monster Rex, [[monsters#infant-dragon|infant dragons]] are treated as the bosses of the [[upper-levels|upper levels]].[@fm04-dragons]

## Irregular behaviour

{{nocite}} Floor bosses do not always behave as expected:

- In DanMachi 5 the Dungeon produces a Black Goliath on [[floor-18|Floor 18]] after [[hestia|Hestia]]'s divine presence is revealed.[@fm05-black]
- At the end of DanMachi 13 the Amphisbaena appears about two weeks before the Guild expected it.[@fm13-amphisbaena, fm14-early]
- In Sword Oratoria 13 a Goliath appears while the Guild board still gives two days until its interval, and shows unusual intelligence; the text treats the intelligence as variation between individual floor bosses.[@so13-early] DanMachi 19 shows that it was not early: some adventurers had faked the Goliath report to scare the students, and Bell had noticed that the reported last kill, twelve days earlier, did not fit.[@fm19-goliath-report]

## Monster Rexes and Level Ups

Defeating a strong enemy such as a Monster Rex alone can be the great feat that a [[level|Level]] Up requires.[@so02-container] Aiz reaches Level 6 after defeating Udaeus alone.[@so03-udaeus] Killing Udaeus one-on-one or with very few people may also yield the rare drop item Udaeus Black Sword.[@fc02-udaeus]

[[astrea-familia|Astrea Familia]], before its destruction, had cleared twenty-one floor bosses.[@fc01-astrea]
