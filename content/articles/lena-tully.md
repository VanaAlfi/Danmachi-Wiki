**Lena Tully** is a young Amazon of the [[ishtar-familia#berbera|Berbera]], formerly of [[ishtar-familia|Ishtar Familia]].[@fm07-lena, so10-copy] She falls in love with [[bete-loga|Bete Loga]] after he beats her at Port Meren.[@so08-meet] In Sword Oratoria 8 she trades what she once saw of Ishtar's key to [[knossos|Knossos]] for a "Dungeon date". Caught with Bete when assassins hunt down former Ishtar Familia members, she runs to draw them away from him and is left for dead.[@so08-deal, so08-run, so08-dying] Days later she reappears, saved by [[amid|Amid]]'s anti-curse [[potions#elixir|elixir]].[@so08-alive]

## Ishtar Familia

As the Familia collapses in DanMachi 7, [[aisha-belka|Aisha]] tells Lena to lead the others out.[@fm07-lena] Lena later says that [[phryne-jamil|Phryne]] and the older Amazons were always sending her round the temple on errands. Once Phryne told her to find Ishtar's weak point; she slipped into Ishtar's room and saw, through a hidden door, a secret vault with a [[metals#mythril|mythril]] ball in a small box. The vice captain, [[ishtar-familia#tammuz-berrilli|Tammuz]], caught her and chased her out, telling her to "forget everything I saw".[@so08-vault]

Sword Oratoria 8 recalls that at Port Meren in Sword Oratoria 6 she aimed a scimitar at Bete's back after he had subdued Phryne, and he punched her in the stomach. He does not remember her face; she remembers him.[@so08-meet]

## Sword Oratoria 8

### Bete Loga

Lena runs after Bete in the street, delighted, and introduces herself: "I'm Lena! Lena Tully!"[@so08-meet] She explains that Amazons do not fall for every strong man who beats them; for her it was "like an alarm going off", and it made her believe in fate.[@so08-fate] Bete needs to know whether Ishtar had a ball like the key he is searching for. Lena thinks she has seen one, but reminds him that adventurers pay for information, and her price is a date in the Dungeon.[@so08-deal]

On the seventeenth floor Bete sees that she is a Level 2 close to her next Level.[@so08-level] When he sneers at weaklings, she takes it as a promise: once she is no longer "fish bait", she can be with him.[@so08-fishbait] Outside a flower shop she tells him that nothing would make her happier than a gift of forget-me-nots.[@so08-flowers] Aisha, whom Lena treats as an elder sister, catches them together and is disgusted.[@so08-aisha]

### The Amazon hunt

The next day the pair search the ruins of the Pleasure Quarter for the vault.[@so08-vault] Assassins attack them there, and soon they concentrate on Lena.[@so08-ambush] The narration says Lena had not been dragged into Bete's trouble: the assassins were after her, and Bete had been caught up in it.[@so08-ambush] Bete cannot attack while he has to protect her. Seeing it, she says, "If it weren't for me…you'd be strong…wouldn't you, Bete Loga?" She asks him to win and not die, and runs.[@so08-ambush, so08-run]

[[valletta|Valletta]] sends the assassins after her. When Bete catches up she is lying in the rain with a black dagger in her stomach. She apologises for being weak and for not keeping her promise, and says she wanted to stand beside him.[@so08-run, so08-dying]

### Alive

After the fighting, [[riveria|Riveria]] tells Bete that Lena survived. On the day of the attack Amid finished an elixir against the curse, distilled from her own curse-exposed blood. Riveria's supply was running short when she found Lena, but it was just enough to lift the curse, and she took her to hospital. Unsure whether Lena would live, and with Amazons still being targeted, Riveria kept quiet and let her death be believed.[@so08-alive] Bete greets the cheerful Lena with a hand on her head, then a barrage of knees to the stomach, which she takes as a blessing.[@so08-blows]

At the graveyard Lena prays for the friends who did not survive. On the gravestone made for her she finds a bouquet of forget-me-nots, which only Bete could have left, and Aisha bursts out laughing.[@so08-graves]
