**The Guild** governs matters concerning [[dungeon|the Dungeon]] in [[orario|Orario]]. It oversees adventurers and access to the Dungeon, keeps their records, runs [[the-exchange|the Exchange]] where [[magic-stone|magic stones]] and drop items are sold, and guards [[babel|Babel]], the lid over the Dungeon.[@fm01-guild] It was founded by the god [[ouranos|Ouranos]], who is its true leader; its day-to-day head is the elf [[royman|Royman Mardeel]].[@so02-ouranos, fm09-ouranos, fm09-royman] Its origin goes back almost a thousand years, to the alliance of many races formed to contain the monsters emerging from the great hole; the remains of that alliance were reassembled under Ouranos as one large organisation.[@so02-guild-origin] Ouranos gives its employees no Blessing, to avoid a struggle for power, so the Guild has no fighting force of its own.[@so02-guild-no-blessing]

## What the Guild does

| Function | Details |
|---|---|
| Registration | Familias register with the Guild; only an adventurer's Level and Familia are reported to it.[@fm04-rank, fm02-privacy] |
| [[familia-rank|Familia ranks]] | Every Familia is ranked from I to S; higher ranks bring greater public trust and higher taxes.[@fm04-rank] |
| Advisers | The Guild offers dedicated advisers, mainly to new Familias and novice adventurers (Loki Familia has no use for one); staff such as [[eina-tulle|Eina Tulle]] serve as advisers.[@fm01-eina, fm08-guild, so09-guild-advisers] The Guild also provides starting equipment, which has to be paid off.[@ss01-guild] |
| [[quest|Quests]] | The Guild posts quests on the bulletin board of its headquarters and guarantees their rewards; quests it has not approved, and requests from townspeople, are found in bars.[@fm04-guild-quests] |
| Dungeon limits | By the Guild's reference standards of ability for Dungeon exploration, the eleventh and twelfth floors are the deepest that Level 1 adventurers are considered able to clear.[@fm02-limit] The Guild sets such standards to prevent deaths among adventurers caught off guard on their first visit to a floor.[@fm12-guild-floor-standards] |
| Expeditions | Familias of rank D and above must carry out expeditions of their own at set intervals when the Guild orders them, and produce results such as reaching a new floor; a failure is penalised, generally with a fine.[@fm12-expedition] |
| Missions | A mission is a direct Guild order that a [[familia|Familia]] cannot refuse.[@fm09-mission] |
| [[bounty|Bounties]] | When an extraordinarily strong monster appears, such as an enhanced species that has fed on the magic stones of its own kind, the Guild places a bounty on it and issues subjugation orders.[@fm12-guild-bounties] |
| Penalties | Blacklisting can revoke registration, confiscate loot without payment and lead to imprisonment.[@fm02-blacklist] |
| Leaving the city | The Guild's greatest fear is losing its first-tier adventurers: except in a few special cases they may not leave the city as they please, and its permission is exasperating to obtain and can take days.[@so06-guild-permission] |
| Events | The Guild proposed and organises the [[monsterphilia|Monsterphilia]], with [[ganesha-familia|Ganesha Familia]] providing the tamers; DanMachi 9 reveals that [[ouranos|Ouranos]] was its driving force, to soften people's hatred of monsters.[@fm09-guild-monsterphilia] It officially approves [[war-game|war games]], and its employees prepare them.[@fm06-guild-war-game] |

Receptionists are chosen partly for their looks, because their rapport with adventurers affects morale and the flow of magic stones. Guild staff, the receptionists above all, keep adventurers at arm's length, since any of them may not live to see tomorrow; the senior receptionist Rose warns her younger colleagues never to get too close to one, and Eina is the only receptionist who forms personal connections with hers.[@fm08-guild]

Among Eina's fellow receptionists are [[misha-frot|Misha]], [[rose|Rose]] and [[#sophie|Sophie]]. Rose is a werewolf with long red hair and "a capable coordinator", who wants nothing to do with adventurers, since nothing good comes from being with someone with a death wish; in DanMachi 15's flashback to Bell's registration she offers to bet "on how long that kid has". Sophie, "a beautiful elven woman who had entered the Guild at the same time Rose had", turns him down: "It’s a waste to put time and effort into adventurers who aren’t going to last."[@fm08-rose, fm15-rose, fm15-rose2] In Sword Oratoria 9's recollections Rose is "the bored werewolf receptionist" at the counter when [[riveria|Riveria]] brings the young [[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz]] to register, and an orange-haired god teases "a lovely elf" receptionist, printed *Sofi*.[@so09-rose, so09-sofi] The Japanese writes her name {{ja|ソフィ}}, the name of the elf receptionist printed *Sophie* in DanMachi 15.[@so09-sofi-ja]

[[eina-tulle|Eina]]'s boss is Chief Rehmer, a chienthrope, who appears in the flashback of DanMachi 15; in DanMachi 19 he brings her to the meeting of Royman and the Guild's leadership on the second floor of the headquarters, where the Shaft plan is discussed.[@fm15-rehmer, fm19-rehmer]

### Sophie {#sophie}

**Sophie** ({{ja|ソフィ}})[@fm15-sophie-ja] is an [[races#elf|elven]] employee of the Guild and a colleague of [[eina-tulle|Eina Tulle]] and [[rose|Rose]].[@fm15-employee] She entered the Guild at the same time as Rose and ranks among its two most popular employees.[@fm15-employee]

## Headquarters

The Guild headquarters stands on Northwest Main Street, known as Adventurers Way.[@so01-northwest, fm08-store, fm01-ja-hq] Its bulletin board carries public announcements, such as [[bell-cranell|Bell]]'s promotion to Level 3.[@fm07-board]

The building is called the **Pantheon**, "temple of the gods": it is built of white pillars, with monuments in a broad front court that greet adventurers at the doors.[@so01-pantheon] Astrea Record 1 calls it "a grand construction meant to resemble the Pantheon", one of the city centre's primary landmarks, and has the top adventurers of the Familias meet there in a large conference room seating more than a hundred.[@ar01-pantheon] Its crowds of adventurers can rival those on the street outside.[@so02-crowds] In DanMachi 8 the soldiers captured from [[rakia|Rakia]] are held in chambers deep inside it.[@fm08-chambers] In Astrea Record 3, before the final battle of the [[great-conflict|Great Conflict]], [[finn-deimne|Finn]] plans Orario's defence over the maps in the headquarters' war room.[@ar03-guild-war-room] Once the battle begins he directs it from the building's rooftop, his orders carried by messengers and magic-stone beacons.[@ar03-guild-rooftop]

### Chamber of Prayers {#chamber-of-prayers}

Beneath the headquarters lies the **Chamber of Prayers** ({{ja|祈禱の間}}, *chamber of prayer*)[@chamber-of-prayers.fm13-ja], an underground temple where [[ouranos|Ouranos]] sits at his altar, lit by four torches; DanMachi 13 calls it "a stone room reminiscent of a temple".[@fm11-chamber, fm13-chamber] Ouranos is "the god who prayed to the Dungeon", and an [[oculus|oculus]] is kept there.[@fm14-oculus] Much of the Guild's secret business is done there: Ouranos summons [[hestia|Hestia]] there and tells her the truth about [[wiene|Wiene]] in DanMachi 9, [[hermes|Hermes]] reports to him there after the [[xenos|Xenos]] crisis, and [[royman|Royman]] kneels there to receive his orders.[@fm09-hestia, fm10-hermes, fm11-chamber, so12-royman]
