**Wiene** is a vouivre, one of the [[xenos|Xenos]]. [[bell-cranell|Bell Cranell]] finds her newly born on Floor 19 and brings her home to [[hestia-familia|Hestia Familia]].[@fm09-wiene, fm09-found] Hestia names her Wiene after a name Bell suggests.[@fm09-name]

## Birth

DanMachi 8 shows a newborn with blue, scaled skin and bluish-silver hair emerging from a Dungeon wall; DanMachi 9 identifies her as Wiene. Where exactly she was born is not established.[@fm08-birth]

## With Hestia Familia

Wiene lives with Hestia Familia for a time and experiences family life on the surface. [[haruhime|Haruhime]] becomes her closest carer after Bell. She grows a dragon wing while saving a child, and in the end stays with [[lido|Lido]]'s group.[@fm09-wiene]
