**Lilliluka Erde**, usually called **Lilly**, is a prum supporter. Born into [[soma-familia|Soma Familia]], she meets [[bell-cranell|Bell Cranell]] in DanMachi 2 and converts to [[hestia-familia|Hestia Familia]] in DanMachi 6.[@fm02-lilly, fm06-lilly] She becomes the party's tactician, and in DanMachi 18 she commands the [[familia-coalition|coalition]] in the Familia War.[@fm12-commander, fm18-command]

## Soma Familia

Lilly was born into Soma Familia. As a supporter she stole from adventurers to save up for her release, and was exploited by others in the Familia.[@fm02-lilly] Her prum parents, also members, were killed by a monster in the Dungeon while chasing money for Soma's wine.[@fm02-lilly-past] She once ran away and lived with an elderly couple at their flower shop, but members of Soma Familia found her, robbed her and ransacked her home there, and the couple threw her out.[@fm02-lilly-past] In DanMachi 2 Bell rescues and forgives her after she steals from him.[@fm02-lilly] Just before that rescue, Kanu, a former comrade from Soma Familia, forces her to hand over the key to her savings in gnome jewels, so she loses everything she has saved.[@fm03-stolen, fm02-kanu-key] Afterwards she lets Soma Familia believe she has died,[@fm06-feigned-death] and she hides her identity from it with a disguise.[@fm03-disguise] By DanMachi 3 she has gone nearly half a year without a Status update: she reached the quota but did not hand the money in, so as not to stand out, and being unable to update was the side effect.[@fm03-status, fm03-ja-status] When Bell fights a Minotaur in DanMachi 3, the wounded Lilly begs [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]]'s adventurers to save him; their arrival is "all thanks to Lilly".[@fm03-lilly-loki-help]

In DanMachi 6 [[zanis-lustra|Zanis]] demands at least ten million valis for her release and later captures her. She resists the Familia's [[soma#divine-wine|Divine Wine]] through willpower and persuades [[soma|Soma]] to stop the fighting.[@fm06-soma] Hestia puts up the [[hestia-knife|Hestia Knife]] as collateral, Soma consents, and the two gods rewrite her [[falna|Falna]] so that she joins Hestia Familia.[@fm06-lilly] Two days after the [[war-game|War Game]] she pays Soma Familia, and Soma apologises to her.[@fm06-paid]

## Hestia Familia

In DanMachi 4 [[welf-crozzo|Welf]] joins Bell and Lilly, making a three-person party.[@fm04-cell] In DanMachi 5, when the party is lost in the middle levels with Welf's leg crushed in a rockslide, it is Lilly who proposes going down to the [[safe-point|safe point]] on [[floor-18|Floor 18]] instead of looking for a way up.[@fm05-lilly-floor18] During the battle against the Black [[goliath|Goliath]] there, she finds a large weapon while picking out arms at the base (the English has her find it "when fumbling through an old cargo box") and throws it to Bell, who holds the massive black sword as he charges [[skills#argonaut|Argonaut]] against the giant.[@fm05-lilly-black-sword] In the War Game against [[apollo-familia|Apollo Familia]] she uses [[#cinder-ella|Cinder Ella]] to pass as the enemy's Luan for nearly four days and opens the castle's west gate.[@fm06-luan] In DanMachi 8 she turns down a marriage proposal from [[finn-deimne|Finn]] of [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]].[@fm08-card] Finn tells Bell that what impressed him was her conduct on the day of the Minotaur fight in DanMachi 3: "She was so determined to save your life that she ignored her own injuries and threw away her pride in order to convince us to help you."[@fm08-finn-reason] She declines because, as she tells him, she has devoted her life to Bell, just as Finn has devoted his to the prums.[@fm08-lilly-bell] The same volume shows her looking after Bom Cornwall, owner of the shop the Gnome Trader, who puts her up for a night when she runs from home.[@fm08-bom] She at first argues that sheltering the [[xenos|Xenos]] girl [[wiene|Wiene]] endangers the Familia, then uses Cinder Ella to rescue Wiene from a mob.[@fm09-wiene] In DanMachi 11, as the Xenos escape through [[daedalus-street|Daedalus Street]], she takes the form of the al-miraj [[al-miraj#aruru|Aruru]] to draw pursuing adventurers after her, and copies Finn to deceive Loki Familia.[@fm11-daedalus]

In DanMachi 12 she trains as an expedition commander under [[daphne|Daphne]].[@fm12-commander] After Bell is swept away in the [[water-capital|Water Capital]], Daphne entrusts her with command of the party.[@fm12-lilly-command] When the wounded adventurers Dormul and Luvis offer to stay behind as decoys, the logical choice for a leader, she refuses and saves them: "If he were here, he wouldn’t leave you behind!"[@fm12-lilly-refuses] In DanMachi 14 she takes command of the party against the floor boss [[amphisbaena|Amphisbaena]] once [[aisha-belka|Aisha]] moves to the front line,[@fm14-amphisbaena-command] and later Xenos join her party to search for Bell and [[lyu-leon|Lyu]].[@fm14-xenos-rescue] In DanMachi 15 she reaches Level 2 and chooses the Development Ability Resistance.[@fm15-card, fm15-resistance] Disguised as an elf, she buys flowers from the couple who once turned her away; they express regret, and she leaves without revealing herself.[@fm15-flowers] In DanMachi 17 [[freya|Freya]]'s charm rewrites her memories: she insists she would never have become Bell's supporter and believes that Welf saved her from Soma Familia.[@fm17-lilly-charm] When she remembers everything, she collapses in shock at how she treated the boy she had fallen for and keeps apologising.[@fm17-lilly-remembers]

In the Familia War of DanMachi 18 she commands the coalition and uses Cinder Ella to break the Bringar's coordination.[@fm18-command] In DanMachi 20 the gods give her the title *Little Marshal*.[@fm20-marshal] In Sword Oratoria 12, disguised as Finn, she takes command of two of Loki Familia's fronts.[@so12-fronts, so12-command]

## Abilities

- **Cinder Ella** changes her appearance to the form she pictures while casting. It works best when copying a real form, has limits on how far it can change her body size, and can even imitate small monsters, but it does not raise her Status.[@fm15-card, fm03-cinder, fm11-aruru]
- **[[skills#artel-assist|Artel Assist]]** helps her carry loads: once the weight of her equipment passes a threshold, it applies a correction that grows with the weight. DanMachi 15 prints the name as *Arter Assist*.[@fm08-card, fm15-card]
- **[[skills#command-call|Command Call]]**, which appears at the update that lets her reach Level 2, extends how far her shouted instructions carry, telepathically, but only to people with the same Falna.[@fm15-card]
- **Mind Call** is the name DanMachi 18 gives to the Skill she developed at her Level Up, which allows telepathic contact with people who share her blessing. It is Command Call: the Japanese writes the same Skill name, with Mind Call as a second reading.[@fm18-mindcall, fm15-card]

### Cinder Ella {#cinder-ella}

**Cinder Ella** is the magic of Lilliluka Erde. It changes her body into the form she clearly pictures while casting, and it has two chants: one to transform and one to change back.[@cinder-ella.fm08-card, cinder-ella.fm15-card] She uses it first to escape and steal as a supporter, and later as a tool of deception for [[hestia-familia|Hestia Familia]].[@cinder-ella.fm15-origin, cinder-ella.fm06-luan]

- **User:** Lilliluka Erde[@cinder-ella.fm08-card, cinder-ella.fm15-card]
- **Status entry:** Shape-shifting magic[@cinder-ella.fm08-card, cinder-ella.fm15-card]
- **Limit:** Cannot give her a higher Status than her own[@cinder-ella.fm11-limits]
- **Activation:** "Your scars are mine. My scars are mine."[@cinder-ella.fm08-card, cinder-ella.fm15-card]
- **Release:** "Stroke of midnight’s bell."[@cinder-ella.fm08-card, cinder-ella.fm15-card]
- **Japanese (DanMachi 8 card):** the activation is {{ja|貴方の刻印は私のもの。私の刻印は私のもの|anata no kizu wa watashi no mono. watashi no kizu wa watashi no mono}}: she says *kizu*, *scars*, and *marks* is the written meaning. The release is {{ja|響く十二時のお告げ|hibiku jūniji no otsuge}}, *the resounding twelve-o'clock tidings*: the Japanese names twelve o'clock, not midnight, and has no bell.[@cinder-ella.fm08-card]

#### Effects {#cinder-ella-effects}

- **Appearance, not power.** She can take the form of a target whose build closely resembles her own, as an imitation only, and that includes monsters of that build. Cinder Ella does not raise her Status above her own or gives her a monster's potential.[@cinder-ella.fm05-release, cinder-ella.fm05-ja-release, cinder-ella.fm11-limits] Within that limit it does copy a target's inborn traits: in DanMachi 5 she explains that it copies "basic characteristics of the target" as well as the form, such as an animal person's inborn sense of smell (the Japanese names smell and hearing), and she follows Hestia's perfume to rescue her.[@cinder-ella.fm05-senses]
- **Imitation.** In DanMachi 3 she can basically only turn into prums or children of about her own build. As her Magic ability rose she gained some leeway with clothing, but only as imitation and only in appearance.[@cinder-ella.fm03-limits, cinder-ella.fm03-ja-limits] In DanMachi 11 she notes that fooling people also means copying the target's personality.[@cinder-ella.fm11-finn]
- **Ending it.** She releases it with the release chant, for example to save Mind in the Dungeon, and, apparently, a hit can end it. In DanMachi 18 a spear strike dispels her disguise.[@cinder-ella.fm06-mind, cinder-ella.fm03-limits, cinder-ella.fm03-ja-limits, cinder-ella.fm18-dispel]
- **Speed.** After she reaches Level 2 in DanMachi 15 the spell is "blazing fast": she transforms in the moment a crowd hides her, and the narration credits the "astounding casting speed" to her new Level.[@cinder-ella.fm15-speed]

#### Magic or Skill? {#cinder-ella-magic-or-skill}

> [!NOTE] Magic, not a Skill
> Both Status cards list Cinder Ella under Magic,[@cinder-ella.fm08-card, cinder-ella.fm15-card] and DanMachi 9's narration calls it transformation magic in the Japanese, where the English prints "her Cinder Ella skill" and "her magic skill, Cinder Ella".[@cinder-ella.fm09-skill] Other English volumes call it "transformation magic".[@cinder-ella.fm10-transformation, cinder-ella.so12-finn]
