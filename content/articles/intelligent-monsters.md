The term **intelligent monsters** describes the [[xenos|Xenos]], while *humanoid monsters* describes humanlike forms, including a [[monsters#vouivre|Vouivre]].[@fm09-xenos, fm09-form] [[ouranos|Ouranos]] says the Xenos have intellect above that of normal monsters and hearts with will and emotions.[@fm09-xenos] A Vouivre is described as humanoid, with its usual form having a humanlike upper body and a snakelike lower body.[@fm09-form]
