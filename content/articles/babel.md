**Babel** is the white tower at the centre of [[orario|Orario]], built directly over the entrance to [[dungeon|the Dungeon]], which it seals like a lid.[@fm01-babel, fm01-layout] The city's eight Main Streets run out from it and are named for the directions in which they leave it.[@fm01-layout, fm01-streets]

## History

The first Babel was shorter. Lilly has heard that it was destroyed when the gods first came down to the Lower World; it was rebuilt with their cooperation.[@fm02-babel] Since then it has been known as Babel the Falling Tower; the people of the time, revering the gods' blessings, let them live in it, and the tower was built higher and higher until it gained "the image of a shrine to the power of the gods".[@fm02-falling-tower] [[fels|Fels]] names its builder as [[daedalus|Daedalus]], a famous architect of the time when the gods came to this world, whose buildings became the foundation of Orario.[@fm10-daedalus-babel]

|---|---|
| 4–8 | [[hephaistos|Hephaistos]] Familia's shops; [[bell-cranell|Bell]] first meets [[welf-crozzo|Welf]] in its shop and rest area on the eighth floor.[@fm02-babel, fm04-babel] |
| Up to 20 | [[guild|Guild]] tenants.[@fm02-babel] |
| 30 | Where the [[denatus|Denatus]] is held.[@fm04-babel] |
| 50 | [[freya|Freya]]'s vantage point over the city; in DanMachi 18 she gives up her right to reside on Babel's highest floor.[@fm03-babel, fm18-freya-floor] |
| Above | Residences of the gods.[@fm02-babel] |

Babel also houses a medical centre, where Bell recovers after his fight with the [[minotaur|Minotaur]] in DanMachi 3.[@fm03-clinic] Managed by the [[guild|Guild]], the tower is used to monitor and control the Dungeon entrance; besides the shops it holds shower rooms and other public facilities for adventurers, a cafeteria and an [[the-exchange|Exchange]], and its ground floor is ringed with arches so that adventurers can enter from any direction.[@fm02-facilities] From the basement, which connects directly to the Dungeon, a great staircase spirals down into it.[@fm15-staircase]
