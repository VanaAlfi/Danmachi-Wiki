An **oculus** (plural **oculi**) is a magic item made by [[fels|Fels]]: one of a pair of twin crystals, each of which displays what its twin is seeing and hearing.[@fm10-babel] Oculi let [[ouranos|Ouranos]] and Fels watch and speak to people far away, including deep in the Dungeon, and they are used to direct several of the novels' large operations.[@fm10-pairs, so11-floors, fm18-supply]

## Making them

DanMachi 10 credits the crystals to Fels, "once known as the Sage". They were extremely hard to make: Fels lacked the right materials, and the work needed a mastery that even Perseus ([[asfi|Asfi]]) had not yet reached. At that point in the story the narration calls the oculus the only magic item in existence capable of long-distance communication.[@fm10-babel] In Sword Oratoria 11 Asfi's professional pride is stung by the existence of an item that allows communication across different floors of the Dungeon, and of its maker.[@so11-floors] How they are made, and when Fels first made them, are not stated.

## How they work

- **Pairs only.** An oculus can interact only with its paired twin, so Fels needs a separate pair for every place to be watched and every person to be contacted. Fels's robe is bulky with crystals.[@fm10-pairs]
- **Sound and picture.** The image can be switched off: in DanMachi 11 Bell hears Hestia through a blue crystal on the back of his hand that is "off so as to not show an image".[@fm11-voice]
- **Range.** They work between the surface and the Dungeon, and across Dungeon floors.[@fm10-pairs, so11-floors]
- **Not a map.** An oculus carries voices and pictures, not positions. In DanMachi 14 Ouranos, speaking through [[lido|Lido]]'s oculus, tells the Xenos which floor Bell and Lyu are on, but they still have to search that floor for them.[@fm14-rescue] In DanMachi 11 the positions on Hestia's map come from a separate item of Fels's, Seeker Powder.[@fm11-rescue]
- **Colours.** Oculi are described as blue, yellow or white; no difference in function is given.[@fm10-babel, fm10-yellow, fm18-white]

## Uses

- **Surveillance.** Fels hides one crystal in the artwork of Babel's basement ceiling, above the way down to the Dungeon, and fits another into the empty eye socket of a rescued owl familiar, to watch outlaws and blacklisted adventurers.[@fm10-babel] {{inference}} In Sword Oratoria 4 Ouranos and Fels watch Aiz's battle through "the magic crystal attached to Aiz's loin guard"; the passage does not call it an oculus.[@so04-aiz]
- **The Great Conflict.** Ouranos hears Erebus's proclamation through the oculus his mage gave him in Astrea Record 1, and in Astrea Record 2 he speaks with Fels in the field through one.[@ar01-erebus, ar02-ouranos]
- **The Xenos.** Fels gives the [[xenos|Xenos]] several sets; Lido carries the only one linked to the surface.[@fm10-pairs]
- **Hestia Familia's rescue.** In DanMachi 11 Hestia and [[haruhime|Haruhime]] direct Welf, Mikoto and the others through the streets of [[daedalus-street|Daedalus Street]] through the twins of their oculi.[@fm11-rescue]
- **Knossos.** In Sword Oratoria 12 Fels gives Lilly oculi connected to both assault squads in [[knossos|Knossos]]. Because people near a crystal can hear the voice coming from it, she takes [[finn-deimne|Finn]]'s form with [[magic#cinder-ella|Cinder Ella]] before taking over command.[@so12-lilly]
- **The Familia War.** In DanMachi 18 there are not enough oculi for everyone, so Lilly gives them to groups she trusts, such as Aisha's and Tsubaki's.[@fm18-supply] Their advantage in speed is largely cancelled when [[hedin|Hedin]] keeps Freya Familia's whole force within earshot of his orders.[@fm18-hedin]

## Breaking the link

Destroying one crystal of a pair cuts the connection. In DanMachi 10 [[gros|Gros]] tears Lido's surface crystal from his hand and destroys it, leaving the Xenos with no link to the surface.[@fm10-gros] In DanMachi 11 Fels crushes a blue crystal at [[hermes|Hermes]]'s unspoken command, and Hestia's voice is cut off.[@fm11-hermes] At the climax of the Familia War, Lilly's Skill Mind Call carries the critical information to Welf and Bell "in place of the broken oculus".[@fm18-mindcall]

## A possible exception

> [!NOTE] Watching through the wrong crystal
> In Minor Myths and Legends 1 the Xenos, hiding in the sewers, watch what happens to Bell through their own oculus, "possibly due to interference from Hestia’s paired crystal".[@ss01-xenos] The narration offers this only as a possibility. It does not fit easily with DanMachi 10's statement that an oculus works only with its twin, and this wiki leaves the question open.[@fm10-pairs, ss01-xenos]
