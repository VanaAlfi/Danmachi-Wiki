**Noir**, **Dyne** and **Bahra** were [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]]'s three oldest members: the human swordsman Noir Sachsen, the dwarf Dyne and the Amazon Bahra.[@ar01-front, ar02-veterans] They converted to Loki Familia from other Familias, but had been fighting in the Dungeon since before [[finn-deimne|Finn]], [[riveria|Riveria]], [[gareth|Gareth]] or even [[loki|Loki]] came to the city, and they taught those three the basics of adventuring.[@ar02-veterans, ar03-veterans] In Astrea Record 3, during the [[great-conflict|Great Conflict]], they defy orders to charge into the monster horde converging on the city centre, and die there.[@ar03-charge-off, ar03-deaths]

## The three veterans

Astrea Record 2 introduces Noir Sachsen as an old, bearded human, over seventy and still adventuring at Level 4, a master swordsman as spry as a man decades younger.[@ar02-veterans] Astrea Record 3 describes him in old-fashioned combat gear like a [[far-east|Far Eastern]] kimono, and [[falgar-batros|Falgar]] recognises him as "the Bowstring Blade".[@ar03-veterans] His sword is a single-edged blade based on Far Eastern designs, named Undying Vow.[@ar03-manor] Dyne is a grizzled dwarf with a beard even bigger than Noir's, still robust in old age. Bahra is an Amazon with the mature beauty of a woman in her forties and a beast's fighting instincts.[@ar02-veterans, ar03-veterans]

The narration counts them as Loki Familia's oldest members "in terms of age, at least", and says their long Dungeon experience alone puts them on the level of any first-class adventurer.[@ar02-veterans] Bahra claims they were Finn's equals in their prime.[@ar02-veterans] Finn recalls the "old-man wisdom" with which they "beat sense into us back in the day". Bahra says Finn, Gareth and Riveria knew nothing of the Dungeon then, and Dyne remembers how strong those children already were.[@ar03-manor] Bahra jokes that the carnage of the dark age is nothing beside the nightly wars between [[zeus-and-hera-familias|Zeus and Hera]] and Horus and Sobek.[@ar03-veterans]

> [!NOTE] Noir's height
> Astrea Record 2 gives Noir's height as over 170 celches; Astrea Record 3 gives it as 180 celches.[@ar02-veterans, ar03-veterans]

## The last charge

In Astrea Record 3, monsters pour down the eight main streets toward the barrier at the city centre, and no one can leave the strongholds to stop them.[@ar03-despair] Noir calls Dyne and Bahra to his side and tells them it has been "a real pleasure workin' alongside you old dogs". Loki sees what they mean to do, but Noir only says, "Loki… See you", and the three leave Twilight Manor against their goddess's wishes.[@ar03-charge-off]

They cut into the parade of monsters from behind on North Main Street. Dyne hands Noir something he has picked up, and Bahra waves the Trickster flag she took from the manor.[@ar03-devices] Finn understands what they have realised: there is only one way out, and the veterans have gone ahead to spare him the decision.[@ar03-devices] As they hack their path, Finn screams Noir's name, dropping his commander's mask for the first and last time.[@ar03-dirge] Dyne calls on "all you tired old dogs out there" to show the young how it is done, and Bahra invites them to "try to keep up".[@ar03-dirge] Many of the city's oldest adventurers follow them into the centre. Noir tells [[allen-fromel|Allen]] that the rest is up to him, and Loki says they have all chosen to die.[@ar03-allen, ar03-deaths]

[[olivas-act|Olivas Act]] arrives with his followers and taunts them as "dusty fossils"; Noir dares him to come down and fight.[@ar03-deaths] Olivas orders a volley. Lightning kills Bahra and flames kill Dyne, both smiling, and arrows riddle Noir.[@ar03-deaths] Raul begs Finn to send help. Finn refuses: they have no one to spare from the strongholds, and Noir's insubordination has left gaps to fill.[@ar03-deaths] Noir runs on into the horde and leaps into the air. He sets off dozens of the Evils' self-destruction devices, taken from fallen cultists, in an explosion greater than any seen in the war.[@ar03-explosion]

The narration later credits the veterans with giving [[ottar|Ottar]] just enough time to win, and Olivas, wondering whether he should have stopped them, tears at his hair.[@ar03-ottar] Their charge also left holes in the defences of the other strongholds, since many of the veterans who followed them were Level 3 and above; that was why Finn had been reluctant to send them.[@ar03-holes]
