**Zanis Lustra** {{small|printed *Zanis Rustra* in DanMachi 10}} is [[soma-familia|Soma Familia]]'s Level 2 captain, who exploits its members before losing his position and being imprisoned.[@fm06-rank, fm06-control, fm10-punishment] His title is Gandharva, the Wine-Guardian.[@fm06-rank] He takes advantage of [[soma|Soma]]'s neglect of leadership, using the god's name to manipulate members for his own gain.[@fm06-control]

## History {#history}

| Volume | Events |
|---|---|
| DanMachi 6 | Zanis proposes a payment of ten million valis for [[lilliluka-erde|Lilliluka Erde]] to leave the Familia.[@fm06-price] |
| DanMachi 6 | Lilliluka accuses him of cooperating with [[apollo-familia|Apollo Familia]] for money, and his acceptance of her surrender is followed by a flare signalling his Familia to withdraw.[@fm06-collusion] |
| DanMachi 6 | [[welf-crozzo|Welf Crozzo]] defeats Zanis in their duel, after which [[chandra-ihit|Chandra]] places the defeated man in handcuffs.[@fm06-defeat] |
| DanMachi 10 | Zanis has lost his command for exploiting and selling [[soma#divine-wine|divine wine]], and Soma has sealed his Status as punishment while he remains confined in a holding cell.[@fm10-punishment] |
| DanMachi 10 | Lilliluka recalls his offer, made during negotiations with Apollo Familia before the [[war-game|War Game]], to use her [[magic#cinder-ella|transformation magic]] to capture monsters for sale.[@fm10-offer] |
| DanMachi 10 | When questioned, he points them toward [[daedalus-street|Daedalus Street]] but refuses a precise location; Lilliluka declines Chandra's suggestion of torture.[@fm10-clue] |
| DanMachi 15 | In an earlier recollection, Lilliluka hears rumors that Zanis obtained command by disposing of rival candidates for the position.[@fm15-rumor] |
| DanMachi 15 | He distributes divine wine stolen from Soma, imposes funding quotas and uses the wine to control the Familia's members.[@fm15-wine] |
