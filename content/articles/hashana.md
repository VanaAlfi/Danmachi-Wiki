**Hashana Dorlia** was a Level 4 adventurer of [[ganesha-familia|Ganesha Familia]].[@so02-status, so05-ganesha] He secretly accepted a [[quest|quest]] to go down alone to the thirtieth floor and bring back a crystal orb holding a fetus.[@so02-quest, so03-orb] In Sword Oratoria 2 he hands the orb over in [[rivira|Rivira]] and is murdered at an inn by the red-haired creature [[levis|Levis]], who then wears his face as a mask while she searches for it.[@so02-handover, so02-face]

## Death in Rivira

A body with its head crushed is found in a room at Willy's inn in Rivira. The man had paid with an extraordinarily valuable [[magic-stone|magic stone]] instead of a Familia [[emblem|emblem]], so no one knows who he is, and a woman in a robe who went into the room with him has vanished.[@so02-inn] [[bors|Bors]] has a [[status-thief|Status Thief]] used to open the dead man's Status. [[riveria|Riveria]] and [[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz]] read it: he was Hashana Dorlia, of Ganesha Familia.[@so02-status] The room panics, and Bors remembers him as "the Strong Fist Warrior Hashana", a Level 4.[@so02-status] Poison is ruled out, because his Status shows the Development Ability Immunity in its ability column.[@so02-immunity]

[[finn-deimne|Finn]] finds that the killer broke his neck before crushing his head, with no sign of a struggle, and that his pack had been torn apart in a search.[@so02-body] A bloodstained quest form survives: "Floor thirty…Alone, collect…Secretly…"[@so02-quest] Bors says Hashana was famous but rarely came to Rivira. The innkeeper [[willy|Willy]] remembers that he always wore a helmet much like [[ganesha|Ganesha]]'s mask, but never full plate armour, and the plate armour in the room bears no Ganesha Familia emblem.[@so02-quest] Riveria concludes that he hid his identity to carry out the quest without telling his Familia, and Finn has Bors seal the town, sure that the killer has not yet found what she was looking for.[@so02-quest, so02-seal]

[[lulune-louie|Lulune Louie]] later explains that she had been hired to meet a man in full plate armour at a bar and take a package from him; he handed it over at once. Free of his quest, he then let his guard down with the woman who invited him to the inn.[@so02-handover] The killer turns out to be the red-haired woman Levis. After breaking his neck she cut off his face to wear as a mask and crushed the head to hide it, and she stayed in Rivira disguised as a man while the investigation looked only for women.[@so02-face]

## Afterwards

In Sword Oratoria 2 [[fels|Fels]] tells [[ouranos|Ouranos]] that "Hashana, the one who took up that quest", has been killed, and Ouranos asks after "the delivery girl".[@so02-fels] In Sword Oratoria 3 Fels tells Aiz that the thirtieth floor is where Fels had sent Hashana, and tells Ouranos that they had dealt with the pantry there themselves, which is how Hashana could collect the orb.[@so03-orb]
