**Telskyura** is a nation of [[races#amazon|Amazon]] warriors, "the Holy Land of the Amazons", ruled by the goddess [[kali|Kali]] and her [[kali-familia|Kali Familia]].[@so06-power, so06-kali] [[riveria|Riveria]] calls it a nation of blood and war, one of the few world powers besides [[orario|Orario]], and completely isolated: little is known of what goes on inside its borders.[@so06-power] [[hyrute-sisters|Tiona and Tione Hyrute]] were born and raised there.[@so06-born] Kali Familia and its rites are described on the [[kali-familia|Kali Familia]] page; this page covers the country.

## Where it lies

[[anakity-autumn|Anakity]] (Aki) calls Telskyura "a peninsular country far to Orario’s southeast", and Riveria agrees that it is a land cut off from the world, walled in by sea and sheer cliffs and known as a country of Amazons alone.[@so06-where] When the Hyrute twins leave, they sail "far, far away from the vast peninsula".[@so06-peninsula] In Minor Myths and Legends 2 its name is written with characters meaning *battle nation*.[@ss02-island, ss02-state, ss02-isle]

Its warriors travel by sea. In Sword Oratoria 6 Kali Familia comes to [[njordr#port-meren|Port Meren]] in a ship whose "engine" is a host of Telskyuran warriors at the oars, and the wounded are sent home on it.[@so06-ship, so06-home]

## The country

Loki compares it to [[ares|Ares]]'s kingdom of Rakia: "A nation-state familia".[@so06-where] The only men allowed in the country are slaves or those kept for breeding.[@so06-where] Riveria has heard that no day passes there without battle cries and cheering crowds, and that its people possess an incredible capacity for war.[@so06-power]

- **The Falna from birth.** Its girls are christened children of their goddess from the moment they are born, and it was said that they knew how to kill a goblin before they could speak.[@so06-girls]
- **The rites.** To be respected in Telskyura one had to be a "true warrior", and the rites, the daily fights to the death in the arena against captured monsters and fellow warriors, are "a time-honored national custom". Survivors are known simply as True Warriors; the inhabitants receive no aliases, with one exception, Argana's *Kalima*.[@so06-rites, so06-alias] See [[kali-familia|Kali Familia]].
- **Language.** The Amazons of Telskyura have a phrase of their own, "Se wehga". Young Tiona, who could not read or write even the Amazonian language, had never seen Koine letters, and a scrap of Koine she found "wasn’t of Telskyuran origin".[@so06-sewehga, so06-koine]
- **Its reputation.** Loki recalls *The Curious Tales of Rastillo Furough*, the chronicle of an explorer who sneaked into Telskyura and barely escaped with his life; Loki Familia's archive has a copy.[@so06-explorer] [[lefiya|Lefiya]] learned at school that it was a very savage country.[@so06-explorer]

## Kali and the land

Kali claims the country was "like that even before I came along", and that she only tried, as far as she could, to make it the holy land the Amazons wanted. No Amazon had ever asked to leave until the Hyrute twins did, and no one did after them; Kali let them go, even leaving their Statuses unsealed.[@so06-leave] The twins then travelled from country to country, sleeping in the open.[@so06-travels] After Kali Familia's defeat at Meren, Kali mourns: "This may very well be the end for Telskyura… My poor beloved kingdom…"[@so06-home]
