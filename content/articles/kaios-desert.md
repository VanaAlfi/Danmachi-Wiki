The **Kaios Desert**, also called the Grand Sand Sea, is an arid region south-west of the centre of the [[geography#the-continent|Continent]], where many countries are clustered.[@fc02-region] It is the setting of Familia Chronicle 2, in which [[freya|Freya]] travels there and helps [[ali|Ali]] of [[ali#shalzad|Shalzad]] win the [[ali#calamity-of-the-hot-sands|Calamity of the Hot Sands]]; that story and its kingdoms are on the [[ali|Ali]] page.[@fc02-region, fc02-war]

## Geography

The giant [[geography#kaios-desert-countries-and-towns-nire-river|Nire River]] runs through the desert and splits it into east and west. Countries close to the river tend to flourish, and those far from it tend to stay small.[@fc02-nire] [[israfan|Israfan]], "the country of merchants", lies in the middle of the western desert, surrounded by other countries; its town of [[geography#kaios-desert-countries-and-towns-leodo|Leodo]], near its border, has an oasis and stands almost at the exact centre of the western Kaios, a crucial place for trade.[@fc02-nire] Shalzad and [[geography#kaios-desert-countries-and-towns-warsa|Warsa]] are both powers of the central western Kaios.[@fc02-powers] The desert is hot by day and cold by night, and thieves and monsters make it dangerous to cross without bodyguards.[@fc02-heat, fc02-danger] A legend holds that the desert itself spread through the ravages of [[monsters#basilisk|basilisks]], which destroyed many cities in ancient times.[@fc02-basilisk]

## Kingdoms and Familias

Every country in the Kaios Desert is a kingdom, and most Familias there serve as the armies of their countries, so a god mostly deals with a nation's elite.[@fc02-kingdoms, fc02-military] The kavir, the desert's second-tier adventurers, are an overwhelming military power in the western Kaios; Warsa's army, [[resheph|Resheph Familia]], has several.[@fc02-kavir] King Aram later writes that the harsh desert is famous for producing many kavir, and that [[aisha-belka|Aisha Belka]] of the Berbera found her start among its dunes.[@ss02-kavir] [[freya-familia|Freya Familia]] is one of the strongest factions, famous enough that word of it reaches even the Kaios Desert.[@fc02-freya]

No country had become the dominant power in the western Kaios, because its crowded kingdoms always watched for the chance to strike a neighbour that grew too strong.[@fc02-balance] After the Calamity of the Hot Sands, Shalzad became the first great power of the central western Kaios, "arguably the first great nation to rise from the vast swathes of the west-central region", as King Aram puts it.[@fc02-power, ss02-nation]

## Slavery

Bofman Fazoul tells Freya that the whole Kaios region accepts slavery, unlike [[orario|Orario]]. Slavery exists in several other parts of the world too; Orario, with no slave market at all, is the exception, and Familia Chronicle 2 attributes that chiefly to Ouranos and the gods working with him, who reject the system.[@fc02-slavery] Freya finds Ali among slaves in the desert.[@fc02-war]

## Trade and culture

- **[[desert-ship|Desert ships]].** [[altena|Altena]] recently began selling desert ships in the Kaios, and the firms that own one can be counted on one hand; Bofman's Fazoul Trading Company is one.[@fc02-ship] By Minor Myths and Legends 2 the nations of the Kaios depend more and more on trade with Altena. King Aram proposes to promote the magic-stone trade with Orario instead, in return for sending Shalzad's top warriors to train in the Dungeon.[@ss02-trade]
- **Halvan** ({{ja|戦盤}}, written *war board*)[@fc02-halvan-ja]. One of the most popular games of the region, comparable to the Continent's chess or the Far East's shogi. Its pieces include the king, *malik*, written *king-emperor*, and the *malikah*, written *princess*.[@fc02-halvan] See [[ali|Ali]] for Ali's skill at it.
- **Architecture in Orario.** Part of Orario's [[pleasure-quarter|Pleasure Quarter]] uses "Cultural designs of the Kaios Desert", and Sword Oratoria 8's hideout in the [[third-district|third district]] is a four-storey building drawing heavily on Kaios culture and architecture.[@fm07-designs, so08-building]
- **A place of stories.** As a child [[lefiya|Lefiya]] dreamed of "a sea of sand called the Kaios Desert". The Kaios is among the destinations where graduates of the [[school-district|School District]] leave the ship, and Lefiya's old roommate hears stories of her "from the Empire to the Kaios Desert".[@so13-dream, so13-students, so13-roommate]
