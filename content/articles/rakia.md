The **Kingdom of Rakia**, a western monarchy, is in reality a country-sized Familia under [[ares|Ares]]; its sixth invasion of [[orario|Orario]] fails in DanMachi 8.[@fm08-rakia-kingdom, fm08-rakia-end] It lies in the western part of the continent, relatively close to Orario, and more than 600,000 subjects are said to live under its rule.[@fm08-rakia-kingdom, fm04-rakia-lyu] Its royal capital, Barva, has a huge castle and a castle town, and the green, fertile country has the barbarous side of a so-called military state.[@fm08-rakia-kingdom, fm15-rakia-familia]

## A Familia the size of a country {#a-familia-the-size-of-a-country}

Ares, the God of War, is in fact its head and rules the country: Rakia is a Familia on a national scale, grown from Ares and a handful of followers into a kingdom with a long history.[@fm08-rakia-kingdom] Every soldier has received a [[falna|Falna]], the subjects who run its industries are its non-combatants, and Ares, its one and only patron god, has chosen its kings, who lead the Familia.[@fm08-rakia-kingdom] Its first prince, [[ares-familia#marius-victrix-rakia|Marius Victrix Rakia]], is the young second-in-command of [[ares-familia|Ares Familia]].[@fm08-rakia-gambit]

Its soldiers and knights number near a hundred thousand, too many for Ares to bless and update alone, so vassal-gods such as [[phobos|Phobos]], who lost a war to Rakia, take on the weaker fighters; the fallen Crozzo family belongs to her Familia.[@fm15-rakia-familia] To prevent rebellion all its main fighting strength, the Level 2 and Level 3 knights and the royal guard among them, stays under Ares's direct control, and any vassal-god's follower who excels is made to convert to Ares Familia.[@fm15-rakia-familia] Its soldiers are Level 3 at best, and its army has a history of expanding by invading other countries and cities.[@so09-rakia-orario]

## Magic swords and old wars {#magic-swords-and-old-wars}

[[lyu-leon|Lyu]] tells [[bell-cranell|Bell]] that the Crozzo family supplied Rakia with large numbers of [[magic-sword|magic swords]] in exchange for nobility, and that armed with them its army advanced unchecked, wiping out its enemies by sheer firepower.[@fm04-rakia-lyu] In her account its fires reached a forest of the [[races#elf|elves]] and burned it down.[@fm04-rakia-lyu] [[welf-crozzo|Welf]] tells Bell that all its magic swords later shattered without warning before they could be used, that the kingdom then lost battle after battle, and that the Crozzo family took the blame and lost its nobility.[@fm04-rakia-welf] In Lyu's account the surviving elves took Falna and attacked Rakia, which no longer had its magic swords.[@fm04-rakia-lyu]

At the height of its power Rakia long used [[geography#ruins-near-orario-shreme-castle-ruins|Shreme Castle]], an ancient fort near Orario, as a strategic point until more than a century ago, and rebuilt it with a showy main tower.[@fm06-rakia-shreme]
