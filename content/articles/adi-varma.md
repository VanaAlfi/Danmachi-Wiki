**Adi Varma** {{small|printed *Ardee* in Astrea Record 1 and 2}} is a [[races#human|human]] member of [[ganesha-familia|Ganesha Familia]] and the younger sister of [[shakti-varma|Shakti Varma]].[@ar01-identity, ar01-human] She serves in the city watch and is a Level 3 adventurer, one year older than [[lyu-leon|Lyu Leon]].[@ar01-identity, fc03-age] With [[alize-lovell|Alize Lovell]], she is one of Lyu's closest friends.[@fc03-adi-skill] Her character sheet at the end of Astrea Record 2 lists two [[skills#adi-skills|Skills]] and two [[magic#adi-varma-spells|spells]].[@ar02-sheet]

## Looks and character {#looks-and-character}

Her short pale blue hair gives her a boyish look, and her eyes are the colour of her hair.[@ar01-adi-introduced, ar01-adi-thief, fc03-adi-twilight] Unlike her strict sister she is warm, kind, open with everyone and childlike, with a habit of hugging people, Lyu included.[@fc03-adi-twilight, ar01-adi-thief] Though she shrinks from hurting people, she fights with a one-handed sword, Sacred Oath.[@ar01-adi-death] Her justice, she tells Lyu, is turning weapons into things that make people smile, and it can change form and go on.[@ar01-adi-twilight]
