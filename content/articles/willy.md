**Willy** owns Willy's Inn, an inn built into a natural cave in [[rivira|Rivira]].[@so02-owner, fm13-inn] He is an [[races#animal-people|animal person]] of medium height and build, with untidy hair and red painted lines on both cheeks.[@so02-owner]
