**Takemikazuchi** is a god of war and martial arts from the [[far-east|Far East]] and the god of [[takemikazuchi-familia|Takemikazuchi Familia]].[@fm18-domain, fm07-shrine] With other gods he ran a poor shrine that took in orphans, and he came to [[orario|Orario]] with the oldest of them to earn money for it.[@fm07-shrine, fm08-money] He raised [[mikoto|Yamato Mikoto]] as his daughter and trained her, [[ouka|Kashima Ouka]] and [[chigusa|Hitachi Chigusa]] in combat; his own skill, with no more than an ordinary body, can exceed a first-tier adventurer's.[@fm08-daughter, fm12-training] [[hestia|Hestia]], who knew him in Tenkai, calls him *Také*.[@fm15-job, fm05-stall]

## Appearance and character

Takemikazuchi's "masculine refinement" is the opposite of [[hermes|Hermes]]'s effeminate charm.[@fm05-looks] He wears his black hair in mizura, an old style bound in loops at the sides, and has violet eyes.[@fm05-looks, fm05-eyes, fm07-reunion] He is poor: at a gods' party the others jeer at "the head of the dirt-poor Familia", and Haruhime finds him, years after the Far East, with his mizura unchanged and still a little shabby.[@fm01-poor, fm07-reunion] When Hestia arrives in Orario she finds him working for the same potato-snack business; in DanMachi 5 he works at another food stall, where his manager scolds him for wasting time.[@fm15-job, fm05-stall]

Hestia's nickname for him is *Také* (once *Take*, in DanMachi 18). He taught her the *dogeza*, the bow on all fours, telling her it "has the power to make people forgive whatever you have done and grant any request"; DanMachi 6 calls the prostrate bow his Familia's special technique.[@fm01-dogeza, fm18-domain, fm06-conversion]

He is kind to women without noticing their reactions. In DanMachi 8 Mikoto watches him touch and charm women in the street, "Young or old", while he has "no idea what he was doing", and she cries out that he is a natural-born gigolo, the nickname the gods mock him with at DanMachi 4's Denatus.[@fm08-women, fm04-naming] [[miach-familia|Nahza]] says people call him insensitive, and [[miach|Miach]] tells Bell that he would "take a more fatherly role" and draw a line with any mortal girl who loved him.[@fm08-gift, fm08-fatherly]

## The god of martial arts

He presides over "war and martial arts"; the narration also calls him a god of "styles of combat" and of military arts.[@fm18-domain, fm07-shrine, fm04-naming] He trains his followers in many weapons and in fighting empty-handed, and taught Mikoto styles of combat, ninjutsu among them, before they left their homeland.[@fm05-combat, fm07-ninjutsu]

His skill does not depend on a Status. Training Mikoto and Chigusa in the courtyard of [[hearthstone-manor|Hearthstone Manor]] before DanMachi 12's expedition, he throws Mikoto, a Level 2, and Chigusa again and again, though he would be as helpless as any ordinary person against a monster: "Alone, his combat skills exceeded those of a first-tier adventurer."[@fm12-training] His lesson is that a Status cannot be improved overnight, but skill and tactics can.[@fm12-training] In the same weeks before the expedition, DanMachi 15 recalls, he also teaches [[haruhime|Haruhime]] self-defence and tactics: "You must also train your wits."[@fm15-haruhime] In DanMachi 17 he runs across the rooftops "like a ninja" despite having only an average person's body.[@fm17-roofs]

In DanMachi 18's war against [[freya-familia|Freya Familia]] the gods on Hestia's side wear flowers that the enemy must take, and he chooses a purple chrysanthemum. Whether a god of war may defend himself is "a blurry line".[@fm18-domain] When the einherjar reach him, he throws one of them with a one-armed throw before they take his flower. Afterwards twenty-two einherjar lie around him, collapsed from exhaustion, and his followers except Ouka and Chigusa have passed out too. He and [[hephaistos|Hephaistos]] win Hestia enough time to escape.[@fm18-einherjar, fm18-time]

## The shrine in the Far East

Takemikazuchi and other gods ran a shrine on a mountain in the Far East that took in children with no relatives and raised them.[@fm07-shrine, fm15-shrine] The goddess Tsukuyomi is also at the shrine.[@fm08-daughter, fm07-reunion] When the young Mikoto, an orphan, is crying alone, he asks her to become his daughter: one day he will give her his Falna, and "we will share a bond of blood like a true familya familia". He will teach her so many martial arts that she has no time for loneliness. From that day he became her father.[@fm08-daughter]

Haruhime, a noble's daughter, lived in a manor at the foot of the mountain. Takemikazuchi took pity on her and told the children to bring her into the outside world. When her father found out, he apologised on all fours until her father agreed to let them off.[@fm07-shrine] Mikoto says she received his Falna at that time to repay Haruhime's kindness; when Haruhime came to the shrine he thanked her for helping them through a hard winter.[@fm07-blessing, fm15-shrine]

The shrine could not feed the orphans it took in, so the oldest and strongest of them went with Takemikazuchi to Orario to earn money in the Dungeon and send it home.[@fm07-shrine, fm08-money] In DanMachi 16 Mikoto says that a scant two years have passed since she came to the city with him.[@fm16-two-years] In Orario he and his Familia live in old communal housing on a narrow street in the north-west of the city.[@fm08-home]

## Mikoto

At DanMachi 4's [[denatus|Denatus]], when the gods start naming Mikoto, he jumps to his feet. They mock him as a "Natural-born gigolo" and trade joke titles for her while he protests that he raised Mikoto with loving care; she is named *Eternal † Shadow*, and he wails in humiliation and cries tears of blood.[@fm04-naming] In DanMachi 5 his followers' pass parade leaves Bell's party lost in the Dungeon; he apologises to Hestia for it, and his followers join the rescue.[@fm05-apology]

In DanMachi 6 Mikoto bows to the floor and asks to go and help [[bell-cranell|Bell]] in the [[war-game|War Game]]. He had come to the same conclusion: "One year…Such a long time." He tells her to learn from Hestia's followers and "come back stronger than ever", and she converts to [[hestia-familia|Hestia Familia]] for a year.[@fm06-conversion] He watches the War Game from his home.[@fm06-watch]

In DanMachi 8 he refuses the money Mikoto brings for the shrine and tells her to spend it on her party.[@fm08-money] After her outburst at his behaviour with women, it is he who suggests a going-away party for her. There he gives her the dagger [[equipment#tenka-and-chizan|Chizan]], the female of a pair, and keeps the male, Tenka: "And the other one shall be yours on the day you come back to us."[@fm08-gift, fm08-chizan] He carries Tenka when he trains her in DanMachi 12.[@fm12-training]
