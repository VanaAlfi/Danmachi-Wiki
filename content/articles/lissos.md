**Lissos** is an [[races#elf|elf]] squad leader of [[apollo-familia|Apollo Familia]] who fights in its pursuit of [[bell-cranell|Bell Cranell]] and the [[war-game|War Game]].[@fm06-pursuit, fm06-sortie] He wears a scarf over his mouth and commands the attack on the church before confronting Bell Cranell and [[hestia|Hestia]] in the streets.[@fm06-pursuit]

## History {#history}

| Volume | Events |
|---|---|
| DanMachi 6 | His attempt to capture the fugitives is interrupted by [[takemikazuchi-familia|Takemikazuchi Familia]], which clashes with his group to protect its allies.[@fm06-pursuit] |
| DanMachi 6 | During the War Game, he leads fifty adventurers out of the east gate and orders them to spread into ten groups against the hooded attacker.[@fm06-sortie] |
| DanMachi 6 | After [[magic-sword|magic-sword]] bombardment strikes his troops, he orders an assault when one of the weapons breaks.[@fm06-sortie] |
| DanMachi 6 | His dagger exposes the attacker's elven ear, and he angrily condemns her use of the magic swords associated with the destruction of elven forests.[@fm06-defeat] |
| DanMachi 6 | [[lyu-leon|Lyu Leon]] breaks his dagger and defeats him after declaring that helping her friend matters more than racial animosity.[@fm06-defeat] |
| DanMachi 6 | A messenger subsequently reports his group's apparent defeat to [[daphne|Daphne Laulos]], without knowing how many warriors remain able to fight.[@fm06-report] |
