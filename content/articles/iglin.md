**Iglin** is a Level 2 dwarf in the [[balder#third-squad|3rd Squad]] at the [[school-district|School District]].[@fm19-identity] He is about 150 celches tall, dresses like an aristocrat and carries a hammer, and his personality changes when he takes it up.[@fm19-identity, fm19-hammer] He aims to become a master smith, studying combat to understand weapon users while improving his smithing, and cites [[tsubaki|Tsubaki Collbrande]]'s advice about testing weapons in the [[dungeon#deep-floors|deep levels]].[@fm19-ambition]
