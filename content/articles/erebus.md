**Erebus** is a god who leads the [[evils|Evils]] in the [[great-conflict|Great Conflict]]. He first appears under the name Eren, asking the young [[lyu-leon|Lyu]] mocking questions about justice, before revealing himself as the architect of the attack.[@ar01-erebus, ar01-eyes]

## His aim

Erebus calls himself absolute evil.[@ar01-erebus] His real goal is to force heroes to emerge by putting [[orario|Orario]] through a truly lethal trial; he recruited [[zald|Zald]] and [[alfia|Alfia]] of the old [[zeus-and-hera-familias|Zeus and Hera Familias]] for it.[@ar03-erebus] If Orario failed, he was prepared, with them, to topple [[babel|Babel]] and send the world back to the ancient age.[@ar03-fallback] His wish for successors does not excuse the killings.[@ar03-erebus]

## The war

In Astrea Record 2 Erebus's plan uses a god as bait in the Dungeon to lure a god-slaying monster to the surface, while the massacre above hides it.[@ar02-bait, ar02-arcanum] At the end of the war he is taken into custody; [[astrea|Astrea]] confronts him over his aims and has him brought to the surface before he is returned to the heavens.[@ar03-erebus, ar03-end]
