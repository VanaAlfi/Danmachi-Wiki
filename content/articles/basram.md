**Basram** is an animal-person priest of [[apate-and-alecto-familias#apate-familia|Apate Familia]] who creates and controls spirit warriors for the [[evils|Evils]].[@ar03-priest, ar01-experiment, ar02-command] He controls spirit warriors through a staff and participates in the Evils' attacks on [[freya-familia|Freya Familia]].[@ar02-command, ar03-priest]

## History {#history}

| Volume | Events |
|---|---|
| Astrea Record 1 | Basram takes the front line after Freya Familia has killed Apate Familia's captain, vice-captain and officers.[@ar01-remnant] |
| Astrea Record 1 | He and his faction subject former [[minor-familias#osiris-familia|Osiris Familia]] members to drugs, curses and captured-spirit infusion, producing spirit warriors.[@ar01-experiment] |
| Astrea Record 1 | Experiments and Dungeon training reduce forty-two spirits and thirty-four warriors to twelve spirit warriors, whom Basram says have reached Level 5.[@ar01-experiment] |
| Astrea Record 2 | Basram says preparations need two more days; the warriors require adjustment after battle to prevent rejection between their bodies and infused spirits.[@ar02-tuning] |
| Astrea Record 2 | He assembles his warriors underground and commands them with a swing of his staff.[@ar02-command] |
| Astrea Record 3 | At the Amphitheatrum, Basram uses an ally as a shield against the [[gulliver-brothers|Gulliver brothers]] and loses his right middle and ring fingers.[@ar03-priest, ar03-shield] |
| Astrea Record 3 | Alfrik outflanks him and severs his left arm, staff and all; Basram then inserts a spirit dagger into the stump and transforms.[@ar03-last] |
| Astrea Record 3 | The brothers destroy his transformed limbs, and Alfrik's spear pierces his fallen body.[@ar03-last] |
| Astrea Record 3 | The surviving Apate members recover Basram's staff and use it to direct four remaining spirit warriors.[@ar03-after] |
