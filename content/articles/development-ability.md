A **Development Ability** is a special ability that an adventurer can add to their [[status|Status]] on reaching a new [[level|Level]].[@fm02-named, fm04-choice] Many English volumes print the term as **Advanced Ability**.[@fm02-named, fm04-choice]

## Name

The English editions use two forms. DanMachi 2 and 4–10 and Sword Oratoria 1–3 print *Advanced Ability*; DanMachi 12, 15 and 18 and Minor Myths and Legends 1 print *development ability*.[@fm02-named, so01-aiz, fm12-term, fm18-term] This wiki uses *Development Ability* as its heading and treats both as the same term.[@fm12-term]

{{nocite}} Some individual abilities are also printed under different names in different volumes; see [[#names-of-abilities|Names of abilities]].

## How they are gained

A Development Ability is chosen at a Level Up, from the options that the adventurer's accumulated excelia has made available.[@fm02-named, fm04-choice] Only one can be chosen at each Level Up, so each choice closes off the others offered that time.[@fm04-choice]

Like basic abilities, they carry a rank letter that can rise. [[bell-cranell|Bell Cranell]]'s Luck starts at I when he chooses it at Level 2, and has reached F by DanMachi 20.[@fm04-luck, fm20-luck]

Some are tied to particular Levels or experience. Hunter is available only at Level 2 and requires extensive experience fighting the same kinds of monster.[@fm04-hunter]

## Names of abilities {#names-of-abilities}

{{nocite}} The English editions sometimes print what look like the same abilities under different names. [[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz]]'s Status cards show the pattern:

| Volume | Aiz's Development Abilities as printed |
|---|---|
| Sword Oratoria 1 (Level 5) | Hunter G, Immunity G, Knight I[@so01-aiz] |
| Sword Oratoria 3 (after reaching Level 6) | Hunter G, Immunity G, Knight H, Regen I[@so03-aiz] |
| Sword Oratoria 6 | Hunter G, Abnormal Resistance G, Swordsman H, Spirit Healing I[@so06-cards] |
| Sword Oratoria 11 (Level 6) | Hunter G, Immunity G, Knight I, Spirit Healing I[@so11-aiz] |

> [!INFERENCE] Likely the same abilities
> The positions and ranks line up closely, so Immunity and Abnormal Resistance, Knight and Swordsman, and Regen and Spirit Healing are probably different English renderings of the same three abilities. Lefiya's Mage H in Sword Oratoria 6 likewise matches her Conjure H in Sword Oratoria 12, and Lilly's choice between Resistance and Compounding in DanMachi 15 may correspond to Immunity and Synthesize. No passage says so, so each name is listed separately above.[@so03-aiz, so06-cards, so12-lefiya, fm15-lilly]

> [!UNRESOLVED] Knight's rank
> Sword Oratoria 11 prints Aiz's Knight at rank I, lower than the H printed in Sword Oratoria 3. The text does not explain the difference.[@so03-aiz, so11-aiz]
