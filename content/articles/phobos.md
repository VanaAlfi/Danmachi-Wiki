**Phobos** is the goddess of Phobos Familia, which includes the Crozzo family under [[ares|Ares]]'s rule.[@fm15-patron] She is a goddess who looks after the Crozzo family and serves under Ares.[@fm15-patron] She had fought a defensive war against Rakia's army, Ares Familia, and lost; as the loser of a [[war-game|War Game]] she had to become a vassal-god of Ares, one of the gods who confer Falna for him.[@fm15-phobos-vassal] She looks like a beautiful young woman with long black hair and narrow, bewitching eyes, though her words and ways are childish (the English prints "vulgar speech and laughter").[@fm15-phobos-looks] The family bears her [[falna|Falna]] as Phobos Familia, while [[welf-crozzo|Welf Crozzo]] views her as an annoying older companion rather than an object of worship.[@fm15-patron, fm15-companion]
