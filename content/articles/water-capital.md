The **Water Capital** is a region of the [[dungeon|Dungeon]] spanning the twenty-fifth to twenty-seventh floors. The Great Falls connect all three floors, and on each floor a massive cavern holds the falls and a plunge pool as large as a lake.[@fm13-region] It is one of the great mysteries of the part of the Dungeon that adventurers call the New World.[@fm12-capital] The twenty-fifth floor begins the lower levels: adventurers call this threshold the Second Line, which "only a select group" of upper-class adventurers has ever crossed, and the Guild allows Level 3 adventurers to venture there.[@fm12-second-line, fm07-lower-levels] DanMachi 19 also calls the region the *Water Metropolis*.[@fm19-metropolis]

## Layout

- **Three floors, one waterfall.** The three great caverns, linked across three floors by a single waterfall, are together called the Water Capital of the Dungeon.[@fm12-capital] The floors are multistory: to go down, adventurers descend the height of the Great Falls through connecting passages at the depth of the plunge pools.[@fm12-layout]
- **The Great Falls.** By Bell's eye the falls measure about four hundred meders wide and easily twice as tall (the English prints "easily as many tall"), and their water looks emerald blue; all the streams of the twenty-fifth floor run to their head in the centre of the floor.[@fm12-great-falls]
- **Source of the falls.** The Great Falls pour out near the twenty-fifth floor's ceiling, where the roots of an enormous tree from the [[colossal-tree-labyrinth|Colossal Tree Labyrinth]] above spread outward.[@fm12-tree]
- **Arrival.** From the twenty-fourth floor, the connecting passage ends at a sheer cliff at the far southern tip of the twenty-fifth floor, with the cavern and the Great Falls in the centre.[@fm12-entrance]
- **The bottom.** The end point of the falls is on the twenty-seventh floor, the lowest level of the Water Capital.[@fm13-region, fm13-depths] Below it, the twenty-eighth floor is the [[floor-28|Under Garden]], the second [[safe-point|safe point]] after the Under Resort of [[floor-18|Floor 18]], used as a base for taking on the Tangled Gorge, which begins on the [[floors-29-31-and-32#floor-29|twenty-ninth floor]].[@fm20-garden, fm20-gorge]
- **One floor to the Dungeon.** [[rudra-familia#jura-harma|Jura Harma]] of Rudra Familia explains that the Dungeon treats the whole region as a single floor: "The whole area connected by the Great Falls is like one floor! Damage in one place is shared everywhere!"[@fm13-one-floor]
- **Monsters.** [[monsters#harpy|Harpies]] and other bird-type monsters fly around the falls; [[monsters#merman|mermen]], blue-scaled fish-men with nature weapons that Bell likens to "an underwater version of [[monsters#lizardman|lizardmen]]", appear from the twenty-sixth floor and are among the Water Capital's stronger enemies, and the [[monsters#kelpie|kelpie]] ranks among the region's strongest.[@fm12-entrance, fm13-lizardmen, fm14-kelpie] [[monsters#iguacu|Iguaçu]], swallow monsters that adventurers call "invisible monsters", live in the cliff behind the falls.[@fm12-iguacu] The lamia, afanc and dodora first appear on the twenty-seventh floor, the only home of the rare [[monsters#voltemeria|voltemeria]], stone-bodied fish that swim through the air.[@fm14-floor-27] The floor boss [[amphisbaena|Amphisbaena]] appears on the twenty-seventh floor.[@fm13-boss]
- **Gear.** Adventurers wear [[equipment#undine-cloth|Undine cloth]], a waterproof "spirit protective cloth" linked to water spirits; its protection works fully underwater, reducing water resistance and pressure. It is the counterpart of the [[equipment#salamander-wool|salamander wool]] worn against the fire of the thirteenth floor.[@fm12-undine]
- **Few reach it.** Many fewer parties are able to get through the Water Capital and reach the twenty-eighth floor than reach the floors above.[@fm20-few]
- **Xenos village.** The [[xenos|Xenos]] keep a hidden village on the twenty-seventh floor, where the mermaid [[mari|Mari]] is their lookout.[@ss01-village]
