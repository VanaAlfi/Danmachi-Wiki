A **trigger spell** is an incantation used to activate [[magic|Magic]].[@fm02-formula, so02-wind] [[hestia|Hestia]] reports hearing that an incantation appears in a [[status|Status]] magic slot when [[magic|Magic]] manifests, allowing its user to obtain the trigger.[@fm02-formula] The explanation during the [[war-game|War Game]] connects incantation length with [[magic|Magic]]'s power and range.[@fm06-length]

## Preparation and use {#preparation-and-use}

| Volume | Events |
|---|---|
| DanMachi 6 | The magic users ambushing [[bell-cranell|Bell Cranell]] have finished chanting and remain ready to attack.[@fm06-waiting] |
| DanMachi 6 | In the [[war-game|War Game]], [[bell-cranell|Bell Cranell]]'s [[magic#firebolt|Firebolt]] astonishes the watching gods because it is cast with no trigger spell; DanMachi 9 calls it the first Magic of its kind that needs none.[@fm06-no-trigger, fm09-firebolt] |
| DanMachi 7 | [[mikoto|Yamato Mikoto]] pours all her Mind into [[magic|Magic]] without completing her incantation and lets the power run uncontrolled, causing an explosion.[@fm07-incomplete] |
| DanMachi 8 | [[lilliluka-erde|Lilliluka Erde]]'s [[magic#cinder-ella|Cinder Ella]] [[status|Status]] entry lists a trigger formula and a separate formula for ending its effect.[@fm08-formulas] |
| Sword Oratoria 2 | [[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz Wallenstein]] uses a super-short incantation, after which wind surrounds her body and armor.[@so02-wind] |
| Sword Oratoria 3 | [[filvis|Filvis Challia]] is described as exceptionally fast among magic swordsmen using incantations.[@so03-filvis] |


[@fm02-formula]: FM02 | Chapter 3: Magic, Magic That Summons a Lap | Hestia's reported explanation of obtaining an incantation from Status. The Japanese edition (file c3GP, paragraph 523) says the incantation text appears in the Status's magic slot; the English prints that the Magic shows up in the status.
[@fm06-length]: FM06 | Chapter 5: Our War Game | The connection between incantation length, power and range.
[@fm06-waiting]: FM06 | Chapter 3: Outbreak | Prepared magic users wait after chanting. The Japanese edition (file c295, paragraph 45) says they had finished chanting and were on standby.
[@fm07-incomplete]: FM07 | Chapter 6: Yearning of a Hero | Mikoto's unfinished incantation and uncontrolled magical explosion.
[@fm08-formulas]: FM08 | Chapter 2: The Prum’s Proposal | Lilly's Cinder Ella incantation and a separate formula for ending it.
[@so02-wind]: SO02 | Chapter 6: Parched Scream | Aiz's super-short incantation activates wind around her body. The Japanese edition (file c42M, paragraph 371) calls it a super-short chant; the English prints short trigger spell.
[@so03-filvis]: SO03 | Chapter 4: White-Haired Devil | Filvis's exceptionally fast magic-swordsman style.
[@fm08-trigger-ja]: FM08 | Chapter 2: The Prum’s Proposal | The Japanese edition's Status sheet writes chant formula, in kanji, where the English prints Trigger Spell (file part0018, paragraph 114); in prose the Japanese says chant or chant text (FM02, file c3GP, paragraph 523).
[@fm06-no-trigger]: FM06 | Chapter 5: Our War Game | "No trigger spell?!"; "Most of the gods and goddesses were filled with a mix of shock and admiration"; "trigger-less spell"
[@fm09-firebolt]: FM09 | Chapter 4: Mission | "his magic attack, Firebolt—the first of its kind that didn’t require a trigger spell"
