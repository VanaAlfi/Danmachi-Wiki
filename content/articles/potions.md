**Potions** are the healing items that adventurers carry into the [[dungeon|Dungeon]]. In a shop in DanMachi 2, potions are blue liquid in round-bottomed flasks, antidotes green fluid in cylindrical tubes, and elixirs come in fancy bottles; all are made by Familias that specialise in sales.[@fm02-shop] Ordinary potions restore physical strength, while only high potions and elixirs can stop bleeding and save someone with deep wounds or broken bones.[@fm05-severe] Magic potions restore Mind, and [[miach-familia|Miach Familia]]'s dual potions restore both.[@fm02-mind, fm04-double]

## Makers and limits {#makers}

Potions come from Familias that specialise in sales, among them [[miach-familia|Miach Familia]], whose cheapest potions cost 500 valis, and [[dian-cecht-familia|Dian Cecht Familia]], whose stock includes elixirs.[@fm02-shop, fm02-cheapest, so01-elixirs] The Development Ability [[development-ability#compounding|Compounding]], often acquired by herbalists, is used chiefly to create medicines and can make items such as recovery potions so effective that they "almost seemed like magic".[@fm15-compounding]

Healing items have limits. High potions can mend a broken bone, but they can force it to heal at the wrong angle if it is not set first, and other healing items and magic can do the same.[@fm09-bones] Even an elixir cannot bring back Wiene's lost claws and wing.[@fm11-limbs] On the expedition of DanMachi 12, potions and antidotes are useless against an ivy that has taken root in the wounded; they even "do the opposite of what we want".[@fm12-ivy]

## Potion {#potion}

The basic potion ({{ja|回復薬}}, written *recovery medicine*)[@potion.fm05-ja] restores physical strength.[@fm05-severe] In DanMachi 1 Bell finds that "one healing potion costs 500 vals a pop", and Miach Familia's cheapest potions cost the same.[@fm01-price, fm02-cheapest] Against critical injuries "A normal potion would have almost no effect".[@fm05-critical] The text also prints *healing potion*, *health potion* and *recovery potion*.[@fm01-price, ar02-health, fm15-compounding]

## High potion {#high-potion}

In DanMachi 2 [[miach-familia|Nahza]] offers Bell a high potion ({{ja|高等回復薬}}, written *high-grade recovery medicine*)[@high-potion.fm05-ja] "worth tens of thousands of vals"; both belong to poor Familias.[@fm02-highprice] In Minor Myths and Legends 1 Nahza and [[miach|Miach]] press a pile of items, high potions among them, on Bell after he ranks up, Nahza calling it an investment: advertising for their shop.[@ss01-high-potion-gift] [[dian-cecht-familia|Dian Cecht Familia]] sells them too: in Sword Oratoria 1 [[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz Wallenstein]] buys some for her own use from [[amid|Amid Teasanare]], to replace those used on the expedition.[@so01-high-potion-amid] With elixirs, high potions are the only potions that can clot blood and save someone with deep gashes, broken bones and other severe injuries.[@fm05-severe] In DanMachi 5 Hestia revives Bell by pouring one of Miach's high potions over his face.[@fm05-hestia] Miach Familia gives Hestia Familia substantial discounts on its high potions and dual potions.[@fm06-discount] Running out is dangerous: after their fall in DanMachi 5 the party counts "Four potions and two antidotes; no high potions made it", and after the Goliath fight there are "no high potions left" for Bell, against whose injuries "A normal potion would have almost no effect".[@fm05-nohigh, fm05-goliath] Plain [[#potion|potions]] mainly restore stamina, so Bell's party cannot treat [[welf-crozzo|Welf Crozzo]]'s crushed leg.[@fm05-high-potion-severe] They also have limits: a high potion can mend a broken bone "but…apparently they can force it to heal at the wrong angle if it isn’t set properly", as can other healing items and magic.[@fm09-bone] In Sword Oratoria 2 Tiona asks Amid for "high potions, please! The good stuff, and lots of them!"; in Sword Oratoria 4 an exhausted dwarf drinks one "in place of liquor"; and in the Great Familia War [[mia-grand|Mia]] closes her own wound with half a high potion and pours the rest over Bell and Lyu.[@so02-amid, so04-liquor, fm18-mia] In DanMachi 9 Bell pours a Miach Familia high potion over [[wiene|Wiene]]'s torn shoulders and splints her left leg, which may be broken, with a strip of his [[equipment#salamander-wool|salamander wool]] and his knife's sheath.[@fm09-dual-wiene]

In DanMachi 6 Bell's high potions only suffice to close the chest wound [[hyacinthus|Hyacinthus]] gave him, and Lilly hands him one she had bought from Nahza.[@fm06-high-potion-war] In DanMachi 7 [[mikoto|Yamato Mikoto]] recovers Bell's gear from a vault and fills his leg holsters with as many high potions as she can.[@fm07-high-potion-vault] In DanMachi 10 [[lyu-leon|Lyu Leon]] gives Bell a pouch of high potions and other items.[@fm10-high-potion-lyu] In DanMachi 12, badly hurt, Bell uses vial after vial from his reinforced leg holsters.[@fm12-high-potion-holster] In Sword Oratoria 3 [[lulune-louie|Lulune Louie]] and the others pour high potions, and Lulune's treasured elixir, over the fallen [[asfi|Asfi Al Andromeda]].[@so03-high-potion-asfi] In Sword Oratoria 7 [[gareth|Gareth Landrock]], running through the great labyrinth, sprinkles one of his few high potions over his battered fists.[@so07-high-potion-gareth] In Sword Oratoria 8 [[bete-loga|Bete Loga]], hiding in a back street, drinks a high potion that Loki had given him.[@so08-high-potion-bete]

## Elixir {#elixir}

Elixirs ({{ja|万能薬}}, written *all-purpose medicine*)[@elixir.fm18-ja] are sold in fancy bottles.[@fm02-shop] The shop in DanMachi 2 buys them, like its potions, from Familias that specialize in commerce.[@fm02-elixir-bottles] In Sword Oratoria 1 an elixir is "The most expensive healing potion in Dian Cecht Familia’s inventory", at no less than 500,000 valis a bottle.[@so01-elixirs] In that volume [[lefiya|Lefiya]] treats [[hyrute-sisters|Tione]] with elixirs after an acid attack, and it takes many bottles to restore her.[@so01-elixir-price-1, so01-elixir-price-2] Sword Oratoria 11 says it is called the most instant product of the Development Ability [[development-ability#enigma|Enigma]].[@so11-ultimate] They can close deep wounds, stop bleeding and even restore broken bones.[@fm05-elixir-injuries] It still cannot restore what has been lost.[@fm11-limbs] In Sword Oratoria 3 Lulune pours her own treasured elixir over the fallen Asfi.[@so03-tiger]

In DanMachi 7 [[ottar|Ottar]] pours one over [[mikoto|Mikoto]], burned by her own Ignis Fatuus, and her body begins to heal at once.[@fm07-elixir-ottar] In DanMachi 13 Jura of [[rudra-familia|Rudra Familia]] pours an elixir over his self-inflicted wounds, healing them.[@fm13-elixir-jura] In DanMachi 17 [[hegni|Hegni Ragnar]] treats Bell with an elixir and sits him up.[@fm17-elixir-hegni]

[[amid|Amid Teasanare]] gives Loki Familia high potions and elixirs for its expedition in Sword Oratoria 4, and when Aiz and her friends visit her clinic afterwards they credit her potions and elixirs with pulling them through.[@so04-elixir-amid, so06-elixir-thanks] In Sword Oratoria 8 she distils an anti-curse medicine from her own curse-exposed blood, but the supply is too small to save every afflicted Amazon.[@so08-elixir-curse-1, so08-elixir-curse-2] In Sword Oratoria 11 Ottar presses a heap of elixirs on Aiz during her training.[@so11-elixir-aiz-1] In Sword Oratoria 12 elixirs fail to heal [[bete-loga|Bete Loga]] after [[filvis|Filvis Challia]]'s black lightning strikes him.[@so12-elixir-knossos-2] In Familia Chronicle 2 [[hedin|Hedin Selrand]] tosses Ottar an elixir after a battle, and in Familia Chronicle 3 [[astrea|Astrea]] gives one to the exhausted spirit mare Yufie, who still cannot call up the wind for a while.[@fc02-elixir-hedin, fc03-elixir-yufie] In Astrea Record 3 [[finn-deimne|Finn Deimne]] shares a great many elixirs with [[astrea-familia|Astrea Familia]], and Astrea later brings one for each member.[@ar03-elixir-astrea-1, ar03-elixir-astrea-2]

{{nocite}} Special elixirs appear in the story:
- **For the Xenos:** in Sword Oratoria 12 the [[xenos|Xenos]] are running out of the elixirs made by [[fels|Fels]]. In DanMachi 19 Fels, who now uses [[knossos|Knossos]] as a base, plans a plant there to produce medicinal herbs and a spring of miracle medicine.[@so12-fels, fm19-spring]
- **Nahza's elixir:** in DanMachi 18 Nahza resolves to try "the elixirs that were always out of reach", and her newly developed elixir is carried into the Familia War.[@fm18-nahza, fm18-newelixir] [[daphne|Daphne Laulos]] helps refine potions and elixirs, and in the Familia War Bell is still gravely hurt after using up Nahza's elixir.[@fm18-elixir-nahza-2, fm18-elixir-nahza-4]

The School District's electives include the study of secret medicines, a different word from this elixir's.[@fm19-electives]

## Magic potion {#magic-potion}

A magic potion ({{ja|精神力回復薬}}, written *Mind-recovery medicine*)[@magic-potion.fm05-ja] is "Medicine that healed the mind".[@fm02-mind] In DanMachi 2 [[miach-familia|Nahza]] sells Bell one for 8,700 valis, throwing in two potions to make an even 9,000; in DanMachi 4 he counts that magic potion as the deciding factor in a desperate battle.[@fm02-highprice, fm04-mind] Nahza had made it herself.[@fm04-magic-potion-nahza] Magic potions keep casters from Mind Down: Astrea hands one to Lyu on the verge of it, and in the Familia War Haruhime works through her whole stock.[@fc03-mind, fm18-mindown] In DanMachi 19 [[nina-tulle|Nina Tulle]] would have fallen into Mind Down if Rapi, Bell under his School District alias, had not handed her magic potions at the perfect moment.[@fm19-magic-potion-nina] In Sword Oratoria 4 Finn calls "Raul, ready a magic potion!" and Aiz drains it mid-run before returning to the front; in Sword Oratoria 14 [[riveria|Riveria]], kept going by the magic potions Aina supplies, passes out on reaching the sunlight, having "blown through Mind Down and all the way to absolute empty".[@so04-raul, so14-riveria] After the Xenos affair Lido offers Haruhime "a really old magic potion that belonged to an adventurer", and in Astrea Record 2 Kaguya, with no waterskin, tosses the parched Lyra a magic potion instead.[@fm11-lido, ar02-lyra] In Sword Oratoria 13 [[cole|Cole]] presses the last half of a magic potion on [[natalinoe-cladfield|Nano]].[@so13-magic-potion-students]

Stronger kinds are printed in three forms: *high-mind potion* (DanMachi 7), *high-magic potion*, bearing the Dian Cecht Familia seal (Sword Oratoria 4), and *high magic potion* (DanMachi 18).[@fm07-highmind, so04-highmagic, fm18-highmagic] In Sword Oratoria 4 [[amid|Amid]] gives Lefiya her Familia's high-magic potions as a parting gift for the expedition.[@so04-magic-potion-dian-cecht-c1, so04-magic-potion-dian-cecht-c2] In DanMachi 7 Bell takes the high potion and the high-mind potion Mikoto gave him before storming the palace.[@fm07-magic-potion-high-kind] In DanMachi 18 [[mia-grand|Mia]] pours the high potion and the high magic potion over Bell's and Lyu's heads.[@fm18-magic-potion-war-stock]

## Dual potion {#dual-potion}

The dual potion ({{ja|二属性回復薬}}, written *two-attribute recovery medicine*)[@dual-potion.fm05-ja] restores physical strength and Mind together. In DanMachi 4 [[miach|Miach]] presents it to [[dian-cecht|Dian Cecht]] as his Familia's latest product, "the first of its kind", which Dian Cecht Familia could sell at a high price; Miach and Nahza race to produce it after collecting eggs in the [[geography#surroundings-of-orario-deep-forest-seoro|Deep Forest Seoro]].[@fm04-double] It is Nahza's creation and Miach Familia's signature product.[@fm05-nahza, fm08-signature] Dual potions normally cost tens of thousands of valis.[@fm06-discount] In the War Game of DanMachi 6 Bell fights on after Argonaut "despite drinking one of Nahza’s dual potions"; in DanMachi 9 Mikoto, her Mind "little more than fumes", drinks a whole one; in DanMachi 11 he gulps down "several dual potions that Hestia gave him".[@fm06-wargame, fm09-dual-mikoto, fm11-dual]

**Name:** DanMachi 4, 5 and 8 print *double potion*; DanMachi 6 and later volumes print *dual potion*, sometimes capitalised *Dual Potion*.[@fm04-double, fm05-nahza, fm08-signature, fm06-discount, fm09-dual, fm18-newelixir]

**High dual potion:** in DanMachi 12 Nahza's new *High Dual Potion* completely restores the physical and mental strength of whoever drinks it.[@fm12-highdual] In DanMachi 13 a party carries two High Dual Potions as a treasured reserve.[@fm13-tiger]

## Antidote {#antidote}

Antidotes ({{ja|解毒薬}})[@antidote.fm09-ja] are green fluid in cylindrical tubes, and Miach Familia's carry its [[emblem|emblem]].[@fm02-shop, fm09-emblem] On floors whose monsters inflict status effects a large supply of antidotes helps, but the Development Ability Immunity is held to be the key.[@fm09-immunity] Some poisons need more: the [[monsters#poison-vermis|poison vermis]]'s calls for a "rare, expensive antidote", which Bete fetches from the surface for Loki Familia in Sword Oratoria 5.[@so05-antidote] The [[amphisbaena|Amphisbaena]]'s blue napalm is another matter: Amid's anti-napalm heal puts out the flames and heals the burns, and Asfi secretly developed a magic item that also puts them out, though it does not heal.[@fm14-asfi] Against another poison in DanMachi 14 "The only treatment was a high-quality antidote or detoxifying magic".[@fm14-quality]

Against curses there are remedies of another kind: on the day of the attack in Sword Oratoria 8, [[amid|Amid]] makes a curse-breaking magic item, an anti-curse secret remedy, from her own curse-exposed blood; it is not an elixir.[@so08-anticurse]
