The **Far East** is a distant region across the ocean from the Continent. Travellers from it come to [[orario|Orario]] by sea and pass through [[njordr#port-meren|Port Meren]].[@so06-meren, fm07-shrine] It is the homeland of [[takemikazuchi-familia|Takemikazuchi Familia]], of [[haruhime|Haruhime]] and of [[gojouno-kaguya|Gojouno Kaguya]], and most [[races#renart|renarts]] live there.[@fm07-shrine, fm14-kaguya, fm07-renart] No map of the region and no name for any of its countries were located in the checked text; it appears through its people, customs, stories and fighting styles.

## Places

{{nocite}} No name for a Far Eastern country or town was located in the checked text. The places the novels describe are these:

- **The shrine and the manor.** [[takemikazuchi|Takemikazuchi]] and other gods ran a poor shrine on a mountain that took in orphans, among them [[mikoto|Yamato Mikoto]], [[ouka|Kashima Ouka]] and [[chigusa|Hitachi Chigusa]]; it was rather isolated. Haruhime's family manor stood at the foot of the mountain, and she never left it until the shrine's children brought her out to play.[@fm07-shrine, fm08-isolated] The goddess Tsukuyomi is also at the shrine.[@fm07-tsukuyomi]
- **Childhood friends.** Mikoto and Chigusa both come from the Far East and are childhood friends.[@fm07-chigusa]
- **Island nations.** [[tsubaki|Tsubaki]], whose mother was a human from the Far East, wears island-country clothes that, with her tachi, recall a Far Eastern swordswoman.[@fm08-island, fm08-tsubaki]
- **The sea route.** Travellers from the Continent can reach Orario by its northern and eastern roads, but those from the Far East or the island and ocean nations must first pass through Meren. Mikoto's group "would travel across the ocean" with Takemikazuchi to Orario.[@so06-meren, fm07-shrine]

## People

- **Hard lives.** War, plagues and monsters often leave children in the Far East orphaned, as Mikoto was.[@fm08-orphans] Takemikazuchi's shrine was too poor to feed the orphans it took in, so its oldest and strongest went to Orario to earn money in the Dungeon and send it home.[@fm07-shrine]
- **Nobility.** Haruhime is of noble birth and was taught the arts of noble court ladies, such as dance and flower arrangement. Kaguya is rumoured to have grown up in a noble family there, and [[alize-lovell|Alize]] has heard that she "went through a lot" in the Far East.[@fm09-bath, fm15-court, fm14-kaguya]
- **Renarts.** The fox-eared renarts are "an extremely rare race that mostly lives in the Far East". Their magic is unusual, and Bell has heard that they are called sorcerers there.[@fm07-renart, fm07-sorcerers]
- **Names.** At DanMachi 4's Denatus, [[loki|Loki]] reads Mikoto's name and remarks that she is from "one of those Far East places, so her name’s backward". This wiki writes Far Eastern names in that order, family name first.[@fm04-backward]
- **Character.** [[cassandra|Cassandra]] respects Ouka, Chigusa and Mikoto for their strong sense of duty, "characteristic of people from the Far East", and DanMachi 15 calls the girls from the Far East humble, hardworking and proactive.[@fm13-duty, fm15-girls] Astrea Record 1 calls Kaguya a *yamato nadeshiko*, the Far Eastern ideal of modest femininity.[@ar01-nadeshiko]

Stories also travel the other way: Haruhime says that many stories of Orario have reached the Far East, and that the city always appealed to her.[@fm07-stories]

## Fighting styles

Takemikazuchi, a god of martial arts, trained the shrine's children in many styles of combat before they left, ninjutsu among them for Mikoto; her judo-style throws are a Far Eastern style that works well on human opponents.[@fm07-shrine, fm07-ninjutsu, fm07-judo] Kaguya's quick-draw, *iai*, is "A technique from the Far East", and in DanMachi 14 [[lyu-leon|Lyu]] uses a quick-draw that "her departed battle companion from the Far East" had prided herself on.[@ar02-iai, fm14-quickdraw] In Astrea Record 3 [[noir-dyne-and-bahra|Noir]] of Loki Familia fights with a single-edged blade modelled on the katana of the East.[@ar03-noir]

## The Far East in Orario

Part of the [[pleasure-quarter|Pleasure Quarter]] is built in Far Eastern style, with roofs shaped like "the stark triangles of the Far East". Bell finds a street of brothels with kimono-clad women, "meant to be the Far East", and a brothel inside with *fusuma* and *tatami*. Haruhime, a renart from the Far East, works there.[@fm07-quarter, fm07-kimono, fm07-brothel] The room Mikoto and Haruhime share in [[hearthstone-manor|Hearthstone Manor]] is full of things from the Far East, kimonos among them, and the manor has a Far Eastern–style bath, installed at Mikoto's request.[@fm08-room, fm09-bath]
