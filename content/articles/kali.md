**Kali** is the goddess of [[kali-familia|Kali Familia]], which rules [[telskyura|Telskyura]], the land of Amazon warriors whose rites are fights to the death; its Amazons revere her as "their one and only god".[@so06-blessing] A goddess of war who says she descended "seeking war and bloodshed", she favoured [[hyrute-sisters|Tiona and Tione Hyrute]] and let them leave, then came to Port Meren in Sword Oratoria 6 to make them fight their old mentors.[@so06-lefiya, so06-release, so06-sisters] Her Familia, its rites and the Meren campaign are covered on the [[kali-familia|Kali Familia]] and [[telskyura|Telskyura]] pages.

## Appearance and character

Kali has copper skin like her followers', blood-red hair and the short stature of a child. She wears a necklace of fake bones and a fanged mask, and the eyes glinting behind it "projected nothing if not pure authority".[@so06-arrival] Her eyes are printed bloodred and crimson, and she has pointed canines.[@so06-war, so06-defeat, so06-epilogue] Her looks and voice are a young girl's, but "the bite behind her words and the way she stared daggers made it plain that she was no child"; she is the only one in the room unaffected by [[ishtar|Ishtar]]'s charm.[@so06-ishtar] She can pass for a mortal child: in Sword Oratoria 6 she poses as a human girl of Meren (see [[#sword-oratoria-6-meren|below]]), and in Minor Myths and Legends 2 she walks Orario as a dark-skinned girl in a pale blue dress with a black wig, so that [[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz]] knows her only when she puts the mask on.[@so06-chandie, ss02-orario]

She is a goddess of war, blood and mayhem, and what gratifies her most, the reason she descended, is "nothing but war itself".[@so06-sisters] "There’s nothing that excites me like the spray of blood," she tells Ishtar; real war with real lives at stake is "the true thrill of the lower world…and exactly what I seek". Her last words to Ishtar are that where the fighting leads is what she wants to see.[@so06-war] Her aim, she tells [[lefiya|Lefiya]], is to "Create a future of war": "The perfect warrior born from the very limits of ceaseless bloodshed."[@so06-lefiya] Lefiya later remembers her as "the hedonistic Goddess of War who sought nothing but pleasure and the excitement of the unknown in the mortal realm".[@so07-lefiya]

She loves her followers, but not above war: "Don’t get me wrong—I love my children. But even they can’t come between me and my life’s one true pleasure." She denies being "an affectionate goddess": "All of us came down to this world seeking the excitement of our own. We’re nothing but hedonists."[@so06-lefiya] She has a child's manner as well. She teases the twins about their figures and trades insults with Tiona in the middle of a rite: when Tiona calls her *stingy*, she answers that stingy suits her fine, sticking out her tongue.[@so06-meren, so06-cave, so06-rite]

## Kali and Telskyura

Telskyura's ways are older than her rule. When Lefiya blames her for the country's deaths, Kali answers, "Telskyura was like that even before I came along. We gods highly condemn distortin’ a country’s history and culture for our selfish whims"; "The only thing I did when I got there was bequeath ’em my blessing."[@so06-lefiya] That blessing made the Amazons stronger and their battles more violent, and they revere her as "this bringer of strength".[@so06-blessing] As she puts it, she tried "to make Telskyura the holy land of Amazons they wanted it to be". Her rule for those who leave is "Reject none who come, chase none who leave": anyone may go, though she will take something in return for releasing them.[@so06-lefiya] "The only truth my children know is that of fighting and bloodshed," she tells Ishtar.[@so06-war]

She also teaches. [[kali-familia#argana-kalif|Argana]] learned Koine from her, to understand what the prey outside are saying: "Kali taught it to me. She teaches us many things. The pleasure of growing stronger…"[@so06-koine] When [[kali-familia#bache-kalif|Bache]] could not read a scrap of a story that the child Tiona had brought her, she went to Kali red-faced to ask what it said; Kali "guffawed quite heartily" but translated it for her.[@so06-books]

## Tiona and Tione

Of all her Amazons, the Hyrute twins were the only ones who could talk back to Kali. Tione cursed her, and Kali "couldn’t get enough of the girl’s abuse"; Tiona made her laugh and was often invited to her chambers.[@so06-release] When Kali asked Tiona after a rite what she wanted, Tiona asked to know the end of a story. Kali had the complete book sent for and helped her learn to read Koine, and from then on Tiona asked for a new book after each victory, which "drew favor from Kali".[@so06-books] To Tione, Tiona "clearly had Kali’s favor".[@so06-release]

Just before their seventh birthday, when the twins were about to be set against each other, Tiona told Kali in front of the day's victors, "Kali, I don’t wanna fight Tione." "We wanna leave." A few days later Kali let them go. She released them from their contract but left their Statuses untouched, so that they could take a new god's blessing without losing what they had gained, and called it a "parting gift for her two adorable daughters".[@so06-release] She tells Lefiya why she let them go: "Because they were the first ones. No one had ever requested to leave Telskyura."[@so06-lefiya]

In Sword Oratoria 6 she still calls them "my kids. Well, former kids, at least"; she let them go out of a little mercy, and regrets it a little. She wants to see which pair grew stronger, "Two sisters who left…two sisters who stayed", "in a shower of blood".[@so06-sisters] With Lefiya her captive, she asks how the twins live now ("A mother wants to know!") and roars with laughter to hear that Tione is in love: "So that little runaway of mine has fallen in love…Huh! She really has changed."[@so06-lefiya] Watching Tiona get back up against Bache, she howls with glee: "I knew I liked you better than Tione!"[@so06-cave] When Tiona wins, Kali applauds ("Letting you go truly was my one big mistake, Tiona. I was too soft.") and tells her to kill Bache to complete the rite. Tiona refuses: "I’m not gonna kill anyone else…not anymore, Kali!" Kali murmurs, "…You really have changed", then has her warriors surround Tiona to take her "back to Telskyura".[@so06-rite]

## Sword Oratoria 6: Meren

Ishtar's letters, envoys and gifts of Superior-grade armour won Kali over, and she agreed for the chance to fight [[freya-familia|Freya Familia]]: "It’s a great opportunity! To do battle with the famed Freya Familia".[@so06-ishtar] Landing in Meren, she tells Tione that she has come for "A little sightseeing" and "A little stimulation", and her first meeting with [[loki|Loki]] ends in a trade of insults ("runt number two", Loki calls her).[@so06-meren] When she meets Ishtar, she observes that "The jealousy of a goddess truly is a terrifying thing", and she doubts that Ishtar's Familia can hold the front, since the [[ottar|Warlord]] alone "would be enough to take down my Argana and Bache".[@so06-ishtar] She also asks Ishtar to keep the rest of [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]] at bay, since she wants "a fight between sisters only".[@so06-sisters]

To take a hostage, Kali disguises herself as **Chandie**, a little human girl of Meren with shoulder-length black hair, and leads Lefiya's group into a back alley with a story of a "scary scream". When her Amazons strike, she takes off her wig and puts on her fanged mask: "—There are those among the gods capable of suppressing their divine will." Like "Zeus and Odin and the other great kings of the gods", she says, such gods "disguise themselves as children, blending in among the populace unnoticed". Bache stands beside her as Lefiya blacks out.[@so06-chandie]

She stages the two rites herself. She has Argana and Tione fight out at sea, "where no one could interfere", and stays in the sea cave to watch Bache and Tiona from the highest rock: "Let the feast begin."[@so06-feast, so06-cave, so06-rite] When Loki Familia storms the cave she loses her calm ("That damn Ishtar…Were they defeated already?!"), and, mocked by Loki, snarls "Don’t patronize me!"[@so06-defeat]

Loki Familia heals her wounded Amazons and sends them home but takes Kali back for questioning, tied to a chair. She mourns that "Everyone except Bache has done the dishonor of losing to a man" and that "This may very well be the end for Telskyura…" She mocks Loki's demand that the killing end ("Love will save the world and all that. Love and peace, love and peace. All hail Lady Aphrodite."), will not say why she came, knows "Nothing, actually" about the violas but notes that Ishtar seemed to know a great deal about them, and warns that Ishtar "is terrifying" and has "Another one of her hidden aces". Ishtar, for her part, calls her an "incompetent fool".[@so06-epilogue]
