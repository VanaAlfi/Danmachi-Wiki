**Orario**, the Labyrinth City, is built over [[dungeon|the Dungeon]], and the [[guild|Guild]] governs matters concerning the Dungeon.[@fm01-orario] It is the world's only "Labyrinth City", a great city since the Ancient Times before the gods descended, and because it holds the world's only Dungeon it has come to be known as the "Center of the World".[@fm01-orario-name, fm08-orario-center] Its founding god, [[ouranos|Ouranos]], has reigned without governing since he laid its foundation, and the Guild is the closest thing the city has to a governing body: it can requisition any Familia by issuing a mission.[@so02-orario-ouranos, so03-orario-guild] Orario is the world's only source of magic stones, which only the Guild may buy, and its magic-stone industry is the city's economic heart.[@so01-orario-stones, so09-orario-industry] The city is circular and organised around the tower [[babel|Babel]], from which eight Main Streets run out to the city wall.[@fm01-layout] It stands at the western tip of the continent, with the lake of [[njordr-familia#port-meren|Port Meren]] to its west and southwest and the [[beor-mountains|Beor Mountains]] to the north.[@ar03-orario-west, fm08-orario-north] The city is easy to enter but very hard to leave: adventurers and anyone in a Familia, gods included, need documents approved by the Guild to pass its gates, and only a few groups, such as [[hermes-familia|Hermes Familia]], may come and go freely.[@fm06-orario-exit, fm08-orario-leave, fm20-orario-hermes]

## Babel

Babel is the white tower standing directly over the Dungeon's entrance, acting as its lid.[@fm01-babel] Lilly has heard that the first, shorter tower was destroyed when the gods first descended; it was rebuilt with their help.[@fm02-babel] [[hephaistos-familia|Hephaistos Familia]] has shops on its lower floors, Guild tenants occupy floors up to the twentieth, and gods live on the floors above.[@fm02-babel] Freya watches the city from the fiftieth floor.[@fm03-babel] In DanMachi 17 its roof, the point in Orario closest to the heavens, becomes the centre of the altar from which [[hestia|Hestia]] breaks Freya's charm.[@fm17-babel]

## Events in the city

[[zeus-and-hera-familias|Zeus and Hera Familias]] ruled Orario for a thousand years, until a dragon king destroyed them during the Three Great Quests.[@ar01-orario-zeus, fm15-orario-history] Their fall let the [[evils|Evils]] rise, the old rulers were driven out of the city, and two rising Familias, each led by a goddess, contended to become Orario's new hope.[@fm15-orario-history] In that era the Evils set the city ablaze on the night of the [[great-conflict|Great Conflict]], when "Orario faced its longest night", and later besieged it from its own walls, until its defenders won and the remaining Evils fled or went into hiding.[@ar01-orario-night, ar02-orario-siege, ar03-orario-victory]
