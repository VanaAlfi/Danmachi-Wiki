**Raul Nord** is a human adventurer of [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]], twenty-one in Sword Oratoria 4.[@so04-raul] He is the third son of a poor farming family who left his country home and came to Orario "filled with big dreams and just a little manly ambition", soon joining Loki Familia.[@so04-origin] He is a trusted Level 4 who supervises the Familia's lower ranks.[@so04-level] DanMachi 11 calls him "a lackluster second-tier adventurer" with an "exceedingly average appearance", to whom the gods gave the alias "High Novice"; [[valletta|Valletta]] taunts him with the name in Sword Oratoria 7.[@fm11-novice, so07-novice]

## Character {#character}

Sword Oratoria 10 calls him "the spitting image of a normal person who lacked self-confidence": strong as a Level 4, yet "plainly daunted" by the Familia's first-tier adventurers, from Finn down.[@so10-normal] Yet Sword Oratoria 7 names one of his few merits: because he is "a hopeless mess", the others pull together, and he lifts the party's spirits "in a way only he could", a talent not even Finn, Riveria, Gareth or Aiz has.[@so07-merit] Sword Oratoria 12 calls it "the unintentional charisma he brought to the group as a boring human".[@so12-charisma] He still remembers [[bete-loga|Bete]] standing over him "with a sardonic laugh" while he fought back tears.[@so04-bete] [[anakity-autumn|Anakity]], who joined the Familia at about the same time, steadies him; when his talk of the depths silences the lower-ranking members in the tent, [[riveria|Riveria]] reminds him: "It’s your duty as their superior to encourage, not start a panic."[@ar02-raul, so04-aki] He waits a long time to rise: in Sword Oratoria 6 the Familia's alternates are "stuck at Level 4", and in Sword Oratoria 13 he does not level up.[@so06-stuck, so13-raul] He reveres Finn: to him, Anakity says, "Finn Deimne is a symbol, someone who would never betray him", and in Sword Oratoria 14 she calls him "a dyed-in-the-wool Captain worshipper".[@so10-symbol, so14-worshipper]

## In the Great Conflict {#great-conflict}

Raul had been in [[orario|Orario]] only a short time when the [[great-conflict|Great Conflict]] began (not yet a full year in Astrea Record 1, a year and a half in Astrea Record 2), and Loki had just told him he was "almost ready for a Rank Up".[@ar02-raul, ar01-young] [[riveria|Riveria]] counted him and Aki as "just supporters", and [[gareth|Gareth]] says that Raul came to Orario to become an adventurer, though at the time he went pale at having arrived in such a dreadful period.[@ar01-age] During the fighting he serves as one of Finn's messengers.[@ar01-young] Pale and shaking, he still guides the townsfolk to safety, and the veteran [[noir-dyne-and-bahra|Noir]] sends him back to that work: "Stay off the battlefield, Raul. That’s where you work best".[@ar02-raul] Astrea Record 2 says that, stronger than others though he was, "he had an ordinary person’s heart".[@ar02-heart] In the same chapter he and [[anakity-autumn|Anakity]] follow the sound of a hammer to the Bridge of Heroes, where [[goibniu|Goibniu]] is repairing the damaged statues alone: "We need this bridge", he tells them, "If it falls, we all fall." Moved to tears by the thirty-one heroes, the two fight off six Evils cultists on the bridge, and "Under the eternal gaze of heroes past, Raul and Anakitty earned their promotion to Level 2."[@ar02-bridge]

In Astrea Record 3 he watches from the roof of Guild Headquarters as the veterans [[noir-dyne-and-bahra|Noir, Dyne and Bahra]] cut their way alone into the monster horde; he begs Finn to send someone to save them, is told, "Do not defy me, Raul. This is an order.", and weeps after "watching the veterans sacrifice themselves".[@ar03-veterans]
