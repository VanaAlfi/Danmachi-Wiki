In a **Familia coalition**, members of different [[familia|Familias]] join forces for purposes including [[quest|quests]], expedition support and military campaigns.[@so03-quest, so04-smiths, fm08-command] Against [[ares-familia|Ares Familia]], [[finn-deimne|Finn Deimne]] commands a loosely bound coalition whose constituent groups retain freedom to act across their battlefronts.[@fm08-command]

## History {#history}

| Volume | Events |
|---|---|
| DanMachi 8 | The war against Ares Familia is settled by the capture of [[ares|Ares]], which Finn Deimne had prioritized, and a search party for [[bell-cranell|Bell Cranell]] sets out in earnest.[@fm08-end] |
| DanMachi 12 | [[hestia-familia|Hestia Familia]], [[miach-familia|Miach Familia]] and [[takemikazuchi-familia|Takemikazuchi Familia]] form a joint expedition party.[@fm12-expedition] |

| DanMachi 18 | Hestia Familia heads the coalition for the [[war-game|War Game]] against [[freya-familia|Freya Familia]], and Finn Deimne undertakes to train [[lilliluka-erde|Lilliluka Erde]] for command.[@fm18-formation-1, fm18-formation-2] |
| DanMachi 18 | If she falls, command is entrusted to [[aisha-belka|Aisha Belka]], [[daphne|Daphne Laulos]] and [[tsubaki|Tsubaki Collbrande]].[@fm18-command] |
| DanMachi 18 | The coalition wins, and confiscated assets are divided among its members except [[folkvangr|Folkvangr]], which comes under [[guild|the Guild]] management.[@fm18-victory] |
| DanMachi 19 | [[school-district|The School District]] is described as a Familia union managed by multiple Familias.[@fm19-union] |
| DanMachi 20 | A report announces the defeat of the expedition alliance on [[floor-60|Floor 60]], while also indicating that its captain and others remain below awaiting aid.[@fm20-news] |
| Sword Oratoria 3 | [[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz Wallenstein]] joins [[hermes-familia|Hermes Familia]] temporarily for a joint quest, with the agreement marked by a handshake.[@so03-quest] |
| Sword Oratoria 4 | [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]] requests smiths from [[hephaistos-familia|Hephaistos Familia]], which accepts expedition cooperation in exchange for deep-floor [[magic-stone|drop items]] usable as weapon materials.[@so04-smiths] |
| Sword Oratoria 6 | Loki Familia, [[dionysus-familia|Dionysus Familia]] and Hermes Familia unite against the underground forces behind which the mastermind Enyo is glimpsed, with motives including revenge and settling accounts.[@so06-enyo] |
| Sword Oratoria 6 | [[ishtar-familia|Ishtar Familia]] and [[kali-familia|Kali Familia]] prepare an alliance against Freya Familia, with [[kali|Kali]] seeking a fight against powerful opponents.[@so06-kali] |
| Sword Oratoria 11 | The alliance suffers a rout with many deaths during the attack on [[knossos|Knossos]].[@so11-rout] |
| Sword Oratoria 13 | The forces led by Loki Familia subsequently conquer Knossos.[@so13-knossos] |
| Astrea Record 2 | Finn Deimne serves as coalition commander during the conflict with [[evils|the Evils]], predicting that further enemy attacks will amount to harassment.[@ar02-command] |
