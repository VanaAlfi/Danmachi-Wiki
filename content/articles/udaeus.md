**Udaeus** is the [[monster-rex|Monster Rex]] of [[floor-37|Floor 37]], a deep-level floor boss rated Level 6.[@so02-udaeus] It is a skeleton monster, "a spartoi that had just kept on growing", with jet-black bones.[@so02-form] [[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz Wallenstein]] defeats it alone in Sword Oratoria 2, the feat that makes her Level 6; [[ottar|Ottar]] later kills it alone to win its rare drop item, the Udaeus Black Sword.[@so02-udaeus, so03-levelup, fc02-sword]

## The monster

- **Form.** Close to ten meders tall from the pelvis up, with its lower body underground, with a forward-leaning spine, horns like an [[monsters#ogre|ogre]]'s on its skull, and small crimson flames for eyes.[@so02-form] In truth it has no lower body at all.[@so02-pikes]
- **Pikes.** It fills its chamber with black pikes (pila) that shoot from the floor, makes anyone who throws caution to the wind "an instant pincushion", and seals the exits so that no one can leave until it is defeated.[@so02-pikes, fc02-awake] Its bones are bound by magic energy at the joints, which lets the skinless monster move in any direction.[@so02-joints]
- **Spartoi.** Floor 37 is also home to ordinary spartoi, which fight alongside it.[@so02-spartoi]
- **Respawn.** Like every Monster Rex it has a fixed respawn time. Loki Familia had defeated it with its full strength three months before Aiz's fight.[@so02-udaeus]
- **Name.** DanMachi 2 prints the name *Udaios*.[@fm02-udaios]

## Aiz's solo fight (Sword Oratoria 2)

Aiz goes to the thirty-seventh floor to fight Udaeus alone, with [[riveria|Riveria]] watching from the chamber's entrance.[@so02-udaeus, so02-riveria] She breaks its spine and brings it down.[@so02-spine] Then, unexpectedly, it pulls a jet-black longsword from the ground, a weapon like the nature weapons of other monsters; its strokes burn and gash the floor.[@so02-sword] When her wind gives out and she falls, Riveria starts to cast to save her, but Aiz cries out to stop her and fights on alone.[@so02-stop] She defeats it, and the victory takes her to Level 6.[@so03-levelup]

## Ottar and the Black Sword (Familia Chronicle 2)

No record in Orario's history had ever shown Udaeus with a sword; after Aiz's report, the Guild announced that the drop item Udaeus Black Sword might appear when it is faced one-on-one, or perhaps by very few.[@fc02-sword] Ottar tells Freya he wants to defeat Udaeus to obtain that sword.[@fc02-sword]

Three months after Aiz's victory, Udaeus awakens again when Ottar enters its chamber.[@fc02-awake] He evades or smashes its pikes; when it summons its huge black sword, six meders long, he judges it among the highest tier of nature weapons. He breaks its bones; his final blow knocks the black sword from its grip, and having lost "the king's blade" it dies, leaving a giant purple [[magic-stone|magic stone]].[@fc02-fight, fc02-death] From the drop item, a first-tier blade over two meders long is made, and Ottar kneels to present it to his goddess.[@fc02-blade]

## The first Black Sword

To pay off the debt for a broken [[desperate#rapier|rapier]], Aiz sold Udaeus's other drop items but kept the black sword; Minor Myths and Legends 2 says she entrusted it to [[bors|Bors]], who promised to make a weapon from it and keeps telling her it is not finished.[@so03-sold, ss02-bors]
