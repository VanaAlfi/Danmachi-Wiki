**Basram** is an animal-person priest of [[apate-and-alecto-familias#apate-familia|Apate Familia]] who creates and controls spirit warriors for the [[evils|Evils]].[@ar03-priest, ar01-experiment, ar02-command] He is also the familia's strategist.[@ar01-basram-front] He controls spirit warriors through a staff and participates in the Evils' attacks on [[freya-familia|Freya Familia]].[@ar02-command, ar03-priest] An aging, stout man, he wears a gentle priest's face until defeat looms.[@ar03-basram-amphitheatrum, ar03-basram-end]

## Spirit warriors {#spirit-warriors}

Following the doctrine of his goddess Apate, whose domain is injustice, he turned former [[minor-familias#osiris-familia|Osiris Familia]] members who had lost their reason to fight into mindless beasts.[@ar01-basram-warriors] A dagger in each warrior's neck is a captured spirit forced into a weapon; Basram says he sought the spirits' blessing that heroes had before the gods came down, and some of the warriors now heal their own wounds.[@ar01-basram-warriors] A team of mages and hexers tunes them, and he commands them with his golden ringed staff, the only magic item that controls them.[@ar02-basram-tuning, ar03-basram-amphitheatrum, ar03-basram-end] He admits that nothing matches a purebred first-class adventurer, yet his warriors are the backbone of the Evils' elite forces.[@ar02-basram-tuning]
