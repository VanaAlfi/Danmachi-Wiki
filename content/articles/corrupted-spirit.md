The **corrupted spirit** is a spirit of the Ancient Times that went down into the [[dungeon|Dungeon]], was consumed by a monster, and has kept its own will for over a thousand years as a monster itself.[@so04-ouranos] The creatures who serve it call it "Her".[@so03-olivas, so06-her] Its crystal-orb fetuses feed on monsters and grow into **demi-spirits**, half-spirit and half-monster hybrids that can cast magic.[@so04-finn, so05-demi] [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]] fights one on Floor 59 in Sword Oratoria 4. In Sword Oratoria 11–12 Enyo, the god [[dionysus|Dionysus]], uses seven in [[knossos|Knossos]] in a plan to erase [[orario|Orario]].[@so04-titan, so12-heavens-gate, so12-nidhogg]

## The spirits of old

When the gods began their descent, spirits acted as their antennas and carried out their will; some gods released them to guide the civilised peoples and drive monsters off the surface. Sword Oratoria 4 compares them to the Falna of the present day. They gave their protection to many heroes, and many were sent into the Dungeon, which is how the [[dungeon-oratoria|Dungeon Oratoria]] came about.[@so04-ouranos] In Sword Oratoria 5 [[welf-crozzo|Welf]] traces the [[magic-sword|Crozzo magic swords]] to a spirit's blood given to his ancestor, and Loki Familia's members silently wonder whether [[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz]]'s wind comes from spirit blood too.[@so05-blood]

## The corrupted spirit

Watching Floor 59 through Fels's eye on Aiz's loin guard, [[ouranos|Ouranos]] recognises one of the spirits of old. It must have been consumed by a monster and has kept its sense of self for all those years, though its state depends on the monster that consumed it; it has become a monster governed by feeding and stealing. "That which you see there…is already corrupted."[@so04-ouranos] Sword Oratoria 6 sums it up as a messenger of the gods, devoured again and again by the monsters of the depths, still alive and constantly changing its form.[@so06-her]

[[olivas-act|Olivas Act]] tells Loki Familia on Floor 24 that She wants to see the sky, and that the city blocking her view from underground must be destroyed.[@so03-olivas] In the [[guild#chamber-of-prayers|Chamber of Prayers]], Fels and Ouranos take the hybrid creatures to be a result of the corrupted spirit as well.[@so04-creatures] In Sword Oratoria 12 [[filvis|Filvis]] explains that a fragment of it was on Floor 27 on the day of the Twenty-Seventh-Floor Nightmare, drawn by the smell of blood and looking for new feelers among the adventurers there.[@so12-feelers]

Its colourful monsters, the caterpillars and the [[monsters#violas|violas]], attack other monsters to collect [[magic-stone|magic stones]] for it. Finn calls them its "tentacles".[@so04-tentacles, so06-her] Loki explains in Sword Oratoria 6 that the violas prefer other monsters' magic stones even to human flesh.[@so06-dust] From the transformed terrain of Floor 59, and the tentacles that came up from far below to protect the demi-spirit, Ouranos concludes that the spirit's true form lies much deeper, past the sixtieth floor.[@so04-main]

## Demi-spirits

### Floor 59 {#floor-59}

On Floor 59 Loki Familia finds a woman with a giant plant-like lower half: Riveria thinks it has absorbed a titan alm, the deep-level Corpse Flower King. Caterpillars and violas offer it the magic stones on their tongues, then rot to ash.[@so04-titan] It laughs and calls Aiz "Aria", asking whether she won't let it eat her.[@so04-aria] Its spells include Fire Storm and Thunder Ray.[@so04-spells] [[riveria|Riveria]]'s [[magic#rea-laevateinn|Rea Laevateinn]] burns away its armour of ten petals, and Aiz's wind-driven sword cuts it in two.[@so04-rea, so04-aiz] Loki Familia barely wins.[@so04-finn] The terms overlap in the text: Sword Oratoria 4 calls the creature on Floor 59 the corrupted spirit, and Sword Oratoria 5 calls the same fiend both the "corrupted spirit" and a demi-spirit, while Ouranos concludes that the spirit's main body lies deeper.[@so04-ouranos, so05-demi, so04-main]

### Gugalanna, the Bull of Heaven {#gugalanna}

In Sword Oratoria 7 a demi-spirit like a giant ox, with a woman's upper body jutting from its forehead, breaks into Knossos while Loki Familia is there; Levis calls its arrival an [[irregular|Irregular]].[@so07-bull] It is the "Bull of Heaven" that [[ishtar|Ishtar]] was to receive from the Evils for her revenge on [[freya|Freya]].[@so07-ishtar] [[gareth|Gareth]] guesses it parasitised a power bull. Its bovine half has the strength while its upper half casts magic at great speed.[@so07-cast, so07-frail] [[hyrute-sisters|Tiona]] destroys its legs, and Gareth, Tione and Tiona pull on [[magic#restrict-iorum|Restrict Iorum]]'s whip of light until the spirit's head is torn off.[@so07-legs, so07-head]

### The six demi-spirits of Knossos {#heavens-gate}

In Sword Oratoria 11, when Thanatos asks Ein to unleash the demi-spirits against Loki Familia, Ein refuses: the spirits have their own roles, and moving them would go against Enyo's will.[@so11-six] When a god returns to Heaven during the battle, the altar activates; six demi-spirits placed around Knossos begin to sing, swell and pour out green flesh.[@so11-altar] Gareth reports Thanatos's words that Knossos is an altar for a sacrifice, not a fortress.[@so12-altar]

From the Knossos mural of a dragon ringed by six maidens, Bell tells Loki Familia that there is no hero in the story of Nidhogg: six great spirits sealed the dragon, sang, and gave their lives to destroy it.[@so12-mural] Loki calls the ritual **Heaven's Gate**: spirits placed around a great magic circle act as its mediums and call down a pillar approaching the gods' power, strong enough to blow away Orario and its surroundings. The six demi-spirits were to recreate it and erase the city, the lid on the Dungeon.[@so12-heavens-gate]

Each altar combines a demi-spirit chanting above with three faces below, which fire spells at the attackers; Tiona destroys one face by breaking the magic stone of the Treant at its core.[@so12-faces] [[fels|Fels]] blows up the hearts that circulate magic through the altar, disrupting it.[@so12-hearts]

### Nidhogg {#nidhogg}

Dionysus reveals the seventh demi-spirit, **Nidhogg**, on the eleventh floor of Knossos. Its fetus was attached to a nameless [[monsters#infant-dragon|infant dragon]] that Levis and the others captured in the deep floors, and it was given the name of the dragon of legend. Absorbing the six spirits' magic, it would explode upward and kill the adventurers fighting above it. It would be weaker than the six-ring spell, but enough to deal Orario a fatal blow, while below the eleventh floor stays safe for Dionysus.[@so12-nidhogg] On Finn's order [[rei|Rei]] carries [[bell-cranell|Bell]] to it, and Bell's charged attack obliterates it.[@so12-bell] Finn confirms the defeat of all seven spirits.[@so12-seven] Afterwards the green flesh in the labyrinth rots, and the colourful monsters that acted as the spirit's feelers stop working.[@so12-rot]
