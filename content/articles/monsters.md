**Monsters** are the creatures of [[dungeon|the Dungeon]] that adventurers fight. Each has a [[magic-stone|magic stone]] as its core, and destroying the stone turns the whole monster to ash.[@so03-core] This page collects what the novels say about monsters in general, indexes the species, and gives a section to each species that has no page of its own.

## Monsters in general {#general}

- **Levels:** the Guild rates monsters by Level. The al-miraj, weak alone but "surprisingly dangerous in groups", are "classified as Level 2 monsters"; the Guild rates lizardman elites between Level 3 and Level 4.[@fm05-rating, lizardman.fm14-elite] A rampaging vouivre is called "a rare large-category monster".[@vouivre.fm10-large]
- **Upper and middle levels:** the monsters of the [[upper-levels|upper levels]] only charge. In the middle levels some have learned to attack from a distance, and DanMachi 5 says the middle-level monsters' intelligence is far higher (the Yen Press text says the upper-level monsters "could only attack from short range and charge with claws, clubs, and whatnot" and that the difference in intelligence is "worlds apart").[@fm05-middle, fm05-ja-middle]
- **Nature weapons:** the Dungeon supplies weapons that monsters pick up, such as tree-stump clubs, stone tomahawks and the metal flowers of the twentieth floor, which serve as shield and blades.[@fm09-nature] See [[al-miraj|Al-Miraj]] and [[#lizardman|Lizardman]].
- **Monster against monster:** monsters of different kinds can fight each other in the Dungeon (the Yen Press text says they "tended to attack anything they perceived as a threat, including other monsters").[@fm05-other, fm05-ja-other] Some species prefer other monsters' magic stones: see [[#violas|Violas]] and [[corrupted-spirit|Corrupted spirit and demi-spirits]].[@violas.so03-stones]

## Upper levels {#upper-levels}

{{nocite}} Floors 1 to 12. The sections run roughly from the shallowest floors to the deepest.

### Goblin {#goblin}

Goblins are "The weakest monster in the entire Dungeon", as Hestia puts it; DanMachi 15 calls the goblin a creature "whose name was synonymous with weakness".[@goblin.fm04-weakest, goblin.fm15-haruhime]

- **Where:** the first four floors, with their pale blue walls, usually hold only goblins and kobolds. On floors 8 and 9, stronger goblins and kobolds appear rather than new species.[@goblin.fm02-floors, goblin.fm02-eighth]
- **Drops:** in DanMachi 1 Bell picks up a goblin fang as a drop item.[@goblin.fm01-fang]
- **On the surface:** a goblin nearly killed Bell as a child. He supposes it descended from goblins that escaped the Dungeon before the Guild existed, since monsters breed out in the world. His grandfather saved him armed with a garden hoe.[@goblin.fm01-child, goblin.fm03-gramps]
- **Firsts:** right after joining Hestia Familia, Bell slew a goblin and ran back to tell Hestia. A goblin is also the first monster he hits with [[magic#firebolt|Firebolt]].[@goblin.fm04-joined, goblin.fm04-first, goblin.fm02-firebolt] In DanMachi 15 [[haruhime|Haruhime]]'s clumsy attack on a lone goblin goes wrong, and a gang of goblins beat her until Lilly flying-kicks them away.[@goblin.fm15-haruhime]

### Kobold {#kobold}

Kobolds are dog-headed monsters that usually wander alone or in pairs and attack with sharp claws and fangs. Bell does not think them "too bright".[@kobold.fm01-kobolds]

- **Strength:** in DanMachi 1 Bell has "only recently got strong enough to slay kobolds". Their magic stones are mere shards.[@kobold.fm01-strong, kobold.fm01-shard]
- **Drops:** when Bell takes a kobold's shard, one sharp claw stays behind instead of turning to ash: a drop item.[@kobold.fm01-shard]
- **Where:** with goblins, on the first four floors, and in stronger forms on floors 8 and 9.[@goblin.fm02-floors, goblin.fm02-eighth]

### Dungeon lizard {#dungeon-lizard}

A four-legged lizard with scaly brown skin, as long as Bell is tall including its tail. It appears on floors 2 to 4 and is in the "same class as goblins and kobolds".[@dungeon-lizard.fm01-lizard]

### War shadow {#war-shadow}

DanMachi 1 prints it as the **Wall Shadow**, "the lower-sixth-floor monster", and DanMachi 16 once as *woe shadows*, but the Japanese originals of both volumes use the same word as everywhere else, {{ja|ウォーシャドウ|Wō Shadō}}, *war shadow*; *Wall* and *woe* are Yen Press's variants.[@war-shadow.fm01-wall, war-shadow.fm01-ja, war-shadow.fm16-woe, war-shadow.fm16-ja] Other volumes print *war shadow*.[@war-shadow.fm10-orde]

- **Arms:** its long arms end in knife-like fingers. DanMachi 1 gives it three; DanMachi 10 gives a war shadow five.[@war-shadow.fm01-fingers, war-shadow.fm10-orde]
- **Danger:** in DanMachi 1 it is the most dangerous monster of the sixth floor "in terms of pure power", and adventurers of the upper floors must be careful when one appears. Like the killer ant, it is called a "newbie killer".[@war-shadow.fm01-fingers, killer-ant.fm02-ant]
- **Weakness:** in Eina's quiz it has high attack and high speed, but defence "roughly comparable to that of a goblin or a kobold".[@war-shadow.ss01-quiz]
- **Drops:** a finger blade.[@war-shadow.fm01-drop]
- **Xenos:** Orde, a war shadow, scouts for [[xenos#ranieh|Ranieh]]'s party in full plate armour, so that he passes for an adventurer. He dies when the hunters ambush them in DanMachi 10.[@war-shadow.fm10-orde]

### Frog shooter {#frog-shooter}

A frog monster with one giant eye that shoots out a long tongue to attack adventurers; Yen Press's DanMachi 1 prints "bulb-eyed" and "spear-like".[@frog-shooter.fm01-ja, frog-shooter.fm01-frog] Frog shooters are "barely stronger" than goblins and kobolds, but in Sword Oratoria 4 a swarm surrounds [[lefiya|Lefiya]] while [[filvis|Filvis]] trains her.[@frog-shooter.so04-lefiya] In DanMachi 7 Bell likens a shock to "getting licked by a frog shooter".[@frog-shooter.fm07-licked]

### Killer ant {#killer-ant}

A **killer ant** looks like a giant red ant with four legs, two thin arms and two big eyes; it stands as tall as Bell when it rears up. It first appears on the lower seventh floor and is called a "newbie killer".[@killer-ant.fm02-ant]

- **Armour and claws:** its skin "might as well be armor", and four curved claws hang from its arms. The recommended way to kill one is to strike the soft flesh at the joints between its shells.[@killer-ant.fm02-ant, killer-ant.fm02-joints]
- **Size:** killer ants are human-sized, unlike the small purple moths and needle rabbits found with them.[@killer-ant.fm02-size]
- **Calling for help:** close to death, a killer ant releases a pheromone that calls other killer ants. [[soma-familia#kanu|Kanu]] uses a dying one to bring a swarm down on Lilly in DanMachi 2.[@killer-ant.fm02-pheromone]

### Purple moth {#purple-moth}

**Purple moths** spread poisonous wing-scales in battle. The effect is slow, but breathing in too much poisons the body.[@purple-moth.fm02-scales] There are so many in the upper levels that many adventurers have breathed their spores, which makes the [[development-ability|Development Ability]] Immunity relatively easy to acquire.[@purple-moth.fm04-immunity] Their drop item is the Purple Moth Wing ({{ja|パープル・モスの翅|Pāpuru Mosu no hane}}; the Yen Press text prints "Purple Moth Scales"), and the poisonous scale powder collected from it goes into Lilly's fast-acting poison powder and Bell's poison grenades.[@purple-moth.fm03-drop, purple-moth.fm03-ja-drop, purple-moth.fm19-grenades]

### Needle rabbit {#needle-rabbit}

A small horned rabbit; Bell blocks a needle rabbit's tusk with his knee plate on the seventh floor.[@needle-rabbit.fm02-rabbit] Needle rabbits are "small" monsters, met alongside killer ants, and one strays as far as the tenth floor.[@killer-ant.fm02-size, needle-rabbit.fm02-tenth] Bell calls the [[al-miraj|al-miraj]] "needle rabbits that learned how to walk on two legs".[@needle-rabbit.fm05-almiraj]

### Orc {#orc}

The **orc** is a large-category monster: about three meders tall, with brown skin, a boar's head and old hide round its waist, squat and fat rather than muscled like a Minotaur.[@orc.fm02-orc]

- **Where:** large-category monsters first appear on the tenth floor; there are none down to the ninth.[@orc.fm02-tenth]
- **Arms:** an orc pulls a dead tree out of the floor and swings it as a club.[@orc.fm02-club]
- **Strength:** a direct hit keeps a Level 1, or even a Level 2, down for a long time. But its weight makes it slow, and it easily loses its balance.[@orc.fm04-strong, orc.fm02-slow]
- **Birth:** in DanMachi 4 Bell watches an orc break out of the wall. From around the tenth floor many monsters can emerge in the same room at once, a "monster party".[@orc.fm04-birth]

### Imp {#imp}

**Imps** are little demons (the Yen Press text has them flying; the Japanese DanMachi 3 shows them rushing and hopping on the ground and has no word for flight). They look like goblins, but they are clever: they never attack one at a time, and they use the fog of the tenth floor to surround a target from every side. On that floor a pack of imps is said to be more troublesome than a single orc (the Yen Press text says "more feared than even the gigantic orcs").[@imp.fm03-imps, imp.fm03-ja-imps] Lilly's purple powder sets them coughing violently (the Yen Press text has them fall out of the air).[@imp.fm03-powder, imp.fm03-ja-powder] In DanMachi 19 imps happen to be near the infant dragon that Bell's school squad must hunt.[@imp.fm19-dragon]

### Bad bat {#bad-bat}

A dark-coloured, bat-type monster with sharp fangs and eerie sound waves that break an adventurer's concentration (the Yen Press text says "a black bat–type monster with sharp claws and a really distracting scream").[@bad-bat.fm03-bat, bad-bat.fm03-ja-bat] Bad bats have "hardly any raw combat ability", but their shrieks disrupt adventurers' movements; they lurk in the shadows of the Cave Labyrinth.[@bad-bat.so13-shriek] In DanMachi 5 a swarm is born from a tunnel ceiling.[@bad-bat.fm05-swarm] In Sword Oratoria 13 a mass spawning of bad bats around the fifteenth floor brings a ceiling down near the [[balder-class#seventh-squad|7th Squad]].[@bad-bat.so13-collapse]

### Silverback {#silverback}

**Silverbacks** look like massive gorillas covered in thick white fur. They are named for the silver mane round the neck and the silver stripe down the back, and they first appear on the lower eleventh floor.[@silverback.fm04-look, silverback.fm01-floor] They are smaller than orcs but powerful and agile, and with the Hard Armoreds they make the lower eleventh floor very dangerous for Level 1s.[@silverback.fm04-look] The al-miraj are more agile still.[@silverback.fm05-agile] Bell fights one at the [[monsterphilia|Monsterphilia]] of DanMachi 1.[@silverback.fm04-look]

### Hard Armored {#hard-armored}

**Hard Armoreds** are armadillo-like monsters whose defence was famed as an iron wall in the upper levels (the Yen Press text says they had "the best defense in the upper levels"). They curl into a ball and roll at adventurers at speed, and rolled up they bounce off all physical attacks (the Yen Press text says "most blades"); their underbelly is unprotected.[@hard-armored.fm05-mikoto, hard-armored.fm05-ja-armored, hard-armored.fm04-armored] They first appear on the eleventh floor, and are met in the middle levels too.[@hard-armored.fm04-armored, hard-armored.fm04-middle] DanMachi 4 says Level 1s need Basic Abilities of B to S to survive the lower eleventh and twelfth floors because of them.[@hard-armored.fm04-armored] [[mikoto|Yamato Mikoto]]'s small shield is made from their plating.[@hard-armored.fm05-mikoto]

### Infant dragon {#infant-dragon}

The **infant dragon** is a wingless, four-legged dragon with thick amber scales and red eyes: only about 150 celch tall, but over 4 meders long.[@infant-dragon.fm04-dragon]

- **Rarity:** it appears only on the lower eleventh and twelfth floors. DanMachi 4 says only four or five roam the Dungeon at a time; DanMachi 19 says there are usually not even five on the twelfth floor.[@infant-dragon.fm04-rare, infant-dragon.fm19-rare]
- **The upper levels' boss:** with no [[monster-rex|Monster Rex]] on these floors, infant dragons are the effective floor bosses, and they have wiped out parties of Level 1s.[@infant-dragon.fm04-rare, infant-dragon.so09-aiz]
- **DanMachi 4:** on the eleventh floor, Bell destroys one with a charged Firebolt, despite dragons' natural resistance to flame. Asfi later reports it to Hermes.[@infant-dragon.fm04-firebolt, infant-dragon.fm05-asfi]
- **Sword Oratoria 9:** in her early years, Aiz goes into the Dungeon alone and fights infant dragons, to [[riveria|Riveria]]'s fury.[@infant-dragon.so09-aiz]
- **DanMachi 10:** the god [[ganesha|Ganesha]] of [[ganesha-familia|Ganesha Familia]] approaches a tamed infant dragon, fitted with a magic item, inside a fence, and it snaps at his mask.[@infant-dragon.fm10-tamed]
- **DanMachi 19:** the [[school-district|School District]] sets an infant dragon's drop item as a grade requirement, and the [[balder-class#third-squad|3rd Squad]] finally wins an infant dragon fang.[@infant-dragon.fm19-grade, infant-dragon.fm19-fang]
- **Nidhogg:** the demi-spirit [[corrupted-spirit#nidhogg|Nidhogg]] grew from a crystal-orb fetus attached to an infant dragon that Levis's group brought up from the deep floors.[@infant-dragon.so12-nidhogg]

## Middle levels {#middle-levels}

{{nocite}} From the thirteenth floor down. For the Floor 37 monsters, see also [[floor-37|Floor 37]] and [[colosseum|Colosseum]].

### Hellhound {#hellhound}

**Hellhounds**, also called Baskervilles, are doglike monsters of the middle levels: black, heavily muscled and four-legged, with deep red eyes.[@hellhound.fm05-baskerville, hellhound.fm05-look]

- **Fire:** they are physically formidable for middle-level monsters, but the real danger is the fire they spit; in the Japanese it melts ordinary armour with ease (the Yen Press text says they are "not all that physically powerful" and that the flames crack "even some of the better types of armor"). A pack's inferno can reduce a party to ash. When a party is wiped out on the thirteenth or fourteenth floor, hellhounds are most likely to blame (in the Japanese, overwhelmingly so).[@hellhound.fm05-fire, hellhound.fm05-ja-fire] No monster of the upper levels breathes fire like them.[@hellhound.fm05-wisp]
- **Tells:** before spitting fire, a hellhound raises its hindquarters, lowers its head, and shows sparks between its teeth.[@hellhound.fm05-spit]
- **Defences:** hellhounds are the reason [[eina-tulle|Eina]] insists on salamander wool for Bell's party; the Japanese says it is solely because of them (the Yen Press text says "the main reason").[@hellhound.fm05-baskerville, hellhound.fm05-ja-baskerville] Being fire-breathers, they resist fire magic unusually well.[@hellhound.fm05-resist] [[welf-crozzo|Welf]]'s [[magic#will-o-the-wisp|Will-o'-the-Wisp]] makes the hellhounds detonate as their own fire builds up; the Japanese says their flame is magic power burned inside the body, which the spell triggers.[@hellhound.fm05-wisp, hellhound.fm05-ja-wisp] [[asfi|Asfi]] glues their mouths shut with sticky vials before they can fire.[@hellhound.fm05-asfi]
- **Drops:** a quest posted in DanMachi 4 asks for "Hellhound Fangs x10".[@hellhound.fm04-fangs]
- **Company:** they often hunt alongside [[al-miraj|al-miraj]]. In Sword Oratoria 13 Lefiya singles out their fire breath as a threat to her squad.[@hellhound.fm07-pack, hellhound.so13-lefiya] Among the Xenos, the hellhound Helga goes about with the al-miraj [[al-miraj#aruru|Aruru]]; in Sword Oratoria 12 the two track down Asterios on Fels's orders.[@hellhound.so12-helga]

### Liger fang {#liger-fang}

The **liger fang** is "A tigerlike monster with a body that’s solid like a rock", with an "armor-thick coat" that a knife can hardly pierce; [[mikoto|Mikoto]], who has fought one before, warns that "It’s faster than a Minotaur!"[@liger-fang.fm07-irregular]

- **Where:** in DanMachi 7 one strays upward, an [[irregular|Irregular]], "a monster that shouldn’t show up until level fifteen" (the printed "level" is the floor).[@liger-fang.fm07-irregular] In DanMachi 8 the roars of liger fangs fill the ash-coloured caves of the [[cave-labyrinth|Cave Labyrinth]].[@liger-fang.fm08-cave]
- **Materials:** Aiz collects "a drop item, liger fang fur" in Sword Oratoria 2, and in DanMachi 8 [[welf-crozzo|Welf]] forges with "a metal synthesized from a liger fang’s tooth and noh steel mined from floor twenty-seven".[@liger-fang.so02-fur, liger-fang.fm08-metal]
- **The School District squad:** Sword Oratoria 13 calls liger fangs "the squad’s archenemy" for the [[school-district|School District]] students Lefiya guides, though on a later dive "The liger fangs that were so much trouble the first time were like nothing." A liger fang that has "feasted on the corpses of the monsters they had left lying there" chases down the student Cole "with its now enhanced body", because he forgot the post-battle cleanup.[@liger-fang.so13-squad, liger-fang.so13-enhanced] See [[magic-stone|enhanced species]].

### Vouivre {#vouivre}

Vouivres are a species of dragon, which DanMachi 9 calls "the greatest monsters in the Dungeon". Bell remembers the vouivre as one of the rarest of rare monsters, "On par with the unicorn". It is said to appear between the nineteenth and twenty-fourth floors, in the [[colossal-tree-labyrinth|Colossal Tree Labyrinth]].[@vouivre.fm09-rare, vouivre.fm09-berserk]

- **Form:** a vouivre usually has a woman's upper body and a snakelike lower body, like a lamia; "women attached to a dragon tail". It has a red jewel in its forehead.[@vouivre.fm09-form, vouivre.fm09-rare]
- **Senses:** like other dragons, vouivres have particularly acute senses, such as hearing.[@vouivre.fm10-senses]
- **Drop items:** a vouivre's scales and claws fetch "tremendous sums". Its forehead gem, the **Vouivre's Tear**, is worth far more; adventurers call it the "Prosperity Stone".[@vouivre.fm09-rare]
- **The jewel and the rage:** taking the jewel from its forehead sends a vouivre berserk, and killing it shatters the jewel, so many adventurers have died trying to get one.[@vouivre.fm09-berserk] Sword Oratoria 10 adds that a vouivre that loses the stone rages until it gets it back.[@vouivre.so10-rage]

The Xenos [[wiene|Wiene]] is a vouivre. Because she is a girl in form, Bell wonders whether she is a subspecies.[@vouivre.fm09-form] In DanMachi 10 the hunter [[ikelos-familia|Dix]] tears the jewel from her forehead. She turns into a rampaging dragon with a dragon's tail, and yet shows no interest in Dix, who holds her stone.[@vouivre.fm10-dix, vouivre.fm10-rage, vouivre.so10-tail] Her story is on [[wiene|her own page]].

### Lizardman {#lizardman}

**Lizardmen** are red-scaled monsters, about 170 celch tall, that stand upright and fight with weapons in both hands. Bell's party meets them on the twentieth floor, in the Colossal Tree Labyrinth, and for the first time Bell feels he is fighting other adventurers.[@lizardman.fm09-twentieth]

- **Arms:** the twentieth floor's lizardmen carry nature weapons: metal flowers that grow from the walls. With the stem removed, the flower is a round shield fifty celch across, and its petals, called "cutters", serve as daggers.[@lizardman.fm09-flowers] On the twenty-fourth floor, in Sword Oratoria 3, three lizardmen challenge Aiz with a hardened flower for a shield and its thorny stem as a shortsword.[@lizardman.so03-aiz]
- **Lizardman elite:** "a higher-rank version of the lizardmen" of the Colossal Tree Labyrinth, with blue scales "as hard as armor" and two axes of milky white stone. The Guild rates them between Level 3 and Level 4, and they specialise in close combat.[@lizardman.fm14-elite] They are among the warrior-type monsters of [[floor-37|Floor 37]], where Bell and Lyu fight them, and they fill the [[colosseum|Colosseum]].[@lizardman.so02-elite, lizardman.fm14-elite, lizardman.fm14-colosseum] On Floor 37 in Familia Chronicle 2, [[ottar|Ottar]] cuts them down with a single slash each.[@lizardman.fc02-ottar]
- **Xenos:** [[lido|Lido]], leader of the [[xenos|Xenos]], is a scarlet lizardman with a longsword and a scimitar. Fighting him in Sword Oratoria 10, Tione murmurs that he "doesn't seem like your regular lizardman".[@lizardman.so10-lido]

Sword Oratoria 2 calls the elite "an upgraded form of the lizardman monster" and says it "first appeared on the nineteenth floor". The sentence does not make clear whether that refers to the elite or to the ordinary lizardman.[@lizardman.so02-elite] {{inference}}

### Bugbear {#bugbear}

**Bugbears** are bearlike monsters. Bell remembers that "their Strength and Defense are on par with a Minotaur", but that what makes them dangerous is a "deceptively high Agility" for their size; they first appear on the nineteenth floor and "corner their prey before tearing it apart".[@bugbear.fm05-desc]

- **Food and smell:** they eat the honey cloud fruit of [[floor-18|Floor 18]]. In DanMachi 5 three hungry bugbears chase off the outlaws guarding Hestia once they are smeared with the fruit.[@bugbear.fm05-desc, bugbear.fm05-honey, bugbear.fm09-honey] Bugbears are "known for their keen sense of smell".[@bugbear.fm09-smell]
- **Where:** with mad beetles, gun libellulas and firebirds they are among the dangers of the [[colossal-tree-labyrinth|Colossal Tree Labyrinth]], and they wander into Floor 18 from the floors next to it.[@bugbear.fm09-dangers, bugbear.ar03-safe]
- **Fights:** in DanMachi 5 Bell saves [[mord-latro|Mord]] from three bugbears with Mord's own sword.[@bugbear.fm05-mord] In Sword Oratoria 1 Aiz cuts off a bugbear's arm before its claws can reach her, and makes room in her backpack for "a bugbear claw", a drop item.[@bugbear.so01-aiz]

### Mad beetle {#mad-beetle}

**Mad beetles** are "medium-size, two-legged insect monsters" with "hooked pincers".[@mad-beetle.fm05-beetles, mad-beetle.fm09-pincers] They swarm with bugbears and gun libellulas in the [[colossal-tree-labyrinth|Colossal Tree Labyrinth]]: a swarm of them is Bell and Welf's first encounter on the nineteenth floor, where the [[goliath-robe|Goliath Robe]] Welf wears repels their pincers.[@mad-beetle.fm09-pincers] In DanMachi 19 [[lyu-leon|Lyu]] passes through a flanking swarm "like the wind", and a touch of her new Alvs Iustitia sets off a chain of ash explosions.[@mad-beetle.fm19-lyu]

### Gun libellula {#gun-libellula}

**Gun libellulas** are "dragonfly monsters" that attack from a distance with "spear-like abdomens".[@gun-libellula.fm09-ranged] DanMachi 5 prints the name once as *gun liberla*.[@gun-libellula.fm05-liberla]

- **Tactics:** because of their long-range attacks Bell shoots them down first with [[magic#firebolt|Firebolt]]; those in the spell's path are incinerated.[@gun-libellula.fm09-ranged, gun-libellula.fm09-firebolt] In DanMachi 19 he cuts down a swarm "spinning through the air like tops" at close range.[@gun-libellula.fm19-tops]
- **Company:** they fly above bugbears on the ground. In Familia Chronicle 3 a merchant's hired Familia is nearly wiped out by "massive bugbears and flying gun libellulas".[@gun-libellula.fc03-merchant]

### Firebird {#firebird}

**Firebirds** are "a rare kind of monster normally found on the nineteenth floor and below": birds that attack mainly with fire.[@firebird.fm09-outbreak] The one that attacks [[wiene|Wiene]] in DanMachi 9 is a crimson bird "over two meders long from end to end", with bloodshot eyes and a massive beak, and its fire is "more powerful than a hellhound could ever produce".[@firebird.fm09-wiene]

- **Danger:** left unchecked, firebirds can turn an area into "a sea of flames", and they sometimes fly up to Floor 18 and endanger [[rivira|Rivira]].[@firebird.fm09-outbreak]
- **The outbreak of DanMachi 9:** firebirds overflow on the nineteenth floor, an [[irregular|Irregular]], and Rivira's adventurers draw Hestia Familia into the hunt; during it Bell finds Wiene.[@firebird.fm09-outbreak, firebird.fm09-wiene]

### Dark fungus {#dark-fungus}

**Dark fungi** are dark-purple, mushroom-like monsters that hide among the giant fungi of the [[colossal-tree-labyrinth|Colossal Tree Labyrinth]], wait for prey to come to them, and release clouds of poisonous gas. In DanMachi 9 they slip past [[mikoto|Mikoto]]'s detection Skill, because she has never met them before.[@dark-fungus.fm09-ambush] Their "highly toxic pollen" harms friend and foe alike, but it cannot overpower Aiz's Immunity ([[development-ability#abnormal-resistance|Abnormal Resistance]]); in Sword Oratoria 3 [[filvis|Filvis]]'s lightning roasts a cluster and burns their spores out of the air.[@dark-fungus.so03-aiz, dark-fungus.so03-filvis]

### Battle boar {#battle-boar}

The **battle boar** is a colossal boar, "Nearly two meders tall" and "truly a large-category monster", that charges with brute strength.[@battle-boar.fm09-charge]

- **In the Dungeon:** in DanMachi 9 one bursts through a wall of fire in the Colossal Tree Labyrinth at the head of a swarm of bugbears.[@battle-boar.fm09-charge]
- **The Monsterphilia:** DanMachi 1's main event opens with a captured "Battle Boar", more than two meders tall, charging a [[ganesha-familia|Ganesha Familia]] tamer, who dodges every charge; the narration likens it to "a bull fight".[@battle-boar.fm01-monsterphilia]
- **Xenos:** in DanMachi 10 "the battle boar’s acute sense of smell" leads the [[xenos|Xenos]] through Knossos to their captured comrades.[@battle-boar.fm10-xenos]
- **Outside the Dungeon:** in Sword Oratoria 14 battle boars roam a mountain outside Orario, among the monsters Finn fights there.[@battle-boar.so14-mountain]

### Swordstag {#swordstag}

The **swordstag** is a deer monster with "bladelike antlers".[@swordstag.so03-antlers] DanMachi 1 says that swordstags and trolls "were born on Level Twenty and below" (the printed "level" is the floor), and a swordstag is among the monsters that escape at the [[monsterphilia|Monsterphilia]].[@swordstag.fm01-floor] In Sword Oratoria 3 swordstags come in a pass parade with deadly hornets, lizardmen and dark fungi, and Aiz cuts two down in mid-air.[@swordstag.so03-parade, swordstag.so03-antlers] DanMachi 19 and Sword Oratoria 14 print *sword stag*; in Sword Oratoria 14 they roam a mountain outside Orario.[@swordstag.fm19-stag, battle-boar.so14-mountain]

### Troll {#troll}

**Trolls** are "large, humanoid monsters", born from the twentieth floor down; one in Sword Oratoria 12 swings "a giant club".[@troll.ss02-aiz, swordstag.fm01-floor, troll.so12-club]

- **The Monsterphilia:** a troll is among the monsters loose in the city in DanMachi 1, and [[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz]] kills it in the street "in one decisive blow".[@swordstag.fm01-floor, troll.fm01-aiz, troll.so01-aiz]
- **Young Aiz:** in Minor Myths and Legends 2 the young Aiz brings down a horde of trolls by slashing at their ankles.[@troll.ss02-aiz]
- **The Bloodstained Troll:** the best-known enhanced species, a troll that grew strong by eating other monsters' magic stones. It killed many adventurers and even the elite parties sent against it, grew "so powerful that the Guild couldn’t classify its Level", and took five top-class adventurers to kill; [[freya-familia|Freya Familia]] carried out the extermination.[@troll.so03-bloodstained] In DanMachi 12 Aisha has heard that "more than fifty people died"; Familia Chronicle 2 recalls "the terrible blood-drenched troll incident—where an enhanced species was born of stones left lying around".[@troll.fm12-bloodstained, troll.fc02-incident] See [[magic-stone|magic stone]].
- **Xenos:** the [[xenos|Xenos]] include trolls. At their party in DanMachi 9 a troll hands out wooden tankards of cheap brew, and in DanMachi 11 "the flat-footed troll" runs clumsily beside Wiene.[@troll.fm09-party, troll.fm11-flat]

### Deadly hornet {#deadly-hornet}

**Deadly hornets** are four-winged insects as tall as an adult human, covered in thick black plates like armour, with scissor-shaped pincers and "a poisonous stinger shaped like a pike". They normally appear from the twenty-second floor down, among the monsters that keep third- and second-tier adventurers out of the deep levels.[@deadly-hornet.fm09-hornets]

- **Sting:** the stinger can pierce heavy armour and kill a Level 2 adventurer in one thrust, and most who survive a sting die of blood loss.[@deadly-hornet.fm09-hornets]
- **Nickname:** as killer ants are the upper levels' "novice killers", deadly hornets are the "upper-class killer bee" (DanMachi 9), printed "high killer bees" in DanMachi 12.[@deadly-hornet.fm09-hornets, deadly-hornet.fm12-hive]
- **Armour:** their exoskeletons are even harder than killer ants', and arrows bounce off them.[@deadly-hornet.fm12-hive]
- **The bloody hive:** a rare, immobile trap monster that lives with them: a seven-meder, pinecone-shaped nest buried in a wall. It bursts out when prey comes near and shoots an adhesive liquid that pins victims down for the hornets, which, because the hive is joined to the Dungeon, spawn far faster than usual. Bell's party meets one in DanMachi 12.[@deadly-hornet.fm12-hive]
- **Xenos:** among the armed monsters is "a deadly hornet wearing armor" (DanMachi 11), and in Sword Oratoria 12 "A giant deadly hornet held lances in both hands".[@deadly-hornet.fm11-armor, troll.so12-club]

### Mammoth fool {#mammoth-fool}

**Mammoth fools** stand six to seven meders at the withers, with blood-red fur and upturned tusks "as long as spears". Unlike most monsters of the [[colossal-tree-labyrinth|Colossal Tree Labyrinth]], with their special abilities or hard insect shells, they are a monster "whose danger came simply from brute strength", and they are "the largest of the ordinary middle-level monsters".[@mammoth-fool.fm13-herd] In DanMachi 13 a herd attacks the party hunting Gale Wind; in DanMachi 19 Bell's party and an adventurer of Rivira each deal with a large one without trouble.[@mammoth-fool.fm13-herd, mammoth-fool.fm19-bell, mammoth-fool.fm19-rivira]

### Wyvern {#wyvern}

**Wyverns** are dragons with "a pair of wings as forelimbs" that breathe fireballs. The ordinary wyvern is "A monster that inhabited the middle-floor region", with a pale-red body.[@wyvern.so04-ill, wyvern.so09-black]

- **Ill wyverns:** in Sword Oratoria 4 "ill wyverns, bluish-purple dragons boasting heights of some three meders with their tails included", come out of the tunnels that the [[#valgang-dragon|valgang dragons]]' fireballs bore through the floors and attack [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]] with salvos of fireballs. An enhanced one, "this king of the skies", hunts [[hyrute-sisters|Tiona]] and [[bete-loga|Bete]] until [[lefiya|Lefiya]]'s arrow of light destroys it.[@wyvern.so04-ill, wyvern.so04-king]
- **The Black Wyvern:** the wyvern young [[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz]] fights in Sword Oratoria 9 is more than five meders long and "pure black": "It was clearly an Irregular, a subspecies hiding far more strength than the usual."[@wyvern.so09-black] Astrea Record 2 says an evil god lured Aiz, then Level 1, down to the twelfth floor and "summoned the Black Wyvern" with his arcanum; Sword Oratoria 10 says the god used [[knossos|Knossos]], and Sword Oratoria 12 names him as [[thanatos|Thanatos]]. Aiz first used her magic to defeat it.[@wyvern.ar02-arcanum, wyvern.so10-knossos, wyvern.so12-thanatos] See also [[upper-levels|Upper levels]].
- **On the surface:** in Astrea Record 3 a flock of wyverns approaches the Casino, and Falgar wonders whether they were brought "all the way from the Valley of Dragons" (see [[valley-of-dragons|Valley of Dragons]]).[@wyvern.ar03-casino]

### Moss huge {#moss-huge}

The **moss huge** is "a type of rare monster that appears on the twenty-fourth floor": a plant in human shape whose body is made of moss.[@moss-huge.fm12-rare] Its "main distinguishing feature" is its ability to "produce replicas of itself that lack magic stones" when cut, so adventurers believe they have killed one while the real monster escapes; moss huges are "not so much bellicose monsters as highly intelligent ones that make ample use of mimicry, ambushes, and getaways".[@moss-huge.fm12-rare]

In DanMachi 12 an **enhanced** moss huge appears in the [[water-capital|Water Capital]], below the middle levels where its kind lives: two meders tall, covered in moss and armoured in tree roots, with hornlike pieces of wood on its head that make Bell think it "probably closer to an ogre".[@moss-huge.fm12-ogre, moss-huge.fm12-rare] It cuts off the arm of the elf adventurer Luvis and plants parasitic vines in [[chigusa|Chigusa]] before Bell and his allies overcome "the Irregular moss huge".[@moss-huge.fm13-luvis, moss-huge.fm13-irregular]

### Green dragon {#green-dragon}

**Green dragons** are "treasure keepers inhabiting the middle levels of the Dungeon": "Monstrous enough to be called the strongest monsters of their floors, they possessed the highest potential a dragon type could have."[@green-dragon.so14-keepers] They guard rare items such as the jewel trees, whose red and blue gems sell for a great deal of money; Sword Oratoria 3 rates them "a good match for even Level Four adventurers", and Minor Myths and Legends 2 says they are "no match for an adventurer of Tiona’s caliber".[@green-dragon.so03-jewel, green-dragon.ss02-jewel] Sword Oratoria 14 describes one that confronts elves outside the Dungeon: ten meders long and well over five high, with ragged membranous wings and bark-like scales.[@green-dragon.so14-keepers]

The [[xenos|Xenos]] have a green dragon too: the scarred, wizened dragon "more than ten meders long" in the Hidden Village of DanMachi 9, the lookout of their village on the twentieth floor.[@green-dragon.fm09-xenos, green-dragon.ss01-lookout]

## Lower levels {#lower-levels}

{{nocite}} The floors from the twenty-fifth down to the deep levels. The sections below are the species of the [[water-capital|Water Capital]], floors 25 to 27, roughly in the order the novels meet them; its floor boss is the [[amphisbaena|Amphisbaena]].

The Guild allows Level 3 adventurers into "the lower levels that start on the twenty-fifth floor", and Level 3 is the standard for these floors.[@lower-levels.fm07-guild, lower-levels.fm12-standard] Their monsters are cleverer than those above: Bell finds them "really intelligent. Way more so than in the upper and middle levels".[@merman.fm13-leader] Aisha warns that the Water Capital has a large number "not just of water monsters but of humanoid monsters as well", which Bell takes to mean sirens, harpies, mermaids and lamias; apart from the winged harpies and sirens, its aquatic monsters "are scarcely impacted by fire-type magic".[@lower-levels.fm12-humanoid, lower-levels.fm13-fire] Purely water-dwelling monsters are born by "breaking through the beds or crystal walls of the streams", which makes them hard to track.[@blue-crab.fm12-land]

### Blue crab {#blue-crab}

**Blue crabs** are "categorized as metal monsters" and live both on land and in the water. They have "irregularly developed claws on either the right or left side, depending on the individual", and strike like hammers; their steel carapaces are "even stronger than those of the deadly hornets" (see [[#deadly-hornet|Deadly hornet]]), but the seams are wide. Level 2 adventurers can take them down by working together, and to the party's surprise they advance head-on instead of scuttling sideways.[@blue-crab.fm12-metal, blue-crab.fm12-land] Ten "steel shells from blue crabs" were among the drop items Lilly lists as proof of the party's Guild mission in DanMachi 12; in DanMachi 14 Welf reinforces his portable hearth with the shells.[@lower-levels.fm12-quest, blue-crab.fm14-hearth]

### Raider fish {#raider-fish}

**Raider fish** are huge fanged fish; the first one Bell's party meets measures 160 celch and leaps out of the water at them.[@raider-fish.fm12-leap] Unlike aqua serpents they "could not emerge onto land", and in DanMachi 14 a school of them attacks Mikoto when she falls into the water.[@raider-fish.fm14-mikoto] Thirty raider fish fangs could stand in for the aqua serpent fins on DanMachi 12's list of drop items.[@lower-levels.fm12-quest]

Raider fish also live outside the Dungeon. Sword Oratoria 2 calls the one [[bete-loga|Bete]] kicks to death in Orario's sewers "a monster native to the open ocean that must have found its way in through the brackish lake".[@raider-fish.so02-sewers] In Sword Oratoria 6 one lurking in a sunken ship was "likely the reason the ship had capsized in the first place"; the Guild has long struggled with raider fish that use the river from Lolog Lake to reach Orario's sewers, and the fishermen of [[njordr#port-meren|Port Meren]] "still get some raider fish attacks".[@raider-fish.so06-ship, raider-fish.so06-river, raider-fish.so06-meren]

### Aqua serpent {#aqua-serpent}

The **aqua serpent** is a large-category water monster with light-green scales and a snake's head; its finned head looks like a dragon's, and "According to information collected by the Guild, aqua serpents can grow up to ten meders long".[@aqua-serpent.fm12-guild] Unlike raider fish, aqua serpents can come out onto land.[@raider-fish.fm14-mikoto] Their fins were one of the drop items on the list for DanMachi 12's mission.[@lower-levels.fm12-quest] In DanMachi 12 one fastens on Bell's shoulder, and he struggles to get it off; in DanMachi 14 aqua serpents rise from the water between the ice-islands as harpies dive at the party.[@aqua-serpent.fm12-shoulder, aqua-serpent.fm14-ice]

### Crystal turtle {#crystal-turtle}

**Crystal turtles** are large-category monsters with "multiple crystals projecting from its body"; on the twenty-fifth floor Bell worries that a rocky bank might be one.[@lower-levels.fm12-shore, crystal-turtle.fm13-large, crystal-turtle.fm12-bank]

### Devil mosquito {#devil-mosquito}

**Devil mosquitoes** hover in the air of the Water Capital; Bell calls the devil mosquito "hideous", a monster "that latches on to adventurers and sucks their blood".[@lower-levels.fm12-shore, devil-mosquito.fm12-air]

### Iguaçu {#iguacu}

The **iguaçu** are swallow monsters of the Water Capital that live in the cliff behind the Great Falls. Adventurers call them "invisible monsters" for their speed, and Minor Myths and Legends 1 calls them "the fastest creature found on the lower floors".[@iguacu.fm12-swallows, iguacu.ss01-fastest] They dive-bomb their prey; an iguaçu that hits a shield or another hard object is crushed by its own speed, and upper-class adventurers say "if you encounter an iguaçu, you should drop anything you’re holding and run". Their severed feathers are drop items.[@iguacu.fm12-swallows, iguacu.ss01-fastest]

In DanMachi 12 Bell, separated from his party by the current at the foot of the Great Falls, survives "a mass outbreak of iguaçu" alone; afterwards stories spread about "the weirdo who massacred an entire pack of iguaçu by himself".[@iguacu.fm12-swallows, iguacu.ss01-fastest]

### Harpy {#harpy}

**Harpies** are "half-human/half-bird, bizarrely female-looking monsters". Some live on the surface: in DanMachi 8 a flock attacks Bell and [[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz]] in the Beor Mountain Range, descendants of monsters that "emerged from the Dungeon millennia ago" and settled there, and Aiz cuts them down.[@harpy.fm08-beor] In the Dungeon they fly with sirens and other bird-type monsters around the Great Falls of the Water Capital, where Bell finds them "far swifter than those who live on land".[@lower-levels.fm12-falls, harpy.fm12-siren] Harpies fly better than sirens and attack at close range with hooked claws; in DanMachi 14 a flock dives at the party while aqua serpents attack from the water.[@harpy.fm12-siren, aqua-serpent.fm14-ice] Fia of the [[xenos|Xenos]] is a red-haired harpy.[@harpy.fm14-fia]

### Siren {#siren}

**Sirens** are bird-human monsters like harpies: both have "the faces of women and the bodies of birds", and the wild ones Bell meets in the Water Capital are wrinkled, smell foul and are "uglier than old crones". Sirens fly less well than harpies but "emit irregular sound waves as they fly", so they attack from a distance.[@harpy.fm12-siren] Their high-pitched songs fill the air over the Great Falls, and in DanMachi 14 harpies, sirens and other winged monsters force [[tsubaki|Tsubaki]]'s group off the cliff route below the twenty-fifth floor.[@lower-levels.fm12-falls, siren.fm14-cliff] Seeing the wild ones, Bell realises "how far Rei and Fia have deviated from the norms of their race": [[rei|Rei]] of the Xenos is a siren, and Fia a harpy.[@harpy.fm12-siren, harpy.fm14-fia]

### Mermaid {#mermaid}

**Mermaids** are half-fish, half-human monsters with hair "the drab green of a withered plant", pure-white eyes and bluish-white skin; DanMachi 12 says "Mermaids are among the rare monsters of this floor", more attractive than harpies but "distinctly creepy".[@mermaid.fm12-rare] The mermaid Mari of the [[xenos|Xenos]], whose blood heals Bell, guides him through the twenty-fifth floor with her songs in DanMachi 12; see [[water-capital|Water Capital]].[@mermaid.fm12-mari, lower-levels.fm12-shore]

### Merman {#merman}

**Mermen** are "half-fish, half-human monsters" covered in blue scales. They walk on two feet and wield the Dungeon's nature weapons, such as crystal maces, "a type of lower-level nature weapon", and Bell likens them to "an underwater version of lizardmen" (see [[#lizardman|Lizardman]]).[@merman.fm13-mermen] They are "among the stronger opponents" of the twenty-sixth floor and fight in groups under a leader: once the leader is killed, "the group falls into chaos".[@merman.fm13-mermen, merman.fm13-leader] In DanMachi 13 Bell kills a merman leader with his black knife; in DanMachi 14 Lilly's arrow pierces a leader's eye, a rare shot for a supporter.[@merman.fm13-leader, merman.fm14-lilly]

### Light quartz {#light-quartz}

**Light quartzes** are "inorganic monsters that float about adventurers’ heads": purple crystals the size of bucklers with a single pale-yellow, eyelike organ. They cannot fight at close range; their one attack is a beam of light that burns lines into the Dungeon's crystal floor and walls. "The typical way to defeat a light quartz is to get it to emit all its power, then attack it while it’s recharging."[@light-quartz.fm13-beam] They are met from the twenty-fifth floor to the twenty-seventh.[@lower-levels.fm12-shore, light-quartz.fm14-27th]

### Kelpie {#kelpie}

**Kelpies** are horse monsters "with blue pelts and manes as well as finned bodies" that gallop through water as if on land. They normally appear on the twenty-seventh floor, and their beauty "belied a potential that was among the greatest of any in the Water Capital". In DanMachi 14 one comes up to a higher floor, a specimen whose potential "might even have exceeded Welf’s and Ouka’s statuses", and Aisha cuts it down.[@kelpie.fm14-kelpie] In DanMachi 10 Hestia watches tamers bring a rampaging kelpie to heel with whips; [[ganesha-familia|Ganesha Familia]] alone may keep live monsters in the city.[@kelpie.fm10-tamers]

### Lamia {#lamia}

**Lamias** have "a humanoid upper body with a snakelike lower body", as a vouivre usually does (see [[#vouivre|Vouivre]]); Sword Oratoria 10 describes a lamia as "a half snake, half human".[@lamia.fm09-vouivre, lamia.so10-half] The lamia, afanc and dodora that turn up in DanMachi 14 are all "monsters that normally appeared for the first time on the twenty-seventh floor".[@lamia.fm14-27th] Several [[xenos|Xenos]] are lamias: in DanMachi 14 one saves Cassandra from a voltemeria and winks at Lilly, and Minor Myths and Legends 1 names one, Laura.[@lamia.fm14-xenos, lamia.ss01-laura]

### Voltemeria {#voltemeria}

The **voltemeria** is "a rare monster found only on" the twenty-seventh floor, ranked with the kelpie among the strongest of the Water Capital: a black fish of stone, "exceedingly resistant to physical attack", with jaws and fangs that "could crush even the heaviest armor donned by adventurers", and a single eye in its forehead.[@voltemeria.fm14-rare, voltemeria.fm14-eyes] Built like light quartzes, voltemerias float about three meders above the ground but swim through the air far faster, so adventurers call them "flying fossils". They normally live where several waterways meet in deep pools.[@voltemeria.fm14-rare] In DanMachi 14 a school of about thirty, drawn by the smell of blood, traps Lilly's party, until a pack of [[xenos|Xenos]] arrives and destroys them.[@voltemeria.fm14-rare, voltemeria.fm14-xenos, lamia.fm14-xenos]

### Poison vermis {#poison-vermis}

**Poison vermis** are small maggot-like monsters. "Of all the poison-inflicting monsters in the Dungeon, poison vermis were the most dangerous": the toxin they spew from their mouths and secrete from their pores can afflict even upper-class adventurers with strong status resistances, though their attack power is low. They spring from carcasses in droves like maggots, hence the nickname "poison graveyards".[@poison-vermis.so05-swarm] The poison needs a treatment "made from its own secretions", and that antivenin is hard to find, because "Poison vermis inhabited only the lower levels" and are not numerous there.[@poison-vermis.so05-antivenin, poison-vermis.so05-rare, poison-vermis.ss02-rare]

- **Sword Oratoria 5:** on the way back from its expedition, [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]] meets an Irregular, a continuous spawning of poison vermis; giant maggots "easily more than thirty celches" drop on the party from the ceiling "like purple rain". The poisoned members wait on the eighteenth floor while others look for antivenin in Orario; in Minor Myths and Legends 2 Bete and Loki find it hard to gather. See also [[dian-cecht-familia|Dian Cecht Familia]].[@poison-vermis.so05-swarm, poison-vermis.so05-rain, poison-vermis.so05-rare, poison-vermis.ss02-rare]
- **Sword Oratoria 7:** poison vermis appear in [[knossos|Knossos]], to the surprise of Tiona's group ("But why would poison vermis be in a place like this…?!"), and Arcus is poisoned.[@poison-vermis.so07-knossos]
- **As a substance:** in Sword Oratoria 2 Aiz is told that "Injecting a dead body with ‘Poison Vermis’ prevents skin decay", and Familia Chronicle 1 names a "Liquid Poison Vermis".[@poison-vermis.so02-corpse, poison-vermis.fc01-liquid]

### Bloodsaurus {#bloodsaurus}

**Bloodsauruses** are dinosaur-type monsters with "A jaw big enough to swallow a person whole", razor-sharp teeth and scales the colour of blood; in the Dungeon they are "Over ten meders long" and "comparable to a floor boss", and swift as well.[@bloodsaurus.fm20-jaw] They "usually appear starting at the thirtieth floor": DanMachi 12 calls that floor the place "where you really start to need a high level and status", with "swarms of bloodsauruses", and DanMachi 19 describes the forested floors where they "begin to appear", with Level 3 "a requirement for survival there".[@bloodsaurus.fm20-jaw, bloodsaurus.fm12-thirtieth, bloodsaurus.fm19-forest] Their carnivorous nature makes them "prone to becoming enhanced types". In DanMachi 20 three of them come up to the twenty-ninth floor, and Bell destroys the largest, an enhanced one, by wrapping its magic stone in the Goliath Scarf; bloodsaurus fangs are among the expedition's drop items. The same chapter names other monsters of these floors, the spinoaxes ("comparable in size to the bloodsauruses") and the almalosauruses.[@bloodsaurus.fm20-jaw, bloodsaurus.fm20-fangs]

Bloodsauruses also live on the surface. In DanMachi 4 Bell's party meets several in the Seoro Forest on Nahza's quest; there they "might be a little bit stronger than an orc in the Dungeon", and the one met was only five meders long.[@bloodsaurus.fm04-surface, bloodsaurus.fm20-jaw]

### Blue dragon {#blue-dragon}

The **blue dragon**, "also known as an aurora dragon", is "A rare monster from the lower floors", "up there with carbuncles in rarity": a long dragon of about ten meders with blue-and-white scales and winglike fins, which swims through the air trailing "red, green, blue, and purple folds of light".[@blue-dragon.fm19-aurora] In DanMachi 19 one appears above the adventurers and students of the [[school-district|School District]]; flying out of reach, it can only be brought down with magic, arrows or other long-range attacks.[@blue-dragon.fm19-aurora]

## Deep levels {#deep-levels}

{{nocite}} The warrior, skeleton and rock monsters of the White Palace, [[floor-37|Floor 37]], then the ooze and the valgang dragons of the fifty-eighth floor. See also the [[#lizardman|lizardman elite]], the [[colosseum|Colosseum]] and the floor boss [[udaeus|Udaeus]].

Floor 37's monsters are mostly warrior types that fight hand to hand, "specialists in hand-to-hand combat who bring brute strength, agility, and mastery of nature weapons to their fights"; the floor is "absolute torture for magic users", who have no time to cast.[@loup-garou.fm14-sword, barbarian.so02-white]

### Barbarian {#barbarian}

The **barbarian** is a large-category warrior monster with "a body on par with a Minotaur", two twisted horns, and sharp fangs and claws; it swings nature weapons and lashes a long tongue.[@barbarian.so02-white, barbarian.fm14-ambush]

- **Drops:** a mixture of barbarian hearts and other organs is a drop item "normally used like powerful smelling salts"; Lyu carries a bag of it to keep monsters from sniffing them out. The barbarians' "oily hair" burns extremely well.[@barbarian.fm14-hearts, barbarian.fm14-hair]
- **In DanMachi 14:** one ambushes [[lyu-leon|Lyu]] at the head of a pack of lizardmen; in the Colosseum an enhanced barbarian crushes a loup-garou's head; and Bell kills a wounded barbarian that survived the fall from the Colosseum by letting it come to him and piercing its magic stone.[@barbarian.fm14-ambush, barbarian.fm14-colosseum, barbarian.fm14-last]

### Loup-garou {#loup-garou}

**Loup-garous** are wolf-headed warrior monsters that carry nature weapons such as stone swords and white stone cudgels.[@loup-garou.fm14-sword, loup-garou.fm14-cudgel] Werewolf-type monsters have invaded the surface: "Most of the time when a village gets ransacked on a moonlit night, it’s by a pack of these loup-garous", and animal people beat any such monster to a pulp.[@loup-garou.fm14-sword]

### Skull sheep {#skull-sheep}

**Skull sheep** are skeleton monsters covered to the hooves by "a long, wide skin", dark and torn like "a ragged old robe"; Bell first mistakes one for the grim reaper.[@skull-sheep.fm14-robe] The skin lets them "conceal themselves in the darkness" of the floor and strike from it. They pierce with their elongated bones and shoot bone lances, "long-distance projectiles"; DanMachi 14 calls them "The hermits who feasted on death".[@skull-sheep.fm14-robe, skull-sheep.fm14-lance, skull-sheep.fm14-hermits] The [[juggernaut|Juggernaut]] of DanMachi 14 absorbs them and uses their pila.[@skull-sheep.fm14-juggernaut]

### Spartoi {#spartoi}

**Spartoi** are skeleton warriors of white bone, Bell's height or taller, armed with swords or lances of bone and kite-shield-like protectors. DanMachi 14 rates them "stronger than a lizardman elite or a loup-garou, and more agile by far than a barbarian", moving with the skill of an adventurer, and advises fleeing from a spartoi "if at all possible".[@spartoi.fm14-pack, spartoi.fm14-flee] The floor boss [[udaeus|Udaeus]] looks like "a spartoi that had just kept on growing", and summons spartoi around its room.[@spartoi.fm02-udaeus, spartoi.so02-udaeus, spartoi.fc02-ottar] In the [[colosseum|Colosseum]] a spartoi born from the floor beneath Bell gives him and Lyu away.[@spartoi.fm14-found]

### Obsidian soldier {#obsidian-soldier}

**Obsidian soldiers** are rock monsters with bodies of solidified lava: slower than the warrior monsters, strong only in defence, and "Said to be among the poorest fighters on the thirty-seventh floor". Their bodies counteract magic "as effectively as amulet stones", so mages can do little against them.[@obsidian-soldier.fm14-rock, obsidian-soldier.so02-magic] Their stone is a valued drop item, used for superior-grade magic-resistant shields and armour, and [[knossos|Knossos]] is built with it to weaken magic.[@obsidian-soldier.fm14-rock, obsidian-soldier.so07-drop, obsidian-soldier.so10-knossos] The Juggernaut reinforces its shell with obsidian soldiers' stone.[@obsidian-soldier.fm14-juggernaut]

### Peluda {#peluda}

**Peludas** are monsters of [[floor-37|Floor 37]] that cling close to the rocks like lizards. Lyu names them, with the [[#spartoi|spartoi]], as the floor's greatest threats: "Peludas attack with poison", and "If we run into a spartoi or a peluda, we should flee if at all possible."[@peluda.fm14-lyu, peluda.fm14-colosseum] They shoot the poisoned spines on their backs, which "could easily penetrate the defensive ability of even an upper-tier adventurer", and "Befitting their dragon bloodline" they breathe fire; around the [[colosseum|Colosseum]] they make the floor "A hell of poison and flame".[@peluda.fm14-spines, peluda.fm14-colosseum]

### Ooze {#ooze}

The **ooze**, "Also known as a slime", is "a rare monster that only starts to appear on the deep floors and below". Oozes come in many colours, such as red, white and green, all with the same mucus body, and attack with the poison or acid in their mucus, which depends on the colour ("The yellow ones are acidic"); the worst enter the body through the ears, nose or mouth and dissolve the organs.[@ooze.ss01-ooze] They are slow, but their liquid bodies are hard to damage: they can be burned or frozen with spells or magic swords, or killed by "piercing the magic stone inside their body with a long, sharp weapon", an amethyst crystal. Their drop item, ooze fluid, is "literally just the creature itself". In Minor Myths and Legends 1, stranded in the deep levels, [[lyu-leon|Lyu]] looks for blue oozes, kills one and boils its fluid for Bell and herself to drink.[@ooze.ss01-ooze]

### Fomoire {#fomoire}

The **Fomoire** (so printed in Sword Oratoria 1; *formoire* in DanMachi 9 and 10) are hulking monsters of the deep levels. In Sword Oratoria 1 a horde of them howls beyond [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]]'s shield wall on the forty-ninth floor, and an abnormally tall one breaks through the front line ("The forty-ninth floor is always rough").[@fomoire.so01-wall, fomoire.so01-rough] Black rhinos are "walking fortresses compared to the Fomoire on floor forty-nine", and in Sword Oratoria 4 "a large group of Moitra formoires" comes at the party on the fifty-eighth floor.[@black-rhino.so01-rhinos, fomoire.so04-moitra] Among the [[xenos|Xenos]], Foh is "a formoire from the deep levels", a hulking monster with a kind heart; in DanMachi 10 he falls to Ikelos Familia's hunters while guarding Wiene's party.[@fomoire.fm10-foh]

### Black rhino {#black-rhino}

**Black rhinos** are "Rhinoceros monsters that walked on two legs", just over two meders tall, with two horns, one long and one short; their hide is "as strong and thick as high-quality armor".[@black-rhino.so01-rhinos] In the deep levels they burst out of the walls in hordes, more than thirty at a time in Sword Oratoria 1; in Sword Oratoria 4 [[tsubaki|Tsubaki]] picks up a black rhino horn, a drop item.[@black-rhino.so01-rhinos, black-rhino.so04-horn] The Guild officially designated the black minotaur [[asterios|Asterios]] "a subspecies of black rhino, a creature found in the deep levels", with a potential of Level 7.[@black-rhino.fm12-asterios]

### Deformis spider {#deformis-spider}

The **deformis spider** is "an absolutely terrifying red and purple spider, with eight hairy legs and many menacing eyes" of the deep levels. In Sword Oratoria 1 one bursts out of the wall beside [[lefiya|Lefiya]]; in Sword Oratoria 4, where black rhinos and deformis spiders attack from every angle, another drags her toward its hole with its massive thread.[@deformis-spider.so01-spider, black-rhino.so04-horn, deformis-spider.so04-thread]

### Cadmus {#cadmus}

**Cadmus**, "the Great Dragon", is "an extremely rare monster that had been strong enough to contend with a floor boss", the guardian of the Cadmus Springs on the fifty-first floor; Sword Oratoria 3 counts "the Cadmus dragons on the fifty-first floor" among the treasure keepers that guard rare items.[@cadmus.so01-springs, cadmus.so01-guardian, green-dragon.so03-jewel] In Sword Oratoria 1 [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]] takes a [[dian-cecht-familia|Dian Cecht Familia]] quest for water from the springs, and finds the Cadmus already reduced to ash, its drop items uncollected: "Something strong enough to kill Cadmus. Not adventurers."[@cadmus.so01-springs, cadmus.so01-ash] Its drop item, the **Cadmus Hide**, is "An extremely rare drop item", worth the armour and weapons of the whole expedition, used for "nearly unbreakable armor" and as "a key ingredient for potent healing items"; Tione later sold one.[@cadmus.so01-hide, cadmus.so01-uses, cadmus.ss02-sold]

### Valgang dragon {#valgang-dragon}

**Valgang dragons** are the great red dragons of the fifty-eighth floor. [[zeus-and-hera-familias|Zeus Familia]], which holds the record for the farthest floor reached, nicknamed that area of the Dungeon *The Dragon's Urn* (also printed *Dragon Urn*) because of "the valgang dragons inhabiting the fifty-eighth floor".[@valgang-dragon.so04-urn, wyvern.so04-ill] Like wyverns they have "enormous wings for forelimbs"; they bombard the floors above with fireballs that bore tunnels through them, and specialise in "long-range magic salvos", with only their tails for close combat, so the area in front of one is the safest place for an attacker.[@valgang-dragon.so04-close, wyvern.so04-ill] In Sword Oratoria 4 Lefiya, Bete and their group fall down "the shaft created by the valgang dragon", while Finn's group takes the standard route. Valgang-dragon fangs and scales were among the drop items Loki Familia delivered to [[hephaistos-familia|Hephaistos Familia]] afterwards, and Riveria's Veil Breath can protect an adventurer "from even a valgang dragon’s big fireballs".[@valgang-dragon.so12-shaft, valgang-dragon.so05-drops, valgang-dragon.so04-veil, valgang-dragon.so12-veil]

## Other monsters {#other-monsters}

{{nocite}} Species whose home floors the text does not pin down, or that belong to the corrupted spirit's brood.

### Violas {#violas}

**Violas** are carnivorous plant monsters with snakelike bodies, vivid flower heads and gaping jaws. They belong to the brood of the [[corrupted-spirit|corrupted spirit]], whose crystal-orb fetuses use them "as arms to collect magic stones".[@violas.so03-name, violas.so05-buds, violas.so06-arms]

- **Names:** Sword Oratoria 1 and 2 call them carnivorous plants, man-eating flowers and flower monsters. The name *violas* is first located in Sword Oratoria 3, where [[levis|Levis]] offers to "lend you some violas".[@violas.so01-plants, violas.so02-flowers, violas.so03-name]
- **Body:** their tails are thick and bulbous, with tendrils and root hairs like a tree's roots.[@violas.so02-tails] They fight with whip-like tentacles and body slams, and attack other monsters as well as adventurers.[@violas.so03-whips]
- **Magic stone:** a viola's magic stone lies deep in its throat, not in the chest as in most monsters.[@violas.so02-throat, so03-core]
- **Magic:** they respond to magic and attack its source at once. Blunt force does little to them, but they are weaker against sharp weapons and pinpoint attacks.[@violas.so03-aiz] When [[lefiya|Lefiya]] chants, they are "drawn to her like moths to a flame".[@violas.so03-lefiya]
- **Magic stones:** they share the caterpillars' type of magic stone. Thrown magic stones make them ignore adventurers and go for the stones.[@violas.so03-stones, violas.so03-thrown] On Floor 59, caterpillars and violas offer the magic stones in their mouths to the demi-spirit.[@violas.so04-offer]

### Viskum {#viskum}

The **viskum** are the huge flower-headed monsters that [[olivas-act|Olivas Act]] unleashes on the adventurers in a pantry in Sword Oratoria 3 ("Go, my viskum!"). Feeding on the "nutritious liquid" of the pantry's quartz tower, they have grown "to a scale that outclassed other monsters", with skin too thick for any but strong magic to pierce; Aiz beheads one with a single slash.[@viskum.so03-olivas, viskum.so03-grow] When the last one is left, it drinks the glowing red quartz dry and withers, sacrificing itself to flood the cavern with monsters.[@viskum.so03-sacrifice] See also [[#violas|Violas]] and [[corrupted-spirit|Corrupted spirit and demi-spirits]].

### Gargoyle {#gargoyle}

A **gargoyle** is a winged monster with a body of ash-coloured rock.[@gargoyle.fm09-gros] Its stone wings shield it from magic and from arrows, and serve as a blunt weapon; its claws are stone.[@gargoyle.fm09-shield, gargoyle.fm10-wings, gargoyle.fm11-plaza] In DanMachi 11 [[eina-tulle|Eina]] sees one prove "nearly impervious to long-range weapons", and suspects that it cannot be brought down without magic.[@gargoyle.fm11-plaza] It can hide among stone statues to ambush adventurers.[@gargoyle.fm11-statues]

Every gargoyle located in the checked text is [[gros|Gros]], one of the founding [[xenos|Xenos]] and leader of those who distrust surface people.[@gargoyle.fm09-gros, gargoyle.fm10-faction] One of his companions remarks that "Gargoyles like Gros are always stubborn as stone."[@gargoyle.fm10-stone] The floors where gargoyles appear and their drop items were not located in the checked text.

### Unicorn {#unicorn}

**Unicorns** are monsters "treated more like divine beasts, with beautiful, snow-white coats and a single prized horn". They are among "the most elusive denizens of the Dungeon", and many upper-class adventurers never see one; DanMachi 9 ranks the vouivre "On par with the unicorn" in rarity.[@unicorn.ss02-beasts, unicorn.fm09-rare] Their floors were not located in the checked text. The horn "was said to possess the ability to neutralize any poison": it is a rare drop item, prized for recovery items and sold at an exorbitant price.[@unicorn.ss02-beasts, unicorn.fm14-hakugen]

- **Unicorn-horn items:** [[welf-crozzo|Welf]] makes Bell's longknife Hakugen from unicorn horn (DanMachi 12), and in DanMachi 14 it draws the black poison from Bell's wound. [[fels|Fels]]'s hidden storeroom holds "a silvery-white cup made from a unicorn’s horn", and in Minor Myths and Legends 2 [[amid|Amid]] makes the Unicorn's Cup, which "can turn any poisoned or impotable fluid into clean, drinkable water".[@unicorn.fm12-hakugen, unicorn.fm14-hakugen, unicorn.fm11-cup, unicorn.ss02-cup]
- **On the surface:** in Minor Myths and Legends 2 unicorns that sought "a new life on the surface" have started a herd "deep in the sacred mountains". Amid of [[dian-cecht-familia|Dian Cecht Familia]] asks Aiz, Tiona, Tione and Lefiya to obtain a horn and, if possible, to let the unicorn live, since unicorns "are attracted to those pure of heart" and a trapped one may kill itself; in the end [[riveria|Riveria]] asks the unicorn for its horn, and it gives it freely.[@unicorn.ss02-beasts, unicorn.ss02-noble, unicorn.ss02-riveria] In Sword Oratoria 14 Riveria leaves the high-elf village on "the only tamed unicorn in the entire high elf village".[@unicorn.so14-riveria]
- **Xenos:** a unicorn is among the [[xenos|Xenos]]; in DanMachi 11 it fights in the vanguard with [[lido|Lido]].[@unicorn.fm11-xenos]

### Ogre {#ogre}

The **ogre** is a horned monster that the text mostly uses as a comparison: [[udaeus|Udaeus]]'s skull bears "two horns similar to an ogre’s", and Bell thinks the enhanced [[#moss-huge|moss huge]], with "hornlike pieces of wood" on its head, "probably closer to an ogre".[@ogre.so02-udaeus, moss-huge.fm12-ogre] In DanMachi 7 [[haruhime|Haruhime]] tells Bell that monsters attacked the carriage taking her from home and that the prum aristocrat with her "ran away from the horde of ogres"; marauders rescued her and sold her to Orario.[@ogre.fm07-haruhime] The legendary samurai of the [[far-east|Far East]], Issen Douji, "had fought a horde of over a thousand ogres in order to protect one little girl".[@ogre.fm07-issen] No ogre in the Dungeon was located in the checked text.

### Basilisk {#basilisk}

The **basilisk** is a giant serpent and "unmistakably a member of the dragon family", which breathes fire and spreads a paralyzing toxin "that seemed to petrify those afflicted by it". In ancient times basilisks destroyed countless cities, and tales of them are still told all over Kaios (see [[kaios-desert|Kaios Desert]]).[@basilisk.fc02-tales] In Familia Chronicle 2 a tamed basilisk is "Resheph’s trump card" in the war of [[ali|Ali]]'s story; it kills its own tamer, and [[ottar|Ottar]] splits it down the middle with one tremendous slash.[@basilisk.fc02-ottar]

### Arachne {#arachne}

The **arachne** is "the humanoid spider". The arachne of the [[xenos|Xenos]], [[xenos#ranieh|Ranieh]], has "the shapely upper body of a human female" but walks "with the many legs of a spider"; her red, humanlike eyes are unusual, because "arachnes typically had compound eyes".[@arachne.fm09-humanoid, arachne.fm10-ranieh] She ensnares enemies with spider webbing, and the acid of her poison leaves a trail that other Xenos can follow ("You reek of arachne acid!!").[@arachne.fm10-ranieh, arachne.fm10-acid] No arachne outside the Xenos was located in the checked text.

### Hippogriff {#hippogriff}

The only **hippogriff** located in the checked text is Cliff of the [[xenos|Xenos]], who "preferred to be airborne" and was "lighthearted and cheerful". In DanMachi 10 he travels with Ranieh's party, dives at Ikelos Familia's hunters "over and over from high above", and is brought down by a spell and killed with spears.[@hippogriff.fm10-cliff]

### Griffin {#griffin}

**Griffins** appear in the checked text only among the [[xenos|Xenos]]. In DanMachi 9 a griffin circles above the Hidden Village with a harpy and a gargoyle when Bell's party arrives; in DanMachi 10 a griffin's eyes smoulder "with anger that the hippogriff had been murdered", and when the Xenos run wild, a griffin catches Bell between jumps before a troll's club strikes them both.[@griffin.fm09-village, griffin.fm10-bell, griffin.fm10-wild]
