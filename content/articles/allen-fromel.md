**Allen Fromel** is a cat-person spearman of [[freya-familia|Freya Familia]], titled *Vana Freya*, and the Familia's second-in-command.[@fc02-allen, fm16-allen, fm16-second] He is the older brother of [[anya-fromel|Anya Fromel]], his only blood relation.[@fm16-allen] He is known as the fastest adventurer.[@fc01-fastest]

## Early life

Allen and Anya lost their parents and then their home, a country destroyed in a single night; Allen carried his crying sister through the ruins, known as the Scrap Heap. Two years later, when he was six, Freya found them, and Allen took her hand.[@fc02-past] In DanMachi 18 he reveals that a dragon destroyed their home.[@fm18-dragon]

In Freya Familia he adapted quickly to [[folkvangr|Folkvangr]] and reached Level 2 within a year. When he reached Level 3, Anya reached Level 2.[@fm17-past] After Anya forced her way onto a Deep Levels expedition that nearly killed them both, she was sent away from the Familia.[@fm17-past] DanMachi 18 reveals that Allen himself had asked Freya to take Anya off battlefields she could not survive and give her another family at the tavern, offering to fight for both of them.[@fm18-request]

## Levels

In Astrea Record 2, during the [[great-conflict|Great Conflict]], Allen is Level 5 and asks [[ottar|Ottar]] to be his doorway to a Level Up.[@ar02-allen] In Familia Chronicle 2 and Sword Oratoria 4 he is Level 6.[@fc02-allen, so04-allen]

## Magic {#magic}

{{nocite}} Allen Fromel's spells, with their incantations as printed in the English novels. The combined [[magic|Magic]] page describes every spell on this wiki together.

### Glarinese Fromel {#glarinese-fromel}

**Glarinese Fromel** is Allen Fromel's one and only magic.[@glarinese-fromel.fm18-effect] It gives him superspeed that keeps increasing the longer he runs, and his strength rises with his speed, so that the faster he goes the more destructive his charge becomes.[@glarinese-fromel.fm18-effect] He had never recited it in front of his sister, and [[anya-fromel|Anya]] learns in the DanMachi 18 Familia War that he has magic at all.[@glarinese-fromel.fm18-chant]

- **User:** Allen Fromel, his only magic[@glarinese-fromel.fm18-effect]
- **Effect:** Ever-increasing speed; power rises with speed[@glarinese-fromel.fm18-effect]
- **Chant:** Several lines, printed in full[@glarinese-fromel.fm18-chant]

#### Incantation {#glarinese-fromel-incantation}

**Complete printed chant (FM18).**[@glarinese-fromel.fm18-chant]

> Golden wheel, silver collar. Hated love, illusory corpse, thy destiny is here. Be gone, gold wheel, before the rut kills you. Whip of honor, lips of favor, thy payment is here. Spin, silver wheel, until thy head falls. Run bearing the goddess’s will—until death and distant heavens when you can hear the wheel’s song again.

{{nocite}} **Notes and other printed variants**

DanMachi 18 prints the chant from its first words, where Allen "began singing the chariot’s song", to its last lines and the name, with narration between the pieces.[@glarinese-fromel.fm18-chant] 

- **Opening:** "Golden wheel, silver collar."[@glarinese-fromel.fm18-chant]
- **Second line (excerpt):** "Hated love, illusory corpse, thy destiny is here."[@glarinese-fromel.fm18-chant]
- **Third line (excerpt):** "Whip of honor, lips of favor, thy payment is here."[@glarinese-fromel.fm18-chant]
- **Last lines (excerpt):** "Run bearing the goddess’s will—until death and distant heavens"[@glarinese-fromel.fm18-chant]
- **Name:** "Glarinese Fromel!"[@glarinese-fromel.fm18-chant]

The narration calls it a "disgraceful spell that reflected the depths of his heart" and what he truly felt toward his sister.[@glarinese-fromel.fm18-chant]

#### Effect {#glarinese-fromel-effect}

Allen is swathed in a silver-blue gleam and runs like a chariot across the battlefield, accelerating the more he runs.[@glarinese-fromel.fm18-chant] The magic massively raises his agility and gives him strength in proportion to his speed; the narration states there is no upper limit, so in theory the force of his charge grows as far as he can accelerate. Clad in "an armor of light", he could run down even a monster rex.[@glarinese-fromel.fm18-effect]

- **Casting while moving:** he chants while leaping backward again and again, putting tens of meders between himself and his attackers until the song is finished.[@glarinese-fromel.fm18-chant]
- **Control:** even Allen cannot control the speed perfectly; his swerving track sweeps up adventurers on both sides.[@glarinese-fromel.fm18-chant]

#### Limits {#glarinese-fromel-limits}

Glarinese Fromel has to be chanted, and Allen has to slow down to do it. On his second attempt in DanMachi 18 [[welf-crozzo|Welf]]'s [[magic#will-o-the-wisp|Will-o'-the-Wisp]] turns the gathering magic into an Ignis Fatuus before the spell completes.[@glarinese-fromel.fm18-second]
