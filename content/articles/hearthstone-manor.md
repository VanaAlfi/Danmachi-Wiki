**Hearthstone Manor** is the home of [[hestia-familia|Hestia Familia]] from DanMachi 6: the three-storey manor and gardens that belonged to [[apollo|Apollo]], which pass to Hestia after she wins the [[war-game|War Game]].[@fm06-manor] It is called Hearthstone Manor from DanMachi 7.[@fm07-hearthstone, fm08-hearthstone] Before it, the Familia lived in a basement room under an abandoned church.[@fm15-church] It stands in the city's sixth district.[@fm20-manor-district] Inside are a reception room, a study that still holds the books [[apollo-familia|Apollo Familia]] left behind, a spa-like bath on the third floor and a courtyard used for training, and [[welf-crozzo|Welf]]'s workshop stands in the rear garden.[@fm12-manor-rooms, fm09-manor-bath, fm14-manor-workshop]
