**Kokonoe** is [[haruhime|Haruhime]]'s second spell. It gives her tails of light and charges them with the effect of the spell she chants straight afterwards, so that she can release that spell as many times as she has tails with a single effort of will.[@fm12-kokonoe] With [[uchide-no-kozuchi|Uchide no Kozuchi]], this lets her give Level Boosts to several people at once.[@fm12-kokonoe]

## Status entry

Haruhime's DanMachi 15 card lists Kokonoe under Magic, beside Uchide no Kozuchi. It describes the spell as an enchantment that connects with a chant, charges tails with the effect of the connected spell, and allows up to nine tails.[@fm15-card]

## Tails

The spell can make up to nine tails, but in DanMachi 12 she can produce only five.[@fm12-kokonoe] After reaching Level 2 before the Familia War of DanMachi 18, she can handle six; each extra tail costs more Mind, and she can use fewer when she needs to.[@fm18-six]

| Volume | Use |
|---|---|
| DanMachi 12 | First use; five people boosted at once.[@fm12-kokonoe] |
| DanMachi 13 | Saves Mind by making only two boosts, for Welf and Ouka, against the lambton.[@fm13-two] |
| DanMachi 18 | Deploys five of her six tails to hold the line in the Familia War.[@fm18-six] |
| Sword Oratoria 12 | Sustains boosts on five fighters; her last tail raises the Level 6 Bete to pseudo-Level 7.[@so12-boost] |
