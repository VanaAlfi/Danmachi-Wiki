**Anakity Autumn** is a cat person of [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]], called Aki by her friends because her name is hard to pronounce.[@so04-aki] A Level 4 in Sword Oratoria 4, she leads the Familia's second-string members, linking the rank and file to the first-tier elite.[@so04-aki, so11-xenos] After the battles in [[knossos|Knossos]] she reaches Level 5 and becomes Loki Familia's eighth first-tier adventurer.[@so13-level] Her title is *Alsha*.[@so10-alsha]

## Appearance and character

Anakity has black hair and black-furred ears and tail.[@so04-aki, so04-tail] Sword Oratoria 4 describes her hair as waist-length in one passage and shoulder-length in another.[@so04-tail, so04-aki] Her eyes are as black as her hair.[@so11-xenos] Loki scouted her personally for her looks; Sword Oratoria 10 calls her a catgirl gifted with beauty and intelligence, and one of the best Level 4s in the city.[@so04-aki, so10-alsha]

[[raul-nord|Raul]] has always thought her a far better adventurer than himself: calm, with guts like iron, and always ready with advice.[@so04-aki] The two joined the Familia at around the same time.[@so05-joined, so13-level] She knows him best, which is why she is angrier than anyone when [[lilliluka-erde|Lilly]] fools him in Sword Oratoria 10 by disguising herself as [[finn-deimne|Finn]].[@so10-angry]

## The key to Knossos

In Sword Oratoria 10 Anakity sees through Lilly's disguise, and Finn changes his plan: Lilly, transformed into an armed monster, is made to run around carrying a replica key to draw out the [[evils|Evils]]' detached force.[@so10-detachment] When five of them come for it, Anakity cuts down three in the blink of an eye.[@so10-detachment] She tells Lilly she is "pretty angry" about Raul, smiles, says "this just makes us even", and lets her go.[@so10-angry] From the force's leader she takes a real Daedalus Orb, the key Loki Familia has been chasing since it fled Knossos, and hands it to [[riveria|Riveria]].[@so10-orb]

## Trusting the Xenos

When Finn tells the Familia in Sword Oratoria 11 that he trusts the [[xenos|Xenos]], Anakity asks whether he still does, given that some members have had friends, family or lovers killed by monsters. He says he does. She studies him, then says "Then I have nothing else to say" and sits down. Because she has agreed, the other members accept his decision.[@so11-xenos] The narration says only she could have won them over, as the head of the second-string forces; she respects the leaders but would defy them if their actions were illogical.[@so11-xenos]
