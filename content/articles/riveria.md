**Riveria Ljos Alf** is a high elf of royal blood and [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]]'s leading mage, called Orario's most powerful magic user.[@so01-riveria, fm05-leaders] The gods gave her the title *Nine Hell*.[@so03-titles] She is Level 7 from Sword Oratoria 14.[@so14-sevens]

## Early life

At seventy-one she left the elves' royal forest together with Aina, Eina Tulle's mother, accepted Loki's blessing while being pursued, and defeated a green dragon. She later helped recruit [[gareth|Gareth]].[@so14-riveria, so14-aina]

## Magic

Her spells come in three kinds, attack, defence and healing, each at three levels, and she can chain them together as the situation demands. These nine spells are the source of her title.[@so04-nine] Among them are Rea Laevateinn and Wynn Fimbulvetr.[@so01-riveria]

## Aiz's teacher

Riveria registered the seven-year-old [[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz]] and raised her as her main teacher and a parent. On Floor 12 she gave Aiz the trigger for her wind magic, told her she loved her, and made peace with her.[@so09-riveria]
