**Healers** treat wounded companions with healing magic, while ointments and bandages also serve to treat lighter injuries.[@so05-treatment] They normally work at the back of a group; pure healers are scarce, and their healing can reduce expenditure on items.[@so11-position, fm20-value]

## Work and limits {#work-and-limits}

[[amid|Amid Teasanare]], Orario's greatest healer, produced an anti-napalm healing solution, an item that extinguishes flames and heals burned skin.[@fm14-items] [[heith-velvet|Heith Velvet]] says her specialty is wounds and that curse removal is outside her abilities.[@fm17-specialty] After healing her broken leg herself, [[lyu-leon|Lyu Leon]] still needs a professional healer to correct the bone alignment.[@fm14-limit]
