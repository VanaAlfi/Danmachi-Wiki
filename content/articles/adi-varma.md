**Adi Varma** {{small|printed *Ardee* in Astrea Record 1 and 2}} is a [[races#human|human]] member of [[ganesha-familia|Ganesha Familia]] and the younger sister of [[shakti-varma|Shakti Varma]].[@ar01-identity, ar01-human] She serves in the city watch and is a Level 3 adventurer, one year older than [[lyu-leon|Lyu Leon]].[@ar01-identity, fc03-age] Her character sheet at the end of Astrea Record 2 lists two [[skills#adi-skills|Skills]] and two [[magic#adi-varma-spells|spells]].[@ar02-sheet]

## History {#history}

| Volume | Events |
|---|---|
| Astrea Record 1 | After the factory attack, she reports to Shakti Varma that the [[evils|Evils]] there have all been captured.[@ar01-arrests] She offers a thief forgiveness if he promises to stop committing crimes, despite Lyu Leon's insistence that he must be punished.[@ar01-forgiveness] During the sisters' raid on a smugglers' church, she enters first and discovers that the people inside have already been incapacitated.[@ar01-church] Searching the seized goods, she finds stolen [[holy-tree|branches]] she had promised to recover for Lyu Leon.[@ar01-branches] She later takes Lyu Leon's hand and leads her into a dance along the street.[@ar01-dance] While fighting the Evils, she lowers her sword and offers a child safety, but the child activates an explosive while holding her hand.[@ar01-death] |
| Astrea Record 2 | The man she spared saves Lyu Leon, who understands this as justice being passed onward.[@ar02-legacy] |
| Astrea Record 3 | Lyu Leon carries Adi's sword, Sacred Oath, into the final battle with Shakti Varma's permission.[@ar03-sword] |
| DanMachi 14 | [[gojouno-kaguya|Gojouno Kaguya]] challenges Lyu Leon's argument by asking why Shakti Varma's younger sister died.[@fm14-memory] |
| Familia Chronicle 3 | Seven years earlier, Adi lost her life in the conflict between justice and evil.[@fc03-teaching] In a dream of a twilight wheat field, Adi and Lyu Leon fall into the wheat together, laugh, and share an embrace.[@fc03-embrace] At twilight, Adi taught Lyu Leon that justice passes onward, words that supported Lyu Leon's heart.[@fc03-teaching, ar02-legacy] Lyu Leon believes her new [[skills#astrae-varmas|Astrae Varmas]] reflects her inheritance of Adi's justice.[@fc03-teaching] |
