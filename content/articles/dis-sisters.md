The **Dis sisters**, **Dina Dis** and **Vena Dis**, are Level 5 elf sisters who lead [[apate-and-alecto-familias#alecto-familia|Alecto Familia]].[@ar01-identity] The elder sister Dina is a golden-haired [[races#white-elf|white elf]], while the younger Vena is a silver-haired [[races#dark-elf|dark elf]].[@ar01-identity] Dina is the Familia's captain and Vena its vice-captain; each bears a tear-shaped tattoo, one on the right side and one on the left.[@ar01-dis-intro] Alecto Familia is one of two groups thought extreme even among the [[evils|Evils]], and the sisters are infamous for killing anyone who displeases them, friend or foe.[@ar01-dis-intro, ar03-dis-amphitheatrum] Obsessed with the purity of the elves yet unable to meet it, they profess a twisted love for [[hegni|Hegni Ragnar]] and [[hedin|Hedin Selrand]] while trying to kill them, and only through each other do they cling to sanity.[@ar01-dis-intro, ar03-dis-end]

## Dina Dis {#dina-dis}

Dina fights at close range with two stiletto daggers, while Vena supports her from a distance.[@ar01-weapons] Her curse [[magic#dialv-stige|Dialv Stige]] strengthens their complementary fighting roles.[@ar03-battle] It takes half the Strength and Agility of the one she touches and pays for it with an equal share of her own Defense and Magic; with it each sister nears Level 6, Dina in close combat and Vena in magic.[@ar03-dis-end] In Astrea Record 3, [[hegni|Hegni Ragnar]] cuts her down and severs her head while [[hedin|Hedin Selrand]] supports him with lightning.[@ar03-dina] Having given her Magic and Defense to Vena, she cannot withstand Hedin's lightning, and Hegni's blow cuts her from shoulder to hip.[@ar03-dis-end]

## Vena Dis {#vena-dis}

Vena uses magic and a [[magic-sword|magic sword]] for ranged fighting.[@ar01-weapons] In Astrea Record 1, Dina supplies power as Vena releases [[magic#dialv-dis|Dialv Dis]] against the cathedral and churches sheltering civilians.[@ar01-churches] Prepared in advance, the spell sends down four columns of fire; Hedin saves only the cathedral, and civilians burn in the other churches.[@ar01-dis-crossroads] Her third and last spell sets fire to whatever she sees and judges heretical, cannot be dodged and leaves Dina unharmed.[@ar03-dis-end] At the Amphitheatrum, Hedin, who has swapped swords with Hegni, thrusts Hegni's cursed sword Victim Abyss through her magic circle and her breast, and her spell explodes in an Ignis Fatuus.[@ar03-dis-end] In Astrea Record 3, Vena survives the initial magical explosion through the Defense shared by Dina, but Hedin Selrand kills her with lightning after Dina's death.[@ar03-vena] Before his lightning ends her, she picks up Dina's severed head, throws it away crying that so dirty a thing cannot be her sister, and laughs as she weeps.[@ar03-dis-end]
