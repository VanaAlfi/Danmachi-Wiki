**Floor 58** of the [[dungeon|Dungeon]] is a single hall in the deep levels, at the bottom of the [[dungeon#deep-floors|Dragon's Urn]].[@so04-hall, so04-dragons] Its graphite-colored walls and ceiling form a rectangular room without a maze or partitions blocking the view.[@so04-hall] [[monsters#valgang-dragon|Valgang dragon]] monsters occupy the floor and bombard adventurers above through holes opened by their fireballs.[@so04-dragons]

| Sword Oratoria 7 | [[gareth|Gareth Landrock]] recalls falling in the Dragon's Urn from [[floor-52|Floor 52]] onward and uses fall time to estimate his position.[@so07-memory] |
| Sword Oratoria 12 | The account recalls Floor 58 as the meeting place for [[finn-deimne|Finn Deimne]]'s group and the adventurers who fell down the shaft four months earlier.[@so12-memory] |
