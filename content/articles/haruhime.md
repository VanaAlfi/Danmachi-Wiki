**Haruhime** is a renart from the Far East and a sorceress whose spell [[#uchide-no-kozuchi|Uchide no Kozuchi]] temporarily raises another person by a Level.[@fm07-name, fm07-history] Held by Ishtar Familia until DanMachi 7, she then joins [[hestia-familia|Hestia Familia]].[@fm07-join]

## Name

DanMachi 7 prints her name in the Far Eastern order, *Sanjyouno Haruhime*; later volumes print *Haruhime Sanjouno*.[@fm07-name, fm15-name] This wiki uses *Haruhime*, as the novels mostly do.

## Ishtar Familia

Haruhime was disowned at eleven, five years before DanMachi 7, and ended up with Ishtar Familia in [[pleasure-quarter|the Pleasure Quarter]]. [[mikoto|Mikoto]] knew her as a child in the Far East.[@fm07-history] [[ishtar|Ishtar]] plans to seal her magic and her soul in a [[killing-stone|Killing Stone]], a forbidden magic item made by combining a tamamo stone and a toba stone.[@fm07-history, fm07-stone] In Sword Oratoria 6 she is Ishtar Familia's veiled caster, whose unnamed boosting spell raises Phryne's strength.[@so06-caster]

[[bell-cranell|Bell]] breaks the Killing Stone and rescues her. After Ishtar is sent back to the heavens, Haruhime asks to join Hestia Familia.[@fm07-rescue, fm07-join]

## Hestia Familia

She joins Bell's party as a supporter and sorceress, and in DanMachi 8 secretly boosts Mikoto during the fight with the [[goliath|Goliath]].[@fm08-party] In DanMachi 9 she becomes the closest carer, after Bell, of the [[xenos|Xenos]] girl [[wiene|Wiene]], and she embraces Wiene again when they are reunited.[@fm09-wiene, fm11-wiene]

In DanMachi 15 her Status qualifies her for Level 2, but Hestia holds the promotion back on [[aisha-belka|Aisha]]'s advice, without telling her.[@fm15-held] She has levelled up by the Familia War of DanMachi 18.[@fm18-level2] In DanMachi 20 the former [[freya-familia|Freya Familia]] members stand guard around [[hearthstone-manor|Hearthstone Manor]] to protect her and [[lyu-leon|Lyu]].[@fm20-guards]

## Magic

| Spell | Effect |
|---|---|
| [[#uchide-no-kozuchi|Uchide no Kozuchi]] | Raises one other person by a Level. At Level 1 it lasts up to fifteen minutes with enough Mind, with a recast interval of a little over ten minutes; at Level 2 it lasts twenty minutes.[@fm09-duration, fm14-interval, fm20-twenty] |
| [[#kokonoe|Kokonoe]] | Gives her tails of light that hold the effect of the next spell, so she can boost several people at once. Her Status allows up to nine tails; she can make five in DanMachi 12 and six after reaching Level 2.[@fm12-kokonoe, fm18-level2] |

Her DanMachi 15 card lists both spells under Magic, and one Skill, [[skills#mikuzume-no-hou|Mikuzume no Hou]], which makes her magic more effective and her use of Mind more efficient.[@fm15-card]

In Sword Oratoria 12 she sustains Level Boosts on five fighters at once, and her last tail raises the Level 6 [[bete-loga|Bete]] to pseudo-Level 7.[@so12-boost]

### Uchide no Kozuchi {#uchide-no-kozuchi}

**Uchide no Kozuchi**, usually called **Level Boost**, is Haruhime's magic. It temporarily raises another person's [[level|Level]]; in every case shown, the target fights with the power of one Level higher.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-named, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm11-rescue, uchide-no-kozuchi.so12-five] It affects one person at a time and cannot be cast on Haruhime herself.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm15-limits]

- **Caster:** Haruhime[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-named]
- **Known as:** Level Boost[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-named, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm09-duration]
- **Effect:** Raises one person's [[level|Level]] for a limited time[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-named, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm15-limits]
- **Target:** One person, not Haruhime herself[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm15-limits]
- **Chant:** Two lines with the refrain "Grow"[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-chant, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm12-kokonoe]
- **Duration:** Up to 15 minutes, given enough Mind[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm09-duration, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm15-limits]
- **Recast:** About 10 minutes[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm11-rescue, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm15-limits]
- **Duration:** 20 minutes (DanMachi 18)[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm18-level2]
- **Recast:** 9 minutes (DanMachi 18)[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm18-level2]
- **First:** Sword Oratoria 6 (unnamed); named in DanMachi 7[@uchide-no-kozuchi.so06-phryne, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-named]

#### Name {#uchide-no-kozuchi-name}

The spell is named *Uchide no Kozuchi* from DanMachi 7 onward; *Level Boost* is the name used for its effect in DanMachi 7–9, and DanMachi 14 writes it in lower case.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-named, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm09-duration, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm14-boost] Sword Oratoria 6 shows the spell without naming it.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.so06-phryne]

#### Incantation {#uchide-no-kozuchi-incantation}

**Complete printed chant (FM07).**[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-chant]

> —Grow. That power and that vessel. Breadth of wealth and breadth of wishes. Until the bell tolls, bring forth glory and illusion. —Grow. Confine divine offerings within this body. This golden light bestowed from above. Into the hammer and into the ground, may it bestow good fortune upon you. —Grow.

{{nocite}} **Notes and other printed variants**

{{nocite}} The chant is printed with the same wording in every full print. 

- **Structure:** Haruhime begins with "—Grow.", recites two lines, each followed by "Grow", and then names the spell.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-chant, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm12-kokonoe]
- **Excerpt (first line):** "That power and that vessel. Breadth of wealth and breadth of wishes."[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-chant, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm12-kokonoe, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm18-chant]
- **Second line:** asks that divine offerings be confined in the body and that the golden light, sent into the hammer and the ground, bring good fortune.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-chant]
- **Release:** "Uchide no Kozuchi."[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-chant, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm11-hammer]

| Volume | What is printed |
|---|---|
| DanMachi 7 | The full sequence: three "Grow"s, both lines and the name. It is the first print of the chant.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-chant] |
| DanMachi 11 | The opening "—Grow.", then narration of the chant, then the name.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm11-hammer] |
| DanMachi 12 | The full sequence, straight after [[#kokonoe|Kokonoe]]'s chant, then "Dance!" to send the charged tails out.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm12-kokonoe] |
| DanMachi 14 | Only "Grow. Uchide no Kozuchi!"[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm14-boost] |
| DanMachi 18 | The full sequence after Kokonoe, ending "Uchide no Kozuchi—Dance!"[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm18-chant] |

As she chants, a cloud of golden light forms above her and becomes a hammer of light, which falls on the target. DanMachi 11 notes that she forms no magic circle, a sign of the spell's peculiar nature.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-chant, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm11-hammer] Casting it through Kokonoe is described as linking two spells' chants: DanMachi 12 calls this "Concatenated Casting", and DanMachi 18 "chained casting".[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm12-kokonoe, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm18-chant]

#### Effects {#uchide-no-kozuchi-effects}

{{inference}} Every boost shown lifts the target by exactly one Level, though no passage states this as a rule.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.so06-phryne, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-named, uchide-no-kozuchi.so12-five] In DanMachi 7 it raises [[bell-cranell|Bell]] from Level 3 to Level 4 power, and gives [[aisha-belka|Aisha]] Level-4-class strength.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-named] In Sword Oratoria 12 it lifts Level 4 adventurers to pseudo-Level 5 and [[bete-loga|Bete]] from Level 6 to pseudo-Level 7.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.so12-five]

Once cast, the boost lasts until its time runs out or Haruhime ends it; knocking her unconscious does not stop it.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-chant]

The boost is temporary and does not count as a Level Up. In DanMachi 18, [[anya-fromel|Anya Fromel]], a Level 4, fights at pseudo-Level 5 during the [[war-game|War Game]] only.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm18-anya]

Fighting under the boost costs the target experience: they receive less than half the normal excelia, while Haruhime's own development is not reduced by casting it.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm15-excelia]

#### Limits {#uchide-no-kozuchi-limits}

The limits changed as Haruhime grew. At Level 1 a boost lasts up to fifteen minutes if she supplies enough Mind, and she must wait about ten minutes before casting it again.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm09-duration, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm15-limits] Because the wait is shorter than the effect, a second person can be boosted for about five minutes while the first boost is still running; this does not show that two boosts stack on one person.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm15-limits]

After Haruhime reaches Level 2, before the War Game in DanMachi 18, a boost lasts twenty minutes and the wait falls to nine minutes.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm18-level2]

In DanMachi 20, [[nina-tulle|Nina]]'s [[magic#magia-kreis|Magia Kreis]] extends a boost from twenty minutes to about twenty-five. That is an outside enhancement, not a change to the spell itself.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm20-kreis]

| Haruhime's Level | Duration | Wait before recasting |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Up to 15 minutes with enough Mind[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm09-duration, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm15-limits] | About 10 minutes[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm11-rescue, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm15-limits] |
| 2 (from DanMachi 18) | 20 minutes[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm18-level2] | 9 minutes[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm18-level2] |

> [!NOTE] An early estimate
> The earliest description suggested a boost lasted about a minute. Later experiments in DanMachi 9 established the longer duration, so the one-minute figure should not be treated as the spell's limit.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-named, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm09-duration]

#### With Kokonoe {#uchide-no-kozuchi-with-kokonoe}

Haruhime's second spell, [[#kokonoe|Kokonoe]], stores the effect of a spell chanted afterwards in magical tails and releases it through them, so she can boost several people at once.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm12-kokonoe] She can use five tails in DanMachi 12, boosting [[mikoto|Mikoto]], [[ouka|Ouka]], [[welf-crozzo|Welf]], [[daphne|Daphne]] and Aisha together, and six after reaching Level 2.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm12-kokonoe, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm18-level2] The printed maximum is nine tails; each extra tail costs more Mind, and she can choose to use fewer.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm12-kokonoe]

{{nocite}} Notable uses and open questions for Uchide no Kozuchi are on the combined page: [[magic#uchide-no-kozuchi|Magic § Uchide no Kozuchi]].

### Kokonoe {#kokonoe}

**Kokonoe** is Haruhime's second spell. It gives her tails of light and charges them with the effect of the spell she chants straight afterwards, so that she can release that spell as many times as she has tails with a single effort of will.[@kokonoe.fm12-kokonoe] With [[#uchide-no-kozuchi|Uchide no Kozuchi]], this lets her give Level Boosts to several people at once.[@kokonoe.fm12-kokonoe]

- **User:** Haruhime[@kokonoe.fm12-kokonoe]
- **Type:** Enchantment, listed under Magic[@kokonoe.fm15-card]
- **First used:** DanMachi 12[@kokonoe.fm12-kokonoe]
- **Source:** A [[grimoire|grimoire]][@kokonoe.fm12-chant]
- **Chant:** Name first, then five printed pieces[@kokonoe.fm12-chant, kokonoe.fm18-chant]
- **Maximum:** Nine[@kokonoe.fm12-kokonoe, kokonoe.fm15-card]
- **Usable:** Five in DanMachi 12; six at Level 2[@kokonoe.fm12-kokonoe, kokonoe.fm18-six]

#### Status entry {#kokonoe-status-entry}

Haruhime's DanMachi 15 card lists Kokonoe under Magic, beside Uchide no Kozuchi. It describes the spell as an enchantment that connects with a chant, charges tails with the effect of the connected spell, and allows up to nine tails.[@kokonoe.fm15-card]

#### Incantation {#kokonoe-incantation}

**Name first, then complete printed chant; followed by Uchide no Kozuchi (FM12).**[@kokonoe.fm12-chant]

> Kokonoe. Beloved snow. Beloved crimson. Beloved white light. Please let me be beside you—this love I have found at the end of two thousand nights. My name is Magic Fox, former destroyer. My name is Ancient Song, former dreamer. For you who beat your wings like a bird, I shall allow the nine spirits to dwell within me. Echo song of gold, sacred poem of Tamamo. White face, golden fur, king of nine tails. Oh tails of the auspicious beast, consume all, grant all wishes—

{{nocite}} **Notes and other printed variants**

{{nocite}} Kokonoe's chant is printed in full twice, with the same wording. 

- **Name first:** unusually, the spell "began not with a chant but with the announcement of the spell's name": Haruhime says "Kokonoe." and then chants.[@kokonoe.fm12-chant]
- **Excerpt (first piece):** "Beloved snow. Beloved crimson. Beloved white light."[@kokonoe.fm12-chant, kokonoe.fm18-chant]
- **The rest:** four more printed pieces, in which she calls herself Magic Fox and Ancient Song, lets the nine spirits dwell in her, and invokes the golden song of Tamamo and the tails of the auspicious beast.[@kokonoe.fm12-chant, kokonoe.fm18-chant]
- **Linking:** the last piece runs straight into the "—Grow." that begins [[#uchide-no-kozuchi|Uchide no Kozuchi]]'s chant. When that spell is complete, "Dance!" sends the charged tails out to their targets.[@kokonoe.fm12-chant, kokonoe.fm18-chant]

| Volume | What is printed |
|---|---|
| DanMachi 12 | The full sequence: name, five pieces, Uchide no Kozuchi's chant, "Uchide no Kozuchi!", "Dance!"[@kokonoe.fm12-chant] |
| DanMachi 14 | Only the name, "Kokonoe!"[@kokonoe.fm14-name] |
| DanMachi 18 | The full sequence, in front of the whole city, ending "Uchide no Kozuchi—Dance!"[@kokonoe.fm18-chant] |

DanMachi 12 calls this linking of two chants "Concatenated Casting"; DanMachi 18 calls it "chained casting". It is different from [[riveria|Riveria]]'s Concatenated Chanting, which joins levels of one spell; see [[magic#casting-techniques|Magic]].[@kokonoe.fm12-chant, kokonoe.fm18-chant]

#### Tails {#kokonoe-tails}

The spell can make up to nine tails, but in DanMachi 12 she can produce only five.[@kokonoe.fm12-kokonoe] After reaching Level 2 before the Familia War of DanMachi 18, she can handle six; each extra tail costs more Mind, and she can use fewer when she needs to.[@kokonoe.fm18-six]

| Volume | Use |
|---|---|
| DanMachi 12 | First use; five people boosted at once.[@kokonoe.fm12-kokonoe] |
| DanMachi 13 | Saves Mind by making only two boosts, for [[welf-crozzo|Welf]] and [[ouka|Ouka]], against the lambton.[@kokonoe.fm13-two] |
| DanMachi 18 | Deploys five of her six tails to hold the line in the Familia War.[@kokonoe.fm18-six] |
| Sword Oratoria 12 | Sustains boosts on five fighters; her last tail raises the Level 6 [[bete-loga|Bete]] to pseudo-Level 7.[@kokonoe.so12-boost] |
