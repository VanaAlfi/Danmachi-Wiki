**Gran** belongs to [[ikelos-familia|Ikelos Familia]] and serves under [[ikelos-familia#dix-perdix|Dix]].[@fm10-identity-1, fm10-identity-2] He is a large human with a bald head and a black facial tattoo, and fights with a greatsword.[@fm10-identity-1, fm10-identity-2]
