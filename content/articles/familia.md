A **Familia** is a god's faction whose members receive that god's [[falna|Blessing]] and provide support, with membership, leadership and obligations illustrated throughout the novels.[@fm01-definition] [[bell-cranell|Bell]] describes a Familia as a faction formed around a god, whose mortal members receive the god's Blessing and provide for that deity.[@fm01-definition] He regards joining one as joining the god's family; this family comparison is his own explanation of the relationship.[@fm01-definition]

## Membership and Status {#membership-and-status}

A member's [[status|Status]] is inscribed with [[ichor|divine blood]], and the god updates the writing to reflect growth.[@fm01-status]

[[falna#conversion|Conversion]] is a ceremony of renewed contract that transfers a follower from one Familia to another, as [[soma|Soma]] and [[hestia|Hestia]] do for [[lilliluka-erde|Lilly]].[@fm06-conversion]

A transferred follower must wait one year before another Conversion; a faction that loses all its members can no longer call itself a Familia.[@fm06-conversion, fm06-empty]

[[bell-cranell|Bell]]'s [[guild|Guild]] profile includes Familia affiliation alongside race and personal history.[@fm01-guild]

## Relations between Familias {#relations-between-familias}

A War Game provides a conflict governed by rules and a promised reward to the winner.[@fm06-war]

[[bell-cranell|Bell]] explains that hostility between gods makes their Familias enemies and that members must avoid endangering their faction.[@fm01-relations]
