**Floor 59** of the [[dungeon|Dungeon]] lies below [[floor-58|Floor 58]], whose southern end has a large hole opening on a long staircase down to it.[@so04-floor59-glacial, so04-floor59-nocold] By the records of [[zeus-and-hera-familias|Zeus Familia]], the Glacial Territories begin there, but [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]] finds a jungle on the floor in Sword Oratoria 4.[@so04-floor59-glacial, so04-floor59-jungle] It was unreached territory, beyond the farthest Loki Familia had been, when the Familia made it the goal of its expedition.[@so04-floor59-plan]

## Landscape and climate {#landscape-and-climate}

At the hole [[finn-deimne|Finn]] recalls Zeus Familia's records, [[hyrute-sisters|Tione]] adds that they tell of glacial streams and a cold that slows the body, and [[raul-nord|Raul]] has brought [[equipment#salamander-wool|salamander wool]].[@so04-floor59-glacial] But no cold air comes up from the hole, and on the stairs the air is humid instead.[@so04-floor59-nocold]

Floor 59 itself holds no ice but a jungle: an enclosed room larger than Floor 58, full of trees, vines, grass and poisonous-looking flowers.[@so04-floor59-jungle] A single road crosses the jungle, under a phosphorescent ceiling several tens of meders high.[@so04-floor59-jungle] Beyond the jungle lies a hall of ash, the remains of countless monsters, at whose centre caterpillars and [[monsters#violas|violas]] offer magic stones to a woman with a giant plantlike lower half.[@so04-floor59-hall]

In Loki Familia's battle with this [[corrupted-spirit|demi-spirit]] the jungle burns away, leaving only ash around her.[@so04-floor59-battle, so04-floor59-ouranos] She summons masses of caterpillars and violas through a great hole behind her that leads down to the lower floors.[@so04-floor59-battle] From the floor's transformed terrain, environment and ecosystem, [[ouranos|Ouranos]], who watched through a crystal [[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz]] carried, concludes that the enemy's true form lies deeper, on [[floor-60|Floor 60]] or below.[@so04-floor59-ouranos]
