**Phobos** is the goddess of Phobos Familia, which includes the Crozzo family under [[ares|Ares]]'s rule.[@fm15-patron] She is a goddess who looks after the Crozzo family and serves under Ares.[@fm15-patron] The family bears her [[falna|Falna]] as Phobos Familia, while [[welf-crozzo|Welf Crozzo]] views her as an annoying older companion rather than an object of worship.[@fm15-patron, fm15-companion]
