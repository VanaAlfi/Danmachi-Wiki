**Idun** {{small|printed *Iðunn* in DanMachi 20 and Sword Oratoria 13}} is a goddess who presides over youth and teaches at the [[school-district|School District]].[@fm18-youth, fm19-teaching] She has long blond hair and begins an Ancient History lesson by encouraging the students to celebrate youth.[@fm19-teaching] Within the School District, her Familia is called Iðunn Class, and students receive their respective deities' [[falna|Falna]].[@so13-class]
