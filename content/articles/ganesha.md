**Ganesha** is the god of [[ganesha-familia|Ganesha Familia]], whose members act as Orario's city watch.[@fm18-watch] Known as the "God of the Masses", he hides his face behind an elephant mask, answers almost anything with "I am Ganesha!", and puts the safety of the city's people before his Familia's reputation.[@fm01-masses, fm08-trusted] He stages the yearly [[monsterphilia|Monsterphilia]], and he is one of the few gods whom [[ouranos|Ouranos]] has told about the [[xenos|Xenos]].[@fm09-informed, fm10-xenos]

## Appearance and manner

Ganesha is a handsome, dark-skinned god with black hair and a tall, toned body, but what people notice first is the elephant mask that hides his face.[@fm01-statue, fm08-looks] Sword Oratoria 9 calls him "swarthy and muscle-bound", and DanMachi 20 *the elephant god*.[@so09-float, fm20-hermes] His voice booms: [[hermes|Hermes]] asks him to keep it down because he is noisy.[@fm01-celebration, fm20-hermes] He strikes strange poses and introduces himself again and again; on a parade float in Sword Oratoria 9 he does so for the twenty-seventh time.[@fm08-looks, so09-float] Asked why he shouts, Astrea Record 1 says, he would only answer "I am Ganeshaaaaa!!": "At the end of the day, there is no reason."[@ar01-shout]

The gods find him tiring. [[loki|Loki]] and [[freya|Freya]] leave him out of a tea with [[astrea|Astrea]] "Because he’s annoying", and when he chairs a meeting of the [[denatus|Denatus]] one god threatens to go home.[@ar01-annoying, fm12-chair] [[hestia|Hestia]], who knew him in Tenkai, does her best to tolerate him.[@fm08-hestia] The people of the city are fond of him: DanMachi 8 calls him one of the most bizarre deities in Orario and also one of the most trusted.[@fm08-trusted] Discussing who could have released the Monsterphilia's monsters, Loki rules him out at once: "He loves kids."[@so02-loki]

Behind the noise he can be serious. When the Monsterphilia's monsters escape he hears the report calmly and gives his orders in a low voice, and when Hestia raises the Xenos with him his comical manner "instantly disappeared".[@fm01-calm, fm10-serious]

In the Japanese originals of DanMachi 1, 8 and 18 the title is {{ja|群衆の主|Gunshū no Aruji}}, literally *lord of the masses*, written with the reading *Ganesha*.[@fm01-ja-title, fm08-ja-title, fm18-ja-home]

## Iam Ganesha {#iam-ganesha}

Ganesha Familia's home stands in the south-west of Orario, near the trading post. A tall white fence surrounds open grounds, and in the centre sits a giant statue of a man in an elephant mask, with his arms and legs crossed.[@fm10-home] Ganesha built it with his Familia's savings; its members tear up as they pass in and out, and the entrance is in the statue's crotch.[@fm01-statue, fm18-watch] Inside are a great hall and, in Sword Oratoria 1, a ballroom for the gods' parties.[@fm01-celebration, so01-banquet] The grounds are a pasture where tamers break monsters from the Dungeon. Ganesha spends his spare moments there playing with them "To find the meaning of true friendship!", and an infant dragon he is scratching nearly bites him.[@fm10-pasture, fm10-dragon] In Astrea Record 3, during the [[great-conflict|Great Conflict]], his home is one of five strongholds where the city's residents shelter.[@ar03-strongholds]

The name is printed *Iam Ganesha* in DanMachi 10 and 11 and Astrea Record 3, the most frequent form, which this page uses; DanMachi 1 prints *Aiam Ganesha* and DanMachi 18 *I Am Ganesha*.[@fm01-statue, fm10-home, fm11-shakti, ar03-iam, fm18-watch] The Japanese originals of DanMachi 1 and 18 write the English words "I am Ganesha" in katakana, his own catchphrase: DanMachi 18 as {{ja|アイ・アム・ガネーシャ|Ai Amu Ganēsha}}, DanMachi 1 without the first dot.[@fm01-ja-home, fm18-ja-home]

## Parties and the Denatus

In DanMachi 1 Ganesha holds a *Celebration of the Gods* in his home. His Familia is prominent enough for him to invite every god and goddess in the city. He greets them from a stage, dressed to match his statue, and reminds them that his Familia's yearly festival is three days away; most of the gods ignore him.[@fm01-celebration] Sword Oratoria 1 shows a party at his home from Loki's side and calls it a *Banquet of the Gods*: a ballroom under a magic-stone chandelier, food from distant mountains and oceans and from the Dungeon, and a dance. Gods there remark that most of Orario's gods come to Ganesha's Banquets, and that he must entertain lavishly because he is asking the gods to cooperate with the Monsterphilia.[@so01-banquet, so01-guests] Both parties fall a few days before the Monsterphilia, so they are very probably the same event. {{inference}} [[takemikazuchi|Takemikazuchi]] knows of only one god who prefers to hold Celebrations at home: Ganesha.[@fm06-home]

He sits in his elephant mask among the gods at DanMachi 4's Denatus, and chairs DanMachi 12's. There the briefing is over almost before it begins, which depresses Ganesha, "who had devoted himself day and night to maintaining the peace in Orario"; like Hestia, he says nothing about the Xenos.[@fm04-denatus, fm12-chair]

## The Monsterphilia and the Xenos

When monsters escape during DanMachi 1's Monsterphilia, Ganesha orders his Familia to pursue them and to ask the other Familias and the gods in the stadium for help. To a follower worried about the Familia's reputation he answers "I am Ganesha, God of the Masses! I cannot allow any of the citizens to be harmed!", and he keeps the show going to avoid a panic.[@fm01-masses] Because he puts safety first, [[eina-tulle|Eina]] dares to ask other Familias for help without fear of offending him.[@fm01-eina]

The festival is not his own idea. In DanMachi 9 Ouranos tells Hestia that, apart from himself, only Hermes and Ganesha know about the Xenos; he told Ganesha to gain his support, and could only win his confidence by revealing his divine will.[@fm09-informed] In DanMachi 10 Ganesha explains to Hestia that Ouranos put him in charge of the Monsterphilia, whose name truly means *friendship with monsters*. He had not believed Ouranos until [[fels|Fels]] smuggled a goblin in a red hat out of the Dungeon to meet him and he heard it speak; after that he went along with everything.[@fm10-xenos] Asked what will happen, he answers "To be blunt, I don’t know." But if monsters truly desire peace, he will stop being the God of the Masses and become "Neo Ganesha, God of All Beings". For the first time, Hestia thinks he is cool.[@fm10-neo]

When the [[armed-monsters|armed Xenos]] break loose on [[floor-18|Floor 18]] in DanMachi 10, Ouranos directs his "trusted ally" Ganesha to settle the matter quickly and in secret, and the Guild orders Ganesha Familia to tame the monsters rather than kill them. Shakti accepts the order as her god's will: "Our faith lies with him, and we follow."[@fm10-ally, fm10-lord] Ganesha also sees [[ikelos|Ikelos]] off at the city gate when the Guild exiles him: "I never thought you’d be the one to see me off, Ganesha." "It is because…I am Ganesha."[@fm10-ikelos]

In DanMachi 11 he visits Shakti, who is recovering in his home, and apologises for his "unreasonable request". He tells her that the road to Neo Ganesha is a difficult one and that he is still "man of the people": his Familia will stop searching for the Xenos and will protect the city's people instead.[@fm11-shakti, fm11-neo] During the battle in [[central-park|Central Park]] he orders his followers to help the unconscious adventurers and put the evacuation of the townsfolk first.[@fm11-rescue]

At a Denatus in Sword Oratoria 5 he apologises for the debacle and swears "on the name of Ganesha, Lord of Hosts" that the violas have nothing to do with his Familia.[@so05-oath] He asks for help to avenge his follower [[hashana|Hashana Dorlia]], killed on Floor 18, and weeps; then it turns out he believes Hashana died in a woman's arms, which Loki corrects.[@so05-hashana] In Sword Oratoria 11, when [[ilta-faana|Ilta]] wants revenge for Hashana, he tells his followers: "We don’t have to be avengers. We’ll continue to be the shield protecting the masses."[@so11-avengers]

## Commentator

At the [[war-game|War Game]] of DanMachi 6 Ganesha joins his follower Ibly Archer, "the chattering fireball himself", also known as "the Fire Inferno Flame", on a stage in front of Guild headquarters as guest commentator. He adds nothing but "—I am Ganesha!" and his poses, and Ibly tells him to go home if he has nothing to say.[@fm06-commentary, fm06-poses] His Familia does not fight in DanMachi 18's war against [[freya-familia|Freya Familia]], and he commentates again. Asked whether the rules favour the [[familia-coalition|coalition]], he answers seriously: "Even that’s not enough of a handicap."[@fm18-watch, fm18-handicap] In DanMachi 20 he commentates the [[orariad|Orariad]] and calls Ilta and Shakti, the winners of its third round, "my beloved followers". For a round held aboard the School District's ship, he and Ibly are the only commentators allowed on board; later he shares the commentators' table with Hermes.[@fm20-commentary, fm20-hermes]

## Hestia at the gate

In DanMachi 8, while Ganesha Familia is called out against Rakia, Ganesha rides up to the north gate and lets Hestia through to fetch herbs for [[jyaga-maru-kun|Jyaga Maru Kun]] on his own authority, over his bodyguards' protests.[@fm08-gate] When Rakia's soldiers capture her, Loki berates him and tells him to go and get a grilling from the Guild; he then leads the merchants and citizens away from the staging area.[@fm08-loki, fm08-evacuate]

## The dark age

In Astrea Record, set in Orario's dark age, his Familia and [[astrea-familia|Astrea Familia]] keep order in the city, "Following the will of their god, the Lord of Hosts".[@ar01-watch] Day after day he goes out into the streets to raise the townsfolk's spirits with his voice; people tell him to shut up, then thank him.[@ar01-shout] When the [[evils|Evils]] attack the city he stands "at the forefront of battle, inspiring the people", and Hermes says he is taking "a leaf out of Ganesha’s book".[@ar01-front] Sword Oratoria 9's recollections of the same era show him buying a parade float to cheer up frightened children, to Loki's dismay at the cost, and greeting [[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz]], "the rumored Doll Princess".[@so09-float, so09-evils]

In Astrea Record 2 he wails beside Astrea that he has failed his children: "That I, the god of the masses, can do nothing but scream and holler!!"[@ar02-failed] When Shakti, who has lost her sister [[adi-varma|Adi]] {{small|printed *Ardee* in Astrea Record 1 and 2}}, asks him whether she was wrong, he tells her that she "chose a future for these people over honoring the fallen".[@ar02-shakti] Later, when she says she cannot cry yet, he weeps loudly for Adi in her place; when she then says the rain is coming, he agrees and turns his back to her.[@ar02-ardee] In Astrea Record 3 he watches the fighting from the battlements of his home and shouts "Never give up!!" during the final battle.[@ar03-battlements, ar03-cheer] In Sword Oratoria 12, when Freya remarks that it has been a while since a battle involving all of Orario, he answers: "Yes, ever since the conflict with the Evils."[@so12-evils]
