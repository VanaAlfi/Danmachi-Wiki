**Lett** is a red-cap [[monsters#goblin|goblin]] among the [[xenos|Xenos]] who helps [[bell-cranell|Bell Cranell]] and his companions.[@fm14-rescue, fm11-key] He fights with a battle-ax larger than his body would suggest and disguises himself in a robe.[@fm14-rescue] He speaks fluent human language.[@so12-faces]
