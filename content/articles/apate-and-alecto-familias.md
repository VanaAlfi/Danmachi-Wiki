**Apate Familia** and **Alecto Familia** are the two Familias that Astrea Record 1 calls extreme even by the standards of the [[evils|Evils]].[@ar01-alecto] Apate Familia, which has the Evils' strongest forces, is led in the Dark Age by the priest [[basram|Basram]] and fields "spirit warriors" made from captured adventurers.[@ar01-basram, ar01-spirit] Alecto Familia is led by the elf sisters [[dis-sisters|Dina and Vena Dis]].[@ar01-alecto, ar03-twins] Together they form the bulk of the Evils' strike force in the [[great-conflict|Great Conflict]], and in Astrea Record 3 [[freya-familia|Freya Familia]] breaks them and kills their leaders, and [[finn-deimne|Finn]] wipes out the survivors.[@ar03-strike, ar03-report, ar03-finn]

## Apate Familia {#apate-familia}

Apate, Basram's goddess, has injustice as her domain.[@ar01-apate] Astrea Record 1 says the Familia's forces were the strongest among the Evils, on a par with Alecto Familia's. By then Freya Familia had already killed its captain, vice-captain and all its notable officers, so Basram, a past-middle-aged animal person in a black and purple priest's habit, has to lead from the front.[@ar01-basram] The [[gulliver-brothers|Gulliver brothers]] call him Apate's old man (written *priest*) and hate him.[@ar01-basram] Before them he proclaims the Familia's creed: they are Apate's apostles, the holy brethren of a heresy who will repaint the world with injustice.[@ar01-creed]

Astrea Record 2 calls the spirit warriors "the backbone of the Evils’ elite forces": without them [[valletta|Valletta]] and the other Evils could not "initiate any large-scale maneuvers", so the fighting had been "mostly limited to skirmishes". Basram himself, "an older yet well-built animal man", admits that "there is no substitute for a purebred first-class adventurer", which goes against every teaching of his [[patron-deity|patron goddess]], Apate.[@ar02-backbone] In Astrea Record 3, when the battle at the Amphitheatrum turns against him, he drops his "gentle facade" in fury. His ringed staff is "the only means of controlling" the warriors: after his death an officer carries it off with the survivors, and their acting leader uses it to set the last four spirit warriors on [[finn-deimne|Finn]].[@ar03-facade, ar03-staff]

### The spirit warriors

Basram's warriors are former members of [[minor-familias#osiris-familia|Osiris Familia]], which had fought [[zeus-and-hera-familias|Zeus Familia and Hera Familia]] more than twenty years earlier, before the Dark Age, and lost. Their second-tier members converted from their patron Osiris, who had been banished from the city, to Apate Familia in the hope of revenge. When Zeus and Hera fell to the Black Dragon and that hope died, Basram turned them into mindless soldiers with drugs and curses rather than let them leave.[@ar01-osiris] He then drove daggers holding captured spirits into the backs of their necks, trying to reproduce the spirits' blessings that heroes received before the gods came. He regrets having had no great spirits for this.[@ar01-spirit] Of forty-two spirits and thirty-four warriors, only twelve spirit warriors survived the infusion and their training in the Dungeon. All twelve had reached Level 5, could heal their own wounds and threw fire and lightning without chanting at the ring of his staff.[@ar01-spirit]

They need regular tuning by skilled mages and [[hexers|hexers]] after every battle. Without it, body and spirit reject each other, and they end in complete loss of control.[@ar02-tuning]

## Alecto Familia {#alecto-familia}

Dina Dis is the captain and Vena Dis the vice-captain.[@ar01-alecto] They are elf sisters, Dina the elder with fair skin and Vena the younger with tanned skin.[@ar03-twins] Astrea Record 1 calls them broken: they take pleasure only in their victims' suffering, and they rival Valletta in the number of adventurers and townspeople they have killed.[@ar01-alecto] They are feared for combining Dina's close-range blades, a pair of stilettos, with Vena's long-range magic, and their own subordinates know that they kill anyone who displeases them, friend or foe.[@ar01-hegni, ar03-strike] Their spells are on the Magic page: Vena's [[magic#dialv-dis|Dialv Dis]], Dina's curse [[magic#dialv-stige|Dialv Stige]], and [[magic#dialv-otua|Dialv Otua]].

In Astrea Record 3 Dina's curse lets the sisters share their abilities, which brings them close to the power of two Level 6s.[@ar03-dina] At the Amphitheatrum, Hedin and Hegni secretly swap weapons. Hedin lures Vena into casting her third spell, then, with Hegni's [[cursed-weapons|cursed sword]] [[equipment#victim-abyss|Victim Abyss]], makes a fifty-meder thrust that pierces her magic circle and her breast, so that the spell backfires on her. Hegni, holding Hedin's rhomphaia, cuts Dina down. Vena survives the explosion only because of the Defense she took from her sister, and Hedin's lightning destroys her.[@ar03-swap, ar03-dis, ar03-vena]

## Defeat

Valletta's officers report that Apate Familia and Alecto Familia have both taken heavy losses from Freya Familia, and that the Dis sisters and Basram are dead.[@ar03-report] As the survivors head south to join Valletta, [[finn-deimne|Finn]] ambushes them and wipes out both Familias' remaining forces.[@ar03-finn]
