The **Dis sisters**, **Dina Dis** and **Vena Dis**, are Level 5 elf sisters who lead [[apate-and-alecto-familias#alecto-familia|Alecto Familia]].[@ar01-identity] The elder sister Dina is a golden-haired [[races#white-elf|white elf]], while the younger Vena is a silver-haired [[races#dark-elf|dark elf]].[@ar01-identity]

## Dina Dis {#dina-dis}

Dina fights at close range with two stiletto daggers, while Vena supports her from a distance.[@ar01-weapons] Her curse [[magic#dialv-stige|Dialv Stige]] strengthens their complementary fighting roles.[@ar03-battle] In Astrea Record 3, [[hegni|Hegni Ragnar]] cuts her down and severs her head while [[hedin|Hedin Selrand]] supports him with lightning.[@ar03-dina]

## Vena Dis {#vena-dis}

Vena uses magic and a [[magic-sword|magic sword]] for ranged fighting.[@ar01-weapons] In Astrea Record 1, Dina supplies power as Vena releases [[magic#dialv-dis|Dialv Dis]] against the cathedral and churches sheltering civilians.[@ar01-churches] In Astrea Record 3, Vena survives the initial magical explosion through the Defense shared by Dina, but Hedin Selrand kills her with lightning after Dina's death.[@ar03-vena]
