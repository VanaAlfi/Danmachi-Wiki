**Monsters** are the creatures of [[dungeon|the Dungeon]] that adventurers fight. Each has a [[magic-stone|magic stone]] as its core, and destroying the stone turns the whole monster to ash.[@so03-core] This page collects what the novels say about monsters in general, indexes the species, and gives a section to each species that has no page of its own.

## Monsters in general {#general}

- **Levels:** the Guild rates monsters by Level. The al-miraj, weak alone but "surprisingly dangerous in groups", are "classified as Level 2 monsters"; the Guild rates lizardman elites between Level 3 and Level 4.[@fm05-rating, lizardman.fm14-elite] A rampaging vouivre is called "a rare large-category monster".[@vouivre.fm10-large]
- **Upper and middle levels:** the monsters of the upper levels attack only at close range, with claws and clubs. In the middle levels some have learned to attack from a distance, and DanMachi 5 says the difference in intelligence between upper- and middle-level monsters is "worlds apart".[@fm05-middle]
- **Nature weapons:** the Dungeon supplies weapons that monsters pick up, such as tree-stump clubs, stone tomahawks and the metal flowers of the twentieth floor, which serve as shield and blades.[@fm09-nature] See [[al-miraj|Al-Miraj]] and [[#lizardman|Lizardman]].
- **Monster against monster:** monsters tend to attack anything they see as a threat, other monsters included.[@fm05-other] Some species prefer other monsters' magic stones: see [[#violas|Violas]] and [[corrupted-spirit|Corrupted spirit and demi-spirits]].[@violas.so03-stones]

## Upper levels {#upper-levels}

{{nocite}} Floors 1 to 12. The sections run roughly from the shallowest floors to the deepest.

### Goblin {#goblin}

Goblins are "The weakest monster in the entire Dungeon", as Hestia puts it; DanMachi 15 calls the goblin a creature "whose name was synonymous with weakness".[@goblin.fm04-weakest, goblin.fm15-haruhime]

- **Where:** the first four floors, with their pale blue walls, usually hold only goblins and kobolds. On floors 8 and 9, stronger goblins and kobolds appear rather than new species.[@goblin.fm02-floors, goblin.fm02-eighth]
- **Drops:** in DanMachi 1 Bell picks up a goblin fang as a drop item.[@goblin.fm01-fang]
- **On the surface:** a goblin nearly killed Bell as a child. He supposes it descended from goblins that escaped the Dungeon before the Guild existed, since monsters breed out in the world. His grandfather saved him armed with a garden hoe.[@goblin.fm01-child, goblin.fm03-gramps]
- **Firsts:** right after joining Hestia Familia, Bell slew a goblin and ran back to tell Hestia. A goblin is also the first monster he hits with [[magic#firebolt|Firebolt]].[@goblin.fm04-joined, goblin.fm04-first, goblin.fm02-firebolt] In DanMachi 15 [[haruhime|Haruhime]]'s clumsy attack on a lone goblin goes wrong, and a gang of goblins beat her until Lilly flying-kicks them away.[@goblin.fm15-haruhime]

### Kobold {#kobold}

Kobolds are dog-headed monsters that usually wander alone or in pairs and attack with sharp claws and fangs. Bell does not think them "too bright".[@kobold.fm01-kobolds]

- **Strength:** in DanMachi 1 Bell has "only recently got strong enough to slay kobolds". Their magic stones are mere shards.[@kobold.fm01-strong, kobold.fm01-shard]
- **Drops:** when Bell takes a kobold's shard, one sharp claw stays behind instead of turning to ash: a drop item.[@kobold.fm01-shard]
- **Where:** with goblins, on the first four floors, and in stronger forms on floors 8 and 9.[@goblin.fm02-floors, goblin.fm02-eighth]

### Dungeon lizard {#dungeon-lizard}

A four-legged lizard with scaly brown skin, as long as Bell is tall including its tail. It appears on floors 2 to 4 and is in the "same class as goblins and kobolds".[@dungeon-lizard.fm01-lizard]

### War shadow {#war-shadow}

DanMachi 1 prints it as the **Wall Shadow**, "the lower-sixth-floor monster", and DanMachi 16 once as *woe shadows*, but the Japanese originals of both volumes use the same word as everywhere else, {{ja|ウォーシャドウ|Wō Shadō}}, *war shadow*; *Wall* and *woe* are Yen Press's variants.[@war-shadow.fm01-wall, war-shadow.fm01-ja, war-shadow.fm16-woe, war-shadow.fm16-ja] Other volumes print *war shadow*.[@war-shadow.fm10-orde]

- **Arms:** its long arms end in knife-like fingers. DanMachi 1 gives it three; DanMachi 10 gives a war shadow five.[@war-shadow.fm01-fingers, war-shadow.fm10-orde]
- **Danger:** in DanMachi 1 it is the most dangerous monster of the sixth floor "in terms of pure power", and adventurers of the upper floors must be careful when one appears. Like the killer ant, it is called a "newbie killer".[@war-shadow.fm01-fingers, killer-ant.fm02-ant]
- **Weakness:** in Eina's quiz it has high attack and high speed, but defence "roughly comparable to that of a goblin or a kobold".[@war-shadow.ss01-quiz]
- **Drops:** a finger blade.[@war-shadow.fm01-drop]
- **Xenos:** Orde, a war shadow, scouts for [[xenos#ranieh|Ranieh]]'s party in full plate armour, so that he passes for an adventurer. He dies when the hunters ambush them in DanMachi 10.[@war-shadow.fm10-orde]

### Frog shooter {#frog-shooter}

A frog monster with one giant eye that shoots out a long tongue to attack adventurers; Yen Press's DanMachi 1 prints "bulb-eyed" and "spear-like".[@frog-shooter.fm01-ja, frog-shooter.fm01-frog] Frog shooters are "barely stronger" than goblins and kobolds, but in Sword Oratoria 4 a swarm surrounds [[lefiya|Lefiya]] while [[filvis|Filvis]] trains her.[@frog-shooter.so04-lefiya] In DanMachi 7 Bell likens a shock to "getting licked by a frog shooter".[@frog-shooter.fm07-licked]

### Killer ant {#killer-ant}

A **killer ant** looks like a giant red ant with four legs, two thin arms and two big eyes; it stands as tall as Bell when it rears up. It first appears on the lower seventh floor and is called a "newbie killer".[@killer-ant.fm02-ant]

- **Armour and claws:** its skin "might as well be armor", and four curved claws hang from its arms. The recommended way to kill one is to strike the soft flesh at the joints between its shells.[@killer-ant.fm02-ant, killer-ant.fm02-joints]
- **Size:** killer ants are human-sized, unlike the small purple moths and needle rabbits found with them.[@killer-ant.fm02-size]
- **Calling for help:** close to death, a killer ant releases a pheromone that calls other killer ants. [[soma-familia#kanu|Kanu]] uses a dying one to bring a swarm down on Lilly in DanMachi 2.[@killer-ant.fm02-pheromone]

### Purple moth {#purple-moth}

**Purple moths** spread poisonous wing-scales in battle. The effect is slow, but breathing in too much poisons the body.[@purple-moth.fm02-scales] There are so many in the upper levels that many adventurers have breathed their spores, which makes the [[development-ability|Development Ability]] Immunity relatively easy to acquire.[@purple-moth.fm04-immunity] Their drop item is the Purple Moth Wing ({{ja|パープル・モスの翅|Pāpuru Mosu no hane}}; the Yen Press text prints "Purple Moth Scales"), and the poisonous scale powder collected from it goes into Lilly's fast-acting poison powder and Bell's poison grenades.[@purple-moth.fm03-drop, purple-moth.fm03-ja-drop, purple-moth.fm19-grenades]

### Needle rabbit {#needle-rabbit}

A small horned rabbit; Bell blocks a needle rabbit's tusk with his knee plate on the seventh floor.[@needle-rabbit.fm02-rabbit] Needle rabbits are "small" monsters, met alongside killer ants, and one strays as far as the tenth floor.[@killer-ant.fm02-size, needle-rabbit.fm02-tenth] Bell calls the [[al-miraj|al-miraj]] "needle rabbits that learned how to walk on two legs".[@needle-rabbit.fm05-almiraj]

### Orc {#orc}

The **orc** is a large-category monster: about three meders tall, with brown skin, a boar's head and old hide round its waist, squat and fat rather than muscled like a Minotaur.[@orc.fm02-orc]

- **Where:** large-category monsters first appear on the tenth floor; there are none down to the ninth.[@orc.fm02-tenth]
- **Arms:** an orc pulls a dead tree out of the floor and swings it as a club.[@orc.fm02-club]
- **Strength:** a direct hit keeps a Level 1, or even a Level 2, down for a long time. But its weight makes it slow, and it easily loses its balance.[@orc.fm04-strong, orc.fm02-slow]
- **Birth:** in DanMachi 4 Bell watches an orc break out of the wall. From around the tenth floor many monsters can emerge in the same room at once, a "monster party".[@orc.fm04-birth]

### Imp {#imp}

**Imps** are little demons (the Yen Press text has them flying; the Japanese DanMachi 3 shows them rushing and hopping on the ground and has no word for flight). They look like goblins, but they are clever: they never attack one at a time, and they use the fog of the tenth floor to surround a target from every side. On that floor a pack of imps is said to be more troublesome than a single orc (the Yen Press text says "more feared than even the gigantic orcs").[@imp.fm03-imps, imp.fm03-ja-imps] Lilly's purple powder sets them coughing violently (the Yen Press text has them fall out of the air).[@imp.fm03-powder, imp.fm03-ja-powder] In DanMachi 19 imps happen to be near the infant dragon that Bell's school squad must hunt.[@imp.fm19-dragon]

### Bad bat {#bad-bat}

A dark-coloured, bat-type monster with sharp fangs and eerie sound waves that break an adventurer's concentration (the Yen Press text says "a black bat–type monster with sharp claws and a really distracting scream").[@bad-bat.fm03-bat, bad-bat.fm03-ja-bat] Bad bats have "hardly any raw combat ability", but their shrieks disrupt adventurers' movements; they lurk in the shadows of the Cave Labyrinth.[@bad-bat.so13-shriek] In DanMachi 5 a swarm is born from a tunnel ceiling.[@bad-bat.fm05-swarm] In Sword Oratoria 13 a mass spawning of bad bats around the fifteenth floor brings a ceiling down near the [[balder-class#seventh-squad|7th Squad]].[@bad-bat.so13-collapse]

### Silverback {#silverback}

**Silverbacks** look like massive gorillas covered in thick white fur. They are named for the silver mane round the neck and the silver stripe down the back, and they first appear on the lower eleventh floor.[@silverback.fm04-look, silverback.fm01-floor] They are smaller than orcs but powerful and agile, and with the Hard Armoreds they make the lower eleventh floor very dangerous for Level 1s.[@silverback.fm04-look] The al-miraj are more agile still.[@silverback.fm05-agile] Bell fights one at the [[monsterphilia|Monsterphilia]] of DanMachi 1.[@silverback.fm04-look]

### Hard Armored {#hard-armored}

**Hard Armoreds** are armadillo-like monsters with "the best defense in the upper levels". They curl into a ball and roll at adventurers at speed, and most blades bounce off them rolled up; their underbelly is unprotected.[@hard-armored.fm05-mikoto, hard-armored.fm04-armored] They first appear on the eleventh floor, and are met in the middle levels too.[@hard-armored.fm04-armored, hard-armored.fm04-middle] DanMachi 4 says Level 1s need Basic Abilities of B to S to survive the lower eleventh and twelfth floors because of them.[@hard-armored.fm04-armored] [[mikoto|Yamato Mikoto]]'s small shield is made from their plating.[@hard-armored.fm05-mikoto]

### Infant dragon {#infant-dragon}

The **infant dragon** is a wingless, four-legged dragon with thick amber scales and red eyes: only about 150 celch tall, but over 4 meders long.[@infant-dragon.fm04-dragon]

- **Rarity:** it appears only on the lower eleventh and twelfth floors. DanMachi 4 says only four or five roam the Dungeon at a time; DanMachi 19 says there are usually not even five on the twelfth floor.[@infant-dragon.fm04-rare, infant-dragon.fm19-rare]
- **The upper levels' boss:** with no [[monster-rex|Monster Rex]] on these floors, infant dragons are the effective floor bosses, and they have wiped out parties of Level 1s.[@infant-dragon.fm04-rare, infant-dragon.so09-aiz]
- **DanMachi 4:** on the eleventh floor, Bell destroys one with a charged Firebolt, despite dragons' natural resistance to flame. Asfi later reports it to Hermes.[@infant-dragon.fm04-firebolt, infant-dragon.fm05-asfi]
- **Sword Oratoria 9:** in her early years, Aiz goes into the Dungeon alone and fights infant dragons, to [[riveria|Riveria]]'s fury.[@infant-dragon.so09-aiz]
- **DanMachi 10:** the god Ganesha of [[ganesha-familia|Ganesha Familia]] approaches a tamed infant dragon, fitted with a magic item, inside a fence, and it snaps at his mask.[@infant-dragon.fm10-tamed]
- **DanMachi 19:** the [[school-district|School District]] sets an infant dragon's drop item as a grade requirement, and the [[balder-class#third-squad|3rd Squad]] finally wins an infant dragon fang.[@infant-dragon.fm19-grade, infant-dragon.fm19-fang]
- **Nidhogg:** the demi-spirit [[corrupted-spirit#nidhogg|Nidhogg]] grew from a crystal-orb fetus attached to an infant dragon that Levis's group brought up from the deep floors.[@infant-dragon.so12-nidhogg]

## Middle levels {#middle-levels}

{{nocite}} From the thirteenth floor down. For the Floor 37 monsters, see also [[floor-37|Floor 37]] and [[colosseum|Colosseum]].

### Hellhound {#hellhound}

**Hellhounds**, also called Baskervilles, are doglike monsters of the middle levels: black, heavily muscled and four-legged, with deep red eyes.[@hellhound.fm05-baskerville, hellhound.fm05-look]

- **Fire:** they are not especially strong for the middle levels; the danger is the fire they spit. It can crack good armour, and a pack's inferno can reduce a party to ash. When a party is wiped out on the thirteenth or fourteenth floor, hellhounds are most likely to blame.[@hellhound.fm05-fire] No monster of the upper levels breathes fire like them.[@hellhound.fm05-wisp]
- **Tells:** before spitting fire, a hellhound raises its hindquarters, lowers its head, and shows sparks between its teeth.[@hellhound.fm05-spit]
- **Defences:** hellhounds are the main reason [[eina-tulle|Eina]] insists on salamander wool for Bell's party.[@hellhound.fm05-baskerville] Being fire-breathers, they resist fire magic unusually well.[@hellhound.fm05-resist] [[welf-crozzo|Welf]]'s [[magic#will-o-the-wisp|Will-o'-the-Wisp]] makes their own fire explode in their mouths.[@hellhound.fm05-wisp] [[asfi|Asfi]] glues their mouths shut with sticky vials before they can fire.[@hellhound.fm05-asfi]
- **Drops:** a quest posted in DanMachi 4 asks for "Hellhound Fangs x10".[@hellhound.fm04-fangs]
- **Company:** they often hunt alongside [[al-miraj|al-miraj]]. In Sword Oratoria 13 Lefiya singles out their fire breath as a threat to her squad.[@hellhound.fm07-pack, hellhound.so13-lefiya] Among the Xenos, the hellhound Helga carries the al-miraj [[al-miraj#aruru|Aruru]].[@hellhound.so12-helga]

### Vouivre {#vouivre}

Vouivres are a species of dragon, which DanMachi 9 calls "the greatest monsters in the Dungeon". Bell remembers the vouivre as one of the rarest of rare monsters, "On par with the unicorn". It is said to appear between the nineteenth and twenty-fourth floors, in the [[colossal-tree-labyrinth|Colossal Tree Labyrinth]].[@vouivre.fm09-rare, vouivre.fm09-berserk]

- **Form:** a vouivre usually has a woman's upper body and a snakelike lower body, like a lamia; "women attached to a dragon tail". It has a red jewel in its forehead.[@vouivre.fm09-form, vouivre.fm09-rare]
- **Senses:** like other dragons, vouivres have particularly acute senses, such as hearing.[@vouivre.fm10-senses]
- **Drop items:** a vouivre's scales and claws fetch "tremendous sums". Its forehead gem, the **Vouivre's Tear**, is worth far more; adventurers call it the "Prosperity Stone".[@vouivre.fm09-rare]
- **The jewel and the rage:** taking the jewel from its forehead sends a vouivre berserk, and killing it shatters the jewel, so many adventurers have died trying to get one.[@vouivre.fm09-berserk] Sword Oratoria 10 adds that a vouivre that loses the stone rages until it gets it back.[@vouivre.so10-rage]

The Xenos [[wiene|Wiene]] is a vouivre. Because she is a girl in form, Bell wonders whether she is a subspecies.[@vouivre.fm09-form] In DanMachi 10 the hunter [[ikelos-familia|Dix]] tears the jewel from her forehead. She turns into a rampaging dragon with a dragon's tail, and yet shows no interest in Dix, who holds her stone.[@vouivre.fm10-dix, vouivre.fm10-rage, vouivre.so10-tail] Her story is on [[wiene|her own page]].

### Lizardman {#lizardman}

**Lizardmen** are red-scaled monsters, about 170 celch tall, that stand upright and fight with weapons in both hands. Bell's party meets them on the twentieth floor, in the Colossal Tree Labyrinth, and for the first time Bell feels he is fighting other adventurers.[@lizardman.fm09-twentieth]

- **Arms:** the twentieth floor's lizardmen carry nature weapons: metal flowers that grow from the walls. With the stem removed, the flower is a round shield fifty celch across, and its petals, called "cutters", serve as daggers.[@lizardman.fm09-flowers] On the twenty-fourth floor, in Sword Oratoria 3, three lizardmen challenge Aiz with a hardened flower for a shield and its thorny stem as a shortsword.[@lizardman.so03-aiz]
- **Lizardman elite:** "a higher-rank version of the lizardmen" of the Colossal Tree Labyrinth, with blue scales "as hard as armor" and two axes of milky white stone. The Guild rates them between Level 3 and Level 4, and they specialise in close combat.[@lizardman.fm14-elite] They are among the warrior-type monsters of [[floor-37|Floor 37]], where Bell and Lyu fight them, and they fill the [[colosseum|Colosseum]].[@lizardman.so02-elite, lizardman.fm14-elite, lizardman.fm14-colosseum] On Floor 37 in Familia Chronicle 2, [[ottar|Ottar]] cuts them down with a single slash each.[@lizardman.fc02-ottar]
- **Xenos:** [[lido|Lido]], leader of the [[xenos|Xenos]], is a scarlet lizardman with a longsword and a scimitar. Fighting him in Sword Oratoria 10, Tione murmurs that he "doesn't seem like your regular lizardman".[@lizardman.so10-lido]

Sword Oratoria 2 calls the elite "an upgraded form of the lizardman monster" and says it "first appeared on the nineteenth floor". The sentence does not make clear whether that refers to the elite or to the ordinary lizardman.[@lizardman.so02-elite] {{inference}}

## Other monsters {#other-monsters}

{{nocite}} Species whose home floors the text does not pin down, or that belong to the corrupted spirit's brood.

### Violas {#violas}

**Violas** are carnivorous plant monsters with snakelike bodies, vivid flower heads and gaping jaws. They belong to the brood of the [[corrupted-spirit|corrupted spirit]], whose crystal-orb fetuses use them "as arms to collect magic stones".[@violas.so03-name, violas.so05-buds, violas.so06-arms]

- **Names:** Sword Oratoria 1 and 2 call them carnivorous plants, man-eating flowers and flower monsters. The name *violas* is first located in Sword Oratoria 3, where [[levis|Levis]] offers to "lend you some violas".[@violas.so01-plants, violas.so02-flowers, violas.so03-name]
- **Body:** their tails are thick and bulbous, with tendrils and root hairs like a tree's roots.[@violas.so02-tails] They fight with whip-like tentacles and body slams, and attack other monsters as well as adventurers.[@violas.so03-whips]
- **Magic stone:** a viola's magic stone lies deep in its throat, not in the chest as in most monsters.[@violas.so02-throat, so03-core]
- **Magic:** they respond to magic and attack its source at once. Blunt force does little to them, but they are weaker against sharp weapons and pinpoint attacks.[@violas.so03-aiz] When [[lefiya|Lefiya]] chants, they are "drawn to her like moths to a flame".[@violas.so03-lefiya]
- **Magic stones:** they share the caterpillars' type of magic stone. Thrown magic stones make them ignore adventurers and go for the stones.[@violas.so03-stones, violas.so03-thrown] On Floor 59, caterpillars and violas offer the magic stones in their mouths to the demi-spirit.[@violas.so04-offer]

### Gargoyle {#gargoyle}

A **gargoyle** is a winged monster with a body of ash-coloured rock.[@gargoyle.fm09-gros] Its stone wings shield it from magic and from arrows, and serve as a blunt weapon; its claws are stone.[@gargoyle.fm09-shield, gargoyle.fm10-wings, gargoyle.fm11-plaza] In DanMachi 11 [[eina-tulle|Eina]] sees one prove "nearly impervious to long-range weapons", and suspects that it cannot be brought down without magic.[@gargoyle.fm11-plaza] It can hide among stone statues to ambush adventurers.[@gargoyle.fm11-statues]

Every gargoyle located in the checked text is [[gros|Gros]], one of the founding [[xenos|Xenos]] and leader of those who distrust surface people.[@gargoyle.fm09-gros, gargoyle.fm10-faction] One of his companions remarks that "Gargoyles like Gros are always stubborn as stone."[@gargoyle.fm10-stone] The floors where gargoyles appear and their drop items were not located in the checked text.
