**Central Park** is the open space at the heart of [[orario|Orario]], around [[babel|Babel]] Tower and the entrance to the [[dungeon|Dungeon]]. All eight of the city's main streets meet there.[@fm06-streets, fm02-babel] It makes "a perfect circle" with the white tower at its centre, planted with trees and set with fountains, and even full of adventurers it never feels crowded.[@fm02-park]

## The park {#park}

- **Adventurers:** most people in Central Park carry big swords and long spears. Adventurers stream in from every main street on their way to the Dungeon, and pour out of Babel at the end of the day.[@fm02-park, so03-circle, fm03-killing]
- **Meeting place:** Bell and [[lilliluka-erde|Lilly]] meet in a corner near Babel, under a broad-leaved tree whose brick planter is just the right height to sit on.[@fm04-spot]
- **Clock:** a large clock stands in Central Park.[@so01-clock]
- **A measure of size:** the Dungeon's fifth floor is said to be about as wide as Central Park, and the floors below it wider.[@fm03-size, fm10-size, fm02-floors] Sword Oratoria 12 calls the area around the Dungeon's entrance "a little bigger than Central Park".[@so12-size]
- **Gatherings:** tens of thousands of people can comfortably gather there.[@fm16-gather]

## The Great Conflict {#great-conflict}

{{nocite}} See [[great-conflict|The Great Conflict]] for the war as a whole.

- **Astrea Record 1:** when the Evils attack, [[loki|Loki]] moves her Familia to Central Park, and [[freya|Freya]] sends hers there too. [[finn-deimne|Finn]] has a perimeter raised, and lower-class adventurers build barricades from broken planks and empty ale casks.[@ar01-base]
- **Astrea Record 2:** Central Park becomes the allies' forward base and true headquarters, with Guild Headquarters second. On the night before the final battle, every Familia gathers there to hear Finn speak.[@ar02-base, ar02-guild, ar02-speech]
- **Astrea Record 3:** layers of ice encircle Central Park, and the foot of Babel becomes the most fortified place in the city.[@ar03-ice, ar03-fortress] When [[zald|Zald]] reaches it and meets [[ottar|Ottar]], mages on the rooftops raise barriers that trap him inside; most of the city's forces had gathered there to deal with Zald and [[alfia|Alfia]].[@ar03-barrier, ar03-forces] The first duel ends with Ottar on one knee, but the sound of fighting from Central Park starts up again: "Ottar still stands".[@ar03-duel, ar03-stands] The Evils then drive their monsters at Central Park, and a ring of silver and black light tears apart every monster that approaches.[@ar03-monsters, ar03-ring]
- **The end:** the defeated conqueror lies in Central Park, just south of Babel's gates. Later crowds pack it to see their foe's execution.[@ar03-aftermath, ar03-execution]
