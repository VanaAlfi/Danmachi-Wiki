**Maris Hackard** is a human adventurer in DanMachi 15's account of [[eina-tulle|Eina Tulle]]'s early years at [[guild|the Guild]]: the first adventurer Eina took responsibility for as an adviser.[@fm15-meeting] The two became friends, and Maris reached the tenth floor within a year, but she was killed in the Dungeon with her party; she was the first of Eina's adventurers to die.[@fm15-friends, fm15-tenth, fm15-death]

## Eina's first adventurer

Maris was a fifteen-year-old human girl with short-cropped blue hair and "a somehow imperious expression", slender and, at 160 celch, slightly taller than Eina; she had just registered, and her Familia was "just getting started".[@fm15-meeting] Their first consultation turned into a shouting match. Maris complained that she had asked for "a real tough-looking old dwarf" and got a rookie; Eina retorted that Maris was a rookie herself; Maris declared, "I’m gonna be a first-tier adventurer, got it?"[@fm15-meeting]

## Friendship

Over the following year they became "rather good friends", still arguing, now with "enjoyable banter". Eina had four adventurers by then, and Maris's progress was "a sight to behold": she came to tell Eina, "Eina! I reached the tenth floor!", though Eina had warned her about the fog there.[@fm15-friends, fm15-tenth] (See [[upper-levels#floors-10-12|floors 10–12]].) For "essentially a brand-new familia", reaching the tenth floor within a year was impressive progress.[@fm15-tenth]

That evening Maris dragged Eina to The Flaming Wasp, a tavern she frequented in the southern quarter's shopping district, and over its ruby-red mead thanked her: "I was lucky. To get you as my adviser, I mean." "I’m real glad I met you."[@fm15-tenth, fm15-friends] Asked why she became an adventurer, she said: "I wanna show ’em all. My parents, for throwing me away. Everyone who ever made fun of me. I wanna become somebody amazing, like a first-tier adventurer." She had been a street urchin, and she also wanted to do it for her god, "for picking me up outta the dirt".[@fm15-reason]

## Death

About a month later, Eina, on duty in Babel, saw the bodies of a wiped-out party carried up from the Dungeon by upper-class adventurers who had found them. One was Maris, in battered leather armour, a broken sword in her hand; her body was missing an arm. Onlookers guessed at an infant dragon, but the cause was not settled. It was a year since Eina had become an adviser, and Maris was the first of her adventurers to die.[@fm15-death] (See [[monsters#infant-dragon|infant dragon]].)

## Afterwards

After Maris, every adventurer Eina advised died, even an upper-class adventurer she had taken over from another adviser. With [[misha-frot|Misha]]'s help she let herself cry and began to mourn. She tried to keep her distance from her adventurers like the other receptionists, but stopped short of it: she "saw Maris in each of the adventurers she advised" and faced them head-on.[@fm15-after] Years later she registered [[bell-cranell|Bell Cranell]]: "He was a human, like Maris had been. And he was even younger than she had been."[@fm15-after]
