**Hermes** is a god and the head of [[hermes-familia|Hermes Familia]], whose captain is [[asfi|Asfi Al Andromeda]].[@fm05-hermes, fm05-asfi] Sent to check on [[bell-cranell|Bell Cranell]] by Bell's grandfather, he comes to see Bell as his candidate for the world's last hero and repeatedly arranges trials for him.[@fm05-death, fm07-candidate]

## Hermes Familia

The Familia works nominally as a delivery service, which lets it pass through checkpoints freely. The [[guild|Guild]] also sends it outside the city to investigate black-market smuggling.[@fm09-delivery]

## Bell and Zeus

In DanMachi 5 Hermes tells [[hestia|Hestia]] that the man who raised Bell faked his death and is in hiding, and that he sent Hermes to see how Bell was doing.[@fm05-death] He joins the rescue party sent down to Bell, lends the adventurer Mord a helmet of invisibility to test Bell against human malice, and afterwards addresses Zeus by name as Bell's grandfather.[@fm05-hermes, fm05-zeus] The Guild penalises Hestia and Hermes for the [[floor-18|Floor 18]] incident.[@fm05-penalty]
