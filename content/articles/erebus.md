**Erebus** is a god who leads the [[evils|Evils]] in the [[great-conflict|Great Conflict]]. He names himself "primordial darkness, and god of the underworld", and his one follower is [[vito|Vito]].[@ar01-underworld, ar03-follower] He first appears under the name Eren, asking the young [[lyu-leon|Lyu]] mocking questions about justice, before revealing himself as the architect of the attack.[@ar01-erebus, ar01-eyes]

## His aim

Erebus calls himself absolute evil.[@ar01-erebus] His real goal is to force heroes to emerge by putting [[orario|Orario]] through a truly lethal trial; he recruited [[zald|Zald]] and [[alfia|Alfia]] of the old [[zeus-and-hera-familias|Zeus and Hera Familias]] for it.[@ar03-erebus] Asked by Astrea at the end what he thinks justice is, he answers that it "is a dream": not to choose between two options but to take a third, to make the impossible possible, "what mortals call justice…and what we call heroism".[@ar03-dream] If Orario failed, he was prepared, with them, to topple [[babel|Babel]] and send the world back to the ancient age.[@ar03-fallback] His aim does not spare him: Astrea still sends him to judgment for his crimes, and the narration calls what he did evil, as he had said himself.[@ar03-erebus]

## The war

On the night the Great Conflict begins, nine gods are sent back to the heavens at once, a tragedy the narration says the Evils' leader wrote; Erebus then reveals himself atop a temple with [[zald|Zald]] and [[alfia|Alfia]] at his sides and declares, "Perish, Orario. For we are absolute evil!!"[@ar01-declaration]

In Astrea Record 2 Erebus's plan uses a god as bait in the Dungeon to lure a god-slaying monster to the surface, while the massacre above hides it.[@ar02-bait, ar02-arcanum] In the same volume he seeks Lyu out again in a ruined church, with Alfia beside him, to hear whether her answer has changed.[@ar02-church] He then forces a choice on her: leave to save her dying friend [[asfi|Asfi]] and he will have a crowd of townspeople killed, or stay and let Asfi die so that they go free.[@ar02-choice] [[astrea|Astrea]] comes to him in the church and tells him, "There is no such thing as absolute justice."[@ar02-no-absolute] In Astrea Record 3 he is on the eighteenth floor of the Dungeon when the monster [[delphyne|Delphyne]] appears.[@ar03-floor18] Most of his followers die shielding him from it, and Astrea, coming down with an escort, finds him alone.[@ar03-alone] At the end of the war he is taken into custody; [[astrea|Astrea]] confronts him over his aims and has him brought to the surface before he is returned to the heavens.[@ar03-erebus, ar03-end]
