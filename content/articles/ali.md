**Ali** is a girl raised as **Aram Raza Shalzad**, the prince of the desert kingdom of Shalzad. In Familia Chronicle 2, [[freya|Freya]] finds her among slaves in the [[kaios-desert|Kaios Desert]]. With the help of Freya's eight followers she wins back her country from Warsa, and Freya then sends her away to rule.[@fc02-market, fc02-names, fc02-parting] She becomes the fifteenth king of Shalzad, King Aram the Wise.[@fc02-legacy]

## Background {#background}

Ali bore two names from birth: her true name, Ali, and her royal name, Aram Raza Shalzad.[@fc02-names] Her father, the king, could get no heir. In a country that adored its king, he gave his long-awaited first child the life of a prince, though she was born a girl. At her mother's death Ali swore that Aram, not Ali, was the one needed.[@fc02-names] The people knew only Prince Aram, "the first and only son of the king". The king was executed when the capital fell.[@fc02-prince]

## Familia Chronicle 2 {#story}

Freya leaves [[orario|Orario]] after threatening [[royman|Royman]] with a secret of his, and travels to the Kaios Desert. There the merchant Bofman Fazoul acts as her guide.[@fc02-departure, fc02-bofman] In the merchant town of Leodo in Israfan she hears of the war, notices a girl among newly arrived slaves, and buys "every item you have in your inventory" so as to take her.[@fc02-war, fc02-market]

- **Halvan:** Ali is the best Halvan player at the court of Shalzad, and as king is hailed as the best in the Kaios Desert.[@fc02-halvan, fc02-legacy]

## Shalzad {#shalzad}

**Shalzad** is a kingdom of the western Kaios Desert, directly north of Israfan; Warsa lies to its east.[@fc02-war] Every country in the Kaios is a kingdom, and most Familias there serve as the military arms of kingdoms.[@fc02-war] Shalzad's belt of oases has long made it a target for Warsa. Its capital is Solshana, and its people revere their royal family.[@fc02-gorza] Its flag shows a crescent moon and a wreath of jasmine.[@fc02-fortress]

## Calamity of the Hot Sands {#calamity-of-the-hot-sands}

The wars of Shalzad, Warsa and later Israfan came to be known as the **Calamity of the Hot Sands**.[@fc02-legacy]

- **Warsa:** a desert kingdom that hired a powerful mercenary Familia, declared war on Shalzad and took its capital.[@fc02-war] Its general Gorza fears the war will never end unless every member of Shalzad's royal family is hunted down.[@fc02-gorza]
- **Resheph Familia:** a god and followers from outside the desert, summoned by Warsa's royal family. They broke Shalzad's defences, and burned, pillaged and raped wherever they went. Gorza calls the god Resheph a "country-destroying pestilence" who spreads war for entertainment.[@fc02-resheph]
- **The Battle of the Sindh:** it led to Warsa's decline. Neither Warsa nor Resheph Familia threatened Shalzad again.[@fc02-legacy]

## Aftermath {#aftermath}

Shalzad recovered and grew at a pace that awed its neighbours. Under King Aram it had a golden age, the first great power of the central western Kaios.[@fc02-legacy] Rumours from Orario said that a certain strongest Familia protected the country. A statue of eight heroes stands in the plaza of the rebuilt Solshana, and people argue over whether its faces resemble certain adventurers.[@fc02-legacy] He is remembered as "The Heroic King" and "He Who Rules the Board", and in "Aram and the Eight Warriors".[@fc02-legacy]

- **Minor Myths and Legends 2:** in "Mirage in a Sea of Sand", Ali, newly on the throne after Freya's departure, dreams of being the grown King Aram at a gathering of nations. In the dream, Aram plans to send Shalzad's warriors to train in Orario's Dungeon. On waking she can recall only feelings of joy and love.[@ss02-dream]

## Bofman Fazoul {#bofman}

**Bofman Fazoul** is the merchant who serves as Freya's guide. He calls himself "a no-name merchant" but is skilled, and reminds her of Royman, though taller and broader.[@fc02-bofman] He gathers information for Ali during the war. Afterwards his Fazoul Trading Company leaves the slave trade and prospers alongside the kingdom, and Bofman is "the man of the hour".[@fc02-legacy] In Ali's dream he has lost weight, become a dignified statesman, and received a Falna and levelled up twice.[@ss02-dream]

- **Name:** printed *Bofman Fazoul*; the DanMachi Fandom wiki spells it *Bofman Fazur*.[@fc02-bofman]
