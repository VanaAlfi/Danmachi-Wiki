**Mikoto Yamato** is an adventurer from the Far East. A Level 2 member of Takemikazuchi Familia when she first appears, she converts to [[hestia-familia|Hestia Familia]] in DanMachi 6.[@fm05-mikoto, fm06-join, ss01-name] She knew [[haruhime|Haruhime]] as a child, which drives her loyalty to her.[@fm15-card]

## Abilities

- **Futsu no Mitama** creates a gravity field that crushes everything inside it to the ground, including Mikoto herself. DanMachi 6 prints it as *Futsu no Tama*.[@fm05-mikoto, fm06-gravity]
- **Yatano Black Crow** and **Yatano White Crow** are spells revealed in DanMachi 7; she uses the first to keep watch in DanMachi 9.[@fm07-crow, fm09-crow]
- Her DanMachi 8 card lists the Development Ability Immunity.[@fm08-card]
