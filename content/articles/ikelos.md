**Ikelos** is the god of [[ikelos-familia|Ikelos Familia]], whose hunters, led by Dix Perdix, capture monsters in the Dungeon, above all the intelligent [[xenos|Xenos]], and smuggle them out of [[orario|Orario]] to sell.[@fm09-dix, fm09-hermes, fm10-money, so10-interrogation] He gives them no orders: as long as they keep him entertained, he says, he will not get in their way.[@fm09-hermes] After the battle on [[daedalus-street|Daedalus Street]] in DanMachi 10 he is handed over to the Guild, takes the blame for the monsters on the surface and is exiled from the city.[@fm10-capture, fm10-exile] His Familia, Dix Perdix and their end are covered on the [[ikelos-familia|Ikelos Familia]] page.

## Appearance and character

Ikelos looks like a young man of average height, with a god's graceful features, dark skin and blue hair and eyes, and he dresses mostly in black.[@fm09-dix, fm09-bell, so10-interrogation] The printed shades vary. DanMachi 9 first gives him navy-blue hair and eyes of one colour over "dark-brown skin", then "deep-blue eyes and hair" and "darkly tanned skin"; DanMachi 10 gives him navy-blue hair and brown skin and calls his clothing "cheap"; Sword Oratoria 10 gives him "navy hair, dark skin" and navy eyes.[@fm09-dix, fm09-bell, fm10-rooftop, so10-interrogation]

He is a god who lives for entertainment. [[loki|Loki]] calls him "the quintessential god who’s starved for entertainment…A guy who goes overboard to kill his boredom", and Sword Oratoria 10 calls his grin "the smile of a hedonistic, ephemeral, destructive, stubborn god".[@so10-loki, so10-interrogation] He tells [[hermes|Hermes]] that his followers "can’t resist pleasure. Are we gods not the same? I can relate, painfully so." Hermes sighs that there is "Nothing nastier than a god desperate for some entertainment", and his own hidden followers heckle him: "Look who’s talking."[@fm09-hermes] Dix knows his god's "hunger for entertainment" well; DanMachi 9 says Ikelos thinks of his followers "as nothing more than pieces on a board that he could manipulate for his own amusement".[@fm09-epilogue]

He grumbles about his followers but enjoys them. There are "way fewer idiots in my familia these days; just a lot more arrogant wise guys", he complains, "But…everything they do is ridiculous crap. It’s hilarious."[@fm09-hermes] [[daedalus|Daedalus]]'s age had "so many crazy children", while "Now they’re all just stuck-up brats"; yet of his own followers he says, "Those brats are a real pain, but I love them all in my own way."[@fm10-knossos, fm10-money]

## His Familia and the Evils

Ikelos Familia is a Dungeon-crawling Familia of [[familia-rank|Rank B]] that has been in Orario for over twenty years. Its members made trips into the deep levels, but nowadays their name seldom comes up among adventurers, and around the time they reached the deep levels their records of clearing floors disappeared.[@fm10-rank, so10-loki] The Guild suspected it of belonging to the [[evils|Evils]], and Hermes reminds Ikelos that his Familia was once named as a suspected member of the Evils. Ikelos waves the charge away: "At the very least, I never claimed to be an evil god."[@so10-loki, fm09-hermes] In the [[great-conflict|Great Conflict]] of Astrea Record 3, however, [[valletta|Valletta Grede]] has "Hazer and other hunters of Ikelos Familia" capture monsters from the Dungeon for the Evils' assault: "Good thing I told Ikelos’s goons to get all these monsters together."[@ar03-valletta]

By DanMachi 9 the Familia's home stands empty. "The place just didn’t feel like home anymore, so I guess I moved," Ikelos tells Hermes, who suggests he give the Guild "a heads-up" next time; in Sword Oratoria 10 Loki and [[dionysus|Dionysus]] hear that the home has been "an empty shell for a long time now".[@fm09-hermes, so10-loki]

He watches his followers rather than leads them. When Dix asks, "Lord Ikelos, may I rely on your cooperation once more?", he answers, "is that how you ask your god for a favor, you arrogant little shit?" Dix wants him to check suspicious people because "Gods can see right through our lies"; Ikelos complains, "I’m a god, and you’re sending me on an errand?", then agrees: "You better make me laugh this time as well, Dix."[@fm09-dix] To Hermes he says, "I didn’t give those orders. My brats are the ones going wild." Hermes could "smash my head in" and give him "a one-way trip back to the upper world", but that would not stop them: "it’s only a matter of time before they sign up with someone else". He says this "at the risk of ruining himself and his followers—perhaps even looking forward to the demise of his own familia".[@fm09-hermes]

## DanMachi 9: the talking vouivre

After Dix asks for his help, Ikelos stops [[bell-cranell|Bell]] on a street in the city's southwest: "The name’s Ikelos. Good meeting you." He complains that "Those arrogant kids of mine are pushing me around at the moment", circles Bell, peers at his face and pats his shoulder, then whispers from behind: "Know anything about a talking vouivre?"[@fm09-dix, fm09-bell] Bell, who is hiding [[wiene|Wiene]], freezes. Hermes arrives, sends Bell away and tells Ikelos he "just couldn’t stand to watch a god sink his poisonous fangs into such a sweet child".[@fm09-bell, fm09-hermes]

The two gods talk alone in a small plaza. They know each other well, "hinting at a long relationship", and Hermes calls him "an old friend from our days back in the heavenly realm".[@fm09-hermes] Hermes says he has heard, from an Elurian noble, that Ikelos Familia is part of an Orario smuggling ring, and that monsters "are being taken out of Orario and sold around the world". Ikelos bursts out laughing: "That I have the dream of beasts—to strew nightmares across the mortal realm?! Now that’s interesting!!" He denies giving any orders and invites Hermes to watch: "Use all the little brats of yours hiding around here to give me and mine a once-over. I couldn’t care less."[@fm09-hermes]

The meeting stays with Bell: "The boy’s mind had been racing nonstop since his encounter with the god Ikelos."[@fm09-bell] When Hermes Familia shadows the hunters at the end of the volume, Ikelos has warned Dix in passing that "Hermes’s kiddies may have noticed…", but he stays away, most likely "keeping a close eye on the show involving his own familia" from somewhere nearby; Dix mutters "Damn that god".[@fm09-epilogue] In DanMachi 10, when Bell learns that hunters have taken Wiene, "The god Ikelos’s unnerving smile appeared in the back of Bell’s mind."[@fm10-wiene]

## DanMachi 10: the rooftop

As tension builds across the city in DanMachi 10, Ikelos watches from a rooftop high above the streets and chuckles: "Failed, now did you, Dix…?" "Now this is what I call interesting…!"[@fm10-watching] Hermes tracks him down on the roof of a tall brick tower in the middle of Daedalus Street. Ikelos had hidden his trail with his divine power, which Hermes calls a breach of the rules: though the rule has become a dead letter, using it freely for one's own ends is a desecration of the mortal world. Ikelos concedes, "You’ve won this round of hide-and-seek, Hermes," and as Hermes's reward answers his questions.[@fm10-rooftop]

He tells Hermes about [[knossos|Knossos]]. Daedalus, "Dix’s forefather", went "a bit off the deep end after seeing the Dungeon"; sensing his end, he left the half-built labyrinth, with his notebook and a blueprint, to his descendants, who have been building it for nearly a thousand years.[@fm10-knossos] The work needed materials and money, so the descendants dealt with the Evils and the black market, and "Word has it" they did whatever it took, "Becoming obsessed with acquiring Enigma, kidnapping women to make sure there would always be someone working on their piece". Dix, Ikelos says, was born of one such abductee: "Must be something in their blood." "Cursed blood…as Dix puts it."[@fm10-knossos] (See [[knossos#daedalus-line|Daedalus's line]].)

He also tells how the trade in Xenos began. "It was all for the money at first": Knossos needs more money than "no amount of treasure brought up from the Dungeon’s deep levels can ever hope to cover", and the risk of losing allies had grown too high. The Xenos were found by accident and the black market began. Then Dix changed: "his eyes started lighting up when he heard them scream and saw them cry", and the goal and the method of capturing Xenos switched places. Ikelos "loved it". To Hermes's "That’s worse than horrible, Ikelos." he answers that it does not go like this with gods; Daedalus's last wish "might die with Dix’s generation", for now Dix is a beast's dream, the thing he loves.[@fm10-money]

When [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]] arrives in Daedalus Street, Ikelos slumps, bored: "Practically all my brats bit the dust…So much for loose ends." But when Bell turns his knife on the adventurers to protect Wiene, he doubles over laughing: "I thought they were all cheeky brats nowadays…but it looks like there’s still some crazy ones left!"[@fm10-fool]

Hermes then gives him up to save [[fels|Fels]]. Seeing [[gareth|Gareth]] close in on Ouranos's mage, Hermes "convinced Ikelos to intervene with a one-sided negotiation": Ikelos appears on the rooftop, offers to show Gareth the cages "in the sewers beneath Daedalus Street", and asks him to let "Black Cloak" go: "The poor sap’s got nothing to do with my brats." Asked why he is telling him, he says, "Because I lost…Had to do what the winner wanted." Fels slips away, and Gareth takes Ikelos by the collar instead ("Just be gentle, okay?"). Gareth drops him on a nearby building to rejoin the fight with the black minotaur, [[asterios|Asterios]], amid complaints ("Brats these days, I swear…"), and later carries him to safety with the wounded.[@fm10-capture] Sword Oratoria 10 calls this the dragnet Finn had asked Gareth to set up.[@so10-interrogation]

## Questioned by Loki Familia

That evening, in a run-down house, [[finn-deimne|Finn]] questions him with Loki at his side before the Guild comes to take him away.[@so10-interrogation]

- **Keys and the Evils:** he has no key to Knossos: "After the thing with Ishtar, the Evils took it when we went outside." The Evils' remnants are "just the odds and ends that Thanatos managed to draw to himself", which he used "to get a place to sleep". Of the god called Enyo he says, "Oh, the city destroyer? I don’t remember any god going by such a stupid name". Loki can tell he is not lying, and he is "not at all connected to the plot to destroy Orario". Asked why he joined the Evils: "Just the way the cookie crumbled".[@so10-interrogation]
- **The smuggling:** his Familia led the smuggling through Meren to fund Knossos, and the monsters "sold for a high price to aristocrats with an interest in monsters".[@so10-interrogation]
- **The notebook:** he tells them how Knossos began and of Daedalus's notebook, which he does not have: "Dix had it. But if there aren’t any traces of his Blessing anymore, he must already be dead." A joke about prying open the other descendants' skulls earns him a kick from Loki.[@so10-interrogation]
- **The Xenos:** to Finn's last question, whether people and monsters can understand each other, he answers "No clue": when he spoke to the caged monsters, "they wouldn’t respond at all". "Certainly, he wasn’t lying, but he wasn’t being honest, either." Asked whether Bell is connected to them, he plays dumb: "Maybe he’s friends with that vouivre?"[@so10-interrogation]

In DanMachi 11 Finn tells [[royman|Royman]], "We learned a few tidbits from God Ikelos", and promises the Guild a key to Knossos if one is found, since "The god Ikelos said there was more than one." He doubts that selling to "monster lovers" was all Ikelos was up to: "He hadn’t been lying, but he hadn’t shared the heart of the matter with them, either."[@fm11-finn] What Loki Familia learned from Ouranos's allies "and from Ikelos" confirmed that Knossos reached the eighteenth floor. During the assault of Sword Oratoria 11, [[asfi|Asfi]] confronts [[barca-perdix|Barca]] as Dix's half-brother "according to Ikelos", and what Hermes had learned from Ikelos, that the keys were made from the descendants' eyes, let her build a replica key.[@so11-knossos, so11-asfi]

**The notebook story.** Hermes hands [[ouranos|Ouranos]] what he calls Daedalus's Notebook, with a drawing of Knossos and its entrances: "Before I got Ikelos to let Loki Familia catch him, I had him give this to me."[@fm11-notebook] Later in DanMachi 11 he admits the truth: "Of course I didn’t get the Notebook from Ikelos." The original was Dix's and is probably "lying around somewhere in Knossos"; Daedalus's heritage passed to his descendants, and "Even a patron deity couldn’t take it away from them". The book Hermes gave Ouranos was a fake forged by Asfi.[@fm11-fake]

## Exile

Loki Familia brings Ikelos before the Guild, where he admits his Familia's black-market dealings and its capture of monsters behind the Guild's back. Because those activities caused the monsters' breach onto the surface, he is exiled from Orario for good two days after the incident, "with nothing but the clothes on his back".[@fm10-exile] Early in the morning, after a night of rain, he leaves by the northernmost gate before a large crowd of people and gods, and [[ganesha|Ganesha]] and his followers see him off.[@fm10-exile]

> "So my days in Orario have come to a close…but I never thought you’d be the one to see me off, Ganesha." "It is because…I am Ganesha." "You know, that’s not an answer."[@fm10-exile]

The Guild had considered sending him back to Tenkai, and Ganesha has heard "there was much debate". Ikelos takes it with a grin: "Well, sure beats being sent back up to heaven." He knows the Guild "needed a scapegoat", and he can't really blame them. His one regret is losing his "front-row seat. Just when things were starting to get good." Looking back at the city, he adds: "I’m jealous, Hermes."[@fm10-exile] In the [[guild#chamber-of-prayers|Chamber of Prayers]] Hermes reports to Ouranos that few people are upset with the Guild, "all thanks to Ikelos taking the blame and responsibility".[@fm10-exile]

The city's papers run the headline "The Secret Maneuvers of Ikelos Familia: A Second Door to the Dungeon?", and Bell, blamed by the crowds himself, hears that the god has been "banished from the city".[@fm11-headlines] In Sword Oratoria 13 Royman's public account, "an explanation that carefully blended fact and fiction", ties the recent turmoil to "the incident involving God Ikelos": a raid on one of his hideouts, and "a confederate of God Ikelos" in the lair below Daedalus Street.[@so13-royman] Sword Oratoria 11 counts Ikelos, "who had been cast out from the city the other day", among the gods from Olympus, like Hermes, Dionysus, [[hephaistos|Hephaistos]], [[demeter|Demeter]], Zeus and Hera.[@so11-olympus]
