**Heith Velvet** is a healer of [[freya-familia|Freya Familia]] and the representative of its healers, the Andhrímnir. She is a Level 4 titled *Vana Mardel*.[@fm17-heith, fm18-heith] She and [[amid|Amid Teasanare]] of Dian Cecht Familia are known as [[orario|Orario]]'s two great healers, the golden witch and the silver saint.[@fm18-heith, ss01-healers]

## Background

Heith first went through the "baptism" of [[folkvangr|Folkvangr]] as one of the einherjar. Recognising her limits as a fighter, she changed to healing out of devotion to Freya and found a hidden talent. She can still defeat second-tier adventurers on her own.[@fm18-heith] Amid is thought to win in raw healing power, while Heith probably has the edge in healing range and in sustained output.[@fm18-heith]
