**Ilta Faana** is a red-haired Amazon, second-in-command of [[ganesha-familia|Ganesha Familia]] and a first-tier adventurer with the title *Paluza*.[@fm10-ilta, fm18-past, so12-squads] When she first came to [[orario|Orario]] she was one of its most lawless adventurers, until [[shakti-varma|Shakti Varma]] and her sister caught and punished her; she then joined Ganesha Familia.[@fm18-past] She calls Shakti "sister".[@fm10-sister]

## Background and character

As a lone Amazon who believed that might made right, Ilta went on a rampage after arriving in Orario. Shakti and her sister caught and punished her, and she was shocked both by their strength and by how fiercely they fought to protect people weaker than themselves, which she thought unthinkable for an Amazon. She joined Ganesha Familia and became, in DanMachi 18's words, "another rare Amazon defender of justice".[@fm18-past] DanMachi 10 says she calls Shakti "sister" in recognition of her strength, and falls silent in her presence.[@fm10-sister]

She is hot-tempered and outspoken. She rages at the Guild's unexplained order to tame the armed monsters in DanMachi 10, and at the Guild for not letting her Familia join the Familia War against [[freya-familia|Freya Familia]] in DanMachi 18.[@fm10-ilta, fm18-past] Shakti answers her with politics, a perspective DanMachi 18 says Ilta still lacks: Ganesha Familia, as the neutral city watch, must police the battlefield and hold any trial fairly.[@fm18-politics] She keeps getting the name of her fellow member Modaka wrong ("Mokada", "Morsaga").[@fm18-politics, fm18-judges]
