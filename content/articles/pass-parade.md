A **pass parade** transfers monsters pursuing one party to another.[@fm05-tactic] It is a tactic used inside [[dungeon|the Dungeon]] to escape a monster by passing it to another battle party.[@fm05-tactic] The receiving party becomes a sacrifice and can be overwhelmed when additional monsters join its existing fight.[@fm05-tactic]

## Adventurer practice {#adventurer-practice}

[[bell-cranell|Bell Cranell]] reports that adventurers should show understanding when no malice is involved, since they may themselves initiate a pass parade.[@fm05-understanding]
