**Ali** is a girl raised as **Aram Raza Shalzad**, the prince of the desert kingdom of Shalzad. In Familia Chronicle 2, [[freya|Freya]] finds her among slaves in the [[kaios-desert|Kaios Desert]]. With the help of Freya's eight followers she wins back her country from Warsa, and Freya then sends her away to rule.[@fc02-market, fc02-names, fc02-parting] She becomes the fifteenth king of Shalzad, King Aram the Wise.[@fc02-legacy]

## Background {#background}

Ali bore two names from birth: her true name, Ali, and her royal name, Aram Raza Shalzad.[@fc02-names] Her father, the king, could get no heir. In a country that adored its king, he gave his long-awaited first child the life of a prince, though she was born a girl. At her mother's death Ali swore that Aram, not Ali, was the one needed.[@fc02-names] The people knew only Prince Aram, "the first and only son of the king". The king was executed when the capital fell.[@fc02-prince]

## Familia Chronicle 2 {#story}

Freya leaves [[orario|Orario]] after threatening [[royman|Royman]] with a secret of his, and travels to the Kaios Desert. There the merchant Bofman Fazoul acts as her guide.[@fc02-departure, fc02-bofman] In the merchant town of Leodo in [[israfan|Israfan]] she hears of the war, notices a girl among newly arrived slaves, and buys "every item you have in your inventory" so as to take her.[@fc02-war, fc02-market] The slave market's manager is Rozzo. Two of the freed slaves, the boy Yona and the girl Haara, thank Freya and ask to join her Familia; she tells them to help out at Bofman's company first.[@fc02-freed] At a bar where wealthy merchants gamble, Freya challenges Nahzo, "one of the big four in this town", to a board game for "all the money that you currently have on you".[@fc02-nahzo]

## Shalzad {#shalzad}

**Shalzad** is a kingdom of the western Kaios Desert, directly north of [[israfan|Israfan]]; Warsa lies to its east.[@fc02-war] Every country in the Kaios is a kingdom, and most Familias there serve as the military arms of kingdoms.[@fc02-war] Shalzad's belt of oases has long made it a target for Warsa. Its capital is Solshana, and its people revere their royal family.[@fc02-gorza] Its flag shows a crescent moon and a single jasmine flower.[@fc02-fortress] Its royal house bears the country's name: Ali's father is "King Shalzad", and Ali proclaims herself "the prince of the Shalzad family".[@fc02-house, fc02-speech] After the capital fell, "Shalzad forces were still continuing to fight back all around the country", among them an elite division at Serein in the north, which was soon reported "wiped out".[@fc02-resistance] In neighbouring Israfan some merchants "had close ties to the Shalzad royal family", while "Many merchants are wagging their tails for Warsa".[@fc02-merchants] Gathered for the first time since the fall of the capital, its army marches "beneath the battle flag of the moon and jasmine".[@fc02-flag]

## Calamity of the Hot Sands {#calamity-of-the-hot-sands}

The wars of Shalzad, Warsa and later Israfan came to be known as the **Calamity of the Hot Sands**.[@fc02-legacy]

- **Warsa:** a desert kingdom that hired a powerful mercenary Familia, declared war on Shalzad and took its capital.[@fc02-war] Its general Gorza fears the war will never end unless every member of Shalzad's royal family is hunted down.[@fc02-gorza]
- **Resheph Familia:** a god and followers from outside the desert, summoned by Warsa's royal family. They broke Shalzad's defences, and burned, pillaged and raped wherever they went. Gorza calls the god Resheph ({{ja|ラシャプ}}, read *Rashap*)[@fc02-resheph-ja] a "country-destroying pestilence" who spreads war for entertainment.[@fc02-resheph]
- **The Battle of the Sindh:** it led to Warsa's decline. Neither Warsa nor Resheph Familia threatened Shalzad again.[@fc02-legacy]

## Aftermath {#aftermath}

Shalzad recovered and grew at a pace that awed its neighbours. Under King Aram it had a golden age, the first great power of the central western Kaios.[@fc02-legacy] Rumours from Orario said that a certain strongest Familia protected the country. A statue of eight heroes stands in the plaza of the rebuilt Solshana, and people argue over whether its faces resemble certain adventurers.[@fc02-legacy] King Aram is remembered as "The Heroic King" and "He Who Rules the Board", and in "Aram and the Eight Warriors".[@fc02-legacy]

- **Minor Myths and Legends 2:** in "Mirage in a Sea of Sand", Ali, newly on the throne after Freya's departure, dreams of being the grown King Aram at a gathering of nations. In the dream, Aram plans to send Shalzad's warriors to train in Orario's Dungeon. Among the guests is Princess Talvi of Beltane, a country "nestled in the mountains" that she calls "just some backwater village", with two escorts, Uskali and Lidari; Aram tells her "everything about the circumstances of my birth and my country".[@ss02-talvi] On waking she can recall only feelings of joy and love.[@ss02-dream]

## Bofman Fazoul {#bofman}

**Bofman Fazoul** is the merchant who serves as Freya's guide. He calls himself "a no-name merchant" but is skilled, and reminds her of Royman, though taller and broader.[@fc02-bofman] Meeting her in a tavern, he leaps "at the chance to volunteer himself"; his company owns a desert ship, and "the number of firms with a desert ship can be counted on one hand!"[@fc02-ship] In Leodo he trades insults with the slave trader Rozzo, "the weakest of the big four".[@fc02-rozzo] Freya leaves him the slaves she has bought and an oasis estate: "Bofman, hire any of these children who wish to work for you."[@fc02-estate] For leering at her he is pinned down by the Gulliver brothers "in a pose exactly like that of a pig splayed on all fours" and carried off.[@fc02-swine] During the war he gathers "boatloads of information on Warsa" for Ali and draws in merchants for her speech: "There was no question that he rendered distinguished service from the shadows." On the night before the battle he tries to join the goddess and Ali, and Ottar takes him away: "Come. We’ll house-train you."[@fc02-housetrain] The next day Freya does not know him: he is "not a fattened blob of flesh but a well-sculpted mass of muscle", and explains that muscle is everything, the same words as Altena's motto that magic is everything.[@fc02-muscle] Afterwards his Fazoul Trading Company, "which had apparently undergone a muscle revolution", leaves the slave trade and prospers alongside the kingdom, and Bofman is "the man of the hour".[@fc02-legacy] In Ali's dream he has lost weight, become a dignified statesman, and received a Falna and levelled up twice.[@ss02-dream]

- **Name:** printed *Bofman Fazoul*; the DanMachi Fandom wiki spells it *Bofman Fazur*.[@fc02-bofman]
