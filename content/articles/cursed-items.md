**Cursed items** are magic items with troublesome effects.[@ss02-definition] They can acquire unwanted effects during creation or receive deliberate restrictions.[@ss02-definition] The text also describes gods ordering their followers to curse items for amusement.[@ss02-definition] A typical effect prevents equipment from being removed once worn.[@ss02-definition]

## History {#history}

| Volume | Events |
|---|---|
| Astrea Record 1 | Alfrik, one of the [[gulliver-brothers|Gulliver brothers]], suspects that the [[evils|Evils]] have forcibly enhanced adventurers using drugs and cursed items.[@ar01-enhancement] |
| Minor Myths and Legends 2 | A boy wears a black helmet that [[hyrute-sisters|Tiona Hyrute]] cannot pull off, and that makes other people appear to have different faces and races.[@ss02-helmet] |
| Minor Myths and Legends 2 | The helmet makes Tiona Hyrute appear to him as an elf girl.[@ss02-helmet] |
| Minor Myths and Legends 2 | Tiona Hyrute says the usual remedies are items made with moly or having a mage or [[healer|healer]] break the curse.[@ss02-cure] |
| DanMachi 19 | [[bell-cranell|Bell Cranell]] disguises himself with a beast-person wig that [[asfi|Asfi Al Andromeda]] made earlier, covering his distinctive features.[@fm19-wig] |
| DanMachi 19 | Bell Cranell reports that the wig carries a mild curse without a cost, one that hinders recognition.[@fm19-wig] |


[@ss02-definition]: SS02 | My Memory | Cursed items have troublesome effects, accidental or intentional; some cannot be removed.
[@ar01-enhancement]: AR01 | Chapter 9: The Opening Act of Evil | Alfrik suspects forced physical enhancement with drugs and cursed items.
[@ss02-helmet]: SS02 | My Memory | A helmet cannot be removed and changes the wearer’s perception of others.
[@ss02-cure]: SS02 | My Memory | Tiona explains standard ways to break the curse. The Japanese edition (file part0021, paragraph 161) names an item made with moly; the English prints "people use moly".
[@fm19-wig]: FM19 | Chapter 2: School Heaven and Hell | Asfi’s wig obscures Bell’s appearance through a mild curse. The Japanese edition (file part0019, paragraph 667) calls it a beast-person wig that Asfi made earlier, under a mild curse of recognition-hindrance without a cost, as Bell has heard; the English prints "the curse is extremely light and shouldn’t have any negative repercussions".
[@ss02-cursed-ja]: SS02 | My Memory | The Japanese edition writes the term in kanji meaning tools of curses, with the reading items (file part0021, paragraph 144).
