**Hathor** is a harvest goddess residing in [[orario|Orario]] and one of the [[elegia#goddess-festival|Goddess Festival]]'s symbols.[@fm16-festival, fm18-appearance] She has black hair, fig-adorned jewelry and a cow mask, and stands 155 celches tall.[@fm18-appearance] She says fertility is incidental to her role and that she does not hold a grudge against [[freya|Freya]].[@fm18-leader]
