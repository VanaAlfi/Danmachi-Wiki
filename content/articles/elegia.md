**Elegia** is [[orario|Orario]]'s yearly festival of mourning. It honours the heroes of ancient times, who "sacrificed themselves to stem the tide of monsters that swarmed out of the pit", and mourns the adventurers lost to [[dungeon|the Dungeon]].[@fm15-explain] The Guild organises it: for one night the city puts out its magic-stone lamps, walks by candlelight to the heroes' monuments, and sings until dawn.[@fm15-night, fm15-procession, fm15-song] With the [[#goddess-festival|Goddess Festival]] that follows soon after, it is one of the city's "two most major festivals".[@fm16-festivals]

## The festival {#festival}

- **Meaning:** Welf calls it "a festival to mourn dead heroes"; for Haruhime it is "also a thanksgiving festival to show appreciation for their great deeds", and for Lilly "also a festival for adventurers in the present day…to mourn those lost to the Dungeon".[@fm15-explain]
- **Candlelight:** until the sun comes up "not a single magic-stone lamp will shine in Orario and candles will be the only source of light", in the shopping district and the [[pleasure-quarter|Pleasure Quarter]] as in every tavern and home, "As though to relive the nights those ancient heroes themselves lived". Central Park is decked with posts draped in flowers, mostly white and blue.[@fm15-night]
- **The procession:** the people gather in [[central-park|Central Park]], most of them in white robes and carrying candleholders, then walk in lines to the heroes' monuments: the courtyard in front of the Guild, the [[#first-graveyard|First Graveyard]] and others on the city's outskirts. Guild employees oversee it to prevent disorder. Most residents give flowers to the adventurers they know.[@fm15-procession]
- **The elegy:** the crowd returns to Central Park and sings until dawn, a song "Transcending borders of species and faction" whose prayer carries "grief and gratitude simultaneously". The gods sing too.[@fm15-song]
- **The Guild's part:** the Guild prepares the festival, and Welf supposes that it has told [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]], [[freya-familia|Freya Familia]] and the like "to behave themselves for a while".[@fm15-guild, fm15-night]
- **Afterwards:** until the Goddess Festival the city stays quieter than usual, since "It was considered poor taste to immediately return to the usual carousing"; no governing body arranges this.[@fm16-festivals, fm16-mood]

## In DanMachi 15 {#danmachi-15}

Elegia closes DanMachi 15. Bell, who has not yet lived in Orario a full year and has never heard of it, comes up from the Dungeon with a party of Dellingr Familia carrying the body of their comrade Celia, and his friends explain the festival to him.[@fm15-bell, fm15-explain]

[[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz]] walks the candlelit city alone in a white dress. A half-elf girl tells her that the festival is for praying to the great heroes to protect people from the dragon at the edge of the world, and asks her to beat it. Aiz promises, "I swear I’ll beat it", but alone afterwards she says, "…I can’t be anyone’s hero."[@fm15-dragon] (See [[three-great-quests|Three Great Quests]].) Listening to the elegy, Bell hears the gods singing as well, remembers his grandfather's words about heroes, and whispers: "I…want to be a hero…" "So…why is it all so sad?"[@fm15-song, fm15-bell]

The next morning Bell lays flowers at Celia's new grave in the First Graveyard and meets Aiz there; she leaves hers before the heroes' monument.[@fm15-graveyard, fm16-albert]

## The First Graveyard {#first-graveyard}

The **First Graveyard**, "also known as the Adventurers Graveyard", is a public burial ground in the south-eastern quarter of Orario and "the final resting place for those who fall in the Dungeon".[@fm15-firstday, fm15-graveyard] Its countless gravestones are of white stone, which mortals have come to associate with the heavens.[@fm15-graveyard] At its centre stands a giant jet-black monument to the heroes of ancient times, carved with their names; the gravestone in the middle of it was placed for the hero Albert.[@fm15-firstday, fm15-graveyard, fm16-albert] Bell first sees it on his first day in Orario. After Elegia the monument is heaped with flowers and burned-out candles.[@fm15-firstday, fm15-graveyard]

## The Goddess Festival {#goddess-festival}

The **Goddess Festival** is the other of the two great festivals: "a harvest festival, a banquet of plenty", centred on the goddesses who preside over bountiful harvests. It is held close after Elegia, which "leaves the city in a sad and somber mood", in order to brighten things up; in DanMachi 16 it is "only six days away".[@fm16-festivals] Lilly names Orario's other famous celebrations as the Holy Night Festival, the [[monsterphilia|Monsterphilia]], Grand Day and the month of the gods.[@fm16-festivals] In DanMachi 17 Freya Familia attacks "another familia in broad daylight during the Goddess Festival that directly followed the Elegia holiday".[@fm17-holiday]

## In Sword Oratoria 13 {#sword-oratoria-13}

After saying her farewells to [[filvis|Filvis]], [[lefiya|Lefiya]] arranges a small expedition and hides in the Dungeon to avoid Elegia. She means to go alone, but [[elfie-collette|Elfie]], [[alicia-forestlight|Alicia]] and other women of the Familia go with her, and she feels she has robbed them of "the one time a year set aside for sorrow".[@so13-lefiya]
