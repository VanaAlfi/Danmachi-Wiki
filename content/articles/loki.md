**Loki** is a goddess and the head of [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]], whose [[emblem|emblem]] is "the mark of the trickster: a comedic smile".[@fm01-loki, so01-emblem] She is an old acquaintance of [[freya|Freya]] and [[hestia|Hestia]].[@fm01-loki] With Freya, she took down [[zeus|Zeus]] and [[hera|Hera]], whose Familias had lost their strongest followers to the Black Dragon, and banished them from Orario.[@fm06-zeus]

## Appearance and character {#character}

- **Looks:** Loki has vermilion hair and eyes. Her eyes are so long and thin that they usually look like lines across her face, and she is often described squinting.[@fm02-vermilion, fm01-eyes, fm04-ponytail] She wears her vermilion hair tied back; for the Banquet of the Gods in Sword Oratoria 1 she wears a black dress and puts her hair up in a bun.[@fm04-ponytail, so01-dress]
- **The trickster:** in [[tenkai-and-gekai|Tenkai]] Loki was a prankster who enjoyed causing confusion, "quite the troublemaker" as Freya remembers her. She has changed a good deal since starting her Familia.[@fm01-tenkai, so01-tenkai] To Hestia she is "a trickster with an unmatched reputation for mischief in the heavens".[@fm15-hestia] By her own account she was "busy trying to kill gods" in the heavens.[@so12-gods] There she once tried to kill [[balder|Balder]] because his smug smile annoyed her, a plot he foiled with the help of mistletoe and his brother Hodur.[@so13-balder]
- **Her children:** to her Familia Loki is not a deity to be revered but "more like the head of a family who treated all her children well and received their love in return", and she does not ask to be worshipped.[@so01-family, so02-worship] She has "a rather particular taste in women": her followers expect her to try to feel them up, and she keeps flinging herself at Aiz and Tiona while protesting "This ain’t sexual harassment!"[@so01-family, so05-harassment] With "a female Casanova" as their patron, Loki Familia has decidedly more women than men.[@so05-casanova] She generally leaves the management of the Familia to Finn and its other leaders.[@so13-management]
- **Hestia:** whenever Loki sees Hestia she has to tease her, and the narration gives the reason as Hestia's bust, which Loki lacks. At [[ganesha|Ganesha]]'s *Celebration of the Gods* in DanMachi 1 their quarrel turns into a brawl in which Loki pulls Hestia's cheeks, but it is Loki who runs off in tears, vowing to get her next time.[@fm01-banquet, fm01-tears] Loki calls her "Itty-Bitty" (printed *Itty Bitty* in DanMachi 1 and 11).[@so01-itty, fm15-hestia, fm01-itty, fm11-bell]
- **Freya:** in DanMachi 1 Loki confronts Freya in a café, demands to know what she is scheming, and warns that if it gets in her way she will end it herself.[@fm01-cafe] She calls Freya's power to see souls the "rot in hell, you lousy cheater" power.[@fm02-eyes]
- **Drink:** Astrea teases her for enjoying "getting drunk and complaining". In the Great Conflict Loki longs for a bathtub full of booze, and in DanMachi 18 she brings her own drink to [[the-benevolent-mistress|The Benevolent Mistress]] after Mia refuses to serve her.[@ar01-tea, ar02-booze, fm18-mia]
- **Justice:** in Astrea Record 1 she mocks [[astrea|Astrea]]'s charity as self-satisfied hypocrisy and says that the gods, all-knowing but powerless in the lower world, cannot be fair to everyone.[@ar01-tea]

## Founding her Familia {#founding}

Newly come down to the Lower World near [[geography#preblica-karna-and-lonza-preblica|Preblica]], Loki made the fourteen-year-old [[finn-deimne|Finn]] her first follower. She recruited [[riveria|Riveria]], who had left the elves' royal forest, and helped Finn free [[gareth|Gareth]] from his ties to [[geography#preblica-karna-and-lonza-lonza|Lonza]] by talking a gnome into helping the town; she also created the three's ritual of stacking their hands for a vow.[@so14-founding, so14-gnome]

Finn had offered her "a relationship that was strictly business" on two conditions: that she support him in restoring his people, and that she never obstruct him in what he would do to achieve that wish.[@so14-terms]
