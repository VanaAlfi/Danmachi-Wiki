**Van** is a Level 4 [[races#half-prum|half-prum]] of [[freya-familia|Freya Familia]], assigned to look after [[bell-cranell|Bell Cranell]].[@fm16-background, fm17-minder] He is the son of a prum and a human, is over thirty years old and stands 150 celches tall.[@fm16-background] He has an androgynous face and fights with twin blades.[@fm16-van-syr, fm16-van-aiz] He swears loyalty to [[freya|Freya]] and Freya Familia for accepting him and dispelling his inferiority and resentment.[@fm16-background]
