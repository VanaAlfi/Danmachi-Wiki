**Lido** is a scarlet [[monsters#lizardman|lizardman]] and the current leader of the [[xenos|Xenos]], a skilled fighter with two swords. He leads because the old-timer [[xenos#gryuu|Gryuu]] is too big to move about freely.[@fm09-lido] The strongest fighter among them is not Lido but the newer arrival [[asterios|Asterios]]; leadership and strength are separate.[@fm09-lido] He, [[rei|Rei]] and [[gros|Gros]] have been Xenos from the start; [[fels|Fels]] first met Lido and his kind fifteen or sixteen years before DanMachi 9.[@fm09-lido-origin] Like other Xenos he kills monsters that are not his comrades and eats their magic stones.[@fm09-lido-stones] His strength is comparable to a Level 5 adventurer's; he fights in armor that once belonged to adventurers, and he can breathe fire.[@so12-lido-strength]
