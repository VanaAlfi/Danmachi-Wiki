**The Exchange** is where adventurers sell the [[magic-stone|magic stones]] and drop items they bring up from [[dungeon|the Dungeon]].[@fm01-hq, so01-drops] [[guild|The Guild]], the only body legally allowed to buy magic stones, runs Exchanges in its headquarters, its branches and [[babel|Babel]].[@so01-monopoly, fm01-hq, fm02-babel] In [[rivira|Rivira]], on [[floor-18|Floor 18]], adventurers run Exchange shops of their own.[@fm05-rivira]

## The Guild's Exchanges {#guild}

- **Where:** the main Exchange is inside Guild Headquarters, and adventurers come to the Guild for its procedures and support.[@fm01-hq, fm01-west] The Guild also has Exchanges at its branches and in Babel. The one in Babel is understaffed, so its lines are long.[@fm02-babel] [[hestia-familia|Hestia Familia]] normally sells at Babel, but goes to Guild Headquarters when the Familia tax is due as well.[@fm08-tax] In DanMachi 19, [[balder-class#third-squad|Bell's School District squad]] could sell at Babel, but its dwarf member insists on Guild Headquarters to celebrate, since "A tiny little exchange spot" does not suit a triumphant return.[@fm19-squad]
- **The monopoly:** only the Guild may legally buy magic stones, so every adventurer comes to the Exchange to sell them. Magic stones are "the lifeblood of the world economy", and the Guild used the money to build the city.[@so01-monopoly] Drop items can also be sold directly to Familias. The Guild can be trusted to pay, but its price "would never be at the best price", so many adventurers sell drop items elsewhere.[@so01-drops]
- **Appraisers:** the Guild's appraisers at the counter "had a lot of guts". Adventurers who think an offer too low often shout, and the appraisers shout back.[@fm02-appraisers] Members of [[soma-familia|Soma Familia]] argue over the terms every day; "Give us more money!"[@fm02-appraisers] [[eina-tulle|Eina]] notices that those who have reached Level 2 are much calmer.[@fm02-soma]
- **The blacklist:** if an adventurer's registration is revoked, the Exchange stops paying for their magic stones and drop items and simply takes them.[@fm02-blacklist]
- **Records:** when Bell goes missing in DanMachi 5, Eina checks with the Exchange whether anyone matching him came in the day before.[@fm05-eina]

## Rivira's Exchanges {#rivira}

Rivira has Exchange shops of its own (the Japanese narration calls them *buy-back places*, though Mikoto's group first exclaims at an *exchange office*, the Guild's word; Yen Press prints "Exchange" for both); one has a sign with drawings of a Minotaur and purple stones.[@fm05-rivira, fm05-ja-rivira] They buy drop items and magic stones for "less than half their value" and sell them to the Guild for full price on the surface. Adventurers sell there anyway, because they can carry only so much, and selling lets them keep exploring. The strongest shop's owner keeps the other shops quiet by force, so there is no price competition.[@fm05-rivira, fm05-ja-rivira] [[ouka|Kashima Ouka]] calls it "a scam".[@fm05-rivira] Payment is by a written note issued by the shop, which the seller claims from the shop owners' own Familia on the surface.[@fm05-receipt, fm05-ja-receipt]

[[bors|Bors Elder]] owns the largest Exchange shop in Rivira; DanMachi 5 calls him "the owner of Rivira’s Exchange".[@so03-bors, fm05-bors] The crystals of Floor 18 can also be turned in for money "at the Exchange aboveground".[@fm05-crystals]
