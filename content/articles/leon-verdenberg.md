**Leon Verdenberg** is the captain of [[balder#balder-class|Balder Class]] and the leading professor of [[school-district|the School District]], titled *Knight of Knights* and also *Ultra Page*.[@so13-leon] He is a Level 7, one of five named by DanMachi 20.[@fm20-leon, fm20-hearing] He was born to two [[races#half-dwarf|half-dwarf]] parents.[@fm20-parents] He is nonetheless a [[races#dwarf|dwarf]], one who strongly inherited human traits: he is tall and long-limbed, with no beard or thick body hair.[@fm20-leon-dwarf] His hair is the colour of a lion's mane, his eyes are the same colour, and he is probably at least 180 celches tall.[@so13-leon-looks]

## Teacher

Leon grew up among dwarves who envied his human traits and bullied him; when the persecution extended to his parents he beat his tormentors half to death, was banished from the village and became a delinquent.[@fm20-leon-youth] Disgusted with himself, he ran away to Orario, spat at the feet of [[balder|Balder]], the god who gave him his blessing, and threw himself into the Dungeon. There he, [[ottar|Ottar]], [[finn-deimne|Finn]] and others were beaten back again and again by the followers of [[zeus-and-hera-familias|Zeus and Hera]]; chasing them, he stowed away on the battle against the [[three-great-quests|Leviathan]], where he "saw the light of those heroes who would save this world".[@fm20-leon-heroes] After Zeus and Hera fell to the Black Dragon, he was among the young fighters who faced the one-armed Maxim, "once the strongest of all", in the fight that ended that hero; then, guided by Balder, he gave up his delinquent youth to become a teacher and pass on the heroes' ultimate technique, which he calls the afterglow, "to even just one more person".[@fm20-leon-teacher]

In Sword Oratoria 13 Leon teaches that instructors are also taught by their students, and he joins the rescue after a collapse in the Dungeon.[@so13-leon] His students call him Ultra Page because he takes up their problems and sticks with them to the end, even going five days without sleep, and he is the most popular teacher among them; [[lefiya|Lefiya]], once his student, knows no one worthier of the title of teacher.[@so13-leon-ultra-page] He keeps the legacy of [[zeus-and-hera-familias|Zeus and Hera Familias]] alive through his teaching.[@fm20-legacy]

Many of the students he guided to Orario became promising adventurers, but none proved able to make the afterglow their own until he met Bell, who "raced up the hero’s stairs faster even than Zeus and Hera’s followers could match".[@fm20-leon-students] After becoming an instructor he visited his parents, who cried tears of joy at what he had become.[@fm20-leon-parents-visit]

## The other Level 7

In Sword Oratoria 6, [[riveria|Riveria]] speaks of only two Level 7s in the world; one is [[ottar|Ottar]].[@so06-sevens] DanMachi 19 hints that Leon is the other, and DanMachi 20 confirms his Level. By then [[finn-deimne|Finn]], Riveria and [[gareth|Gareth]] have also reached Level 7.[@fm19-other, fm20-leon]

Fifteen years before DanMachi 20, when Zeus and Hera ruled Orario, he was an up-and-comer whose name was mentioned with Finn's and Ottar's; he later reached Level 7 outside the city, and stories of him cutting a castle in half and cutting down ten thousand soldiers with a single sword have made him known as the modern-day hero.[@fm20-leon-hero] Bell learns that the strength of Leon and the other School District instructors comes from years of fighting the monsters that come out of the [[valley-of-dragons|Valley of Dragons]].[@fm20-leon-dragons] His Level 7 Status sheet at the end of DanMachi 20 shows Strength S999, Defense S907, Dexterity A815, Agility C637 and Magic C616; the Development Abilities Pummel E, Immunity E, Fracture F, Slash H and Supreme Attack I; and two Skills, Dvergr Gauntlet, which greatly boosts his Strength and the power of smashing attacks, and Dvergr Rebellion, which greatly boosts his Defense and, in proportion to his anger, increases his Strength and Agility boosts and decreases the effects of Defense, Dexterity and Magic. See [[skills#leon-skills|Skills]] and [[development-ability|Development Abilities]].[@blaze-of-the-round.fm20-sheet]

## DanMachi 19–20

In DanMachi 19 Bell, enrolled in disguise as the student [[balder#rapi-flemish|Rapi]], joins Leon's class, and Leon introduces him to his classmates.[@fm19-leon-class] He and Balder have placed Bell in the 3rd Squad, problem students whom the Dungeon Practical would "totally crush", because watching over that squad is the condition of [[hermes|Hermes]]'s deal for his enrolment, and Leon also asks him to support [[nina-tulle|Nina]].[@fm19-leon-squad] When students are trapped by an accident in the Dungeon, Leon answers [[idun|Idun]]'s plea to save them and does more than anyone to rescue the people caught in it.[@fm19-leon-rescue, fm19-leon-saved]

Leon proposes an adventure to [[bell-cranell|Bell]] in DanMachi 19.[@fm19-proposal] In DanMachi 20 he takes Bell and [[nina-tulle|Nina]] north to the [[valley-of-dragons|Valley of Dragons]], shows them its seal, fights Bell in the [[orariad|Orariad]] and helps defeat an escaped dragon.[@fm20-valley, fm20-leon-duel] Leon has the better of their fight, but it is recorded as a draw.[@fm20-draw] Before the journey, Balder tells Leon that a single large escapee from the valley is caught in the barrier but will soon break free, and Leon resolves to go with a companion: "The promised adventure…and a new hero. I would like to dream."[@fm20-leon-escapee] When the dragon interrupts their fight, Leon tells Bell to defeat it: "Hit it with the afterglow. As you are now, you can do it." After it falls, Bell is sure that Leon brought him north so that he could slay it and "grasp a glimmer of that afterglow".[@fm20-afterglow]

### Blaze of the Round {#blaze-of-the-round}

**Blaze of the Round** is the magic of Leon Verdenberg, the Knight of Knights. After a short chant a weapon of golden light forms in his hand; each time one of these weapons is destroyed the next one appears and his Status grows stronger, so the longer the fight goes on, the stronger he becomes.[@blaze-of-the-round.fm20-cast, blaze-of-the-round.fm20-buff] Leon himself calls it "a very inefficient spell".[@blaze-of-the-round.fm20-buff]

- **User:** Leon Verdenberg[@blaze-of-the-round.fm20-cast, blaze-of-the-round.fm20-sheet]
- **Type:** A buff that forms successive weapons of light[@blaze-of-the-round.fm20-cast, blaze-of-the-round.fm20-buff]
- **Stages:** Eleven trials and a pinnacle[@blaze-of-the-round.fm20-sheet]
- **Chant:** Short; printed in full[@blaze-of-the-round.fm20-cast]

#### Incantation {#blaze-of-the-round-incantation}

DanMachi 20 prints the chant once, when Leon sheathes his longsword and sets it aside to fight [[bell-cranell|Bell]] with magic:[@blaze-of-the-round.fm20-cast]

- **Chant:** "Ring out, remnant light. Twelve seats of greatness."[@blaze-of-the-round.fm20-cast] The Japanese chant is {{ja|轟け残光。すなわち雄たる十二席|todoroke zankō. sunawachi yū taru jūni seki}}, *Resound, afterglow: that is, the twelve mighty seats*; *zankō* is the word the English elsewhere renders as the *afterglow*, the name of his technique.[@blaze-of-the-round.fm20-cast]
- **Name:** "Blaze of the Round."[@blaze-of-the-round.fm20-cast]

#### How it works {#blaze-of-the-round-how-it-works}

- **Weapons of light.** The first weapon is a one-handed sword of golden light; Bell first takes it for an enchantment or a spell that makes a weapon out of magic power.[@blaze-of-the-round.fm20-cast] When it shatters, the fragments return to Leon and form the next weapon: twin blades, then a great battle-ax, then a greatshield from which he draws daggers of light.[@blaze-of-the-round.fm20-gable, blaze-of-the-round.fm20-dragon]
- **A buff, not a weapon spell.** [[nina-tulle|Nina]] realises that each of the first eleven weapons improves his Status when it breaks. Leon confirms that eleven weapons have to be destroyed for the full effect, and that starting with Percil his agility, dexterity, strength, endurance, magic, Skills and spells are all boosted.[@blaze-of-the-round.fm20-buff]
- **Named stages.** Leon names each stage as it falls: "You’ve cleared Percil. Next is Gable.", "Gable has been cleared. Now Darbazar.", and later "Darbazar clear. Phyron unlocked."[@blaze-of-the-round.fm20-gable, blaze-of-the-round.fm20-dragon]
- **Each weapon has its own traits.** The ax, Darbazar, cannot be swung as freely as the sword or the twin blades, but each blow is heavy.[@blaze-of-the-round.fm20-buff] One diagonal swing sends out an enormous slash of light that leaves half the plateau a deep gash; the arena's announcer and [[asfi|Asfi]] take it for Leon's famous castle-splitting slash.[@blaze-of-the-round.fm20-ax]

#### Compared with Ottar's transformation {#blaze-of-the-round-compared-with-ottars-transformation}

Leon contrasts it with [[ottar|Ottar]]'s beast transformation, which boosts his Status "in a single go". Bell judges that, unlike Ottar's transformation, it seems to carry no risk, and guesses that once the twelfth weapon is unlocked the boost might surpass it; that is Bell's guess, not something the text confirms.[@blaze-of-the-round.fm20-buff]
