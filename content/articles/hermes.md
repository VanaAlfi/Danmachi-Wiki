**Hermes** is a god and the head of [[hermes-familia|Hermes Familia]], whose captain is [[asfi|Asfi Al Andromeda]].[@fm05-hermes, fm05-asfi, fm05-ja-asfi] Sent to check on [[bell-cranell|Bell Cranell]] by Bell's grandfather, he comes to see Bell as his candidate for the world's last hero and repeatedly arranges trials for him.[@fm05-death, fm07-candidate] He is an orange-haired, orange-eyed god with a dandy smile who usually dresses in traveller's clothes.[@fm07-hermes-looks]

## Hermes Familia

The Familia works nominally as a delivery service. The [[guild|Guild]] also sends it outside the city to investigate black-market smuggling, which is one of the reasons it can pass through checkpoints freely.[@fm09-delivery] Hermes reports false Levels for his followers so as to stay "in the middle of the pack".[@so03-hermes-levels] By Sword Oratoria 10 his Familia has formed a united front with [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]] and [[dionysus-familia|Dionysus Familia]] against the remnants of the [[evils|Evils]] and their monsters.[@so10-hermes-front]

## Bell and Zeus

In DanMachi 5 Hermes tells [[hestia|Hestia]] that the man who raised Bell faked his death and is in hiding, and that he sent Hermes to see how Bell was doing.[@fm05-death] He joins the rescue party sent down to Bell, lends the adventurer [[mord-latro|Mord]] a helmet of invisibility to test Bell against human malice, and afterwards addresses [[zeus|Zeus]] by name as Bell's grandfather (in the Japanese he says *mago*, grandson, and the kanji write *adoptive grandson*, {{ja|義孫|mago}}).[@fm05-hermes, fm05-zeus, fm05-ja-zeus] When a black shadow stirs in the crystal ceiling of the eighteenth floor, he judges that the Dungeon has noticed the gods' presence, since "It hates the fact that gods are all the way down here", and sends Asfi to [[rivira|Rivira]] for reinforcements.[@fm05-hermes-goliath] The Guild penalises Hestia and Hermes for the [[floor-18|Floor 18]] incident.[@fm05-penalty] DanMachi 17 shows that, as the god of messengers, he keeps in regular, secret contact with Zeus, who is no longer in the city.[@fm17-hermes-messenger]
