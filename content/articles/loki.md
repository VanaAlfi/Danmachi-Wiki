**Loki** is a goddess and the head of [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]]. She is an old acquaintance of [[freya|Freya]] and [[hestia|Hestia]].[@fm01-loki]

## Founding her Familia

Newly come down to the Lower World near Preblica, Loki made the fourteen-year-old [[finn-deimne|Finn]] her first follower. She recruited [[riveria|Riveria]] in the elves' royal forest, arranged [[gareth|Gareth]]'s release from Lonza, and created the three's ritual of stacking their hands for a vow.[@so14-founding]
