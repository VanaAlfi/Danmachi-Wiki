**Dionysus** is the god who heads [[dionysus-familia|Dionysus Familia]], whose captain is [[filvis|Filvis Challia]].[@so01-dionysus, so11-captain] Through most of Sword Oratoria he works alongside [[loki|Loki]] and [[hermes|Hermes]] against the hidden enemy known as Enyo; Sword Oratoria 12 reveals that he is Enyo.[@so05-enyo, so12-enyo]

## Appearance and character {#character}

- **Looks:** Dionysus is a golden-haired god with glass-coloured eyes. To the girls of a flower shop he has "the presence of a prince from a far-off kingdom", unlike the other gods "with their sick sense of humor".[@so03-prince, so04-eyes]
- **Wine:** he enjoys wine, sends Loki fine wines to soothe her, and built a wine cellar at his home; after his apparent death, Loki and [[soma|Soma]] find divine wine in it.[@so05-wine, so11-cellar, so12-cellar]
- **His true nature:** unmasked in Sword Oratoria 12, he is "a deviant god" who delights in derangement and chaos. What he wants is the *orgia*: the terror of mortals fleeing monsters in the age of heroes, before the gods came down, which he watched from the skies, and which he calls better "than the finest wine".[@so12-orgia]
- **Ouranos:** that age ended when [[ouranos|Ouranos]] sealed the hole, so Dionysus loathes him "with a homicidal rage"; his drunken warnings about Ouranos came from that hatred.[@so12-ouranos]
- **In the heavens:** he says [[hestia|Hestia]] stopped him when he tried to draw the gods into a murderous fight there, and admits that without her he would have been consumed by murder.[@so12-hestia] In Sword Oratoria 11 he explains that their territory in the heavens chose Twelve Gods as its representatives, and that Hestia gave him her own place when he despaired at not being chosen; Hestia recalls that back then he had a really bad temper and quarrelled with Ouranos and others over small things.[@so11-twelve-gods]

## The deception {#deception}

After [[monsterphilia|Monsterphilia]], Dionysus receives a coloured monster stone from one of his elf followers, who collected it before the Guild could. He uses the stones, and his own murdered former followers, to pose as a victim investigating the crimes and to undermine Loki's trust in [[ouranos|Ouranos]].[@so01-stone, so12-enyo] Loki points out that he always kept her from cooperating with the [[guild|Guild]].[@so12-guild] In Sword Oratoria 11, during the first assault on [[knossos|Knossos]], he leaves the main squad and goes down a dark passage alone, telling Loki over the oculus that he has found "The accursed god who killed my children"; then a god's disappearance seals his followers' Falna, the green flesh overruns Dionysus Familia, and the city takes the pillar of light for his return to the heavens.[@so11-revenge, so12-two-pillars]

He drinks Divine Wine on purpose until a righteous false persona sincerely believes in its role; while intoxicated, his apparent sincerity is real.[@so12-wine] He also intoxicates the goddess [[penia|Penia]] and his followers, secretly moves them to her Familia, and sends her back to the heavens so that their sealed [[falna|Falna]] makes it look as if he himself has died.[@so12-penia] Penia, who rules over poverty and has no followers, was the ideal cover: no one would notice his children converted to her.[@so12-cover] [[demeter|Demeter]] was another scapegoat: after she went looking for a god who was behaving oddly, he kidnapped [[minor-characters#persephone|Persephone]] and her other followers, and she took the blame as Enyo.[@so12-demeter]

Loki unmasks him in Sword Oratoria 12. A wine bottle that Penia held, with the same label as the wine in his cellar, is one clue, but what convinces her is [[hestia|Hestia]]'s word: Hestia had called him not strange but "scary", and Loki believes her over him.[@so12-scary]

## Enyo {#enyo}

At the time of the Twenty-Seventh-Floor Nightmare, Dionysus was secretly "a hidden third faction", pretending to support the Guild's goals while watching for a chance to overthrow Orario. Filvis, killed in that disaster and turned into a creature, showed him that the corrupted spirit existed, and through her he contacted it and brought it over to his side.[@so12-third-faction]

As Enyo, Dionysus works through Filvis and the creature [[levis|Levis]] to unite the remnants of the [[evils|Evils]] and the [[corrupted-spirit|corrupted spirit]]'s underground forces. His goal is [[orario|Orario]]'s destruction and the terror of mortals, not the revenge he professes to Loki.[@so12-enyo, so12-orders] His real target, as he reveals in Knossos, is the adventurers: as long as they live, they would build "another lid" even if Orario and Babel were destroyed, so he lures the city's main forces into Knossos to be wiped out by the seventh demi-spirit, [[corrupted-spirit#nidhogg|Nidhogg]], "a calamitous bomb" charged with the magic of the six others.[@so12-true-aim] His fifteen-year plan fails because [[bell-cranell|Bell]]'s six months of growth fall outside it.[@so12-plan]

When his plan with the demi-spirit [[corrupted-spirit#nidhogg|Nidhogg]] fails, he returns himself to the heavens while embracing one real self of Filvis.[@so12-end]

Afterwards his deeds are never made public, and, ironically, he is remembered as "a just god who died in battle with his familia".[@so13-just-god]
