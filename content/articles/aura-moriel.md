**Aura Moriel** is introduced as [[dionysus-familia|Dionysus Familia]]'s Level 2 second-in-command, with the alias *Krater*.[@so11-identity] She is an [[races#elf|elf]] magic user with tied white hair, deep purple eyes, red-and-black battle clothing and a long staff.[@so11-identity]
