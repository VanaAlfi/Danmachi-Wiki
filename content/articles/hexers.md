**Hexers** make weapons imbued with curses, a craft the text sets against that of mages.[@so07-craft] [[barca-perdix|Barca Perdix]] acquires [[development-ability#enigma|Enigma]], becomes a hexer and mass-produces [[cursed-weapons|cursed weapons]].[@so11-barca]

## History {#history}

| Volume | Events |
|---|---|
| Sword Oratoria 8 | [[amid|Amid Teasanare]] judges the maker of the enemy's cursed weapons to possess extraordinary ability or obsession.[@so08-amid] |
| Sword Oratoria 11 | A captive, apparently a curse user, kills himself through the price of his curse and kills fellow prisoners with it.[@so11-captive] |
| Familia Chronicle 2 | [[magic#hal-reshef|Seal]] is described as a Level 4 hexer who gains [[excelia|excelia]] by catching stronger opponents off guard.[@fc02-seal] |
| Familia Chronicle 2; DanMachi 16, 18 | An unnamed hexer participates in making [[hegni|Hegni Ragnar]]'s [[equipment#victim-abyss|Victim Abyss]].[@fc02-maker, fm16-maker, fm18-maker] |
| Familia Chronicle 3 | [[lyu-leon|Lyu Leon]] identifies [[magic#lunus-wolfsbane|Uranda]] as a curse user and infers that she binds her target by paying with her own pain.[@fc03-uranda] |
| Astrea Record 2 | Mages and hexers adjust [[apate-and-alecto-familias#apate-familia|spirit warriors]] after each battle to prevent rejection between the adventurer and the implanted spirit.[@ar02-adjust] |
| Astrea Record 3 | [[valletta|Valletta Grede]] forces a hexer with Enigma to make a prototype whip that subjugates monsters regardless of the tamer's skill.[@ar03-whip] |


[@so07-craft]: SO07 | Chapter 3: Feast of the Dead | Hexers are contrasted with mages as makers of cursed weapons.
[@so11-barca]: SO11 | Chapter 5: Obsession Manifest | Barca acquired Enigma, became a hexer and mass-produced cursed tools.
[@so08-amid]: SO08 | Chapter 2: Did Someone Order a Wolf? | Amid judges the maker’s ability or obsession beyond ordinary bounds.
[@so11-captive]: SO11 | Chapter 2: The Decisive Battle Intermission | One captive, apparently a curse user, dies by his curse’s price and kills the other prisoners with a powerful curse.
[@fc02-seal]: FC02 | Ali and the 8 Followers | Seal is described as a Level 4 hexer rather than a warrior.
[@fc02-maker]: FC02 | Ali and the 8 Followers | An unnamed hexer took part in making Hegni’s weapon.
[@fm16-maker]: FM16 | Chapter 6: The Wish’s Cost | The unnamed hexer participated in creating Victim Abyss.
[@fm18-maker]: FM18 | Chapter 8: The Great Familia War | The hexer’s involvement is repeated.
[@fc03-uranda]: FC03 | The Locus of Stars | Lyu infers that Uranda binds a target by paying with her own pain. The Japanese edition (file c1JZ, paragraph 143) ends her explanation with a supposition (she probably binds the target so); the English prints it as a statement, "in exchange for her pain, she could bind the target of her curse".
[@ar02-adjust]: AR02 | Chapter 11: Warriors’ Last Supper: FINAL WAR EVE | Mages and hexers adjust spirit warriors after each battle.
[@ar03-whip]: AR03 | Chapter 5: Playing the Violence Card | A hexer with Enigma is forced to make a monster-taming whip.
[@so07-hexer-ja]: SO07 | Chapter 3: Feast of the Dead | The Japanese edition writes the term in kanji meaning curse practitioner, with the reading hexer, and calls hexers the counterpart of mages (file c2DZ, paragraph 41).
