A **quest** is a request that an adventurer undertakes in exchange for a reward supplied by the client.[@so01-definition] Clients include [[familia|Familia]] groups, merchants and the [[guild|Guild]], while ordinary citizens can place requests through other venues.[@so01-definition, fm04-clients]

## Posting, acceptance and payment {#posting-acceptance-and-payment}

Guild notices are pinned to a bulletin board and record the requested work, reward, client signature and Familia [[emblem|emblem]].[@fm04-posting] [[lilliluka-erde|Lilliluka Erde]] explains that the Guild's posted clients include Familias and merchants, while taverns gather ordinary citizens' requests and work the Guild does not approve.[@fm04-clients] Adventurers remove chosen notices and submit them at reception, where registration prevents duplicate acceptance.[@so02-registration] After completion, they present the contract and proof or requested items at the counter to receive the reward held by the Guild.[@so02-registration] Available work includes collecting monster drops and protecting caravans, and successfully completing official quests contributes to the Guild's evaluation and a Familia's [[familia-rank|rank]].[@so02-work]
