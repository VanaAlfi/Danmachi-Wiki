**Maria's Orphanage** is an orphanage in an old abandoned church in [[daedalus-street|Daedalus Street]], run by Mother Maria. It is not part of any organised programme; Maria and the children live there poor but happy.[@fm08-church] [[bell-cranell|Bell]] finds it in DanMachi 8 while looking for [[syr-flover|Syr]], who visits the children.[@fm08-names, fm08-syr]

## The orphanage {#orphanage}

Syr explains that "this kind of place isn’t all that uncommon on Daedalus Street". Many children are born to adventurers without settled parents, some from the [[pleasure-quarter|Pleasure Quarter]], and many adventurers die in the [[dungeon|Dungeon]]. Children left behind are often abandoned in the slum.[@fm08-why] Maria says there are other orphanages there, all funded by a group of Familias, and that "several compassionate goddesses" help hers.[@fm08-funding]

- **Children:** besides humans, there are "many mixed children", animal people, prums "and even a timid Amazon".[@fm08-races] Many children Maria raised ignored her pleas and went into the Dungeon to help the orphanage, and became like the adventurers who left families behind.[@fm08-dungeon]
- **Building:** the church's main chamber is at least ten meders across, with doors to other rooms along both walls, an altar at the back and a cracked tile floor where wild grass grows; at least twenty children gather around Bell and Syr in its cafeteria.[@fm08-chamber]
- **Garden:** Maria and the children grow vegetables in the yard behind the church. Beyond it, among ruins, a stone slab door opens onto an underground passage.[@fm08-garden, fm11-passage] Bell later realises that this stairway, like the tunnels under the Pleasure Quarter, is part of Daedalus's legacy (see [[knossos|Knossos]]).[@fm10-legacy] In DanMachi 11 Bell leads [[haruhime|Haruhime]] and the [[xenos|Xenos]] through the garden to the underground room where he fought the barbarian, which had escaped from the hunters through a sealed door beyond it; he blasts the door open with Firebolt, finds that the passage leads to Knossos, and the Xenos go home down it.[@fm11-escape]
- **Syr:** Syr tells Bell she "grew up in this slum" without parents, and comes for reasons similar to Maria's.[@fm08-syr]
- **Hestia:** by Sword Oratoria 12, [[hestia|Hestia]] has grown attached to the orphanage, gives it a modest donation after talking it over with her captain, and tells the children tales of her followers.[@so12-hestia]

## Mother Maria {#maria}

**Mother Maria** ({{ja|マリア・マーテル}}, *Maria Mater*) is an elderly woman with black hair tied up on her head, thin but calm; all the children call her "Mother".[@fm08-maria, fm11-maria-ja] DanMachi 11 calls her an elderly woman, black-haired and thin.[@fm11-maria] She was once one of the women left behind by an adventurer, and never joined a Familia. She took in a child abandoned on a rainy night and raised it as her own; as that happened again and again, the orphanage came to be. "At first, it was out of pity."[@fm08-maria, fm08-origin] She fears for the children who dream of the Dungeon.[@fm08-dungeon] In DanMachi 11 she and [[syr-flover|Syr]] visit the other orphanages of the Labyrinth District to urge them to evacuate.[@fm11-evacuate] When Ruu goes missing, Maria begs Ouka and Chigusa to find the child and goes with them to show the way to the church; in the plaza, separated from the children, she screams "Run, everyone!!"[@fm11-maria, fm11-search, fm11-plaza] Afterwards she leads the children to Hestia Familia's home to apologise to Bell.[@fm12-visit] At the harvest festival of DanMachi 16, when Bell explains why he has brought Syr, "Maria’s concern is replaced by a gentle, motherly smile"; in DanMachi 18 she can only hug the children tight as they watch Bell in the Great Familia War.[@fm16-maria, fm18-hug]

## Lai {#lai}

**Lai** ("just Lai") is a human boy, the eldest child at the orphanage, who turns eleven in DanMachi 8. His skin is covered in small scratches, and he keeps asking Bell about the Dungeon; Bell thinks he may long to be an adventurer.[@fm08-lai] He has brown hair. In DanMachi 8 he tells the worried Maria that he will go to the [[school-district|Education District]], which is returning to Orario that year, and pay with a scholarship, so as to study, grow strong and make lots of money; Fina and many of the other children say they will go too.[@fm08-education] DanMachi 17 and 18 print his name as *Rai*.[@fm17-rai, fm18-rai] When a [[monsters#barbarian|barbarian]] attacks Bell in the passage behind the orphanage, Lai throws a rock at it to draw it away.[@fm08-barbarian]

In DanMachi 11, after Bell has defended a monster in Daedalus Street, Lai steps in front of Fina and Ruu "as if to protect the other two" and asks Bell, "Why did you do it?", his voice full of "the same judgment, hatred, and disappointment as the other townsfolk", before walking away.[@fm11-why, fm11-traitor] When Ruu goes missing he and Fina beg Ouka and Chigusa for help and cling to Ouka, ignoring Maria.[@fm11-search] In the plaza, when the black minotaur steps toward the three children, Lai, who "had felt Bell had betrayed him", watches Bell fight, hears Mord and yells "Go get ’im, big brotheeeerrrrrrr!!"[@fm11-lai-cheer] He comes with Maria to apologise in DanMachi 12.[@fm12-visit] In Sword Oratoria 10 he meets Finn but "could not bring himself to be happy", and drags Ossian away in case "One of the monsters might show up".[@so10-lai] At the harvest festival of DanMachi 16 he proudly tells Bell that the children helped a dwarf brew the ale they sell, and in DanMachi 18 he chokes up cheering Bell on.[@fm16-stall, fm18-watch]

## Fina {#fina}

**Fina** is a [[races#chienthrope|chienthrope]] girl with long cream-coloured hair and a cream-coloured tail. She is the same age as Lai and one of the older children.[@fm08-fina, fm16-fina] Her long hair falls straight.[@fm08-fina-appearance] DanMachi 18 prints her name twice as *Fiona*.[@fm18-watch] In DanMachi 8 she and Lai tell Bell they heard the voice in the ruins "as a test of courage", and when they go down to find it "Fina is a little frightened".[@fm08-courage] In DanMachi 11, after the Xenos affair, she can only say "B-big brother…" before she and Ruu follow Lai away from Bell;[@fm11-turn] later, in the plaza, her and Ruu's "earnest cheers" ring out with Mord's shouts during Bell's fight with [[asterios|Asterios]].[@fm11-cheers] Meeting Bell after the Xenos affair, she tucks her tail and steps back, then follows Lai into the orphanage without a word.[@fm11-fina-withdrawal] When Ruu goes missing, she and Lai, after Maria, ask to go with Chigusa and Ouka to look for Ruu.[@fm11-fina-search] In DanMachi 12, when Maria brings the children to Bell, Lai and Fina, blushing, are the ones who put their apology and thanks into words.[@fm12-fina-apology] At the harvest festival in Daedalus Street in DanMachi 16 she clings to Bell and asks where he and Syr will "spend the night", until Maria covers her mouth.[@fm16-party] At the children's festival stall she announces that they have ale, and they also sell their homemade cookies and fried vegetables.[@fm16-fina-stall] She and Ruu take visitors by the hand to widen the dancing circle.[@fm16-fina-dance]

In DanMachi 17 Bell counts Fina among those he fears he would forget if his supposed curse were broken.[@fm17-fina-memory] In DanMachi 18, watching Bell, she cries with Ruu as Maria hugs them, and later watches him stand again through the mirror.[@fm18-fina-war] In DanMachi 20 she cheers with Lai and Ruu in the amphitheater.[@fm20-fina-amphitheater] In Sword Oratoria 12 [[hestia|Hestia]] brings snacks to the children's temporary home, and Fina hugs her.[@so12-fina-hestia]

## Ruu {#ruu}

**Ruu** is a [[races#half-elf|half-elf]], a year younger than Lai, and dreamy; with short dirty-blond hair, Ruu is probably the handsomest of the children.[@fm08-ruu] Bell cannot tell whether Ruu is a boy or a girl, even in DanMachi 16 ("little brother (sister?)"); DanMachi 11 does not say either.[@fm08-ruu, fm16-ruu, fm11-ruu]

- **DanMachi 11:** Ruu runs back to the orphanage alone and is found holding a kitten when a building collapses. [[wiene|Wiene]] shields Ruu; to onlookers it looks as if the monster attacked the child.[@fm11-ruu, fm11-wiene] As Maria, Lai and Fina hold Ruu and cry, Ruu whispers, "No, you’re all wrong…Mother, Lai, Fina", and "Big brother…was right all along."[@fm11-ruu-words]
- **DanMachi 12:** "Big brother…You were right. Thank you…for fighting for all of us." Bell feels the words affirm the Xenos.[@fm12-ruu]
- **Sword Oratoria 10 and later:** Sword Oratoria 10 tells the same rescue from Loki Familia's side, as the other orphans scream Ruu's name and chase after the half-elf.[@so10-ruu] At the harvest festival Ruu clings to Bell's arm and, with Fina, brings guests into the dance; in DanMachi 18 Ruu sobs "as he tried to cheer Bell on", and in DanMachi 20 Lai, Fina and Ruu lean over the railings, "screaming their lungs out" for Bell.[@fm16-ruu, fm16-dance, fm18-hug, fm20-cheer]

## Ossian {#ossian}

**Ossian** is a small [[races#prum|prum]] boy of the orphanage.[@so10-ossian, fm16-ossian] Even among prums he is a child, a good head shorter than [[finn-deimne|Finn Deimne]], with curly brown hair.[@so10-ossian-finn] Meeting Finn, he hails him as Braver, hero of the prums, says he wants to become a prum like him, and recalls that everyone called prums amazing when [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]] returned from the [[floor-59|fifty-ninth floor]].[@so10-ossian-finn] In Sword Oratoria 10 he tells [[finn-deimne|Finn]] that he is "short and not very strong" and teased at the orphanage, but that Finn's adventures make him feel brave. Finn answers as Braver, though he feels empty.[@so10-ossian] Syr then comes to take the children home, as Maria is worried.[@so10-syr] At the harvest festival of DanMachi 16 he falls while dancing, and Syr hugs him.[@fm16-ossian] He scrapes his arm, and Syr reaches him before Bell or [[#maria|Maria]]; he cries into her chest.[@fm16-ossian-fall] Encouraged by her, he "manages to crack a smile", and Syr says, "I just wanted Ossian and everyone to smile."[@fm16-smile] After Bell and Maria bandage the scrape, he thanks Mom and Big Brother and joins in when the dance resumes.[@fm16-ossian-fall, fm16-ossian-dance]

In Sword Oratoria 10, Ossian asks Finn whether he is different from [[bell-cranell|Bell Cranell]], whom he calls big brother.[@so10-ossian-bell] He repeats that Bell hurt other adventurers for money and let monsters escape, calling him a thug like those of [[daedalus-street|Daedalus Street]], until [[#lai|Lai]] lunges at him.[@so10-ossian-bell] Finn defends Bell as Ossian leans forward.[@so10-ossian-leave]

In Sword Oratoria 10 Lai drags him away from Finn in case the monsters appear, and he pleads that he does not want "to be betrayed again"; leaving, he glances back at Finn once more.[@so10-lai] During Bell's fight with the gargoyle he watches "in shock", and "The disappointment in his eyes gradually faded".[@so10-ossian-plaza]
