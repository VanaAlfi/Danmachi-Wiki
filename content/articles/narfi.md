**Narfi** is a human adventurer of [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]], a blonde fighter with twin blades.[@so05-blondes, so07-greenhorns] A second-tier member in Sword Oratoria 7 and 8, she is a Level 4 by Sword Oratoria 10, when she and [[cruz-bussell|Cruz Bussell]] lead the Familia's secondary forces.[@so08-resent, so10-leaders] She is one of the support members on the expedition to Floor 59, and in Sword Oratoria 7 she is trapped in [[knossos|Knossos]] with [[gareth|Gareth]].[@so04-support, so07-greenhorns]

## Character

[[tsubaki|Tsubaki]] picks out Narfi and [[alicia-forestlight|Alicia]] as "the two beautiful blondes, elf and human respectively".[@so05-blondes] Narfi joined the Familia after [[anakity-autumn|Anakity]] and [[raul-nord|Raul]], who is a few years older than her, and she listens wide-eyed to Anakity's memories of the young [[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz]].[@so05-camp] Trapped in Knossos, she admits that with Gareth there they will probably get out alive, but she refuses to let him do everything: "Do we wanna be first-tiers someday or not?!"[@so07-hands]

> [!NOTE] Hair colour
> The novels' only description found calls her blonde, in Sword Oratoria 5.[@so05-blondes] Some adaptation artwork shown on this page gives her a different colour; images are labelled by source and are not evidence.
