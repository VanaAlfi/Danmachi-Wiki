**Haruhime** is a [[races#renart|renart]] from the [[far-east|Far East]] and a sorceress whose spell [[#uchide-no-kozuchi|Uchide no Kozuchi]] temporarily raises another person by a Level.[@fm07-name, fm07-history] Held by [[ishtar-familia|Ishtar Familia]] until DanMachi 7, she then joins [[hestia-familia|Hestia Familia]].[@fm07-join] She has long golden hair with a matching fox tail and ears, and green eyes; in DanMachi 7 she wears a red kimono.[@fm07-looks]

## Name

DanMachi 7 prints her name in the Far Eastern order, *Sanjyouno Haruhime*; later volumes print *Haruhime Sanjouno*.[@fm07-name, fm15-name] This wiki uses *Haruhime*, as the novels mostly do, and gives her full name in the Japanese order, *Sanjouno Haruhime*.

## Ishtar Familia

Haruhime was disowned at eleven, five years before DanMachi 7, after she ate, half asleep, "an extremely valuable divine offering": sacred food that a visiting prum official was carrying to their deity Amaterasu.[@fm07-offering] She came from a long line of nobles and had been raised by servants.[@fm07-sold] After the incident she was handed over to the prum guest, who abandoned her when ogres attacked their carriage; the marauders who saved her sold her in Orario, where Ishtar, who happened to be nearby, bought her for her Familia.[@fm07-sold] She ended up with Ishtar Familia in [[pleasure-quarter|the Pleasure Quarter]]. [[mikoto|Mikoto]] knew her as a child in the Far East.[@fm07-history] Her family's manor stood at the foot of the mountain where [[takemikazuchi|Takemikazuchi]]'s shrine took in orphans; she begged her father to share food with the poor shrine, and on Takemikazuchi's word Mikoto, [[chigusa|Chigusa]] and [[ouka|Ouka]] sneaked her out of the manor to play, until she was disowned.[@fm07-friends, fm15-shrine] [[ishtar|Ishtar]] plans to seal her magic and her soul in a [[killing-stone|Killing Stone]], a forbidden magic item made by combining a tamamo stone and a toba stone.[@fm07-history, fm07-stone] Sealed in the stone, her sorcery could be passed on to others, and each shard of it can unleash the same power, so Ishtar's Familia could use it to bring down [[freya-familia|Freya Familia]]; Haruhime would be left a soulless shell, and if any shard were lost she would never be normal again.[@fm07-plan] In Sword Oratoria 6 she is Ishtar Familia's veiled caster, whose unnamed boosting spell raises [[phryne-jamil|Phryne]]'s strength.[@so06-caster] In DanMachi 7 [[bell-cranell|Bell]], chased by Amazons through the Pleasure Quarter, hides in her room; they share a love of heroic tales, but she tells him that "No hero would ever come for someone as lowly as myself", because "Prostitutes are the ruin of heroes".[@fm07-meeting] At the Killing Stone ritual, when Bell demands to know what she really wants, she casts Uchide no Kozuchi on him against her own Familia and, with Phryne choking her, cries "I do not want to sell my body ever again…!"[@fm07-choice] When Bell comes back for her after the ritual and says that being a prostitute was too painful for her, Aisha tells him that Haruhime is in fact a virgin: the sight of a man's skin always made her pass out before the main event, though she believed she had given herself to many men.[@fm07-virgin] In DanMachi 15 she still wrongly believes she is no longer a virgin, and Mikoto has been too embarrassed to tell her.[@fm15-virgin]

[[bell-cranell|Bell]] breaks the Killing Stone and rescues her. After Ishtar is sent back to the heavens, Haruhime asks to join Hestia Familia.[@fm07-rescue, fm07-join]

## Hestia Familia

She joins Bell's party as a supporter and sorceress, and in DanMachi 8 secretly boosts Mikoto during the fight with the [[goliath|Goliath]].[@fm08-party] At home she insists on serving as the Familia's maid, to "finally stand on my own two feet", and Mikoto sees that her heart is "clearly fixed on Bell", who has become her hero.[@fm15-maid] In DanMachi 9 she becomes the closest carer, after Bell, of the [[xenos|Xenos]] girl [[wiene|Wiene]], and she embraces Wiene again when they are reunited.[@fm09-wiene, fm11-wiene] In DanMachi 11 she leaps from Hestia's lookout tower to go after Wiene; when [[bete-loga|Bete Loga]] corners them, she steps out alone ("I am alone!!") so that Bell can escape with Wiene, and her Level Boost lets [[aisha-belka|Aisha]], who calls her "my sweet little sister", take Bete on.[@fm11-bete] On the expedition of DanMachi 14, when the [[amphisbaena|Amphisbaena]]'s blue flames engulf the shore, she shelters under her [[goliath-robe|Goliath Robe]] and keeps singing in the fire; her accelerated casting gives Aisha the boost that kills the floor boss, and she collapses with Mind Down.[@fm14-amphisbaena]

In DanMachi 15 her Status qualifies her for Level 2, but Hestia holds the promotion back on [[aisha-belka|Aisha]]'s advice, without telling her.[@fm15-held] In DanMachi 17, when Freya Familia attacks Hestia Familia during the Goddess Festival, its four prum brothers cut her down despite Aisha's efforts to protect her, and afterwards Freya's Charm erases Bell from her memory.[@fm17-attack, fm17-memory] She has levelled up by the Familia War of DanMachi 18.[@fm18-level2] In the war game [[lilliluka-erde|Lilly]] keeps her hidden under a [[reverse-veil|reverse veil]], ready to boost the coalition's fighters; in the final battle against [[ottar|Ottar]] she boosts [[hedin|Hedin]], [[lyu-leon|Lyu]] and [[mia-grand|Mia]], then gives Bell the last tail she had kept for him and passes out.[@fm18-veil, fm18-final] Earlier in the battle, when a blast of magic swords blows her veil away, she keeps casting in full view of the city, although the broadcast will reveal her Level Boost: "The Haruhime who could only be protected and rescued is already dead!"[@fm18-exposed] With her Level Boost public after the war game, she faces kidnapping attempts and shady offers; at [[syr-flover|Syr]]'s request former Freya Familia members guard her around the clock, and [[hegni|Hegni]] alone stops seventy-one attempts in under a week.[@fm19-targeted] At the Denatus of DanMachi 20 the gods name her *Rikkou Kinshu* ("She’s only Level Two, but that’s some final boss energy") and [[lilliluka-erde|Lilly]] *Little Marshal*; with help from Hephaistos, Miach and Takemikazuchi, Hestia shuts down the other gods' joke names and wins "inoffensive names for the two of them".[@fm20-title] In DanMachi 20 the former [[freya-familia|Freya Familia]] members stand guard around [[hearthstone-manor|Hearthstone Manor]] to protect her and [[lyu-leon|Lyu]].[@fm20-guards]

### Uchide no Kozuchi {#uchide-no-kozuchi}

**Uchide no Kozuchi**, usually called **Level Boost**, is Haruhime's magic. It temporarily raises another person's [[level|Level]]; in every case shown, the target fights with the power of one Level higher.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-named, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm11-rescue, uchide-no-kozuchi.so12-five] It affects one person at a time and cannot be cast on Haruhime herself.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm15-limits]

- **Caster:** Haruhime[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-named]
- **Known as:** Level Boost[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-named, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm09-duration]
- **Effect:** Raises one person's [[level|Level]] for a limited time[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-named, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm15-limits]
- **Target:** One person, not Haruhime herself[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm15-limits]
- **Chant:** Two lines with the refrain "Grow"[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-chant, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm12-kokonoe]
- **Duration:** Up to 15 minutes, given enough Mind[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm09-duration, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm15-limits]
- **Recast:** About 10 minutes[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm11-rescue, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm15-limits]
- **Duration:** 20 minutes (DanMachi 18)[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm18-level2]
- **Recast:** 9 minutes (DanMachi 18)[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm18-level2]
- **First:** Sword Oratoria 6 (unnamed); named in DanMachi 7[@uchide-no-kozuchi.so06-phryne, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-named]

#### Name {#uchide-no-kozuchi-name}

The spell is named *Uchide no Kozuchi* from DanMachi 7 onward, and *Level Boost* names its effect from DanMachi 7 to DanMachi 20 and in Sword Oratoria 12.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-named, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm09-duration, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm20-kreis, uchide-no-kozuchi.so12-five] The capitals vary: DanMachi 11, 14 and 15 print only lower-case *level boost*, and DanMachi 12 and 18 mostly do.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm11-rescue, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm12-kokonoe, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm14-boost, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm15-excelia, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm18-level2] Sword Oratoria 6 shows the spell without naming it.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.so06-phryne]

#### Incantation {#uchide-no-kozuchi-incantation}

**Complete printed chant (FM07).**[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-chant]

> —Grow. That power and that vessel. Breadth of wealth and breadth of wishes. Until the bell tolls, bring forth glory and illusion. —Grow. Confine divine offerings within this body. This golden light bestowed from above. Into the hammer and into the ground, may it bestow good fortune upon you. —Grow.

In the Japanese edition the first line agrees in sense with the print, and the second is {{ja|神饌を食らいしこの体。神に賜いしこの金光。槌へと至り土へと還り、どうか貴方へ祝福を|kami o kuraishi kono karada. kami ni tamaishi kono konkō. tsuchi e to itari tsuchi e to kaeri, dōka anata e shukufuku o}}, *This body that devoured the sacred offering; this golden light granted by the gods; reaching the hammer and returning to the earth, please, a blessing upon you*. It speaks of Haruhime herself, who ate the offerings as a child: she says *kami*, the word for a god, and *sacred offering* is the written meaning; *tsuchi* is both *hammer* and *earth*. The third call is drawn out, {{ja|大きくなぁれ|ōkiku naare}}.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-chant, fm07-offering]

{{nocite}} **Notes and other printed variants**

{{nocite}} The chant is printed with the same wording in every full print. 

- **Structure:** Haruhime begins with "—Grow.", recites two lines, each followed by "Grow", and then names the spell.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-chant, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm12-kokonoe]
- **Excerpt (first line):** "That power and that vessel. Breadth of wealth and breadth of wishes."[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-chant, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm12-kokonoe, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm18-chant]
- **Second line:** speaks of the body that devoured the sacred offering and the golden light granted by the gods, which reaches the hammer and returns to the earth, and asks a blessing on the target.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-chant]
- **Release:** "Uchide no Kozuchi."[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-chant, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm11-hammer]

|---|---|
| DanMachi 7 | The full sequence: three "Grow"s, both lines and the name. It is the first print of the chant.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-chant] |
| DanMachi 11 | The full sequence twice: in Bell's narration for his own boost, and for Aisha's boost against Bete.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm11-hammer] |
| DanMachi 12 | The full sequence, straight after [[#kokonoe|Kokonoe]]'s chant, then "Dance!" to send the charged tails out.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm12-kokonoe] |
| DanMachi 14 | "Grow. Uchide no Kozuchi!" for the first boost through Kokonoe, the opening and first line for a later one, and the full sequence for Aisha's last charge against the Amphisbaena, sung so fast ("Accelerated casting") that her casting speed surpasses upper-class magicians'.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm14-boost, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm14-accelerated] |
| DanMachi 18 | The full sequence after Kokonoe, ending "Uchide no Kozuchi—Dance!"[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm18-chant] |

As she chants, a cloud of golden light forms above her and becomes a hammer of light, which falls on the target. DanMachi 11 notes that, magic circle or not, the spell gives off golden magic light even before it takes effect, which marks it out as peculiar.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-chant, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm11-hammer] Casting it through Kokonoe is described as linking two spells' chants: DanMachi 12 calls this "Concatenated Casting", and DanMachi 18 "chained casting".[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm12-kokonoe, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm18-chant]

#### Effects {#uchide-no-kozuchi-effects}

Every boost lifts the target by one Level: DanMachi 7 says it lets the recipient move "with power and speed one level above their Status", and Sword Oratoria 12 calls it a sorcery that "increased the target’s level by one".[@uchide-no-kozuchi.so06-phryne, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-named, uchide-no-kozuchi.so12-five] In DanMachi 7 it raises [[bell-cranell|Bell]] from Level 3 to Level 4 power, and gives [[aisha-belka|Aisha]] Level-4-class strength.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-named, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-aisha] In Sword Oratoria 12 it lifts Level 4 adventurers to pseudo-Level 5 and [[bete-loga|Bete]] from Level 6 to pseudo-Level 7.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.so12-five]

Once cast, the boost lasts until its time runs out or Haruhime ends it; knocking her unconscious does not stop it.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-chant]

The boost is temporary and does not count as a Level Up. In DanMachi 18, [[anya-fromel|Anya Fromel]], a Level 4, fights at pseudo-Level 5 during the [[war-game|War Game]] only.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm18-anya]

Fighting under the boost costs the target experience: they receive half the normal [[excelia|excelia]] or less, while Haruhime's own growth is unaffected, because the spell cannot be cast on herself.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm15-excelia] The sudden power is also hard to control: Mikoto often feels her body run out of control because her mind cannot keep up (DanMachi 12), Asfi is "tossed about by the newfound power" (Sword Oratoria 12), and in DanMachi 18 the fighters boosted against Ottar must "grab the reins with all of their mental fortitude".[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm12-control, uchide-no-kozuchi.so12-asfi, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm18-ottar]

#### Limits {#uchide-no-kozuchi-limits}

The limits changed as Haruhime grew. At Level 1 a boost lasts up to fifteen minutes if she supplies enough Mind, and she must wait about ten minutes before casting it again.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm09-duration, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm15-limits, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm11-rescue] Because the wait is shorter than the effect, a second person can be boosted for about five minutes while the first boost is still running; this does not show that two boosts stack on one person.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm15-limits, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm14-overlap]

After Haruhime reaches Level 2, before the War Game in DanMachi 18, a boost lasts twenty minutes and the wait falls to nine minutes.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm18-level2]

In DanMachi 20, [[nina-tulle|Nina]]'s [[magic#magia-kreis|Magia Kreis]] extends a boost from twenty minutes to about twenty-five. That is an outside enhancement, not a change to the spell itself.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm20-kreis]

| Haruhime's Level | Duration | Wait before recasting |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Up to 15 minutes with enough Mind[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm09-duration, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm15-limits] | About 10 minutes[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm11-rescue, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm15-limits] |
| 2 (from DanMachi 18) | 20 minutes[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm18-level2] | 9 minutes[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm18-level2] |

> [!NOTE] Aisha's one minute
> In DanMachi 7 Aisha tells Bell he has "only one minute" left because the boost cast on him earlier is about to wear off; that is the time remaining, not the spell's duration.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-one-minute] Experiments in DanMachi 9 show that a boost lasts up to fifteen minutes.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm09-duration]

#### With Kokonoe {#uchide-no-kozuchi-with-kokonoe}

Haruhime's second spell, [[#kokonoe|Kokonoe]], stores the effect of a spell chanted afterwards in magical tails and releases it through them, so she can boost several people at once.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm12-kokonoe] She can use five tails in DanMachi 12, boosting [[mikoto|Mikoto]], [[ouka|Ouka]], [[welf-crozzo|Welf]], [[daphne|Daphne]] and Aisha together, and six after reaching Level 2.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm12-kokonoe, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm18-level2] The printed maximum is nine tails; each extra tail costs more Mind, and she can choose to use fewer.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm12-kokonoe, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm14-tails]

### Kokonoe {#kokonoe}

**Kokonoe** is Haruhime's second spell. It gives her tails of light and charges them with the effect of the spell she chants straight afterwards, so that she can release that spell as many times as she has tails with a single effort of will.[@kokonoe.fm12-kokonoe] With [[#uchide-no-kozuchi|Uchide no Kozuchi]], this lets her give Level Boosts to several people at once.[@kokonoe.fm12-kokonoe]

- **User:** Haruhime[@kokonoe.fm12-kokonoe]
- **Type:** Enchantment, listed under Magic[@kokonoe.fm15-card]
- **First used:** DanMachi 12[@kokonoe.fm12-kokonoe]
- **Source:** A [[grimoire|grimoire]][@kokonoe.fm12-chant]
- **Chant:** Name first, then five printed pieces[@kokonoe.fm12-chant, kokonoe.fm18-chant]
- **Maximum:** Nine[@kokonoe.fm12-kokonoe, kokonoe.fm15-card]
- **Usable:** Five in DanMachi 12; six at Level 2[@kokonoe.fm12-kokonoe, kokonoe.fm18-six]

#### Status entry {#kokonoe-status-entry}

Haruhime's DanMachi 15 card lists Kokonoe under Magic, beside Uchide no Kozuchi. It describes the spell as an enchantment with chant linking that loads the effect of the linked spell, with a maximum of nine activations.[@kokonoe.fm15-card]

#### Incantation {#kokonoe-incantation}

**Name first, then complete printed chant; followed by Uchide no Kozuchi (FM12).**[@kokonoe.fm12-chant]

> Kokonoe. Beloved snow. Beloved crimson. Beloved white light. Please let me be beside you—this love I have found at the end of two thousand nights. My name is Magic Fox, former destroyer. My name is Ancient Song, former dreamer. For you who beat your wings like a bird, I shall allow the nine spirits to dwell within me. Echo song of gold, sacred poem of Tamamo. White face, golden fur, king of nine tails. Oh tails of the auspicious beast, consume all, grant all wishes—

{{nocite}} **Notes and other printed variants**

{{nocite}} Kokonoe's chant is printed in full twice, with the same wording. 

- **Name first:** unusually, the spell "began not with a chant but with the announcement of the spell's name": Haruhime says "Kokonoe." and then chants.[@kokonoe.fm12-chant]
- **Excerpt (first piece):** "Beloved snow. Beloved crimson. Beloved white light."[@kokonoe.fm12-chant, kokonoe.fm18-chant]
- **The rest:** four more printed pieces, in which she calls herself Magic Fox and Ancient Song, lets the nine spirits dwell in her, and invokes the golden song of Tamamo and the tails of the auspicious beast.[@kokonoe.fm12-chant, kokonoe.fm18-chant]
- **Linking:** the last piece runs straight into the "—Grow." that begins [[#uchide-no-kozuchi|Uchide no Kozuchi]]'s chant. When that spell is complete, "Dance!" sends the charged tails out to their targets.[@kokonoe.fm12-chant, kokonoe.fm18-chant]

| Volume | What is printed |
|---|---|
| DanMachi 12 | The full sequence: name, five pieces, Uchide no Kozuchi's chant, "Uchide no Kozuchi!", "Dance!"[@kokonoe.fm12-chant] |
| DanMachi 14 | The name, "Kokonoe!", and the first piece; the rest is narrated up to "Grow. Uchide no Kozuchi!"[@kokonoe.fm14-name] |
| DanMachi 18 | The full sequence, in front of the whole city, ending "Uchide no Kozuchi—Dance!"[@kokonoe.fm18-chant] |

DanMachi 12 calls this linking of two chants "Concatenated Casting"; DanMachi 18 calls it "chained casting". It is different from [[riveria|Riveria]]'s Concatenated Chanting, which joins levels of one spell; see [[magic#casting-techniques|Magic]].[@kokonoe.fm12-chant, kokonoe.fm18-chant]

#### Tails {#kokonoe-tails}

The spell can make up to nine tails, but in DanMachi 12 she can produce only five.[@kokonoe.fm12-kokonoe] After reaching Level 2 before the Familia War of DanMachi 18, she can handle six, and her Mind itself has grown, so using five no longer brings on the Mind Down it caused on the expedition to the lower levels.[@kokonoe.fm18-six]

| DanMachi 18 | Deploys five of her six tails to hold the line in the Familia War.[@kokonoe.fm18-six] |
