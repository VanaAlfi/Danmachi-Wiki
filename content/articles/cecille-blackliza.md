**Cecille Blackliza** is a blue-haired human Level 2 high smith and the captain of [[astrea-familia|Astrea Familia]]'s reestablished group, who makes [[equipment#alvs-iustitia|Alvs Iustitia]] for [[lyu-leon|Lyu Leon]].[@fc03-identity, fc03-captain, fc03-sword] Born in [[zolingam|Solingen]] to the Blackliza family of smiths, she is the youngest of her father's eight children and his only daughter.[@fc03-family]
