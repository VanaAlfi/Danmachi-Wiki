**Uchide no Kozuchi**, usually called **Level Boost**, is [[haruhime|Haruhime]]'s magic. It temporarily raises another person's [[level|Level]]; in every case shown, the target fights with the power of one Level higher.[@fm07-named, fm11-rescue, so12-five] It affects one person at a time and cannot be cast on Haruhime herself.[@fm15-limits]

## Name

The spell is named *Uchide no Kozuchi* from DanMachi 7 onward; *Level Boost* is the name used for its effect in DanMachi 7–9, and DanMachi 14 writes it in lower case.[@fm07-named, fm09-duration, fm14-boost] Sword Oratoria 6 shows the spell without naming it.[@so06-phryne]

## Effects

{{inference}} Every boost shown lifts the target by exactly one Level, though no passage states this as a rule.[@so06-phryne, fm07-named, so12-five] In DanMachi 7 it raises Bell from Level 3 to Level 4 power, and gives Aisha Level-4-class strength.[@fm07-named] In Sword Oratoria 12 it lifts Level 4 adventurers to pseudo-Level 5 and Bete from Level 6 to pseudo-Level 7.[@so12-five]

The boost is temporary and does not count as a Level Up. In DanMachi 18, [[anya-fromel|Anya Fromel]], a Level 4, fights at pseudo-Level 5 during the War Game only.[@fm18-anya]

Fighting under the boost costs the target experience: they receive less than half the normal excelia, while Haruhime's own development is not reduced by casting it.[@fm15-excelia]

## Limits

The limits changed as Haruhime grew. At Level 1 a boost lasts up to fifteen minutes if she supplies enough Mind, and she must wait about ten minutes before casting it again.[@fm09-duration, fm15-limits] Because the wait is shorter than the effect, a second person can be boosted for about five minutes while the first boost is still running; this does not show that two boosts stack on one person.[@fm15-limits]

After Haruhime reaches Level 2, before the War Game in DanMachi 18, a boost lasts twenty minutes and the wait falls to nine minutes.[@fm18-level2]

In DanMachi 20, [[nina-tulle|Nina]]'s [[magia-kreis|Magia Kreis]] extends a boost from twenty minutes to about twenty-five. That is an outside enhancement, not a change to the spell itself.[@fm20-kreis]

| Haruhime's Level | Duration | Wait before recasting |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Up to 15 minutes with enough Mind[@fm09-duration, fm15-limits] | About 10 minutes[@fm11-rescue, fm15-limits] |
| 2 (from DanMachi 18) | 20 minutes[@fm18-level2] | 9 minutes[@fm18-level2] |

> [!NOTE] An early estimate
> The earliest description suggested a boost lasted about a minute. Later experiments in DanMachi 9 established the longer duration, so the one-minute figure should not be treated as the spell's limit.[@fm07-named, fm09-duration]

## With Kokonoe

Haruhime's second spell, [[kokonoe|Kokonoe]], stores the effect of a spell chanted afterwards in magical tails and releases it through them, so she can boost several people at once.[@fm12-kokonoe] She can use five tails in DanMachi 12, boosting [[mikoto|Mikoto]], Ouka, Welf, Daphne and Aisha together, and six after reaching Level 2.[@fm12-kokonoe, fm18-level2] The printed maximum is nine tails; each extra tail costs more Mind, and she can choose to use fewer.[@fm12-kokonoe]
