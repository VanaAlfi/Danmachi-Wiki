**Equipment** in the English novels covers the weapons, armour and magic items that adventurers carry into the [[dungeon|Dungeon]], many of them made to order by smiths such as [[welf-crozzo|Welf Crozzo]] and [[tsubaki|Tsubaki Collbrande]] or by [[hephaistos-familia|Hephaistos Familia]] and [[goibniu-familia|Goibniu Familia]].[@fm12-hakugen, so04-roland, so01-frosvirt, fm08-pair] Weapons are graded by tier, from Mikoto's Kotetsu, "her third-tier adventurer’s weapon", to Hegni's Victim Abyss, "a first-tier blade", and the custom weapons of first-tier adventurers are often called Superiors: Frosvirt is "a second-tier Superior weapon", the Fortia Spear "a Superior weapon with a golden tip".[@fm11-kotetsu, fm16-abyss, so03-frosvirt, so04-spear]

{{nocite}} This page gathers the named gear, one searchable section per item, like the [[magic|Magic]] and [[monsters|Monsters]] pages; gear that is named only once or twice is in the table [[#briefly-named|Briefly named equipment]]. Named gear with a page of its own: the weapons [[hestia-knife|Hestia Knife]], [[ushiwakamaru|Ushiwakamaru]], [[desperate|Desperate]] (with the Durandal weapons), [[urga|Urga]] and the [[magic-sword|magic swords]]; the [[goliath-robe|Goliath Robe]] and Nahza's silver arm [[airgetlam|Airgetlam]]; and the magic items [[hades-head|Hades Head]], [[reverse-veil|Reverse Veil]], [[oculus|Oculus]], [[silence-lyra|Silence Lyra]], [[status-thief|Status Thief]], [[killing-stone|Killing Stone]] and [[grimoire|Grimoire]]. [[potions|Potions]] and [[metals|Metals]] have combined pages like this one.

## Weapons {#weapons}

### Hakugen {#hakugen}

Hakugen is the white longknife Welf makes for [[bell-cranell|Bell]] in DanMachi 12. "Measuring thirty-five celch from tip to base", it falls between the [[hestia-knife|Hestia Knife]] and a baselard in size; "it was made from unicorn horn, a rare drop item", and its blade is "even sharper than the lost Ushiwakamaru". Bell fights with the Hestia Knife in his right hand and Hakugen in his left.[@fm12-hakugen] Welf says he used "an insanely valuable drop item that would make a healer or a mage scream if they found out"; Bell finds it lighter and keener than the [[ushiwakamaru|Ushiwakamaru]] series, though "not so well suited to defense or fending off attacks".[@fm12-sharpen]

In DanMachi 14, poisoned in the deep levels, Bell remembers that unicorn horn neutralizes poisons and stabs Hakugen into his own wound: "the unicorn-horn knife sucked the black poison from Bell’s wound", and the blade returns to its white state.[@fm14-antidote] In the Familia War of DanMachi 18 it is still one of his two weapons, and in his last fight it ignites "with a lightning flame".[@fm18-gear, fm18-hakugen] See also [[monsters#unicorn|Unicorn]].

### Shikou-Kazuki {#shikou-kazuki}

Shikou-Kazuki is the [[magic-sword|magic sword]] Welf forges during the expedition of DanMachi 14, "a gallant crimson longsword". With [[cassandra|Cassandra]] gripping the hilt beside him, he roars its name, "Shikou—Kazuki!", and thrusts it into the ground; its "dramatic tongue of dancing flame" burns a whole swarm of monsters.[@fm14-kazuki] In DanMachi 15 he shows it to [[hephaistos|Hephaistos]]: "It was Shikou-Kazuki, and it had been forged in the Dungeon."[@fm15-kazuki] In the Familia War of DanMachi 18 he is reduced to using it "as a shield", and in DanMachi 20 its "purging fire" protects the party's flank.[@fm18-kazuki, fm20-kazuki] What the sword means for Welf's craft is told on [[welf-crozzo|his page]].[@fm15-kazuki]

**Name:** DanMachi 14 prints *Shikou Kazuki*; DanMachi 15, 18 and 20 print *Shikou-Kazuki*. Welf shouts "Kazuki" when he uses it.[@fm14-kazuki, fm15-kazuki, fm18-kazuki]

### Tenka and Chizan {#tenka-and-chizan}

Tenka and Chizan are "one of a pair of extremely high-quality daggers forged by Goibniu Familia".[@fm08-pair] When [[mikoto|Yamato Mikoto]] leaves for [[hestia-familia|Hestia Familia]] in DanMachi 8, [[takemikazuchi|Takemikazuchi]] slips the black dagger into her sash: "The male is Tenka, the female Chizan…I give this one to you now, and I’ll carry the other." He keeps the white Tenka, which will be hers "on the day you come back to us".[@fm08-chizan] Mikoto fights with Chizan, "one half of a set of twin daggers that she always kept on her person", and Takemikazuchi draws Tenka, "the male half of a paired set of daggers", when he trains her and Chigusa in DanMachi 12.[@fm08-fight, fm09-twin, fm12-tenka]

### Kotetsu {#kotetsu}

Kotetsu is the katana Welf forges for Mikoto in DanMachi 8, with a sheath in a black and silver striped pattern. Welf proposes "Iron Tiger, Kotetsu…No, Stripey, Shimajirou", and Mikoto, begging on her hands and knees, wins "the battle to give the new katana the name Kotetsu".[@fm08-kotetsu] She fights with it from then on: in DanMachi 8 she sheathes Chizan and "drew Kotetsu in one swift motion", and DanMachi 11 calls it "her third-tier adventurer’s weapon".[@fm08-fight, fm11-kotetsu] In DanMachi 12 she returns the "Iron Tiger Kotetsu" to her hip to draw [[#shunsan|Shunsan]].[@fm12-shunsan]

### Shunsan {#shunsan}

Shunsan is "a new blade made by Welf, called Shunsan", whose name means *Spring Hail*. In DanMachi 12 Mikoto draws it from her back, sheath and all; its edge is "far superior to the two blades at her hip, Kotetsu and Chizan".[@fm12-shunsan] DanMachi 14 calls it her longsword, and she draws it again in DanMachi 18.[@fm14-shunsan, fm18-shunsan]

### Kougou {#kougou}

Kougou is the battle-ax of [[ouka|Kashima Ouka]]. Welf forged it from varmath, "a high-quality Dungeon ore" found only in the lower levels that Hephaistos had given him, and made Ouka's shield from the leftovers; he charged 700,000 valis for the set, "a steep discount".[@fm12-kougou] Ouka took out a loan to buy it, and before the expedition of DanMachi 14 he sought from Takemikazuchi "a new special move befitting Kougou"; against a dragon's tough scales its blows throw "A violent storm of sparks".[@fm14-kougou] He still fights with it in DanMachi 18.[@fm18-kougou] For the ore see [[metals#varmath|Metals]].

### Fortia Spear {#fortia-spear}

The Fortia Spear is [[finn-deimne|Finn Deimne]]'s spear, often called the *Brave Fortia Spear*: "it was a Superior weapon with a golden tip". In Sword Oratoria 4 he fights with it in his right hand and the [[#roland-series|Roland Spear]] in his left, and throws it so that it becomes "a beam of light".[@so04-spear] Sword Oratoria 11 calls it "the weapon whose very name was the embodiment of courage".[@so11-courage] In Sword Oratoria 7 [[levis|Levis]] cuts through its handle and into Finn, and the golden spearhead falls, "cleaved from its shaft".[@so07-levis, so07-cleaved] In Minor Myths and Legends 1 [[goibniu|Goibniu]] hands Finn "the prum hero’s bespoke weapon" again, "practically restored to mint condition".[@ss01-fortia]

### Roland series {#roland-series}

The Roland series is the set of five [[desperate#durandal-weapons|Durandal]] Superiors that [[tsubaki|Tsubaki]] forges for [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]]'s first-tier adventurers before the expedition of Sword Oratoria 4: "I call it the Roland series. Each one of them comes exactly as ordered." Finn receives a long spear, Gareth an ax, Bete twin blades, Tiona a giant sword and Tione a halberd.[@so04-roland] Loki Familia had ordered Durandal weapons because the caterpillar monsters from the fiftieth floor onward secrete "corrosive, weapon-destroying fluids".[@so04-durandals]

- **Roland Spear:** Finn's "silver Durandal spear".[@so04-spear]
- **Roland Ax:** [[gareth|Gareth]]'s, carried beside his Grand Ax.[@so04-ax]
- **Dual Roland:** Bete's "new twin Durandal blades".[@so04-dual] In Sword Oratoria 12 [[filvis|Filvis]] steals them and drives them into his thighs, and Minor Myths and Legends 2 calls them "the twin Unbreakables".[@so12-dual, ss02-dual]
- **Roland Blade:** Tiona's large Durandal sword, about which she grumbles, "This isn’t Urga!" (see [[urga|Urga]]).[@so04-blade]
- **Roland Halberd:** Tione's, "the Durandal special" a supporter hands her in Sword Oratoria 11.[@so11-halberd]

**Name:** the English text prints *Roland*; Fandom's *Rolan* forms are search aliases.[@so04-roland]

### Victim Abyss {#victim-abyss}

Victim Abyss is [[hegni|Hegni]]'s sword: "My beloved blade, Victim Abyss, is a sword specialized in destroying vanguards." It is "a superior weapon, a first-tier blade with a keen edge that a certain hexer had been involved in creating".[@fm16-abyss] Familia Chronicle 2 describes "a jet-black blade with a jagged lightning-bolt shape".[@fc02-abyss] It is a curse weapon: it extends its slash range at the cost of its user's stamina, and its wounds are a curse that "couldn’t be immediately and fully healed by potions or magic". Hegni calls on it, "Rip and tear, Victim Abyss!", against Lyu in DanMachi 18.[@fm18-abyss] In Astrea Record 3 [[hedin|Hedin]] and Hegni swap weapons on purpose and Hedin wields it: "Sip and slurp, you rotten sword. Victim Abyss!" (see [[apate-and-alecto-familias|Apate Familia and Alecto Familia]]).[@ar03-swap]

### Dizaria {#dizaria}

Dizaria is [[hedin|Hedin Selland]]'s rhomphaia, with a long blade and a hilt shaped like a holy tree: "Its name was Dizaria." It is "both an excellent polearm while also serving as a staff to boost magic power".[@fc02-dizaria] DanMachi 18 adds that it is made of "a branch of sacred tree, just like Lyu’s Alvs Iustitia", with a magic stone at its top. Hedin swings it like a conductor's baton as he directs his magic against [[ottar|Ottar]], until Ottar cuts both him and the weapon.[@fm18-dizaria] In Astrea Record 3 Hegni wields it in the swap described under [[#victim-abyss|Victim Abyss]].[@ar03-swap]

### Zolas {#zolas}

Zolas are the Kukri knives of [[hyrute-sisters|Tione]], who fights with "her twin Zolas".[@so11-zolas, so12-zolas] In Sword Oratoria 6 she has "ran out of spares for my Zolas" and wants more **Filuka** throwing knives; in Sword Oratoria 7 a Filuka knife flies from her hand before she draws "her Kukri knives—Zolas this time".[@so06-repair, so07-zolas]

### Forest's Teardrop {#forests-teardrop}

Forest's Teardrop is [[lefiya|Lefiya Viridis]]'s staff, "designed specifically for magic users". It is made from "a white ore called seiros that mages were rather fond of" and increases the base strength of Magic; with a magic crystal and "a rare item known as Tear of the Elder Tree", it suits elven magic.[@so03-teardrop] The magic stone at its tip glows as she pours her Mind into a spell.[@so05-teardrop] In Sword Oratoria 12 the staff is "shattered to pieces", and Lefiya fights on with Filvis's weapons (see [[#tear-pain|Tear Pain]]).[@so12-teardrop] In Sword Oratoria 13 its remnants become a rod that joins a wand made from Filvis's Protector's White Torch into "the twin staff Fairy Dust, Lefiya’s new weapon", a staff taller than she is, "much like Riveria’s Magna Alfs"; they are "the first weapons made in Orario with a dedicated linking function".[@so13-fairydust] For the ore see [[metals#seiros|Metals]].

**Name:** printed *Forest's Teardrop* (Sword Oratoria 3) and *Forest Teardrop* (Sword Oratoria 12 and 13).[@so03-teardrop, so12-teardrop]

### Tear Pain {#tear-pain}

Tear Pain is [[filvis|Filvis Challia]]'s spell blade. In Sword Oratoria 12, her own staff destroyed, Lefiya draws "The spell blade Tear Pain and the wand Protector’s White Torch", "the weapons that Filvis had cast aside", to stop her.[@so12-teardrop] In Sword Oratoria 13 she still fights with it, "Wielding Tear Pain like a conductor’s wand", and the wand becomes half of her twin staff Fairy Dust (see [[#forests-teardrop|Forest's Teardrop]]).[@so13-tearpain, so13-fairydust]

**Name:** Fandom's *Guardian's White Torch* is printed *Protector's White Torch*.[@so12-teardrop]

### Magna Alfs {#magna-alfs}

Magna Alfs is [[riveria|Riveria Ljos Alf]]'s silvery-white staff, "the first-tier magic user’s weapon" and "that highest-tier magic staff".[@so04-magna, so12-magna] Lefiya's twin staff Fairy Dust is likened to it.[@so13-fairydust]

**Name:** Sword Oratoria 4 prints *Magna Alf*; Sword Oratoria 12 and 13 print *Magna Alfs*.[@so04-magna, so12-magna, so13-fairydust]

### Alvs Lumina {#alvs-lumina}

Alvs Lumina is [[lyu-leon|Lyu Leon]]'s wooden sword, "the weapon created from a holy tree in her homeland".[@fm05-lumina] In Astrea Record 3 it is newly completed, and Lyu says of its name: "Alvs Lumina. Lady Astrea thought of it."[@ar03-lumina] It is "her second-tier weapon fashioned from the branch of a holy tree", and in DanMachi 13 it bursts into pieces against the [[juggernaut|Juggernaut]].[@fm13-lumina] [[bors|Bors]] recovered a fragment, which Lyu gives for her new weapon in Familia Chronicle 3: "The weapon that was broken was given to me by a dear friend."[@fc03-lumina]

### Alvs Iustitia {#alvs-iustitia}

Alvs Iustitia is the stardust sword made for Lyu in [[zolingam|Zolingam]] from the fragment of Alvs Lumina. In Familia Chronicle 3 Cecille presents "a dark green wooden sword, longer than Alvs Lumina", and names it: "The stardust sword is Alvs Iustitia!" "Iustitia was the justice over which Astrea presided", and also a title.[@fc03-iustitia] DanMachi 18 tells how the broken Alvs Lumina "was processed and reborn into the stardust sword, Alvs Iustitia"; in DanMachi 19 a touch of it sets off a chain of ash explosions.[@fm18-iustitia, fm19-iustitia] Hedin's [[#dizaria|Dizaria]] is made of sacred tree like it.[@fm18-dizaria]

### Futaba {#futaba}

Futaba are Lyu's "twin shortswords".[@fm16-futaba] She fights with them in Familia Chronicle 1 and 3, and in DanMachi 14, alone with Bell in the deep levels, she thinks: "Futaba is my only weapon".[@fc01-futaba, fc03-futaba, fm14-futaba] In DanMachi 16 she holds them against Hegni's black longsword, and in DanMachi 17 an elf who has slipped past her guards drops "Lyu’s Futaba and the key to her restraints".[@fm16-futaba, fm17-futaba] In Astrea Record 2 [[gojouno-kaguya|Gojouno Kaguya]] calls out a technique of the same name: "Iai Strike: Futaba!"[@ar02-futaba]

## Armour and clothing {#armour}

### Frosvirt {#frosvirt}

Frosvirt are [[bete-loga|Bete Loga]]'s metal boots, "A second-tier Superior made by Hephaistos Familia".[@so01-frosvirt] "What set these mythril boots apart from the rest was their ability to absorb magic": jewels set in them take in Aiz's wind or the power of a magic dagger, and Bete's kicks carry it.[@so03-frosvirt, so01-frosvirt, so04-topaz] In Sword Oratoria 8 [[gareth|Gareth]] explains that the boots are "a downgraded version of the spell" [[bete-loga#hati-frosvirt|Hati]]: Bete, refusing to use his own magic, had Tsubaki make them instead.[@so08-broken]

After the fight with Levis on the twenty-fourth floor the boots were "completely destroyed", and [[tsubaki|Tsubaki]] had to rebuild them before the expedition of Sword Oratoria 4; in Sword Oratoria 6 Bete brings money "For fixin’ up my Frosvirt".[@so04-durandals, so06-repair] In Sword Oratoria 8 two blows shatter the inlaid yellow jewels, and with their core gone "the mythril Superiors fell silent"; Sword Oratoria 13 speaks of his "reworked Frosvirt".[@so08-broken, so13-frosvirt] Minor Myths and Legends 2 calls them his "silver boots".[@ss02-dual] For the metal see [[metals#mythril|Metals]].

**Name:** the English text prints *Frosvirt*; Fandom's *Flosvirt* is a search alias.[@so01-frosvirt, so03-frosvirt]

### Pyonkichi {#pyonkichi}

Pyonkichi is the light armour Welf makes for [[bell-cranell|Bell]]. In DanMachi 2 Bell's new armour is "so light that he could barely feel it", and his Status lists his equipment as “Pyonkina” Rabbit Armor MK-II.[@fm02-protector, fm02-sheet] DanMachi 4 recalls that Bell wore the smith's work every day "until it was destroyed by the Minotaur" and that its name was "Pyonkichi"; Bell wishes he could tell Welf that "maybe the problem is the names he gives to his finished pieces". His next armour got the same name: "The current model is the MK-III."[@fm04-pyonkichi, fm04-names, fm04-mk3]

He wears Pyonkichi in the War Game and after it, and by DanMachi 10 it is "the fifth incarnation of Pyonkichi", with dual adamantite mixed in; DanMachi 12 calls his dir-adamantite armour "currently on its fifth reincarnation" (see [[metals#adamantite|Metals]]).[@fm07-pyonkichi, fm10-pyonkichi, fm12-armor] In DanMachi 12 the gods call Welf "that Pyonkichi kid", and one sings a song called "Lucky Pyonkichi".[@fm12-denatus]

**Name:** DanMachi 2 prints *Pyonkina*; DanMachi 4, 7, 10 and 12 print *Pyonkichi*. Fandom lists models MK-II to MK-V; the checked text names MK-II (as Pyonkina) and MK-III and speaks of a fifth incarnation.[@fm02-sheet, fm04-mk3, fm10-pyonkichi]

### Spirit cloth {#spirit-cloth}

Spirit cloth is fabric "infused with the protection of spirits", with high defence against certain elements: "the salamander wool was imbued with fire resistance, undine cloth with water resistance, and so on". Against a spirit's own magic it creates "a massive repulsion, negating the magic".[@so12-flags] DanMachi 5 explains that spirits ("fairies" in its wording), such as salamanders, sylphs, undines and gnomes, are classified by element, so "Fairy Protection" items vary with the kind of spirit that made them.[@fm05-salamander] DanMachi 12 calls the material "spirit protective cloth", and Sword Oratoria 6 says that such cloth "could outperform even the armor of the most advanced smiths".[@fm12-undine, so06-undine]

For the second assault on [[knossos|Knossos]] in Sword Oratoria 12, Finn gathers spirit cloths for every squad. The battle clothes under their armour are "a blend of all the different types of spirit cloths", and the vanguards carry **spirit flags**, "a custom defensive gear created by Hephaistos Familia", crimson of salamander wool or blue of undine cloth, which have to be swung into the oncoming magic.[@so12-flags] In Familia Chronicle 3 [[zolingam|Zolingam]], with its spirit forge, is supplied with "salamander wool, undine cloth, and various other spirit materials".[@fc03-zolingam]

#### Salamander wool {#salamander-wool}

"Salamander wool, made by the fire-wielding salamanders, is very good at protecting against fire and heat"; Bell calls it an "anti-flame material", also said to be good against cold.[@fm05-salamander] Before letting Bell's party into the middle levels in DanMachi 4, [[eina-tulle|Eina]] gives him coupons for it at Babel and makes it a condition: "Everyone in your party must be equipped with salamander wool."[@fm04-coupons] In DanMachi 5 it saves them: "The salamander wool had protected their bodies from the intense heat."[@fm05-heat]

In DanMachi 9 the adventurers of [[rivira|Rivira]] hand out "robes made of burn-resistant salamander wool as an advance payment" to those who help against the firebirds, and Bell hides [[wiene|Wiene]] wrapped in salamander wool.[@fm09-firebirds, fm09-wiene] For Loki Familia's expedition in Sword Oratoria 4 the Guild cannot supply enough ("Babel can’t get all the salamander wool and undine robes we ordered"), and Raul has to "request some from other factions".[@so04-shortage, so04-wool]

#### Undine cloth {#undine-cloth}

Undine cloth is the blue spirit cloth of the water spirits: "It’s waterproof, exactly the opposite of the salamander wool".[@fm12-undine, so12-flags] "The most important thing about Undine cloth is that its protective properties become fully activated underwater. It reduces water resistance and water pressure and improves underwater movement." Hestia Familia's party wears it in the [[water-capital|Water Capital]] in DanMachi 12, where it is called "an absolute must for water quests".[@fm12-undine]

In Sword Oratoria 6 Loki has undine-cloth swimsuits made for the Amazons, whose Dive ability it strengthens; it resists water attacks and intense heat waves and "boosted not only water resistance but movement and speed underwater, as well".[@so06-undine] In DanMachi 12 an enhanced monster wearing undine cloth stolen from dead adventurers withstands Welf's flame magic sword Kouga: the two spirits' powers "had canceled each other out".[@fm12-kouga]

## Magic items {#magic-items}

### Talaria {#talaria}

Talaria are [[asfi|Asfi Al Andromeda]]'s winged sandals: "Winged sandals, Talaria. A high-level magic item reserved for Perseus herself."[@fm05-talaria] Asfi made them ("Talaria—a magic item created by Perseus herself"), and "Her knowledge as the creator of Talaria allowed her to move through the air at any speed or direction she wished".[@so03-talaria] "The effects of Talaria were influenced by the user’s Status": with the Level Boost, Asfi flies much faster than normal.[@so12-talaria]

- In DanMachi 8 she marks the position of Rakia's army from the air and offers to carry her companions "one by one using Talaria".[@fm08-talaria]
- In Sword Oratoria 3 an enemy stops her full-speed dive and smashes the wings underfoot; in Sword Oratoria 12 everything below her right knee, "including the Talaria", is charred black.[@so03-talaria, so12-talaria]
- In DanMachi 16 she searches from the sky, invisible, with Talaria and [[hades-head|Hades Head]]; in the Familia War of DanMachi 18 Talaria is banned.[@fm16-talaria, fm18-talaria]
- In DanMachi 19 [[hermes|Hermes]] borrows Asfi's spares, and as he and Bell fall, "Talaria’s four wings unfurl".[@fm19-talaria]

### Burst Oil {#burst-oil}

Burst Oil is "A special hand grenade that could only be created by an item maker". Asfi makes it from the obia flare, a flower that grows only around the volcanoes of the mainland's northern regions, and one vial of its red liquid can reduce middle-level monsters to cinders.[@so03-burst] "Most monsters in the middle levels would have been incinerated by just one of those Burst Oil grenades", but in DanMachi 5 they leave no mark on the Goliath.[@fm05-burst] In DanMachi 10 one "detonated after an intentional delay", for its smoke rather than to do damage.[@fm10-burst] In Sword Oratoria 3 a magic blade's fire sets off three vials at once, and in Sword Oratoria 12 a Burst Oil blast sets off a magic sword for a second explosion.[@so03-vials, so12-burst]

### Golem {#golem}

The Golem is [[fels|Fels]]'s "own magic item, dispatched into the sewer system after the Monsterphilia incident" (see [[monsterphilia|Monsterphilia]]): a metal warrior over three meders tall with a black symbol above its eyes, steered from a distance with a wand but "autonomous for the most part", "an extremely high-quality magic item requiring a level of skill that only Fels, formerly known as the Sage, possessed". Its body is "a purer form of adamantite mined from the deep levels", worth "one billion valis".[@fm10-golem] In DanMachi 10 Fels sends it to help the Xenos; Tione's Kukri knives snap on it and Bete's kick jars his foot, until Tiona cuts it in two and then through the symbol on its head.[@fm10-golem] Ouranos says that the Guild has collected "the remains of the golems".[@fm11-golems] In Sword Oratoria 10 Tiona destroys a golem again, and Finn concludes that it "wasn’t a monster but a magic item" and that the armed monsters' commander is a mage.[@so10-golem, so10-magic-item]
