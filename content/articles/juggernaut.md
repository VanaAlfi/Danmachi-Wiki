The **Juggernaut** is a monster the Dungeon produces as an immune response. When intruders damage a floor faster than the Dungeon can repair it, it gives birth to one to wipe them out. [[ouranos|Ouranos]] names it Juggernaut, "the destroyer".[@fm13-juggernaut, fm13-damage]

## Nature

A Juggernaut is extraordinarily fast and deadly, with claws that pierce armour and the ability to reflect magic, but comparatively little endurance.[@fm13-juggernaut] It is short-lived and leaves neither a [[magic-stone|magic stone]] nor drop items; the deeper the floor it is born on, the stronger it is. DanMachi 14 speculates that its whole body might be one enormous magic stone.[@fm14-nature, fm14-stone] Losing its head or having its chest pierced need not kill it; destroying its whole body does.[@fm14-nature]

## Astrea Familia

Five years before DanMachi 13, [[rudra-familia|Rudra Familia]]'s ambush with Inferno Stones set off a Juggernaut that killed ten members of [[astrea-familia|Astrea Familia]], leaving [[lyu-leon|Lyu]] as the only survivor.[@fm13-ambush] DanMachi 14 tells how that first Juggernaut ended: after Alize's last stand, Lyu's Luminous Wind engulfs it together with "the girl pinned to his hand"; stripped of its shell, it flees, and later Lyu finds a mountain of purplish-blue ash that she takes for its remains.[@fm14-first-end] The narration says it retreated after the massacre and turned to ash.[@fm14-first-ash]

## DanMachi 13–14

In DanMachi 13 another Juggernaut appears when a floor is badly damaged. It massacres the hunting party on Floor 27, nearly kills [[bell-cranell|Bell]], and kills [[rudra-familia#jura-harma|Jura]] despite the collar meant to control it.[@fm13-juggernaut, fm13-collar] It also knocks Lyu's wooden sword, [[equipment#alvs-lumina|Alvs Lumina]], out of her hands and bursts it into pieces; [[bors|Bors]] later recovers a fragment.[@fm13-lumina, fc03-lumina] The collar drives this one's destructive urge into a fixation on Bell, so it follows him and Lyu down to [[floor-37|Floor 37]] instead of staying on its own floor.[@fm14-pursuit]

On Floor 37 it eats other monsters to graft new limbs and armour, which begin to break down. Bell strips away the armour and Lyu destroys it completely with [[magic#luminous-wind|Luminous Wind]] at point-blank range. The rescue party finds them by the noise of the fight.[@fm14-end, fm14-rescue]
