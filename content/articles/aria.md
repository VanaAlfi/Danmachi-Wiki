**Aria** is identified by [[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz]] as her mother and by [[bell-cranell|Bell]] as the great spirit associated with [[albert|Albert]] in [[dungeon-oratoria|Dungeon Oratoria]].[@so03-mother, so05-spirit] [[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz]] states that Aria is her mother and insists she herself is not Aria.[@so03-mother] [[levis|Levis]] nevertheless addresses [[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz]] by that name, rejecting [[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz]]'s explanation and saying she was sent to find Aria.[@so03-mother] Levis retorts that Aria cannot have children and says she knows only the name, from a voice that keeps asking for Aria.[@so03-aria-levis] [[loki|Loki]] tells [[dionysus|Dionysus]], in truth Enyo, that he had guessed what Aria was: what the corrupted spirit, which has devoured many spirits, seeks could only be a spirit or one with a spirit's blood; and Aria was found by the creatures themselves, a mischance he had not foreseen: Aiz.[@so12-aria-loki] Loki adds that Enyo had to accept the creature's irrational desire to search for Aria, and that the [[monsterphilia|Monsterphilia]] incident happened because the creature pressed him to search for her.[@so12-aria-search] The corrupted spirit, Loki says, first noticed Aiz when she used [[magic#airiel|Airiel]] in the Dungeon after joining Loki Familia, and it was drawn by her to the [[dionysus-familia#twenty-seventh-floor-nightmare|Twenty-Seventh-Floor Nightmare]].[@so12-aria-nightmare]

## The name and the stories {#the-name-and-the-stories}

Talk of Aria and spirits makes [[hyrute-sisters|Tiona]] think of Dungeon Oratoria.[@so05-aria-legend] [[bell-cranell|Bell]] identifies the legendary Aria as the great spirit who accompanied [[albert|Albert]]'s life in [[dungeon-oratoria|Dungeon Oratoria]].[@so05-spirit]

[[hyrute-sisters|Tiona]] knows Aria from several stories whose details vary, including an illustrated tale where the spirit stands with a hero and companions against a monster.[@so03-tales]

[[lefiya|Lefiya]] dismisses her speculation about [[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz]]'s connection because she believes spirits, like gods, should be unable to bear children.[@so03-tales]

Told that the woman spoke the name Aria in answer to Aiz's [[magic#airiel|Airiel]], [[finn-deimne|Finn Deimne]] wonders whether she took Aiz for her mother.[@so03-aria-council]

[[hyrute-sisters|Tiona]] asks about Aria sharing blood, while [[hyrute-sisters|Tione]] asks about a human badly injured protecting Aria and about descendants; [[bell-cranell|Bell]] does not confirm those specific accounts.[@so05-questions]
