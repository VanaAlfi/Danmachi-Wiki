**Magia Kreis** is a ranged healing spell used by [[nina-tulle|Nina Tulle]], a half-elf student of the [[school-district|School District]] and the younger sister of Bell's [[guild|Guild]] adviser, [[eina-tulle|Eina]].[@fm19-nina, fm20-kreis] Besides healing, it passes Mind to others and makes buffs from magic and items last longer.[@fm20-kreis, fm20-buffs]

## Name

DanMachi 19 prints the spell as *Magia Kris*; DanMachi 20 prints *Magia Kreis*.[@fm19-kris, fm20-kreis] This wiki uses the later form.

## Effects

In DanMachi 20, while Nina interns with [[hestia-familia|Hestia Familia]], she tells the party that Magia Kreis also extends buffs from magic and items. Haruhime's Level Boost, which lasts twenty minutes at Level 2, is observed to last about five minutes longer under it.[@fm20-buffs, fm20-twenty] Nina uses it again during the fighting later in the volume.[@fm20-later]

Nina has a second, separate spell, Lagriell Krisheim, a healing barrier that clears poison, paralysis and other negative effects and prevents curses and mental attacks, though it cannot stop direct physical or magic attacks.[@fm19-lagriell]
