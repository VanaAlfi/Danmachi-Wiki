**Miliria** is a Level 2 [[races#elf|elf]] in [[balder#balder-class|Balder Class]]'s [[balder#seventh-squad|7th Squad]], fighting as an archer and serving as its deputy leader.[@so13-identity-1, so13-identity-2, so13-identity-3, fm19-mentor-1, fm19-mentor-2] She uses a longbow and a breastplate covering the right side of her chest, with Agility and Dexterity standing out among her abilities.[@so13-identity-1, so13-identity-2, so13-identity-3]

## Abilities and relationships {#abilities-and-relationships}

Miliria and [[cole|Cole]] support the squad's leading fighters, but their concentration on that role initially leaves them without the decisive power to recover when the formation breaks down.[@so13-support-1, so13-support-2] She can use healing magic, and her [[magic#school-district-spells-silva-vine-miliria|Silva Vine]] produces magical vines from placed circles to bind pursuing monsters.[@so13-magic-1, so13-magic-2] She allows [[lefiya|Lefiya Viridis]] to use her nickname, Milly.[@so13-manners]
