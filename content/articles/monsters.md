**Monsters** are the creatures of [[dungeon|the Dungeon]] that adventurers fight. Each has a [[magic-stone|magic stone]] as its core, and destroying the stone turns the whole monster to ash.[@so03-core] This page collects what the novels say about monsters in general, indexes the species, and gives a section to each species that has no page of its own.

## Monsters in general {#general}

- **Levels:** the [[guild|Guild]] rates monsters by Level. The al-miraj, weak alone but "surprisingly dangerous in groups", are "classified as Level 2 monsters"; the Guild rates lizardman elites between Level 3 and Level 4.[@fm05-rating, lizardman.fm14-elite] A rampaging vouivre is called "a rare large-category monster".[@vouivre.fm10-large]
- **Upper and middle levels:** the monsters of the [[upper-levels|upper levels]] only charge. In the middle levels some have learned to attack from a distance, and DanMachi 5 says the middle-level monsters' intelligence is far higher.[@fm05-middle, fm05-ja-middle]
- **Nature weapons:** the Dungeon supplies weapons that monsters pick up, such as tree-stump clubs, stone tomahawks and the metal flowers of the twentieth floor, which serve as shield and blades.[@fm09-nature] See [[al-miraj|Al-Miraj]] and [[#lizardman|Lizardman]].
- **Monster against monster:** monsters of different kinds can fight each other in the Dungeon.[@fm05-other, fm05-ja-other] Some species prefer other monsters' magic stones: see [[#violas|Violas]] and [[corrupted-spirit|Corrupted spirit and demi-spirits]].[@violas.so03-stones]

## Upper levels {#upper-levels}

{{nocite}} Floors 1 to 12. The sections run roughly from the shallowest floors to the deepest.

### Goblin {#goblin}

Goblins are "The weakest monster in the entire Dungeon", as Hestia puts it; DanMachi 15 calls the goblin a creature "whose name was synonymous with weakness".[@goblin.fm04-weakest, goblin.fm15-haruhime]

- **Where:** the first four floors, with their pale blue walls, usually hold only goblins and kobolds. On floors 8 and 9, stronger goblins and kobolds appear rather than new species.[@goblin.fm02-floors, goblin.fm02-eighth]
- **Drops:** in DanMachi 1 Bell picks up a goblin fang as a drop item.[@goblin.fm01-fang]
- **On the surface:** a goblin nearly killed Bell as a child. He supposes it descended from goblins that escaped the Dungeon before the Guild existed, since monsters breed out in the world. His grandfather saved him armed with a garden hoe.[@goblin.fm01-child, goblin.fm03-gramps]
- **Firsts:** right after joining [[hestia-familia|Hestia Familia]], Bell slew a goblin and ran back to tell Hestia. A goblin is also the first monster he hits with [[magic#firebolt|Firebolt]].[@goblin.fm04-joined, goblin.fm04-first, goblin.fm02-firebolt] In DanMachi 15 [[haruhime|Haruhime]]'s clumsy attack on a lone goblin goes wrong, and a gang of goblins beat her until Lilly flying-kicks them away.[@goblin.fm15-haruhime]

### Kobold {#kobold}

Kobolds are dog-headed monsters that usually wander alone or in pairs and attack with sharp claws and fangs. Bell does not think them "too bright".[@kobold.fm01-kobolds]

- **Strength:** in DanMachi 1 Bell has "only recently got strong enough to slay kobolds". Their magic stones are mere shards.[@kobold.fm01-strong, kobold.fm01-shard]
- **Drops:** when Bell takes a kobold's shard, one sharp claw stays behind instead of turning to ash: a drop item.[@kobold.fm01-shard]
- **Where:** with goblins, on the first four floors, and in stronger forms on floors 8 and 9.[@goblin.fm02-floors, goblin.fm02-eighth]
- **A pack:** in DanMachi 1 six kobolds chase Bell together, which kobolds "aren’t supposed to" do; he turns and kills them one after another, since they "won’t figure anything out".[@kobold.fm01-pack]
- **First opponents:** in Sword Oratoria 9 the young Aiz's brute-force strokes are "more than enough to slay a goblin or a kobold", and Riveria watches her finish one off with a chill; in DanMachi 15 Haruhime parries a kobold's attack and sends it tumbling with her staff; in Sword Oratoria 13 Lefiya, then a School District student, fells "low-level monsters like goblins and kobolds in a single blow", though they are "certainly stronger than the monsters aboveground".[@kobold.so09-aiz, kobold.fm15-haruhime, kobold.so13-students]
- **Outside the Dungeon:** in Sword Oratoria 14 Finn cuts down kobolds, with sword stags, battle boars and silverbacks, on a mountain whose monsters "were behaving strangely".[@kobold.so14-mountain]
- **Mistaken sightings:** when a humanoid monster is reported in the city in Sword Oratoria 10, the narration allows that witnesses may have mistaken "a beast-headed monster with a humanlike body—like a kobold" for one, since humanoid monsters appear "only as high as the nineteenth floor".[@kobold.so10-humanoid]

### Dungeon lizard {#dungeon-lizard}

A four-legged lizard with scaly brown skin, as long as Bell is tall including its tail. It appears on floors 2 to 4 and is in the "same class as goblins and kobolds".[@dungeon-lizard.fm01-lizard] In DanMachi 1 one senses Bell's blade and makes "straight for the wall", but he is faster and plunges his dagger "deep into the lizard’s back"; a goblin was with it, and he dispatches both "even though it was two on one".[@dungeon-lizard.fm01-fight] In DanMachi 10 he meets "a dungeon lizard climbing on the wall" among a group of kobolds.[@dungeon-lizard.fm10-wall]

### War shadow {#war-shadow}

DanMachi 1 prints it as the **Wall Shadow**, "the lower-sixth-floor monster", and DanMachi 16 once as *woe shadows*, but the Japanese originals of both volumes use the same word as everywhere else, {{ja|ウォーシャドウ|Wō Shadō}}, *war shadow*; *Wall* and *woe* are Yen Press's variants.[@war-shadow.fm01-wall, war-shadow.fm01-ja, war-shadow.fm16-woe, war-shadow.fm16-ja] Other volumes print *war shadow*.[@war-shadow.fm10-orde]

- **Arms:** its long arms end in knife-like fingers. DanMachi 1 gives it three; DanMachi 10 gives a war shadow five.[@war-shadow.fm01-fingers, war-shadow.fm10-orde]
- **Danger:** in DanMachi 1 it is the most dangerous monster of the sixth floor "in terms of pure power", and adventurers of the upper floors must be careful when one appears. Like the killer ant, it is called a "newbie killer".[@war-shadow.fm01-fingers, killer-ant.fm02-ant]
- **Weakness:** in Eina's quiz it has high attack and high speed, but defence "roughly comparable to that of a goblin or a kobold".[@war-shadow.ss01-quiz]
- **Drops:** a finger blade.[@war-shadow.fm01-drop]
- **Xenos:** Orde, a war shadow, scouts for [[xenos#ranieh|Ranieh]]'s party in full plate armour, so that he passes for an adventurer. He dies when the hunters ambush them in DanMachi 10.[@war-shadow.fm10-orde]

### Frog shooter {#frog-shooter}

A frog monster with one giant eye that shoots out a long tongue to attack adventurers; Yen Press's DanMachi 1 prints "bulb-eyed" and "spear-like".[@frog-shooter.fm01-ja, frog-shooter.fm01-frog] Frog shooters are "barely stronger" than goblins and kobolds, but in Sword Oratoria 4 a swarm surrounds [[lefiya|Lefiya]] while [[filvis|Filvis]] trains her.[@frog-shooter.so04-lefiya] In DanMachi 7 Bell likens a shock to "getting licked by a frog shooter".[@frog-shooter.fm07-licked] In that Sword Oratoria 4 scene Filvis dashes past Lefiya so that the croaking swarm can leap only at her; they circle her until her flaming arrows rain down, and "The frog shooters’ skin and bulbous single eyes glowed a brilliant red before the raging magic storm swallowed them".[@frog-shooter.so04-swarm] In Sword Oratoria 2 [[riveria|Riveria]] drives one off "in the blink of an eye".[@frog-shooter.so02-riveria]

### Killer ant {#killer-ant}

A **killer ant** looks like a giant red ant with four legs, two thin arms and two big eyes; it stands as tall as Bell when it rears up. It first appears on the lower seventh floor and is called a "newbie killer".[@killer-ant.fm02-ant]

- **Armour and claws:** its skin "might as well be armor", and four curved claws hang from its arms. The recommended way to kill one is to strike the soft flesh at the joints between its shells.[@killer-ant.fm02-ant, killer-ant.fm02-joints]
- **Size:** killer ants are human-sized, unlike the small purple moths and needle rabbits found with them.[@killer-ant.fm02-size]
- **Calling for help:** close to death, a killer ant releases a pheromone that calls other killer ants. [[soma-familia#kanu|Kanu]] uses a dying one to bring a swarm down on Lilly in DanMachi 2.[@killer-ant.fm02-pheromone]

### Purple moth {#purple-moth}

**Purple moths** spread poisonous wing-scales in battle. The effect is slow, but breathing in too much poisons the body.[@purple-moth.fm02-scales] There are so many in the upper levels that many adventurers have breathed their spores, which makes the [[development-ability|Development Ability]] Immunity relatively easy to acquire.[@purple-moth.fm04-immunity] Their drop item is the Purple Moth Wing ({{ja|パープル・モスの翅|Pāpuru Mosu no hane}}; the Yen Press text prints "Purple Moth Scales"), and the poisonous scale powder collected from it goes into Lilly's fast-acting poison powder and Bell's poison grenades.[@purple-moth.fm03-drop, purple-moth.fm03-ja-drop, purple-moth.fm19-grenades] In DanMachi 2 Bell kills three in a day's work, catching one's wing with the [[hestia-knife|Hestia Knife]]; Eina's lesson in Minor Myths and Legends 1 is that "When fighting purple moths, the most important thing is to pay close attention to your positioning".[@purple-moth.fm02-bell, purple-moth.ss01-eina] In the pantry where Bell waits for the blue papillon in DanMachi 4, purple moths drink the sap beside killer ants and needle rabbits and rest their wings on the crystals.[@purple-moth.fm04-pantry] The [[#dark-fungus|dark fungus]] of the middle levels makes "the purple moths’ poisonous pollen in the upper levels look like child’s play".[@purple-moth.fm09-fungus]

### Needle rabbit {#needle-rabbit}

A small horned rabbit; Bell blocks a needle rabbit's tusk with his knee plate on the seventh floor.[@needle-rabbit.fm02-rabbit] Needle rabbits are "small" monsters, met alongside killer ants, and one strays as far as the tenth floor.[@killer-ant.fm02-size, needle-rabbit.fm02-tenth] Bell calls the [[al-miraj|al-miraj]] "needle rabbits that learned how to walk on two legs".[@needle-rabbit.fm05-almiraj] In DanMachi 2 he kills five in a day with Lilly's help, and Lilly finishes off the last "a bit more roughly than necessary"; when a later fight goes wrong he counters one coming at him from behind with his second blade, and reflects that "As long as I took them one at a time, it wouldn’t matter if a needle rabbit suddenly jumped in".[@needle-rabbit.fm02-day, needle-rabbit.fm02-lesson] In the pantry of DanMachi 4 one dips "its tongue into the pool" of sap, and in Sword Oratoria 3 one that had probably strayed down from the floors above meets Aiz's eyes and bounds "away in alarm".[@purple-moth.fm04-pantry, needle-rabbit.so03-aiz]

### Orc {#orc}

The **orc** ({{ja|豚頭人体}}, written *pig-headed human body*)[@orc.so03-ja] is a large-category monster: about three meders tall, with brown skin, a boar's head and old hide round its waist, squat and fat rather than muscled like a Minotaur.[@orc.fm02-orc]

- **Where:** large-category monsters first appear on the tenth floor; there are none down to the ninth.[@orc.fm02-tenth]
- **Arms:** an orc pulls a dead tree out of the floor and swings it as a club.[@orc.fm02-club] DanMachi 2 calls such clubs "landform weapons" and DanMachi 4 a "natural weapon", the [[#general|nature weapons]] the Dungeon supplies.[@orc.fm02-trap, orc.fm04-club]
- **Strength:** a direct hit keeps a Level 1, or even a Level 2, down for a long time. But its weight makes it slow, and it easily loses its balance.[@orc.fm04-strong, orc.fm02-slow]
- **Birth:** in DanMachi 4 Bell watches an orc break out of the wall. From around the tenth floor many monsters can emerge in the same room at once, a "monster party".[@orc.fm04-birth]
- **Drop items:** a request Bell reads in DanMachi 4 asks for "Orc Hide x30".[@orc.fm04-hide]
- **Bell's first orcs:** in DanMachi 2 Lilly tells him not to run, and he knows that if he cannot slay an orc he will "never be able to take down other large-category monsters later on…like the Minotaur". He brings one down by cutting through its leg below the knee and finishes it with a thrust into its skull; the next takes two Firebolts ("my magic isn't strong enough to slay an orc in one blow right now") and "silently turns to ash", its magic stone apparently burned up by the blasts.[@orc.fm02-first] Soon after, four orcs "lured here by the smell of the bloody meat" of scattered trap items surround him, and Lilly takes the [[hestia-knife|Divine Knife]] and leaves him to them: "Sorry, Mr. Bell. This is where it ends."[@orc.fm02-trap]
- **Welf and Bell:** in DanMachi 4 [[welf-crozzo|Welf]] claims the first orc of their trip ("All right, I got dibs on the orc.") and cuts another with a stroke you would think had cut it in two; later Bell's broadsword slices an orc's club in half and Welf takes its head off.[@orc.fm04-welf, orc.fm04-club]
- **Seen by Aiz:** in Sword Oratoria 3 [[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz]] finds Bell alone among orcs and imps on the tenth floor. An orc's tree club hammers the armour on his left arm; she cuts the orcs down before they notice her, and afterwards picks up the armour, torn loose (the [[equipment#briefly-named|green protector]]).[@orc.so03-aiz]
- **Elsewhere:** as a boy, [[bete-loga|Bete]] hunts with his tribe's warriors until he can take on "goblins, orcs, and other similar monsters" alone; in Sword Oratoria 14 [[finn-deimne|Finn]] cuts down an orc among the monsters raiding a village; and in Tiona's memory of the arena of [[telskyura|Telskyura]], where she and Tione grew up, Tione's opponents that day were "Orcs and hellhounds".[@orc.so08-bete, orc.so14-village, orc.ss02-tione]
- **Students:** in DanMachi 19 Bell's School District squad flees from "four orcs approaching like a wall" on the twelfth floor, and Legi's Dark Mine magic circles later scatter the orcs and imps that follow the infant dragon; in Sword Oratoria 13 [[lefiya|Lefiya]], in her student days, beats "that big horde of orcs in a single blast".[@orc.fm19-squad, orc.so13-lefiya]

### Imp {#imp}

**Imps** are little demons. They look like goblins, but they are clever: they never attack one at a time, and they use the fog of the tenth floor to surround a target from every side. On that floor a pack of imps is said to be more troublesome than a single orc.[@imp.fm03-imps, imp.fm03-ja-imps] Lilly's purple powder sets them coughing violently.[@imp.fm03-powder, imp.fm03-ja-powder] On the tenth floor they are "far more common than orcs", but "there is a limit to their intelligence": Lilly hides in the same fog and shoots one in the back of the head, and when an orc arrives the imps that are left "look like minions waiting for the boss's command".[@imp.fm03-fog]

In DanMachi 4 a swarm of imps emerges from the walls on the lower eleventh floor, "growing larger by the second". With his dagger and the [[hestia-knife|Divine Knife]] Bell needs only one hit for each, sends one into the dirt with one of Aiz's spin kicks, and wipes out the swarm of "more than ten individuals".[@imp.fm04-swarm] In Sword Oratoria 3 [[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz]] cuts down every imp in her way as she crosses the tenth floor, and three more that charge at Bell's back.[@imp.so03-aiz] In DanMachi 19 imps surround the infant dragon that Bell's school squad must hunt, as its underlings: on the twelfth floor the squad is "Surrounded by a huge number of imps", the dragon's tail knocks a couple of them over, and the orcs that join the fight "swat aside the imps".[@imp.fm19-dragon, imp.fm19-swarm]

### Bad bat {#bad-bat}

A dark-coloured, bat-type monster with sharp fangs and eerie sound waves that break an adventurer's concentration.[@bad-bat.fm03-bat, bad-bat.fm03-ja-bat] Bad bats ({{ja|バットパット}}, read *bat pat*)[@bad-bat.fm04-ja] have "hardly any raw combat ability", but their shrieks disrupt adventurers' movements; they lurk in the shadows of the Cave Labyrinth.[@bad-bat.so13-shriek] In DanMachi 5 a swarm is born from a tunnel ceiling.[@bad-bat.fm05-swarm] In Sword Oratoria 13 a mass spawning of bad bats around the fifteenth floor brings a ceiling down near the [[balder-class#seventh-squad|7th Squad]].[@bad-bat.so13-collapse] The students had seen no bad bats, which should have warned them: barring an extreme irregularity, the number of monsters in each level zone is fixed, and the bats had probably all been born at once; the narration notes that Finn, Riveria or Gareth "would have noticed the missing bad bats and immediately given the order to fall back".[@bad-bat.so13-missing] DanMachi 19 tells the same event from Bell's side: their screech "impedes movement", and when so many are born from the ceiling rather than the walls, the fifteenth floor loses its balance and caves in.[@bad-bat.fm19-collapse] In DanMachi 4 Lilly's arrow brings down a bad bat flying overhead.[@bad-bat.fm04-arrow]

### Silverback {#silverback}

**Silverbacks** are white-furred monsters that recall a wild ape, their dull-silver head-hair flowing down the back like a tail, and they first appear on the lower eleventh floor.[@silverback.fm04-look, silverback.fm01-floor] They are smaller than orcs but powerful and agile, and with the Hard Armoreds they make the lower eleventh floor very dangerous for Level 1s.[@silverback.fm04-look] The al-miraj are more agile still.[@silverback.fm05-agile] Bell fights one at the [[monsterphilia|Monsterphilia]] of DanMachi 1.[@silverback.fm01-fight, silverback.fm04-look] There [[freya|Freya]] strokes its cheek and holds its head "with both hands, evil smile and all", and the freed monster hunts [[hestia|Hestia]] through the streets.[@silverback.fm01-freya, silverback.fm01-fight] Bell, who has reached only the sixth floor, finds even a killing blow "blocked by the silverback’s fur", until he strikes with the [[hestia-knife|Hestia Knife]] and leaves "the black blade standing straight up out of its chest".[@silverback.fm01-fight] In DanMachi 10 a silverback is among the Xenos who give in to their fury.[@brief.fm10-xenos]

### Hard Armored {#hard-armored}

**Hard Armoreds** are armadillo-like monsters whose defence was famed as an iron wall in the upper levels. They curl into a ball and roll at adventurers at speed, and rolled up they bounce off all physical attacks; their underbelly is unprotected.[@hard-armored.fm05-mikoto, hard-armored.fm05-ja-armored, hard-armored.fm04-armored] They first appear on the eleventh floor, and are met in the middle levels too.[@hard-armored.fm04-armored, hard-armored.fm04-middle] DanMachi 4 says Level 1s need Basic Abilities of B to S to survive the lower eleventh and twelfth floors because of them.[@hard-armored.fm04-armored] [[mikoto|Yamato Mikoto]]'s small shield is made from their plating.[@hard-armored.fm05-mikoto] DanMachi 4 calls a Hard Armored "basically a walking iron fortress that can repel a dwarf’s attack with ease": a Level 1 adventurer cannot beat one alone. Bell splits one's shell "cleanly in two", and an explosion blasts away part of another's.[@hard-armored.fm04-fortress] In DanMachi 5 Mikoto stops one rolling Hard Armored with her shield, its plates hammering it, then forces it aside into the second, and all three are thrown back; in DanMachi 12 she remembers the hard armored that charged her and the injured Chigusa from behind, the fight that led them to lead a pass parade into Bell's party.[@hard-armored.fm05-shield, hard-armored.fm12-chigusa]

### Infant dragon {#infant-dragon}

The **infant dragon** is a wingless, four-legged dragon with thick amber scales and red eyes: only about 150 celch tall, but over 4 meders long.[@infant-dragon.fm04-dragon]

- **Rarity:** it appears only on the lower eleventh and twelfth floors. DanMachi 4 says there are not even five on a whole floor; DanMachi 19 says there are usually not even five on the twelfth floor.[@infant-dragon.fm04-rare, infant-dragon.fm19-rare]
- **The upper levels' boss:** with no [[monster-rex|Monster Rex]] on these floors, infant dragons are the effective floor bosses, and they have wiped out parties of Level 1s.[@infant-dragon.fm04-rare, infant-dragon.so09-aiz]
- **DanMachi 4:** on the eleventh floor, Bell destroys one with a charged Firebolt, despite dragons' natural resistance to flame. Asfi later reports it to Hermes.[@infant-dragon.fm04-firebolt, infant-dragon.fm05-asfi]
- **Sword Oratoria 9:** in her early years, Aiz goes into the Dungeon alone and fights infant dragons, to [[riveria|Riveria]]'s fury.[@infant-dragon.so09-aiz]
- **DanMachi 10:** the god [[ganesha|Ganesha]] of [[ganesha-familia|Ganesha Familia]] approaches a tamed infant dragon, fitted with a magic item, inside a fence, and it snaps at his mask.[@infant-dragon.fm10-tamed]
- **DanMachi 19:** the [[school-district|School District]] sets an infant dragon's drop item as a grade requirement, and the [[balder-class#third-squad|3rd Squad]] finally wins an infant dragon fang.[@infant-dragon.fm19-grade, infant-dragon.fm19-fang]
- **Nidhogg:** the demi-spirit [[corrupted-spirit#nidhogg|Nidhogg]] grew from a crystal-orb fetus attached to an infant dragon that Levis's group brought up from the deep floors, far below the eleventh and twelfth floors where the species is usually found.[@infant-dragon.so12-nidhogg]

## Middle levels {#middle-levels}

{{nocite}} From the thirteenth floor down. For the Floor 37 monsters, see also [[floor-37|Floor 37]] and [[colosseum|Colosseum]].

### Hellhound {#hellhound}

**Hellhounds**, also called Baskervilles, are doglike monsters of the middle levels: black, heavily muscled and four-legged, with deep red eyes.[@hellhound.fm05-baskerville, hellhound.fm05-look]

- **Fire:** they are physically formidable for middle-level monsters, but the real danger is the fire they spit; in the Japanese it melts ordinary armour with ease. A pack's inferno can reduce a party to ash. When a party is wiped out on the thirteenth or fourteenth floor, hellhounds are most likely to blame (in the Japanese, overwhelmingly so).[@hellhound.fm05-fire, hellhound.fm05-ja-fire] No monster of the upper levels breathes fire like them.[@hellhound.fm05-wisp]
- **Tells:** before spitting fire, a hellhound raises its hindquarters, lowers its head, and shows sparks between its teeth.[@hellhound.fm05-spit]
- **Defences:** hellhounds are the reason [[eina-tulle|Eina]] insists on [[equipment#salamander-wool|salamander wool]] for Bell's party; the Japanese says it is solely because of them.[@hellhound.fm05-baskerville, hellhound.fm05-ja-baskerville] Being fire-breathers, they resist fire magic unusually well.[@hellhound.fm05-resist] [[welf-crozzo|Welf]]'s [[magic#will-o-the-wisp|Will-o'-the-Wisp]] makes the hellhounds detonate as their own fire builds up; the Japanese says their flame is magic power burned inside the body, which the spell triggers.[@hellhound.fm05-wisp, hellhound.fm05-ja-wisp] [[asfi|Asfi]] glues their mouths shut with sticky vials before they can fire.[@hellhound.fm05-asfi]
- **Drops:** a quest posted in DanMachi 4 asks for "Hellhound Fangs x10".[@hellhound.fm04-fangs]
- **Company:** they often hunt alongside [[al-miraj|al-miraj]]. In Sword Oratoria 13 Lefiya singles out their fire breath as a threat to her squad.[@hellhound.fm07-pack, hellhound.so13-lefiya] Among the Xenos, the hellhound Helga goes about with the al-miraj [[al-miraj#aruru|Aruru]]; in Sword Oratoria 12 the two track down Asterios on Fels's orders.[@hellhound.so12-helga]

### Liger fang {#liger-fang}

The **liger fang** ({{ja|大虎}}, written *great tiger*)[@liger-fang.fm08-ja] is a tiger monster with a huge body and stiff fur that no half-measure blade will pierce; [[mikoto|Mikoto]], who has fought one before, warns that "It’s faster than a Minotaur!"[@liger-fang.fm07-irregular]

- **Where:** in DanMachi 7 one strays upward, an [[irregular|Irregular]], "a monster that shouldn’t show up until level fifteen" (the printed "level" is the floor).[@liger-fang.fm07-irregular] In DanMachi 8 the roars of liger fangs fill the ash-coloured caves of the [[cave-labyrinth|Cave Labyrinth]].[@liger-fang.fm08-cave]
- **Materials:** Aiz collects "a drop item, liger fang fur" in Sword Oratoria 2, and in DanMachi 8 [[welf-crozzo|Welf]] forges with "a metal synthesized from a liger fang’s tooth and noh steel mined from floor twenty-seven".[@liger-fang.so02-fur, liger-fang.fm08-metal]
- **The School District squad:** Sword Oratoria 13 calls liger fangs "the squad’s archenemy" for the [[school-district|School District]] students Lefiya guides, though on a later dive "The liger fangs that were so much trouble the first time were like nothing." A liger fang that has "feasted on the corpses of the monsters they had left lying there" chases down the student Cole "with its now enhanced body", because he forgot the post-battle cleanup.[@liger-fang.so13-squad, liger-fang.so13-enhanced] See [[magic-stone|enhanced species]].

### Vouivre {#vouivre}

Vouivres are a species of dragon, which DanMachi 9 calls "the greatest monsters in the Dungeon". Bell remembers the vouivre as one of the rarest of rare monsters, "On par with the unicorn". It is said to appear between the nineteenth and twenty-fourth floors, in the [[colossal-tree-labyrinth|Colossal Tree Labyrinth]].[@vouivre.fm09-rare, vouivre.fm09-berserk]

- **Form:** a vouivre usually has a woman's upper body and a snakelike lower body, like a lamia; "women attached to a dragon tail". It has a red jewel in its forehead.[@vouivre.fm09-form, vouivre.fm09-rare]
- **Senses:** like other dragons, vouivres have particularly acute senses, such as hearing.[@vouivre.fm10-senses]
- **Drop items:** a vouivre's scales and claws fetch "tremendous sums". Its forehead gem, the **Vouivre's Tear**, is worth far more; adventurers call it the "Prosperity Stone".[@vouivre.fm09-rare]
- **The jewel and the rage:** taking the jewel from its forehead sends a vouivre berserk, and killing it shatters the jewel, so many adventurers have died trying to get one.[@vouivre.fm09-berserk] Sword Oratoria 10 adds that a vouivre that loses the stone rages until it gets it back.[@vouivre.so10-rage]

The Xenos [[wiene|Wiene]] is a vouivre. Because she is a girl in form, Bell wonders whether she is a subspecies.[@vouivre.fm09-form] In DanMachi 10 the hunter [[ikelos-familia|Dix]] tears the jewel from her forehead. She turns into a rampaging dragon with a dragon's tail, and yet shows no interest in Dix, who holds her stone.[@vouivre.fm10-dix, vouivre.fm10-rage, vouivre.so10-tail] Her story is on [[wiene|her own page]].

### Lizardman {#lizardman}

**Lizardmen** ({{ja|蜥蜴人}}, written *lizard people*)[@lizardman.so03-ja] are red-scaled monsters, about 170 celch tall, that stand upright and fight with weapons in both hands. Bell's party meets them on the twentieth floor, in the Colossal Tree Labyrinth, and for the first time Bell feels he is fighting other adventurers.[@lizardman.fm09-twentieth]

- **Arms:** the twentieth floor's lizardmen carry nature weapons: metal flowers that grow from the walls. With the stem removed, the flower is a round shield fifty celch across, and its petals, called "cutters", serve as daggers.[@lizardman.fm09-flowers] On the twenty-fourth floor, in Sword Oratoria 3, three lizardmen challenge Aiz with a flower for a shield and its petals as shortswords.[@lizardman.so03-aiz]
- **Lizardman elite:** "a higher-rank version of the lizardmen" of the Colossal Tree Labyrinth, with blue scales "as hard as armor" and two axes of milky white stone. The Guild rates them between Level 3 and Level 4, and they specialise in close combat.[@lizardman.fm14-elite] They are among the warrior-type monsters of [[floor-37|Floor 37]], where Bell and Lyu fight them, and they fill the [[colosseum|Colosseum]].[@lizardman.so02-elite, lizardman.fm14-elite, lizardman.fm14-colosseum] On Floor 37 in Familia Chronicle 2, [[ottar|Ottar]] cuts them down with a single slash each.[@lizardman.fc02-ottar]
- **Xenos:** [[lido|Lido]], leader of the [[xenos|Xenos]], is a scarlet lizardman with a longsword and a scimitar. Fighting him in Sword Oratoria 10, Tione murmurs that he "doesn't seem like your regular lizardman".[@lizardman.so10-lido]

Sword Oratoria 2 calls the elite a higher species of the lizardmen, which appear from the nineteenth floor.[@lizardman.so02-elite]

### Bugbear {#bugbear}

**Bugbears** ({{ja|熊獣}}, written *bear beast*)[@bugbear.fm05-ja] are bearlike monsters. Bell remembers that their Strength and Endurance are a little below a Minotaur's, but that what makes them dangerous is a "deceptively high Agility" for their size; they first appear on the nineteenth floor and "corner their prey before tearing it apart".[@bugbear.fm05-desc]

- **Food and smell:** they eat the honey cloud fruit of [[floor-18|Floor 18]]. In DanMachi 5 three hungry bugbears chase off the outlaws guarding Hestia once they are smeared with the fruit.[@bugbear.fm05-desc, bugbear.fm05-honey, bugbear.fm09-honey] Bugbears are "known for their keen sense of smell".[@bugbear.fm09-smell]
- **Where:** with mad beetles, gun libellulas and firebirds they are among the dangers of the [[colossal-tree-labyrinth|Colossal Tree Labyrinth]], and they wander into Floor 18 from the floors next to it.[@bugbear.fm09-dangers, bugbear.ar03-safe]
- **Fights:** in DanMachi 5 Bell saves [[mord-latro|Mord]] from three bugbears with Mord's own sword.[@bugbear.fm05-mord] In Sword Oratoria 1 Aiz cuts off a bugbear's arm before its claws can reach her, and makes room in her backpack for "a bugbear claw", a drop item.[@bugbear.so01-aiz]

### Mad beetle {#mad-beetle}

**Mad beetles** ({{ja|大甲虫}}, written *great beetle*)[@mad-beetle.fm05-ja] are "medium-size, two-legged insect monsters" with "hooked pincers".[@mad-beetle.fm05-beetles, mad-beetle.fm09-pincers] They swarm with bugbears and gun libellulas in the [[colossal-tree-labyrinth|Colossal Tree Labyrinth]]: a swarm of them is Bell and Welf's first encounter on the nineteenth floor, where the [[goliath-robe|Goliath Robe]] Welf wears repels their pincers.[@mad-beetle.fm09-pincers] In DanMachi 19 [[lyu-leon|Lyu]] passes through a flanking swarm "like the wind", and a touch of her new [[equipment#alvs-iustitia|Alvs Iustitia]] sets off a chain of ash explosions.[@mad-beetle.fm19-lyu] In DanMachi 5 Bell blasts a hole in a mad beetles' formation and breaks through it, and later [[mord-latro|Mord]] cuts down a charging one in the middle of a tirade.[@mad-beetle.fm05-hole, mad-beetle.fm05-mord] DanMachi 9 counts "the fearsome potential of bugbears and mad beetles" among the dangers of the Colossal Tree Labyrinth; Welf cleaves one in two with his greatsword, Mikoto puts an arrow through another's head, and mad beetles roam the floors below as well.[@mad-beetle.fm09-labyrinth, mad-beetle.fm09-xenos] Against the armed Xenos in DanMachi 10, [[ganesha-familia|Ganesha Familia]] find that "The bugbears and mad beetles fell easily".[@mad-beetle.fm10-ganesha]

### Gun libellula {#gun-libellula}

**Gun libellulas** ({{ja|狙撃蜻蛉}}, written *sniper dragonfly*)[@gun-libellula.fm05-ja] are "dragonfly monsters" that attack from a distance with their long, slender abdomens.[@gun-libellula.fm09-ranged] DanMachi 5 and Sword Oratoria 1 print the name *gun liberla*.[@gun-libellula.fm05-liberla, gun-libellula.so01-aiz]

- **Tactics:** because of their long-range attacks Bell shoots them down first with [[magic#firebolt|Firebolt]]; those in the spell's path are incinerated.[@gun-libellula.fm09-ranged, gun-libellula.fm09-firebolt] In DanMachi 19 he cuts down a swarm "spinning through the air like tops" at close range.[@gun-libellula.fm19-tops] In DanMachi 9 their volleys target Wiene, and a mob firing from behind pins down the front line until Mikoto and Lilly wipe them out with arrows.[@gun-libellula.fm09-mob] In Sword Oratoria 1 [[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz]], with a rapier, aims for their magic stones "with strikes so precise she could’ve threaded a needle".[@gun-libellula.so01-aiz]
- **Company:** they fly above bugbears on the ground. In Familia Chronicle 3 a merchant's hired Familia is nearly wiped out by "massive bugbears and flying gun libellulas".[@gun-libellula.fc03-merchant]

### Firebird {#firebird}

**Firebirds** are "a rare kind of monster normally found on the nineteenth floor and below": birds that attack mainly with fire.[@firebird.fm09-outbreak] The one that attacks [[wiene|Wiene]] in DanMachi 9 is a crimson bird "over two meders long from end to end", with bloodshot eyes and a massive beak, and its fire is "more powerful than a hellhound could ever produce".[@firebird.fm09-wiene]

- **Danger:** left unchecked, firebirds can turn an area into "a sea of flames", and they sometimes fly up to Floor 18 and endanger [[rivira|Rivira]].[@firebird.fm09-outbreak]
- **The outbreak of DanMachi 9:** firebirds overflow on the nineteenth floor, an [[irregular|Irregular]], and Rivira's adventurers draw Hestia Familia into the hunt; during it Bell finds Wiene.[@firebird.fm09-outbreak, firebird.fm09-wiene] The Familia accepts partly because "a flock of firebirds in our way made it impossible to comfortably progress any farther". Bell splits the firebird attacking Wiene in two with his knife before it can fire: "Its magic stone is cleaved apart" and its fire breaks up "in midair like fireworks". When her hunters arrive, he sends them away by pretending that she is a companion who has been "hit by a firebird and got burned bad".[@firebird.fm09-kill]
- **Range:** DanMachi 9 counts their attacks, with the gun libellulas', among "the ranged attacks" of the Colossal Tree Labyrinth.[@mad-beetle.fm09-labyrinth]

### Dark fungus {#dark-fungus}

**Dark fungi** ({{ja|毒茸}}, written *poison mushroom*)[@dark-fungus.so03-ja] are dark-purple, mushroom-like monsters that hide among the giant fungi of the [[colossal-tree-labyrinth|Colossal Tree Labyrinth]], wait for prey to come to them, and release clouds of poisonous gas. In DanMachi 9 they slip past [[mikoto|Mikoto]]'s detection Skill, because she has never met them before.[@dark-fungus.fm09-ambush] Their highly toxic spores harm friend and foe alike, but it cannot overpower Aiz's Immunity ([[development-ability#abnormal-resistance|Abnormal Resistance]]); in Sword Oratoria 3 [[filvis|Filvis]]'s lightning roasts a cluster and burns their spores out of the air.[@dark-fungus.so03-aiz, dark-fungus.so03-filvis] In DanMachi 9 what looks like a wall is a colony ("It’s not a wall—they’re dark fungi!!"), and Bell's Firebolt burns through their cloud of spores into the colony behind it; in Sword Oratoria 3 a horde of them, with deadly hornets, lizardmen and swordstags, overruns the adventurers on Floor 24, and one adventurer takes it for a pass parade; Aiz dashes through their poisonous cloud to skewer them.[@dark-fungus.fm09-wall, swordstag.so03-parade, dark-fungus.so03-skewer]

### Battle boar {#battle-boar}

The **battle boar** ({{ja|巨猪}}, written *giant boar*)[@battle-boar.fm10-ja] is a colossal boar, "Nearly two meders tall" and "truly a large-category monster", that charges with brute strength.[@battle-boar.fm09-charge]

- **In the Dungeon:** in DanMachi 9 one bursts through a wall of fire in the Colossal Tree Labyrinth at the head of a swarm of bugbears.[@battle-boar.fm09-charge] Another time Mikoto's iai-like draw cuts the battle boar's neck half through, and it topples.[@battle-boar.fm09-mikoto]
- **The Monsterphilia:** DanMachi 1's main event opens with a captured "Battle Boar", fully two meders tall, charging a [[ganesha-familia|Ganesha Familia]] tamer, who dodges every charge; the narration likens it to "a bull fight".[@battle-boar.fm01-monsterphilia]
- **Xenos:** in DanMachi 10 "the battle boar’s acute sense of smell" leads the [[xenos|Xenos]] through Knossos to their captured comrades.[@battle-boar.fm10-xenos]
- **Outside the Dungeon:** in Sword Oratoria 14 battle boars roam a mountain outside Orario, among the monsters Finn fights there; he finishes off a battle boar charging at Loki with one turning blow.[@battle-boar.so14-mountain, battle-boar.so14-loki]

### Swordstag {#swordstag}

The **swordstag** ({{ja|剣鹿}}, written *sword deer*)[@swordstag.so03-ja] is a deer monster with "bladelike antlers".[@swordstag.so03-antlers] DanMachi 1 says that swordstags and trolls "were born on Level Twenty and below" (the printed "level" is the floor), and a swordstag is among the monsters that escape at the [[monsterphilia|Monsterphilia]].[@swordstag.fm01-floor] In Sword Oratoria 3 a horde of swordstags, with deadly hornets, lizardmen and dark fungi, overruns the adventurers on Floor 24, and one adventurer takes it for a pass parade; Aiz cuts two down in mid-air.[@swordstag.so03-parade, swordstag.so03-antlers] DanMachi 19 and Sword Oratoria 14 print *sword stag*; in Sword Oratoria 14 they roam a mountain outside Orario.[@swordstag.fm19-stag, battle-boar.so14-mountain] In DanMachi 12 Bell's new knife [[equipment#hakugen|Hakugen]] goes "proclaiming death to herds of swordstag monsters", and in DanMachi 19 his squad splits one in two before taking on a mammoth fool.[@swordstag.fm12-hakugen, swordstag.fm19-split] During the Monsterphilia of Sword Oratoria 1, "Even the robust four-legged deer monster Sword Stag burst into chunks of flesh" as Aiz ran by; in Sword Oratoria 3 the swordstag she downs is the first fighting she sees on the way down with Hermes Familia.[@swordstag.so01-aiz, swordstag.so03-hermes]

### Troll {#troll}

**Trolls** are large-class monsters, born from the twentieth floor down; one in Sword Oratoria 12 swings "a giant club".[@troll.ss02-aiz, swordstag.fm01-floor, troll.so12-club]

- **The Monsterphilia:** a troll is among the monsters loose in the city in DanMachi 1, and [[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz]] kills it in the street "in one decisive blow".[@swordstag.fm01-floor, troll.fm01-aiz, troll.so01-aiz]
- **Young Aiz:** in Minor Myths and Legends 2 the young Aiz brings down a horde of trolls by cutting through their thick legs.[@troll.ss02-aiz]
- **The Bloodstained Troll:** the best-known enhanced species, a troll that grew strong by eating other monsters' magic stones. It killed about fifty upper-class adventurers, turned back even the elite party sent to subjugate it, and grew far stronger than the Level the Guild had estimated; [[freya-familia|Freya Familia]] finally killed it.[@troll.so03-bloodstained] In DanMachi 12 Aisha has heard that "more than fifty people died"; Familia Chronicle 2 recalls "the terrible blood-drenched troll incident—where an enhanced species was born of stones left lying around".[@troll.fm12-bloodstained, troll.fc02-incident] See [[magic-stone|magic stone]].
- **Xenos:** the [[xenos|Xenos]] include trolls. At their party in DanMachi 9 a troll hands out wooden tankards of cheap brew, and in DanMachi 11 "the flat-footed troll" runs clumsily beside Wiene.[@troll.fm09-party, troll.fm11-flat]

### Deadly hornet {#deadly-hornet}

**Deadly hornets** ({{ja|巨大蜂}}, written *giant bee*)[@deadly-hornet.so03-ja] are four-winged insects as tall as an adult human, covered in thick black plates like armour, with scissor-shaped pincers and "a poisonous stinger shaped like a pike". They normally appear from the twenty-second floor down, among the monsters that keep third- and second-tier adventurers out of the lower levels.[@deadly-hornet.fm09-hornets]

- **Sting:** the stinger can pierce heavy armour and kill a Level 2 adventurer in one thrust, and even one who escapes instant death cannot avoid a fatal wound.[@deadly-hornet.fm09-hornets]
- **Nickname:** as killer ants are the upper levels' "novice killers", deadly hornets are the "upper-class killer bee" (DanMachi 9), printed "high killer bees" in DanMachi 12.[@deadly-hornet.fm09-hornets, deadly-hornet.fm12-hive]
- **Armour:** their exoskeletons are even harder than killer ants', and arrows bounce off them.[@deadly-hornet.fm12-hive]
- **The bloody hive:** a rare, immobile trap monster that lives with them: a seven-meder, pinecone-shaped nest buried in a wall. It bursts out when prey comes near and shoots an adhesive liquid that pins victims down for the hornets, which, because the hive is joined to the Dungeon, spawn far faster than usual. Bell's party meets one in DanMachi 12.[@deadly-hornet.fm12-hive]
- **Xenos:** among the armed monsters is "a deadly hornet wearing armor" (DanMachi 11), and in Sword Oratoria 12 "A giant deadly hornet held lances in both hands".[@deadly-hornet.fm11-armor, troll.so12-club]

### Mammoth fool {#mammoth-fool}

**Mammoth fools** stand six to seven meders at the withers, with blood-red fur and upturned tusks "as long as spears". Unlike most monsters of the [[colossal-tree-labyrinth|Colossal Tree Labyrinth]], with their special abilities or hard insect shells, they are a monster "whose danger came simply from brute strength", and they are "the largest of the ordinary middle-level monsters".[@mammoth-fool.fm13-herd] In DanMachi 13 a herd attacks the party hunting Gale Wind; in DanMachi 19 Bell's party and an adventurer of Rivira each deal with a large one without trouble.[@mammoth-fool.fm13-herd, mammoth-fool.fm19-bell, mammoth-fool.fm19-rivira]

### Wyvern {#wyvern}

**Wyverns** are dragons with "a pair of wings as forelimbs" that breathe fireballs. The ordinary wyvern is "A monster that inhabited the middle-floor region", with a pale-red body.[@wyvern.so04-ill, wyvern.so09-black]

- **Ill wyverns:** in Sword Oratoria 4 ill wyverns, bluish-purple dragons three meders long with the tail, come out of the tunnels that the [[#valgang-dragon|valgang dragons]]' fireballs bore through the floors and attack [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]] with salvos of fireballs. An enhanced one, the king of the wyverns, hunts [[hyrute-sisters|Tiona]] and [[bete-loga|Bete]] until [[lefiya|Lefiya]]'s arrow of light destroys it.[@wyvern.so04-ill, wyvern.so04-king]
- **The Black Wyvern:** the wyvern young [[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz]] fights in Sword Oratoria 9 is more than five meders long and "pure black": "It was clearly an Irregular, a subspecies hiding far more strength than the usual."[@wyvern.so09-black] Astrea Record 2 says an evil god lured Aiz, then Level 1, down to the twelfth floor and, releasing his divine might, "summoned the Black Wyvern"; Sword Oratoria 10 says the god used [[knossos|Knossos]], and Sword Oratoria 12 names him as [[thanatos|Thanatos]]. Aiz first used her magic to defeat it.[@wyvern.ar02-arcanum, wyvern.so10-knossos, wyvern.so12-thanatos] See also [[upper-levels|Upper levels]].
- **On the surface:** in Astrea Record 3 a flock of wyverns approaches the Casino, and Falgar wonders whether they were brought "all the way from the Valley of Dragons" (see [[valley-of-dragons|Valley of Dragons]]).[@wyvern.ar03-casino]

### Moss huge {#moss-huge}

The **moss huge** is "a type of rare monster that appears on the twenty-fourth floor": a plant in human shape whose body is made of moss.[@moss-huge.fm12-rare] Its "main distinguishing feature" is its ability to "produce replicas of itself that lack magic stones" when cut, so adventurers believe they have killed one while the real monster escapes; moss huges are "not so much bellicose monsters as highly intelligent ones that make ample use of mimicry, ambushes, and getaways".[@moss-huge.fm12-rare]

In DanMachi 12 an **enhanced** moss huge appears in the [[water-capital|Water Capital]], below the middle levels where its kind lives: two meders tall, covered in moss and armoured in tree roots, with hornlike pieces of wood on its head that make Bell think it "probably closer to an ogre".[@moss-huge.fm12-ogre, moss-huge.fm12-rare] It cuts off the arm of the elf adventurer Luvis and plants parasitic vines in [[chigusa|Chigusa]] before Bell and his allies overcome "the Irregular moss huge".[@moss-huge.fm13-luvis, moss-huge.fm13-irregular]

### Green dragon {#green-dragon}

**Green dragons** ({{ja|木竜}}, written *wood dragon*)[@green-dragon.fm09-ja] are "treasure keepers inhabiting the middle levels of the Dungeon": "Monstrous enough to be called the strongest monsters of their floors, they possessed the highest potential a dragon type could have."[@green-dragon.so14-keepers] They guard rare items such as the jewel trees, whose red and blue gems sell for a great deal of money; Sword Oratoria 3 rates them "a good match for even Level Four adventurers", and Minor Myths and Legends 2 says they are "no match for an adventurer of Tiona’s caliber".[@green-dragon.so03-jewel, green-dragon.ss02-jewel] Sword Oratoria 14 describes one that confronts elves outside the Dungeon: ten meders long and well over five high, with ragged membranous wings and bark-like scales.[@green-dragon.so14-keepers]

The [[xenos|Xenos]] have a green dragon too: the scarred, wizened dragon "more than ten meders long" in the Hidden Village of DanMachi 9, the lookout of their village on the twentieth floor.[@green-dragon.fm09-xenos, green-dragon.ss01-lookout]

## Lower levels {#lower-levels}

{{nocite}} The floors from the twenty-fifth down to the deep levels. The sections below are the species of the [[water-capital|Water Capital]], floors 25 to 27, roughly in the order the novels meet them; its floor boss is the [[amphisbaena|Amphisbaena]].

The Guild allows Level 3 adventurers into "the lower levels that start on the twenty-fifth floor", and Level 3 is the standard for these floors.[@lower-levels.fm07-guild, lower-levels.fm12-standard] Their monsters are cleverer than those above: Bell finds them "really intelligent. Way more so than in the upper and middle levels".[@merman.fm13-leader] Aisha warns that the Water Capital has a large number "not just of water monsters but of humanoid monsters as well", which Bell takes to mean sirens, harpies, mermaids and lamias; apart from the winged harpies and sirens, its aquatic monsters "are scarcely impacted by fire-type magic".[@lower-levels.fm12-humanoid, lower-levels.fm13-fire] Purely water-dwelling monsters are born by "breaking through the beds or crystal walls of the streams", which makes them hard to track.[@blue-crab.fm12-land]

### Blue crab {#blue-crab}

**Blue crabs** are "categorized as metal monsters" and live both on land and in the water. They have "irregularly developed claws on either the right or left side, depending on the individual", and strike like hammers; their steel carapaces are "even stronger than those of the deadly hornets" (see [[#deadly-hornet|Deadly hornet]]), but the seams are wide. Level 2 adventurers can take them down by working together, and to the party's surprise they advance head-on instead of scuttling sideways.[@blue-crab.fm12-metal, blue-crab.fm12-land] Ten "steel shells from blue crabs" were among the drop items Lilly lists as proof of the party's Guild mission in DanMachi 12; in DanMachi 14 Welf reinforces his portable hearth with the shells.[@lower-levels.fm12-quest, blue-crab.fm14-hearth] After the first fight Lilly already has "two blue crab shells" ("we’ve only just begun!"), Bell finds that [[equipment#hakugen|Hakugen]] cuts through their steel, and later [[ouka|Ouka]] gets round to one's flank with Far Eastern footwork and smashes its shell with his axe.[@blue-crab.fm12-shells, blue-crab.fm12-ouka] In DanMachi 19 a lone blue crab sneaks up while everyone stares at a dragon and its aurora, and leaps into their midst.[@blue-crab.fm19-ambush]

### Raider fish {#raider-fish}

**Raider fish** are huge fanged fish; the first one Bell's party meets measures 160 celch and leaps out of the water at them.[@raider-fish.fm12-leap] Unlike aqua serpents they "could not emerge onto land", and in DanMachi 14 a school of them attacks Mikoto when she falls into the water.[@raider-fish.fm14-mikoto] Thirty raider fish fangs could stand in for the aqua serpent fins on DanMachi 12's list of drop items.[@lower-levels.fm12-quest]

Raider fish also live outside the Dungeon. Sword Oratoria 2 calls the one [[bete-loga|Bete]] kicks to death in Orario's sewers "a monster native to the open ocean that must have found its way in through the brackish lake".[@raider-fish.so02-sewers] In Sword Oratoria 6 one lurking in a sunken ship was "likely the reason the ship had capsized in the first place"; the Guild has long struggled with raider fish that use the river from Lolog Lake to reach Orario's sewers, and the fishermen of [[njordr#port-meren|Port Meren]] "still get some raider fish attacks".[@raider-fish.so06-ship, raider-fish.so06-river, raider-fish.so06-meren]

### Aqua serpent {#aqua-serpent}

The **aqua serpent** is a large-category water monster with light-green scales and a snake's head; its finned head looks like a dragon's, and "According to information collected by the Guild, aqua serpents can grow up to ten meders long".[@aqua-serpent.fm12-guild] Unlike raider fish, aqua serpents can come out onto land.[@raider-fish.fm14-mikoto] Their fins were one of the drop items on the list for DanMachi 12's mission.[@lower-levels.fm12-quest] In DanMachi 12 one fastens on Bell's shoulder, and he struggles to get it off; in DanMachi 14 aqua serpents rise from the water between the ice-islands as harpies dive at the party.[@aqua-serpent.fm12-shoulder, aqua-serpent.fm14-ice] Later in DanMachi 12 a mermaid of the [[xenos|Xenos]] leads Bell away from another, swimming "faster than I can run"; in DanMachi 14 [[aisha-belka|Aisha]]'s podao slices one through.[@aqua-serpent.fm12-mermaid, aqua-serpent.fm14-aisha]

### Crystal turtle {#crystal-turtle}

**Crystal turtles** ({{ja|水晶巨亀}}, written *giant crystal turtle*)[@crystal-turtle.fm12-ja] are large-category monsters with "multiple crystals projecting from its body"; on the twenty-fifth floor Bell worries that a rocky bank might be one.[@lower-levels.fm12-shore, crystal-turtle.fm13-large, crystal-turtle.fm12-bank]

### Devil mosquito {#devil-mosquito}

**Devil mosquitoes** hover in the air of the Water Capital; Bell calls the devil mosquito "hideous", a monster "that latches on to adventurers and sucks their blood".[@lower-levels.fm12-shore, devil-mosquito.fm12-air]

### Iguaçu {#iguacu}

The **iguaçu** ({{ja|閃燕}}, written *flash swallow*)[@iguacu.fm12-ja] are swallow monsters of the Water Capital that live in the cliff behind the Great Falls. Adventurers call them "invisible monsters" for their speed, and Minor Myths and Legends 1 calls them "the fastest creature found on the lower floors".[@iguacu.fm12-swallows, iguacu.ss01-fastest] They dive-bomb their prey; an iguaçu that hits a shield or another hard object is crushed by its own speed, and upper-class adventurers say "if you encounter an iguaçu, you should drop anything you’re holding and run". Their severed feathers are drop items.[@iguacu.fm12-swallows, iguacu.ss01-fastest]

In DanMachi 12 Bell, separated from his party by the current at the foot of the Great Falls, survives "a mass outbreak of iguaçu" alone; afterwards stories spread about "the weirdo who massacred an entire pack of iguaçu by himself".[@iguacu.fm12-swallows, iguacu.ss01-fastest]

### Harpy {#harpy}

**Harpies** ({{ja|半人半鳥}}, written *half human, half bird*)[@harpy.fm12-ja] are "half-human/half-bird, bizarrely female-looking monsters". Some live on the surface: in DanMachi 8 a flock attacks Bell and [[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz]] in the Beor Mountain Range, descendants of the Originals that went out onto the surface and settled there, and Aiz cuts them down.[@harpy.fm08-beor] In the Dungeon they fly with sirens and other bird-type monsters around the Great Falls of the Water Capital, where Bell finds them "far swifter than those who live on land".[@lower-levels.fm12-falls, harpy.fm12-siren] Harpies fly better than sirens and attack at close range with hooked claws; in DanMachi 14 a flock dives at the party while aqua serpents attack from the water.[@harpy.fm12-siren, aqua-serpent.fm14-ice] Fia of the [[xenos|Xenos]] is a red-haired harpy.[@harpy.fm14-fia]

Harpies also shoot their feathers. In DanMachi 9 the Xenos' harpies answer Lilly's arrows with "a volley of feathers", and in DanMachi 10 [[xenos#fia|Fia]] clears a swarm of deadly hornets with "feathery projectiles"; against the hunters, "the harpy’s feathery missiles ricocheted off armor".[@harpy.fm09-feathers, harpy.fm10-feathers] Fire works on them: in DanMachi 9 Bell's Firebolt brings down a harpy and a griffin in midair, and in DanMachi 13 the winged harpies and sirens are the exception among the Water Capital's monsters, the others being "scarcely impacted by fire-type magic".[@harpy.fm09-feathers, harpy.fm13-fire] In the Xenos' hidden village, harpies crowd round Mikoto and Haruhime, most interested in Haruhime's scent, and "A young harpy girl" drags Mikoto into the dance.[@harpy.fm09-village] When a winged creature is sighted in Orario's seventh district in DanMachi 9, witnesses describe it as "a harpy or siren"; in Sword Oratoria 10 a humanoid monster seen in the western quarter is likewise said to be "like a harpy or a siren".[@harpy.fm09-sighting, harpy.so10-sighting]

### Siren {#siren}

**Sirens** ({{ja|歌人鳥}}, written *singer bird*)[@siren.fm12-ja] are bird-human monsters like harpies: both have "the faces of women and the bodies of birds", and the wild ones Bell meets in the Water Capital are wrinkled, smell foul and are "uglier than old crones". Sirens fly less well than harpies but "emit irregular sound waves as they fly", so they attack from a distance.[@harpy.fm12-siren] Their high-pitched songs fill the air over the Great Falls, and in DanMachi 14 harpies, sirens and other winged monsters force [[tsubaki|Tsubaki]]'s group off the cliff route below the twenty-fifth floor.[@lower-levels.fm12-falls, siren.fm14-cliff] Seeing the wild ones, Bell realises "how far Rei and Fia have deviated from the norms of their race": [[rei|Rei]] of the Xenos is a siren, and Fia a harpy.[@harpy.fm12-siren, harpy.fm14-fia]

Their songs are a known danger: [[asfi|Asfi]]'s [[silence-lyra|Silence Lyra]] (Astrea Record 3's *Silence Lyre*) was first made "to protect against the alluring songs of mermaids and sirens", and DanMachi 18 says it was "originally designed to block the songs of sirens and mermaids".[@siren.ar03-lyre, siren.fm18-lyra] Other monsters' cries are measured against theirs: Sword Oratoria 5 likens a cry "capable of restricting adventurers’ movements" to those "of bad bats and sirens", and Sword Oratoria 14 calls an airen's eerie song "a psychic attack, not unlike that of a siren or mermaid".[@siren.so05-cry, siren.so14-airen] A winged creature sighted in the city in DanMachi 9 is described as "a harpy or siren".[@harpy.fm09-sighting]

### Mermaid {#mermaid}

**Mermaids** ({{ja|人魚}})[@mermaid.fm12-ja] are half-fish, half-human monsters with hair "the drab green of a withered plant", pure-white eyes and bluish-white skin; DanMachi 12 says "Mermaids are among the rare monsters of this floor", more attractive than harpies but "distinctly creepy".[@mermaid.fm12-rare] The mermaid Mari of the [[xenos|Xenos]], whose blood heals Bell, guides him through the twenty-fifth floor with her songs in DanMachi 12; see [[water-capital|Water Capital]].[@mermaid.fm12-mari, lower-levels.fm12-shore] Mermaids "have almost no ability to engage in direct battle": their weapon is the charm of their songs, which only blocked ears or "unyielding mental strength" can resist. When their charm fails on Bell, a Firebolt sends them screaming into the water.[@mermaid.fm12-charm] Their drop item, mermaid blood, is a recovery item that "is rumored to restore energy, heal wounds, and even reverse the effects of poison".[@mermaid.fm12-charm] In the water they are so fast that even among water-dwelling monsters they are called the birds of the water.[@mermaid.fm12-birds]

### Merman {#merman}

**Mermen** are "half-fish, half-human monsters" covered in blue scales. They walk on two feet and wield the Dungeon's nature weapons, such as crystal maces, "a type of lower-level nature weapon", and Bell likens them to "an underwater version of lizardmen" (see [[#lizardman|Lizardman]]).[@merman.fm13-mermen] They appear from the twenty-sixth floor and are among the stronger enemies of the Water Capital, and they fight in groups under a leader: once the leader is killed, "the group falls into chaos".[@merman.fm13-mermen, merman.fm13-leader] In DanMachi 13 Bell kills a merman leader with his black knife; in DanMachi 14 Lilly's arrow pierces a leader's eye, a rare shot for a supporter.[@merman.fm13-leader, merman.fm14-lilly] In DanMachi 13 Bell's kick topples several mermen and Bors's adventurers beat them "to a pulp"; Bell knows their habits from "Eina’s lessons", and finds the monsters there, "like the merman leader", far more intelligent than "in the upper and middle levels".[@merman.fm13-fight] In DanMachi 14 one merman crushes the corpse of another that Welf has cut in half as it chases him, and [[anya-fromel|Anya]] {{small|printed *Ahnya*}} mows down a pack "with one swing of her golden lance".[@merman.fm14-welf, merman.fm14-ahnya]

### Light quartz {#light-quartz}

**Light quartzes** are "inorganic monsters that float about adventurers’ heads": purple crystals the size of bucklers with a single pale-yellow, eyelike organ. They cannot fight at close range; their one attack is a beam of light that burns lines into the Dungeon's crystal floor and walls. "The typical way to defeat a light quartz is to get it to emit all its power, then attack it while it’s recharging."[@light-quartz.fm13-beam] They are met from the twenty-fifth floor to the twenty-seventh.[@lower-levels.fm12-shore, light-quartz.fm14-27th] In DanMachi 13, travelling with Bors's party, Bell hears "Shit, it’s a light quartz!" and swings his black scarf "like a whip, or rather a chain": "It blocks and repels the beams from multiple light quartzes before crashing straight into them!" The crushed light quartzes "either go silent as the light disappears from their eyes or lose their magic stones and turn to ash".[@light-quartz.fm13-scarf]

### Kelpie {#kelpie}

**Kelpies** are horse monsters "with blue pelts and manes as well as finned bodies" that gallop through water as if on land. They normally appear on the twenty-seventh floor, and their beauty "belied a potential that was among the greatest of any in the Water Capital". In DanMachi 14 one comes up to a higher floor, a specimen whose potential "might even have exceeded Welf’s and Ouka’s statuses" (Welf's "knees quaked at the sight of the kelpie thrashing wildly"), and Aisha cuts it down.[@kelpie.fm14-kelpie] In DanMachi 10 Hestia watches tamers bring a rampaging kelpie to heel with whips; [[ganesha-familia|Ganesha Familia]] alone may keep live monsters in the city.[@kelpie.fm10-tamers] In DanMachi 14 Runoa's knuckledusters tear through a kelpie "rearing up to strike", and the voltemeria's potential is ranked "among the highest in the Water Capital, right alongside the kelpie".[@kelpie.fm14-runoa, kelpie.fm14-rank]

### Lamia {#lamia}

**Lamias** ({{ja|半人半蛇}}, written *half human, half snake*)[@lamia.fm12-ja] have "a humanoid upper body with a snakelike lower body", as a vouivre usually does (see [[#vouivre|Vouivre]]); Sword Oratoria 10 describes a lamia as "a half snake, half human".[@lamia.fm09-vouivre, lamia.so10-half] The lamia, afanc and dodora that turn up in DanMachi 14 are all "monsters that normally appeared for the first time on the twenty-seventh floor".[@lamia.fm14-27th] Several [[xenos|Xenos]] are lamias: in DanMachi 14 one saves Cassandra from a voltemeria and winks at Lilly, and Minor Myths and Legends 1 names one, Laura.[@lamia.fm14-xenos, lamia.ss01-laura]

DanMachi 10 likens the rampaging vouivre, "a rare large-category monster", to a lamia in shape.[@lamia.fm10-rare] Their humanlike look draws "a perverse attraction" from some people, and the hunters' "hellish prison" holds lamias with Scylla, mermaids and other "dazzling monsters with human characteristics", all scarred from torture.[@lamia.fm10-fetish, lamia.fm10-prison] In the Xenos' rampage of DanMachi 10 a green-haired lamia, splattered with blood, slashes at Bell with "bladelike claws"; in DanMachi 11 [[gareth|Gareth]] cuts off a lamia's "flailing tail"; and in Sword Oratoria 12 a Xenos lamia screams in broken human speech.[@lamia.fm10-claws, lamia.fm11-gareth, lamia.so12-accent]

### Voltemeria {#voltemeria}

The **voltemeria** is "a rare monster found only on" the twenty-seventh floor, ranked with the kelpie among the strongest of the Water Capital: a black fish of stone, "exceedingly resistant to physical attack", with jaws and fangs that "could crush even the heaviest armor donned by adventurers", and a single eye in its forehead.[@voltemeria.fm14-rare, voltemeria.fm14-eyes] Built like light quartzes, voltemerias float about three meders above the ground but swim through the air far faster, so adventurers call them "flying fossils". They normally live where several waterways meet in deep pools.[@voltemeria.fm14-rare] In DanMachi 14 a school of about thirty, drawn by the smell of blood, traps Lilly's party, until a pack of [[xenos|Xenos]] arrives and destroys them.[@voltemeria.fm14-rare, voltemeria.fm14-xenos, lamia.fm14-xenos]

### Poison vermis {#poison-vermis}

**Poison vermis** are small maggot-like monsters. "Of all the poison-inflicting monsters in the Dungeon, poison vermis were the most dangerous": the toxin they spew from their mouths and secrete from their pores can afflict even upper-class adventurers with strong status resistances, though their attack power is low. They spring from carcasses in droves like maggots, hence the nickname "poison graveyards".[@poison-vermis.so05-swarm] The poison needs a treatment "made from its own secretions", and that antivenin is hard to find, because "Poison vermis inhabited only the lower levels" and are not numerous there.[@poison-vermis.so05-antivenin, poison-vermis.so05-rare, poison-vermis.ss02-rare]

- **Sword Oratoria 5:** on the way back from its expedition, [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]] meets an Irregular, a continuous spawning of poison vermis; giant maggots about thirty celches in size drop on the party from the ceiling "like purple rain". The poisoned members wait on the eighteenth floor while others look for antivenin in Orario; in Minor Myths and Legends 2 Bete and Loki find it hard to gather. See also [[dian-cecht-familia|Dian Cecht Familia]].[@poison-vermis.so05-swarm, poison-vermis.so05-rain, poison-vermis.so05-rare, poison-vermis.ss02-rare]
- **Sword Oratoria 7:** poison vermis appear in [[knossos|Knossos]], to the surprise of Tiona's group ("But why would poison vermis be in a place like this…?!"), and Arcus is poisoned.[@poison-vermis.so07-knossos]
- **As a substance:** in Sword Oratoria 2 Aiz is told that human skin steeped in poison vermis fluid does not rot, and Familia Chronicle 1 names a "Liquid Poison Vermis".[@poison-vermis.so02-corpse, poison-vermis.fc01-liquid]

### Bloodsaurus {#bloodsaurus}

**Bloodsauruses** ({{ja|紅の捕食者}}, written *crimson predator*)[@bloodsaurus.fm20-ja] are dinosaur-type monsters with "A jaw big enough to swallow a person whole", razor-sharp teeth and scales the colour of blood; in the Dungeon they are "Over ten meders long" and "comparable to a floor boss", and swift as well.[@bloodsaurus.fm20-jaw] They "usually appear starting at the thirtieth floor": DanMachi 12 calls that floor the place "where you really start to need a high level and status", with "swarms of bloodsauruses", and DanMachi 19 describes the forested floors where they "begin to appear", with Level 3 "a requirement for survival there".[@bloodsaurus.fm20-jaw, bloodsaurus.fm12-thirtieth, bloodsaurus.fm19-forest] Their carnivorous nature makes them "prone to becoming enhanced types". In DanMachi 20 three of them come up to the twenty-ninth floor, and Bell destroys the largest, an enhanced one, by wrapping its magic stone in the Goliath Scarf; bloodsaurus fangs are among the expedition's drop items. The same chapter names other monsters of these floors, the spinoaxes ("comparable in size to the bloodsauruses") and the almalosauruses.[@bloodsaurus.fm20-jaw, bloodsaurus.fm20-fangs]

Bloodsauruses also live on the surface. In DanMachi 4 Bell's party meets several in the Seoro Forest on Nahza's quest; there they "might be a little bit stronger than an orc in the Dungeon", and the one met was only five meders long.[@bloodsaurus.fm04-surface, bloodsaurus.fm20-jaw]

### Blue dragon {#blue-dragon}

The **blue dragon**, "also known as an aurora dragon", is "A rare monster from the lower floors", "up there with carbuncles in rarity": a long dragon of about ten meders with blue-and-white scales and winglike fins, which swims through the air trailing folds of light that shine red, greenish white and bluish purple.[@blue-dragon.fm19-aurora] In DanMachi 19 one appears above the adventurers and students of the [[school-district|School District]]; flying out of reach, it can only be brought down with magic, arrows or other long-range attacks.[@blue-dragon.fm19-aurora] Bors spots it first: "Oh! You’re in luck, brats! It’s a blue dragon!" From "more than twenty meders overhead" it unleashes breath attacks; "The crystals corroded all around them", and the water's surface gives off "a putrid smoke". [[nina-tulle|Nina]]'s purifying barrier, Lagriell Krisheim, cleanses even "the aurora of the blue dragon, which had such a higher potential than the half-elf girl’s".[@blue-dragon.fm19-breath]

## Deep levels {#deep-levels}

{{nocite}} The warrior, skeleton and rock monsters of the White Palace, [[floor-37|Floor 37]], then the ooze and the valgang dragons of the fifty-eighth floor. See also the [[#lizardman|lizardman elite]], the [[colosseum|Colosseum]] and the floor boss [[udaeus|Udaeus]].

Floor 37's monsters are mostly warrior types that fight hand to hand, "specialists in hand-to-hand combat who bring brute strength, agility, and mastery of nature weapons to their fights"; the floor is "absolute torture for magic users", who have no time to cast.[@loup-garou.fm14-sword, barbarian.so02-white]

### Barbarian {#barbarian}

The **barbarian** is a large-category warrior monster with "a body on par with a Minotaur", two twisted horns, and sharp fangs and claws; it swings nature weapons and lashes a long tongue.[@barbarian.so02-white, barbarian.fm14-ambush]

- **Drops:** a mixture of barbarian hearts and other organs is a drop item "normally used like powerful smelling salts"; Lyu carries a bag of it to keep monsters from sniffing them out. The barbarians' "oily hair" burns extremely well.[@barbarian.fm14-hearts, barbarian.fm14-hair]
- **Under [[daedalus-street|Daedalus Street]]:** in DanMachi 8, at the request of the children of [[marias-orphanage|the orphanage]], Bell meets a barbarian "over two meders tall" in a secret underground passage ("These things belong in the Deep Zone!"). It fights with everything it has, as if to drive off something that would harm it, perhaps even in fear, until a single javelin from [[allen-fromel|Vana Freya]] of Freya Familia punches through its chest; its drop item, Barbarian Fur {{small|printed *Barbarian Skin*}}, is left still burning.[@barbarian.fm08-tunnel, barbarian.fm16-allen] DanMachi 9 records an adventurer's report of "a barbarian in an underground tunnel close to an orphanage in Daedalus Street"; Bell later wonders whether it was a Xenos, and in DanMachi 11 reasons that the hunters' smuggling route from [[knossos|Knossos]] must have a door there "that the barbarian escaped through". DanMachi 14 calls it "the heretical barbarian".[@barbarian.fm09-report, barbarian.fm10-xenos, barbarian.fm11-door, barbarian.fm14-hair]
- **In DanMachi 14:** one ambushes [[lyu-leon|Lyu]] at the head of a pack of lizardmen until Bell's flying kick cracks its chest bones; when Lyu falls to the Colosseum's third plate she is "grabbed by a waiting barbarian"; in the Colosseum an enhanced barbarian crushes a loup-garou's head; and Bell kills a wounded barbarian that survived the fall from the Colosseum by letting it come to him and piercing its magic stone.[@barbarian.fm14-ambush, barbarian.fm14-kick, barbarian.fm14-plate, barbarian.fm14-colosseum, barbarian.fm14-last]

### Loup-garou {#loup-garou}

**Loup-garous** ({{ja|狡狼}}, written *cunning wolf*)[@loup-garou.fm14-ja] are wolf-headed warrior monsters that carry nature weapons such as stone swords and white stone cudgels.[@loup-garou.fm14-sword, loup-garou.fm14-cudgel] Werewolf-type monsters have invaded the surface: "Most of the time when a village gets ransacked on a moonlit night, it’s by a pack of these loup-garous", and animal people beat any such monster to a pulp.[@loup-garou.fm14-sword]

On Floor 37 they rank with the lizardman elites: a barbarian is "as fierce as a lizardman elite or a loup-garou", a spartoi stronger than either.[@barbarian.fm14-ambush, spartoi.fm14-pack] Bell turns one's stolen white stone cudgel against them; with sword and [[equipment#hakugen|Hakugen]] he cuts one's leg away and halves another as it leaps over him, and turns their claws aside with the Goliath Scarf.[@loup-garou.fm14-cudgel, loup-garou.fm14-bell] A peluda's poisonous spines kill one, its skin turning black as it convulses; in the Colosseum the scarf sweeps three away at once, and Lyu rips one open with a dead adventurer's sword.[@loup-garou.fm14-spines, loup-garou.fm14-colosseum] In Familia Chronicle 2 [[ottar|Ottar]] disintegrates them with single slashes of his greatsword.[@loup-garou.fc02-ottar]

### Skull sheep {#skull-sheep}

**Skull sheep** are skeleton monsters covered to the hooves by "a long, wide skin", dark and torn like "a ragged old robe"; Bell first mistakes one for the grim reaper.[@skull-sheep.fm14-robe] The skin lets them "conceal themselves in the darkness" of the floor and strike from it. They pierce with their elongated bones and shoot bone lances, "long-distance projectiles"; DanMachi 14 calls them "The hermits who feasted on death".[@skull-sheep.fm14-robe, skull-sheep.fm14-lance, skull-sheep.fm14-hermits] The [[juggernaut|Juggernaut]] of DanMachi 14 absorbs them and uses their pila.[@skull-sheep.fm14-juggernaut]

A skull sheep is "a midsize sheep-like monster", "around 140 celches high".[@skull-sheep.fm14-size] The first Bell meets bites a small piece out of his arm in a mere graze, and its fangs clack uselessly on the [[goliath-robe#goliath-scarf|Goliath Scarf]]; later his Firebolts blow three apart, and one retreats into the darkness when he aims his magic at it.[@skull-sheep.fm14-first] One skull sheep's bone lance, meant for Bell, skewers a [[#loup-garou|loup-garou]] instead; in the Colosseum a herd of skull sheep tears apart an enhanced barbarian.[@skull-sheep.fm14-misfire, skull-sheep.fm14-colosseum]

### Spartoi {#spartoi}

**Spartoi** are skeleton warriors of white bone, Bell's height or taller, armed with swords or lances of bone and kite-shield-like protectors. DanMachi 14 rates them "stronger than a lizardman elite or a loup-garou, and more agile by far than a barbarian", moving with the skill of an adventurer, and advises fleeing from a spartoi "if at all possible".[@spartoi.fm14-pack, spartoi.fm14-flee] The floor boss [[udaeus|Udaeus]] looks like "a spartoi that had just kept on growing", and summons spartoi around its room.[@spartoi.fm02-udaeus, spartoi.so02-udaeus, spartoi.fc02-ottar] In the [[colosseum|Colosseum]] a spartoi born from the floor beneath Bell gives him and Lyu away.[@spartoi.fm14-found]

Spartoi are rated at Level 4: DanMachi 2 calls the species "categorized at level four", and Sword Oratoria 2 Aiz's "level-four-category opponents".[@spartoi.fm02-aiz, spartoi.so02-aiz] Sword Oratoria 2 names them "the most physically dangerous monsters" of Floor 37, "extremely strong and fast", so that facing one is "similar to fighting against a skilled adventurer"; [[lyu-leon|Lyu]] adds that they are "spawned with weapons made of bone" and that "Some individuals even carry javelins."[@spartoi.so02-aiz, spartoi.fm14-flee] Their look is so well known that "even lower-tier adventurers who can’t make it past the middle levels know about them", and when [[fels|Fels]] shows their skull in DanMachi 9, three voices shriek "A spartoi?!"[@spartoi.fm14-known, spartoi.fm09-fels] Like Udaeus, a spartoi is held together by centres of magic energy between its bones, though they glow far less brightly, and "a common strategy to cripple them was to strike those areas".[@spartoi.so02-joints]

In DanMachi 14 spartoi spring from the floor around Bell and Lyu "like undead rising from the grave" and fight as a team with swords, shields, lances and axes: in all the levels he has crossed, Bell "had never encountered a species that worked together so well". Lyu breaks one's arm and Bell takes its shield as cover from the [[#peluda|peludas]]' spines, and in the Colosseum a pack of spartoi has "the upper hand" over a pack of lizardman elites.[@spartoi.fm14-team, spartoi.fm14-colosseum] In DanMachi 2 Aiz alone destroys "a group of at least ten spartois" in the deep levels, and [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]]'s supporters collect the magic stones from the remains.[@spartoi.fm02-aiz] In Sword Oratoria 2 Udaeus calls up "a mass of spartois" like foot soldiers guarding their king; the "small army" that Aiz and Riveria slay leaves many drop items besides Udaeus's black sword.[@spartoi.so02-summon, spartoi.so02-items]

### Obsidian soldier {#obsidian-soldier}

**Obsidian soldiers** are rock monsters with bodies of solidified lava: slower than the warrior monsters, strong only in defence, and "Said to be among the poorest fighters on the thirty-seventh floor". Their bodies counteract magic "as effectively as amulet stones", so mages can do little against them.[@obsidian-soldier.fm14-rock, obsidian-soldier.so02-magic] Their stone is a valued drop item, used for superior-grade magic-resistant shields and armour, and [[knossos|Knossos]] is built with it to weaken magic.[@obsidian-soldier.fm14-rock, obsidian-soldier.so07-drop, obsidian-soldier.so10-knossos] The Juggernaut reinforces its shell with obsidian soldiers' stone.[@obsidian-soldier.fm14-juggernaut] In DanMachi 14 an obsidian soldier joins the spartoi around Bell and Lyu ("Shit—an obsidian soldier?!") and is one of the few monsters still moving under the peludas' spines, until it and the spartoi "exploded into countless shards"; later Bell shatters several behind a barbarian.[@obsidian-soldier.fm14-shards] In Sword Oratoria 2 Aiz slices "a stout obsidian soldier" in half.[@obsidian-soldier.so02-aiz] When the flesh walls are blasted away in Sword Oratoria 12, Knossos's original labyrinth shows "gleams of adamantite shining from behind the battered and collapsing stone from obsidian soldiers".[@obsidian-soldier.so12-knossos]

### Peluda {#peluda}

**Peludas** are monsters of [[floor-37|Floor 37]] that cling close to the rocks like lizards. Lyu names them, with the [[#spartoi|spartoi]], as the floor's greatest threats: "Peludas attack with poison", and "If we run into a spartoi or a peluda, we should flee if at all possible."[@peluda.fm14-lyu, peluda.fm14-colosseum] They shoot the poisoned spines on their backs, which easily get through even an upper-class adventurer's Abnormal Resistance, and "Befitting their dragon bloodline" they breathe fire; around the [[colosseum|Colosseum]] they make the floor "A hell of poison and flame".[@peluda.fm14-spines, peluda.fm14-colosseum] Lyu blames "poisoned darts from a peluda" for the deaths of adventurers they find in a room.[@peluda.fm14-darts] In DanMachi 14 three peludas pin Bell and Lyu down with spines and, when that fails, turn their surroundings into "a sea of fire with their scorching breath"; a fourth ends "Pinned to the wall like a scientific specimen with a knife through its magic stone". Around the Colosseum the din does not disturb them, and Bell guesses that Lyu used a peluda's fiery breath to ignite her last Inferno Stone.[@peluda.fm14-fight]

### Ooze {#ooze}

The **ooze**, "Also known as a slime", is "a rare monster that only starts to appear on the deep floors and below". Oozes come in many colours, such as red, white and green, all with the same mucus body, and attack with the poison or acid in their mucus, which depends on the colour ("The yellow ones are acidic"); the worst get into the body through the mouth or ears and ravage it from within.[@ooze.ss01-ooze] They are slow, but their liquid bodies are hard to damage: they can be burned or frozen with spells or magic swords, or killed by piercing the magic stone inside the body with one sharp blow, an amethyst crystal. Their drop item, ooze fluid, is "literally just the creature itself". In Minor Myths and Legends 1, stranded in the deep levels, [[lyu-leon|Lyu]] looks for blue oozes, kills one and boils its fluid for Bell and herself to drink.[@ooze.ss01-ooze]

### Fomoire {#fomoire}

The **Fomoire** ({{ja|フォモール}})[@fomoire.so01-ja] (so printed in Sword Oratoria 1; *formoire* in DanMachi 9 and 10) are hulking monsters of the deep levels. In Sword Oratoria 1 a horde of them howls beyond [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]]'s shield wall on the forty-ninth floor, and an abnormally tall one breaks through the front line ("The forty-ninth floor is always rough").[@fomoire.so01-wall, fomoire.so01-rough] Black rhinos are "walking fortresses compared to the Fomoire on floor forty-nine", and in Sword Oratoria 4 the monsters massed on the fifty-eighth floor are likened to the Fomoire horde of the Moitra wasteland.[@black-rhino.so01-rhinos, fomoire.so04-moitra] Among the [[xenos|Xenos]], Foh is "a formoire from the deep levels", a hulking monster with a kind heart; in DanMachi 10 he falls to Ikelos Familia's hunters while guarding Wiene's party.[@fomoire.fm10-foh] His mace sends hunters flying, and "Even with five ganging up on the formoire, they couldn’t suppress him", until Dix's "rubellite spearhead" pierces his breastplate.[@fomoire.fm10-fight] In the Xenos' trial of DanMachi 9 a formoire batters Welf's defence with "a metallic club", and at the Xenos' party the formoires pound the floor with their fists "like drums".[@fomoire.fm09-xenos]

### Black rhino {#black-rhino}

**Black rhinos** ({{ja|深層種}}, written *deep-level species*)[@black-rhino.fm12-ja] are "Rhinoceros monsters that walked on two legs", just under two meders tall, with two horns, one long and one short; their hide is "as strong and thick as high-quality armor".[@black-rhino.so01-rhinos] In the deep levels they burst out of the walls in hordes, more than thirty at a time in Sword Oratoria 1; in Sword Oratoria 4 [[tsubaki|Tsubaki]] picks up a black rhino horn, a drop item.[@black-rhino.so01-rhinos, black-rhino.so04-horn] The Guild officially designated the black minotaur [[asterios|Asterios]] "a subspecies of black rhino, a creature found in the deep levels", with a potential of Level 7.[@black-rhino.fm12-asterios] In DanMachi 10 an onlooker says the black minotaur "Looks more like a black rhino subspecies", and in Sword Oratoria 10 Gareth guesses that it is a subspecies of the black rhino, whose hide is hard to begin with.[@black-rhino.fm10-subspecies, black-rhino.so10-hide]

In Sword Oratoria 1 [[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz]] and Tiona take on a horde of black rhinos head-on. Later a caterpillar monster melts a black rhino that comes out of a side passage and swallows its upper body, and the caterpillars' exploding acid strikes the black rhinos fighting Tiona's group.[@black-rhino.so01-horde, black-rhino.so01-caterpillars] In Sword Oratoria 3 Aiz recalls the caterpillars on the fifty-first floor ignoring the adventurers to devour "a group of black rhinos".[@black-rhino.so03-devoured]

### Deformis spider {#deformis-spider}

The **deformis spider** is a giant red-and-purple spider of the deep levels, with eight legs and compound eyes. In Sword Oratoria 1 one bursts out of the wall beside [[lefiya|Lefiya]]; in Sword Oratoria 4, where black rhinos and deformis spiders attack from every angle, another drags her toward its hole with its massive thread.[@deformis-spider.so01-spider, black-rhino.so04-horn, deformis-spider.so04-thread] In Sword Oratoria 1 [[hyrute-sisters|Tione]] kills the first by twisting the Kukri knife she threw into its face and cutting it apart. The same volume ranks Cadmus, "the strongest monster on the fifty-first floor", far above them: "Even swarms of black rhinos and deformis spiders wouldn’t stand a chance."[@deformis-spider.so01-tione] In Sword Oratoria 11 [[asfi|Asfi]] names "the compound eye of a deformis spider" among the Dungeon items with which "it was possible to replicate" the eye of Daedalus's descendants, which serves as the key to [[knossos|Knossos]], and so makes a copy of the Daedalus Orb.[@deformis-spider.so11-eye]

### Cadmus {#cadmus}

**Cadmus** ({{ja|強竜}}, written *strong dragon*)[@cadmus.so01-ja], "the Great Dragon", is "an extremely rare monster that had been strong enough to contend with a floor boss", the guardian of the Cadmus Springs on the fifty-first floor; Sword Oratoria 3 counts "the Cadmus dragons on the fifty-first floor" among the treasure keepers that guard rare items.[@cadmus.so01-springs, cadmus.so01-guardian, green-dragon.so03-jewel] In Sword Oratoria 1 [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]] takes a [[dian-cecht-familia|Dian Cecht Familia]] quest for water from the springs, and finds the Cadmus already reduced to ash, its drop items uncollected: "Something strong enough to kill Cadmus. Not adventurers."[@cadmus.so01-springs, cadmus.so01-ash] Its drop item, the **Cadmus Hide**, is "An extremely rare drop item", worth all the equipment of a large party, an excellent material for armour and valued for healing items; Tione later sold one.[@cadmus.so01-hide, cadmus.so01-uses, cadmus.ss02-sold]

### Valgang dragon {#valgang-dragon}

**Valgang dragons** are the great red dragons of the fifty-eighth floor. [[zeus-and-hera-familias|Zeus Familia]], which holds the record for the farthest floor reached, nicknamed that area of the Dungeon *The Dragon's Urn* (also printed *Dragon Urn*) because of "the valgang dragons inhabiting the fifty-eighth floor".[@valgang-dragon.so04-urn, wyvern.so04-ill] Like wyverns they have "enormous wings for forelimbs"; they bombard the floors above with fireballs that bore tunnels through them, and specialise in "long-range magic salvos", with only their tails for close combat, so the area in front of one is the safest place for an attacker.[@valgang-dragon.so04-close, wyvern.so04-ill] In Sword Oratoria 4 Lefiya, Bete and their group fall down "the shaft created by the valgang dragon", while Finn's group takes the standard route. Valgang-dragon fangs and scales were among the drop items Loki Familia delivered to [[hephaistos-familia|Hephaistos Familia]] afterwards, and Riveria's Veil Breath can protect an adventurer "from even a valgang dragon’s big fireballs".[@valgang-dragon.so12-shaft, valgang-dragon.so05-drops, valgang-dragon.so04-veil, valgang-dragon.so12-veil]

## Other monsters {#other-monsters}

### Violas {#violas}

**Violas** ({{ja|食人花}}, written *man-eating flower*)[@violas.so03-ja] are carnivorous plant monsters with snakelike bodies, vivid flower heads and gaping jaws. They belong to the brood of the [[corrupted-spirit|corrupted spirit]], whose crystal-orb fetuses use them "as arms to collect magic stones".[@violas.so03-name, violas.so05-buds, violas.so06-arms]

- **Names:** Sword Oratoria 1 and 2 call them carnivorous plants, man-eating flowers and flower monsters. The name *violas* is first located in Sword Oratoria 3, where [[levis|Levis]] offers to "lend you some violas".[@violas.so01-plants, violas.so02-flowers, violas.so03-name]
- **Body:** their tails are thick and bulbous, with tendrils and root hairs like a tree's roots.[@violas.so02-tails] They fight with whip-like tentacles and body slams, and attack other monsters as well as adventurers.[@violas.so03-whips]
- **Magic stone:** a viola's magic stone lies deep in its throat, not in the chest as in most monsters.[@violas.so02-throat, so03-core]
- **Magic:** they respond to magic and attack its source at once. Blunt force does little to them, but slashing attacks hurt them.[@violas.so03-aiz] When [[lefiya|Lefiya]] chants, they are "drawn to her like moths to a flame".[@violas.so03-lefiya]
- **Magic stones:** they share the caterpillars' type of magic stone. Thrown magic stones make them ignore adventurers and go for the stones.[@violas.so03-stones, violas.so03-thrown] On Floor 59, caterpillars and violas offer the magic stones in their mouths to the demi-spirit.[@violas.so04-offer]

### Varg {#varg}

**Vargs** ({{ja|晶黽}}, written *crystal water strider*)[@varg.so11-ja] are "a new monster" that Loki Familia meets in [[knossos|Knossos]] in Sword Oratoria 7; [[valletta|Valletta]] carries a small crystal that keeps them off: "So long as we’ve got this crystal, those vargs won’t even come close".[@varg.so07-new] They are "a small breed with a red crystal instead of a magic stone", "insectile", "vividly colored monsters in the shape of water spiders", and they come in swarms beside the [[#violas|violas]].[@varg.so10-crystal, varg.so11-spiders]

- **Birth:** vargs grow from plants. In Sword Oratoria 10 Thanatos is told, "We lost one of the varg plants"; in Sword Oratoria 11 a plant of green meat, with crystals like those on vargs, gives birth to them from the ooze of the egg at its centre.[@varg.so10-plants, varg.so11-spiders]
- **In battle:** they well up from manholes and gaps in the walls of Daedalus Street (Sword Oratoria 10), and in Sword Oratoria 12 the labyrinth summons a swarm of violas and vargs; many of the smaller ones try to spit acid from midrange.[@varg.so10-crystal, varg.so12-swarm]

**Name:** printed *varg* / *vargs*; Fandom's *Valg* is a search alias.[@varg.so07-new]

### Viskum {#viskum}

The **viskum** ({{ja|巨大花}}, written *giant flower*)[@viskum.so03-ja] are the huge flower-headed monsters that [[olivas-act|Olivas Act]] unleashes on the adventurers in a pantry in Sword Oratoria 3 ("Go, my viskum!"). Feeding on the "nutritious liquid" of the pantry's quartz tower, they have grown "to a scale that outclassed other monsters", with skin too thick for any but strong magic to pierce; Aiz beheads one with a single slash.[@viskum.so03-olivas, viskum.so03-grow] When the last one is left, it drinks the glowing red quartz dry and withers, sacrificing itself to flood the cavern with monsters.[@viskum.so03-sacrifice] See also [[#violas|Violas]] and [[corrupted-spirit|Corrupted spirit and demi-spirits]].

### Gargoyle {#gargoyle}

A **gargoyle** is a winged monster with a body of ash-coloured rock.[@gargoyle.fm09-gros] Its stone wings shield it from magic and from arrows, and serve as a blunt weapon; its claws are stone.[@gargoyle.fm09-shield, gargoyle.fm10-wings, gargoyle.fm11-plaza] In DanMachi 11 [[eina-tulle|Eina]] sees one prove "nearly impervious to long-range weapons", and suspects that it cannot be brought down without magic.[@gargoyle.fm11-plaza] It can hide among stone statues to ambush adventurers.[@gargoyle.fm11-statues]

Every gargoyle located in the checked text is [[gros|Gros]], one of the founding [[xenos|Xenos]] and leader of those who distrust surface people.[@gargoyle.fm09-gros, gargoyle.fm10-faction] Rei remarks that Gros is always like this.[@gargoyle.fm10-stone]

What the text shows of the species' strength comes from Gros's fights. In DanMachi 10 a gargoyle, a siren and a lizardman of the Xenos prove able "to go toe to toe with the subjugation team’s first-tier adventurers and come out on top", yet [[bete-loga|Bete]] "shattered the swiping stone claws with a kick".[@gargoyle.fm10-wings, gargoyle.fm10-bete] In DanMachi 11 Bell's knife chips "a fragment of stone" from those claws, which look like "giant stone spears", and Bell is forced into "an inferior position", perhaps because the gargoyle's potential is higher; in DanMachi 14 the "huge, nearly indestructible stone wings" overpower a flight of [[#voltemeria|voltemerias]].[@gargoyle.fm11-claws, gargoyle.fm14-wings] While the Xenos hide in the city in DanMachi 11, a scroll offering prize money that [[mord-latro|Mord]] carries shows "a fiendish lizardman and a gargoyle" among the armed monsters, and [[gareth|Gareth]] disposes of "the lizardman and the gargoyle, who were the strongest of the bunch" within seconds.[@gargoyle.fm11-scroll, gargoyle.fm11-gareth] The floors where gargoyles appear and their drop items were not located in the checked text.

### Unicorn {#unicorn}

**Unicorns** are monsters "treated more like divine beasts, with beautiful, snow-white coats and a single prized horn". They are among "the most elusive denizens of the Dungeon", and many upper-class adventurers never see one; DanMachi 9 ranks the vouivre "On par with the unicorn" in rarity.[@unicorn.ss02-beasts, unicorn.fm09-rare] Their floors were not located in the checked text. The horn "was said to possess the ability to neutralize any poison": it is a rare drop item, prized for recovery items and sold at an exorbitant price.[@unicorn.ss02-beasts, unicorn.fm14-hakugen]

- **Unicorn-horn items:** [[welf-crozzo|Welf]] makes Bell's longknife [[equipment#hakugen|Hakugen]] from unicorn horn (DanMachi 12), and in DanMachi 14 it draws the black poison from Bell's wound. [[fels|Fels]]'s hidden storeroom holds "a silvery-white cup made from a unicorn’s horn", and in Minor Myths and Legends 2 [[amid|Amid]] makes the Unicorn's Cup, which "can turn any poisoned or impotable fluid into clean, drinkable water".[@unicorn.fm12-hakugen, unicorn.fm14-hakugen, unicorn.fm11-cup, unicorn.ss02-cup]
- **On the surface:** in Minor Myths and Legends 2 unicorns that sought "a new life on the surface" have started a herd "deep in the sacred mountains". Amid of [[dian-cecht-familia|Dian Cecht Familia]] asks Aiz, Tiona, Tione and Lefiya to obtain a horn and, if possible, to let the unicorn live, since unicorns are drawn to chastity and a trapped one may kill itself; in the end [[riveria|Riveria]] asks the unicorn for its horn, and it gives it freely.[@unicorn.ss02-beasts, unicorn.ss02-noble, unicorn.ss02-riveria] In Sword Oratoria 14 Riveria leaves the high-elf village on "the only tamed unicorn in the entire high elf village".[@unicorn.so14-riveria]
- **Xenos:** a unicorn is among the [[xenos|Xenos]]; in DanMachi 11 it fights in the vanguard with [[lido|Lido]].[@unicorn.fm11-xenos]

### Ogre {#ogre}

The **ogre** ({{ja|鬼}}, written *demon*)[@ogre.so02-ja] is a horned monster that the text mostly uses as a comparison: [[udaeus|Udaeus]]'s skull bears "two horns similar to an ogre’s", and Bell thinks the enhanced [[#moss-huge|moss huge]], with "hornlike pieces of wood" on its head, "probably closer to an ogre".[@ogre.so02-udaeus, moss-huge.fm12-ogre] In DanMachi 7 [[haruhime|Haruhime]] tells Bell that monsters attacked the carriage taking her from home and that the prum aristocrat with her "ran away from the horde of ogres"; marauders rescued her and sold her to Orario.[@ogre.fm07-haruhime] The legendary samurai of the [[far-east|Far East]], Issen Douji, small of stature, slew a thousand ogres to protect one girl.[@ogre.fm07-issen] Otherwise the word marks a fearsome face: Eina's zeal to keep Bell alive "was turning her into an ogre" (DanMachi 2), an elf of the tavern dreads "the ogre-like expression Mia would make" (Familia Chronicle 1), and an elf's grin is "like a damn ogre’s" (Familia Chronicle 2).[@ogre.fm02-eina, ogre.fc01-mia, ogre.fc02-hedin] No ogre in the Dungeon was located in the checked text.

### Basilisk {#basilisk}

The **basilisk** is a giant serpent and "unmistakably a member of the dragon family", which breathes fire and spreads a paralyzing toxin "that seemed to petrify those afflicted by it". In ancient times basilisks destroyed countless cities and spread devastation across the world; a legend even holds that their ravages spread the present Kaios Desert (see [[kaios-desert|Kaios Desert]]).[@basilisk.fc02-tales] In Familia Chronicle 2 a tamed basilisk is "Resheph’s trump card" in the war of [[ali|Ali]]'s story. It appears behind Gorza's main force from the direction of the supply lines ("I-it can’t be…a basilisk?!"), a name that "every resident of the desert realm knew from bedtime stories", and tramples the soldiers in its path.[@basilisk.fc02-supply] The collar around one of its fangs, a fang "as large as a grown man", "might not have been a finished product": the basilisk kills its own tamer with its tail and turns its gaze on [[ottar|Ottar]], who splits it down the middle with one tremendous slash.[@basilisk.fc02-supply, basilisk.fc02-ottar]

### Arachne {#arachne}

The **arachne** is "the humanoid spider". The arachne of the [[xenos|Xenos]], [[xenos#ranieh|Ranieh]], has "the shapely upper body of a human female" but walks "with the many legs of a spider"; her red, humanlike eyes are unusual, because "arachnes typically had compound eyes".[@arachne.fm09-humanoid, arachne.fm10-ranieh] She ensnares enemies with spider webbing, and the acid of her poison leaves a trail that other Xenos can follow ("You reek of arachne acid!!").[@arachne.fm10-ranieh, arachne.fm10-acid] Ranieh leads the party of six Xenos, "an arachne, a harpy, a formoire, a hippogriff, a war shadow, and the vouivre herself", that sets out through the Dungeon in DanMachi 10; caught by [[ikelos-familia|Ikelos Familia]]'s hunters, she lays a "nearly invisible thread" that Dix spots, springs a last "unexpected trap" on three hunters and dies, "disintegrated into a large pile of ash".[@arachne.fm10-party, arachne.fm10-death] An arachne is among the Xenos who glare at Bell from a cliff beside Gros in DanMachi 9, and in Minor Myths and Legends 1 Lido worries about "whatever sort of baptism" an arachne has in mind for a newcomer.[@arachne.fm09-cliff, arachne.ss01-baptism] No arachne outside the Xenos was located in the checked text.

### Hippogriff {#hippogriff}

The only **hippogriff** located in the checked text is Cliff of the [[xenos|Xenos]], who "preferred to be airborne" and was "lighthearted and cheerful". In DanMachi 10 he travels with Ranieh's party, dives at Ikelos Familia's hunters "over and over from high above", and is brought down by a spell and killed with spears.[@hippogriff.fm10-cliff] Earlier, when giant insects attack the party, Ranieh calls "Fia, Cliff!", and the harpy leaps "with the mightily howling hippogriff" to clear the skies with "feathery projectiles and a razor-sharp beak"; after his death, "A griffin’s eyes smoldered with anger that the hippogriff had been murdered."[@hippogriff.fm10-skies]

### Griffin {#griffin}

**Griffins** appear in the checked text only among the [[xenos|Xenos]].
