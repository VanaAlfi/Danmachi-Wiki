**Takemikazuchi Familia** is the Familia of the god [[takemikazuchi|Takemikazuchi]], whose members come from the [[far-east|Far East]]. Its captain is [[ouka|Ouka]].[@fm05-mikoto, fm05-ouka] Its former member [[mikoto|Mikoto]] converts to [[hestia-familia|Hestia Familia]] in DanMachi 6.[@fm06-mikoto]

## Origins

In the Far East, Takemikazuchi and other gods led a poor shrine that took in children with no relatives. Ten years before DanMachi 15, [[haruhime|Haruhime]] visited it and befriended Mikoto, [[chigusa|Chigusa]] and Ouka.[@fm15-shrine]

## Asuka {#asuka}

**Asuka** ({{ja|飛鳥}})[@fm11-asuka-ja] is a member of Takemikazuchi Familia whom [[bell-cranell|Bell Cranell]] thanks for help in the [[war-game|War Game]], naming her at the gathering alongside [[chigusa|Hitachi Chigusa]] and [[ouka|Kashima Ouka]].[@fm11-evacuation, fm19-thanks] [[takemikazuchi|Takemikazuchi]] reports that she and Hitachi Chigusa reached Level 2 during the fighting involving [[ishtar-familia|Ishtar Familia]].[@fm08-level]
