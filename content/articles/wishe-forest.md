**Wishe Forest** is the elven village where [[lefiya|Lefiya Viridis]] was born and raised, "An elf of the Wishe Forest", as she calls herself.[@so01-lefiya, so13-open] It lies in the great river of trees that spreads across the centre of the Continent and, unusually for an elven forest, is a transit hub for merchants and travellers crossing the Continent.[@so13-open] It is named after Wishe, one of the three great bards whose names survive from the distant past.[@so13-bard]

{{nocite}} *The elven café in Orario called Wishe is a different place; see the end of this page.*

## An open forest

Among the many elven villages of the world, Wishe Forest is widely known outside. Most elves living in the forests protect their [[holy-tree|sacred trees]], follow their people's teachings and draw a distinct line between themselves and the outside world. Many people of other races, though, visit Wishe Forest and pass through it.[@so13-open] Its people seek out other races, dream of the world outside and eventually set out on journeys of their own. "Sociable, compatible with other races, and possessing magic powers that stood out even among fellow elves": that is the trademark of a Wishe Forest elf.[@so13-open] [[filvis|Filvis Challia]] tells Lefiya her homeland is "known for possessing high magic power even among our kin".[@so03-magic]

## The bard Wishe

The village's way of life comes from a work of Wishe's that is still one of its core teachings: "My fellow elves, leave the woods and gaze upon the world."[@so13-bard] Wishe was an uninhibited, rootless traveller who never stayed in one place long enough to build a village. Elves who looked up to the bard took back the forest where Wishe was born from the monsters that had taken it over, founded a village there and named it after the bard.[@so13-bard] Following that teaching, the villagers encourage their children to set out into the world.[@so13-school]

Lefiya's spell [[magic#elf-ring|Elf Ring]] opens with the words "I beseech the name of Wishe!"[@so01-chant]

## The Great Tree

The village's sacred tree, the Great Tree, is famed for its crown of light. In Sword Oratoria 11 Lefiya says the crown is unique to Wishe Forest and cannot be seen in any other village: the tree seems to wear a crown of flowers, with glittering specks of light all around it.[@so11-crown, so13-dream]

## Lefiya's childhood

As a child Lefiya begged travellers at the village tavern for stories and dreamed of "a sea of sand called the Kaios Desert" and of the Dungeon. She had a secret garden of little white flowers, reached through a tunnel of tilted trees.[@so13-dream] When she was eight, news came that the [[school-district|School District]] had stopped at the port town to the south; her parents let her go without argument, and asked only to hear what she saw and learned in the world, "just like Wishe did".[@so13-school] Back in the forest she had also heard countless stories about the Dungeon.[@so13-pit]

## The café Wishe

In Orario a café named Wishe, "an elf establishment", stands on a narrow side street in the south-west of the city. [[hedin|Hedin]] once took Bell there, Finn consulted Bell there, and in Sword Oratoria 13 Lefiya visits it alone.[@fm18-cafe, so13-cafe]
