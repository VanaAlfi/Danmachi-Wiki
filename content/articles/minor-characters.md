## Loki Familia's minor members {#loki-familias-minor-members}

Eight people are named with [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]]: Cynthia, Lloyd, Sharon, Anju, Kalos, Liza, Carmillia and Olba.[@so07-cynthia, so07-lloyd, so12-sharon, so07-anju, so07-kalos, so07-liza, so12-carmillia, so12-olba, fm11-olba, ar01-olba] The text calls Sharon a second-tier adventurer of Loki Familia and Olba a Level 3 member of it, while Lloyd, Anju, Kalos and Liza are named only as dead.[@so12-sharon, so12-olba, so07-lloyd, so07-anju, so07-kalos, so07-liza, so08-lloyd, so11-lloyd] Cynthia, Sharon, Carmillia and Olba appear in Sword Oratoria 7, 11, 12 and 13, and Olba also in DanMachi 11 and Astrea Record 1.[@so07-cynthia, so11-cynthia, so12-carmillia, so13-sharon, so07-olba, fm11-olba, ar01-olba]

### Cynthia {#cynthia}

In Sword Oratoria 7 [[hyrute-sisters|Tiona Hyrute]] tells Cynthia ({{ja|シンシア}})[@so07-loki-names-ja] and [[elfie-collette|Elfie Collette]] to get out of the way as she cuts a [[monsters#poison-vermis|poison vermis]] off [[loki-familia#arcus|Arcus]]'s arm, and the two run to his side.[@so07-cynthia] In Sword Oratoria 11 she is a mage and supporter: when Tione Hyrute shouts "Cynthia! Halberd!", she pulls a Durandal halberd from her backpack for Tione.[@so11-cynthia] In Sword Oratoria 12 [[finn-deimne|Finn Deimne]] calls her to him before he leaves the first squad to [[shakti-varma|Shakti Varma]] and [[allen-fromel|Allen Fromel]] and sets off with a small force for [[raul-nord|Raul Nord]] on the eleventh floor.[@so12-cynthia]

### Lloyd {#lloyd}

Lloyd ({{ja|ロイド}})[@so07-loki-names-ja] is one of the seven dead, counting the missing, that [[riveria|Riveria Ljos Alf]] names when Finn asks about casualties in Sword Oratoria 7, with Crea, Anju, Liza, Kalos, Remilia and [[leene-arshe|Leene Arshe]].[@so07-lloyd] In the prologue of Sword Oratoria 8, set in [[knossos|Knossos]], Lloyd's name is cried out when members of Loki Familia find their companions dead in their blood.[@so08-lloyd] In Sword Oratoria 11 [[cruz-bussell|Cruz Bussell]] wants revenge on those who killed Leene, Lloyd and the others.[@so11-lloyd]

### Sharon {#sharon}

Sharon ({{ja|シャロン}})[@so12-loki-names-ja] is a second-tier adventurer of Loki Familia who, in Sword Oratoria 12, can hardly believe how quickly the spirit altar has been silenced.[@so12-sharon] In Sword Oratoria 13 Finn and [[gareth|Gareth Landrock]] report that Sharon, Olba and Arcus have reached Level 4; everyone who fought the [[corrupted-spirit|demi-spirit]] directly leveled up.[@so13-sharon]

### Anju {#anju}

Anju ({{ja|アンジュ}})[@so07-loki-names-ja] is one of the seven dead, counting the missing, that Riveria lists in Sword Oratoria 7.[@so07-anju]

### Kalos {#kalos}

Kalos ({{ja|カロス}})[@so07-loki-names-ja] is one of the seven dead, counting the missing, that Riveria lists in Sword Oratoria 7.[@so07-kalos]

### Liza {#liza}

Liza ({{ja|リザ}})[@so07-loki-names-ja] is one of the seven dead, counting the missing, that Riveria lists in Sword Oratoria 7.[@so07-liza]

### Carmillia {#carmillia}

In Sword Oratoria 12, after [[bell-cranell|Bell Cranell]] asks [[narfi|Narfi]] for a bigger sword and eyes a girl of the rescue squad with large weapons on her backpack, Carmillia ({{ja|カルミリア}})[@so12-loki-names-ja] is told to give him one.[@so12-carmillia]

### Olba {#olba}

Olba ({{ja|オルバ}})[@so07-loki-names-ja] is an animal person of Loki Familia; in Astrea Record 1 he brings Finn's instructions to [[hedin|Hedin Selrand]] of [[freya-familia|Freya Familia]] and watches in disbelief as Hedin's lightning strikes the soldiers of [[evils|the Evils]].[@ar01-olba] In Sword Oratoria 7 he and Cruz bring light with their magic-stone lanterns when Tione calls for it, revealing black-robed figures, and in DanMachi 11 [[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz Wallenstein]], keeping watch on Bell, recalls that Olba and other low-level members told her he was alone for now.[@so07-olba, fm11-olba] In Sword Oratoria 12 he is a Level 3 member who holds up [[amid|Amid Teasanare]] while she heals herself, and in Sword Oratoria 13 he is among those reported to have reached Level 4.[@so12-olba, so13-olba]

## Astrea Familia's minor members {#astrea-familias-minor-members}

Iska, Maryu, Neze, Noin, Asta and Lyana were members of [[astrea-familia|Astrea Familia]] with [[lyu-leon|Lyu Leon]], seen in Astrea Record 1 to 3 and massacred by the [[juggernaut|Juggernaut]] in DanMachi 14.[@fc03-iska, fc03-maryu, fc03-neze, fc03-noin, fc03-asta, fc03-lyana, ar01-noin-prologue, fm14-iska] In Astrea Record 1 Lyu's mind goes back seven years to a battle with evil, and DanMachi 14 goes back to the day [[rudra-familia|Rudra Familia]] lured them into a trap, where the Juggernaut massacred the party.[@ar01-noin-prologue, fm14-noin, fm14-iska]

### Iska {#iska}

Iska Bra ({{ja|イスカ・ブラ}})[@fc03-astrea-names-ja] is the Amazon brawler of Astrea Familia, an eccentric and the most fashionable of them.[@fc03-iska] In Astrea Record 1 she declares that they will beat the Black Dragon some day, and in Astrea Record 2 she tends the wounded [[lyra|Lyra]] and [[gojouno-kaguya|Gojouno Kaguya]] with every potion at hand.[@ar01-iska, ar02-iska] In DanMachi 14, as the Juggernaut massacres the party, [[alize-lovell|Alize Lovell]] cries out her name and Maryu's, her voice heavy with tears.[@fm14-iska]

### Maryu {#maryu}

Maryu Réage ({{ja|マリュー・レアージュ}}; printed *Marieux* in Astrea Record 2)[@fc03-astrea-names-ja] is the human healer of Astrea Familia, one of its older members, who embodied tolerance and motherliness.[@fc03-maryu] In Astrea Record 3, when even she can raise only a feeble glow of healing, she tells Alize that as a healer she cannot let them fight any longer; later she treats the party's wounds behind the greatshields of [[gareth|Gareth Landrock]] and Asta.[@ar03-maryu] In DanMachi 18 Lyu uses an [[magic#rea-vindemia|area healing]] she has inherited, the specialty of Maryu, Astrea Familia's one and only healer.[@fm18-maryu] In DanMachi 14 Alize cries out her name and Iska's as the Juggernaut massacres the party.[@fm14-maryu]

### Neze {#neze}

Neze Rankett ({{ja|ネーゼ・ランケット}})[@fc03-astrea-names-ja] is the werewolf middle guard of Astrea Familia and one of its older members.[@fc03-neze] In the epilogue of Astrea Record 1 she tries to stem a wounded young man's bleeding in a field hospital and breaks down in tears.[@ar01-neze] In Astrea Record 3, fighting from the middle rank, she blocks one of [[alfia|Alfia]]'s attacks with her throwing daggers.[@ar03-neze] In DanMachi 14 she is the second to die: as she calls the dead Noin's name, the same purplish-blue claws strike her.[@fm14-neze]

### Noin {#noin}

Noin Unic ({{ja|ノイン・ユニック}})[@fc03-astrea-names-ja] is the human attacker of Astrea Familia, always deft and tactful; in Astrea Record 1 she is sixteen, older than Lyu, with short dark brown hair.[@fc03-noin, ar01-noin] In Astrea Record 3 she passes Gareth Landrock a new shield as Alfia wears through their equipment.[@ar03-noin] In DanMachi 14 she is the first of them killed when a monster breaks out of a Dungeon wall and cuts her in three.[@fm14-noin]

### Asta {#asta}

Asta Nox ({{ja|アスタ・ノックス}})[@fc03-astrea-names-ja] is the dwarf defender of Astrea Familia, brave, who protected everyone with her small frame.[@fc03-asta] In Astrea Record 3 her greatshield and Gareth Landrock's guard the back ranks in the fight with [[delphyne|Delphyne]].[@ar03-asta] In DanMachi 14 she thrusts out her shield but is crushed when the monster leaps on her.[@fm14-asta]

### Lyana {#lyana}

Lyana Lietz ({{ja|リャーナ・リーツ}})[@fc03-astrea-names-ja] is the human wandering mage of Astrea Familia, one of its older members, who came from the magical land of [[altena|Altena]].[@fc03-lyana] In Astrea Record 1, in a clearing of the eighteenth floor, she asks to be buried there if she dies.[@ar01-lyana] In Astrea Record 3 her detection magic finds the magic stone of Delphyne.[@ar03-lyana] In DanMachi 14 the spells she and [[celty-srowa|Celty Srowa]] fire at the Juggernaut are hurled back by its magic-repelling shell, and the two burst into flame.[@fm14-lyana]

## Other minor people of Sword Oratoria {#other-minor-people-of-sword-oratoria}

Seldia and Rishena of the elves' tales, the Amazon empress Ivelda, Elnea and Belnas of [[kali-familia|Kali Familia]], Bernadette and Marta under Amid, and Bete's family are named in Sword Oratoria.[@so05-seldia, so05-rishena, so05-ivelda, so06-elnea, so06-belnas, so11-bernadette, so11-marta, so08-luna, so08-bete-father, so08-bete-mother] They appear in Sword Oratoria 5, 6, 8, 11 and 14, and Seldia and Ivelda also among the statues of heroes in DanMachi 16; no names for Bete's father and mother were located in the checked text.[@so05-seldia, so06-elnea, so08-luna, so11-bernadette, so14-seldia, fm16-seldia, fm16-ivelda, so08-bete-father, so08-bete-mother]

### Seldia {#seldia}

Seldia ({{ja|セルディア}}; printed *Celdia* in Sword Oratoria 5, once *Salida* in Sword Oratoria 14)[@so05-oratoria-names-ja] is the high-elf saint of the elves' tales: in Sword Oratoria 5 [[bell-cranell|Bell Cranell]] names her among the women of [[albert|Albert]]'s party, and [[alicia-forestlight|Alicia Forestlight]] calls her an eternal saint, the pride of the elves, who left her home and saved the world from its crisis.[@so05-seldia] Sword Oratoria 14 calls her the eternal saint of [[dungeon-oratoria|Dungeon Oratoria]], said to be the first high elf to leave the forest, and in DanMachi 16 her statue stands among those of the greatest heroes on a bridge in Orario.[@so14-seldia, fm16-seldia]

### Rishena {#rishena}

Rishena ({{ja|リシェーナ}}; printed *Lishen* in Sword Oratoria 14)[@so05-oratoria-names-ja] is Seldia's younger sister: in Sword Oratoria 5 [[lefiya|Lefiya Viridis]] says that all the elves' nobles, [[riveria|Riveria Ljos Alf]] among them, descend from her.[@so05-rishena] In Sword Oratoria 14 she is the ancient queen of the elves who stayed in the royal forest.[@so14-rishena]

### Elnea {#elnea}

Elnea ({{ja|エルネア}})[@so05-oratoria-names-ja], a Level 5 Amazon, was one of the leading candidates for captain of Kali Familia; in Sword Oratoria 6 Bache tells [[hyrute-sisters|Tiona Hyrute]] that she killed Elnea, the trial that took her to Level 6.[@so06-elnea]

### Belnas {#belnas}

Belnas ({{ja|ベルナス}})[@so05-oratoria-names-ja], also a Level 5 Amazon and a leading candidate for captain, was killed by Argana, which took Argana to Level 6.[@so06-belnas]

### Bernadette {#bernadette}

In Sword Oratoria 11, as [[amid|Amid Teasanare]] sets out to break the chain of the curse, she tells Bernadette ({{ja|ベルナデット}})[@so05-oratoria-names-ja] and Marta to take over the recovery in her place and support the battle lines.[@so11-bernadette]

### Luna {#luna}

Luna ({{ja|ルーナ}})[@so05-oratoria-names-ja] is the younger sister of [[bete-loga|Bete Loga]]; in Sword Oratoria 8 he remembers that she was crushed to death when a colossal monster fell on his tribe.[@so08-luna]

### Bete's father {#betes-father}

Bete's father kept to the teachings of his ancestors and refused the envoys who sought the help of his tribe, the Beastmen of the Plains, and Bete asked him to be treated as one of the tribe's warriors.[@so08-bete-father] In Sword Oratoria 8 he and Bete's mother are torn to shreds by the colossal monster, and Bete at last understands what his father had always taught him: the weak can lose their happiness in an instant.[@so08-bete-father]

### Bete's mother {#betes-mother}

In Sword Oratoria 8 Bete's mother is torn to shreds with his father by the same monster.[@so08-bete-mother]

### Renee {#renee}

Renee ({{ja|レーネ}})[@so08-renee-ja] is the girl born on the same day as Bete Loga, his childhood friend, who was killed with his family when his tribe on the plains was destroyed; he had trained day in and day out until he was the strongest of the tribe's children and "could claim her for his own", and over her remains he cries "Renee…!"[@so08-plains, so08-renee]

### Ivelda {#ivelda}

Ivelda ({{ja|イヴェルダ}})[@so05-oratoria-names-ja] is an Amazon empress: in Sword Oratoria 5 Bell names her and Seldia as the women of Albert's party, and in DanMachi 16 her statue stands on the same bridge of heroes.[@so05-ivelda, fm16-ivelda]

### Marta {#marta}

Marta ({{ja|マルタ}})[@so05-oratoria-names-ja] is named with Bernadette when Amid leaves the two in charge of the recovery in Sword Oratoria 11.[@so11-marta]

## Freya Familia's minor members and the slave market {#freya-familias-minor-members-and-the-slave-market}

Remilia and Rask of [[freya-familia|Freya Familia]], [[heith-velvet|Heith Velvet]]'s healers Rona and Ilde {{small|printed *Ilda*}}, and Haara, Yona and Rozzo of a slave market in Familia Chronicle 2 are named in the volumes.[@fm18-remilia, fm18-rask, fm18-rona, fm18-ilda, fc02-haara, fc02-yona, fc02-rozzo] Remilia and Rask appear in DanMachi 17 and 18, Rona and Ilde in the war of DanMachi 18, and Haara, Yona and Rozzo in the slave market where [[freya|Freya]] finds [[ali|Ali]].[@fm17-remilia, fm18-remilia, fm18-rona, fc02-haara, fc02-yona, fc02-rozzo]

### Remilia {#remilia}

In DanMachi 17 [[bell-cranell|Bell Cranell]] names Remilia ({{ja|レミリア}})[@fm18-freya-names-ja] and Rask as the ones who, with Van, often spoke with him.[@fm17-remilia] In DanMachi 18, as guards chosen for the house of the gods, she and Rask carry Freya's order to [[allen-fromel|Allen Fromel]] at [[hedin|Hedin Selrand]]'s bidding, telling him they heard it from Freya themselves.[@fm18-remilia] Sword Oratoria 7 names a Remilia among the seven dead, counting the missing, that [[riveria|Riveria Ljos Alf]] lists for Loki Familia.[@so07-remilia]

### Rask {#rask}

Rask ({{ja|ラスク}})[@fm18-freya-names-ja] is named with Remilia in DanMachi 17 and is the other guard of the house of the gods whom Hedin sends with the order in DanMachi 18.[@fm17-rask, fm18-rask]

### Rona {#rona}

Rona ({{ja|ロナ}})[@fm18-freya-names-ja] is a healer and the deputy of Heith Velvet: before the war of DanMachi 18 Heith asks Rona and Ilde to support her, and while healing [[hegni|Hegni]] she sends Rona and the others to tend the Einherjar.[@fm18-rona, fm18-rona-deputy]

### Ilde {#ilde}

Ilde ({{ja|イルデ}}; printed *Ilda*)[@fm18-freya-names-ja] is the second healer Heith asks to support her once the battle begins.[@fm18-ilda]

### Haara {#haara}

Haara ({{ja|ハーラ}})[@fm18-freya-names-ja] is the younger girl who, with the boy Yona, thanks Freya for freeing the slaves in Familia Chronicle 2; Freya later names the two first among her children killed by the troops of Warsa.[@fc02-haara]

### Rozzo {#rozzo}

Rozzo ({{ja|ロッゾ}})[@fm18-freya-names-ja] is the manager of the slave market, who has no choice but to obey Freya.[@fc02-rozzo]

### Nahzo {#nahzo}

In Familia Chronicle 2, at a bar where wealthy merchants gamble, Freya challenges Nahzo ({{ja|ナァーゾ}})[@fc02-nahzo-ja], "one of the big four in this town", to a board game for "all the money that you currently have on you".[@fc02-nahzo]

### Yona {#yona}

Yona ({{ja|ヨナ}})[@fm18-freya-names-ja] is the nervous boy who thanks Freya with Haara, and his name heads her list of her children killed by Warsa's troops.[@fc02-yona]

## Dald, Lulu, Selenia and Orde {#dald-lulu-selenia-and-orde}

Dald, a dwarf who hones adventurers' weapons, the Amazon stall owner Lulu, Selenia of Vidar Familia and the war shadow Orde of the [[xenos|Xenos]] are named in the volumes.[@ss01-dald, ss02-lulu, ar01-selenia, fm10-orde] Dald appears in Minor Myths and Legends 1, Lulu in Minor Myths and Legends 2, Selenia in Astrea Record 1 and 3, and Orde in DanMachi 10.[@ss01-dald, ss02-lulu, ar01-selenia, ar03-selenia, fm10-orde]

### Dald {#dald}

Dald ({{ja|ダルド}})[@ss01-other-names-ja] is the dwarf proprietor of the Baby Bird's Anvil, a shop on the backstreets off the main road, who makes a living honing adventurers' weapons; [[eina-tulle|Eina Tulle]] introduced [[bell-cranell|Bell Cranell]] to him when Bell was starting out.[@ss01-dald] In Minor Myths and Legends 1 he sharpens the knife [[welf-crozzo|Welf Crozzo]] made for Bell and hangs Bell's old knife in a frame on a wall of weapons whose owners, he says, are mostly dead, with perhaps not even ten left alive.[@ss01-dald]

### Lulu {#lulu}

Lulu ({{ja|ルル}})[@ss01-other-names-ja] is an Amazon stall owner with babyish looks and a sugary voice who, in Minor Myths and Legends 2, haggles with [[hyrute-sisters|Tiona Hyrute]] and a boy, and whose eyes change when the boy offers to add a broadsword and a shield, spare gear of [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]] that he had been carrying for Tiona.[@ss02-lulu] When Tiona counters with three hundred thousand, Lulu agrees to pay on condition that they buy some of her goods and tries to sway the boy with her body, but he refuses, and Tiona runs off with him and the coins.[@ss02-lulu]

### Selenia {#selenia}

Selenia ({{ja|セレニア}})[@ss01-other-names-ja] is a member of Vidar Familia: in Astrea Record 1 she is told to take a prum girl to Central Park, and she leads another group of rescued townsfolk through the barricades.[@ar01-selenia] In Astrea Record 3 [[bete-loga|Bete Loga]] fights on, ignoring her tears.[@ar03-selenia]

### Orde {#orde}

Orde ({{ja|オード}})[@ss01-other-names-ja] is a [[monsters#war-shadow|war shadow]] among the Xenos in DanMachi 10, unable to make any sound, who was always the first into combat and scouts ahead of the group in full-plate armor.[@fm10-orde] When he rushes at [[ikelos-familia|Dix]], a big human man cuts him in half with a greatsword from behind Dix's shadow, Dix crushes his head, and he is left a pile of ash.[@fm10-orde]

## Persephone, Damia and Víðarr {#persephone-damia-and-vidarr}

Persephone, a follower of [[demeter|Demeter]] rather than a goddess, the harvest goddess Damia, and Víðarr, the god whose Familia [[bete-loga|Bete]] joined, are named in the volumes.[@so12-persephone, fm16-damia, so08-vidarr] Persephone appears in Familia Chronicle 1, Minor Myths and Legends 1 and Sword Oratoria 12, Damia in DanMachi 16, and Víðarr in Sword Oratoria 8.[@fc01-persephone, ss01-persephone, so12-persephone, fm16-damia, so08-vidarr]

### Persephone {#persephone}

Persephone ({{ja|ペルセフォネ}})[@so12-gods-names-ja] is a girl among the followers of Demeter: in Sword Oratoria 12 Demeter says that a god she had looked into kidnapped Persephone and her other followers, and [[elfie-collette|Elfie Collette]] later hugs Persephone, who had been trapped in the darkness all that time.[@so12-persephone, so12-persephone-rescue]

### Damia {#damia}

Damia ({{ja|ダミアー}})[@so12-gods-names-ja] is one of the goddesses of the harvest living in Orario, with [[hathor|Hathor]], [[freya|Freya]] and Demeter, who are the living symbols of the festival in DanMachi 16.[@fm16-damia]

### Víðarr {#vidarr}

Víðarr ({{ja|ヴィーザル}}; printed *Vidar* in Astrea Record 1)[@so12-gods-names-ja] is a god of few words, more of a hermit, with long auburn hair and eyes of the same colour; Bete Loga joined his Dungeon-type Familia.[@so08-vidarr] In Sword Oratoria 8 Víðarr apologizes to Bete; he and his broken Familia then leave Orario, and Bete fights on with Víðarr's half-withdrawn blessing, which lets him convert.[@so08-vidarr-leave] [[loki|Loki]] later tells Bete that Víðarr told her a little about him; the two gods are from the same place in Heaven, but she finds him unapproachable.[@so08-vidarr-loki]
