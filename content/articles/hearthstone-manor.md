**Hearthstone Manor** is the home of [[hestia-familia|Hestia Familia]] from DanMachi 6: the three-storey manor and gardens that belonged to [[apollo|Apollo]], which pass to Hestia after she wins the [[war-game|War Game]].[@fm06-manor] It is called Hearthstone Manor from DanMachi 8.[@fm08-hearthstone] Before it, the Familia lived in a basement room under an abandoned church.[@fm15-church]
