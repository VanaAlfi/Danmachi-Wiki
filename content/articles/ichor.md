**Ichor** is the divine blood used as a medium for [[falna|Falna]].[@so01-medium] Deities inscribe and update the [[status|Status]] with it, and the shared blood also connects a deity with followers.[@so01-medium, so12-sensing]

## Uses by volume {#uses-by-volume}

| Volume | Events |
|---|---|
| DanMachi 6 | [[soma|Soma]] and [[hestia|Hestia]] apply their ichor during [[lilliluka-erde|Lilliluka Erde]]'s [[falna#conversion|Conversion]].[@fm06-conversion] |
| DanMachi 7 | [[mikoto|Yamato Mikoto]] detects companions sharing her ichor, including the [[hestia-knife|Hestia Knife]].[@fm07-recognition] |
| DanMachi 8 | The first ichor remains after Conversion, allowing the original deity to continue sensing the follower.[@fm08-persistence] |
| DanMachi 15 | Gatekeepers use an ichor-responsive device to check for Falna, while [[phobos|Phobos]] uses ichor to inscribe [[welf-crozzo|Welf Crozzo]]'s Status.[@fm15-detection, fm15-grant] |
| DanMachi 17 | Ichor connects [[horn|Hörn]]'s magic to [[freya|Freya]]'s divinity, and Freya releases her followers from her charm through her own ichor that they carry.[@fm17-heln, fm17-charm] |
| DanMachi 17 | [[status-thief#status-snitch|Status Snitch]] combines several deities' ichor, allowing another deity to update ability values after [[status-thief|Status Thief]] unlocks the Status.[@fm17-snitch] |
| DanMachi 17 | Freya uses it and her own ichor on [[bell-cranell|Bell Cranell]].[@fm17-snitch] |
| DanMachi 17 | Hestia uses ichor-treated hearths as a medium to recreate her heavenly temple.[@fm17-hearths] |
| Sword Oratoria 1–2 | [[loki|Loki]] uses ichor to update [[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz Wallenstein]]'s Status, while Status Thief is manufactured from divine ichor.[@so01-medium, so02-thief] |
| Sword Oratoria 12 | A [[patron-deity|patron goddess]] can sense her followers through their shared ichor.[@so12-sensing] |
| Familia Chronicle 3 | [[lyu-leon|Lyu Leon]]'s skill strengthens followers sharing her ichor, and [[astrea|Astrea]]'s blood preserves her connection to her companions' magic even after Conversion.[@fc03-skill, fc03-inheritance] |
