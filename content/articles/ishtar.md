**Ishtar** is a goddess who ruled [[orario|Orario]]'s [[pleasure-quarter|Pleasure Quarter]] through [[ishtar-familia|Ishtar Familia]].[@fm07-ishtar, fm07-quarter] Jealous of [[freya|Freya]], she plans to strike at her, and in DanMachi 7 she abducts [[bell-cranell|Bell Cranell]]. [[freya-familia|Freya Familia]] then overwhelms her Familia and she is sent back to the heavens.[@fm07-ishtar, so07-hermes] She has tan skin and long braided black hair, and wears a golden crown and jewellery over very little cloth; anyone who glimpses her risks being Charmed.[@fm07-looks] A goddess of bounty as well as of love, she had one of the altars of the Goddess Festival, and it was her custom to declare that she was bored and abandon it.[@fm16-bounty]

## Plans against Freya

Ishtar plans to use [[haruhime|Haruhime]]'s Level Boost, sealed with Haruhime's soul in a [[killing-stone|Killing Stone]], and spread it through the stone's shards to her followers for a war against Freya.[@fm07-ishtar] Her hatred dates from the moment the two Goddesses of Beauty first met; Freya, who reached Orario's summit while Ishtar rose only to queen of its red-light districts, laughs off her provocations.[@fm07-rivalry] With letters, envoys and gifts of Superior-grade armaments she wins over [[kali-familia|Kali Familia]] of [[telskyura|Telskyura]], and in Sword Oratoria 6 she meets [[kali|Kali]] at Meren to plan a pincer attack: Ishtar Familia would strike Freya's home first, and Kali's warriors would sneak in from behind.[@so06-kali-pact] Sword Oratoria 7 shows that she also financed the [[evils|Evils]] in [[knossos|Knossos]] for five years, in exchange for the monster [[corrupted-spirit#gugalanna|Gugalanna]], the Bull of Heaven, to use against Freya.[@so07-ishtar] In the same volume she comes into Knossos to see the Bull's power before her revenge, makes [[thanatos|Thanatos]] set it on [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]], which is inside the labyrinth, and watches the fight.[@so07-bull-test] She meant to lure [[ottar|Ottar]] and the rest of Freya Familia into Knossos and strike them down with the demi-spirits.[@so09-lure] Ishtar Familia acted as a go-between for the Evils.[@so06-contact] About five years before DanMachi 7 the Guild, acting on other Familias' charges, forced Ishtar to reveal her followers' Statuses; the exposure gave her leverage over the Guild, which also had to penalize her accusers, and Ishtar Familia then wiped them out and their goddesses were sent back to the heavens.[@fm07-guild]

## Bell and her fall

Bell's Status first catches Ishtar's eye for its Luck, and then for a particular Skill she reads on it.[@fm07-skill] She cannot charm him, because his Skill protects him from her charm.[@fm07-ishtar, fm07-charm] [[hermes|Hermes]] had given Ishtar information about Bell, and Sword Oratoria 7 confirms that he deliberately set Ishtar and Freya against each other, judging Ishtar a danger to Orario.[@fm07-hermes, so07-hermes] Hermes himself had delivered the Killing Stone to her.[@fm07-delivery]

After she abducts Bell, Freya Familia overwhelms Ishtar Familia and Freya sends Ishtar back to the heavens. The Pleasure Quarter is left badly damaged, though no one is killed, and the [[guild|Guild]] fines Freya.[@fm07-quarter] In the attack Freya walks alone into Ishtar's palace and Charms her guards and then Tammuz; when Ishtar charges at her, Freya pushes her off the top of the palace, and the fatal fall sets off her Arcanum, so that a pillar of light takes her back to the heavens.[@fm07-fall] Freya takes in Ishtar's follower [[ishtar-familia#tammuz-berrilli|Tammuz]] and keeps his [[knossos#daedalus-orb|Daedalus Orb]].[@so09-tammuz, so11-orb]
