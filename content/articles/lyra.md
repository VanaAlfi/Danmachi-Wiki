**Lyra** is a prum member of [[astrea-familia|Astrea Familia]] and its strategist.[@ar02-pragmatic, fc03-list] The narration calls her "quite the cynic" and a "master liar", and she fights from the back of the party with bladed boomerangs and bombs she makes herself.[@fm14-teach, fm14-lessons, ar01-vito, fm14-death] In the [[great-conflict|Great Conflict]] she works out how [[alfia|Alfia]]'s magic-cancelling enchantment works, and blocks Alfia's final spell with a shield made by [[asfi|Perseus]].[@ar03-passive, ar03-genos] Five years before DanMachi 13, blinded by the [[juggernaut|Juggernaut]], she gives her life with [[alize-lovell|Alize]] and [[gojouno-kaguya|Kaguya]] so that [[lyu-leon|Lyu]] can live, and is the first of the three to die.[@fm13-ten, fm14-lives, fm14-death]

## Name {#name}

The covered volumes print her name only as *Lyra*, and no surname for her was located in the checked text. When Lyu tells the younger members of Astrea's new Familia about her old companions in Familia Chronicle 3, she gives the other nine their full names but calls her simply "the prum strategist, Lyra".[@fc03-list]

*Slyle* is her title, written with characters meaning *cunning rat*, and three characters call her by it.[@ar02-slyle-ja] [[riveria|Riveria]] credits "Slyle" with Astrea Familia's quick seizure of the city walls, saying that such quick thinking reminds her of "our own prum"; Asfi, after seventy-seven hours of work for Astrea Familia, says that if she dies it is "Slyle's fault"; and [[finn-deimne|Finn]] answers one of Lyra's objections with "You're right, Slyle".[@ar02-slyle, ar03-asfi, ar03-slyle]

The accessory [[silence-lyra|Silence Lyra]] is a different thing, and the checked text does not connect its name with her: Astrea Record 3 calls it a *Silence Lyre*, describes the earpieces as shaped like a miniature harp, and says Finn devised them and Asfi made them.[@ar03-lyre]

## Appearance and character

Lyra is a prum, less than 120 celches tall and physically weaker than Lyu; as a prum she is "the height of other races' children".[@fm14-teach, fm14-lessons, ar02-escape] Her "tender, innocent eyes" do not match her words, and she is two years older than Lyu.[@fm14-teach] DanMachi 14 says her short hair is dyed peach, which she thinks "super-cool", and Familia Chronicle 3 also calls her peach-haired; Astrea Record 2 calls her pink-haired.[@fm14-teach, fc03-traps, ar02-pink]

Lyu remembers her as "the dry-witted prum" and as "sly, coarse Lyra".[@ar01-names, fc03-schau] She speaks bluntly: when [[shakti-varma|Shakti]]'s [[ganesha-familia|Ganesha Familia]] arrives after a fight is over, she asks, "You call yourself the city watch?" The narration calls her scornful remark a warning and a call to caution.[@ar01-watch] Kaguya calls her the most "ill-natured prum" she has ever known.[@ar02-heroic]

Her grumbling has a purpose. Whenever trouble arises she always has something to say and never lets a silence linger, and the others, though they would never admit it, are grateful for the wisecracks that make them smile.[@ar02-chatter] Astrea Record 3 calls her "the coolheaded brain of the party", whose mantra is to plan for the worst and hope for the best.[@ar03-slyle]

### Finn Deimne

Lyra idolises [[finn-deimne|Finn Deimne]], "the hero of our people", and talks of marrying him; Kaguya retorts that he would never go for "a sleazy, cunning little brat".[@ar01-hero] In Lyu's memory in DanMachi 14 she still boasts that Finn will soon propose, though Lyu recalls that he had been avoiding her, and the narration says she dreamed of marrying into wealth.[@fm14-level] When Finn rallies the city in Astrea Record 2, she calls him "a swindler and a cheat" whose lies become the truth because he keeps repeating them, then adds, "His words are our courage." The narration says her eyes were "those of a girl in love".[@ar02-swindler]

## In Astrea Familia

Lyra was one of the Familia's first members. In Familia Chronicle 3 Alize recalls that she herself was there first, "then Kaguya joined, and then Lyra tagged along".[@fc03-drinks] Astrea Record 2 says Lyra and Kaguya had been with Alize "since the very beginning" and that Astrea had saved them both; Astrea counts "the day Lyra took my hand" among the happiest days of her life.[@ar02-search, ar02-pragmatic, ar02-astrea-hand]

She is the Familia's strategist. Astrea Record 2 calls Kaguya and Lyra well suited to supporting Alize "as deputy captain and prum strategist", and Familia Chronicle 3 names Alize the captain, Kaguya the second-in-command and Lyra "the strategist".[@ar02-pragmatic, fc03-drinks] Lyu calls her "more devious and cunning than anyone".[@fc03-list]

### How she fights

- **Boomerangs.** Her bladed boomerang returns to her hand after cutting down the enemy.[@ar01-factory] She usually fights from the rear or the centre of the formation, throwing boomerangs, bombs and other projectiles.[@ar01-vito, ar01-trap, ar03-shield, ar03-center] Beside Kaguya, she watches Kaguya's back, and Kaguya's blade makes up for her own weak attacks.[@ar02-syr]
- **Bombs and crafted items.** She makes her own bombs and is careful not to waste her crafted items. Weaker than the other girls, she schemes and prepares a fallback plan for every situation.[@ar02-bombs, fm14-death] In DanMachi 14's memory her nimble fingers are repairing "a collection of strange instruments".[@fm14-teach]
- **Tricks.** She tells Alfia that because she is small and weak she has to play dead, make bombs and sometimes act as bait, so as not to drag the others down.[@ar02-alfia] In Familia Chronicle 3, when three young members of Astrea's new Familia ambush Lyu, she thinks that the prum she knew would have gathered more information and laid far more traps.[@fc03-traps, fc03-schau]
- **A nose for traps.** She is quick to smell a trap, and in the Dungeon she "sniffed out" the trap that nearly buried the Familia.[@ar01-trap, fm14-sniffed]

She is frank about her weakness. Facing death she calls herself "the weakest of us all", and she once told Lyu: "if I don't use my head, I'll die. I'm a prum, after all."[@fm14-lives, fm14-lessons]

### Lyu's teacher

When something troubled her, Lyu went first to Alize and then to Lyra, whose opinions were "honest and unreserved".[@fm14-teach] Lyra taught her how to see through lies, but also how to negotiate with blackmail, how to intimidate, and how to win at gambling and cheat. Her reasoning was that to beat villains you have to understand how they think.[@fm14-lessons]

Her favourite saying was "Remember it all and use it all." She told Lyu that "Knowledge is a weapon. Information is a meal ticket", that she should improvise what she lacked and study human nature, that she should take everything, Lyra's own advice included, "with a grain of salt", and that she must turn knowledge into wisdom.[@fm14-lessons]

Familia Chronicle 3's short story set two years after the Great Conflict shows Lyra drinking with Alize and Kaguya and talking about Lyu.[@fc03-drinks] Her lesson to Lyu is called "the middle way": observing reality carefully while also building her own. Lyra herself had turned knowledge into wisdom through years of fighting as a weak prum, and Alize says Kaguya and Lyra are both preparing Lyu for hard times to come.[@fc03-wisdom] Lyra and [[minor-characters#neze|Neze]] teasingly called Lyu "the baby of the familia".[@fc03-baby]

{{inference}} Familia Chronicle 1 says that a prum colleague in Astrea Familia "with a wide grin" taught Lyu to gamble successfully. It does not name her, but DanMachi 14 says Lyra taught Lyu to always win at gambling.[@fc01-gamble, fm14-lessons]

{{inference}} When Alize first brings Lyu home in DanMachi 15, one member asks whether she has brought in "another one of your strays"; Alize's reply scolds Kaguya and then Lyra, which suggests the remark was Lyra's.[@fm15-strays]

## The Great Conflict {#great-conflict}

{{nocite}} The Astrea Record novels follow the Familia through the [[great-conflict|Great Conflict]]. The shared events are told on [[astrea-familia|Astrea Familia]] and [[great-conflict|The Great Conflict]]; this section follows Lyra.

### Astrea Record 1

- At a burning factory she clears a warehouse with her boomerang, and Alize sends her with [[minor-characters#iska|Iska]] and [[minor-characters#maryu|Maryu]] to clear the next section.[@ar01-factory]
- She brings Alize an order from Finn ("Who else? My hero!") to watch for suspicious activity. On [[floor-18|Floor 18]] she fights the [[evils|Evils]]' [[vito|Vito]] beside Alize and Kaguya.[@ar01-hero, ar01-vito]
- In a clearing in the eastern forest of Floor 18, [[minor-characters#lyana|Lyana]] asks to be buried there if she dies, and Lyra answers, "Count me in."[@ar01-clearing] {{inference}} Lyu's later grave of mementos is also in the eastern forest of Floor 18.[@ar01-grave]
- On patrol with Lyu and Kaguya she brushes off the god "Eren", who is [[erebus|Erebus]] in disguise; he calls her the level-headed one.[@ar01-eren, ar02-erebus]
- On the night the Great Conflict begins she raids an Evils base with Alize, Kaguya, Lyu and Shakti's Ganesha Familia. She suspects a trap; when a child blows herself up and kills Shakti's sister [[adi-varma|Adi]] {{small|printed *Ardee* in Astrea Record 1 and 2}}, Lyra is the first to understand, holds Lyu back, and warns that the fallen enemies are "all rigged to blow".[@ar01-trap, ar01-ardee] The ambush is [[valletta|Valletta Grede]]'s.[@ar01-trap]
- When Alize senses that Astrea is in danger, Lyra backs her hunch and holds the line so that Alize can go: "If I die 'cause of this, I'm gonna spit on you from heaven!"[@ar01-hunch]

### Astrea Record 2

- After the first night, when townspeople stone the Familia and Lyu collapses, Lyra calls for help to carry her.[@ar02-stones] When Lyu runs away, Lyra goes to find her with Kaguya and tells Alize to stay with the others.[@ar02-search]
- In the north they find [[syr-flover|Syr]] feeding refugees. When the Evils attack, Lyra and Kaguya cut down the man about to kill her and clear out the raiders; afterwards Lyra leaves the guilty townspeople without a word, because saying nothing hurts them most, and adds, "Ain't it way more heroic that way?"[@ar02-syr, ar02-heroic]
- Erebus asks them what justice is. He tells Lyra that her justice is "wisdom masquerading as poison", the last resort of "a powerless rat", and she explodes in fury.[@ar02-erebus]
- In the fight that follows, Alfia steps in. Lyra dodges the blast of [[magic#satanas-verion|Satanas Verion]] but is knocked down, bleeding from her eyes, ears and mouth, and realises that the magic is sound.[@ar02-alfia] She plays dead; Alfia sees through it and puts her at "Level Two or thereabouts". Lyra keeps talking until Kaguya, who has also been playing dead, strikes at Alfia's back; then she covers their escape with a shower of small bombs and drags the injured Kaguya away.[@ar02-alfia, ar02-escape]
- She reports to Astrea that Erebus is after Lyu.[@ar02-report] Kaguya had asked her to think of "some clever trick" against Alfia; Lyra asks Finn about Alfia and then asks Asfi to make her a magic item, although Asfi is already busy with Finn's large order of earrings.[@ar02-escape, ar02-item]
- When Finn announces that the Evils are bringing up a monster from the Dungeon, Lyra panics loudly and demands to know whether he has a plan. It is an act: she tells Lyu to watch, and Finn answers, "We fight."[@ar02-act]

### Astrea Record 3

- Before the battle she carries a round shield almost as big as herself, which is unusual for someone who fights from the back. She says Finn told her to take it, that she has no idea how it will help, and that Perseus made it for her; Asfi, exhausted, blames "Slyle".[@ar03-shield, ar03-asfi] It is the item she asked Asfi for in Astrea Record 2, when she told her there was a prototype of similar armour to work from.[@ar02-item]
- In the battle with Alfia, Kaguya splits off to fight Vito and orders Lyra to make up for her absence.[@ar03-kaguya] Lyra strikes Alfia with a shield bash, then explains that Alfia's protection, [[magic#silentium-eden|Silentium Eden]], is not a barrier spell but an enchantment that is always on and must be draining her Mind. Alfia admits that "that Level Two prum has outwitted me for a second time".[@ar03-passive]
- When Alfia casts her final spell, [[magic#genos-angelus|Genos Angelus]], Lyra throws herself in front of it behind her shield. The shield neutralises the spell and shatters. She explains that the earlier shield bash let it take Alfia's own Silentium Eden; Perseus made it with "that Cyclops" from a prototype shield (the Japanese word means *original form*) that [[hermes|Hermes]] gave them, the one [[zeus-and-hera-familias|Zeus]] is said to have had: the Aegis.[@ar03-genos] (See [[alfia#genos-angelus-stopped-by-lyra|Alfia § Stopped by Lyra]].)
- Facing [[delphyne|Delphyne]], she argues that they cannot win without an edge; she judges it at least Level 6 or 7. After Astrea updates the whole Familia, Lyra proposes "a real raid battle", asks Riveria for a buff, and is one of the eight fighters who close in on the dragon.[@ar03-delphyne, ar03-rankup]
- After Erebus is captured, she kicks a stone in anger.[@ar03-erebus]

Before the battle Lyra tells Neze that she has no idea how the shield is meant to help; after Genos Angelus the narration says she had got every piece of information from Finn, Alfia's third spell included, and that her move to the front line had only been made to look like covering for Kaguya.[@ar03-shield, ar03-genos] {{inference}} Her earlier remark may have been another act.

## Death {#death}

On the day before the Familia's last expedition, the Familia's prum asks whether the Guild's report smells of a trap, "Just like the nightmare on the twenty-seventh floor"; Alize answers that they must go anyway.[@fm14-trap-warning] {{inference}} Lyra is the only prum among Lyu's ten companions, so the speaker is presumably her.[@fc03-list]

In the Dungeon Lyra sniffs out [[rudra-familia|Rudra Familia]]'s trap and warns everyone, so the Familia escapes the blasts of its Inferno Stones.[@fm14-sniffed] Then the [[juggernaut|Juggernaut]] appears. When only four of them are left, magic reflected off the monster's shell has melted both of Lyra's eyes, and she cannot see.[@fm14-blind]

Alize asks Kaguya and Lyra to give their lives to save Lyu. Lyra says she puts her own life first, but as the weakest she will probably die first anyway, so she goes along with the plan, laughing bravely that she never bets on a gamble she cannot win.[@fm14-lives]

She is the first of the three to die. Blind, she falls to one stroke of the Juggernaut's claws, but just before she dies she sets off a bomb she has held behind her back, one of the finest she ever made. It takes off the Juggernaut's right arm, and Kaguya uses the moment to cut at its legs.[@fm14-death] Lyu, unable even to gather her companions' remains, flees past the bodies of Lyra and Kaguya.[@fm14-corpses] The rest of the story is told on [[astrea-familia#destruction|Astrea Familia]] and [[lyu-leon|Lyu Leon]].

## Magic {#magic}

In Familia Chronicle 3, when Lyu uses her magic [[lyu-leon#astrea-record-spell|Astrea Record]] to call on her dead companions' spells, the shout "Moose Mine!" is followed by the line "The shrewd prum tactician who set traps", in the same way that "Gokou!" is followed by a line describing Kaguya.[@fc03-moose] {{inference}} Lyra was the Familia's only prum and its strategist, so *Moose Mine* is probably her magic. The text does not name her there and does not say what the spell does.[@fc03-moose, fc03-list]
