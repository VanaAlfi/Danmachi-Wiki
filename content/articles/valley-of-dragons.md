The **Valley of Dragons** is where the Black Dragon, one of the [[three-great-quests|Three Great Quests]], is sealed. In DanMachi 20 [[leon-verdenberg|Leon Verdenberg]] tells the legend: a hero took the dragon's eye and drove the one-eyed dragon from the western lands, and a great spirit who offered up its own body sealed it here.[@fm20-legend] It lies in the far north, beyond the Great Wall and a black wasteland, and is a nest of dragonids: besides the Black Dragon, many other powerful dragons, survivors of old, roam there.[@fm20-valley-nest]

## The seal

According to the gods, the dragon wanted prey to heal its lost eye. When almost all the dragons of the world had gathered in this land, the great spirit raised a vast storm to seal them.[@fm20-storm] The sleeping Black Dragon snores from time to time; the sound disturbs the wind and opens tears through which dragons escape, which the locals call "descending from the valley". This is the cause of the roaming dragons that trouble the world.[@fm20-breath] The [[geography#the-north-of-the-continent-great-dragon-wall|Great Dragon Wall]] restricts access.[@fm20-wall]

Barrier devices made by [[school-district|the School District]] stand around the base of the storm to maintain and strengthen the seal.[@fm20-barrier] In DanMachi 20 one of these barriers appears to be broken, and an escaped dragon has to be defeated.[@fm20-broken]

## Attacks

Dragon attacks from beyond the School District's barrier are increasing in DanMachi 19.[@fm19-attacks] In Sword Oratoria 13 the student [[luke-fowl|Luke]] of the [[balder#seventh-squad|7th Squad]] points to the valley, where more and more vicious dragons appear, as he argues that the world's suffering is growing.[@so13-valley] [[alicia-forestlight|Alicia Forestlight]] of Loki Familia, whose village lies in the north not far from the valley, tells School District students that she became an adventurer to destroy the dragons that threatened her people's homes and forests.[@so13-alicia-valley]

Sword Oratoria 8 says that the colossal, flightless dragon that massacred [[bete-loga|Bete Loga]]'s tribe on the plains had come from the valley, one of the world's three great frontiers in the far north.[@so08-valley-beast]
