**The Evils** are the coalition of dark gods and their followers who openly attacked [[orario|Orario]]'s order after [[zeus-and-hera-familias|Zeus Familia and Hera Familia]] fell without completing the [[three-great-quests|Three Great Quests]].[@so09-darkage, so09-evil-gods] In that dark age, [[astrea-familia|Astrea Familia]] fought them to keep law and order.[@fc01-darkage] [[hermes|Hermes]] calls the era "the Age of Darkness", "A period of untold chaos unlike any this city has seen before or since".[@ar01-age] The dark age began fifteen years before Sword Oratoria 7 and lasted until five years before the main story, when [[lyu-leon|Lyu]] annihilated the Evils' main force by herself.[@so07-dark-age, so11-five, fm18-lyu-alone] Their leaders included [[valletta|Valletta Grede]], called Arachnia, and [[olivas-act|Olivas Act]], called Vendetta; [[apate-and-alecto-familias|Apate Familia and Alecto Familia]] formed the core of their strike force, and their cultists, the Faithful, had no Falna and fought by blowing themselves up among their enemies.[@so09-leaders, ar03-strike-force, ar02-faithful]

## The Great Conflict

In the [[great-conflict|Great Conflict]] the Evils are led by the god [[erebus|Erebus]], with [[zald|Zald]] and [[alfia|Alfia]].[@ar03-erebus] Erebus's real aim was to put Orario through a trial that would bring forth a future hero, and some of the gods sent back to the heavens on the night the Great Conflict began were the Evils' own, whom he had meant all along to sacrifice.[@ar03-erebus-aim, ar03-sacrifice] They send a god into the Dungeon as bait to lure a god-slaying monster, [[delphyne|Delphyne]], to the surface.[@ar02-bait, ar03-delphyne, ar03-god-slayer] After their defeat, those who remain flee the city or go into hiding underground.[@ar03-scatter]

## The remnants

In the Twenty-Seventh-Floor Nightmare the Evils staged a huge [[pass-parade|pass parade]] that pitted every monster on that floor, its [[monster-rex|Monster Rex]] included, against the adventurers sent by the Guild, sacrificing their own members to prolong the life of their decaying organization; meanwhile [[finn-deimne|Finn]] raided every place that could house their base and, with the help of other gods, sent many of their gods back to the heavens.[@so07-nightmare]

Remnants of the Evils later hide in [[knossos|Knossos]], which serves as their base in Sword Oratoria.[@so07-base] There their patron god is [[thanatos|Thanatos]]; they lacked the money to complete Knossos, and their sources included smuggling through Meren under a contract with [[njordr|Njörðr]] and [[ishtar|Ishtar]]'s investment.[@so07-thanatos, so07-funds] In Sword Oratoria 11 an assault on Knossos crushes the remnants and sends Thanatos back to the heavens, at the cost of the destruction of Dionysus Familia.[@so11-crushed] [[rudra-familia|Rudra Familia]], which ambushed Astrea Familia with the [[juggernaut|Juggernaut]], was one of their allies; [[lyu-leon|Lyu]] destroyed it.[@fm13-ambush, fc01-rudra] Magic items they made in Knossos, whips and collars that let weaker tamers control monsters, are smuggled out by [[rudra-familia#jura-harma|Jura]] in DanMachi 13.[@fm13-remnants]

Sword Oratoria 12 reveals that the god [[dionysus|Dionysus]], under the name Enyo, brought the Evils and the [[corrupted-spirit|corrupted spirit]]'s forces together.[@so12-enyo] His plan to destroy Orario turns out to be a lure: his real aim is to wipe out the adventurers gathered against him, so that no one could hold back the monsters.[@so12-true-aim]
