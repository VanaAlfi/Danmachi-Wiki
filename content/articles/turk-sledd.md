**Turk Sledd** is a werewolf adventurer of [[rivira|Rivira]]. In DanMachi 13, on the orders of [[rudra-familia#jura-harma|Jura Harma]], he stirs up the hunt for the Gale Wind, [[lyu-leon|Lyu Leon]]'s old alias, and with three companions he attacks Aisha's party and sets off the explosions on the twenty-fifth floor.[@fm13-jura, fm13-ambush, fm13-explosion] He dies in the collapse of the [[water-capital|Water Capital]] in DanMachi 14.[@fm14-death]

## In Rivira

Lilly finds out that he has lived in Rivira for about three years and that, "Assuming he hasn’t made any false reports, he’s a Level Two"; he keeps company with second-tier adventurers and has been down to the lower levels himself many times. Rivira's residents place "a certain degree of trust" in him.[@fm13-turk]

## The hunt for the Gale Wind

When the hunting party for the Gale Wind stalls in the Water Capital, Turk calls on the others to go after the fugitive and make a name for themselves. Most side with [[bors|Bors]], but a group of four sets off with Turk for the twenty-seventh floor, west along the cliff's edge; Lilly's group follows them.[@fm13-west, fm13-follow]

Bell later pieces together Turk's part, admitting that it is speculation: Turk killed the injured Jan, made it look as though the Gale Wind had done it, ran to tell Bors and Bell in Rivira, and "spearheaded the formation of a hunting party to pursue Gale Wind, just as the catman had ordered him to". Jura himself says, "I used Jan and Turk to do something about her."[@fm13-jura]

## The whip and the explosions

In the Water Capital Turk and his three companions turn on [[aisha-belka|Aisha]]'s party, "For the sake of Jura’s plan", and Turk summons a monster with a scarlet whip: a wormwell, the [[lambton|lambton]].[@fm13-ambush, fm13-wormwell] The whip, made by the Evils' Remnants in [[knossos|Knossos]] and smuggled out by Jura, lets even an unskilled tamer command a monster wearing a matching collar; Turk is "drunk on a false sense of omnipotence".[@fm13-whip]

His band then sets off Inferno Stones with magic blades in a twenty-fifth-floor passage. Jura's plan was that "once I almost finished destroying the twenty-seventh floor, Turk would take over on the twenty-fifth"; Jura complains that Turk "got nervous about you, Leon, and let off the explosion too soon".[@fm13-explosion, fm13-early]

## Death

In DanMachi 14 Turk, "the ringleader of the plan to blow up the twenty-fifth floor", reappears in the burning cavern with his three companions, screaming, "You lied to us, Juraaaaaa!! We never knew it would turn out like this!" He leads them across the burning root dome, where one of them bursts into flames, and up the western cliff, where the four are caught in the collapse and crushed beneath falling crystals.[@fm14-bridge, fm14-death]
