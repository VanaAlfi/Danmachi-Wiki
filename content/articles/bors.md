**Bors Elder** is a human adventurer who runs the largest [[the-exchange#rivira|Exchange]] shop in [[rivira|Rivira]], the town on [[floor-18|Floor 18]].[@so02-bors, so03-bors] At Level 3 he is Rivira's strongest adventurer, and so the person in charge in an emergency; later volumes call him the head of Rivira.[@so02-bors, fm12-eggs] DanMachi 5 prints his name as "Boris".[@fm05-boris]

## Appearance and character

Bors is a hulking man with a black patch over his left eye.[@fm05-boris, so02-bors] He embodies the formula that adventurers are outlaws, and he owns one of Rivira's storehouses, where adventurers leave spare equipment for a fee.[@so03-bors] He trades information for valis, though he talks quickly enough once [[bete-loga|Bete]] grabs his collar.[@so03-bors] Money moves him: he puts a bounty hunt together in DanMachi 13 and tries to claim a share of a reward in DanMachi 14.[@fm13-bounty, fm14-report]

He once dreamed of becoming a smith and has become something of a weapons expert. After [[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz]] defeats the floor boss [[udaeus|Udaeus]] in Sword Oratoria 2, he talks her into leaving its black sword with him, promising to make it into a great weapon.[@so02-sword] When Aiz asks about it in Minor Myths and Legends 2, he breaks into a sweat and runs off with a fake smile.[@ss02-sword]

## The hunt for the Gale Wind

In DanMachi 13 Bors tells Rivira that the Gale Wind, [[lyu-leon|Lyu Leon]]'s old alias, kills anyone she suspects. When someone mentions the eighty-million-valis bounty on her head, he forms a hunting party to claim it.[@fm13-bounty] He tells the party she is a top-class Level 4 and posts guards between floors so she cannot slip past.[@fm13-hunt] On Floor 27 he recognises the wounded catman as Jura Harma of [[rudra-familia|Rudra Familia]], the faction that trapped Lyu's [[astrea-familia|Astrea Familia]], and he keeps the hunt going for the money.[@fm13-jura, fm13-money]

When Lyu breaks through the hunters, Bors, a "top-grade Level Three", is the last one standing against her. He hesitates for an instant, as if remembering the fight on Floor 18, and her wooden sword hits him in the face.[@fm13-hesitate] Then the [[juggernaut|Juggernaut]] attacks. Most of the party is killed; Bors's eye patch is torn off, and he sees himself die, a vision produced by his own terrified mind. In fact Lyu has stepped in front of the blow, and he recognises her as the elf who fought the Black Goliath. She tells him to escape, and he flees.[@fm13-saved]

In DanMachi 14 he reaches [[hestia-familia|Hestia Familia]]'s rescue party and tells them what he saw. [[aisha-belka|Aisha]] makes him come back down with them.[@fm14-return] As the Level 3 on the front line, he helps hold off the monsters with a borrowed shield and lance, then guides the party to where he last saw the Gale Wind.[@fm14-shield, fm14-guide]

## Clearing her name

Back on the surface, the half-healed Bors shouts at the [[guild|Guild]]'s reception desk that Rudra Familia, not the Gale Wind, caused the deaths, and that "the elf tried to protect us to the last".[@fm14-report] Aisha explains that this is how he repays Lyu for saving his life; as a second-tier adventurer and Rivira's head, he is believed. He also tries to claim a share of the bounty and of the reward for Jura Harma, and is refused. The Guild accepts the report that the Gale Wind died.[@fm14-report]

Bors also recovered a fragment of Lyu's broken wooden sword, [[equipment#alvs-lumina|Alvs Lumina]], and gave it to her when she was leaving [[orario|Orario]].[@fc03-lumina]
