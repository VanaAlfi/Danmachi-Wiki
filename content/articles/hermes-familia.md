**Hermes Familia** is the Familia of the god [[hermes|Hermes]], led by [[asfi|Asfi Al Andromeda]].[@fm05-asfi] It works nominally as a delivery service, which lets it pass through checkpoints freely; the [[guild|Guild]] also sends it outside the city to investigate where black-market goods are being smuggled.[@fm09-delivery]

## Members

| Member | Notes |
|---|---|
| [[asfi|Asfi Al Andromeda]] | Captain, titled Perseus; succeeded Lydis during the [[great-conflict|Great Conflict]].[@fm05-asfi, ar02-captain] |
| Lulune Louie | A chienthrope, secretly Level 3, who carries cargo for [[fels|Fels]] in Sword Oratoria 2.[@so02-lulune, so03-lulune] |
| Falgar | Fights in the Great Conflict.[@ar02-falgar] |
| [[aisha-belka|Aisha Belka]] | Joins after [[ishtar|Ishtar]]'s fall; the Familia keeps her affiliation from the Guild to preserve its neutrality.[@fm10-aisha] |

The Familia hides its members' true Levels. In Sword Oratoria 3 one of them admits this, saying Asfi and the others can fight with the best.[@so03-hide]
