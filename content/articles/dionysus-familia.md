**Dionysus Familia** is the Familia of the god [[dionysus|Dionysus]], captained by the elf [[filvis|Filvis Challia]].[@so01-standing, so11-leader] Allied with [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]] against the [[evils|Evils]], its adventurers are wiped out in the first assault on [[knossos|Knossos]] in Sword Oratoria 11; Sword Oratoria 12 reveals that they "had not actually been Dionysus Familia".[@so10-alliance, so11-destroyed, so12-penia]

## Standing and members {#members}

- **Strength:** according to the Guild's records it was "in the middle of the pack in terms of strength within the Labyrinth City", with quite a few Level 2 adventurers but none with notable achievements: "nothing particularly good or bad about the group, nothing special".[@so01-standing]
- **Home:** a luxurious manor; an underground room holds Dionysus's prized wine cellar.[@so10-cellar, so11-manor]
- **Captain:** Filvis, "A Level 3 magic swordsman".[@so04-filvis, so11-leader] Every party she joined after the Twenty-Seventh-Floor Nightmare was wiped out except for her, so she is called "Banshee", and "Even the members of her own Dionysus Familia kept a good distance from their leader".[@so11-aura, so03-distance]
- **Second-in-command:** Aura Moriel, an elf mage titled *Krater* ({{ja|葡萄の杯}}, written *grape cup*)[@so11-krater-ja]: "A Level 2. Alias: Krater." She joined the Familia at the same time as Filvis, and in Sword Oratoria 11 she asks her: "Are you going to kill us this time, Banshee?"[@so11-aura]
- **The dark age:** in Astrea Record 1 Filvis and Dionysus Familia "were proud to fight for Orario’s safety in the name of their lord".[@ar01-dark]

## The Twenty-Seventh-Floor Nightmare {#twenty-seventh-floor-nightmare}

The Evils' "sacrificial pass parade" on the twenty-seventh floor, masterminded by [[olivas-act|Olivas Act]], caused heavy losses for the Evils and for the adventurers under the Guild's banner. "A young Filvis had been there that day, along with the rest of Dionysus Familia"; the bodies of the rest of the Familia had been implanted with magic stones and turned into moaning "lumps of flesh".[@so12-nightmare] See [[filvis|Filvis]].

## Alliance with Loki Familia {#alliance}

- Filvis tells Lefiya that a member of the Familia had been killed before the Monsterphilia.[@so04-member]
- Loki Familia, Dionysus Familia and [[hermes-familia|Hermes Familia]] form "a united front to chase down the underground forces of the monstrous creatures and the remnants of the Evils".[@so10-alliance]
- In Sword Oratoria 11 the whole Familia joins the first assault on Knossos; Aura blames Filvis for getting "the whole familia wrapped up in it".[@so11-aura]

## Destruction {#destruction}

In the first assault on Knossos "the seemingly sentient flesh" filled the passages "in the blink of an eye, preying upon any adventurers it found"; "The first victims were, of course, Dionysus Familia".[@so12-flesh, so11-destroyed] Loki Familia and Hermes Familia kept their own death toll to zero, but "only at the cost of abandoning Dionysus Familia"; crushing the Evils and sending [[thanatos|Thanatos]] back had cost "the destruction of Dionysus Familia".[@so11-abandon, so11-destroyed] Hours later its luxurious manor "no longer had anyone left to return to it"; the Guild, which reclaims the property of a destroyed Familia, had not yet acted.[@so11-manor] Loki Familia held the funeral ceremonies for Dionysus Familia, "which was wiped out in the first assault" (Sword Oratoria 13).[@so13-funerals]

## The truth {#truth}

Sword Oratoria 12 explains the Familia's "simultaneous Status seal": "They had not actually been Dionysus Familia". All the adventurers of the first assault, from Aura Moriel down, had been Penia's followers ("They had been Penia Familia"), and "Dionysus had even made his former followers" drink the divine wine. See [[dionysus#deception|Dionysus]] and [[penia|Penia]].[@so12-penia]
