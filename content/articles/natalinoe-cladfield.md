**Natalinoe Cladfield**, called **Nano**, is a human Level 3 mage in the [[balder#seventh-squad|7th Squad]], trained by [[lefiya|Lefiya Viridis]].[@so13-introduction, so13-growth] Nano introduces herself as Natalinoe Cladfield and asks to be called Nano.[@so13-introduction] She is a human Level 3 mage who fights from the rear, has strawberry-blond hair, and is smaller than Lefiya Viridis.[@so13-introduction] She loves her childhood friend [[luke-fowl|Luke Fowl]].[@so13-childhood]
