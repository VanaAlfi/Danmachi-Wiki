The **Alv Mountains** {{small|printed *Alb Mountains* in DanMachi 9}} are the elves' sacred peaks in the west of the continent.[@so14-west] They extend north, lying east of the route taken north from [[orario|Orario]] while the [[beor-mountains|Beor Mountains]] lie west.[@fm20-route] In ancient times, dark elves defended the peaks against monsters, while a high elf led white elves down from the mountains.[@fc02-history]

| Sword Oratoria 13 | A girl obtains [[loki|Loki]]'s permission to leave [[orario|Orario]], carries her friend's ashes to the mountains, scatters them at a peak and leaves no gravestone.[@so13-ashes] |
