**The Three Great Quests** are the quests to slay three monsters that escaped [[dungeon|the Dungeon]] in ancient times: the Behemoth, the Leviathan and the Black Dragon.[@fm06-quests] [[zeus-and-hera-familias|Zeus Familia and Hera Familia]] killed the Behemoth and the Leviathan, but the Black Dragon defeated them. It is the last of the three.[@fm06-quests, so09-quests]

## Zeus and Hera

Zeus Familia and Hera Familia were the strongest Familias in [[orario|Orario]]; Zeus Familia had a Level 8 and Hera Familia a Level 9.[@fm06-zeus, so13-levels, fc02-levels] They killed the Behemoth and the Leviathan fifteen years before DanMachi 6.[@fm06-quests] In the campaign against the Leviathan they used a sea fortress, with the help of Poseidon Familia; it is now the Hringhorni, the ship of [[school-district|the School District]].[@so13-ship]

[[zald|Zald]] of Zeus Familia ate the Behemoth's flesh to win, and its poison has slowly eaten away at him ever since.[@ar03-zald] Against the Black Dragon, as [[alfia|Alfia]] of Hera Familia later says, they were powerless.[@ar03-alfia] Their defeat left the power vacuum that the [[evils|Evils]] exploited in Orario's dark age.[@fm15-vacuum]

## The Behemoth and the Leviathan {#leviathan}

DanMachi 6 calls the Behemoth "the Terrestrial Tyrant" and the Leviathan "the Ruler of the Sea".[@fm06-titles] With the Black Dragon they are the three ancient monsters whose slaying the world asks of [[orario|Orario]].[@ar01-behemoth-three, fm06-behemoth-hermes] [[hermes|Hermes]] tells Bell that fifteen years earlier Zeus Familia and Hera Familia, at the height of their power, defeated first the Behemoth and then the Leviathan before the Black Dragon wiped them out.[@fm06-behemoth-hermes] Riveria says that Zald and Alfia, "Glutton and Silence", played important parts in slaying both.[@ar03-parts] Afterwards [[zald|Zald]] had to leave frontline combat and Alfia's condition worsened.[@ar03-behemoth-poison] [[alfia|Alfia]] herself names the Behemoth and the Leviathan as beasts they slew before they proved powerless against the Black Dragon.[@ar03-behemoth-alfia] [[ottar|Ottar]], who had seen the battlefield with [[finn-deimne|Finn Deimne]], asks Zald whether he regrets slaying the Behemoth; Zald says he does not, having done what he could for the great wish and for his comrades.[@ar03-behemoth-regret] In the same volume Gareth speaks of the Evils' believers in [[geography#ruined-and-remote-lands-dedyne|Dedyne]] before the Great Conflict, and Dedyne is recalled as the "home of the Black Desert, where the battle with the Behemoth was fought".[@ar03-dedyne] [[astrea|Astrea]] has heard that in that land, long exposed to the Behemoth's poison, never-withering medicinal herbs grow in clusters.[@ar03-behemoth-dedyne]

[[gareth|Gareth Landrock]] tells [[astrea-familia|Astrea Familia]] that in the battle with the Behemoth Zald was afflicted by its poison and, to win, bit straight into its flesh, which gave him the power to deal the killing blow.[@ar03-behemoth-poison] The poisoned flesh then struck back like a curse, devouring him slowly ever since, and Astrea Familia learn for the first time how the Behemoth was really slain.[@ar03-behemoth-poison] Ottar last saw Zald on the black sand, felling the Behemoth like a great hero and collapsing, exhausted, as the sun rose; Zald then withdrew from the front line and was even rumoured dead.[@ar01-behemoth-ottar] DanMachi 20 recalls the blow that slew the tyrant of the earth as a conqueror's slash, struck with no heed for the wielder's own body.[@fm20-behemoth-slash]

The Leviathan was an ancient monster that came up from the Dungeon to the surface in the Ancient Times, a calamity of the sea.[@so06-leviathan-seal] A giant dragon that ruled the sea, it was apparently so vast that Orario's adventurers needed a great expanse of land at sea to fight it.[@fm20-leviathan-foothold]

In Astrea Record 3 Riveria and Gareth Landrock recognise Alfia's final spell as the one that finished off the Leviathan.[@ar03-leviathan-finish] DanMachi 20 recalls the battle through one who clung to a plank of the wrecked floating fortress: as a witch with ashen hair chanted, the beast howled in fear; heroes held off its blows, magic broke its scales, warriors dived in to harpoon it, and the spell struck it down.[@fm20-leviathan-battle]

- **The Hringhorni:** in DanMachi 20 [[leon-verdenberg|Leon]] explains that the School District's ship was originally a floating fortress, a foothold for fighting the Leviathan, which could capsize ships just by twisting its body. Its round layers are the remnants of that foothold, and its blue wings are the drop item Leviathan's Fins. Leon himself slipped into the battle, and he calls it the cornerstone of the school.[@fm20-hringhorni] He came as a stowaway, meaning, he says, to laugh at the heroes as they died, but also to get back at them all by taking the Leviathan's head himself.[@fm20-leviathan-leon] The fortress was built with countless dwarves at its heart and help from Poseidon Familia, and Sword Oratoria 13 calls its winglike dimmers Leviathan's azure fins.[@fm20-leviathan-foothold, so13-leviathan-fins] The familia union of the School District did not yet exist then, and many of the foothold's helmsmen and engineers still serve in the ship's control layer.[@fm20-leviathan-foothold]
- **The Leviathan Seal:** after the Leviathan's defeat Zeus and Hera Familias brought back its skeleton and used it on the lid of the tunnel under Lolog Lake, completing what the Guild officially calls the Leviathan Seal; monsters flee from its bones as they do from the Black Dragon's scales (see [[njordr-familia#port-meren|Port Meren]]).[@so06-seal] Once the seal was in place fifteen years ago, Poseidon Familia, which had plugged the hole in the lake bed and kept up its lid, left Meren, sure the seal could never be broken, to hunt the dangerous monsters of the world's seas.[@so06-leviathan-poseidon]

## The Black Dragon

The Black Dragon, also called the One-Eyed Dragon, is the last of the Great Quests.[@so07-dragon] It is an ancient dragon, among the mightiest monsters to reach the surface in the Ancient Times.[@so09-black-dragon-quest] Slaying it is the responsibility and duty of Orario, the Labyrinth City.[@fm18-black-dragon-duty] In DanMachi 20 Leon tells of an ancient hero who took its eye and drove it from the western lands, and of a great spirit who gave up its own body to seal it in the [[valley-of-dragons|Valley of Dragons]]. The sleeping Black Dragon's snore sometimes opens a way for other dragons to escape.[@fm20-valley] Leon says it is reputed to have summoned dragons throughout the world to gather when it landed in the valley.[@fm20-black-dragon-gathering] On the Bridge of Heroes, an empty place opposite the hero [[albert|Albert]] waits for the final hero who will defeat the Black Dragon.[@fm16-bridge] Albert's legend tells of a pitch-black calamity born from [[dungeon|the great pit]], which he drove away alone at the cost of his life after his sword took an eye from the King of Dragons.[@fm16-black-dragon-albert] In the [[beor-mountains|Beor Mountains]], [[edas-village|Edas Village]] keeps and reveres scales it says the Black Dragon shed as it fled north after heroes drove it out of Orario; monsters fear them and stay away.[@fm08-scales] Bell sees the shiny black scales, each about the size of his torso, ringing the village where it meets the forest.[@fm08-black-dragon-scale-size]

In DanMachi 6 [[hermes|Hermes]] explains that the defeat by the Black Dragon cost Zeus Familia and Hera Familia their main forces, and the two Familias' strength fell sharply.[@fm06-black-dragon-hermes] In Sword Oratoria 13 [[lefiya|Lefiya Viridis]] tells students that the two Familias were defeated beyond what Orario and even the gods had predicted; the Black Dragon too had gathered strength over a thousand years.[@so13-black-dragon-lefiya] In Astrea Record 3 [[alfia|Alfia]] recalls its slaughter of the strongest adventurers, who were shattered, dismembered and devoured.[@ar03-black-dragon-alfia]

In DanMachi 18 [[royman|Royman Mardeel]] opposes risking first-tier adventurers in the war game, since they are needed to slay the Black Dragon.[@fm18-black-dragon-royman] [[allen-fromel|Allen Fromel]] tells his sister that his battle will not end until he kills the Black Dragon that destroyed their home.[@fm18-black-dragon-allen] In DanMachi 20 Leon warns that if another hunt for the Black Dragon fails, humanity will not be able to recover, and that careful preparation is needed.[@fm20-black-dragon-warning]

## Preparations in the covered volumes

- In DanMachi 17 [[freya|Freya]] offers [[ouranos|Ouranos]] a full expedition into unexplored territory and preparations against the Black Dragon, in exchange for his non-interference.[@fm17-pledge]
- In DanMachi 18 Royman tells Finn that he must slay the Black Dragon, that there will never be another generation like his, and that he will surely command Orario's forces in the coming battle to slay it.[@fm18-royman-finn]
- In DanMachi 19 [[fels|Fels]] is making a crimson jewel and means to have it ready, whatever the cost, for the Black Dragon hunt, "for the promised time".[@fm19-jewel]
- In Sword Oratoria 13 Alicia reminds Lefiya that with Zeus and Hera gone, the duty of completing the quests fell to Orario's adventurers; the quests are the fundamental reason Loki Familia and Freya Familia keep exploring the Dungeon.[@so13-quests-duty]
