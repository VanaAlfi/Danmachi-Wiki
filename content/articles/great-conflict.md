**The Great Conflict** is the war between [[orario|Orario]] and the [[evils|Evils]] seven years before the main story, told in the three volumes of Astrea Record.[@ar01-lyu] So many died that its days came to be called the *Seven Days of Death*; no other time in Orario's history cost so many lives.[@ar01-seven, ar03-seven]

## The first night

It begins at the end of Astrea Record 1, when Orario's Familias, on [[finn-deimne|Finn]]'s order, strike the Evils' fortresses across the city at once.[@ar01-gc-begins] That night [[zald|Zald]] and [[alfia|Alfia]] appear in the southwest district, and Zald hands [[ottar|Ottar]] "complete and utter defeat".[@ar02-gc-southwest, ar03-gc-ottar-night]

On the first night the [[familia-coalition|allied Familias]] suffer a crushing defeat: more Familias are weakened or wiped out than can be counted.[@ar02-coalition] Nine gods are sent back to the heavens that day, six of them allies and three belonging to the Evils, whom Erebus had meant all along as sacrifices.[@ar02-coalition, ar03-gc-evils-gods] The flashes of their return, themselves masses of [[tenkai-and-gekai#arcanum|Arcanum]], hide the release of a god's divine might underground to summon a monster.[@ar02-arcanum]

In Astrea Record 2 Finn reveals that the whole war, the Great Conflict included, was preparation for the Evils' true aim: a monster of the Dungeon, summoned on that night amid the gods' mass return and lured upward by a god sent into the Dungeon, is to destroy [[babel|Babel]] from beneath.[@ar02-gc-true-aim]

## Erebus, Zald and Alfia

The god [[erebus|Erebus]] leads the Evils, joined by [[zald|Zald]] and [[alfia|Alfia]], survivors of [[zeus-and-hera-familias|Zeus Familia and Hera Familia]]. In Astrea Record 3 [[astrea|Astrea]] confronts him with his true aim: to lead the darkness and put Orario through a trial that would produce a future hero.[@ar03-erebus] Alfia and Zald explain their reasoning: Zeus and Hera had slain the Behemoth and the Leviathan but were powerless against the Black Dragon, no hero of the Age of Gods can slay it, and so millions must pay the price to produce the one hero who can.[@ar03-alfia]

Victory does not wipe out the Evils; survivors flee or go into hiding.[@ar03-end] After the war, the other leaders of [[freya-familia|Freya Familia]] reach Level 6, and Aiz reaches Level 4.[@ar03-freya, ar03-end] So do [[finn-deimne|Finn]], [[riveria|Riveria]] and [[gareth|Gareth]] of Loki Familia, who all reach Level 6.[@ar03-gc-loki-six]

## Afterwards

In the epilogue of Astrea Record 3, [[hermes|Hermes]] tells [[bell-cranell|Bell]] about the Great Conflict.[@ar03-seven] The epilogue does not show Bell learning his own family's link to it, which is revealed only to the reader in a separate extra.[@ar03-extra]
