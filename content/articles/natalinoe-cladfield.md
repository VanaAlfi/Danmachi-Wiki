**Natalinoe Cladfield**, called **Nano**, is a human Level 3 mage in the [[balder#seventh-squad|7th Squad]], trained by [[lefiya|Lefiya Viridis]].[@so13-introduction, so13-growth] Nano introduces herself as Natalinoe Cladfield and asks to be called Nano.[@so13-introduction] She is a human Level 3 mage who fights from the rear, has strawberry-blond hair, and is smaller than Lefiya Viridis.[@so13-introduction] Her garbled speech and young looks lead Lefiya to doubt at first that she is Level 3.[@so13-nano-intro] She loves her childhood friend [[luke-fowl|Luke Fowl]].[@so13-childhood]

## Looks and character {#looks-and-character}

She wears a long hooded robe and carries a long staff.[@so13-nano-squad] She comes from the same hometown as Luke and followed him to the School District without telling her parents; from her family name Lefiya guesses that she is noble, though she seems quite a tomboy.[@so13-nano-squad] Talkative and timid, and not eager to fight, she reached Level 3, Lefiya judges, by being dragged along in the rash fights of the boy she loves.[@so13-nano-squad, so13-nano-lessons, so13-nano-rebuke] The squad's plan rests on its two Level 3s, Luke in the van and Nano at the rear as its powerful cannon.[@so13-nano-squad, so13-nano-rebuke]
