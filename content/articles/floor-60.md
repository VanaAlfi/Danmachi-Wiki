**Floor 60** of the [[dungeon|Dungeon]] is mentioned in [[ouranos|Ouranos]]'s theory about the [[corrupted-spirit|corrupted spirit]] and in a later report that an expedition [[familia-coalition|alliance]] has been defeated there.[@so04-theory, fm20-report]

## History {#history}

| Volume | Events |
|---|---|
| Sword Oratoria 4 | The spirit summons caterpillars and [[monsters#violas|Violas]] through a hole to lower floors, as though calling them from Floor 60.[@so04-monsters] |
| Sword Oratoria 4 | Ouranos believes the main corrupted spirit is hiding on Floor 60 or below.[@so04-theory] |
| DanMachi 18 | [[royman|Royman Mardeel]] identifies the gap between Floors 60 and 61 when showing a relic from Thalia's Ice Garden.[@fm18-gulf] |
| DanMachi 20 | A wounded returning adventurer reports the alliance's defeat on Floor 60 and asks for reinforcements because the captain and others remain in the depths.[@fm20-report] |


[@so04-monsters]: SO04 | Last Chapter: To Adventure | Monsters emerge from the lower-floor hole during the spirit battle. The Japanese edition (file c5SA, paragraph 1031) has the female form summon them through the great hole to the lower floors as though calling them from floor 60.
[@so04-theory]: SO04 | Epilogue: Scenario Gone Wild | Ouranos proposes a deeper location for the main corrupted spirit. The Japanese edition (file c8DT, paragraph 16) has him say that it is probably hiding on floor 60 or below.
[@fm18-gulf]: FM18 | Chapter 6: Megami Tensei ~Orario Revelation~ | Royman shows the relic and identifies the gap between Floors 60 and 61.
[@fm20-report]: FM20 | Epilogue: Beautiful World | The returning expedition reports defeat on Floor 60 and requests rescue.
[@so04-floor60-ja]: SO04 | Epilogue: Scenario Gone Wild | The Japanese edition names the floor by number with the counter for Dungeon floors (file c8DT, paragraph 16).
