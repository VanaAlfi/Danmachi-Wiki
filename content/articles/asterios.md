**Asterios** is a black [[minotaur|Minotaur]] among the [[xenos|Xenos]]. He remembers, as a recurring dream, a former battle with [[bell-cranell|Bell Cranell]], and that longing drives his rebirth and his search for Bell.[@fm11-name, fm11-dream] The [[guild|Guild]] classes him as a Level 7 threat; this is a monster classification, not a Status.[@so11-level, fm12-level] Officially he was designated "a subspecies of black rhino" and recorded as "a first-class bounty monster", and the rumours that Ouranos had spread said that Loki Familia "had exterminated the others" while Bell fought him.[@fm12-bounty, fm11-rumours]

## Name

The black Minotaur is first named Asterios in DanMachi 10; in DanMachi 11 he tells Bell, "My name is Asterios", and asks for Bell's in turn: "Tell me your name".[@fm10-asterios, fm11-name, fm11-names] He is the strongest of the Xenos, stronger than their leader [[lido|Lido]].[@fm09-strongest]

## Appearance and weapon {#appearance}

When the adventurers first meet him in DanMachi 10 he is simply "A black minotaur" brandishing "a double-sided ax, a Labrys"; each single-handed swing of the Labrys shatters the floor, and his arms sweep foes aside like great trees.[@fm10-labrys] After losing his right arm to Aiz he fights with the Labrys in his left hand.[@fm11-rematch]

## The rematch {#rematch}

Asterios "had no ulterior motives": "All he sought was a rematch with his old opponent."[@fm11-will] Their fight in DanMachi 11 runs through the streets in front of [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]] and the townsfolk. Bell "abandoned his pride" and aims for the right side, where Aiz had cut off the arm, while Asterios smiles and intercepts every thrust; the narration notes that Asterios "had come to this battle after Aiz and many other adventurers had injured him".[@fm11-rightside] [[ottar|Ottar]] throws a sword down into the plaza at their feet, Asterios loses his left eye, and he charges Bell into the great hall of [[babel|Babel]] before declaring: "Now we are even…"[@fm11-rematch] The narration adds that the onlookers saw his "iron-strong will to fight and kill".[@fm11-will]

## Character {#character}

Asterios shows little of the other Xenos' reasoning. Looking back on the incident in Sword Oratoria 10, [[finn-deimne|Finn]] judges that "Unlike the other armed monsters, it didn’t seem to have any higher reasoning": it seemed "only a creature starved for combat", and while Finn can predict rational monsters, "that black minotaur alone was unreadable".[@so10-unreadable] He speaks rarely; in Sword Oratoria 12 he "spoke in brusque human words for the first time that battle and then smiled", a grin that "masked an irrepressible hunger" before his battle instincts took over again.[@so12-grin] In the rematch he smiles again and again: as Bell prepares to fight, at Bell's thrusts, and after losing his left eye.[@fm11-smiles, fm11-rightside] When Fels has restored his arm, "Asterios had thanked Fels briefly, but that was all."[@fm11-fels]

For Bell the encounter is decisive: "Bell had been lost since he met the Xenos, and Asterios had given him a reason to fight", and DanMachi 14 counts "his worthy opponent Asterios" among the events that changed him.[@fm11-reason, fm14-opponent] In DanMachi 20 the cheering crowds recall "the duel with the Black Minotaur" among "the city’s greatest legends".[@fm20-legend]
