**Hephaistos** is the goddess of the forge and the head of [[hephaistos-familia|Hephaistos Familia]], a Familia of master smiths.[@fm01-hephaistos, fm04-masters] Its store on Northwest Main Street is a branch, and it also has shops in [[babel|Babel]].[@fm08-ja-store, fm08-store, fm02-babel] She has crimson hair and crimson eyes and wears a patch over her right eye.[@fm08-looks] She thinks of herself as a smith before a god: in the Lower World, with no more strength than a mortal, she still forges weapons with her own hands.[@fm01-smith]

## Hestia's friend

When [[hestia|Hestia]] first came down to the Lower World she lived with Hephaistos Familia, and Hephaistos later gave her an abandoned church to live in.[@fm04-hestia, fm15-church] Mocked by other deities all her existence for her true face, she says that Hestia is the only goddess who never laughed at it; that is why Hestia is her one and only friend.[@fm08-friend] At Hestia's request she forges the [[hestia-knife|Hestia Knife]] for [[bell-cranell|Bell]] from her own [[metals#mythril|mythril]], engraved with Hestia's blessing so that only someone with that blessing can wield it.[@fm01-knife] Hestia takes on a large debt for it, confirmed in DanMachi 7 as 200 million valis.[@fm01-loan, fm07-debt]

## Welf Crozzo

[[welf-crozzo|Welf Crozzo]] was a smith of her Familia until DanMachi 6. In DanMachi 5 she sends Hestia the [[magic-sword|Crozzo magic sword]] Welf forged, with a warning to him not to put allies at risk for his pride.[@fm05-sword] When Welf asks in DanMachi 6 to leave and join [[hestia-familia|Hestia Familia]], she accepts once he names friendship as his reason, and gives him one of her crimson hammers as a parting gift.[@fm06-farewell] In DanMachi 8 she oversees the capture of [[rakia|Rakian]] infiltrators from the Crozzo family and accepts the surrender of Welf's grandfather.[@fm08-rakia]

In the same volume Welf sees her uncovered face, both eyes, without recoiling. The narration explains that even with [[tenkai-and-gekai#arcanum|Arcanum]] she could not change the face that made her the goddess of the forge. She challenges him to forge a weapon that satisfies her.[@fm08-face] Welf answers that if he makes a weapon that satisfies her, he wants her to be his.[@fm08-pledge] In DanMachi 15 she judges his magic sword [[equipment#shikou-kazuki|Shikou Kazuki]], which DanMachi 14 calls an imitation of her own work.[@fm15-judged, fm14-imitation] Pleased with it, she begins to keep her promise, "As promised, I will go out with y—", but Welf cuts her off, swearing to craft a blade she will fully approve of, and leaves her fuming.[@fm15-promise]

## Other appearances

In DanMachi 11 she asks her captain, [[tsubaki|Tsubaki]], to help Welf and [[hestia-familia|Hestia Familia]] rescue the [[xenos|Xenos]].[@fm11-tsubaki] When Freya's charm over the city breaks in DanMachi 17, a wrathful Hephaistos leads almost all of her Familia to surround [[folkvangr|Folkvangr]] from the south and west while [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]] takes the east and north, and swears that Freya will pay.[@fm17-siege] In the war game of DanMachi 18 she hides with Hestia; when Freya Familia closes in, she sends Hestia on alone and goes another way as a decoy, so that at least one of them stays in the game.[@fm18-decoy]
