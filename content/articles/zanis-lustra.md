**Zanis Lustra** {{small|printed *Zanis Rustra* in DanMachi 10}} is [[soma-familia|Soma Familia]]'s Level 2 captain, who exploits its members before losing his position and being imprisoned.[@fm06-rank, fm06-control, fm10-punishment] His title is Gandharva, the Wine-Guardian.[@fm06-rank] It reflects a mind strong enough that the divine wine cannot manipulate him. He wears glasses on a narrow, chiseled face, and his sharp black eyes put on an air of intelligence that his vulgar smile betrays (the English prints "narrow black eyes").[@fm06-looks] He takes advantage of [[soma|Soma]]'s neglect of leadership, using the god's name to manipulate members for his own gain.[@fm06-control]
