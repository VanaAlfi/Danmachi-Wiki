**Ares Familia** is the Familia of [[ares|Ares]], the God of War: the [[#kingdom-of-rakia|Kingdom of Rakia]], a monarchy in the west of the main continent that is in truth his Familia.[@fm08-rakia, so09-rakia] Rakia's armies once won war after war with [[magic-sword|Crozzo magic swords]], and its wars burned the forests of the elves.[@fm04-lyu, so05-villages] Rakia has attacked [[orario|Orario]] many times and lost every time.[@so09-sixth, fm08-terms] The Sixth Orario Invasion, which Ares leads himself, is described on [[ares#sixth-orario-invasion|Ares]].

## Kingdom of Rakia {#kingdom-of-rakia}

[[rakia|Rakia]] is a monarchy on the western side of the main continent, with at least 600,000 people. A great castle stands at the centre of its largest settlement, the land is fertile, and it is a military state with a barbarous side.[@fm08-rakia] Its capital is Barva.[@fm15-ball, fm15-light]

In reality the kingdom is a Familia on the scale of a country. Every soldier has received a Falna, the subjects who run its industries are its noncombatants, and Ares, its one and only [[patron-deity|patron god]], has chosen every king in its history. The country grew from Ares and a handful of followers.[@fm08-rakia] Sword Oratoria 9 calls it "nominally a militant monarchy", headed in fact by Ares and Ares Familia.[@so09-rakia] [[loki|Loki]] compares [[kali-familia|Kali]]'s rule of [[telskyura|Telskyura]] to "Ares and Rakia", a nation-state Familia.[@so06-kali]

DanMachi 15 explains how such a Familia works. Rakia's soldiers and knights number close to a hundred thousand, too many for Ares to bless and update alone, so defeated gods serve him as vassal-gods. The goddess [[phobos|Phobos]], who lost a defensive war to Rakia, became one, and the fallen Crozzo family are members of Phobos Familia.[@fm15-vassal] To prevent rebellion, all of Rakia's real military strength, its Level 2 and Level 3 knights and officers and the royal guard, stays under Ares's direct control; anyone the vassal-gods raise to strength or cleverness is made to convert into Ares Familia.[@fm15-vassal] DanMachi 19 calls this vassal system common in Rakia and other country-level Familias.[@fm19-vassal]

### Wars and the Crozzo magic swords

Rakia has been the aggressor in many wars over the centuries.[@fm08-rakia] In DanMachi 4 [[lyu-leon|Lyu]] tells Bell its history. The Crozzo family made its magic swords in exchange for nobility, and an army of common soldiers armed with them became "a mobile inferno". The wars turned every battlefield into scorched earth, and finally their flames burned down a forest of the elves. The surviving elves joined Familias abroad and took their revenge once Rakia no longer had the swords.[@fm04-lyu] Sword Oratoria 5 says the records describe Rakia using Crozzo swords even stronger than ordinary magic.[@so05-records] [[geography#ruins-near-orario-shreme-castle-ruins|Shreme Castle]], the [[war-game|War Game]] site of DanMachi 6, was long a Rakian strategic point, until more than a century ago.[@fm06-shreme]

## Marius Victrix Rakia {#marius-victrix-rakia}

Marius is the crown prince of Rakia, first son of King Martinus, and Ares Familia's young second-in-command. He is twenty, with honey-coloured hair, 180 celch tall and of a knightly bearing.[@fm08-marius, fm15-ball] He was brought on the invasion for battle experience.[@fm08-marius] He blames his god for the nickname his father earned, "Moronic King", and threatens to give up his right of succession and become an adventurer in Orario. He is the only one in the war council who dares tell Ares that the war is pointless.[@fm08-marius]

In Sword Oratoria 9, when Ares rails at Orario's tactics, Marius reminds him that Rakia had prepared artillery for a trap of its own. When Ares begs him to do something, he orders the retreat in a practised, resigned way.[@so09-lefiya] In the Beor Mountains he throws a [[metals#mythril|mythril]] chain around Asfi's arm to keep her from escaping. She tells him he seems to have "a decent head on your shoulders" for the son of the Moronic King, and pities him: both serve whimsical gods.[@fm08-chain] After Ares is captured, a fed-up Marius meets the army on its march home.[@fm08-terms]

DanMachi 15 shows him years earlier, at a royal ball in Barva, stifling a laugh while young Welf beats up three noble boys. Later he has spies watch the Crozzos for the threats his father and Ares ignore.[@fm15-ball, fm15-throne]

> [!INFERENCE] Phobos and the pillar of light
> In DanMachi 15 Phobos gives young Welf his first and last Status update from her and helps him leave Rakia, telling him to say she put him up to it; she says she no longer cares if she is sent away from the mortal plane. Several days later a huge pillar of light rises from the direction of Barva, and Welf, outside Rakia's borders, silently apologises and thanks her.[@fm15-light] The light appears to mark Phobos's return to Heaven, but the passage does not say so.[@fm15-light]
