**Ouranos** is the god who founded [[guild|the Guild]] and remains its true leader.[@so02-ouranos, fm09-ouranos] One of the first gods to descend, he helped the people of the mortal realm complete the "lid" over the Great Hole and was the first god to bestow Falna; he is worshipped as Orario's founding deity and called the "Father of Orario".[@so02-ouranos-founding, fm09-ouranos-father] He takes a stance of "reigning without governing": he gave the Guild's employees no Blessing, so that it has political power but no force of its own, and "Ouranos Familia simply didn't exist".[@so02-ouranos-reign] His continuous prayers restrain [[dungeon|the Dungeon]] and keep it from releasing monsters en masse.[@so02-ouranos, fm09-ouranos] Zeus and Hera Familia shared a special relationship with him; with them gone, he says, the military forces at his command are limited.[@ar03-ouranos-zeus, fm11-ouranos-forces] In secret he supports the [[xenos|Xenos]] and hopes to build a bridge to coexistence, working through his agent [[fels|Fels]].[@fm09-ouranos, fm09-fels]

He is an aged god about two meders tall, with blue eyes, who sits on a throne lit by four torches in the Chamber of Prayers beneath Guild Headquarters, also called the Temple of Ouranos; he stays there because he prays to the Dungeon without pause.[@so04-ouranos-look, fm09-ouranos-chamber, so05-ouranos-torches, so03-ouranos-temple, so02-ouranos-cooped]

## The Dungeon

In Sword Oratoria 5 his prayers stop reaching the Dungeon; he guesses that another god has entered it, and tells Fels that the Dungeon is unstable and, with [[zeus|Zeus]] gone, seems to be changing.[@so05-prayers] In DanMachi 13 he identifies the [[juggernaut|Juggernaut]] as the Dungeon's immune response, the same calamity that destroyed [[astrea-familia|Astrea Familia]] five years earlier.[@fm13-juggernaut] Though the Guild's records do not mention the monster, Ouranos named it the Juggernaut, and he had kept this Dungeon taboo secret, because any regulation would have revealed that something was there.[@fm13-ouranos-name, fm13-ouranos-taboo] His explanation of how monsters are reborn is presented as his theory.[@fm09-theory]

## The Xenos

Ouranos and Fels support the Xenos; [[hermes|Hermes]] and [[ganesha|Ganesha]] know of the plan, while most of the Guild, including its head, [[royman|Royman]], does not.[@fm09-network] After the fighting of DanMachi 10–11, he spreads a false story that [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]] exterminated the monsters.[@fm11-cover] In Sword Oratoria 10 he agrees with [[loki|Loki]] to keep the Xenos secret, in exchange for not exploiting [[finn-deimne|Finn]]'s choices.[@so10-compact] In DanMachi 14 he sends the Xenos to rescue Hestia Familia's rear guard at the Water Capital, and then, through Fels, on to [[floor-37|Floor 37]], where they and the search party reach [[bell-cranell|Bell]] and [[lyu-leon|Lyu]].[@fm14-mission, fm14-ouranos-floor37] Fels tells Bell that clearing the Dungeon's deepest floor is needed for coexistence, without explaining why; Bell takes it as a Will that most likely comes from Ouranos.[@fm12-deepest]
