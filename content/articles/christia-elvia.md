**Christia Elvia**, called **Chris**, is a Level 2 prum in the [[balder#third-squad|3rd Squad]], who fights with a two-handed sword and develops a scouting role.[@fm19-introduction, fm19-weapon, fm19-disaster] Chris introduces himself as Christia Elvia and asks to be called Chris.[@fm19-introduction] A Level 2 prum with blue hair and a boy's uniform, Chris confidently declares an intention to surpass [[finn-deimne|Finn Deimne]].[@fm19-introduction] Meeting him, [[bell-cranell|Bell Cranell]] first wonders whether Chris is a boy or a girl, and thinks him a boy.[@fm19-introduction]
