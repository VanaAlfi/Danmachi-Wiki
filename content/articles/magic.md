**Magic**, in the sense of spells, is one of the things a god's [[falna|Falna]] can record in a follower's [[status|Status]]. A spell appears in the Status with its name and a short description, and Hestia explains that the caster learns its incantation from the Status. Some of the Status sheets printed in the novels include the chant; others do not.[@fm02-trigger, fm15-cards] Casting uses Mind, the caster's mental energy.[@fm02-minddown]

"Magic" is also the name of one of the five basic abilities in a Status. That ability measures magical power and makes spells stronger as it rises, but it is not a spell itself.[@fm03-ability]

## Innate and acquired magic

A book Bell reads in DanMachi 2 divides magic into two kinds. **Innate** magic belongs to certain races by birth and is trained from childhood through rituals; it is limited in type but often strong. **Acquired** magic comes with the Falna, takes almost any form, and depends heavily on the person's excelia.[@fm02-kinds] The same book says that "Magic is interest": what a person cares about, hates or longs for shapes the magic their Falna produces.[@fm02-kinds]

Magic can also be forced into a Status by a **grimoire**, a book that makes its reader learn magic. Only someone who has mastered the Development Abilities Magic Control and Enigma can make one, and it can be used only once.[@fm02-grimoire] [[bell-cranell|Bell]] learns [[#firebolt|Firebolt]] this way, and [[haruhime|Haruhime]]'s [[#kokonoe|Kokonoe]] also comes from a grimoire.[@fm02-grimoire, fm12-grimoire]

## How many spells

> [!NOTE] The novels describe spell slots in different ways
> - DanMachi 1: a person can use up to three kinds of magic; one is common, two is valued. Bell sees only **one** magic slot in his own Status.[@fm01-three]
> - Sword Oratoria 1: **every** Status has exactly three spell slots.[@so01-slots]
> - Sword Oratoria 2: a good grimoire can also **add** a slot, up to an absolute maximum of three.[@so02-grimoire]
> - Sword Oratoria 14: [[riveria|Riveria]]'s chant concatenation gives her nine types of magic from her slots.[@so14-nine]
>
> All sources agree on three spell slots as the ceiling for ordinary casters. They disagree on whether everyone starts with three; this wiki records both statements.

## Incantations

In DanMachi 2 [[hestia|Hestia]] explains that magic normally needs a spoken incantation, which builds a "launchpad" for the spell. A longer incantation makes a bigger, more powerful spell; a shorter one is weaker but can be cast almost instantly. A person learns their incantation by reading it in their Status.[@fm02-trigger] Lilly adds that long-chant spells are hard to use in battle, because monsters will not wait, and are therefore used less and grow more slowly.[@fm03-ability]

Most spells are cast by reciting the chant and then saying the spell's name, as with [[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz]]'s [[#airiel|Airiel]]: "Awaken, Tempest." then "Airiel."[@so01-airiel] The novels show several variations:

| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| No incantation at all; the name alone fires the spell | [[#firebolt|Firebolt]][@fm02-trigger] |
| Very short chant | [[#will-o-the-wisp|Will-o'-the-Wisp]], [[#hell-finegas|Hell Finegas]][@so12-supershort, so04-ultrashort] |
| Long or extremely long chant | [[#rea-laevateinn|Rea Laevateinn]], [[#dia-orpheus|Dia Orpheus]][@so01-long, fm10-long] |
| Name first, then the chant | [[#kokonoe|Kokonoe]], which "began not with a chant but with the announcement of the spell's name"[@fm12-grimoire] |
| Separate chants to start and end the spell | [[#cinder-ella|Cinder Ella]][@fm15-cards] |
| An extra key word after the spell is cast | "Rubele" detonates [[#aro-zephyros|Aro Zephyros]]; "Dance!" sends out Kokonoe's tails[@fm06-rubele, fm12-grimoire] |
| A second spell's chant after the first | [[#elf-ring|Elf Ring]], followed by the summoned spell's chant[@so01-elfring] |

### How this wiki records incantations

> [!NOTE] Recording conventions
> Spell pages on this wiki keep these categories apart:
>
> - **Full chant:** the text shows the chant from beginning to end, for example by saying the spell was complete. Even then, a long chant split by narration may not be printed in full, and the page says so.
> - **Partial print:** only some lines are printed, often the last line and the name. This is not treated as a shorter version of the spell.
> - **Trigger or spell name only:** the name, or a key word such as "Rubele", is printed without the chant. This is not treated as evidence that the spell needs no chant.
> - **Chantless:** used only when the text establishes that the spell has no incantation, as with Firebolt.
> - **Unknown:** the chant is never printed. Unknown is not the same as chantless.
>
> When different volumes print a chant in different English words, each version is recorded separately with its location. Lines from different scenes are never stitched together into one "complete" chant. Long chants are quoted only in short excerpts, and every excerpt is cited to the volume and, where the evidence maps record it, the chapter where the full passage can be read.

## Mind and Mind Down

Magic consumes Mind. Using too much makes the caster pass out, which is called **Mind Down**; it is common among adventurers who have just learned magic. Mind-restoring potions help prevent it.[@fm02-minddown] The novels rarely give a Mind cost for any single spell.

## Ignis Fatuus

**Ignis Fatuus** is the explosion that results when a caster cannot control their own magic. Before the gods came, it was a real danger to anyone casting spells; with the Falna it almost never happens by accident.[@fm05-ignis] Welf's [[#will-o-the-wisp|Will-o'-the-Wisp]] deliberately causes it in enemies.[@fm05-ignis]

## Casting techniques

- **Concurrent Casting:** chanting while moving and fighting. Normally a caster must stand still and concentrate; [[lyu-leon|Lyu]] recites [[#luminous-wind|Luminous Wind]] while attacking and dodging, where one slip could cause an Ignis Fatuus.[@fm05-concurrent]
- **Concatenated Chanting:** a feature of Riveria's own magic. Each of her three types of magic has three levels, and she can join their chants to raise the output, as when [[#wynn-fimbulvetr|Wynn Fimbulvetr]] flows into [[#rea-laevateinn|Rea Laevateinn]].[@so04-concat]
- **Concatenated Casting:** the term DanMachi 12 uses for Haruhime linking two different spells, Kokonoe and [[#uchide-no-kozuchi|Uchide no Kozuchi]], and casting them one after the other.[@fm12-grimoire] It is not the same technique as Riveria's.

## Magic, Skills and Development Abilities

A Status lists spells, Skills and [[development-ability|Development Abilities]] separately, and this wiki keeps them apart.[@fm15-cards, so12-lefiya]

| | What it is | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Magic | A spell in a Magic slot, usually with an incantation | Firebolt, Airiel, Futsu no Mitama[@fm02-trigger, fm15-cards] |
| Skill | A separate ability listed under Skills; it can boost or change how spells work but is not a spell | Bell's Argonaut, which charges Firebolt; Aiz's Avenger; Lefiya's Double Cannon[@fm04-argonaut, so12-avenger, so12-lefiya] |
| Development Ability | Chosen at a Level Up; some help casting | Magic Control, which lets the caster form a magic circle for more power and better Mind efficiency; Conjure, which raises magic power, range and Mind efficiency[@fc03-control, so01-conjure] |

{{nocite}} Some passages use these words loosely. Where the Status card and the narration disagree, this wiki follows the card and records the other wording:

- Welf's Will-o'-the-Wisp is Magic on his card, but Sword Oratoria 12 once calls it "Welf's rare Skill".[@fm15-cards, so12-skill]
- Lilly's Cinder Ella is Magic on her cards, but DanMachi 9's narration calls it a "skill".[@fm15-cards, fm09-skill]
- In Sword Oratoria 8 Loki compares Bete's [[#hati|Hati]] with Tiona and Tione's "Berserk magic", though Berserk is called a Skill elsewhere in Sword Oratoria.[@so08-berserk, so07-berserk]
- Mikoto's Yatano Black Crow and White Crow, which sense enemies and allies, are listed under Skills on her DanMachi 15 card, not Magic.[@fm15-cards]

Magic swords are weapons, not spells. They do not occupy a Magic slot, though DanMachi 14 notes that Welf's new sword also drains its user's Mind.[@fm14-sword]

## Spells of Hestia Familia {#hestia-familia-spells}

### Firebolt {#firebolt}

**Firebolt** is [[bell-cranell|Bell Cranell]]'s spell. His [[status|Status]] lists it as *Swift-Strike Magic* with no incantation, and saying its name is enough to release a bolt of electric flame.[@firebolt.fm02-card, firebolt.fm02-first] It is fast but, especially early on, weak in each hit; it grows stronger the more Bell uses it.[@firebolt.fm03-growth]

- **User:** [[bell-cranell|Bell Cranell]][@firebolt.fm02-card]
- **Status entry:** Swift-Strike Magic[@firebolt.fm02-card]
- **Element:** Electric flame[@firebolt.fm02-first]
- **Source:** A grimoire (DanMachi 2)[@firebolt.fm02-grimoire]
- **Incantation:** None; the spell name alone fires it[@firebolt.fm02-card, firebolt.fm03-growth]
- **First cast:** DanMachi 2, on a goblin[@firebolt.fm02-first]

#### Acquisition {#firebolt-acquisition}

Firebolt appears on Bell's Status in DanMachi 2, after he reads a book borrowed from a friend.[@firebolt.fm02-card, firebolt.fm02-grimoire] [[hestia|Hestia]] identifies the book as a grimoire, "a book that forces the reader to learn magic", made only by someone who has mastered both Magic Control and Enigma; once used, it is worthless.[@firebolt.fm02-grimoire] See [[#innate-and-acquired-magic|Innate and acquired magic]] for how spells are normally acquired.

#### Incantation {#firebolt-incantation}

> [!NOTE] Chantless magic
> Firebolt has no incantation. It is not an unrecorded chant: the text establishes that there is none.

- Bell's first card shows the name *Firebolt* in the Magic slot, described only as Swift-Strike Magic, and nothing that looks like an incantation. Hestia guesses that it needs none and that saying "Firebolt" may trigger it, and stops him saying the name indoors.[@firebolt.fm02-card, firebolt.fm02-trigger]
- The first cast bears this out: Bell says the name and the bolt fires, "conjured in an instant".[@firebolt.fm02-first]
- [[lilliluka-erde|Lilly]] later calls it magic with "no spell" and "lightning speed", and onlookers at the Minotaur fight say they never saw him chant.[@firebolt.fm03-growth, firebolt.fm03-minotaur]

**Trigger:** the spell name, *Firebolt*, spoken aloud. The text often prints it in capitals or stretched out ("FIREBOLT!!"); these are ways of showing him shouting, not different forms of the spell.[@firebolt.fm02-first, firebolt.fm03-minotaur]

{{nocite}} **Fake incantation**

In DanMachi 19, disguised as the student Rapi, Bell pretends to chant so that his instant casting will not give him away. He borrows [[welf-crozzo|Welf]]'s chant, "Blasphemous Burn", loses control of his magic and causes an *Ignis Fatuus*.[@firebolt.fm19-fake] He uses the same fake incantation later in the volume while firing a normal Firebolt.[@firebolt.fm19-fake2] This does not mean Bell has learned Welf's spell; see [[#will-o-the-wisp|Will-o'-the-Wisp]].

#### Effects {#firebolt-effects}

The bolt is described as scarlet lightning, "electric flames", which bursts on impact.[@firebolt.fm02-first] Lilly compares it to a spell with a long chant: Firebolt trades power for speed and cannot be dodged easily, and it gets stronger with use because it can be used often. Bell notes that its bolts have grown thicker and stronger since his first cast.[@firebolt.fm03-growth]

Against a strong opponent its speed is not enough. In DanMachi 3 its hits cannot pierce the Minotaur's hide; Bell wins by driving the Hestia Knife into the monster and firing repeated Firebolts into the wound, burning it from inside.[@firebolt.fm03-minotaur] In DanMachi 6, [[hyacinthus|Hyacinthus]]'s [[#aro-zephyros|Aro Zephyros]] cuts straight through a Firebolt.[@firebolt.fm06-aro]

#### Cost and Mind Down {#firebolt-cost-and-mind-down}

Each cast uses Mind (mental energy). On his first day with the spell Bell fires it over and over, and he collapses on the fifth floor. Nahza later tells him this was *Mind Down*, common among adventurers who have just learned magic, and sells him a Mind-restoring potion.[@firebolt.fm02-collapse, firebolt.fm02-minddown] The Mind cost of a single shot is not given.

#### Charged Firebolt {#firebolt-charged-firebolt}

Bell's Skill Argonaut charges an action with power before it is released; it is a Skill, not part of the spell. In DanMachi 4 an Argonaut-charged Firebolt kills an infant dragon and smashes the Dungeon wall behind it.[@firebolt.fm04-argonaut] From DanMachi 12 he charges Firebolt into his knife and releases it as the technique *Argo Vesta*, after sixty seconds of charging against the enhanced moss huge.[@firebolt.fm12-argo] In DanMachi 13, facing the [[juggernaut|Juggernaut]], whose shell reflects magic, he fires seventeen Firebolts as cover, lets them be reflected, and catches one in his knife for a Dual Charge.[@firebolt.fm13-reflect]

### Cinder Ella {#cinder-ella}

**Cinder Ella** is the magic of [[lilliluka-erde|Lilliluka Erde]]. It changes her body into the form she clearly pictures while casting, and it has two chants: one to transform and one to change back.[@cinder-ella.fm08-card, cinder-ella.fm15-card] She uses it first to escape and steal as a supporter, and later as a tool of deception for [[hestia-familia|Hestia Familia]].[@cinder-ella.fm15-origin, cinder-ella.fm06-luan]

- **User:** [[lilliluka-erde|Lilliluka Erde]][@cinder-ella.fm08-card, cinder-ella.fm15-card]
- **Status entry:** Shape-shifting magic[@cinder-ella.fm08-card, cinder-ella.fm15-card]
- **Limit:** Cannot give her a higher Status than her own[@cinder-ella.fm11-limits]
- **Activation:** "Your scars are mine. My scars are mine."[@cinder-ella.fm08-card, cinder-ella.fm15-card]
- **Release:** "Stroke of midnight’s bell."[@cinder-ella.fm08-card, cinder-ella.fm15-card]

#### Status entry {#cinder-ella-status-entry}

Lilly's Status cards in DanMachi 8 and DanMachi 15 list Cinder Ella under Magic with the same content in slightly different words:[@cinder-ella.fm08-card, cinder-ella.fm15-card]

| DanMachi 8 | DanMachi 15 |
|---|---|
| Shape-shifting Magic[@cinder-ella.fm08-card] | Shape-shifting magic[@cinder-ella.fm15-card] |
| The target takes the envisioned shape; fails without a clear image[@cinder-ella.fm08-card] | The target assumes the form envisioned when casting; fails without a clear image[@cinder-ella.fm15-card] |
| Imitation is recommended[@cinder-ella.fm08-card] | Mimicking an existing form is recommended[@cinder-ella.fm15-card] |
| **Trigger Spell** and **Release Spell**[@cinder-ella.fm08-card] | **Activation chant** and **Deactivation chant**[@cinder-ella.fm15-card] |

#### Incantation {#cinder-ella-incantation}

{{nocite}} Both chants are short and printed identically on both cards, so they are given in full.

| Chant | Wording | Printed |

### Will-o'-the-Wisp {#will-o-the-wisp}

**Will-o'-the-Wisp** is [[welf-crozzo|Welf Crozzo]]'s spell, which the narration calls "anti-magic Magic".[@will-o-the-wisp.fm05-first] When an enemy gathers magical power for a spell or a magical attack, Welf's very short chant makes that power explode, an *Ignis Fatuus*. The stronger the enemy's attack, or the higher their Magic ability, the bigger the blast.[@will-o-the-wisp.fm05-explain]

- **User:** [[welf-crozzo|Welf Crozzo]][@will-o-the-wisp.fm05-first]
- **Status entry:** Anti-magic fire[@will-o-the-wisp.fm15-card]
- **Effect:** Forces an Ignis Fatuus in a target using magic power[@will-o-the-wisp.fm05-explain]
- **Chant:** Blasphemous Burn (very short)[@will-o-the-wisp.fm05-first, will-o-the-wisp.fm15-card]
- **Printed as:** Will-o'-the-Wisp (DanMachi 5); Will-o-Wisp (DanMachi 15, 18; Sword Oratoria 12)[@will-o-the-wisp.fm05-first, will-o-the-wisp.fm15-card, will-o-the-wisp.fm18-hegni, will-o-the-wisp.so12-spirit]

### Futsu no Mitama {#futsu-no-mitama}

**Futsu no Mitama** is the magic of [[mikoto|Mikoto Yamato]]. When her chant is complete, a sword of violet light descends and a dome of increased gravity forms around its target, forcing everything inside to the ground.[@futsu-no-mitama.fm05-goliath, futsu-no-mitama.fm15-card] The field does not spare Mikoto if she stands inside it, and holding it against something stronger than her hurts her badly.[@futsu-no-mitama.fm06-war, futsu-no-mitama.fm14-second]

- **User:** [[mikoto|Mikoto Yamato]][@futsu-no-mitama.fm05-goliath, futsu-no-mitama.fm15-card]
- **Status entry:** Gravity magic; increases gravity within a certain area[@futsu-no-mitama.fm15-card]
- **Maximum range:** Fifty meders in every direction (DanMachi 6)[@futsu-no-mitama.fm06-war]
- **Chant:** Seven printed pieces (DanMachi 6), ending "Shinbu Tousei"[@futsu-no-mitama.fm06-war, futsu-no-mitama.fm14-second]
- **Printed as:** Futsu no Mitama; Futsu no Tama (DanMachi 6)[@futsu-no-mitama.fm05-goliath, futsu-no-mitama.fm06-war, futsu-no-mitama.fm15-card]

#### Name {#futsu-no-mitama-name}

The spell is *Futsu no Mitama* in DanMachi 5, on her DanMachi 15 Status card and in later volumes. DanMachi 6 prints *Futsu no Tama*, a printed variant of the same spell, not a second one.[@futsu-no-mitama.fm05-goliath, futsu-no-mitama.fm06-war, futsu-no-mitama.fm15-card]

#### Status entry {#futsu-no-mitama-status-entry}

Mikoto's DanMachi 15 card lists Futsu no Mitama under **Magic**: gravity magic that increases gravity within a certain area. Her detection abilities Yatano Black Crow and Yatano White Crow are listed under **Skills** on the same card, not as magic.[@futsu-no-mitama.fm15-card]

#### Effects {#futsu-no-mitama-effects}

- **Gravity dome.** A force field drops from the sword's hilt ten meders above the Black [[goliath|Goliath]] and forms a dome that drives it to its knees.[@futsu-no-mitama.fm05-goliath]
- **Area.** In the DanMachi 6 [[war-game|War Game]] it spreads fifty meders in every direction, its maximum range, trapping twenty-two enemies and Mikoto herself. Anything that touches its outer layer, physical or magical, is forced to the ground.[@futsu-no-mitama.fm06-war]
- **Reach.** Against the [[amphisbaena|Amphisbaena]] she places the sword at maximum range so that the gravity pulls down the tree-root dome above the monster.[@futsu-no-mitama.fm14-first] Later she chants underwater and pins the dragon, though the gravity crushes her too.[@futsu-no-mitama.fm14-second]
- **Deflection.** In Sword Oratoria 12, boosted by [[haruhime|Haruhime]]'s Level Boost, her field bends a demi-spirit's blast of light into the floor. She cannot cancel the spell itself, only change its path.[@futsu-no-mitama.so12-shield]
- **Focus.** In DanMachi 18 she narrows it to the smallest possible area to cage the [[gulliver-brothers|Gulliver brothers]]; Berling eventually breaks out.[@futsu-no-mitama.fm18-cage]

#### Limits {#futsu-no-mitama-limits}

- Its strength depends on Mikoto's Status. The Black Goliath slowly rises against it, and she cannot push it back down.[@futsu-no-mitama.fm05-goliath]
- She is inside the field if she casts it around herself, and in DanMachi 14 it breaks her fingers and injures her while she holds it.[@futsu-no-mitama.fm06-war, futsu-no-mitama.fm14-second]
- Takemikazuchi had all but forbidden her to use it in the Dungeon's closed spaces, because it could catch her allies.[@futsu-no-mitama.fm05-goliath]

### Uchide no Kozuchi {#uchide-no-kozuchi}

**Uchide no Kozuchi**, usually called **Level Boost**, is [[haruhime|Haruhime]]'s magic. It temporarily raises another person's [[level|Level]]; in every case shown, the target fights with the power of one Level higher.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-named, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm11-rescue, uchide-no-kozuchi.so12-five] It affects one person at a time and cannot be cast on Haruhime herself.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm15-limits]

- **Caster:** [[haruhime|Haruhime]][@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-named]
- **Known as:** Level Boost[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-named, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm09-duration]
- **Effect:** Raises one person's [[level|Level]] for a limited time[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-named, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm15-limits]
- **Target:** One person, not Haruhime herself[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm15-limits]
- **Chant:** Two lines with the refrain "Grow"[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-chant, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm12-kokonoe]
- **Duration:** Up to 15 minutes, given enough Mind[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm09-duration, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm15-limits]
- **Recast:** About 10 minutes[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm11-rescue, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm15-limits]
- **Duration:** 20 minutes (DanMachi 18)[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm18-level2]
- **Recast:** 9 minutes (DanMachi 18)[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm18-level2]
- **First:** Sword Oratoria 6 (unnamed); named in DanMachi 7[@uchide-no-kozuchi.so06-phryne, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-named]

#### Name {#uchide-no-kozuchi-name}

The spell is named *Uchide no Kozuchi* from DanMachi 7 onward; *Level Boost* is the name used for its effect in DanMachi 7–9, and DanMachi 14 writes it in lower case.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-named, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm09-duration, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm14-boost] Sword Oratoria 6 shows the spell without naming it.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.so06-phryne]

{{nocite}} The chant is printed with the same wording in every full print. Only a short excerpt is quoted here; the references give the locations of the full passages.

- **Structure:** Haruhime begins with "—Grow.", recites two lines, each followed by "Grow", and then names the spell.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-chant, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm12-kokonoe]

As she chants, a cloud of golden light forms above her and becomes a hammer of light, which falls on the target. DanMachi 11 notes that she forms no magic circle, a sign of the spell's peculiar nature.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-chant, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm11-hammer] Casting it through Kokonoe is described as linking two spells' chants: DanMachi 12 calls this "Concatenated Casting", and DanMachi 18 "chained casting".[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm12-kokonoe, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm18-chant]

#### Effects {#uchide-no-kozuchi-effects}

{{inference}} Every boost shown lifts the target by exactly one Level, though no passage states this as a rule.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.so06-phryne, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-named, uchide-no-kozuchi.so12-five] In DanMachi 7 it raises [[bell-cranell|Bell]] from Level 3 to Level 4 power, and gives [[aisha-belka|Aisha]] Level-4-class strength.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-named] In Sword Oratoria 12 it lifts Level 4 adventurers to pseudo-Level 5 and [[bete-loga|Bete]] from Level 6 to pseudo-Level 7.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.so12-five]

Once cast, the boost lasts until its time runs out or Haruhime ends it; knocking her unconscious does not stop it.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm07-chant]

The boost is temporary and does not count as a Level Up. In DanMachi 18, [[anya-fromel|Anya Fromel]], a Level 4, fights at pseudo-Level 5 during the [[war-game|War Game]] only.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm18-anya]

Fighting under the boost costs the target experience: they receive less than half the normal excelia, while Haruhime's own development is not reduced by casting it.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm15-excelia]

#### Limits {#uchide-no-kozuchi-limits}

The limits changed as Haruhime grew. At Level 1 a boost lasts up to fifteen minutes if she supplies enough Mind, and she must wait about ten minutes before casting it again.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm09-duration, uchide-no-kozuchi.fm15-limits] Because the wait is shorter than the effect, a second person can be boosted for about five minutes while the first boost is still running; this does not show that two boosts stack on one person.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm15-limits]

After Haruhime reaches Level 2, before the War Game in DanMachi 18, a boost lasts twenty minutes and the wait falls to nine minutes.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm18-level2]

In DanMachi 20, [[nina-tulle|Nina]]'s [[#magia-kreis|Magia Kreis]] extends a boost from twenty minutes to about twenty-five. That is an outside enhancement, not a change to the spell itself.[@uchide-no-kozuchi.fm20-kreis]

### Kokonoe {#kokonoe}

**Kokonoe** is [[haruhime|Haruhime]]'s second spell. It gives her tails of light and charges them with the effect of the spell she chants straight afterwards, so that she can release that spell as many times as she has tails with a single effort of will.[@kokonoe.fm12-kokonoe] With [[#uchide-no-kozuchi|Uchide no Kozuchi]], this lets her give Level Boosts to several people at once.[@kokonoe.fm12-kokonoe]

- **User:** [[haruhime|Haruhime]][@kokonoe.fm12-kokonoe]
- **Type:** Enchantment, listed under Magic[@kokonoe.fm15-card]
- **First used:** DanMachi 12[@kokonoe.fm12-kokonoe]
- **Source:** A grimoire[@kokonoe.fm12-chant]
- **Chant:** Name first, then five printed pieces[@kokonoe.fm12-chant, kokonoe.fm18-chant]
- **Maximum:** Nine[@kokonoe.fm12-kokonoe, kokonoe.fm15-card]
- **Usable:** Five in DanMachi 12; six at Level 2[@kokonoe.fm12-kokonoe, kokonoe.fm18-six]

#### Status entry {#kokonoe-status-entry}

Haruhime's DanMachi 15 card lists Kokonoe under Magic, beside Uchide no Kozuchi. It describes the spell as an enchantment that connects with a chant, charges tails with the effect of the connected spell, and allows up to nine tails.[@kokonoe.fm15-card]

#### Incantation {#kokonoe-incantation}

{{nocite}} Kokonoe's chant is printed in full twice, with the same wording. Only a short excerpt is quoted here; the references give the locations of the full passages.

- **Name first:** unusually, the spell "began not with a chant but with the announcement of the spell's name": Haruhime says "Kokonoe." and then chants.[@kokonoe.fm12-chant]
- **Excerpt (first piece):** "Beloved snow. Beloved crimson. Beloved white light."[@kokonoe.fm12-chant, kokonoe.fm18-chant]

- **Linking:** the last piece runs straight into the "—Grow." that begins [[#uchide-no-kozuchi|Uchide no Kozuchi]]'s chant. When that spell is complete, "Dance!" sends the charged tails out to their targets.[@kokonoe.fm12-chant, kokonoe.fm18-chant]

| DanMachi 12 | The full sequence: name, five pieces, Uchide no Kozuchi's chant, "Uchide no Kozuchi!", "Dance!"[@kokonoe.fm12-chant] |

## Spells of Loki Familia {#loki-familia-spells}

### Airiel {#airiel}

**Airiel** is [[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz Wallenstein]]'s magic, and her only spell. It is an enchantment: currents of wind gather around her body and sword, raising her attack power and speed and protecting her.[@airiel.so01-airiel, airiel.fm10-airiel] Aiz recognises it as her mother's wind.[@airiel.so09-mother]

- **User:** [[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz Wallenstein]][@airiel.so01-airiel]
- **Type:** Wind enchantment[@airiel.so01-airiel, airiel.fm10-airiel]
- **First cast:** At age seven, on Floor 12 (Sword Oratoria 9 recollection)[@airiel.so09-first]
- **Chant:** Awaken, Tempest (short)[@airiel.so01-airiel]
- **Stronger form:** Rage, Tempest[@airiel.so02-rage, airiel.so07-rage]

#### Incantation {#airiel-incantation}

{{nocite}} Airiel's chant is a short phrase, so every printed form can be given in full. The forms below are recorded separately; this wiki does not combine them into one "complete" chant.

| Form | Wording | Where it appears |
|---|---|---|
| Short chant | "Awaken, Tempest." | Aiz's usual activation. In Sword Oratoria 1 the narration calls it her short spell and she then names the spell, "Airiel."[@airiel.so01-airiel] The same words recur throughout Sword Oratoria and in DanMachi 10.[@airiel.so01-sense, airiel.so02-trigger, airiel.fm10-airiel] |
| Intensified | "Rage, Tempest!" | Sends Airiel's full power down her sword in Sword Oratoria 2.[@airiel.so02-rage] In Sword Oratoria 7 she calls "Awaken, Tempest" twice and then "RAGE, TEMPEST!!", producing a wind stronger than any before.[@airiel.so07-rage] |
| Single word | "Tempest!" | Printed alone when Aiz unleashes Airiel in Sword Oratoria 11 and in the final fight with [[levis|Levis]] in Sword Oratoria 12.[@airiel.so11-tempest, airiel.so12-white] |
| Combined | "Tempest" / "Avenger." | In Sword Oratoria 12 the narration calls this "the forbidden chant" that joins her Skill Avenger to Airiel.[@airiel.so12-avenger] |

The spell name itself is sometimes spoken after the chant (Sword Oratoria 1) or shouted as the wind strikes (Sword Oratoria 9).[@airiel.so01-airiel, airiel.so09-first] The single-word "Tempest!" prints are best read as shortened or narrated activations; the text does not say whether the rest of the chant was skipped.

{{nocite}} **Lil Rafaga**

*Lil Rafaga* is not a separate chant or spell. It is the name of Aiz's finishing move, a charge along the wind; she calls it out because [[loki|Loki]] joked that shouting a skill's name makes it stronger.[@airiel.so01-rafaga]

#### Effects {#airiel-effects}

- **Speed and attack.** Airiel lets her deliver two slashes in the time of one, and a borrowed blade is pushed to breaking point by it.[@airiel.so01-twoslash] Later in Sword Oratoria 1 another weapon falls to pieces under Airiel and her aggressive fighting.[@airiel.so01-blade]
- **Protection.** Its wind armour turns aside blood and, in her first use, a wyvern's fire.[@airiel.fm10-airiel, airiel.so09-first]
- **Sensing.** On the surface, where wind can flow freely, it spreads out and gives her a wide sense of space, letting her find monsters quickly.[@airiel.so01-sense]
- **Scale.** In Sword Oratoria 7 the intensified wind fills the labyrinth and its passages.[@airiel.so07-rage]

#### Costs and limits {#airiel-costs-and-limits}

Holding Airiel at full power for too long exhausts her; in Sword Oratoria 2 its last traces give out mid-fight.[@airiel.so02-overuse] During training with [[ottar|Ottar]] in Sword Oratoria 11 she runs out of Mind before noon, after which the fight is purely physical.[@airiel.so11-mind] No figure is given for its cost.

#### Avenger {#airiel-avenger}

Tempest Avenger is Airiel joined with Aiz's rare Skill Avenger, a black, violent wind. In Sword Oratoria 12 the black flame nearly consumes her; after it subsides, a pure white "Tempest" carries her into the decisive exchange with Levis.[@airiel.so12-avenger, airiel.so12-white] Avenger is a Skill, not part of the spell.

#### History {#airiel-history}

In Sword Oratoria 9's recollection, seven-year-old Aiz first speaks "Awaken, Tempest!" on Floor 12 when [[riveria|Riveria]] shouts at her to call it forth, and her wind blows away a black wyvern's head.[@airiel.so09-first] In Sword Oratoria 3, Riveria reports that the red-haired woman Levis first spoke the name "Aria" just after seeing Airiel.[@airiel.so03-aria]

#### Open questions {#airiel-open-questions}

> [!UNRESOLVED] Not settled by the covered English volumes
> - Its Mind cost, and whether "Tempest!" alone is a genuine shorter trigger or just a narrated shortening.[@airiel.so11-mind, airiel.so11-tempest]

### Fusillade Fallarica {#fusillade-fallarica}

**Fusillade Fallarica** is [[lefiya|Lefiya Viridis]]'s wide-range attack spell, listed with fire element on her Status.[@fusillade-fallarica.so12-card] It rains countless flaming arrows on everything in range; in Sword Oratoria 1 it burns a room full of monsters to nothing.[@fusillade-fallarica.so01-room]

- **User:** [[lefiya|Lefiya Viridis]][@fusillade-fallarica.so12-card]
- **Status entry:** Wide-range attack magic; fire element[@fusillade-fallarica.so12-card]
- **Effect:** A rain of flaming arrows over a wide area[@fusillade-fallarica.so01-room]
- **Chant:** Several lines, printed in three pieces[@fusillade-fallarica.so01-room, fusillade-fallarica.so04-training]

#### Status entry {#fusillade-fallarica-status-entry}

Lefiya's Level 4 card in Sword Oratoria 12 lists Fusillade Fallarica under Magic: wide-range attack magic, containing fire element. The same card lists [[#arcs-ray|Arcs Ray]] and [[#elf-ring|Elf Ring]].[@fusillade-fallarica.so12-card]

#### Incantation {#fusillade-fallarica-incantation}

{{nocite}} The chant runs to several lines, so only one short excerpt is quoted here. The full chant is printed in Sword Oratoria 1 and again, with small differences, in Sword Oratoria 4.

- **Structure:** three printed pieces. The first calls on proud forest marksmen and kin to take up their bows; the second calls for the forest's torches and the fairies' flaming arrows; the third is the excerpt below.[@fusillade-fallarica.so01-room]
- **Excerpt (last line):** "Fall like rain, burn the savages to ash."[@fusillade-fallarica.so01-room]
- **Release:** "Fusillade Fallarica!"[@fusillade-fallarica.so01-room]

In Sword Oratoria 1 the narration marks the chant as complete ("Her spell complete") before she calls out to her allies and releases it.[@fusillade-fallarica.so01-room] In Sword Oratoria 4 she casts it concurrently while monsters hit her; the wording differs slightly, for example "flames" for "flame" and "nock your arrows" without "and".[@fusillade-fallarica.so04-training] Several other volumes print only the release.[@fusillade-fallarica.so09-rakia, fusillade-fallarica.so13-crowd]

#### Effects {#fusillade-fallarica-effects}

- Countless burning bolts fall from above, pierce and ignite every monster in the room, and leave a fence of fire where they miss. Nothing is left of the monsters, not even ash.[@fusillade-fallarica.so01-room]
- Casting it summons a golden magic circle at her feet, which grows more complex with each syllable. The narration ties this to her Development Ability Conjure, which increases magic strength, range and Mind efficiency.[@fusillade-fallarica.so01-room]
- It serves as long-range bombardment: in Sword Oratoria 9's opening she fires it at an advancing Rakian formation.[@fusillade-fallarica.so09-rakia] [[lyu-leon|Lyu]]'s [[#luminous-wind|Luminous Wind]] is compared to it.[@fusillade-fallarica.so05-compare]

#### Limits {#fusillade-fallarica-limits}

In a crowded melee in Sword Oratoria 13, Lefiya can use it only for her first attack, because Concurrent Casting opportunities are so limited.[@fusillade-fallarica.so13-crowd]

### Elf Ring {#elf-ring}

**Elf Ring** is the third spell of [[lefiya|Lefiya Viridis]]. It is a *Summon Burst*: after chanting it, she can chant and cast another elf's magic as if it were her own.[@elf-ring.so12-card, elf-ring.so01-summon] Because it gives her access to a whole library of spells, the gods gave her the title *Thousand Elf*.[@elf-ring.so01-summon]

- **User:** [[lefiya|Lefiya Viridis]][@elf-ring.so12-card]
- **Status entry:** Summon Burst; elves only; must know chant and effects; Mind for both spells[@elf-ring.so12-card]
- **Chant:** Five printed pieces, ending "Please—give me strength"; then the summoned spell's own chant[@elf-ring.so01-summon, elf-ring.so03-rea]
- **Sign:** Her golden magic circle turns jade[@elf-ring.so01-summon, elf-ring.so03-rea]

#### Status entry {#elf-ring-status-entry}

Lefiya's Level 4 card in Sword Oratoria 12 lists Elf Ring under Magic with four notes: Summon Burst; only able to be cast by an elf; she must know the chant and effects beforehand; and it expends Mind for this spell and the summoned magic.[@elf-ring.so12-card]

#### Incantation {#elf-ring-incantation}

{{nocite}} Elf Ring has its own chant, which is printed with the same wording in Sword Oratoria 1, 3 and 12. Only short excerpts are quoted here.

- **Structure:** five printed pieces. It opens "I beseech the name of Wishe!", calls on the ancestors of the forest and the bonds of paradise, and reaches "Come, ring of fairies." before a final plea for strength.[@elf-ring.so01-summon, elf-ring.so03-rea, elf-ring.so12-final]
- **Release:** "Elf Ring."[@elf-ring.so01-summon, elf-ring.so03-rea]
- **Then:** the chant of the spell being summoned, which Lefiya must recite in full. In Sword Oratoria 1 she goes straight on into [[riveria|Riveria]]'s [[#wynn-fimbulvetr|Wynn Fimbulvetr]].[@elf-ring.so01-summon]

Sword Oratoria 4 describes Summon Burst as needing "a two-part chant": Elf Ring's own chant and then the summoned spell's.[@elf-ring.so04-reqs] When she names Elf Ring, her golden magic circle turns jade (emerald in Sword Oratoria 3).[@elf-ring.so01-summon, elf-ring.so03-rea] In Sword Oratoria 12 the circle is described as yellow.[@elf-ring.so12-final]

#### What she can summon {#elf-ring-what-she-can-summon}

- Riveria's spells: Wynn Fimbulvetr in Sword Oratoria 1, [[#rea-laevateinn|Rea Laevateinn]] in Sword Oratoria 3 and 13, and Riveria's healing magic in Sword Oratoria 12.[@elf-ring.so01-summon, elf-ring.so03-rea, elf-ring.so12-heal, elf-ring.so13-rea]
- [[filvis|Filvis]]'s protective barrier Dio Grail, which blocks the corrupted spirit's magic attack (recalled in Sword Oratoria 5) and is turned against Filvis's own lightning in Sword Oratoria 12.[@elf-ring.so05-grail, elf-ring.so12-final]
- The ice spell of her familia colleague Alicia Forestlight, in Sword Oratoria 13.[@elf-ring.so13-alicia]

#### Requirements: a conflict between volumes {#elf-ring-requirements-a-conflict-between-volumes}

> [!NOTE] Two descriptions of what Lefiya must know
> - Sword Oratoria 1 says she can duplicate any elvish magic whose incantation she can recite **or** whose effects she fully understands, after seeing it at least once.[@elf-ring.so01-summon]
> - Sword Oratoria 4 says it requires a complete understanding of the magic's effects **and** the proper chant.[@elf-ring.so04-reqs]
> - The Sword Oratoria 12 Status card says she must know the chant **and** the effects beforehand.[@elf-ring.so12-card]
>
> This wiki follows the Status card, which agrees with Sword Oratoria 4, and records the earlier "either/or" wording rather than silently dropping it.

#### Cost and limits {#elf-ring-cost-and-limits}

Elf Ring takes a long time to cast and uses an enormous amount of Mind, since she pays for Elf Ring and then for the summoned spell.[@elf-ring.so01-summon, elf-ring.so12-card] In Sword Oratoria 3 she needs about three minutes of chanting to summon Rea Laevateinn, while allies hold off the monsters.[@elf-ring.so03-rea] In Sword Oratoria 13 she realises she had assumed every summon had to be large and powerful to be worth the cost, and starts summoning smaller spells.[@elf-ring.so13-assume] In a crowded melee later in the volume, she rules out summons with long chants altogether.[@elf-ring.so13-crowd]

Lefiya's Skill Double Cannon, which holds one finished spell in reserve while she chants another, is a separate Skill, not part of Elf Ring.[@elf-ring.so12-card]

#### Slots {#elf-ring-slots}

Sword Oratoria 1 explains that every Status has three spell slots, and that Elf Ring is Lefiya's third, special one.[@elf-ring.so01-summon] See [[#how-many-spells|How many spells]] for other statements about slots, which do not all agree.

#### Open questions {#elf-ring-open-questions}

> [!UNRESOLVED] Not settled by the covered English volumes
> - Whether she must actually have seen a spell cast, and how much Mind the Elf Ring chant itself costs.[@elf-ring.so01-summon, elf-ring.so12-card]

### Wynn Fimbulvetr {#wynn-fimbulvetr}

**Wynn Fimbulvetr** is an ice spell of [[riveria|Riveria Ljos Alf]]. It releases a freezing blast that turns its targets and the ground around them to ice.[@wynn-fimbulvetr.so01-lefiya, wynn-fimbulvetr.so14-first] It is the first spell she ever cast, in Sword Oratoria 14's account of her joining [[loki|Loki]], and its chant can be carried on into her longer fire spell, [[#rea-laevateinn|Rea Laevateinn]].[@wynn-fimbulvetr.so14-first, wynn-fimbulvetr.so04-concat] [[lefiya|Lefiya]] can also cast it by summoning it with [[#elf-ring|Elf Ring]].[@wynn-fimbulvetr.so01-lefiya]

- **User:** [[riveria|Riveria Ljos Alf]][@wynn-fimbulvetr.so01-riveria, wynn-fimbulvetr.so14-first]
- **Also cast by:** [[lefiya|Lefiya Viridis]], through [[#elf-ring|Elf Ring]][@wynn-fimbulvetr.so01-lefiya]
- **Effect:** Freezing wind and ice[@wynn-fimbulvetr.so01-lefiya, wynn-fimbulvetr.so14-first]
- **Chant:** Two printed pieces, ending "My name is Alf"[@wynn-fimbulvetr.so01-riveria, wynn-fimbulvetr.so01-lefiya]
- **Continues into:** [[#rea-laevateinn|Rea Laevateinn]] (concatenated chant)[@wynn-fimbulvetr.so04-concat]

#### Incantation {#wynn-fimbulvetr-incantation}

{{nocite}} The chant is two printed pieces long. Only a short excerpt is quoted; the complete prints are listed in the references.

- **Excerpt (opening):** "Harbinger of the end, white snow. Gust before the twilight."[@wynn-fimbulvetr.so01-riveria, wynn-fimbulvetr.so01-lefiya, wynn-fimbulvetr.so14-first]
- **Second piece:** it continues with fading light, freezing land and "the third harsh winter", and ends with the elven formula "—My name is Alf!"[@wynn-fimbulvetr.so01-riveria, wynn-fimbulvetr.so01-lefiya]

| Print | Ending of the second piece |
|---|---|

#### First cast {#wynn-fimbulvetr-first-cast}

In Sword Oratoria 14's founding-era story, Loki updates Riveria's first Status and tells her to repeat the words after her. Riveria speaks the chant for the first time and the spell freezes the royal knights around her; their commander is left covered in ice except for his face.[@wynn-fimbulvetr.so14-first] Loki then tells her she has manifested an "incantation concatenation" and that her slots hold not three but nine types of magic.[@wynn-fimbulvetr.so14-concat]

#### Concatenated chanting {#wynn-fimbulvetr-concatenated-chanting}

Riveria's magic has three types, offensive, defensive and healing, each with three levels of chant. By joining the chants of successive levels she raises the output and changes the effect, which gives her nine spells and her title Nine Hell.[@wynn-fimbulvetr.so04-concat] In Sword Oratoria 4 she chants Wynn Fimbulvetr, links it with "advent of the end" and carries on into Rea Laevateinn; the narration calls this the move from ice to flame and from short to long.[@wynn-fimbulvetr.so04-concat] This is a feature of Riveria's own magic: Sword Oratoria 4 says it is possessed "solely" by her.[@wynn-fimbulvetr.so04-concat]

#### Effects {#wynn-fimbulvetr-effects}

- Lefiya's summoned cast in Sword Oratoria 1 freezes three giant flower monsters solid and turns the street around them to ice.[@wynn-fimbulvetr.so01-lefiya]
- In Sword Oratoria 6 Riveria narrows it and extends it into a bridge of sea ice several kirlos long, by focusing an immense amount of Mind.[@wynn-fimbulvetr.so06-bridge]
- In Sword Oratoria 14's founding-era story she uses it to shelter the party in a cave of ice while a cavern collapses, holding it until rescue and going beyond Mind Down.[@wynn-fimbulvetr.so14-cave]

> [!UNRESOLVED] Not settled by the covered English volumes
> - Whether "advent of the end" is part of every full cast, and the spell's Mind cost.[@wynn-fimbulvetr.so14-first, wynn-fimbulvetr.so04-concat]

- **User:** [[riveria|Riveria Ljos Alf]][@rea-laevateinn.so04-concat, rea-laevateinn.so14-first]

### Hati {#hati}

**Hati** is the magic of [[bete-loga|Bete Loga]]. It wraps his arms and legs in fire and feeds on magic and on damage: magic that touches it is absorbed and strengthens it, and the more he is hurt, the stronger it becomes.[@hati.so08-explain] Bete avoided using it for years, because it forces him to face the scars of his past.[@hati.so08-explain]

- **User:** [[bete-loga|Bete Loga]][@hati.so08-cast]
- **Type:** Fire enchantment on his arms and legs[@hati.so08-explain]
- **Effect:** Magic drain and damage drain[@hati.so08-explain]
- **Substitute:** The boots Frosvirt, a downgraded version made by [[tsubaki|Tsubaki]][@hati.so08-explain]
- **Chant:** Seven printed pieces in Sword Oratoria 8[@hati.so08-cast]

#### Incantation {#hati-incantation}

{{nocite}} Hati's chant runs to several lines. This page quotes only its first and last words and gives the locations of the full prints.

- **Opening (excerpt):** "Chained Fros, king of the wolves"[@hati.so08-cast]
- **Last line (excerpt):** "Bare your fangs—and devour all."[@hati.so08-cast]
- **Release:** "Hati."[@hati.so08-cast, hati.so12-filvis]

Sword Oratoria 8 prints the chant in seven pieces, from the opening to the last line; between them it speaks of three wounds and of rage, fate, tears and lost companions. His enemies attack him throughout, and he makes no move to dodge.[@hati.so08-cast]

Sword Oratoria 12 prints three of the middle lines again, split by other action, before the release. The wording differs slightly from Sword Oratoria 8, for example "Dry the tears!" for "And dry thy tears", and "unleash" for "release".[@hati.so08-cast, hati.so12-filvis] These are recorded as print variants, and the Sword Oratoria 12 print is partial.

#### Effects {#hati-effects}

- **Four flames.** Hati sets four enchantments of fire on his arms and legs; they have firepower of their own.[@hati.so08-explain, hati.so08-cast]
- **Magic drain.** Any magic-based attack it touches is absorbed and raises its output and destructive power.[@hati.so08-explain]
- **Damage drain.** Every injury makes it stronger, including the burns its own flames give him. Loki says it "basically has no limits", and it can grow into a pillar of fire.[@hati.so08-explain]
- **Allied magic.** In Sword Oratoria 12 [[lefiya|Lefiya]] fires [[#fusillade-fallarica|Fusillade Fallarica]] into Bete's attack on [[filvis|Filvis]]; the flames explode and, through the magic and damage drains, make his strike far stronger.[@hati.so12-filvis]

#### Limits {#hati-limits}

The drained damage does not heal him: in Sword Oratoria 8 his wounds remain however much power Hati has absorbed, and he knows he could die before reaching his enemy.[@hati.so08-wounds] Loki's "no limits" is a description of its growth, not a demonstrated absence of any limit.

#### Frosvirt {#hati-frosvirt}

Bete's usual boots, Frosvirt, were made for him by [[tsubaki|Tsubaki]] as a downgraded version of the spell, because he refused to use his own magic. Before Sword Oratoria 8 his companions had seen Hati only once, on an expedition five years earlier.[@hati.so08-explain]

|---|---|

| Sword Oratoria 12 | Against Filvis, boosted by Lefiya's Fusillade Fallarica.[@hati.so12-filvis] |

### Aro Zephyros {#aro-zephyros}

**Aro Zephyros** is the magic of [[hyacinthus|Hyacinthus]], Apollo Familia's general in the War Game. After a chant, a ring of fire the size of his body forms between his hands; he throws it like a discus, and the burning disk homes in on its target. A second word, *Rubele*, makes it explode.[@aro-zephyros.fm06-duel, aro-zephyros.fm06-rubele] It is shown once, in his duel with [[bell-cranell|Bell]] at the end of the DanMachi 6 [[war-game|War Game]].[@aro-zephyros.fm06-duel]

- **User:** [[hyacinthus|Hyacinthus]][@aro-zephyros.fm06-duel]
- **Effect:** A homing disk of fire, the size of a torso[@aro-zephyros.fm06-duel]
- **Chant:** Four printed pieces[@aro-zephyros.fm06-duel]
- **Detonation key:** Rubele[@aro-zephyros.fm06-rubele]

#### Incantation {#aro-zephyros-incantation}

{{nocite}} DanMachi 6 prints the chant in four pieces, interrupted by the fight. Only short excerpts are quoted.

- **Excerpt (first printed line):** "My name is love, child of light. Glorious son, I offer you my body!"[@aro-zephyros.fm06-duel]
- A second line, also beginning "My name is…", calls forth the wind's gust; the source prints "jealously of the wind" in it, apparently for "jealousy".[@aro-zephyros.fm06-duel]
- "Come forth, ring of fire—!" is cut off as Bell's [[#firebolt|Firebolt]] hits him; he keeps chanting, and the next printed words are "—on westerly winds!"[@aro-zephyros.fm06-duel]
- **Release:** "Aro Zephyros!"[@aro-zephyros.fm06-duel]
- **Detonation key:** "Rubele!"[@aro-zephyros.fm06-rubele]

{{nocite}} The first printed piece starts with a dash, and the fourth piece picks up mid-sentence, so the print is probably not the whole chant.

#### Effects {#aro-zephyros-effects}

- **Survives interruption.** Bell's Firebolt burns Hyacinthus mid-chant, but the magic power gathering in his hands is unaffected and he finishes the spell.[@aro-zephyros.fm06-duel]
- **Overpowers Firebolt.** The burning disk cuts straight through Bell's electric flame; the narration names Firebolt's lack of destructive force as its weakness.[@aro-zephyros.fm06-duel]
- **Homing.** When Bell dodges, the disk turns in the air and follows him. Its magical energy does not disperse until it hits its target.[@aro-zephyros.fm06-duel]
- **Detonation.** Hyacinthus says "Rubele!" and the disk explodes, throwing Bell several meders and disabling his right arm.[@aro-zephyros.fm06-rubele]

#### Open questions {#aro-zephyros-open-questions}

> [!UNRESOLVED] Not settled by the covered English volumes
> - The full chant, its Mind cost, and whether Hyacinthus can hold the disk in the air indefinitely.[@aro-zephyros.fm06-duel]

### Dia Orpheus {#dia-orpheus}

**Dia Orpheus** is a spell of [[fels|Fels]]: taboo resurrection magic, which the narration says was granted only to the Sage of old. It is cast with an extremely long chant and, in its one completed casting, costs all of Fels's Mind.[@dia-orpheus.fm10-cast] In DanMachi 10 it brings [[wiene|Wiene]] back to life after she crumbles to ash; Fels calls it the first success in some eight hundred years.[@dia-orpheus.fm10-result]

- **User:** [[fels|Fels]][@dia-orpheus.fm10-cast]
- **Type:** Resurrection magic, "granted to only the Sage of old"[@dia-orpheus.fm10-cast]
- **Cost (as shown):** All of Fels's Mind[@dia-orpheus.fm10-cast]
- **Known success:** [[wiene|Wiene]], DanMachi 10[@dia-orpheus.fm10-result]
- **Chant:** Extremely long; eight printed pieces[@dia-orpheus.fm10-cast]

#### Incantation {#dia-orpheus-incantation}

{{nocite}} The chant is extremely long and is printed in eight pieces, split by the narration and by scenes elsewhere in Orario. Only short excerpts are quoted; the full passage is in DanMachi 10, Chapter 10.

- **Opening (excerpt):** "O untrodden domain, O forbidden wall."[@dia-orpheus.fm10-cast]
- **Middle pieces:** they invoke the Rod of Asclepius, the gates of Charon and the river of time, and say the price has already been paid.[@dia-orpheus.fm10-cast]
- **Last piece (excerpt):** "Yes, I will not turn away."[@dia-orpheus.fm10-cast]
- **Release:** "Dia Orpheus."[@dia-orpheus.fm10-cast]

{{nocite}} The narration says the conjuring was complete before the release, but because the chant is described as extremely long and is cut between scenes, the eight printed pieces may not be the whole of it.

#### The casting {#dia-orpheus-the-casting}

After Wiene dies, only the reddish jewel from her forehead remains among her ashes. Fels begins to chant; a white magic circle spreads, and a pillar of white light bursts through the ground and is seen all over Orario.[@dia-orpheus.fm10-cast] At the release the pillar breaks into countless fragments of light, which spiral into Bell's chest; Wiene returns in his arms, breathing, with humanlike limbs and without her dragon wings.[@dia-orpheus.fm10-result]

Fels then sits down, spent, and says that this was their first success. Fels calls it a "pointless magic" that took up one of their Status slots for eight hundred years.[@dia-orpheus.fm10-result]

#### Limits {#dia-orpheus-limits}

The novels show one success and give no rules for it. Which targets it can revive, how long after death, whether remains are needed, and whether it can be repeated are not stated.[@dia-orpheus.fm10-result] The only cost shown is all of Fels's Mind.[@dia-orpheus.fm10-cast]

#### Resurrection and the gods {#dia-orpheus-resurrection-and-the-gods}

In Astrea Record 2, during the Great Conflict, Fels wonders whether their magic could give a dead elf boy a second life. [[ouranos|Ouranos]] forbids it: he will not abide resurrection, and would take back any life Fels restored. Fels submits.[@dia-orpheus.ar02-ouranos] The scene does not name Dia Orpheus or show a casting.

#### Not to be confused with {#dia-orpheus-not-to-be-confused-with}

**Dia Panacea**, a separate healing spell Fels casts earlier in DanMachi 10, which heals Bell's wounds, his broken fist and his fatigue.[@dia-orpheus.fm10-panacea]

#### Open questions {#dia-orpheus-open-questions}

> [!UNRESOLVED] Not settled by the covered English volumes
> - Why this casting succeeded after centuries of failure, and whether it can succeed again.[@dia-orpheus.fm10-result]

### Magia Kreis {#magia-kreis}

**Magia Kreis** is a ranged healing spell used by [[nina-tulle|Nina Tulle]], a half-elf student of the [[school-district|School District]] and the younger sister of [[bell-cranell|Bell]]'s [[guild|Guild]] adviser, [[eina-tulle|Eina]].[@magia-kreis.fm19-nina, magia-kreis.fm20-kreis] Besides healing, it passes Mind to others and makes buffs from magic and items last longer.[@magia-kreis.fm20-kreis, magia-kreis.fm20-buffs]

- **User:** [[nina-tulle|Nina Tulle]][@magia-kreis.fm20-kreis]
- **Effects:** Healing at range; Mind transfer; longer buffs[@magia-kreis.fm20-kreis, magia-kreis.fm20-buffs]
- **Chant:** One short line (quoted below)[@magia-kreis.fm19-kris, magia-kreis.fm20-chant]
- **Printed as:** Magia Kris (DanMachi 19); Magia Kreis (DanMachi 20)[@magia-kreis.fm19-kris, magia-kreis.fm20-kreis]

#### Name {#magia-kreis-name}

DanMachi 19 prints the spell as *Magia Kris*; DanMachi 20 prints *Magia Kreis*.[@magia-kreis.fm19-kris, magia-kreis.fm20-kreis] This wiki uses the later form.

#### Incantation {#magia-kreis-incantation}

{{nocite}} The chant is a single short line and is printed with the same words each time, so it is given in full.

- **Chant and release:** "Swaying stem, breath of white. Sing of flowers, and of the pristine hill—Magia Kreis!"[@magia-kreis.fm20-chant]
- DanMachi 19 prints the same line ending "—Magia Kris!".[@magia-kreis.fm19-kris]
- In DanMachi 20, when Lilly and Lyu press her to heal at once, only the name is printed: "M-Magia Kreis!"[@magia-kreis.fm20-buffs] This is a name-only print; it does not show that the spell can be cast without its chant.

When cast, fragments of white magic like flower petals envelop the targets.[@magia-kreis.fm19-kris, magia-kreis.fm20-buffs]

#### Effects {#magia-kreis-effects}

In DanMachi 20, while Nina interns with [[hestia-familia|Hestia Familia]], she tells the party that Magia Kreis also extends buffs from magic and items. [[haruhime|Haruhime]]'s Level Boost, which lasts twenty minutes at Level 2, is observed to last about five minutes longer under it.[@magia-kreis.fm20-buffs, magia-kreis.fm20-twenty] Nina uses it again during the fighting later in the volume.[@magia-kreis.fm20-later]

Nina has a second, separate spell, Lagriell Krisheim, a healing barrier that clears poison, paralysis and other negative effects and prevents curses and mental attacks, though it cannot stop direct physical or magic attacks.[@magia-kreis.fm19-lagriell]

#### Open questions {#magia-kreis-open-questions}

> [!UNRESOLVED] Not settled by the covered English volumes
> - How much longer Magia Kreis makes other buffs last, beyond the one observation with Haruhime's Level Boost.[@magia-kreis.fm20-buffs]
