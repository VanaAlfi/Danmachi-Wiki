The DanMachi novels describe many peoples. [[orario|Orario]] is home not only to humans but to "many kinds of demi-humans".[@fm01-orario] Mortals are divided into **humans** and five **demi-human** races; above them stand the [[#god|gods]], transcendent beings one dimension apart, and beside them the [[#spirit|spirits]].[@fm07-amazons, fm01-goddess, fm01-ja-deusdia, fm04-fairies] It was the gods who created the humans and demi-humans and gave the mortal races their different characteristics, or so the mortals believe.[@fm15-created]

{{nocite}} This page follows the forms printed by the English editions. Where they print one race under several names, every printed form is listed. Some names used elsewhere in English, such as *Pallum*, *Renard* and *Beast Human* on the DanMachi Fandom wiki, were not located in the checked text; they are kept only as search terms.

## Terms {#terms}

- **Demi-human:** Bell counts "all five races of demi-human", and the races named in that sense are elves, dwarves, animal people, prums and Amazons. Humans are counted apart: the text speaks of humans who can interbreed with demi-humans, and of six races in all.[@fm07-amazons, fm07-assault, fm08-children, so14-six]
- **Humanity:** *Humanity* takes in all the mortal races, "the various races of humanity".[@so03-human-struggle] When the monsters poured out of the [[dungeon|Hole]] and overran the surface, all its races set aside their differences to fight back.[@so01-human-hole] The gods' [[falna|Blessing]], which brings endless possibility to the people of the mortal world, then let humanity grow quickly in strength and develop.[@so01-human-hole] Humanity's struggle with the monsters has brought tragedy again and again, and there is no way around it: monsters and humanity are enemies (see also the [[three-great-quests|Three Great Quests]] and the [[geography#the-north-of-the-continent-great-dragon-wall|Great Dragon Wall]]).[@so03-human-struggle, fm06-human-enemy] [[alfia|Alfia]] says that in the Ancient Times humanity met the monsters with nothing but its own bodies, without the Falna, and though the spirits helped, pushed them back by its own strength.[@ar03-human-ancient]
- **Animal people:** the commonest name for the demi-humans with animal ears and tails.[@fm01-animal] The same people are also printed *beast person* or *beast people*, *beastpeople*, *beastman* (plural *beastmen*) and *beastfolk*.[@fm03-beast, fm07-front, fm18-transform, so04-beastman, so08-plains, ar02-beastfolk] They count as one race among the six, but are themselves split into "races" or "species" such as [[#werewolf|werewolves]].[@so14-six, fm18-transform, so06-transform]
- **Mixed race:** a child of two races is called a *Half* or a *half-breed*, and in the plural *halfs*. The text also speaks of "people of mixed race".[@fm08-children, so09-kam, so09-halfs, fm07-front] Kam's three sons in Sword Oratoria 9 are Halfs of elf or animal-person stock, that is half-elves and half-beastmen.[@so09-kam]
- **Fairy:** in DanMachi 4 and 5 *fairy* names the [[#spirit|spirits]], "Nymphs, spirits, elementals, jinn".[@fm04-fairies, fm05-elements] Later volumes also call [[#elf|elves]] fairies.[@so12-fairies, fc02-fairies]
- **Koine:** "the common language of humans and demi-humans".[@fm04-koine] The [[#amazon|Amazons]] of [[telskyura|Telskyura]] also have their own Amazonian language.[@so06-language, so06-sewehga]

## Children between races {#children}

DanMachi 8 sets out the rule while [[finn-deimne|Finn]] explains why his heir must be a full-blooded prum: "All the races of demi-humans can reproduce only with themselves."[@fm08-children] Pairs such as an elf and a dwarf, or an animal person and a prum, have no children, and spirits cannot have offspring at all. The exceptions are humans, who can interbreed with demi-humans, and Amazons, whose children are always Amazon daughters.[@fm08-children] DanMachi 7 adds that "Half-Amazons don't exist."[@fm07-amazons]

- **Halfs:** each mixed race named in the text is half human. [[eina-tulle|Eina Tulle]] is half human and half elf, [[tsubaki|Tsubaki Collbrande]] is the child of a human and a dwarf, and [[van|Van]] of [[freya-familia|Freya Familia]] is the child of a prum and a human.[@fm02-eina, so04-tsubaki, fm16-van]
- **Inherited traits:** [[nina-tulle|Nina]] reminds Bell of the dominant traits taught in the School District's Racial History class, which they took together. A child of two half-dwarves is a human (one chance in four), a half-dwarf (one in two) or a dwarf (one in four).[@fm20-traits] [[leon-verdenberg|Leon Verdenberg]], born to half-dwarf parents, is a dwarf with a human's height and long limbs; the dwarves of his home village shunned him for it.[@fm20-traits, fm20-leon]
- **Prejudice:** Van grew up mocked for his height, and had heard that mixed-race children were discriminated against "much more before the era of gods".[@fm16-van] Finn says that a half-prum heir could not be the symbol of the prums' hope.[@fm08-children]
- **Edas Village:** in [[edas-village|Edas Village]], home to many races, "the unusually high percentage of halfs was noticeable".[@so09-halfs]

## Strength and standing {#standing}

Sword Oratoria 14 sets out how a god starting a Familia might see the six races. Most gods would pick a human first, "average perhaps, but humans were filled with possibility". A dwarf could get through most early challenges by brute strength, and elves are the finest casters but notoriously fussy. Animal people tend to be steadier and more reliable than humans, and Amazons, properly directed, can start off as strong as dwarves. Prums were "widely considered the weakest".[@so14-six]

When Bell looks for a Familia, he has heard that humans and prums compare poorly with the other races, and he supposes that fierce Amazons and keen-sensed animal people are favoured, to say nothing of elves gifted at magic and dwarves strong enough to hold their own against weak monsters without a Falna.[@fm15-barriers] Sword Oratoria 14 calls dwarves "the strongest of all demi-humans".[@so14-dwarves]

Race still counts after the [[falna|Falna]]: a prum's potential for growth stays "lackluster", while a dwarf's strength and endurance "should tend to grow faster than the others".[@so14-standing-prum-growth, fm20-standing-dwarf-growth]

## Humans and demi-humans {#mortal-races}

### Human {#human}

**Humans** are counted apart from the five demi-human races. They can have children with demi-humans; they and the [[#amazon|Amazons]] are the two exceptions to the rule that demi-humans have children only within their own race.[@fm08-children, so14-six] Gods choosing a first follower usually pick a human, since humans are average but full of possibility. Even so, humans and prums are often passed over as adventurers in favour of the other races.[@so14-six, fm15-barriers] DanMachi 4 calls humans "a race that was physically and mentally weaker than many of its counterparts", which made it the more remarkable that [[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz Wallenstein]], a human, became at eight the fastest adventurer on record to reach Level 2.[@fm04-human-weaker] Bell's home village had "only humans and the occasional animal person".[@fm15-village]

- **Among the other races:** Since the gods came down, humans and demi-humans have mixed far more than before, though some closed-minded people remain even now.[@fm04-human-contact] In the busy streets humans and many kinds of demi-human mingle.[@fm04-human-streets] Romances between [[#spirit|spirits]] and humans or demi-humans are an old subject of stories.[@fm08-human-romance]
- **Examples:** [[bell-cranell|Bell Cranell]] ("Bell Cranell. Human."), [[runoa|Runoa Faust]] and [[narfi|Narfi]].[@fm01-bell, fm08-runoa, so08-narfi]

### Elf {#elf}

**Elves** ({{ja|妖精}}, written *fairy*)[@elf.fm18-ja] are known for beauty, long life and magic. They are praised as beautiful even among other races, and they are a long-lived race among the demi-humans.[@fm02-eina, fm02-longest] They are "magically attuned" and widely regarded as the ultimate magic casters, though notoriously fussy.[@fm15-barriers, so14-six] Their magic is older than the [[falna|Falna]]: before the gods came down, elves and other magic users made their own spells, and elves still have an innate magic woven by their ancestors, though it is hard to control and those who receive a Falna are said to stop using it (see [[magic#elven-innate-magic|innate elven magic]]).[@fm05-elf-old-magic, so14-elf-innate-magic] Many elves have Skills that increase magic, for the race shares "a common seed" that lets similar Skills manifest, and some Skills appear across the whole race.[@fm01-elf-skills, ar03-elf-seed, fm15-elf-skill] Long-lived as they are, elves have fewer children than humans or other demi-humans.[@so14-fruitful] Their long, pointed ears are the proof of their heritage.[@so03-elf-ears] Their bodies set limits: [[riveria|Riveria]] explains that as an elf and magic user she can reach S in Magic, but that the physical limits of her body make it futile to raise her Strength and Defense beyond a certain point.[@fm04-elf-limits]

- **Touch:** elves do not let anyone they don't trust touch their skin. Sword Oratoria 3 calls this an elven custom, observed differently by region and by individual.[@fm04-skin, so03-custom, fc01-lyu]
- **Pride:** in the age of the gods, when the other races mixed freely, some elves, out of pride, disliked mingling with other races and shut themselves away deep in their forests, like Lyu's home, [[geography#elven-forests-lumirua-forest|Lumirua Forest]].[@fm15-shunned, fm04-interact]
- **Dwarves:** elves and dwarves are "not on the best of terms" and rarely see eye to eye. Their mutual loathing is old and rooted in history, which is why [[riveria|Riveria]]'s apology to [[gareth|Gareth]] in Sword Oratoria 14 means so much.[@ar01-dwarves, fm08-eye, ss01-bad-blood, so14-loathing]
- **One race:** whatever the terms *white* and *dark*, "elves were all the same race", and a child's skin colour can follow either parent.[@fc02-hedin]
- **Examples:** [[lyu-leon|Lyu Leon]], [[lefiya|Lefiya Viridis]] and [[filvis|Filvis Challia]].[@fm06-lyu, so01-lefiya, so02-filvis]

#### High elf {#high-elf}

**High elves** ({{ja|王族}}, written *royalty*)[@high-elf.so14-ja] are elven royalty. Riveria is a high elf because "royal blood flowed through her veins".[@so01-riveria] For all elves the high elves are sublime beings and objects of veneration, revered even above the gods who came down to the mortal world.[@ar02-high-elf-revere, so14-high-elf-elitism] Elves including [[lefiya|Lefiya Viridis]] treat her with respect, although she finds this special treatment uncomfortable.[@so01-high-elf-royal] Her name Alf means "primogenitor of elves", and her home, the [[geography#elven-forests-alf-royal-woods|Alf Royal Woods]], is an elven holy land on a par with the [[alv-mountains|Alv Mountains]].[@so14-alf] In Sword Oratoria 2 an elf hands his bow to Riveria at once, obeying her voice without condition.[@so02-order] As a high elf Riveria has tremendous charisma among elves, and in Sword Oratoria 10 their fighting spirit rises even in the enemy's camp.[@so10-high-elf-charisma] In Sword Oratoria 12 [[hedin|Hedin Selrand]] and [[hegni|Hegni Ragnar]] do not kneel to her but still pay her their respects, Hedin reminding Hegni that they stand before royalty.[@so12-high-elf-salute] High elves live even longer than other elves and have even fewer children, which is why Riveria's father would not let her leave.[@so14-fruitful] Her father, King Rafale, says that for the sake of their race the royal line must not be severed.[@so14-high-elf-life] Rafale, the very summit of the elves, has no child but Riveria.[@so14-high-elf-rafale, so14-high-elf-fruitful]

- **Appearance:** Riveria's graceful features are described as beautiful enough to make goddesses jealous.[@so02-high-elf-beauty] Outside the forest, Riveria's beauty and royal bearing draw attention across racial boundaries.[@so14-high-elf-town] In [[geography#preblica-karna-and-lonza-karna|Karna]], elves of every walk of life crowd around Riveria, as Rafale had warned would happen if one of the royal family left the forest.[@so14-high-elf-karna] An elf in Karna asks whether Riveria's jade hair and eyes mark her as noble, Rafale shares her jade eyes, and a star spirit stone's jade colour calls the high elves to mind.[@so14-high-elf-karna, so14-high-elf-rafale, fc03-high-elf-jade]
- **Their forest:** [[finn-deimne|Finn Deimne]] describes high elves as especially proud, and their village is said to be the most insular of the secluded elven settlements.[@so14-high-elf-seclusion] At the root lies elitism: they praise their own beauty and scorn other races as ugly and base.[@so14-high-elf-elitism] Those who approach it carelessly face high-elf magic, even if they are gods.[@so14-high-elf-seclusion] Finn explains that elven guardians admit only fellow elves and the few others the royal family allows, and that beyond the entrance, it is said, a maze of trees bars the way to anyone without a guide.[@so14-high-elf-guards] [[gareth|Gareth Landrock]] has heard that high elves never leave their village; Finn calls them the fussiest of races and finds it rare for one of them, the very embodiment of pride, to give thanks.[@so14-high-elf-outside, so14-high-elf-finn] Rafale holds that the high elves must remain the light and pride of their race, or the elves would decline as the [[#prum|prums]] did; Riveria calls the customs of the royal woods chains.[@so14-high-elf-views] Finn fears that a call from the high elves would bring elves from all over the world to take Riveria back.[@so14-high-elf-rescue] After she leaves, Rafale asks all the world's elves to bless Princess Riveria's journey; they welcome it, and no one attacks her Familia.[@so14-high-elf-notice]
- **Example:** [[riveria|Riveria Ljos Alf]].[@so01-riveria]
- **Other high elves:** Sword Oratoria 14 names Rafale Ljos Alf as the high-elf king.[@so14-high-elf-king] It names [[minor-characters#seldia|Seldia]], the eternal saint in [[dungeon-oratoria|Dungeon Oratoria]], as the first high elf said to have left the forest.[@so14-high-elf-saint] Over their long history many high-elf women have sought to leave the Alf Royal Woods for the world beyond, all sharing an interest in it, as Riveria's late mother did.[@so14-high-elf-leavers] In Sword Oratoria 5, Lefiya Viridis says that the elven nobles, Riveria among them, descend from Seldia's younger sister [[minor-characters#rishena|Rishena]].[@so05-high-elf-line] [[alicia-forestlight|Alicia Forestlight]] counts it the greatest honour to fight with Riveria, of the same royal line as Seldia.[@so13-high-elf-lineage] [[legi|Legi]] hopes for a [[#dark-elf|dark-elf]] high elf who will destroy the [[#white-elf|white elves]]; Bell interprets her wish as a desire for royal restoration.[@fm19-high-elf-dark]

#### White elf {#white-elf}

The **white elves** are "the lineage considered normal elves in current times". Their high elf led them down the Alv Mountains rather than sacrifice them to the monsters of the Ancient Times.[@fc02-alv] DanMachi 16 calls a white elf "the standard of elfdom".[@fm16-standard] That white elf is [[hedin|Hedin Selrand]], and side by side with the dark elf [[hegni|Hegni Ragnar]], of the same race, his looks are extraordinary: long golden hair, fine white skin, coral-red eyes and glasses.[@fm16-white-elf-hedin]

- **Heodenings:** On [[geography#ruined-and-remote-lands-heodenings|Heodenings]], a forested island in a great lake on the far frontier of the continent, a closed world unknown to others, the white elves had a nation of their own beside the dark elves' in the same forest.[@fc02-white-elf-heodenings] There Hedin was praised as the King of Reason of the white elves' forest capital.[@fc02-white-elf-heodenings] Their war with the dark elves at last drew in both whole peoples; the white elves, with an able commander, held the advantage until [[hegni|Hegni Ragnar]] engaged Hedin, but the dark elves were the stronger soldiers, and the white elves paid for having leaned on Hedin's command.[@fc02-white-elf-war]
- **Examples:** [[hedin|Hedin Selrand]], king of the white elves of [[geography#ruined-and-remote-lands-heodenings|Heodenings]], and Dina Dis, an enemy in Astrea Record 1.[@fc02-hedin, ar01-dis] [[dis-sisters|Dina]], a golden-haired white elf, is the elder sister of the dark elf Vena Dis, who has silver hair.[@ar01-white-elf-dina] Hedin is called the very embodiment of the elves who look down on all but themselves.[@fc02-white-elf-heodenings] In DanMachi 17, as the brain of [[freya-familia|Freya Familia]], he gives an order that no one questions.[@fm17-white-elf-strategist]

#### Dark elf {#dark-elf}

**Dark elves** differ from white elves in "magic and magical ability and skin color". They were rare in the age of the gods because they lost so many of their people defending the Alv Mountains when monsters poured out over the land in the Ancient Times.[@fc02-alv, so12-rare] They are seldom seen even in Orario, now that the white elves flourish.[@fm16-dark-elf-hegni, so12-dark-elf-rare] The white elves, led by their high elf of the time, came down from the mountains and survived.[@fc02-dark-elf-decline] They cursed the white elves as cowards and dreamed of the day "the dark high elf, whose lineage was said to have continued", would lead them again.[@fc02-alv] Only a stubborn group shut away in a forest was set on stopping the flourishing white elves.[@fc02-dark-elf-restoration] For all that, dark and white elves are one race.[@fc02-dark-elf-kinship] A child of the two can take either skin colour.[@fc02-dark-elf-children]

- **Heodenings:** on this forested island, dark and white elves fought for generations, and [[hegni|Hegni]] was made the dark elves' warrior king. [[hedin|Hedin]] descends from [[hedin#hildr|Hildr]], a dark-elf [[healer|healer]] given over during a truce, but inherited the white elves' traits most strongly.[@fc02-hegni, fc02-hedin] To Hedin, king of the [[#white-elf|white elves]], the dark elves were true barbarians who shared the forest yet thought only of wiping out their own kin.[@fc02-dark-elf-hedin] Hegni was not a high elf.[@fc02-dark-elf-king] He fears the compatriots who press pride and duty on him, and his clan berates him whenever he takes too few heads.[@fc02-dark-elf-pressure] He has the dark skin of his race and silver hair that looks almost purple.[@fm18-dark-elf-appearance]
- **Legi:** a dark-elf student in DanMachi 19 who is waiting for "a high elf. A dark one."[@fm19-legi, fm19-dream] She keeps a mask over her lower face, and the dark high elf she awaits is one who will destroy the white elves.[@fm19-dark-elf-legi, fm19-dream] Bell takes her wish to become an assassin as preparation for the return of the dark royal line against the far more numerous white elves.[@fm19-dark-elf-interpretation]
- **Examples:** [[hegni|Hegni Ragnar]]; Vena Dis, Dina's sister, whom Hegni will not count as one of his own race; and Legi of the School District.[@fc02-hegni, ar01-dis, fm19-legi, fm20-squad] Vena, the younger of the two, has silver hair.[@ar01-dark-elf-vena]

### Half-elf {#half-elf}

A **half-elf** is part human and part elf. [[eina-tulle|Eina]] is half human, with elven blood.[@fm02-eina] Eina's father is human, and her mother comes from the forest of the [[#high-elf|high elves]].[@fm15-half-elf-family] She left it with a certain princess and, perhaps because the outside air does not suit her, is often ill.[@fm15-half-elf-mother] Half-elf ears are "longer than a human's but not so pointed as an elf's"; Bell tells an elf from Eina because the elf's ears "come to a full point".[@fm19-nina, fm02-ears]

- **Appearance:** Bell notices Eina's elven beauty alongside her unexpectedly friendly, approachable manner.[@fm01-half-elf-appearance] With half the elves' blood in her, Eina has to accept that men are drawn to her.[@fm02-half-elf-beauty] [[nina-tulle|Nina Tulle]] has brown hair, emerald eyes and a jade-coloured lock that Bell associates with her noble blood.[@fm19-half-elf-ears] [[lyu-leon|Lyu Leon]] notes Nina's noble jade hair and says that her magic seems to approach that of royalty.[@fm20-half-elf-lyu]
- **Identity and treatment:** Eina feels that her blood shares [[riveria|Riveria Ljos Alf]]'s nobility and does not want to disgrace it.[@fm03-half-elf-pride] Perhaps from her earnest elven blood, she regrets afterwards that she could have done more for [[bell-cranell|Bell]].[@fm12-half-elf-earnest] In her journal she despairs of feelings for the adventurer she advises that are unworthy of the elven blood in her, and begs her mother Aina to scold her and Riveria to judge her.[@fm17-half-elf-aina] Eina suspects [[royman|Royman Mardeel]] privately despises her as a half-blood.[@fm09-half-elf-prejudice] [[maris-hackard|Maris Hackard]] contemptuously calls Eina a half-blood when disappointed with her new adviser.[@fm15-half-elf-insult] Eina grumbles at Maris's prejudice as though her elven blood made her.[@fm15-half-elf-maris] Eina feels her elven blood compel her to bow to Riveria, although Riveria rejects excessive deference outside the homeland.[@fm02-half-elf-deference] Born in a free city open to every race, Eina knows the elves' villages only from what she has learned.[@fm02-half-elf-bow]
- **Children:** Nina explains that a human and half-elf can have children, with equal chances of human or half-elf inheritance.[@fm20-half-elf-inheritance]
- **Examples:** [[eina-tulle|Eina Tulle]] and her sister [[nina-tulle|Nina Tulle]].[@fm02-eina, fm19-nina, fm19-half-elf-sister]
- **Other half-elves:** [[marias-orphanage#ruu|Ruu]] is an androgynous half-elf child whose elven ancestry Bell associates with a particularly well-proportioned face.[@fm08-half-elf-ruu] An unnamed half-elven swordswoman fights with both magic and sword slashes during Astrea Record 1's street battles.[@ar01-half-elf-combat] An unnamed half-elf adventurer draws a magic blade after his companions fall in DanMachi 13.[@fm13-half-elf-blade] The history of the [[geography#ruins-near-orario-orza-ruins|Orza city ruins]] credits the labour of half-elves and half-dwarves with sustaining its self-sufficient island settlement.[@fm18-half-elf-history]

### Dwarf {#dwarf}

**Dwarves** are short and robust: "As with most dwarves, Dormul was a stout, thick man", even though he is taller than most of his kin. [[mia-grand|Mia]], by contrast, is "tall for a dwarf".[@fm08-dormul, so01-shops, fc01-mia] They are known for strength. Sword Oratoria 14 calls them the strongest demi-humans, and DanMachi 15 says they can hold their own against weak monsters even without a Falna.[@so14-dwarves, fm15-barriers] Dwarves favour axes and hammers.[@fm08-arms] Young dwarves digging in a mine sing that they are "the children of the earth", and Gareth's band are big drinkers.[@so14-earth] For their feud with elves, see [[#elf|Elf]].

- **Beards:** Beards and thick body hair belong to the dwarven type: [[leon-verdenberg|Leon Verdenberg]], a dwarf with no thick beard or body hair, says he took after the human side.[@fm19-dwarf-build, fm20-dwarf-leon] [[finn-deimne|Finn]] has read that among dwarves the finer the beard, the more it is admired.[@so14-dwarf-village]
- **Toughness:** Their race is known for its toughness: when a party is worn down, no race is more reliable than the dwarves, one reason adventurers often recruit them as heavy fighters.[@fm12-dwarf-tough] Dwarves with great shields serve as the wall that protects archers and mages in battle: in Sword Oratoria 1 "a line of stout, muscular dwarves wielding large shields in both arms" covers the bowmen and casters, and in DanMachi 5 "the wall of dwarves held strong" against the Goliath's howl while the mages cast their spells.[@so01-dwarf-wall, fm05-dwarf-wall] Brute labour, as one dwarf puts it, is dwarves' work and no job for dainty young ladies.[@fm12-dwarf-tough]
- **Earth and excavation:** No one can match a dwarf at excavation; theirs is "a race inseparable from mines and shafts".[@so13-dwarf-excavation] [[gareth|Gareth]] calls examining the ground "a dwarf's specialty", and in the attack on [[knossos|Knossos]] every squad is told to take at least one dwarf so as not to miss any hidden passages.[@so09-dwarf-ground, so11-dwarf-squads]
- **Village life and drink:** One dwarf village lies at the foot of an impassable mountain range, far from the trade routes, and few but dwarves are said to come there.[@so14-dwarf-village] Many dwarves are said to have a craftsman's temperament, and in this village they repair their tools and equipment by the road and make shoes from whatever is at hand.[@so14-dwarf-village] Poor as it is, the village throws a hearty dwarven feast, opening barrels of drink kept for special days, and when a monster strikes, even old dwarves long retired stand at the front with pickaxes and shovels.[@so14-dwarf-village] Dwarven fire liquor is strong: one bottle leaves [[raul-nord|Raul Nord]] red to the ears and dead drunk at once.[@so05-dwarf-liquor]
- **Dwarves and elves:** The dwarf Dormul and the elf Luvis of the [[modi-and-magni-familias|Modi and Magni Familias]] quarrel as if to symbolise relations between the two races, yet when the elves are wounded, Dormul and the other dwarves carry them on their shoulders.[@so12-dwarf-elves, fm12-dwarf-tough] Out of her gilded cage, [[riveria|Riveria Ljos Alf]] learns that dwarves are not merely savage: they are exasperatingly rough, she says, but perhaps only because elves are delicate and fussy, and the village has taught her that a change of viewpoint can change values and even the world.[@so14-dwarf-village]
- **Examples:** [[gareth|Gareth Landrock]], [[mia-grand|Mia Grand]], Dormul of [[modi-and-magni-familias|Magni Familia]], and [[leon-verdenberg|Leon Verdenberg]], a dwarf born to half-dwarf parents.[@so01-gareth, fc01-mia, fm08-dormul, fm12-dwarf-tough, fm20-traits]

### Half-dwarf {#half-dwarf}

A **half-dwarf** is part human and part dwarf: [[tsubaki|Tsubaki Collbrande]]'s parents are a human from the [[far-east|Far East]] and a dwarf from the continent.[@so04-tsubaki] Tsubaki's mother is the human and her father the dwarf, and at 170 celch tall she is often taken for a human.[@fm08-half-dwarf-tsubaki] Her long, slender limbs are perhaps a strong inheritance of her human blood, and some dwarves, basically short in leg and arm, apparently envy her for them.[@so04-half-dwarf-tsubaki] DanMachi 20 gives the chances that a child of two half-dwarves is born a human, a half-dwarf or a dwarf (see [[#children|Children between races]]).[@fm20-traits]

- **Example:** [[tsubaki|Tsubaki Collbrande]].[@so04-tsubaki] In Sword Oratoria 5 the narration calls Tsubaki, who wears an eye patch, "the half-dwarf high smith" and "the half-dwarf master smith".[@so05-half-dwarf-smith]
- **In the text:** in DanMachi 2 the store manager who lectures [[hestia|Hestia]] before she returns to her post at work is a half-dwarf.[@fm02-half-dwarf-manager] The Orza city ruins of DanMachi 18, a fortress city in a caldera lake northwest of Orario that fought the monsters pouring out of the [[dungeon|Great Hole]] over 2,000 years ago, are said to have prospered as a self-sufficient city by putting half-elves and half-dwarves to work, until a powerful monster flew in.[@fm18-half-dwarf-orza]

### Prum {#prum}

**Prums** ({{ja|小人族}}, written *small folk*)[@prum.fm08-ja] are "the race shorter than all the others" and are considered the weakest of all the races. They lack the physical capabilities of humans, the magic of elves, the strength of dwarves, the fighting skill of Amazons and the senses of animal people.[@fm08-weakest, so14-six] Their one strength is their eyesight, which excels among the demi-humans, though it pales beside the keen senses of the animal people.[@fc02-vision, fm02-eyesight, so14-prum-eyesight] It also serves them in poor light, though even prums would struggle to see a pitch-black blade in the dark of a back alley; the Gulliver brothers could live in their dim cliff workshop only because of their prum vision.[@fm02-dark, fc02-workshop] Riveria says prums excel in eyesight among the demi-humans; DanMachi 6 describes Luan as a prum with characteristically big eyes.[@so07-perception, fm06-eyes]

- **Stature and looks:** [[lyra|Lyra]], for one, measures under 120 celches.[@fm14-prum-lyra] Their small, feeble-looking appearance makes prums in general easy targets of prejudice.[@fm06-prum-prejudice]
- **Phiana:** Phiana, "a fictional goddess" of prum faith, is the strong, proud prum knight order of the Ancient Times personified as a god; Finn calls her the great ancestor. When she was not among the gods who descended, prum faith collapsed, a blow the race had not recovered from. Finn became an adventurer to give prums a new hope.[@fm08-phiana] Minor Myths and Legends 2 prints her name *Fianna*: "Fianna was a fictional goddess whom the prum worshipped", and Finn prays to her before important battles.[@ss02-fianna] Sword Oratoria 10 calls her "the hero of his race", who in the Ancient Times "killed multitudes of monsters and rescued countless people"; in Sword Oratoria 14 Finn wants to travel north to [[geography#world-powers-and-nations-maelstra|Maelstra]], whose famed opera is staging "a performance about the Phiana knights".[@so10-phiana, so14-maelstra] The knight order fought as the spear of the battlefield, showed its courage to the world before Argonaut appeared, and was the prums' first and last glory.[@fm08-prum-knights, so10-prum-phiana] [[finn-deimne|Finn Deimne]] strove to become the next Phiana, a light for the prums in place of their great forebear.[@so10-prum-phiana] In Sword Oratoria 10 he gives up matching her and resolves to surpass her instead, and on the eve of the second attack on [[knossos|Knossos]] he tells Tione that he has stopped looking up to her and has sworn to become "an even brighter beacon".[@so10-prum-surpass, ss02-prum-beacon]
- **Renown:** Their small, endearing looks add to the view that prums have the least potential of any race, and from the distant past to the present they have made overwhelmingly few heroic tales known to the world.[@fm08-prum-knights] Finn knows of no famed prum in Orario but himself and the [[gulliver-brothers|Gulliver brothers]], and hardly knows the names of his kin elsewhere in the world; prums of renown are still few enough to count.[@so04-prum-renown] [[bell-cranell|Bell Cranell]] notes that prums who have levelled up are rare among adventurers, though Finn and the Gulliver brothers are first-tier adventurers.[@fm19-prum-chris]
- **Bravery:** Finn holds that courage is the prums' "forgotten weapon".[@fm08-weakest]
- **Treatment:** Sword Oratoria 14 says prums were "widely derided and often exploited"; in young Deimne's mountain village they were "at the bottom of the pile".[@so14-deimne] Finn says that other races tend to look down on prums, and the elves above all.[@so14-prum-elves] Elves scorn prums as stunted, a scorn neither new nor reserved for prums, though Finn grants that a race which has lost its courage is bound to be judged merely small.[@so14-prum-elves]
- **Language and customs:** Prums have a language of their own, in which the name Finn means light, and the [[school-district|School District]] offers it as a class, Prummish, beside Koine, Elvish, Dwarvish, the animal people's languages and Amazonian.[@so14-prum-language, fm19-prum-language] At the west-southwest edge of Orario near the city wall, the Hidden Home of the Prums is a tavern that admits prums only; it is busy for its location, perhaps because prums, apt to feel small in the shadow of other races, come to it to rest.[@fm08-prum-hidden-home]
- **Examples:** [[finn-deimne|Finn Deimne]], [[lilliluka-erde|Lilliluka Erde]], [[lyra|Lyra]] and [[gulliver-brothers|the Gulliver brothers]].[@fm05-finn, fm02-lilly, fm14-lyra, fc02-vision] The books also name [[apollo-familia#luan-espel|Luan Espel]] of Apollo Familia (DanMachi 6), the boy [[marias-orphanage#ossian|Ossian]] (Sword Oratoria 10), [[merrill|Merrill]] (Sword Oratoria 11) and [[christia-elvia|Christia Elvia]], a Level 2 boy of the School District who, unlike prums prone to put themselves down, brims with confidence (DanMachi 19).[@fm06-prum-prejudice, so10-prum-ossian, so11-prum-merrill, fm19-prum-chris]

### Half-prum {#half-prum}

A **half-prum** has one prum parent and one human parent. Van of [[freya-familia|Freya Familia]] is one: at 150 celches he is tall for a prum but often mocked by men of other races.[@fm16-van] He is shorter than [[bell-cranell|Bell Cranell]], who is scolded for staring at him.[@fm17-half-prum-short] Finn rules out a half-prum heir.[@fm08-children]

- **Example:** Van, an einherjar of Freya Familia.[@fm16-van, fm18-half-prum-einherjar]

### Amazon {#amazon}

**Amazons** ({{ja|女戦士}}, written *woman warrior*)[@amazon.fm07-ja] are aggressive hand-to-hand fighters, in clans all over the world, each with its own martial art. Of the five demi-human races they look the most like humans, but they can bear only female children.[@fm07-amazons] Every child of an Amazon mother is an Amazon, so they need a male of any race to have children. Old stories tell of Amazons kidnapping men.[@fm07-amazons, ss02-girls] They are aggressive and predatory toward the opposite sex, and [[aisha-belka|Aisha Belka]] says that Amazons carry men off and devour them.[@ss02-amazon-lovers, fm07-amazon-race] [[hyrute-sisters|Tiona Hyrute]]'s warm friendliness, by contrast, makes her seem born into the wrong race.[@fm03-amazon-tiona]

- **Character:** Unlike the [[#elf|elves]], who bare as little skin as they can, Amazons have no shame at all about showing it, one of the differences between races seen daily in a Familia where many races live under one roof.[@so01-amazon-skin]
- **Strong men:** "Amazons were attracted to strong men." They often fall for powerful men who can beat them, an "irresistible attraction toward men who beat them in battle".[@so08-strong, so06-hearts, ss02-trait] [[hyrute-sisters|Tione]]'s love for Finn began that way.[@so06-hearts] Many Amazons wish to bear the child of the strong man who beats them.[@so06-amazon-love]
- **Holy land:** [[telskyura|Telskyura]], a peninsular country far to Orario's southeast, is the holy land of the Amazons and embodies their instincts in every way.[@so06-amazon-telskyura] It is said that not a day passes there without battle cries and cheering as the Amazons fight one another to hone their strength, and it is called a country of blood and war.[@so06-amazon-telskyura] There strength is justice: the strong win praise, rank and honour, the defeated weak, denied even the honour of dying in battle, become labourers for the country and the strong, and bloody combat, the road to becoming a true warrior, is an old custom of the land.[@so06-amazon-telskyura] The Amazons of Telskyura are said to learn to kill a [[monsters#goblin|goblin]] before they can speak: with their potential unlocked by the Falna from the start and a first trial before a young monster, even infants who can barely stand are made warriors.[@so06-amazon-telskyura] [[kali|Kali]] says that Telskyura was already such a country before she came, and that twisting a country's history and culture for a god's own whims would be an outrage against the mortal world.[@so06-amazon-kali]
- **Telskyura:** the Amazons of Telskyura, such as Argana of [[kali-familia|Kali Familia]], speak an Amazonian language of their own, with phrases unique to them.[@so06-language, so06-sewehga] Argana speaks Koine only haltingly.[@so06-amazon-language]
- **Ishtar Familia:** In Orario, most members of [[ishtar-familia|Ishtar Familia]], whose territory takes in the whole [[pleasure-quarter|Pleasure Quarter]], are Amazons, and a group of its Amazons, most of them Level 3 or above and led by the Level 5 [[phryne-jamil|Phryne Jamil]], are known as the Berbera.[@fm07-amazon-ishtar]
- **Amazoness:** Aisha once calls Ilta "Amazoness". This form was located only once in the English; in the Japanese it is the race's usual reading, here written over *my own kind*.[@fm10-amazoness, fm10-amazoness-ja]
- **Examples:** [[hyrute-sisters|Tiona and Tione Hyrute]], [[aisha-belka|Aisha Belka]], [[phryne-jamil|Phryne Jamil]] and [[ilta-faana|Ilta Faana]].[@fm05-hyrute, fm07-aisha, fm10-amazoness]

## Animal people {#animal-people}

**Animal people** ({{ja|獣人}}, *beast people*)[@animal-people.fm01-ja] are demi-humans with animal ears and tails.[@fm01-animal] They are known above all for their senses: they have a better sense of smell than humans, and the senses of some kinds are sharper even than those of adventurers strengthened by a Status.[@so01-smell, fm05-senses, fm05-ja-senses, so10-noses] Their noses can track enemies, so in Astrea Record 1 smugglers use an item to hide their scent "so even animal people noses can't track them". In Knossos the animal people formed the main mapping squad.[@so10-noses, ar01-scent, so11-mapping] They are also known for their agility: in DanMachi 11 the first line of troops is made up of animal people "who moved with their race's characteristic agility".[@fm11-animal-agility]

- **Transformation:** "confirmed only among a limited number of races of beast people". It unleashes their bestial strength, "trading rationality for strength".[@fm18-transform, so06-transform] Once they receive a Falna, the transformation becomes tied to their Skills. [[ottar|Ottar]] can transform whenever he chooses (see [[skills#vana-arganture|Vana Arganture]]).[@fm18-transform]
- **Tribes:** [[bete-loga|Bete]] was the son of the chief of the Beastmen of the Plains, a hunting tribe.[@so08-plains]

### Cat person {#cat-person}

**Cat people** ({{ja|猫人}})[@cat-person.fm08-ja] have cat ears and long, thin tails.[@fc01-ears, fm07-allen, so04-aki] Several are described by their colouring: [[allen-fromel|Allen]] is a small catman about 160 celch tall with black and gray fur, [[anakity-autumn|Anakity]] is black-haired, and a catgirl on the staff of the Benevolent Mistress has brown fur.[@fm07-cat-allen, so12-cat-anakity, fc01-cat-brown] The English editions print *cat person* or *cat people*, *catperson*, *catman*, *catgirl* and *cat girl*.[@fm07-cats, fc01-catperson, fm07-allen, fc01-cat-brown, so04-aki]

- **Examples:** [[anya-fromel|Anya Fromel]] and [[chloe|Chloe Lolo]] of the Benevolent Mistress, [[allen-fromel|Allen Fromel]] and [[anakity-autumn|Anakity Autumn]].[@fm07-cats, fm07-allen, so04-aki]
- **In the text:** Allen's harsh words do not suit his handsome looks, and in battle he wields a silver spear.[@fm07-cat-allen, fm18-cat-spear] His temper is quick: murderous intent wells up in him in an instant while the four [[gulliver-brothers|Gulliver brothers]] sneer at him.[@fc02-cat-temper] Attacking [[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz]] with four prums, he gradually speeds up and shows signs of outpacing her Agility even after her level-up.[@so04-cat-speed] When [[bell-cranell|Bell]] flees from him in the Familia War, he knows that hiding would be hopeless before an animal person's nose.[@fm18-cat-nose] To Anya, a single cat person who appears before her is harder to face than five first-tier adventurers.[@fm16-cat-anya] Two of the shop's cat people, Anya Fromel among them, cry "meow" at the end of their words when a long-legged Amazon appears in front of it.[@fm10-cat-meow] In Familia Chronicle 1 a human and a cat-person man stand among the bodyguards beside the casino owner Terry.[@fc01-cat-guard] In DanMachi 13 the one-armed catman Jura, also called an animal person, drives a lambton to attack with his whip.[@fm13-cat-jura, fm13-cat-whip]

### Chienthrope {#chienthrope}

### Boaz {#boaz}

The **boaz** ({{ja|猪人}}, written *boar person*)[@boaz.fm18-ja] have boar-like ears; [[ottar|Ottar]]'s are "evidence of his boaz heritage". Ottar stands more than two meders tall.[@so04-boaz, fm02-ottar] Ottar's limbs are muscled like steel, and his eyes share the rust colour of his hair.[@so04-boaz-identity] His body is as solid as a rock.[@fm02-boaz-height] The race is known for its ferocity.[@so04-boaz-identity] While training with Ottar, [[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz Wallenstein]] considers his race and is certain that he still has an unused trump card.[@so11-boaz-ace] In that training he faces her with his bare abilities alone, using neither magic nor Skills, and is not fighting at full strength.[@so11-boaz-full] Transformation into a beast always carries some activation condition or risk.[@fm18-boaz-falna] [[bete-loga|Bete Loga]] judges that, unlike a werewolf, Ottar can transform during the day or even in the Dungeon.[@fm18-boaz-trigger] Ottar's transformation is an active-trigger Skill, [[skills#vana-arganture|Vana Arganture]] ({{ja|戦猪招来}}), that strengthens all his Abilities, basic and Development Abilities alike, so much that it could be mistaken for a level-up, but costs much stamina and Mind each time it is used.[@fm18-boaz-transform] The longer he stays transformed, the more his body is worn down beyond what his auto-heal can mend, where werewolves transform without risk once under the moon.[@fm18-boaz-transform]

- **Printed forms:** The volumes print boaz from DanMachi 7 on; DanMachi 6 calls Ottar a boar man, and DanMachi 7 also prints boarman.[@fm06-boaz-boar-man, fm07-boaz-first, fm07-boaz-boarman]
- **Example:** [[ottar|Ottar]].[@so04-boaz] He is captain of [[freya-familia|Freya Familia]] and a first-tier adventurer opposed to [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]].[@so04-boaz-captain] Under the healer [[heith-velvet|Heith Velvet]]'s reproachful look Ottar's imposing face does not change, but his boar ears bend slightly: he finds her hard to deal with, because he always leaves her and the Andhrímnir to deal with the aftermath of the daily "baptism" on the killing grounds and feels indebted to her.[@fm18-boaz-ears] As a fellow animal person, [[anakity-autumn|Anakity Autumn]] feels awe before relief or joy when the boaz warrior arrives carrying his enormous black sword.[@so12-boaz-awe]

### Werewolf {#werewolf}

**Werewolves** ({{ja|狼人}}, written *wolf person*)[@werewolf.fm11-ja] are wolf animal people, with wolf ears.[@fm18-transform, ss02-ears] They also have tails.[@fm06-werewolf-form] Long golden hair is rare among them.[@so08-werewolf-tribe] They are the classic example of transformation: bathed in moonlight, they transform and gain a strength behind the saying that no race can match a werewolf under the moon.[@fm18-transform, so08-moon] They can transform only on a moonlit night, but once under the moon they do so without risk, unlike [[ottar|Ottar]], whose transformation wears his body down.[@fm18-werewolf-transform] For the same reason they were long thought the race least suited to the Dungeon, where there is no moon.[@so06-transform] DanMachi 3 adds that werewolf beast people have a lone-wolf streak and can be inflexible.[@fm03-werewolf, fm03-ja-werewolf] A lone-wolf werewolf and a proud elf make a poor match, as Bete and [[filvis|Filvis Challia]] show from the start.[@so03-werewolf-elves]

- **Traits:** Their superior sense of smell lets [[bete-loga|Bete Loga]] detect recent human activity in the old sewer.[@so02-werewolf-scent] Werewolves particularly detest [[monsters#loup-garou|loup-garous]], the warlike wolf monsters.[@fm14-werewolf-enemy] To call it a monster version of the werewolf on the surface is to be beaten up by animal people, for packs of loup-garous brought most of the reports of villages destroyed on moonlit nights.[@fm14-werewolf-loup-garou]
- **Transformation:** Transformation releases their bestial nature and strength, making them more ferocious and stronger.[@so06-werewolf-change] Bete's transformed appearance includes bristling gray hair that seems longer and split pupils in amber eyes.[@so06-werewolf-appearance] On the surface, the transformation is what one must most beware of when fighting a werewolf, for a werewolf under the moon is said to surpass every race in physical ability.[@so08-werewolf-surface]
- **Northern tribe:** Bete was born in the north among the Beastmen of the Plains, a wandering hunting tribe independent of cities and countries.[@so08-werewolf-north] His tribe worshipped no god, fought without a [[falna|Falna]], refused recruitment under its chief's ancestral teachings, and taught its children to take what they wanted.[@so08-werewolf-custom] Werewolves were among them, and the tribe was strong down to its women and children: it brushed aside the monsters of the surface and had, besides the great power of a race that awakens on moonlit nights, wild skills and tactics built up over its history.[@so08-werewolf-tribe] Bete considers howling an oath that marks his resolve.[@so08-werewolf-oath]
- **Example:** [[bete-loga|Bete Loga]], who transforms in Sword Oratoria 6 and 8.[@so01-bete, so06-transform, so08-howl]
- **Other werewolves:** DanMachi 8 names [[rose|Rose]] as a werewolf.[@fm08-werewolf-rose] DanMachi 13 names [[turk-sledd|Turk Sledd]] as a werewolf.[@fm13-werewolf-turk] Sword Oratoria 13 identifies [[cole|Cole]] as a werewolf student.[@so13-werewolf-cole] Familia Chronicle 3 identifies Iselina as a werewolf.[@fc03-werewolf-iselina] It also names [[minor-characters#neze|Neze Rankett]] as a werewolf middle guard of [[astrea-familia|Astrea Familia]].[@fc03-werewolf-neze] Rose is relied on as a coordinator among the Guild receptionists (DanMachi 15), and Cole is so frank and good-natured that [[lefiya|Lefiya]] doubts he is a werewolf like Bete (Sword Oratoria 13).[@fm15-werewolf-rose, so13-werewolf-cole-nature]

### Renart {#renart}

**Renarts** ({{ja|狐人}}, written *fox person*)[@renart.fm07-ja] have fox ears and tails.[@fm08-fox] They are "the only ones who are natural-born magic users" among animal people. Their spells differ from elves' magic and are sometimes counted as Rare Magic, and in the Far East they are called sorcerers.[@fm07-renart] Renarts are extremely rare.[@fm07-rare] In Orario the trade in people goes on because customers seek rare races, renarts first among them.[@fm07-renart-trade] A [[killing-stone|killing stone]] can seal a renart's magic so that a third party can use their sorcery.[@fm07-killing]

- **Language:** Renarts have a language of their own: a coded letter in DanMachi 11 mixes words of the prums' language and of the renarts' language.[@fm11-renart-language]
- **Sorcery and the killing stone:** Their magic is called sorcery; [[haruhime|Haruhime]]'s, the Level Boost, raises its target's Level by one.[@fm09-renart-sorcery, so12-renart-haruhime] The killing stone is an item for renarts alone, and one of its materials is made from a renart's remains.[@fm07-renart-killing-stone] Forebears of humanity made it, and its makers, it is said, were themselves renarts.[@fm07-renart-killing-stone] The sacrificed renart is left a soulless shell; tearing the soul out of the living is why the stone is forbidden.[@fm07-renart-killing-stone] Moving the power into the stone is said to tear the soul from the body with extraordinary pain; put back into the body, the stone wakes the renart, who should then live on normally if the body is unharmed.[@fm07-renart-ritual, fm07-renart-killing-stone]
- **Example:** [[haruhime|Haruhime]].[@fm07-haruhime] Bell guesses that Haruhime comes from the [[far-east|Far East]] from the lands where renarts are found, her unusual name and her manner.[@fm07-renart-far-east] She converted from [[ishtar-familia|Ishtar Familia]] to [[hestia-familia|Hestia Familia]].[@so12-renart-haruhime]

## Other beings {#other-beings}

### Gods {#god}

{{nocite}} See [[falna|Falna]] for the gods' blessing and the deity pages for individual gods.

**Gods and goddesses** are not mortal: Bell says that [[hestia|Hestia]] is a god, a *[[deusdea|Deusdia]]* or transcendent being, one dimension apart from humans, demi-humans and monsters, and will not age as mortals do; the word is explained under [[tenkai-and-gekai#names|Tenkai and Gekai]].[@fm01-goddess, fm01-ja-deusdia] They descended to the mortal world about a thousand years ago; the Ancient Times ended and the Divine Era began.[@so07-divine, fm08-phiana] According to Hestia, as [[bell-cranell|Bell]] tells it, the gods came down because they were bored in [[tenkai-and-gekai|Tenkai]], their upper world, with endless time and nothing to do, and found entertainment in the mortals, their children; many of them apparently decided to stay for good.[@fm01-god-world, so01-god-descent] Most of what the gods do in the mortal world is for amusement or pleasure, but their true goal, for all but the evil gods who seek destruction, is the birth of a chosen one, a hero.[@fm17-god-hero] By a rule agreed among themselves, they may not use their divine power, the [[tenkai-and-gekai#arcanum|Arcanum]], in Gekai, so they need food, clothing, a home and money.[@fm01-god-world] Every human or demi-human who has received a god's Blessing carries the gods' hieroglyphs on their back.[@so01-blessing] Mortals believe the gods created the humans and demi-humans; Lyu blames them for creating the elves, a superficial race unable to accept others.[@fm15-created]

- **Nature:** They take many forms, from old men to young girls.[@so01-god-banquet] Every god gives off a divine aura by which mortals know a god; a god can hold it back, and Bell has heard that using the Arcanum always releases a certain amount of it, so that the other gods find out.[@fm05-god-aura] Even with the Arcanum sealed, the children hold the gods in awe; the gods do not use their aura to press them down, because life in Gekai is a game to them and, above all, because they love the children.[@fm05-god-awe] Without the Arcanum they are physically equal to unblessed mortals, or weaker; they never age or die, but can fall ill.[@fm08-god-weak] No mortal can deceive them: they see through every lie, though not what it is, while [[loki|Loki]] says that gods often cannot see through other gods' lies.[@fc02-god-lies, so06-god-lies] Gods cannot polish themselves as mortals do, and [[miach|Miach]] watches the followers train with a little envy.[@fm12-god-growth] [[lefiya|Lefiya Viridis]] takes it that, like the spirits, the gods cannot bear children.[@so03-god-children] For a mortal to kill a god is taboo, an absolute rule, and only a god can strike down a god.[@fm06-god-fatal]

- **The Arcanum:** The rule follows their own aesthetic, so that they can enjoy life in Gekai as a game: powerless, they give mortals the Blessing in return for support, and raising rival factions is part of their entertainment.[@so01-god-game] A fatal wound makes the sealed Arcanum activate and keep the body alive; that counts as using it, and the god is forcibly sent back to Tenkai.[@fm06-god-fatal] When a patron god is sent back, the followers' [[status|Statuses]] are sealed for a time and they cannot fight.[@ar01-god-sealed] The one Arcanum permitted in Gekai is the Divine Mirror, a one-way clairvoyance allowed so that the gods can enjoy the mortal world's events; private use, if found out, gets a god sent back at once.[@fm03-god-mirror] Some gods can reduce their aura to nothing and live in the town as one of the children.[@fm17-god-mortal] When [[hermes|Hermes]] decides to go down himself, [[asfi|Asfi Al Andromeda]] objects that gods are forbidden to enter the [[dungeon|Dungeon]]; there Hermes says that the Dungeon hates the gods.[@fm05-god-dungeon, fm05-god-awe]

- **Among mortals and among themselves:** Each [[familia|Familia]] is formed under a god, who decides what it does, from opening a restaurant to even forming a country.[@so01-god-game, fm01-god-familia] Not all gods are on good terms, and when two are at odds, their Familias are enemies.[@fm01-god-world] Some gods call themselves "evil": under their direction the [[evils|Evils]] stood for the downfall of order, inciting rebellions across Orario.[@so04-evil-gods] A Banquet of the Gods is a gathering that only gods may attend.[@so01-god-banquet] Bell has heard that the mortals' ancestors did not refuse the gods but prized them as the givers of the Blessing.[@fm01-god-world]

- **Ancient Times and the Divine Era:** Before the gods came, magic was the preserve of certain races; the gods' Blessing lets anyone develop it.[@fm01-god-world] The present age, the Divine Era, is said to be one of "quality over quantity".[@fm08-god-era] After the [[three-great-quests#the-black-dragon|Black Dragon]] left, the first gods came down to the mortal world.[@fm16-god-dragon] In the Ancient Times, before the descent, people menaced by monsters worshipped imagined gods.[@fm18-god-worship] The heroes of history were guided by the gods through the [[#spirit|spirits]].[@so04-god-spirits]

### Spirits {#spirit}

**Spirits**, also called fairies, have many names: "Nymphs, spirits, elementals, jinn". They are very few compared with the other races. Stories call them the children most loved by the gods and the gods' alter egos, and say they are the closest of all to the gods.[@fm04-fairies] They use magic like the elves, and in the time before the gods having a fairy on one's side was the equivalent of a Falna today.[@fm04-fairies] They raise great fires and call storms, make lakes in forests and refine gold, silver and jewels, and their power may in part equal the gods', so that they work miracles.[@fm04-spirit-stories] In stories of heroes, many said to be based on truth, fairies guide the hero and lend him strength. For the spirits of old and the one corrupted in the Dungeon, see [[corrupted-spirit|Corrupted spirit]].[@fm04-fairies, fc03-spirits]

- **Kinds:** "Just like animal people", fairies are "classified by their element and place of origin": salamanders, sylphs, undines, gnomes and others.[@fm05-elements] In their chants the demi-spirits take the elemental spirits' names as their own: Tonitrus, "Incarnate of thunder", and Lux, Shade and Undine.[@so04-chants, so12-chants]
- **Rank:** there are many lesser spirits such as gnomes in Orario; intermediate spirits have dwindled, and great spirits are now the stuff of legend.[@fc03-spirits]
- **Gnomes:** spirits of the earth, who love jewels and rare metals and live in mines. Gnomes get on well in human society, and some run safe points for valuables.[@fm04-fairies, so14-gnomes, so12-gnomes]
- **Spirit cloths:** [[equipment#salamander-wool|salamander wool]] resists fire and [[equipment#undine-cloth|undine cloth]] resists water; Gareth carries gnome toga and Tonitrus mohair flags.[@fm05-elements, so12-cloth] Cloth that carries their blessing is said to lack all-round strength but to be strong in one element, and in that field to outdo even High Smiths' work.[@fm05-spirit-cloth] In [[zolingam|Solingen]], the sword-making city, the spirits are said to have given the smiths a spirit forge and spirit cloths such as salamander wool and undine cloth.[@fc03-spirit-forge]
- **No children:** spirits cannot have offspring.[@fm08-children, so05-children] Even so, [[welf-crozzo|Welf]]'s Crozzo line traces its magic swords to a fairy's blood given to its Ancestor (see [[magic-sword|Magic sword]]).[@fm04-fairies] [[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz Wallenstein]] too is called the one "who inherited the blood of spirits", and Loki says that the [[aria|Aria]] whom the corrupted spirit seeks can only be "either an actual spirit or someone who has the blood of a spirit in their veins", that is, Aiz.[@so11-aiz-spirit-blood, so12-aria-spirit-blood]
- **The spirits of old:** Before the gods descended, up to the Ancient Times, the spirits were receivers of the gods' will: guides and weapons that some of the gods sent out to rid the mortal world of monsters.[@so04-spirit-old] Humanity, and many heroes above all, received their protection and drove out the monsters at their voices, and many spirits were sent to the Dungeon, the old Orario.[@so04-spirit-old] On Orario's Bridge of Heroes, beside several of the heroes' statues stand the great spirits said to have aided their feats.[@fm16-spirit-statues] In fairy tales, above all tales of heroes, romances between spirits and humans or demi-humans often end in tragedy.[@fm08-spirit-romance]
- **Spirits today:** Bell has heard that since the gods came down contact with the spirits has begun but, apart from a few, has hardly progressed; he supposes they are whimsical.[@fm04-spirit-stories] The gods' Blessing has made the spirits less valued than before, but humans and demi-humans still long for them.[@fm04-spirit-stories] Welf Crozzo says that spirits like to live in lands rich in nature, and supposes that the magic swords drove them, like the elves, from their homes.[@fm04-spirit-stories] A spirit storm seals the sleeping [[three-great-quests#the-black-dragon|Black Dragon]], and Bell reflects that the peace of the mortal world is kept even now by many people and spirits.[@fm20-spirit-peace]
