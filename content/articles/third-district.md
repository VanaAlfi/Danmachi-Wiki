The **Third District** is in southeastern [[orario|Orario]], between East Main Street and Southeast Main Street.[@fm10-location] It contains [[soma-familia|Soma Familia]]'s headquarters and [[daedalus-street|Daedalus Street]].[@fm10-location] [[pleasure-quarter|The Pleasure Quarter]] occupies the third and fourth districts around Southeast Main Street, with [[ishtar-familia|Ishtar Familia]]'s prostitutes living in the third.[@fm07-quarter]
