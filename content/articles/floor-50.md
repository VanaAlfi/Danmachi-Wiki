**Floor 50** is a [[dungeon|Dungeon]] [[safe-point|safe point]] containing an ashen forest, used by [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]] as an expedition base.[@so01-safe] Monsters are not born on this floor, substantially reducing the risk of sudden attacks and making it useful for rest.[@so01-safe] A steep passage through a hole in its western wall connects it to [[floor-51|Floor 51]], with ascent more difficult than descent.[@so01-exit, so04-exit]

## Landscape {#landscape}

Blue streams branch among tall trees beneath faintly phosphorescent ceiling pillars, and [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]] places its camp on a huge boulder overlooking the gray forest.[@so04-landscape]
