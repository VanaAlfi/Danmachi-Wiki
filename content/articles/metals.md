**Rare metals** are the materials of the best weapons and armour in the English novels. Some come from the [[dungeon|Dungeon]] itself: on DanMachi 14's expedition [[welf-crozzo|Welf]] finds "a natural Dungeon ingot, glittering with the sheen of rare metal", and in Sword Oratoria 2 Tiona is delighted to find rare metal just beneath the surface of a Dungeon wall.[@fm14-ingot, so02-rare] Gnomes, the spirits of the earth, love rare metals, ores and jewels and can find veins of minerals and ores.[@so14-gnomes]

{{nocite}} This page gathers the named metals, one searchable section per metal, like the [[magic|Magic]] and [[monsters|Monsters]] pages.

## Adamantite {#adamantite}

"Adamantite was among the finest of rare metals." It is exceedingly hard, which makes it difficult to process and forge; even famous High Smiths struggle to control it.[@fm14-adamantite] It is mined in the Dungeon, and the purest comes from the deep levels.[@fc01-vault, fc01-purity] In Familia Chronicle 1 a merchant buys up large amounts of it to build himself an underground vault.[@fc01-vault]

[[knossos|Knossos]] has "hallways constructed with adamantite" behind its orichalcum doors.[@fm10-knossos, so07-knossos]

**Dir adamantite** is "a lighter, processed version of the metal".[@fm14-adamantite] Welf forges Bell's armour from it: durable and lightweight, by DanMachi 12 it is on its "fifth reincarnation".[@fm12-armor] In DanMachi 13 its shield, which had withstood even the black minotaur's blows, is demolished.[@fm13-shield] In DanMachi 15 a healer's cast is made of dir adamantite instead of plaster.[@fm15-cast] The text prints both *dir adamantite* and *dir-adamantite*.[@fm12-armor, fm14-adamantite]

## Mythril {#mythril}

"Lighter and stronger than iron, mythril was also far more malleable and easier to work with."[@fm01-mythril] The [[hestia-knife|Hestia Knife]] is forged from Hephaistos's mythril.[@fm01-knife] Forged mythril is very strong ("Even top-class adventurers can’t snap ’em") and very conductive of magic.[@fm07-shackles] Welf explains that mithril is easy to work with and conducts magical power well, so "Mithril weapons are common for fighters who combine magic and ordinary combat ability".[@fm12-welf]

- Bete's mythril boots, Frosvirt, absorb magic.[@so03-frosvirt]
- In Meren a mythril grating seals the sewer outlet against large monsters, and mythril, the most expensive ingot, is reserved for the boats of the wealthy.[@so06-grate, so06-boats]
- In Sword Oratoria 11 [[asfi|Asfi]] holds up a mythril orb, the key she made for Knossos.[@so11-key]

**Name:** DanMachi 1, 7, 8 and 10, Astrea Record 3 and the Sword Oratoria volumes print *mythril*; DanMachi 12, 18 and 20 print *mithril*.[@fm01-mythril, fm12-welf]

## Orichalcum {#orichalcum}

Orichalcum is "a masterpiece ingot that can be forged into unbreakable Durandal-class items" and "surpasses even adamantite". It is the densest rare metal in the world, made by blending various materials with human and demi-human techniques, and "physically impossible to destroy".[@fm10-orichalcum] Sword Oratoria 7 calls it the master ingot of the Durandal from which Superior-grade weapons such as Aiz's [[desperate|Desperate]] are forged, "easily the highest-grade material in the entire world"; its method of manufacture, like mythril's, is said to have been first established in the Ancient Times, when the monsters forced the races to unite.[@so07-orichalcum]

The doors of [[knossos|Knossos]] are orichalcum.[@fm10-knossos, so07-knossos]

The [[school-district|School District]]'s Alchemy Department is "a world-leader in rare metals work, including the manufacture of orichalcum", including master ingots, "pieces of orichalcum refined to the limit".[@fm19-alchemy] In DanMachi 19 its orichalcum production is up forty percent on the year before, and the [[guild|Guild]] means to use it for a giant shaft into the Dungeon without needing adamantite or other alloys; in DanMachi 20 the shaft is to carry people and materials to the lower floors as an elevator.[@fm19-shaft, fm20-shaft]

## Damascus {#damascus}

Damascus steel is "a foreign material used to forge weapons".[@fm10-damascus] In Sword Oratoria 9 Aiz's custom sword, made for her by [[goibniu-familia|Goibniu Familia]], is made of Damascus imported from outside the city; its other name is "rippled steel", for the wave pattern on the blade.[@so09-damascus] The sword cracks in her fight with a dragon.[@so09-cracks] Minor Myths and Legends 2 shows weapons forged from Damascus steel for sale in a market.[@ss02-market]

## Scarletite {#scarletite}

"Scarletite is a miraculous metal created in the Far East", equal to orichalcum in value and rarity; in Sword Oratoria 13 a School District student argues for recycling it into orichalcum.[@so13-scarletite] See also [[far-east|Far East]].[@so13-scarletite]
