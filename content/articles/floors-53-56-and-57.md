**Floors 53, 56 and 57** of the [[dungeon|Dungeon]] share maze-like passages, while expedition accounts describe an ambush on Floor 53 and dragons emerging from Floors 56 and 57.[@so04-layout, so04-ambush, so04-wyverns] These floors belong to the stretch from [[floor-51|Floor 51]] through 57, with graphite-colored, flat ceilings and walls and passages connecting rooms.[@so04-layout] [[monsters#wyvern|Ill wyverns]] use shafts opened by [[monsters#valgang-dragon|dragon]] fireballs to attack adventurers.[@so04-wyverns]

## Floor 53 {#floor-53}

| Volume | Events |
|---|---|
| Sword Oratoria 4 | On Floor 53, a masked figure directs caterpillar monsters whose acid attacks force [[finn-deimne|Finn Deimne]]'s party to keep moving.[@so04-ambush] |
| Sword Oratoria 7 | Finn Deimne recognizes the masked creature from the encounter on Floor 53.[@so07-recall] |
| Sword Oratoria 12 | The attack on Floor 53 is recalled during the investigation of the masked creature.[@so12-recall] |
| Sword Oratoria 12 | [[lefiya|Lefiya Viridis]] reports that it escaped [[riveria|Riveria Ljos Alf]]'s magic when the blizzard obscured visibility.[@so12-magic] |

## Floor 56 {#floor-56}

| Volume | Events |
|---|---|
| Sword Oratoria 4 | Ill wyverns emerge from side tunnels on Floors 56 and 57, exploiting shafts opened by the fireballs from [[floor-58|Floor 58]].[@so04-wyverns] |

## Floor 57 {#floor-57}

| Volume | Events |
|---|---|
| Sword Oratoria 4 | [[hyrute-sisters|Tione Hyrute]] proposes sheltering in the connecting passage to Floor 57, at the north end of Floor 58, to wait for the rest of the expedition.[@so04-retreat] |
| Sword Oratoria 4 | Caterpillar swarms subsequently block that route of retreat while the party fights below.[@so04-blocked] |
