**Raul Nord** is a human adventurer of [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]], twenty-one in Sword Oratoria 4.[@so04-raul] He is a trusted Level 4 who supervises the Familia's lower ranks.[@so04-level] DanMachi 11 calls him "a lackluster second-tier adventurer" with an "exceedingly average appearance", to whom the gods gave the alias "High Novice"; [[valletta|Valletta]] taunts him with the name in Sword Oratoria 7.[@fm11-novice, so07-novice]

## Character {#character}

Sword Oratoria 10 calls him "the spitting image of a normal person who lacked self-confidence": strong as a Level 4, yet "plainly daunted" by the Familia's first-tier adventurers, from Finn down.[@so10-normal] He still remembers [[bete-loga|Bete]] standing over him "with a sardonic laugh" while he fought back tears.[@so04-bete] [[anakity-autumn|Anakity]], who joined the Familia at about the same time, steadies him; when his nerves spread to the members he supervises, she reminds him: "It’s your duty as their superior to encourage, not start a panic."[@ar02-raul, so04-aki] He waits a long time to rise: in Sword Oratoria 6 the Familia's alternates are "stuck at Level 4", and in Sword Oratoria 13 he does not level up.[@so06-stuck, so13-raul]

## In the Great Conflict {#great-conflict}

Raul had been in [[orario|Orario]] only about a year when the [[great-conflict|Great Conflict]] began, and Loki had just told him he was "almost ready for a Rank Up".[@ar02-raul, ar01-young] [[riveria|Riveria]] counted him and Aki as "just supporters", and [[gareth|Gareth]] says that in coming to the city he "bit off a fair bit more than he could chew".[@ar01-age] During the fighting he serves as one of Finn's messengers.[@ar01-young] Pale and shaking, he still guides the townsfolk to safety, and the veteran [[noir-dyne-and-bahra|Noir]] sends him back to that work: "Stay off the battlefield, Raul. That’s where you work best".[@ar02-raul] Astrea Record 2 says that, stronger than others though he was, "he had an ordinary person’s heart".[@ar02-heart]
