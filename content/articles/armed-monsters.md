**Armed monsters** are monsters equipped with weapons and armor, including the intelligent [[xenos|Xenos]] groups encountered at [[rivira|Rivira]], on [[daedalus-street|Daedalus Street]] and in [[knossos|Knossos]].[@so12-equipment, fm10-rivira, so10-street] The armed monsters in Knossos are a group of Xenos carrying equipment, armor and weapons.[@so12-equipment] [[finn-deimne|Finn]] later explicitly calls the armed monsters Xenos to distinguish them from other monsters, citing their intelligence.[@so11-recognition]

## Distinguishing features {#distinguishing-features}

During the [[ganesha-familia|Ganesha Familia]] encounter, equipped monsters can be distinguished from ordinary unarmed monsters, but their exceptional strength is also apparent in combat.[@fm10-battle]

[[lyu-leon|Lyu]] judges them experienced fighters despite differences between individuals, and the attempted taming does not work on them.[@fm10-battle]

[[alicia-forestlight|Alicia]] says an armed siren saved her life deliberately, interpreting its protection as friendship.[@so11-recognition]
