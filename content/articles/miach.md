**Miach** is the god of [[miach-familia|Miach Familia]], a small Familia that brews and sells potions and other healing items.[@fm01-bell, ss01-medicine] He and [[hestia|Hestia]] are "the poorest of all the gods living in Orario", and that gives them a strong bond: from DanMachi 1 to the Great Familia War, he and his Familia help hers again and again.[@fm02-drinking, fm15-soft] He is deep in debt for the silver arm he bought his follower Nahza, and a soft touch who gives his potions away.[@fm04-loan, fm15-soft]

## Appearance and character

Miach is tall and lithe and carries himself like an aristocrat; his grey robe is worn, "worse for wear", but it only adds to his charm, and he looks "every bit the part of someone from Deusdia".[@fm04-looks, fm04-campanella] His long hair, tied behind his neck, is printed sea-blue, aquamarine, ocean-coloured and marine-blue, and Minor Myths and Legends 1 gives him ultramarine eyes of the same colour.[@fm02-drinking, fm04-looks, fm04-campanella, fm08-hair, ss01-medicine] He speaks in a polite, calm voice.[@fm02-drinking]

He is generous to a fault. When [[bell-cranell|Bell]] meets him in the street in DanMachi 1 he hands Bell potions for nothing: "What’s wrong with giving a neighbor a cup of sugar?"[@fm01-bell] [[miach-familia|Nahza]] scolds him for giving free potions to anyone and everyone, "That’s why we never have any money", and DanMachi 15 says he is well known among the gods of business Familias as a soft touch.[@fm04-scolding, fm15-soft] He works hard for customers: he visits Hestia when she founds her Familia with a vial in hand, saying there is no wasted time "when there’s a chance to get a new regular".[@fm04-campanella]

Women fall for him without his noticing. Nahza says girl after girl "is drawn to you like a moth to the flame" and calls him "really dense". In DanMachi 8, when he gives a young elf adventurer a potion as a gift, voices groan that he is "making girls fall for him without realizing it again": "It’s Miach, what do you expect?"[@fm04-scolding, ss01-flower, fm08-girls] In Minor Myths and Legends 1 he fixes a blue flower in a customer's hair as a bonus, admits he is "ignorant in matters of the heart", and wonders why his shop gets few repeat customers apart from Bell.[@ss01-flower] To Bell he says that a god's love "lasts but a moment" against the gods' long existence; like [[takemikazuchi|Takemikazuchi]], he would rather watch a beloved child find a partner and stay by their side as a god.[@fm08-love]

## Debt and the airgetlám

In DanMachi 4 Miach bows deeply to Hestia's group to apologise for Nahza's watered-down potions, of which he had known nothing.[@fm04-apology] He then tells them the story: after Nahza lost her right arm to a monster, he took out a loan to buy her the [[airgetlam|airgetlám]], a silver arm made by [[dian-cecht-familia|Dian Cecht Familia]], and the other members left: "All that stayed with Lord Miach was a useless former adventurer and a colossal loan." His Familia was demoted in rank.[@fm04-loan] The god [[dian-cecht|Dian Cecht]], his business rival, comes in person to ask "Well, Miach, where’s my money?"[@fm04-rival] Later in the story Miach hands Dian Cecht a vial of Nahza's new double potion; see [[miach-familia|Miach Familia]].[@fm04-double]

Poverty shapes his choices. When Bell has not visited the shop for a while in DanMachi 2, Nahza tells him that "Lord Miach was very lonely" and starving.[@fm02-lonely] In DanMachi 6 he did not want to spend money on Apollo's party until Hestia said it would be good for Nahza, and Hestia and Bell paid for their formal clothes and carriage.[@fm06-party] He cannot lend Hestia his only follower for the [[war-game|War Game]]: without members his Familia would be disbanded, and he might have to sell his home to pay his debts.[@fm06-only] After Nahza breaks the airgetlám in DanMachi 18's war, she agrees with him that the return was "well worth the price".[@fm19-arm]

He is fond of his follower. In DanMachi 4 he strokes Nahza's hair and asks a "solemn request" of her as "the god who watches over you more than any of the other children"; she whispers "So dense…".[@fm04-double] In DanMachi 13 he tells Hestia, "I feel the most at ease with Nahza by my side."[@fm13-nahza]

## The dark age

Astrea Record 1 shows a larger Miach Familia. When the [[evils|Evils]] attack the city, Miach and Dian Cecht open their medical stores to anyone in need.[@ar01-front] Miach leaves the north-western front to Dian Cecht and heads south to [[central-park|Central Park]] with an armed escort and his captain, Slane. Before he goes, Nahza, who is to be his escort, and [[amid|Amid]] of Dian Cecht Familia quarrel over him until he stops them.[@ar01-escort, ar01-front] He reproaches the two gods' rivalry: "Please, must we always quarrel like this?"[@ar01-front]

## Hestia's friend

Hestia had never met Miach before descending, but he helped her a few times and they became casual friends.[@fm04-campanella, fm15-soft] In DanMachi 1 he is the only god Bell knows personally besides Hestia; "half the reason", Bell says, is that both are in "bottom-of-the-barrel Familias". Bell sees "the look of royalty" in him, even in a plain grey robe.[@fm01-bell] In DanMachi 2 Hestia drags him into a bar to drown her sorrows; he listens without an angry face, then carts her home in his item cart.[@fm02-drinking]
