**Merrill** is a [[races#prum|prum]] spellcaster belonging to [[hermes-familia|Hermes Familia]].[@so03-mage, ss02-remembrance] She wears a pointed hat and casts using a short rod.[@so03-mage]

## History {#history}

| Volume | Events |
|---|---|
| Sword Oratoria 3 | At [[asfi|Asfi Al Andromeda]]'s request, she launches a fireball that burns an entrance through a fleshy barrier so the party can enter.[@so03-mage] |
| Minor Myths and Legends 2 | At a gathering remembering fallen Familia members, she welcomes [[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz Wallenstein]] with the others and encourages her to tell the dead about the sights she reaches.[@ss02-remembrance] |
| Sword Oratoria 11 | During the [[knossos|Knossos]] assault, Asfi Al Andromeda directs her Familia to support [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]] around Merrill's position.[@so11-support-1, so11-support-2] |
| Sword Oratoria 11 | [[falgar-batros|Falgar Batros]] protects her with a large shield while she prepares a magical barrage, and later tries to cover her and the others during the collapse.[@so11-support-1, so11-support-2, so11-collapse] |
| DanMachi 17 | [[hermes|Hermes]] includes Merrill among followers entrusted with fragments of a message, distributing the information to reduce the danger of their thoughts being reset.[@fm17-messages] |
| DanMachi 17 | Her report also relays that sounds like fighting continue inside [[freya-familia|Freya Familia]]'s home.[@fm17-report] |
