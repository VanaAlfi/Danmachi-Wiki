**Resheph** ({{ja|ラシャプ}}, read *Rashap*) and **Resheph Familia** were recruited by [[geography#kaios-desert-countries-and-towns-warsa|Warsa]]'s royal family after its existing military failed to conquer [[ali#shalzad|Shalzad]].[@fc02-origin, fc02-resheph-ja] The god and his followers were not born in the desert; his aim was to spread chaos in the lower world, rather than win the war.[@fc02-origin, fc02-purpose]

## History {#history}

| Volume | Events |
|---|---|
| Familia Chronicle 2 | The familia broke through Shalzad's defenses and took its capital; a messenger later confirmed Resheph's claim that the soldiers at Serein had been wiped out.[@fc02-origin, fc02-serein] Its officers had all ranked up at least once, and Ali reported that it possessed several [[kaios-desert#kingdoms-and-familias|kavir]], warriors who had ranked up twice.[@fc02-strength, fc02-kavir] Seal, its elf captain and a Level 4 [[hexers|hexer]], used [[magic#hal-reshef|Hal Reshef]]; Marzner was a human follower who announced himself as Level 2.[@fc02-seal, fc02-hexer, fc02-curse, fc02-marzner] Resheph proposed attacking [[israfan|Israfan]] to draw out [[ali|Ali]], while Warsa soldiers attracted by his followers' strength promised to convert to his familia.[@fc02-israfan] Resheph's trump card was a [[monsters#basilisk|basilisk]], entrusted to Iza, his only tamer.[@fc02-tamer, fc02-basilisk] Resheph gave Iza a whip that he said he had obtained from the [[evils|Evils]], but the basilisk resisted its control and killed the tamer.[@fc02-tamer, fc02-basilisk] After the battle, all of the familia's officers, including Seal, were dead; Resheph escaped, and [[freya|Freya]] dismissed pursuing him as troublesome.[@fc02-dead, fc02-fled] Warsa declined after the [[ali#calamity-of-the-hot-sands|Battle of the Sindh]], and Resheph Familia did not threaten Shalzad again.[@fc02-aftermath] |
