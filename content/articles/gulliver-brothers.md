The **Gulliver brothers**, Alfrik, Dvalinn, Berling and Grer, are prum quadruplets of [[freya-familia|Freya Familia]], known together as *Bringar*, the Four Knights of the Golden Flame.[@fc02-bringar] Each is Level 5, but their wordless, simultaneous teamwork is counted the best in Orario and treated as equal to a Level 6.[@fm16-teamwork]

## The brothers

| Brother | Weapon |
|---|---|
| Alfrik | Spear[@fm16-weapons] |
| Dvalinn | Hammer[@fm16-weapons] |
| Berling | Axe[@fm16-weapons] |
| Grer | Greatsword[@fm16-weapons] |

They joined Freya Familia after Hegni and Hedin and before Allen and Anya.[@fc02-origin] During the Great Conflict of Astrea Record 1 they were Level 4; by Familia Chronicle 2 they are Level 5.[@ar01-levels, fc02-bringar]
