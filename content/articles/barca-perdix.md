**Barca Perdix** is a descendant of the architect Daedalus and the man who keeps building and running [[knossos|Knossos]], the labyrinth whose completion has been his family's obsession for a thousand years.[@so07-orb, so11-life, so11-collapse] He follows [[thanatos|Thanatos]], the god of [[evils|the Evils]]' remnants, and is a hexer who forges their cursed weapons.[@so07-god, so11-life] He is the elder half-brother of [[ikelos-familia#dix-perdix|Dix Perdix]], who shares his mother.[@so07-dix, so11-life] In Sword Oratoria 11 he fuses with a [[corrupted-spirit|corrupted spirit]]'s seed and dies as a monster.[@so11-fusion, so11-end]

## Appearance and character

Barca is a human with sickly white skin and long bangs that hide one eye, with a dark bag under the other.[@so07-barca, so11-life] When he sweeps the bangs aside, his left eye shows the letter D, the mark that lets Daedalus's descendants work the labyrinth's mechanisms.[@so07-eye, so07-orb] His voice is soft and lifeless.[@so07-barca] Introducing himself to [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]], he says only "I am called…Barca. Nothing more", and the narration judges him a mage or hexer rather than a fighter.[@so07-trap]

Sword Oratoria 11 tells his life from his own side. He was shown Daedalus's Notebook moments after he was born and grew up a prisoner of its purpose: digging until his nails tore, expanding the labyrinth until he nearly starved, capturing women and forcing them to bear children, and carving out the eyes of relatives who killed themselves, to make new keys.[@so11-life] When he became Thanatos's follower, the god told him he had "a monster in your heart".[@so11-life] Out of necessity he developed the [[development-ability#enigma|Enigma]] ability, became a hexer and mass-produced cursed weapons; to complete Knossos he levelled up three times.[@so11-life, so11-amid]

He and Dix despised each other. Barca saw his brother, who hated Knossos, as an obstacle; the only fraternal feeling between them was not trying to kill each other, for their mutual benefit.[@so11-life] {{statement}} "We may share the same mother, but that is all," Barca tells Dix.[@so07-dix]

## The final battle

During Loki Familia's assault in Sword Oratoria 11, Barca goes to a hidden room with a mechanism that can bring down every support pillar on a floor. Dix had built it, Barca reflects, as a last act of rebellion against their ancestor. Barca sets it to collapse the eighth floor, where Loki Familia's allies are advancing, but hesitates, because the damage would put Knossos's completion beyond his lifetime.[@so11-collapse] In that moment [[asfi|Asfi]] appears out of thin air with a black helmet in her hand, and her blade cuts his neck. He drops Daedalus's Notebook, and [[hermes-familia|Hermes Familia]] takes it and destroys the mechanism.[@so11-collapse, so11-notebook]

Facing Hermes Familia and Loki Familia, Barca takes out an "extra" crystal orb: the same one, he says, that Hermes Familia bled for on Floor 24. Six seeds have already been released.[@so11-fusion] Crying that Knossos shall not die, he presses it into his chest. The fetus takes over his body, and he becomes a monster that kills with curses, empowered, [[amid|Amid]] realises, by his Enigma ability.[@so11-fusion, so11-amid]

Amid's [[magic#dia-frater|Dia Frater]] burns the curse away while its own curse withers her, and Tiona and Tione cut off its arms. As Finn's spear is about to strike, he stops: "It's dead." The D in its red eye has vanished. The narration leaves open whether Amid's devotion freed the man from his thousand-year curse, or whether he despised her for it.[@so11-end]
