**Bounties** are rewards offered for wanted people or dangerous monsters, issued by [[guild|the Guild]] or private parties.[@fm05-issuers, fm11-xenos-1, fm11-xenos-2] [[runoa|Runoa Faust]] earns her traveling expenses through bounty hunting while moving between Familias.[@fc01-profession]

## History {#history}

| Volume | Events |
|---|---|
| DanMachi 5 | [[lyu-leon|Lyu Leon]] loses her adventurer status, and people angered by her vengeance place a bounty on her.[@fm05-issuers] |
| DanMachi 11 | The Guild places bounties on the [[xenos|Xenos]] after monsters reach the surface, drawing adventurers away from Dungeon exploration.[@fm11-xenos-1, fm11-xenos-2] |
| DanMachi 11 | [[hestia|Hestia]] suggests that competition for the rewards discourages cooperation while making the Guild appear committed to suppression.[@fm11-purpose] |
| DanMachi 11 | False [[magic-stone|drop items]] displayed at headquarters support the announced resolution and disappoint the reward-seeking adventurers.[@fm11-end] |
| DanMachi 12 | The Guild records [[asterios|Asterios]] as a first-class bounty monster, and exceptionally powerful [[magic-stone|enhanced species]] also become bounty targets.[@fm12-monsters-1, fm12-monsters-2] |
| DanMachi 13 | Adventurers in [[rivira|Rivira]] recall an 80,000,000-valis bounty on Lyu Leon and seek the money.[@fm13-amount] |
| DanMachi 14 | According to [[aisha-belka|Aisha Belka]]'s account, [[bors|Bors Elder]] tries to protect Lyu Leon and her reputation through his explanation of the incident.[@fm14-protect] |
| DanMachi 14 | He nevertheless requests a share of bounty money, including the reward for [[rudra-familia|Rudra Familia]]'s Jura Harma.[@fm14-jura] |
| DanMachi 16 | Runoa Faust recalls accepting a contract against the [[gulliver-brothers|Gulliver brothers]] and suffering a crushing defeat.[@fm16-hunter] |
| Sword Oratoria 3 and 9 | [[olivas-act|Olivas Act]] is identified as a wanted man and later described as a bounty-bearing leader of [[evils|the Evils]].[@so03-wanted, so09-wanted] |
| Familia Chronicle 1 | During the city's [[evils|dark period]], hunters and assassins hired from outside pursue wanted people and enemies, changing sides according to payment.[@fc01-darkage] |
| Familia Chronicle 1 | Lyu Leon's privately issued wanted poster offers 80,000,000 valis and depicts a hooded, masked adventurer.[@fc01-poster] |
