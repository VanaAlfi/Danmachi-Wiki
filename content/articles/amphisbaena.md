The **Amphisbaena** is the two-headed [[monster-rex|Monster Rex]] of Floor 27, able to climb the Great Falls of the Water Capital. The [[guild|Guild]] estimates it at Level 6 in water and about Level 5 in raw ability.[@fm13-amphisbaena] DanMachi 14 compares it on paper with the Black [[goliath|Goliath]].[@fm14-level]

## Abilities

Its blue napalm burns even on water, and a crimson mist from its right head scatters magic.[@fm14-napalm] Damage from the battle collapses Floor 25's cavern.[@fm14-napalm]

## DanMachi 13–14

In the crisis of DanMachi 13 the Dungeon brings it back about two weeks early.[@fm13-amphisbaena] In DanMachi 14 the party on Floor 25 kills it: [[mikoto|Mikoto]] pins it with her gravity magic, [[ouka|Ouka]] cuts off the head that makes the mist, and [[aisha-belka|Aisha]], boosted by [[haruhime|Haruhime]], destroys its magic stone.[@fm14-kill] It leaves Amphisbaena bile as a drop item.[@fm14-kill] Cassandra's visions of the party's deaths do not come true.[@fm14-cassandra]
