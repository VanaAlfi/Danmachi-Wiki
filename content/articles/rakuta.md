**Rakuta** is a hume bunny supporter of [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]], newly ranked up to Level 3 in Sword Oratoria 2.[@so02-supporter, so06-tione] Her genius for mapping makes her the Familia's mapper in [[knossos|Knossos]]: [[finn-deimne|Finn]] plans the assaults of Sword Oratoria 11 and 12 on the map she made.[@so10-mapping, so11-map]

## Mapper

Finn sent Rakuta on the deep-level trip of Sword Oratoria 2 with [[lefiya|Lefiya]] because of her "genius at mapping". In Sword Oratoria 10 he sets her to map the hidden passages around a newly found door to Knossos.[@so10-mapping, so10-door] Inside Knossos with [[riveria|Riveria]]'s elf squad, she apologises that her map is "full of holes". Riveria takes it anyway, since it will serve them when they challenge Knossos.[@so10-mapping]

In Sword Oratoria 11 Finn spreads the map she created on his desk while he plans the assault.[@so11-map] During the assault she navigates for the main mapping squad, the animal people around [[bete-loga|Bete]], through the southeastern part of Knossos that she had first explored with Riveria. When they break through into the real Dungeon, she places them on its third floor from the terrain.[@so11-squad, so11-third] In Sword Oratoria 12 she guides [[raul-nord|Raul]]'s squad with a blueprint of Knossos and confirms that no large hidden spaces lie nearby.[@so12-blueprint, so12-hidden]
