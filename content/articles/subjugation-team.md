The **subjugation team** in DanMachi 10 is [[guild|the Guild]]'s [[ganesha-familia|Ganesha Familia]] force dispatched to [[dungeon|the Dungeon]]'s [[floor-18|Floor 18]].[@fm10-orders] [[shakti-varma|Shakti Varma]] commands the force of thirty adventurers, all [[level|Level]] 3 or above.[@fm10-command] Its duties include rescuing adventurers, particularly those exploring the middle floors and below.[@fm10-orders]

## Occurrences by volume {#occurrences-by-volume}

| Volume | Events |
|---|---|
| DanMachi 8 | The adventurers of [[rivira|Rivira]] form a temporary alliance, an extermination team, to kill the [[goliath|Goliath]] on Floor 17, which is reborn every two weeks; this time none of its members is Level 4 or above and only a handful are Level 3 (the English prints "none of the extermination team ranked above Level 4"), since [[rakia|Rakia]]'s invasion has drawn the upper-ranked Familias outside the city wall.[@fm08-extermination] |
| DanMachi 10 | [[guild|The Guild]] orders the team to tame the monsters that destroyed [[rivira|Rivira]], causing confusion before departure.[@fm10-taming] |
| DanMachi 10 | [[bell-cranell|Bell Cranell]] joins disguised as a supporter, with the team keeping his presence secret, while [[fels|Fels]] accompanies him invisibly.[@fm10-companions] |
| DanMachi 10 | On [[floor-18|Floor 18]] the thirty-member team fights the [[armed-monsters|armed monsters]]; bound by the order to tame them, it cannot fight at full strength, and a gargoyle, a siren and a lizardman among them can match its first-tier adventurers.[@fm10-forest] |
| DanMachi 10 | A monster defeats its first-tier adventurers individually before overwhelming the remaining members.[@fm10-defeat] |
| DanMachi 11 | [[bell-cranell|Bell Cranell]] hears from [[lilliluka-erde|Lilliluka Erde]] that [[ganesha-familia|Ganesha Familia]]'s mission party was in danger of destruction.[@fm11-report] |
| Sword Oratoria 3 | Adventurers facing the monster processions wait for [[guild|the Guild]] to dispatch an extermination team before continuing.[@so03-wait] |
| Sword Oratoria 10 | [[guild|The Guild]] dispatches [[ganesha-familia|Ganesha Familia]] to [[floor-18|Floor 18]] and permits only that force to enter [[dungeon|the Dungeon]] through [[babel|Babel]].[@so10-dispatch] |
