**Brigit** is the goddess who loved [[edas-village#kam|Kam]] and saved his life before being returned to heaven.[@fm08-bond, fm08-sacrifice] She has blond hair and red eyes, and [[hestia|Hestia]] calls her a friend from heaven.[@fm08-bond] Kam was her only follower and says that they loved each other.[@fm08-bond]

## History {#history}

| Volume | Events |
|---|---|
| DanMachi 8 | More than fifty years before Kam's account, monsters attacked them while they were traveling, and Brigit saved him by pushing him toward a cliff before she was returned to heaven.[@fm08-sacrifice] |
| DanMachi 8 | Kam later settled in the village, preserving his sealed Status as the bond with his goddess.[@fm08-sacrifice] |
| DanMachi 8 | As he approaches death, Hestia reassures him that Brigit will find him and speaks words of love as she believes Brigit would.[@fm08-promise, fm08-farewell] |
| DanMachi 8 | Kam answers with love for Brigit in his last words.[@fm08-farewell] |
| Sword Oratoria 9 | [[aiz-wallenstein|Aiz Wallenstein]] reflects on Kam's peaceful death and the bond with Brigit that Hestia restored through remembrance.[@so09-remembrance] |
