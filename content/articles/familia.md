A **Familia** is a god's faction whose members receive that god's [[falna|Blessing]] and provide support, with membership, leadership and obligations illustrated throughout the novels.[@fm01-definition] [[bell-cranell|Bell]] describes a Familia as a faction formed around a god, whose mortal members receive the god's Blessing and provide for that deity.[@fm01-definition] He regards joining one as joining the god's family; this family comparison is his own explanation of the relationship.[@fm01-definition]

Not every Familia lives by exploring the [[dungeon|Dungeon]]: Bell finds Familias that run taverns as quest brokers and "a knowledge shop of sorts",[@fm04-familia-types] and [[welf-crozzo|Welf]] explains that as a smithing Familia, [[hephaistos-familia|Hephaistos Familia]] was not required to go on expeditions.[@fm12-familia-smiths]

The Guild registers Familias by type: a Dungeon-type Familia, a kind found only in Orario, has little paperwork and low taxes but must show results in the Dungeon, unlike a commercial Familia, and Hestia registers hers as Dungeon-type.[@fm12-familia-types]

Each god decides what their Familia does.[@fm01-familia-god-chooses] The god also sets the Familia's rules, and being subject to them is called the one major downside of belonging to a Familia.[@fm07-familia-rules]

## Membership and Status {#membership-and-status}

A member's [[status|Status]] is inscribed with [[ichor|divine blood]], and the god updates the writing to reflect growth.[@fm01-status]

[[falna#conversion|Conversion]] is a ceremony of renewed contract that transfers a follower from one Familia to another, as [[soma|Soma]] and [[hestia|Hestia]] do for [[lilliluka-erde|Lilly]].[@fm06-conversion]

A transferred follower must wait one year before another Conversion; a faction that loses all its members can no longer call itself a Familia.[@fm06-conversion, fm06-empty] Leaving a Familia, which means rewriting the Falna on the follower's back, requires the god's permission.[@fm06-familia-leave] In DanMachi 17 Hermes reminds Freya of a broader rule the gods set for themselves: at least one year's membership is required before a child can convert to another Familia.[@fm17-familia-year]

[[bell-cranell|Bell]]'s [[guild|Guild]] profile includes Familia affiliation alongside race and personal history.[@fm01-guild]

An adventurer must first join a Familia, because registering with the Guild requires a Falna.[@fm15-familia-join] Whom to admit is the god's decision, which turns on whether the applicant can benefit the Familia.[@fm07-familia-admission]

Not every member is an adventurer: a noncombatant member belongs to a Familia without having received the god's Blessing, and several commercially focused Familias in Orario employ such members in their businesses.[@fm07-familia-noncombatant]

## Relations between Familias {#relations-between-familias}

A War Game provides a conflict governed by rules and a promised reward to the winner.[@fm06-war]

[[bell-cranell|Bell]] explains that hostility between gods makes their Familias enemies and that members must avoid endangering their faction.[@fm01-relations]

Adventurers normally form parties with members of their own Familia, and a party mixing Familias is treated with caution.[@fm04-familia-parties] Romance with a member of an unfriendly Familia is avoided as well, since it puts one's own Familia in danger.[@fm08-familia-romance]
