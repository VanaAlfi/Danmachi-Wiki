**Hermes Familia** is the Familia of the god [[hermes|Hermes]], led by [[asfi|Asfi Al Andromeda]].[@fm05-asfi] It works nominally as a delivery service, which lets it pass through checkpoints freely; the [[guild|Guild]] also sends it outside the city to investigate where black-market goods are being smuggled.[@fm09-delivery]

The Familia hides its members' true Levels. In Sword Oratoria 3 one of them admits this, saying Asfi and the others can fight with the best.[@so03-hide]

## Lydis {#lydis}

Lydis was the Familia's captain in the dark age, with Asfi as her vice-captain.[@ar01-lydis] She was a human of striking beauty, with long ash-blond hair in a single plait over her right shoulder, who wore a white blouse and necktie. She was beautiful and unapproachable until she opened her mouth.[@ar02-lunch] Asfi complains that she is "like a female version of you, Hermes", leaving her all the boring work.[@ar01-lydis]

Over lunch at [[the-benevolent-mistress|The Benevolent Mistress]] in Astrea Record 2, Lydis tells Asfi to take over as captain if she dies, saying that followers of Hermes must patch up any hole and keep sailing. She tells Asfi, "Nobody else cares more than you", and calls her "our Perseus".[@ar02-lunch] Asfi takes it for one of her tasteless jokes.[@ar02-death]

On the night of the Great Conflict, Lydis gave her life so that a band of upper-class adventurers could escape.[@ar02-death] Hermes tells Asfi, "Lydis is dead. You're the captain now."[@ar02-captain] Afraid of getting everyone killed "just like Lydis", Asfi falters. [[falgar-batros|Falgar]], keeping his promise to Lydis to support the new captain, persuades her to lead.[@ar02-falgar-plea] Hermes later promises that they will all visit Lydis and the other fallen once the war is over.[@ar02-visit] In Astrea Record 3 Asfi fights on, careful not to let the deaths of Lydis and Ardee blind her.[@ar03-lydis]
