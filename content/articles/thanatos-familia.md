**Thanatos Familia** is the Familia of [[thanatos|Thanatos]], the God of Death, and part of the [[evils|Evils]].[@so07-army, so08-evils] Its members are ordinary people who have lost their loved ones and made a contract with the death god in the hope of meeting them again in the next life: "For the members of Thanatos Familia, death was a door to their dearest companions and the reason they so readily gave their lives."[@so07-army] The god and his contracts are described on [[thanatos|Thanatos]].

## Home {#home}

The Familia hides in [[knossos|Knossos]]. Sword Oratoria 11 describes a wide room of round pillars there as if it were the home of a Familia that "had nowhere to go aboveground".[@so11-home]
