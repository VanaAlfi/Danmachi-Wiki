**Apollo** is a god known among the gods as *Phallus the Passionate*.[@fm06-apollo] In DanMachi 5 he declares that he will claim [[bell-cranell|Bell Cranell]] as his own, and in DanMachi 6 he challenges [[hestia|Hestia]] to a [[war-game|War Game]] to get him.[@fm05-apollo, fm06-apollo]

## The War Game

Apollo stages a grievance to force the War Game, and under pressure agrees to allow one outsider, who must come from a Familia outside [[orario|Orario]].[@fm06-apollo, fm06-outsider] In the Castle Siege his Familia defends. Bell defeats his captain, [[hyacinthus|Hyacinthus]], in single combat, and [[hestia-familia|Hestia Familia]] wins.[@fm06-hyacinthus] Hestia then takes all the money held in Apollo's name.[@fm06-money]

## Exile

After losing, Apollo releases his followers, disbands Apollo Familia and is escorted out of Orario for good; he is exiled, not sent back to the heavens. Only a small group, including Hyacinthus, leaves with him; other former members go their own ways.[@fm06-apollo, fm08-alumni] His three-storey manor passes to Hestia Familia and becomes its new home.[@fm06-manor]
