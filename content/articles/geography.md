**Geography** covers the lands of the [[tenkai-and-gekai|lower world]] as the light novels describe them: the continent with [[orario|Orario]] on its westernmost edge, its regions, and the roads and port that connect them.[@fm20-continent-position, so06-continent-travel] The narration counts the [[dungeon|Dungeon]] and the [[valley-of-dragons|Valley of Dragons]] among the world's three great unexplored regions.[@so08-continent-frontiers] This page gives a section to each region and ends with a table of the places the novels name.

## The continent {#the-continent}

The **continent** ({{ja|大陸}}) is the landmass whose westernmost edge contains Orario, positioned toward the northwest at a high northern latitude.[@fm20-continent-position, fm20-continent-ja] The surrounding world includes island and ocean nations whose travellers reach the city through [[njordr-familia#port-meren|Port Meren]].[@so06-continent-travel]

### Regions and directions {#the-continent-regions-and-directions}

Near Orario, travellers point toward the [[beor-mountains|Beor Mountains]] to the north, the ocean connected to brackish lakes to the west, the land leading to the centre of the continent to the east, and unexplored lands far to the south.[@fc02-continent-directions] In the north, [[leon-verdenberg|Leon Verdenberg]] says that dragons descending from the Valley of Dragons repeatedly cause enormous destruction.[@fm20-continent-north] Across the centre, [[wishe-forest|Wishe Forest]] lies in a great river of trees and serves merchants and travellers crossing the continent.[@so13-continent-central-woods] The [[kaios-desert|Kaios Desert]] spreads far southwest of the central region where countries cluster, and [[freya|Freya]] reaches it by travelling southeast from Orario.[@fc02-continent-kaios]

### Routes and the Center of the World {#the-continent-routes-and-the-center-of-the-world}

Travellers already on the continent reach Orario along its northern and eastern roads, while people from the [[far-east|Far East]] and island or ocean nations first pass through Port Meren.[@so06-continent-travel] Port Meren stands about three kirlos southwest of Orario beside Lolog Lake, which connects with the sea.[@fm19-continent-meren] [[bell-cranell|Bell Cranell]] has heard that Orario's magic-stone trade mainly follows sea routes.[@fm19-continent-meren] Orario's title, the Center of the World, is explained by its protection by the world's greatest forces and by its magic-stone industry.[@fm08-continent-center-power, ar01-continent-center-trade]

### Three great unexplored regions {#the-continent-three-great-unexplored-regions}

The narration names the Dungeon and the Valley of Dragons among the world's three great frontiers ({{ja|世界三大秘境}}), and [[bete-loga|Bete Loga]] hears about the Dungeon from a passing peddler.[@so08-continent-frontiers, so08-frontiers-ja] [[riveria|Riveria Ljos Alf]] also calls the Dungeon one of the three great unexplored regions of the world and the reason why Orario is called the center of the world.[@so14-continent-dungeon-frontier]

## World powers and nations {#world-powers-and-nations}

The **world powers** include the [[empire|Empire]] and [[altena|Altena]], the nation of magic, while [[riveria|Riveria Ljos Alf]] describes [[telskyura|Telskyura]] as one of the few world powers besides Orario, with exceptional fighting strength.[@fc01-nations-world-powers, so13-nations-destinations, so06-nations-telskyura] Outside Orario, only famous cities and countries like these, the handful of world powers, can claim several people of Level 3 or higher.[@fc01-nations-world-powers]

### Dizara {#world-powers-and-nations-dizara}

Dizara ({{ja|海洋国}}, written *ocean nation*, read *Dizāra*) is named as a sea nation, and its regional architecture is recognized among Orario's buildings.[@so13-nations-destinations, fm07-dizara-architecture, so13-nations-ja] Dizaran marines are among the careers described to students of the [[school-district|School District]].[@fm19-dizara-marines] A recollection of conflicts with host countries includes Dizara's forced occupation of the School District's ship, the Hringhorni.[@fm20-dizara-hringhorni]

### Maelstra {#world-powers-and-nations-maelstra}

Maelstra {{small|printed *Mayrustra* in Familia Chronicle 1}} ({{ja|歌劇の国}}, written *country of opera*, read *Meirusutora*) is a country of opera that contributed foreign capital and expertise to Orario's entertainment facilities.[@so13-nations-destinations, fc01-maelstra-entertainment, so13-nations-ja] [[finn-deimne|Finn Deimne]] says he would like to go north to see its opera's reported performance about the [[races#prum|Phiana knights]].[@so14-maelstra-opera]

### Eluria {#world-powers-and-nations-eluria}

An investigation by [[hermes-familia|Hermes Familia]] names the mansion of Elurian nobles, and a trafficker expects nobles there to pay highly for a living captive.[@fm09-eluria-nobles] [[hermes-familia#laurier|Laurier]] of Hermes Familia discovers imprisoned [[xenos|Xenos]] in an Elurian noble's mansion.[@fm16-eluria-laurier]

### Beltane {#world-powers-and-nations-beltane}

Princess Talvi describes Beltane as a remote mountain homeland more like a village than a country.[@ss02-beltane-princess] [[ali|Ali]] asks about a mysterious spring said to exist there.[@ss02-beltane-princess]

### Gizia {#world-powers-and-nations-gizia}

A winemaker reports experimenting successfully with Gizia's method of making wine in large jars.[@so11-gizia-vats]

### Orland {#world-powers-and-nations-orland}

A list of deeds by heroes still spoken of in legends includes the revival of Orland, the land of a ruined country.[@ar03-orland-restoration]

### Other countries and cities {#world-powers-and-nations-other-countries-and-cities}

[[ares-familia|Ares Familia]] forms the [[ares-familia#kingdom-of-rakia|Kingdom of Rakia]], a monarchy on the western side of the main continent.[@fm08-rakia-west] The [[far-east|Far East]] is [[haruhime|Haruhime]]'s island homeland.[@fm07-far-east-island] [[el-dorado-resort|Santorio Vega]] is named among Orario's sources of entertainment expertise.[@fc01-maelstra-entertainment] [[zolingam|Solingen]] {{small|printed *Zolingam*}} is an industrial swordmaking city east of Orario beyond the [[alv-mountains|Alv Mountains]].[@fc03-solingen-city] Bofman describes [[israfan|Israfan]] as a country prosperous through trade.[@fc02-israfan-country] [[ali#shalzad|Shalzad]] is described as the first great country of western-central Kaios after the war with Warsa.[@ss02-shalzad-status]

## Surroundings of Orario {#surroundings-of-orario}

The **surroundings of Orario** take in forests, mountains, the eastern plain, nearby settlements and Port Meren, which frame the routes around the city.[@fm04-seoro-landscape, fm08-eastern-plain, fm08-beor-geography, fm19-meren-lolog-geography] The lands around Orario include the eastern Deep Forest Seoro and Alb mountain range, the northern [[beor-mountains|Beor Mountains]], and the port southwest of the city.[@fm04-seoro-landscape, fm08-beor-geography, fm19-meren-lolog-geography]

### Deep Forest Seoro {#surroundings-of-orario-deep-forest-seoro}

Deep Forest Seoro ({{ja|セオロの密林}}, written *the dense forest of Seoro*) spreads at the foot of the Alb mountain range directly east of Orario.[@fm04-seoro-landscape, fm04-east-ja] Its trees are tall and thick-trunked, and wildflowers and moss flourish beneath them.[@fm04-seoro-landscape]

### Agris {#surroundings-of-orario-agris}

Agris is a town southeast of Orario near the Shreme Castle ruins, reached in a full day by carriage.[@fm17-agris-town] Before the [[war-game|War Game]], [[hestia|Hestia]] tells Bell Cranell to ride a caravan's cart to Agris, a town close to the old castle, and to follow the Guild's instructions from there.[@fm06-agris-transfer] In DanMachi 17, [[asfi|Asfi Al Andromeda]] and [[lyu-leon|Lyu Leon]] take refuge there and gather disguise supplies before returning to Orario.[@fm17-agris-town]

### Alb mountain range {#surroundings-of-orario-alb-mountain-range}

The Alb mountain range ({{ja|アルブ山脈}}; not the Alv Mountains, {{ja|アルヴ山脈}}) rises east of Orario, with Seoro at its base and its rugged heights visible from [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]]'s camp.[@fm04-seoro-landscape, fm08-eastern-plain, fm04-east-ja]

### Eastern plain {#surroundings-of-orario-eastern-plain}

Fifty kirlos east of Orario, Loki Familia camps on a plain overlooking the Alb range and Seoro while the city's alliance fights [[ares-familia#kingdom-of-rakia|Rakia]].[@fm08-eastern-plain] Rakia's invading army belongs to [[ares-familia|Ares Familia]].[@fm08-rakia-west]

### Bell's home village {#surroundings-of-orario-bells-home-village}

Bell Cranell describes his birthplace as a small village deep in distant mountains north of Orario, without a name on the map.[@fm07-bell-home-village]

### Other nearby places and roads {#surroundings-of-orario-other-nearby-places-and-roads}

The Beor Mountains stand directly north, while Bell believes the range to the northeast is the [[alv-mountains|Alv Mountains]], the elves' sacred peaks.[@fm08-beor-geography, fm20-alv-orientation] [[edas-village|Edas Village]] is hidden in a valley within the Beor range and is said to have originated as an elf settlement.[@fm08-edas-geography, so09-elf-edas] A hidden passage from the Beor foothills branches into northwestern Orario.[@fm17-beor-secret-road]

## Ruins near Orario {#ruins-near-orario}

Shreme and Orza are ancient defensive **ruins near Orario** that later serve as battlefields.[@fm06-shreme-fortress, fm18-orza-location, fm18-orza-ancient-city, fm20-shreme-later-use, fm18-orza-war-game] Shreme lies southeast of the city and Orza northwest, in the western [[beor-mountains|Beor Mountains]].[@fm06-shreme-location, fm18-orza-location] These sites preserve castles or a fortress city built against the monsters emerging from the great hole.[@fm06-shreme-fortress, fm18-orza-ancient-city]

### Shreme Castle ruins {#ruins-near-orario-shreme-castle-ruins}

Shreme ({{ja|シュリーム古城跡地}}, written *the old castle ruins of Shreme*) stands on a plain without woods or hills and was built before [[babel|Babel]] and the city wall to hold back monsters from nearby settlements.[@fm06-shreme-fortress, fm06-shreme-ja] [[ares-familia#kingdom-of-rakia|Rakia]] used it for a long time as a strategic point until more than a century ago, leaving functioning walls despite its decay.[@fm06-shreme-fortress] [[ganesha-familia|Ganesha Familia]] captured its resident bandits by tunnelling in one day without damaging the castle, and the tunnels were then filled.[@fm06-shreme-thieves] It hosted the [[war-game|War Game]] between [[hestia-familia|Hestia Familia]] and [[apollo-familia|Apollo Familia]], then became the second-round battlefield of the [[orariad|Orariad]] more than six months later.[@fm20-shreme-later-use]

### Orza ruins {#ruins-near-orario-orza-ruins}

Orza ({{ja|オルザの都市遺跡}}, written *the city ruins of Orza*) occupies an island near the southern part of a caldera lake, with its only bridge at the island's south end.[@fm18-orza-location, fm18-orza-bridge, fm18-orza-ja] Its origins go back more than two thousand years, and the account says it flourished as a self-sufficient fortress city until a powerful monster flew there.[@fm18-orza-ancient-city] Its large western temple, the house of the gods, enshrined statues of imagined gods before the gods' descent.[@fm18-orza-temple] Most ancient historical materials had already been recovered when [[hermes-familia|Hermes Familia]] proposed the ruins as a battlefield.[@fm18-orza-removals] [[freya-familia|Freya Familia]] defended the house of the gods during the [[war-game|Familia War]].[@fm18-orza-war-game]

### Other old castles {#ruins-near-orario-other-old-castles}

Many forts like Shreme were built relatively near Orario to obstruct monsters, and most had become ruins by the present.[@fm06-shreme-fortress]

## The north of the continent {#the-north-of-the-continent}

**North of Orario** lie a town destroyed by a dragon, lands whose communities fled south, and the unfinished Great Wall, beyond which a black wasteland leads to the [[valley-of-dragons|Valley of Dragons]].[@fm20-north-refugees, fm20-dragon-wall-builders, fm20-north-wasteland] The journey north from Orario passes between the [[beor-mountains|Beor Mountains]] to the west and the [[alv-mountains|Alv Mountains]] to the east.[@fm20-north-route] Farther north, the Great Dragon Wall separates the lands where humanity lives from an uninhabitable region.[@fm20-dragon-wall-structure]

### Northern communities {#the-north-of-the-continent-northern-communities}

Within less than two days the Beor range disappears from view, and on the journey the party reaches a ruined town.[@fm20-north-route, fm20-north-refugees] [[leon-verdenberg|Leon Verdenberg]] says that half its residents had already been lost when he arrived, and that he fought alongside [[finn-deimne|Finn]]'s [[loki-familia|Loki Familia]] to destroy the dragon that attacked it.[@fm20-north-refugees] He reports that communities north of that town fled south except one tribe, while [[nina-tulle|Nina Tulle]] says the elves of Fanache remain nearby to watch for danger and notify Orario.[@fm20-north-refugees]

### Great Dragon Wall {#the-north-of-the-continent-great-dragon-wall}

The Great Dragon Wall ({{ja|竜の長壁}}), also called the Great Wall ({{ja|大防壁}}), is stone, taller and thicker than Orario's city wall, and Leon says it extends from the continent's westernmost edge to its easternmost edge.[@fm20-dragon-wall-structure, fm20-dragon-wall-builders, fm20-north-ja] He says that Orario and many Familias, [[minor-familias#thor-familia|Thor Familia]] first among them, took part in building it, beginning about nine hundred years ago.[@fm20-dragon-wall-builders] Work and repairs continue without completion in sight.[@fm20-dragon-wall-builders] The breach the party crosses is about fifty meders wide, made by the dragon that destroyed the town, whose strength Leon estimates as Level 5 or perhaps Level 6.[@fm20-dragon-wall-structure, fm20-dragon-wall-damage] Leon explains that repairs east of the Alv range take priority because Orario is relatively close and its adventurers can be dispatched here.[@fm20-dragon-wall-priorities]

### Land beyond the wall {#the-north-of-the-continent-land-beyond-the-wall}

The black wasteland ({{ja|黒の荒野}}) extends from the wall to the Valley of Dragons, beneath a dark sky over dry, desolate ground.[@fm20-north-wasteland, fm20-north-ja] [[bell-cranell|Bell Cranell]] describes its air as like weak poison, survivable by followers who have levelled up but harmful to ordinary people.[@fm20-north-wasteland]

### Roaming dragons {#the-north-of-the-continent-roaming-dragons}

Leon distinguishes the still-sealed [[three-great-quests|Black Dragon]] of the Three Great Quests from other ancient dragons whose descents repeatedly devastate the north.[@fm20-north-valley-dragons] Nina says their cries drive monsters south and that the roaming dragon problem ({{ja|訪竜問題}}) has reportedly grown worse in recent years.[@fm20-north-monster-flight, fm20-north-ja] Professor Adler reports dragons slipping through the barrier established by the [[school-district|School District]].[@fm19-north-school-barrier] Nina says students are entrusted only with small valley dragons, while instructors deal with large ones.[@fm20-north-student-response]

## Preblica, Karna and Lonza {#preblica-karna-and-lonza}

Sword Oratoria 14's journey passes through riverside Preblica, the inn town Karna in the southwest of the continent's central region, the underground dwarf village of Lonza south of Karna, and the Celcebo mines west of Lonza.[@so14-preblica-site, so14-karna-geography, so14-karna-hub, so14-lonza-site, so14-celcebo-site]

### Preblica {#preblica-karna-and-lonza-preblica}

Preblica stands along a river at the base of a mountain, with stone buildings, paved roads and numerous inns serving travelling merchants.[@so14-preblica-site] In Sword Oratoria 14, a horde of monsters from the mountain breaks through its low stone walls while [[loki|Loki]] and [[finn-deimne|Finn Deimne]] are nearby.[@so14-preblica-monsters]

### Karna {#preblica-karna-and-lonza-karna}

Karna ({{ja|カルーナ}}) is an inn town stretching north to south, frequented by merchants and crossed by roads towards neighbouring countries and cities.[@so14-karna-geography, so14-karna-hub, so14-places-ja] The travellers reach it ten days after leaving Preblica.[@so14-karna-geography] Their onward journey to the Alf Royal Woods, north of Karna, takes three days in total, including approximately one day on foot from the nearest town.[@so14-karna-woods-route, so14-lonza-site]

### Lonza {#preblica-karna-and-lonza-lonza}

Lonza is a dwarf village in an underground space at the foot of a mountain range due south of Karna, opposite the royal woods.[@so14-lonza-site] Its cavern is large enough to contain a town, with a ceiling over ten meders high and buildings made of special rock.[@so14-lonza-site] Its sole inn, the Mole Inn ({{ja|土竜亭}}, written *earth-dragon inn* and read *mogura*, mole), has guest rooms on the second floor and a bar on the first.[@so14-lonza-mole-inn, so14-places-ja]

### Celcebo mines {#preblica-karna-and-lonza-celcebo-mines}

The Celcebo mines lie due west of Lonza, with dim magic-stone lighting and cold, exposed rock walls.[@so14-celcebo-site] Village craftsmen supply powerful powder for blasting as miners extend tunnels along deposits.[@so14-celcebo-site] After [[gareth|Gareth Landrock]], Finn Deimne and [[riveria|Riveria Ljos Alf]] slay the [[lambton#sword-oratoria-14-the-celcebo-mines|lambton]] that caused the cave-ins, the weakened miners are rescued and all survive, and Lonza celebrates for three days and nights, longer once Riveria wakes.[@so14-celcebo-rescue]

## Elven forests {#elven-forests}

The **elves' settlements** range from secluded sacred-tree villages to the Alf Royal Woods and the northern Frozen Woods of Fanache.[@so13-elf-seclusion, so14-alf-royal-site, so13-fanache-home] Elven forest settlements include secluded villages, the high elves' royal holy land and communities in the continent's north.[@so13-elf-seclusion, so14-alf-royal-site, so13-fanache-home] Many protect sacred trees and maintain boundaries with the outside world, despite adapting to the era of gods.[@so13-elf-seclusion]

### Alf Royal Woods {#elven-forests-alf-royal-woods}

The Alf Royal Woods ({{ja|アルヴの王森}}, written *the royal forest of Alv*) lie north of Karna and are the high elves' home, revered as an elven holy land alongside the [[alv-mountains|Alv Mountains]].[@so14-alf-royal-site, so14-lonza-site, so14-alf-royal-ja] A castle town surrounds the central royal sacred tree and its castle is built of [[metals#seiros|seiros]]; the dense canopy hides the sky, and what sunlight and moonlight gets through is scattered across the woods.[@so14-alf-royal-site, so14-alf-royal-canopy] [[finn-deimne|Finn Deimne]] describes strict exclusion of other races, with access permitted to a few royal merchants whose relationships date to ancient times; even approaching gods face magic.[@so14-alf-royal-law] The royal knights cast from horseback, while the prospect of retaliation by elves worldwide deters countries and familias from attacking.[@so14-alf-royal-knights, so14-alf-royal-law] In Sword Oratoria 14, a knight reports a goddess's intrusion and an oath with [[riveria|Riveria Ljos Alf]], after which trees and pursuing knights stand frozen.[@so14-alf-royal-incident]

### Lumirua Forest {#elven-forests-lumirua-forest}

[[lyu-leon|Lyu Leon]] comes from Lumirua Forest {{small|printed *Lyumilua Forest* in Astrea Record 1 and 2}} ({{ja|リュミルアの森}}), a secluded settlement beneath thick foliage, with its sacred tree in the deepest part.[@fm15-lyumilua-site, fm15-lumirua-ja]

### Frozen Woods of Fanache {#elven-forests-frozen-woods-of-fanache}

The Frozen Woods of Fanache ({{ja|白氷の森ファナシェ}}, written *the white-ice forest Fanache*) lie in the north of the continent and are [[alicia-forestlight|Alicia Forestlight]]'s homeland.[@so13-fanache-home, so13-fanache-ja] In DanMachi 20, [[nina-tulle|Nina Tulle]] reports that Fanache village's elves remain in the nearby forest to watch for danger and alert Orario, accepting that they would be the first to fall.[@fm20-fanache-watch]

### Elven villages and sacred trees {#elven-forests-elven-villages-and-sacred-trees}

Branches from village sacred trees are valuable weapon and staff materials, normally given to elves leaving home; Astrea Record 1 reports the [[evils|Evils]] pillaging villages and trafficking them into Orario.[@ar01-elf-sacred-branches] The [[holy-tree|holy tree]] is an object of elven protection and worship.[@ar01-elf-sacred-branches]

## Kaios Desert countries and towns {#kaios-desert-countries-and-towns}

The **western [[kaios-desert|Kaios Desert]]** holds Israfan's oasis town Leodo; northeast of Leodo, where Shalzad, Warsa and Israfan meet, lie the rocky Gazoob Wasteland and the sandy Sindh around it, and the Nire River divides the desert east and west.[@fc02-warsa-position, fc02-leodo-site, fc02-gazoob-site, fc02-sindh-site, fc02-kaios-nire] The western Kaios Desert contains neighbouring countries, oasis towns and contrasting sandy and rocky desert tracts.[@fc02-leodo-site, fc02-gazoob-site, fc02-sindh-site] [[israfan|Israfan]] lies west of the Nire River, which divides Kaios into eastern and western halves.[@fc02-kaios-nire]

### Warsa {#kaios-desert-countries-and-towns-warsa}

[[ali#bofman|Bofman]] places Warsa east of [[ali#shalzad|Shalzad]], whose capital is Solshana.[@fc02-warsa-position, fc02-shalzad-capital] The narrative describes Warsa's environment as especially poor and its livelihood as pillage and violence, making Shalzad's extensive oases a coveted target.[@fc02-warsa-position] Its royal family brings in the foreign [[resheph|Resheph Familia]] after its existing military fails to conquer Shalzad; the country also has gods subordinate to its military's patron.[@fc02-warsa-rule] Bofman estimates about eighty thousand troops, including mercenaries, while soldiers swearing to convert to Resheph's familia make the already unruly army increasingly undisciplined.[@fc02-warsa-army] He reports Warsa's soldiers burning and pillaging Leodo; the Battle of the Sindh later leads to Warsa's decline, and the whole series of battles comes to be called the [[ali#calamity-of-the-hot-sands|Calamity of the Hot Sands]].[@fc02-warsa-leodo-attack, fc02-kaios-hot-sands]

### Leodo {#kaios-desert-countries-and-towns-leodo}

Leodo ({{ja|リオードの町}}, the town of *Riōdo*) is a merchant town around a clear-water oasis near Israfan's border, described as almost central to western Kaios and relatively safe from monsters.[@fc02-leodo-site, fc02-kaios-ja] It has a southern [[desert-ship|desert-ship]] port, gates to north, east and west, trading bazaars in the south and goods for residents and travellers in the north.[@fc02-leodo-layout] Its slave market follows the oasis's southwest shore; the largest building is a domed, white-stone, gold-decorated mansion on the central island.[@fc02-leodo-layout] Oasis water and surrounding greenery moderate the night's cold.[@fc02-leodo-night] Rebuilding follows Warsa's attack, while the trading company of Bofman Fazoul withdraws from the slave trade.[@fc02-leodo-rebuilding]

### Gazoob Wasteland {#kaios-desert-countries-and-towns-gazoob-wasteland}

Northeast of Leodo, the Gazoob Wasteland ({{ja|ガズーブの荒原}}) is rocky desert where the borders of Shalzad, Warsa and Israfan meet, with open terrain suitable for a large battlefield.[@fc02-gazoob-site, fc02-kaios-ja]

### Sindh Expanse {#kaios-desert-countries-and-towns-sindh-expanse}

The Sindh Expanse ({{ja|シンドの砂原}}, written *the sand plain of Sindh*) is a sandy desert adjacent to and spreading around Gazoob, where the actual clash later called the Battle of the Sindh ({{ja|シンドの戦い}}) takes place.[@fc02-sindh-site, fc02-kaios-ja]

### Nire River {#kaios-desert-countries-and-towns-nire-river}

The enormous Nire River divides the desert east and west, with the narrative associating proximity to it with larger countries.[@fc02-kaios-nire]

### Silken Road {#kaios-desert-countries-and-towns-silken-road}

An eastward route towards the continent's centre is recommended to [[freya|Freya]] for its bazaars, said to prosper from the Silken Road ({{ja|織の道}}, written *the weaving road*).[@fc02-silken-road, fc02-kaios-ja]

## Ruined and remote lands {#ruined-and-remote-lands}

The novels name **ruined and remote lands**: the Scrap Heap, the remains of a destroyed country; Dedyne far south of Orario, which contains the Black Desert; and Heodenings, an isolated frontier island.[@fc02-scrap-heap-land, ar01-dedyne-location, ar03-dedyne-black-desert, fc02-heodenings-island]

### Scrap Heap {#ruined-and-remote-lands-scrap-heap}

The Scrap Heap ({{ja|廃棄世界}}, written *the discarded world*) is the remains of what had been the continent's largest country, destroyed in a single night.[@fc02-scrap-heap-land, fc02-remote-ja] Debris stretches as far as the eye can see, ferocious monsters inhabit it, and it is described as unsuitable for human habitation.[@fc02-scrap-heap-land] [[allen-fromel|Allen Fromel]] and [[anya-fromel|Anya Fromel]] wander its ruins after losing their parents and home, beneath grey clouds and repeated rain.[@fc02-scrap-heap-children] DanMachi 18 recalls [[freya|Freya]] taking in "two kittens who were all alone in a world of ruins", a place the Japanese edition calls by the Scrap Heap's name; they are evidently Allen and his sister {{inference}}.[@fm18-scrap-heap-rescue]

### Dedyne {#ruined-and-remote-lands-dedyne}

[[loki|Loki]] places Dedyne far south of Orario and relays a report that the [[evils|Evils]]' followers there are moving about as if gathering something.[@ar01-dedyne-location] It contains the Black Desert ({{ja|黒の砂漠}}), where the Behemoth hunt of the [[three-great-quests|Three Great Quests]] was fought.[@ar03-dedyne-black-desert, ar03-dedyne-ja] [[astrea|Astrea]] reports medicinal herbs thriving in land long exposed to Behemoth's poison; [[erebus|Erebus]] confirms sending followers to collect ingredients for an antidote that restrains [[zald|Zald]]'s symptoms.[@ar03-dedyne-herbs]

### Heodenings {#ruined-and-remote-lands-heodenings}

[[hegni#heodenings|Heodenings]] ({{ja|ヒャズニング}}) is a forested island in a giant lake on the continent's remote frontiers, containing isolated dark-elf and white-elf states engaged in continuous fighting.[@fc02-heodenings-island, fc02-remote-ja] [[hegni|Hegni Ragnar]] recalls Freya rescuing him and [[hedin#heodenings|Hedin Selrand]] from their battles there.[@fm18-heodenings-memory]
