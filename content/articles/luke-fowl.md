**Luke Fowl** is a human Level 3 student and leader of the [[balder#seventh-squad|7th Squad]] in [[balder#balder-class|Balder Class]].[@so13-introduction, so13-leader] He has ashen hair and tells [[lefiya|Lefiya Viridis]] that he has attended the [[school-district|School District]] for five years and is older than her.[@so13-introduction] [[leon-verdenberg|Leon Verdenberg]] praises his written and practical results, swordsmanship and hard work, and says other students and teachers trust him.[@so13-leader]
